## About

Metriport is a healthcare data infrastructure company that gives developers a universal API to access and manage patient medical records. The company was founded in 2022 by Dima Goncharov and Colin Elsinga, and went through Y Combinator. Metriport describes its focus as providing longitudinal, structured patient data as a foundation for downstream healthcare AI and workflow applications [^1].
