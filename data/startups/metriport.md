## About

Metriport is a healthcare data infrastructure company that gives developers a universal API to access and manage patient medical records. The company was founded in 2022 by Dima Goncharov and Colin Elsinga, and went through Y Combinator. Metriport describes its focus as providing longitudinal, structured patient data as a foundation for downstream healthcare AI and workflow applications [^1][^3]. Notable customers include Amazon One Medical, Sollis Health, and Color Health [^4]. The company achieved HITRUST r2 Certification in the run-up to the Series A [^4].

## What Founders Say

Dima Goncharov (CEO, Co-Founder): "If you've been lucky enough to have a great healthcare experience in the past few years, there's a good chance Metriport helped make it happen." [^1]

Colin Elsinga (COO, Co-Founder): "Relevant, and usable, data from longitudinal patient records is the foundation that all downstream processes and technologies, including AI, are built on.
