## About

Epitome Therapeutics is a Vienna-based biotech company developing precision epigenome editing technology for programmable gene expression [^1][^2]. Founded in 2025 as a spin-out from the European Molecular Biology Laboratory (EMBL), Epitome's proprietary CADENCE platform enables precise, durable activation of endogenous genes without altering the underlying DNA sequence [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by Guido Gualdoni (Co-Founder & CEO) and Jamie Hackett (Co-Founder & Chief Scientific Officer), and is leveraging research from EMBL Rome published in Nature Genetics [^1][^2]. Epitome intends to use the new financing to advance therapeutic programs and grow its team [^1][^2].
