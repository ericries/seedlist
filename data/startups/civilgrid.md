## About

CivilGrid is a San Francisco–based civil design platform that consolidates thousands of fragmented public utility and infrastructure datasets — sourced directly from utility owners and public agencies — into a single collaborative map used by engineering firms and utilities to plan and de-risk underground construction [^1][^2][^3]. Customers include Pacific Gas & Electric, Atmos Energy, Mark Thomas, and GHD; the company says it reduces due-diligence time by up to 85% and helped PG&E identify $60M in avoidable paving costs across 1,600 planned gas-distribution projects [^1][^2].
