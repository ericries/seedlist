## About

Centralize is a San Francisco-based relationship intelligence platform for enterprise sales teams. Its software assembles a live, collaborative map of everyone inside every deal — drawing on email, calendar, CRM, and call data — and uses AI to surface warm paths, flag blind spots, and recommend each rep's next move [^1]. The company positions itself as filling the gap where traditional CRMs were never built to manage enterprise deal relationships [^1].
