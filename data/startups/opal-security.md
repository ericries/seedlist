## About

Opal Security is a San Francisco-based identity and access governance platform that helps enterprises manage permissions across human employees, service accounts, and AI agents [^1][^2]. Founded in 2020 by Umaimah Khan and co-founders, the company provides what it calls an "AI-native access governance platform" featuring real-time visibility, policy-as-code, and direct control over every identity in an organization [^1][^3]. Customers include Databricks, Notion, and Cloudflare [^1]. Howard Ting joined as CEO in December 2025 [^1][^2].
