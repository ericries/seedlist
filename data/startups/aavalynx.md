## About

Aavalynx is a London-based legaltech company that builds an AI platform helping corporate legal teams, law firms, and litigation funders analyse dispute portfolios, forecast litigation outcomes, and make faster data-driven decisions on legal spend [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by Hanna Roos, a former disputes lawyer with nearly two decades at Freshfields, Latham & Watkins, and Quinn Emanuel [^2]. Its stated mission is "death to disputes" — reducing corporate legal exposure by combining legal, financial, and commercial data into an AI-driven decision layer [^1][^2].
