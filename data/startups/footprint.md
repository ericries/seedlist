## About

Footprint is an AI-native compliance and identity platform providing KYC, KYB and AML capabilities for financial institutions, built around an agentic system it calls "Percy" that runs end-to-end investigations by connecting to trusted data sources and preserving organizational memory across cases [^1][^2]. The company markets to risk teams that need to move faster on watchlist and sanctions matching — the Series B announcement highlights that "watchlist hits that once took an analyst 30 minutes to resolve now take less than a minute" [^1]. QED had previously written a public investment memo on Footprint framing identity/KYC as a market where "someone's going to win here, but I'm just not sure how to underwrite who it will be," and describing Footprint's approach as "if you want someone to switch, you can't simply offer something marginally better, you need to offer something that is different" [^3].
