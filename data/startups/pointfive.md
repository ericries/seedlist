## About

PointFive is a New York-based AI and cloud efficiency platform that helps companies reduce spend across cloud infrastructure, data platforms, AI workloads, and coding agents [^1]. Founded in 2023 by Alon Arvatz (CEO), Gal Ben-David (CPO), and Amir Hozez (CTO), the company markets itself as an "AI Efficiency OS" that continuously surfaces optimization opportunities and translates them into engineer-actionable fixes [^1][^2]. Customers include Nubank, E.ON, Hertz, Fanatics, Swiss Post, and NICE, and the company reports a sixfold increase in annual recurring revenue across 2024 and 2025 [^1][^2].
