## About

Rundoo is an AI-native operating system for independent supply stores — paint, hardware, lawn and garden, and farm and feed retailers — founded in 2021 [^1][^3]. The company serves more than 500 stores across the U.S., Canada and the Caribbean, and its AI assistant, Dooey, helps store owners with inventory management, sales forecasting and business analytics [^1][^3]. Rundoo maintains an engineering team in Redwood City, California and a go-to-market team in Chicago [^2].
