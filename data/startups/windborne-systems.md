## About

WindBorne Systems is a Redwood Shores, California-based weather-intelligence company that operates Atlas, a global constellation of long-duration smart balloons paired with WeatherMesh, an AI weather-forecasting model [^1][^2]. The company launches balloons from roughly 20 sites worldwide, deploying approximately 600 balloons that collect atmospheric data — including in remote areas and extreme weather zones — and combines that proprietary dataset with government agency inputs to power its forecasts [^1]. Founded in 2019 by John Dean, Kai Marshland, Paige Brocidiacono, Andrey Sushko, and Joan Creus-Costa, WindBorne raised a $37M Series B on 2026-08-05 co-led by Khosla Ventures and Galvanize at a $250M post-money valuation [^1][^3].
