## About

Enter is a São Paulo-based AI-native legal automation platform founded in 2023 by three former Wildlife Studios executives—Mateus Costa-Ribeiro, Michael Mac-Vicar, and Henrique Vaz—that builds AI agents for mass litigation in Brazil's complex legal environment [^1][^2]. The platform serves the legal departments of Bradesco, Nubank, Mercado Livre, Airbnb, LATAM Airlines, Azul, and 40+ other large enterprises [^1]. The $100M+ Series B announced May 5, 2026 was led by Founders Fund with participation from Ribbit Capital, Sequoia Capital, ONEVC, Atlantico, and Kaszek, valuing Enter at $1.2B and making it Latin America's first AI unicorn [^1][^2][^3]. The company has grown revenue more than 10x and tripled its client base since Series A [^1].
