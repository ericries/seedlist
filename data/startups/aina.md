## About

Aina is an AI hardware startup building input devices designed to control and trigger AI agents rather than passively record ambient data [^1]. The company was founded by Apoorv Shankar, formerly VP of Hardware at smart-ring maker Ultrahuman, who previously ran LazyCo, a hardware interface design startup that Ultrahuman acquired [^1] [^2]. Aina operates offices in San Francisco and Bengaluru [^3] [^4].

The company's first product, Dune, is a three-key aluminum macro keypad for the Mac that launched in April 2026. It is USB-C powered with no battery, and reassigns its keys to whichever application is in the foreground; users configure it in plain language through an integration with Claude [^1]. Aina is preparing to test additional devices including Radiance (a tabletop video call remote) and Shift (a single-tap agentic button) [^1].
