## About

30 Sundays is an India-based AI-native holiday planning and booking platform, founded in 2024 by Kshitij Chaudhary (Co-Founder & CEO; former Boston Consulting Group, IIT Delhi) and Anuj Punjani (Co-Founder & CTO; former Apple) [^1][^2][^3]. The company positions itself as an AI-driven, video-first, full-stack journey planner that replaces traditional travel agency processes, currently offering trips to Bali, Vietnam, the Maldives, and Thailand, and preparing to open New Zealand, Mauritius, Georgia, Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, and the Philippines [^1][^2][^3]. As of the Series A announcement, the company reports an annualised gross booking value run-rate of approximately Rs 200 crore and cites customer metrics including an NPS of 67 and a 4.6 Google rating [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-21, 30 Sundays announced a Rs 61 crore (approximately $6.7 million) Series A led by Bessemer Venture Partners, with existing investors Info Edge Ventures and Eximius Ventures increasing their stake [^1][^2][^3]. Total capital raised to date is approximately $7.5 million, following an earlier seed round from Info Edge Ventures and Eximius Ventures [^2][^3].
