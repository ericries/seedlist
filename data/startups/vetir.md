## About

Vêtir is a New York-based AI-powered luxury wardrobe operating system founded and led by CEO Kate Davidson Hudson, building a system of record for the wardrobe — what clients own, what they want, how they live — to help luxury brands and stylists deliver more precise, high-touch experiences and increase long-term customer value [^1][^2][^3]. The $5.5M Series A first close announced May 21, 2026 was led by Laidlaw & Company at a $150M valuation, with participation from a curated consortium of technology and luxury-adjacent family offices; the company also added Stephanie Horton (Senior Director of Global Commerce at Google) and Nina Garcia (Editor-in-Chief of ELLE, Project Runway judge) to its Advisory Board and named Jemma Plaue (formerly Mytheresa) as SVP of Global Styling & Strategy [^1][^2][^3].
