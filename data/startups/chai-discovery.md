## About

Chai Discovery is a San Francisco AI-for-drug-discovery startup founded in 2024 by Joshua Meier, Jack Dent, Matthew McPartlon, and Jacques Boitreaud. The company builds AI models to predict and reprogram molecular interactions to accelerate pre-clinical drug discovery, and says its models are already deployed at large pharmaceutical partners including Lilly, Novartis, and Pfizer [^1] [^2].
