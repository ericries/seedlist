## About

Clasp is a Boston-based healthcare workforce platform founded in 2018 by Tess Michaels that ties student loan repayment to clinician tenure — employers commit to clinicians before graduation and repay their student loans over time in exchange for sustained employment [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports retention rates 2.5x higher than traditional hiring models and more than $130M in employer commitments across thousands of programs [^1][^2]. The $20M Series B announced March 26, 2026 was co-led by Crosslink Capital and Digitalis Ventures, with participation from Juvo, Strada Education, and operator-investors Frank Britt (former Penn Foster CEO and Starbucks Chief Strategy Officer) and Jason Nazar (founder of Comparably and Docstoc); the capital will accelerate expansion across health systems and deepen university partnerships [^1][^2][^3].
