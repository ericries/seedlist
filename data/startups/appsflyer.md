## About

AppsFlyer is a mobile attribution and marketing measurement platform founded in 2011 by Oren Kaniel and Reshef Mann; it serves more than 15,000 brands globally and has roughly 1,300 employees, with offices in Israel and the U.S. [^1][^2][^3]. The company is profitable, generates positive cash flow, and was reported to be running at roughly $500 million in annual recurring revenue at the time of the round [^1][^2]. On June 22, 2026 the company announced more than $1 billion of new strategic investment at a $2.7 billion post-money valuation, with Google, Meta, Unity and Moloco each taking minority stakes alongside existing institutional shareholders; the company also said it intends to go public [^1][^2][^4]. AppsFlyer emphasized that all four strategic stakes are minority, without control rights or preferential access to APIs, measurement signals, methodologies or commercial terms [^2].
