## About

State Affairs is a Washington, D.C.-based AI-powered policy and regulatory intelligence platform that combines a large embedded network of statehouse reporters with an AI knowledge graph tracking bills, regulations, and hearings across all 50 U.S. states and the federal government [^1][^2][^3]. The company publishes more than 2,000 originally reported, nonpartisan articles each month and says its platform is in active use by roughly one-third of state and federal elected officials, with enterprise customers including Walmart, Mastercard, and McDonald's [^1][^2][^3]. Co-founder and CEO Evan Burns previously co-founded The Finnish Long Drink (acquired), and co-founder Jamie Roberts Seltzer is a general partner at LightShed Ventures; founding editor-in-chief Alison Bethel joined from a career in journalism leadership [^1][^2][^3].

On 2026-07-14 State Affairs announced a $70 million round led by Founders Fund and Khosla Ventures, with participation from Tru Arrow Partners, Alumni Ventures, former Washington Post executive editor Marcus Brauchli, The Athletic co-founders Alex Mather and Adam Hansmann, and KKR media chairman Richard Sarnoff [^1][^2][^3]. The round type was not disclosed in the announcement [^1][^2][^3].
