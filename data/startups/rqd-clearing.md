## About

RQD Clearing (stylized RQD*) is a technology-driven clearing and custody firm serving broker-dealers, RIAs, and foreign financial institutions accessing U.S. markets [^1]. The company operates proprietary cloud-based clearing infrastructure and reports processing 543 million ledger transactions year-to-date, representing approximately 2.43% of NMS equities market share [^2][^3]. RQD Clearing was founded in 2021 and maintains offices at 1 World Trade Center in New York and 425 S. Financial Place in Chicago [^1][^4].
