## About

Backoffice is a Vilnius-based startup building an operations management platform for restaurants, cafes, hotels, and other hospitality businesses [^1][^2]. Founded in 2025 by Erikas Pakėnas and Denis Stech, the platform is designed to simplify day-to-day operations from shift scheduling and payroll to internal communication [^1]. As of the announcement, Backoffice is operating in more than 40 locations across Lithuania, ranging from Michelin-starred restaurants to late-night bars, with approximately 500 registered users [^1][^2]. On May 25, 2026, Backoffice announced a €150K pre-seed round from venture capital funds FIRSTPICK and Lost Astronaut [^1][^2].

## What Investors Say

> Marijus Andrijauskas of FIRSTPICK has highlighted Backoffice's ability to move fast and learn from the market, noting the potential for the product to become infrastructure for the entire hospitality sector, given that the average hospitality business uses 5 or more different systems [^1].

## What Founders Say

> Co-founder Erikas Pakėnas has said that he grew up watching his mother run a restaurant and bakery business, and built Backoffice to help hospitality business owners drowning in operational work [^1].
