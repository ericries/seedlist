## About

Mafer AI is a Barcelona-based AI startup founded in late 2025 by CEO Fernando Oliver Jané and CTO Marc Montalbo Burges, building MaferOS — an AI-native operating system for R&D teams in formulation industries spanning specialty chemicals, food, beverages, cosmetics, personal care, fragrances, flavours, pharma, and nutraceuticals [^1][^2][^3][^4]. Six months after launch the company reports close to €300K in committed ARR with three production clients in fragrance and flavours, has structured 50,000+ technical documents, and was selected by the Barcelona Supercomputing Centre for its AI Factory programme (batches 1 and 2) with 50,000+ GPU-hours on NVIDIA H100 / MareNostrum V infrastructure [^1][^2]. The €2M pre-seed announced May 29, 2026 was led by Kfund with participation from 4Founders Capital, Masia, Lavanda Ventures (Puig family), and angel investors including Adrián Mato (a16z scout), Manuel Roca (Atrápalo founder), Dídac Lee, Fernando Castiñeiras (Athos Capital managing partner), Juanjo Mostazo, and professionals from MIT, Google, Samaipata, Bain, BCG, Shakers, and Happy Robot [^1][^2][^3][^4].
