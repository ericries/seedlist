## About

Aligned is an AI-powered B2B sales workspace that centralizes deal execution between sellers, buyers, and AI agents, aiming to replace fragmented email/attachment workflows with a single collaborative "deal room" [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2021 by Gal Aga, Gal Deitsch, and Yotam Sela and operates R&D in Tel Aviv with offices in New York and Europe [^3]. Aligned reports serving roughly 70,000 salespeople and 1 million buyers per month, with named customers including Deel, Similarweb, and WordPress [^3]. Following the Series B, the company plans to grow from 55 to 85 employees by year-end [^3].
