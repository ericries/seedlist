## About

Hadrius is a New York-based fintech company building agentic AI compliance infrastructure for financial services firms, consolidating the compliance lifecycle — communications review, marketing review, trade surveillance and other functions — into a single AI-native system of record [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports serving more than 500 financial institutions and investment firms, and claims its platform reduces false positives by 95% and manual compliance work by 70% [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-14 Hadrius announced a combined $27M in seed and Series A funding — including a $22M Series A — led by CRV, with participation from Y Combinator, Pathlight Ventures, and founders from Altruist, Jump AI and FINNY [^1][^2][^3][^4].
