## About

iPronics is a Valencia, Spain-based programmable photonics company founded in 2019 as a spin-off from Universitat Politècnica de València, with an additional office in Santa Clara, California [^1]. The company builds programmable optical networking silicon for AI data centres. On September 2, 2026 iPronics announced a $125 million Series B co-led by Maverick Silicon and Light Street Capital to scale its programmable optical networking platform for AI data centre interconnect [^1][^2]. NVIDIA, Triatomic Capital, Bosch Ventures, Catalight Capital, the European Innovation Council Fund, The Tate Family Trust, Fine Structure Ventures, Amadeus Capital Partners, Build Collective, and Criteria Venture Tech also participated [^1].
