## About

iPronics is a Valencia, Spain-based programmable photonics company founded in 2019 as a spin-off from Universitat Politècnica de València, with an additional office in Santa Clara, California [^1]. The company builds programmable optical networking silicon for AI data centres. On September 2, 2026 iPronics announced a $125 million Series B co-led by Maverick Silicon and Light Street Capital to scale its programmable optical networking platform for AI data centre interconnect, bringing total funding to date to $177 million [^1][^3]. NVIDIA, Triatomic Capital, Bosch Ventures, Catalight Capital, the European Innovation Council Fund, The Tate Family Trust, Fine Structure Ventures, Amadeus Capital Partners, Build Collective, and Criteria Venture Tech also participated [^1][^3]. TechStartups' September 2, 2026 tech-news roundup framed the round as an "AI networking round backed by Nvidia" and noted the strategic thesis that "faster accelerators deliver diminishing returns when processors cannot exchange information quickly enough" [^4].
