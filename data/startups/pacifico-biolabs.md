## About

Pacifico Biolabs is a Leipzig-based food-ingredient company co-founded in 2022 by CEO Zac Austin (formerly McKinsey and the UK Cabinet Office Covid Taskforce) and CSO Washington Logrono (PhD from Helmholtz Centre at University of Leipzig; former fermentation lead at the German Biomass Research Center) that runs a mycelium-fermentation process designed to operate inside standard, idle beer-brewery tanks — converting disused European brewery capacity into alternative-protein production [^1][^2][^3]. The company's flagship product, Viando Chicken, is a whole-cut chicken alternative; the asset-light brewery-tank approach is intended to cut capex, increase "European protein sovereignty," and accelerate the path to price parity with conventional meat [^1][^2]. The €7M Series A announced May 27, 2026 was led by Stray Dog Capital with participation from TGFS, Sprout & About Ventures, Simon Capital, FoodLabs, and a regional brewery partner; the round follows Pacifico's $3.3M pre-seed announced in 2024 and brings total funding to approximately €10.3M+ [^1][^2][^3].
