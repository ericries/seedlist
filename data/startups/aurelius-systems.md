## About

Aurelius Systems is a San Francisco–based defense technology company building autonomous directed-energy platforms for counter-drone defense, led by co-founder and CEO Michael LaFramboise [^1][^2]. Its flagship system, Archimedes, combines AI-powered sensing with robotic directed-energy hardware to autonomously identify, track, and engage aerial threats [^1][^2]. Aurelius previously raised a $10M seed in September 2025 led by General Catalyst and Draper Associates [^3].
