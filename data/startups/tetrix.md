## About

Tetrix is an AI-native intelligence platform that helps institutional limited partners and allocators extract and structure data from private market financial documents, converting unstructured documents into real-time analytics for risk management and investment decisions [^1][^2]. The company says more than $100B in AUM is managed on the platform, with customers spanning endowments, foundations, pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, fund of funds, and family offices [^1][^2].

Co-founded by Olivier Babin (CEO) and Naunidh Singh Bhalla (CTO), Tetrix is headquartered in New York City with an office in Montréal; roughly 10 of its 25 employees are based in Canada [^2]. The company previously raised a $5M seed in 2024 [^3].
