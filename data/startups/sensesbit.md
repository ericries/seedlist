## About

Sensesbit is a Galician foodtech company based in Santiago de Compostela, Spain that applies artificial intelligence to sensory analysis of food and beverage products [^1][^2]. The company was founded by four scientists from a research group at the University of Santiago de Compostela (USC), with Maruxa Quiroga serving as CEO and co-founder [^1][^2]. Sensesbit reports operations in more than ten countries and over 100 recurring industrial clients [^1]. Its technology was named the most innovative startup at the Ftalks Food Summit Startup Awards 2025 [^1]. On July 17, 2026, Sensesbit announced a €1 million funding round to accelerate international expansion across Europe and the Americas, expand its team, and enhance platform capabilities [^1][^2][^3].
