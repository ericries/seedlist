## About

microagi is a Munich-based robotics-deployment company building software to move factory robots from demonstrations into production, and to train the next generation of humanoid robots on real-world work [^1][^2]. The company's core platform, Atlas, captures factory data, expands it in simulation and fine-tunes models for plant-specific tasks; a separate data arm called Shift pays roughly 20,000 people across 15 countries to film themselves performing physical work, generating the training data microagi uses to teach robots factory and household tasks [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-16 microagi announced a $55M seed round — reported as the largest seed round in German startup history — led by Hummingbird with participation from Northzone, LocalGlobe, Village Global and Redalpine [^1][^2][^3][^4]. The company was founded roughly ten months earlier by former Formula 1 engineers from Red Bull Racing and Mercedes-AMG Petronas, together with a WhatsApp entrepreneur and an Alan Turing Institute researcher [^1][^2].
