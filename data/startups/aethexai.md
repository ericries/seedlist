## About

AethexAI is a voice AI startup building customer support and service automation systems for enterprises in Africa and the Middle East [^1]. Founded in 2025 by Mariama Diallo (CEO, formerly Goldman Sachs) and Ayooluwa Odemuyiwa (CTO, formerly Meta), the company develops its own small language models — the Kora series, ranging from 300 million to 1.7 billion parameters — and an orchestration layer to handle automated calls with localized support for English, French, and Arabic dialects [^1]. The platform currently handles over 17,000 calls daily, primarily for use cases including debt collection, customer activation, and KYC verification [^1].
