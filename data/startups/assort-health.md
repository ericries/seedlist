## About

Assort Health is an AI agents platform for the patient journey in healthcare, handling tasks from scheduling and intake to referrals, forms, document processing, medication refills, and payments [^1][^2]. The company says its platform is built on 190 million specialty patient interactions and that customers see a 5% lift in appointment volume, a 115% increase in labor capacity, and a 4.3 out of 5 patient satisfaction score [^1]. Revenue grew 20x in the 15 months preceding the Series C [^1].

The Series C valued Assort Health at $1.2 billion and brought total funding to more than $222 million [^1][^2]. JP Sanday, Partner at Menlo Ventures, joined the board; fellow Menlo partner Matt Murphy serves as a board observer [^1].
