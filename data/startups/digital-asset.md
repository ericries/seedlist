## About

Digital Asset, founded in 2014 and based in New York, is the developer of the Canton Network, a permissioned Layer 1 blockchain designed for institutional finance with configurable privacy and identity controls [^1][^2]. On June 11, 2026, the company announced a $355 million funding round to accelerate Canton's adoption as onchain infrastructure for capital markets [^1][^3]. Yuval Rooz, co-founder and CEO, said the funding will support partnerships, acquisitions, and ecosystem expansion [^1]. Visa joined Canton as a Super Validator in March 2026 and later included Canton in its stablecoin settlement pilot [^3].
