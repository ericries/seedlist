## About

Félix is a Miami-based WhatsApp-native cross-border remittance startup founded in 2020 by Manuel Godoy and Bernardo García. It lets users send money between the US and Latin America directly through WhatsApp. Its September 2026 Series C — $87M in equity plus $113M in debt — brought total funding to roughly $300M and reportedly tripled the valuation set in its earlier $75M Series B, pushing the company into unicorn status [^1].
