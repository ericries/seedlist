## About

Magnitude Biosciences is a Durham, U.K.-based contract research organisation and Durham University spinout, founded in 2018, that develops VivoScan — a proprietary in vivo high-throughput screening platform using C. elegans nematodes combined with automated imaging, robotics, and AI to evaluate how whole organisms respond to potential drug treatments [^1]. On 2026-09-15, the company announced £1.3M (€1.5M) in seed/growth funding led by Northstar Ventures (via the North East Spinout Inspire Fund, North East Innovation Fund, and Northstar EIS Growth Fund), with participation from Maven's regional funds and new angel investors, plus a £217k (€253k) Innovate UK grant [^1].
