## About

Shatterdome Energy is a San Francisco-based AI-driven energy trading platform founded by quantitative trader Amann Shariff that aggregates renewable generation, battery storage, and flexible industrial demand into coordinated virtual power plant networks, then uses real-time grid data (weather forecasts, congestion patterns, ramp-rate pressures, price signals) to autonomously decide when energy should be produced, stored, or sold and to execute those trades programmatically [^1][^2][^3]. The company emerged from stealth on May 21, 2026 with $3.5M in pre-seed funding led by Crucible Capital and joined by Transpose Platform and Entrepreneurs First; Shatterdome has already moved 200 MWh of power within three months of inception and reports 1.5 GW of assets in its pipeline [^1][^2][^3].
