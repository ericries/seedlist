## About

Wrtn Technologies is a Seoul-based AI services platform building a natural-language interface portal that lets users complete tasks across the web [^1][^2]. Founded in 2021 by Lee Se-young, Wrtn also operates OOC, a North American-focused AI entertainment platform launched in May 2026 that surpassed ₩10B ($7.22M) in monthly revenue within three months of launch [^1][^2]. The Series C round makes Wrtn the first Korean AI service startup to pass the ₩1 trillion valuation mark [^1].
