## About

InRisk Labs is an Ahmedabad-headquartered technology-driven risk and reinsurance platform that has evolved from an insurtech software provider into an integrated reinsurance group with licensed risk-bearing capacity [^1][^2][^3]. Its subsidiary, EarthRe Insurance IFSC Limited, received a license from the International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA) to operate as a reinsurer — the first incorporated reinsurer licensed at India's GIFT City IFSC [^1][^2][^3]. The company builds parametric and structured reinsurance products for natural catastrophe and climate risk, integrating climate, geospatial, and satellite data into underwriting [^1][^2].

Proceeds will deepen underwriting, actuarial, catastrophe-modelling, and AI capabilities; expand coverage across natural catastrophe, climate risk, marine cargo and motor segments; and accelerate development of parametric and structured reinsurance products [^1][^2][^3].

## What Investors Say

Per Entrackr's coverage of the Series A, Bessemer Venture Partners Partner Vishal Gupta said the firm backed InRisk at seed stage and has now doubled down on its investment in the Series A [^1]. Northpoint Capital Founder and CIO Sameer Brij Verma said that EarthRe "combines a licensed balance sheet with an AI native risk platform and deep underwriting expertise" [^1].
