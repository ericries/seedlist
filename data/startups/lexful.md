## About

Lexful is a Salt Lake City-based AI-native IT documentation platform built exclusively for managed services providers (MSPs) [^1][^2]. The platform automatically captures and maintains IT documentation — turning static, outdated records and tribal knowledge into contextual intelligence accessible through an "Ask Lex" interface [^2][^3]. Lexful integrates with major PSA and RMM tools including Autotask, ConnectWise, NinjaOne, and HaloPSA, and is set to reach general availability in July 2026 following a February preview launch [^2][^3]. The company is led by CEO Pinar Ormeci and counts IT Glue founder Chris Day as a strategic advisor on product vision [^1][^3].
