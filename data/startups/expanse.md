## About

Expanse is a predictive AI infrastructure platform that forecasts computational requirements (memory, runtime, failure risk) before workloads execute — helping teams running their own clusters (SLURM, Kubernetes, or Nomad) reduce GPU waste and improve utilization [^1][^2]. Its two products are Expanse Analyse (pre-submission prediction) and Expanse Diagnose (root-cause analysis for failed jobs). The company reports that one national supercomputing center customer identified more than $8 million in monthly compute waste, and that Expanse's predictions are "8× more accurate" than frontier LLMs on runtime and memory estimation [^2]. On 2026-09-16, Expanse announced a €4.5M ($5.3M) Seed round led by Crane Venture Partners with participation from PXN Ventures, AIP Seed, and angel investors including former DeepMind researchers [^1]. The company is SOC 2 Type II certified [^2].
