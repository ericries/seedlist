## About

Balance Theory is a Columbia, Maryland-based provider of an AI-native platform for cybersecurity investment decisions and execution [^1][^2]. The company helps CISOs and enterprise security teams manage cybersecurity budgets, portfolios, and vendor relationships with proprietary market intelligence and AI-powered agents [^1][^2]. Balance Theory reports managing more than $1B in security investment spend with customers realizing an average first-year ROI exceeding 300% [^1]. Cybersecurity executive Dan Burns (founder of Accuvant and former CEO of Optiv) joined as executive chairman as part of the round [^1].
