## About

Aeon is a Zurich-based preventive healthcare startup that combines AI-powered whole-body MRI screening (advertised as detecting more than 500 anomalies), blood diagnostics covering 100+ biomarkers, and genetic risk analysis to produce longitudinal health records with personalised risk prediction for consumers [^1]. On 2026-09-14, EU-Startups reported that Aeon had raised a seed extension bringing total seed funding above €12 million, led by Araya Ventures with participation from Concentric, GoHub Ventures, Kadmos Capital, Calm/Storm Ventures, N&V Capital, and Bigwave Capital; Aeon also announced the acquisition of Berlin-based consumer blood diagnostics platform Aware Health as part of the same news [^1]. The company had previously raised a €8.2 million seed round in June 2025 [^1].
