## About

Cyera is an AI-powered data security platform that helps enterprises discover, classify, and protect sensitive information across cloud environments, data stores, and SaaS applications [^1] [^2]. The company operates in the data security posture management (DSPM) category, mapping where sensitive data lives, classifying it by sensitivity, and flagging exposure — with the goal of enabling enterprises to safely feed data into AI tools while tracking access [^2]. Cyera was founded in 2021 and is led by CEO Yotam Segev and CTO Tamar Bar-Ilan [^1] [^3]. Customers reportedly include roughly one-fifth of the Fortune 500 [^2].
