## About

5U AI is a Munich, Germany-based startup building multi-agent AI workers for freight forwarders, automating tasks including quoting, bookings, shipment tracking, invoice reconciliation, data entry, and routine administration across air, sea, and road freight [^1][^2]. The platform's Context Layer captures the reasoning behind each freight decision, helping teams build knowledge over time rather than automating isolated tasks [^1][^2]. Founded in 2025 by Technical University of Munich graduates Yagiz Abik (CEO) and Fehmi Şener, the company announced a $3.2 million pre-seed round on 2026-07-28, led by London-based Emerge Capital (a $75 million pre-seed fund focused on future-of-work and human capital) with participation from senior logistics executives from DHL, GEODIS, DSV, Maersk, and Ceva Logistics [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds will accelerate product development and go-to-market across Europe [^1].
