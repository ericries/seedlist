## About

Astranis is a San Francisco-based satellite communications company founded in 2015 that designs, manufactures, and operates small geostationary (GEO) satellites for commercial customers and U.S. government programs [^1]. CEO and co-founder John Gedmark leads the company [^1]. On May 6, 2026, Astranis announced a $300M Series E co-led by Snowpoint Ventures and Franklin Templeton at a $2.8B valuation, alongside a delayed-draw credit facility of up to $155M from Trinity Capital [^2][^3]. Total capital raised across the company's history exceeds $1.2B [^2].
