## About

The Boring Company is a tunnel construction and infrastructure company founded by Elon Musk in 2016, headquartered in Bastrop, Texas, that develops boring machines and underground transportation systems [^2]. The company operates the Vegas Loop, an underground transportation system in Las Vegas, and is developing the Dubai Loop as part of an expanded partnership with the United Arab Emirates [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-09, The Boring Company announced a $3 billion Series D at a $23 billion valuation led by the United Arab Emirates and affiliated investment entities, with participation from Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Valor Equity Partners, Human Capital, Vy Capital, Temasek, Shamal Holding, and Baron Capital [^1][^2]. The company said it plans to dig more than 150 kilometers of tunnels in the UAE as part of the expanded Dubai Loop partnership [^1].
