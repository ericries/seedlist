## About

Arcade builds an authorization and governance platform for production AI agents, enabling enterprises to control which actions agents can take across business applications [^1]. The company authored the MCP authorization spec and runs agent actions in production across Fortune 500 customers [^1]. Co-founders Alex Salazar (CEO) and Sam Partee (CTO) lead the company [^2].

## What Founders Say

CEO Alex Salazar has stated Arcade aims to be "the secure action layer behind every production AI agent" [^1].

On the origin story of the platform, Salazar wrote in the seed-round announcement post: "We'd created something that didn't just unblock our agent -- it could unblock anyone trying to build an agent that does more than just information retrieval." [^4]
