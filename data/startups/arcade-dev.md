## About

Arcade builds an authorization and governance platform for production AI agents, enabling enterprises to control which actions agents can take across business applications [^1]. The company authored the MCP authorization spec and runs agent actions in production across Fortune 500 customers [^1]. Co-founders Alex Salazar (CEO) and Sam Partee (CTO) lead the company [^2].
