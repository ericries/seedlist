## About

Jedify is a New York-based AI infrastructure company building a "context graph" platform that connects to enterprise knowledge sources via APIs, enabling AI agents to better understand business-specific information including data relationships, permissions, and terminology [^1]. Assaf Henkin is co-founder and CEO [^1].
