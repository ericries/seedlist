## About

CREW Carbon is a New Haven, CT-based climate technology company providing wastewater treatment facilities with a process intensification solution that improves treatment performance, lowers operating costs, and permanently removes greenhouse gases via mineralization [^1][^2]. The company is a Yale University spinout founded in 2022 and has delivered verified carbon removal credits to corporations including JP Morgan, Google, Autodesk, and Stripe through the Frontier consortium [^1][^2]. The $25M Series A announced May 14, 2026 — comprising $19M in equity and $6M in grant/non-dilutive funding — was led by Burnt Island Ventures with participation from AP Ventures, Sony Innovation Fund, Builders Vision, Kibo Invest, Idemitsu Ventures, New York Ventures, and family-office investors, alongside existing investors Counteract, ANIMO Ventures, Connecticut Innovations, Ponderosa Ventures, and Echo River Capital [^1][^2].
