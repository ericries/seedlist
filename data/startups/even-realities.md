## About

Even Realities is a Shenzhen-based consumer hardware company founded in 2023 by ex-Apple engineers to build display-first smart glasses. CEO Will Wang previously worked on Apple Watch and iPhone; co-founders came from tech and luxury eyewear firms including Lindberg. The company launched its first product, the G1, in 2024 and its flagship Even G2 in November 2025 — the G2 pairs with a companion "Even R1" smart ring and beams navigation, translation, and notifications directly into the wearer's line of sight through waveguide optics, deliberately omitting cameras to emphasize privacy [^1][^2][^3].

## What Founders Say

Will Wang, founder and CEO, told TechCrunch that while rivals chase camera-equipped devices built around content capture and AI, Even Realities is "betting on display-first glasses that beam information straight into the wearer's line of sight without giving up privacy" [^1].
