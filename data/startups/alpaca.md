## About

Alpaca is a San Mateo–based agent-first, API-first prime brokerage that lets financial companies embed investing in stocks, ETFs, options, crypto, and tokenized assets into their own products [^1][^2]. Co-founded and led by Yoshi Yokokawa, the company positions itself as infrastructure for both traditional financial firms and AI-native applications that transact on behalf of end users [^1][^3]. On 2026-07-16 Alpaca announced a $135M equity Series E led by Peak XV, bringing its total new financing (including debt) to $435M [^1][^2][^3].
