## About

SyntheticFi is a San Francisco-based fintech platform that enables registered investment advisors (RIAs) to access low-cost, tax-efficient financing solutions for their clients [^1][^2]. The platform helps advisors implement portfolio-backed financing strategies including box spreads and synthetic variable prepaid forwards [^1][^3]. Founded in 2023 by Tony Yang and co-founders, the company serves more than 300 advisory firms and over 3,000 individual financial advisors, and on June 9, 2026 announced $13 million in new funding alongside a milestone of surpassing $2 billion in regulatory assets under management (RAUM) [^1][^2][^3].
