## About

Luxonis builds the perception layer for robots and automated systems, combining multiple sensors and on-device compute into its OAK camera devices, paired with an open-source software ecosystem that lets developers deploy visual perception AI for physical automation use cases [^1][^2]. Founded in 2019 and headquartered in Colorado, Luxonis grew for seven years on product revenue and two Kickstarter campaigns before taking outside institutional capital, serving thousands of customers including more than 60 Fortune 500 companies and 17 of the Dow Jones 30 [^1][^2]. The company announced a $14M Series A on 2026-07-02 led by Denali Growth Partners with participation from Taiwania Capital, to accelerate commercial expansion and scale production of the OAK camera platform [^1][^2][^3].
