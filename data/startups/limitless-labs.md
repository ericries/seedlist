## About

Limitless Labs is a Tel Aviv-based developer of an Agentic Physical AI platform for CAD/CAM in mechanical parts manufacturing [^1][^2][^3]. The company's Physical AI Foundation Model powers an Agent that helps automate CNC programming for precision manufacturing [^1]. Limitless Labs was founded in 2024 by David Priev (CEO), Assaf Peleg, and Shahaf Finder, and is expanding its commercial presence in the United States [^1][^2]. With this round, the company's total funding to date stands at $27.3 million [^1].
