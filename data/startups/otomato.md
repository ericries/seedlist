## About

Otomato is a portfolio-aware intelligence layer for DeFi that monitors users' on-chain positions 24/7 and alerts them when something materially changes their risk, cost, or opportunity [^1][^2]. The platform targets active DeFi users who manage positions across multiple protocols and chains and are otherwise reliant on switching between dashboards and generic alert tools that produce excess noise [^1].

The company was founded by Dylan Breugne and Clément Hecquet [^1]. The May 2026 financing was Improbable's first external investment under its sharpened focus on companies building at the intersection of AI and web3, following the launch of the Somnia Layer 1 blockchain [^1][^2].
