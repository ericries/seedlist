## About

Rillet is an AI-native ERP and accounting platform for modern finance teams, founded in 2024 by CEO Nicolas Kopp (previously U.S. CEO of N26) [^3]. The company embeds AI agents inside a real-time general ledger with human approval and a full audit trail, and reports serving more than 600 customers, including publicly listed enterprises [^1][^2]. Rillet has also launched an alliance with Ernst & Young and is an official partner with more than half of the Accounting Today top 20 CPA firms [^1][^2].
