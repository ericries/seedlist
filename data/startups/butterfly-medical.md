## About

Butterfly Medical is a Yokneam, Israel-based medical device company founded in 2014 that is developing the Butterfly device — a first-line interventional therapy for benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH), a non-surgical implant that retracts the prostate to relieve lower urinary tract symptoms [^1][^2]. The $21M upsized Series C announced May 14, 2026 included participation from the European Innovation Council (EIC), Junson Capital, MedVenture, and New Rhein Healthcare Investors; proceeds will fund the FDA regulatory pathway and global commercialization [^1][^2].
