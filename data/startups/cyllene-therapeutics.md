## About

Cyllene Therapeutics (formerly known as EG 427) is a Paris-based clinical-stage genetic-medicines company developing localized, targeted DNA therapies using its proprietary HERMES platform, which employs a non-replicating herpes simplex virus type 1 (HSV-1) vector to deliver payloads to specific tissue types [^1][^2][^3]. The company's lead candidate, EG110A, is being developed for severe neurogenic bladder conditions, having reduced urinary incontinence episodes by more than 88% in a Phase Ib/IIa trial for neurogenic detrusor overactivity; the company plans to initiate a Phase IIb/III study in 2027 and expand into overactive bladder and additional neurological indications including pain and migraine [^2][^3]. The rebrand from EG 427 to Cyllene, announced with the Series C, references the birthplace of the Greek god Hermes, echoing the HERMES platform name [^2].
