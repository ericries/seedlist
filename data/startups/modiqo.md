## About

Modiqo is an agentic AI infrastructure startup founded by Chetan Conikee that builds Rote, a local execution layer designed to capture what happens during AI agent runs and convert successfully executed tasks into deterministic, reusable workflows [^1][^2]. Rote connects AI agents to APIs without requiring server infrastructure, sidecars, or SDKs, cutting token usage by reserving inference for the parts of a task that genuinely require it [^1][^2]. The $3M pre-seed announced May 28, 2026 was co-led by Heavybit and Seligman Ventures with participation from Irregular Expressions and angel investors [^1][^2][^3].
