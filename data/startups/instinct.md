## About

Instinct is a San Francisco–based personal AI assistant startup operated by Spear Street Technology, founded in 2025 by 23-year-old Noah Shinn (previously at Sierra) [^1][^2][^3]. Users interact with Instinct via text messages and phone calls, and the agent can connect to email, calendars, and other services to book travel, make reservations, manage inboxes, and complete purchases [^1][^2]. The product remains in private beta as of the Series B [^1][^3].
