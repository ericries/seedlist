## About

Xentra is a Leeds-based cybersecurity, safeguarding and compliance provider serving small and medium-sized businesses and the education sector [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-20 the company announced a £2.7 million (approximately €3.18 million) equity investment from the Maven VCTs and NPIF II – Maven Equity Finance, both managed by Maven Capital Partners as part of the Northern Powerhouse Investment Fund II [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports that it quadrupled annual recurring revenue in the period preceding the round and will use the funding to strengthen senior leadership, accelerate customer acquisition, and expand its STAX modular subscription product suite [^1][^2][^3]. Reported clients include Leeds United FC and Leeds Bradford Airport [^3].
