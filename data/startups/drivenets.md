## About

DriveNets is an Israel-based networking company building disaggregated networking solutions for AI infrastructure and large-scale service providers [^1]. The company's Network Cloud architecture transforms the economics of large-scale networks while maximizing performance, utilization, and operational efficiency [^1][^2]. DriveNets reports more than $1 billion in secured business and has been cash-flow positive since 2025 [^1][^3].

Adam Fisher, Partner at Bessemer Venture Partners, stated: "DriveNets is wiring what comes next: Heterogeneous AI." Bessemer led the $410M Series D as an existing portfolio company backed since DriveNets' Series A [^3].
