## About

Shiplog is a Paris-based AI-powered customer intelligence platform for B2B SaaS, built around Ada — an AI agent that evaluates each customer individually and decides the next best action across the customer lifecycle in real time [^1][^2]. Instead of static segments or health scores, Ada continuously updates each customer's profile as behaviour changes and personalises downstream marketing, product interfaces, onboarding, recommendations, support, and lifecycle communications [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2026 by Khushi Mehta (CEO) and Mehdi Gribaa, who met through Entrepreneurs First and operate from Station F in Paris [^2].
