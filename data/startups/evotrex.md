## About

Evotrex is a Los Angeles-based startup developing hybrid recreational vehicles that combine battery power with an onboard gas engine to extend off-grid range, an architecture commonly referred to as an extended-range electric vehicle (EREV) [^1]. The company emerged from stealth in November 2025 to debut the PG5, a power-generating RV travel trailer, at CES 2026 [^2]. Premium trim of the PG5 is priced around $160,000 [^1]. Anker Innovations is among Evotrex's seed-stage backers [^1][^2].
