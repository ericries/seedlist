## About

Advanced Machine Intelligence (AMI) is a Paris-based AI lab founded in 2025 by computer science pioneer Yann LeCun (Turing Award winner, former Meta AI chief, non-executive chairman) and CEO Alexandre Lebrun [^1][^2]. The company develops "world models"—AI systems designed to learn from and interact with three-dimensional physical reality, contrasting with current large language model approaches; LeCun's JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) work informs the technical direction [^1][^2]. AMI operates globally from Paris, New York, Montreal, and Singapore [^2]. The $1.03B Seed round announced March 10, 2026 was led by Bezos Expeditions, Cathay Innovation, Greycroft, Hiro Capital, and HV Capital at a $3.5B pre-money valuation, making it Europe's largest seed round ever [^1][^2][^3].
