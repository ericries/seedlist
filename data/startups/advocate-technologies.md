## About

Advocate Technologies is a New York City-based insurtech that has operated privately since 2020 and publicly launched with its Seed round in August 2026 [^1][^2]. The company builds an AI-powered platform that reads commercial insurance policies, converts them into standardized data, and lets brokers, lenders, risk managers, and commercial property owners compare pricing and coverage against market benchmarks [^1][^2]. The underlying dataset represents approximately $7.3 billion in commercial insurance premiums and 70,000 policies [^2]. As part of the launch, Advocate is opening Market Terminal, a per-seat self-service product for brokers, lenders, risk managers, and property owners [^2].
