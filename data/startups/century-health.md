## About

Century Health is a New York-based health technology company building an AI-powered real-world evidence (RWE) platform that automates curation of unstructured clinical data from electronic health records [^1][^2]. Its proprietary CHARM (Century Health Abstraction & Retrieval Model) platform achieved 97% accuracy in clinical data abstraction when validated against clinical expert judgment [^1]. The company says it expanded its specialty provider data network 60x over the past year, capturing longitudinal data across neurology, nephrology, ophthalmology, and immunology [^1][^2]. The May 19, 2026 $5M seed round was led by Origin Ventures with participation from InnovateHealth Ventures, 25madison, Next Play Ventures, 2048 Ventures, Alumni Ventures, and strategic angels including Citeline founder Zorba Lieberman [^1][^2].
