## About

NOCD is a Chicago-based digital-health company that provides evidence-based treatment for obsessive-compulsive disorder through live video therapy sessions with licensed clinicians, in-app exposure-and-response-prevention (ERP) exercises, and 24/7 community support [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2016 by Stephen Smith, who began building the platform as a college student after his own OCD symptoms disrupted his life [^2].
