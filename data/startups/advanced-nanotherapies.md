## About

Advanced NanoTherapies (ANT) is a clinical-stage medtech company focused on improving outcomes for patients with coronary and peripheral artery disease [^1][^2]. The company is developing SirPlux Duo, a dual-drug nanoparticle-coated balloon (DCB) platform that uses a proprietary nanoparticle delivery system to drive active cellular uptake within the vessel wall, combining sirolimus and paclitaxel — two proven antiproliferation agents [^1][^3]. ANT has clinical experience across two coronary studies and a defined U.S. regulatory path toward pivotal execution [^2].
