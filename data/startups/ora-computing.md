## About

Ora Computing is a Vienna, Austria-based startup developing software that compresses large AI models by up to 80% without significant loss of accuracy, leveraging quantum computing research expertise to reduce energy consumption and computing costs across hardware platforms [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2025 by Stefan Sack and Raimel Medina [^1][^2]. Ora plans to use new funding to grow the team, expand its compression technology to handle the largest AI models, and launch a commercial product for cloud inference providers [^1].
