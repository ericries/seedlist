## About

TaiSan is a Cambridge, UK–based deep-tech startup developing solid-state sodium-ion batteries for mass-market applications including e-bikes, scooters, power tools, and vehicles. Founded by CEO Sanzhar Taizhan — a former Jaguar Land Rover engineer and Kazakh national chess champion — TaiSan's core innovation is a proprietary gel polymer electrolyte paired with a sodium-metal anode. Following its 2026 seed round, the company plans to establish manufacturing operations in Coventry alongside its existing Cambridge laboratory and begin pilot testing with manufacturers [^1][^2][^3].
