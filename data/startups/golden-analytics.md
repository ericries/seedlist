## About

Golden Analytics is a Bellevue, Washington-based AI-native business intelligence startup founded by Francois Ajenstat, formerly the chief product officer at Tableau [^1][^2][^3]. The platform connects to data stored in cloud warehouses or uploaded files, then uses AI to analyze the data and produce charts, dashboards, and written summaries in response to natural-language queries [^2][^3]. Golden Analytics emerged from stealth in April 2026 with a $7M seed round from NEA and Madrona, and announced a $14M seed extension and public beta launch on June 9, 2026 — drawing early-access requests from roughly 1,000 companies, about one in six of them in the Fortune 500 [^1][^2][^3].
