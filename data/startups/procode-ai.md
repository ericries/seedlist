## About

Procode AI is a Los Angeles-based provider of AI-powered medical billing services for surgical specialties [^1][^2]. The company's platform automates surgical coding, and its research has been published in peer-reviewed venues demonstrating that its AI outperforms both human coders and general-purpose LLMs at surgical coding tasks [^1]. Procode is led by CEO Jeff Cripe and co-founded by Kameron Rezzadeh, MD, FACS [^1]. The company acquired The Auctus Group in 2026 [^1][^3].
