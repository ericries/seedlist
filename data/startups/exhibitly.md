## About

Exhibitly is a Ghent-based EventTech startup founded in 2025 by Hendrik Franck and Brent Coppens that brings AI personalisation to B2B events [^1][^2]. Its platform generates personalised "why attend" reports highlighting the most relevant exhibitors, sessions, and event features for each visitor; the company reports that on average, 30% of visitors who use its platform click through to the registration page — 10 to 20 times higher than on a standard event website [^1]. In its first nine months Exhibitly signed 114 events from leading organisers including Informa, Easyfairs, Hyve Group, and the Dubai World Trade Centre [^1][^2]. The €1.4M Pre-Seed round announced May 19, 2026 will be used to expand the team, develop the product, and accelerate international rollout [^1][^2].
