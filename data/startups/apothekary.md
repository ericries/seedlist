## About

Apothékary is a Falls Church, Virginia-based herbal wellness brand that sells liquid herbal tinctures drawing on Japanese Kampo, Traditional Chinese Medicine, Ayurveda, and American herbalism [^1][^2]. Founder and CEO Shizu Okusa is a former Goldman Sachs trader and the founder of cold-pressed juice brand Jrink; she launched Apothékary in early 2020 [^1][^3]. The brand's tinctures are priced around $39 per bottle, with "Blue Burn" reported as its bestseller [^1]. Apothékary sells direct-to-consumer and through Ulta Beauty, Whole Foods, The Vitamin Shoppe, Free People, Sprouts, and Amazon; the company is targeting roughly $40 million in 2026 sales [^1][^2].

Per Beauty Independent, Apothékary raised its Series A across multiple tranches, securing the majority of its venture capital in August 2025 and additional equity and debt financing during the first half of 2026 [^1]. Total funding to date is approximately $32 million between venture capital and debt financing [^1]. Shiseido first invested via its venture arm LIFT in 2024 during a pre-Series A financing [^1]. RSF Social Finance provided the $6M debt tranche in this financing [^1].
