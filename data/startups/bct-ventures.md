## About

BCT Ventures is a Mumbai-based, AI-native consumer brands platform launching in India's nutrition and wellness market before expanding to other consumer categories [^1][^2][^3][^4]. The company operates three proprietary AI engines — Nucleus (product development), Resonance (consumer trust and demand-sensing), and Meridian (performance marketing) — that its founders describe as the infrastructure for building "protocol-led products, practitioner-owned distribution, and AI-driven growth systems" at scale [^1][^2][^4]. The seed round was led by 3one4 Capital, an early-stage Indian VC that manages ~₹2,300 Cr and has previously backed Licious, Darwinbox, and KukuFM [^2][^4].
