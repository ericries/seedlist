## About

Uncovr is a Paris-based surgical AI startup building what it describes as a "system of record for surgery" — automatically turning surgical and endoscopic video into structured clinical records [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2025 by Ines Iraki (CEO), Johann Diep (CTO), and Prof. Eric Vibert (Medical Co-Founder), and is part of Entrepreneurs First's portfolio [^2][^3].

The product addresses the gap between what happens in the operating room and what gets recorded: surgeons today often write operative reports from memory hours after a procedure. Uncovr analyzes intraoperative video in real time to improve clinical accuracy, reimbursement integrity, compliance, and continuity of care [^1][^2].
