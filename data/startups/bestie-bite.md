## About

Bestie Bite is a Rome-based hospitality tech startup that lets diners discover restaurants through short-form, AI-verified customer videos, while giving venues an automated marketing tool that turns a single clip into a month of social content [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Carlotta Robbe Di Lorenzo and Caterina Vertefeuille and has surpassed 100,000 users and 120,000 video reviews across 80 countries [^1]. With the June 2026 financing, Bestie Bite plans to expand to the United States, opening its first U.S. base in San Francisco [^1].
