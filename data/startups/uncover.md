## About

Uncover is a Brazilian marketing measurement startup that operates a marketing mix modeling (MMM) platform helping brands measure, forecast, and optimize marketing performance across fragmented media channels [^1]. Founded in 2020, the company is a certified partner of Google's Meridian framework and works with multinational advertisers including Unilever, Burger King, Reckitt, Vivo, and Bradesco [^1][^2]. Uncover operates in Brazil, Mexico, Canada, and the United States, and reports that the business is already profitable [^1][^3].

Co-founder and Co-CEO Daniel Guinezi has described the company's approach as replacing outdated consultant-led measurement models with real-time data pipelines and AI to enable immediate media-investment decisions rather than relying on theoretical annual budgets [^2].
