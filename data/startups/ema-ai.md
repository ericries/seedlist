## About

Ema builds "AI employees" — agentic AI systems that coordinate multiple AI agents to automate multi-step business processes across HR, IT, and finance functions inside enterprises' existing applications [^1][^2]. Founded in 2023 and headquartered in Mountain View, California, with additional offices in Bengaluru, London, and Vancouver, the company was started by former Google and Coinbase executive Surojit Chatterjee and ex-Okta executive Souvik Sen [^1][^3]. Ema says it now serves more than 50 active enterprise customers, including Google, Microsoft, ADP, and PwC, and reports over one million active enterprise users [^1].
