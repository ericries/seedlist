## About

Ema builds "AI employees" — agentic AI systems that coordinate multiple AI agents to automate multi-step business processes across HR, IT, and finance functions inside enterprises' existing applications [^1][^2]. Founded in 2023 and headquartered in Mountain View, California, with additional offices in Bengaluru, London, and Vancouver, the company was started by former Google and Coinbase executive Surojit Chatterjee (Coinbase Chief Product Officer during its 2021 IPO) and ex-Okta executive Souvik Sen [^1][^3]. Ema says it now serves more than 50 active enterprise customers — including Google, Microsoft, ADP, PwC, NTT DATA, Hitachi, KPMG, and Wipro — and reports over one million active enterprise users, roughly 200 employees, 50x revenue growth over two years, bookings exceeding $150M, and net dollar retention around 180% [^1][^3]. The company's core product is EmaFusion, which combines 100+ AI models to optimize accuracy, cost, and latency, plus 250+ prebuilt integrations and 1,000+ available actions delivered through Teams, Slack, and voice interfaces [^2].
