## About

Blnk is an Egyptian fintech founded in 2021 by Amr Sultan and Tarek Elsheikh that provides AI-powered point-of-sale consumer financing through a merchant network of more than 3,000 stores across categories including electronics, appliances, automotive, and furniture [^1][^2]. The platform uses proprietary underwriting algorithms and machine learning to approve loans in approximately three minutes with minimal documentation, targeting unbanked and underserved consumers in Egypt [^2][^3].

The company reports having onboarded more than one million customers since its seed round closed in November 2022, with a loan portfolio exceeding 1 billion Egyptian pounds. Approximately 75% of its users were previously unbanked or underserved, and more than 35% are women. Blnk says it reached profitability in 2025 after revenue grew 173% year-over-year [^1][^2].
