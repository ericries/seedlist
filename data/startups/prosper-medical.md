## About

Prosper Medical is an AI-powered concierge primary care platform. The company is in-network with major insurance plans and operates across all 50 states, aiming to move beyond transactional telehealth toward continuous, proactive care by using AI to maintain lifetime patient context [^1][^2]. The founders previously built PlushCare, one of the first direct-to-consumer telehealth platforms, which was sold to Accolade in a $450 million deal [^1][^2].
