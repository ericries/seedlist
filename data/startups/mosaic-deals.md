## About

Mosaic is a New York-based AI-driven deal-modeling operating system for private markets founded in 2020 and led by CEO and founder Ian Gutwinski; the platform combines deterministic, rules-based calculations with AI-powered ingestion and agentic workflows to let private-equity, investment-banking, and credit teams complete fundamental LBO and DCF analyses up to 20× faster [^1][^2][^3]. Customers include Warburg Pincus, CVC, and Evercore; the flagship Mosaic Autopilot product (the "Mo" agent) ingests CIMs from an emailed prompt, applies firm-specific defaults, and returns an "MD-ready" model in under five minutes [^2][^3]. The $18M Series A announced April 22, 2026 was led by Radical Ventures with no other named participants; Ryan Shannon (Radical Ventures Partner) and Troy Pospisil (Ontra.ai founder/CEO) join Mosaic's Board, and John Megrue (Radical Ventures Vice Chairman; ex-Apax CEO) serves as strategic advisor [^1][^2][^3].
