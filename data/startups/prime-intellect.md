## About

Prime Intellect is a San Francisco–based AI infrastructure company that provides a full-stack platform for enterprises to build and train their own AI agents, including compute access, reinforcement-learning frameworks, and evaluation tools [^1]. The platform is structured as a modular marketplace so organizations can develop custom models without depending on frontier AI labs [^1]. TechCrunch reports Prime Intellect is running at roughly a $100M annualized revenue run rate, with customers including Ramp, Zapier, and Flapping Airplanes [^1]. The company was founded in 2024 by CEO Vincent Weisser [^1].
