## About

Valor is a Fort Worth, Texas-based tech-enabled professional services and specialty asset management company focused on mineral rights management and oil and gas back-office solutions [^1][^2]. The firm offers accounting, owner relations support, regulatory compliance, and its proprietary `mineral.tech` software platform, which integrates map-based land management, accounting, and data analytics for mineral owners, operators, and investors [^2][^3]. The company is led by Co-Founder and Co-CEO Clifton DuBose [^1][^4].

On June 9, 2026, Valor announced the closing of a Series B round of capital investment [^1][^4]. Financial terms were not disclosed [^1][^2][^4]. The round was backed by Ladd Wilks, a Cisco executive; the Moncrief family of Fort Worth; and the Lamb family of Midland, Texas [^1][^4]. The capital will support continued development of AI across Valor's software and services platform and accelerate growth through acquisitions [^1][^4].
