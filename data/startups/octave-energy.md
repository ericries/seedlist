## About

Octave.energy is a Mechelen, Belgium-based cleantech company that develops smart Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) combined with an intelligent Energy Management System (EMS) for industrial customers, SMEs, and agricultural operators [^1][^2]. The company's platform lets customers store solar energy, reduce peak consumption, and balance the electricity grid [^2]. On September 4, 2026, Octave.energy announced a €10 million Series A round — a mix of equity and flexible debt financing — led by returning seed investor SPDG Growth, with participation from imec.istart, BNP Paribas Fortis, and KBC, to accelerate European expansion of its BESS and EMS solutions [^1][^3].
