## About

Octave.energy is a Mechelen, Belgium-based cleantech company that develops smart Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS) combined with an intelligent Energy Management System (EMS) for industrial customers, SMEs, and agricultural operators [^1][^2]. The company's platform lets customers store solar energy, reduce peak consumption, and balance the electricity grid, and has deployed more than 200 MWh of storage across 400+ businesses; it reported €16 million in 2025 revenue and has been profitable for three consecutive years, with named clients including Colruyt Group, McDonald's, Eneco, Naturgy, and Nextensa [^1][^3][^4]. On September 4, 2026, Octave.energy announced a €10 million Series A round — a mix of equity and flexible debt financing — led by returning seed investor SPDG Growth, with participation from imec.istart, BNP Paribas Fortis, and KBC, to accelerate European expansion of its BESS and EMS solutions into the Netherlands, France, and Germany [^1][^3][^4].
