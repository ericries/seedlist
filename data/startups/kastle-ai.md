## About

Kastle is a San Francisco-based AI workforce platform for financial services, starting with consumer lending and mortgage servicing — it enables banks and independent mortgage banks to deploy specialized AI agents that execute high-volume workflows (payment collection, borrower verification, loan qualification) across existing core systems without infrastructure replacement, and reports having processed over $1.8 billion in transactions [^1][^2][^4]. The company was founded in 2024 by Rishi Choudhary (CEO; previously founding Product Manager for Redfin's Mortgage Marketplace) and Nitish Poddar (CTO; previously tech lead at Verkada), and was part of Y Combinator's Summer 2024 batch [^4]. On 2026-09-17, Kastle announced a $24 million Series A led by Insight Partners, with new participant Fifth Wall and existing investors Y Combinator and Commerce Ventures returning, plus a group of prominent founders and financial services executives [^1][^2][^3].
