## About

Kastle is a San Francisco-based AI workforce platform for financial services, starting with consumer lending and mortgage servicing — it enables banks and independent mortgage banks to deploy specialized AI agents that execute high-volume workflows across existing core systems without infrastructure replacement, and reports having processed over $1.8 billion in transactions [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-17, Kastle announced a $24 million Series A led by Insight Partners, with new participant Fifth Wall and existing investors Y Combinator and Commerce Ventures returning; a number of prominent founders and financial services executives also participated [^1][^2][^3].
