## About

Big Picture Bio is a London-based biotech emerging from stealth on September 18, 2026 with a multi-agent AI platform that designs combination cancer therapies using generative "world models" simulating tumor, immune-cell, and tissue interactions to predict effective drug combinations and prevent resistance [^1] [^2] [^3]. The company was co-founded by two Deep Science Ventures alumni — Dr. Kerstin Papenfuss (CEO) and Dr. Mark Hammond (CTO) — and reports having prospectively predicted 12 of 14 ASCO 2026 phase-3 trial outcomes before public results were disclosed [^2] [^3].
