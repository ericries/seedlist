## About

Big Picture Bio is a London-based biotech emerging from stealth on September 18, 2026 with a multi-agent AI platform that designs combination cancer therapies using generative "world models" simulating tumor and immune-cell interactions to predict effective drug combinations [^1] [^2]. The company was co-founded by two Deep Science Ventures alumni — Kerstin Papenfuss (CEO) and Mark Hammond (CTO) — and reports having prospectively predicted 12 of 14 ASCO 2026 trial outcomes before public results were disclosed [^2].
