## About

Restate is a Berlin-based open-source infrastructure company providing durable workflow execution for AI agents and other multi-step software processes [^1]. Rather than depending on external databases, Restate ships its own storage, replication, and redundancy layers, which the company says keeps the system fast and lightweight while allowing workflows to "put themselves back together" after crashes or network failures [^1]. Replit is among its early customers [^1].
