## About

EnforceShield is a Vilnius, Lithuania-based LegalTech platform (with a U.S. subsidiary in Tennessee) providing autonomous intellectual property enforcement [^1][^2]. The SaaS solution uses AI agents to detect, analyze, and execute enforcement actions against IP violations across platforms, handling the complete workflow from discovery through takedown execution and monitoring [^1][^2]. The company reports serving more than 20 enterprise customers and removing over 33,500 IP violations monthly [^2]. On 2026-09-15, EnforceShield announced a €1.7M seed round led by Vendep Capital with participation from FIRSTPICK VC [^1][^2].
