## About

Hakimo is a Menlo Park-based AI physical-security company that combines computer vision with real-time AI monitoring to help property owners and operators respond to security incidents without adding headcount, integrating with existing camera infrastructure rather than requiring hardware replacement [^1][^2]. The company positions its product as an AI operator category — a human-like autonomous security agent — with more than 300 customers as of the announcement [^1][^2]. Hakimo says it tripled revenue year over year and doubled its team over the trailing 12 months [^1][^3].

On 2026-07-08, Hakimo announced a $12M growth round led by Zigg Capital, with participation from existing investors Neotribe Ventures, Vertex Ventures, Defy.vc, and Rocketship.vc [^1][^2][^3][^4]. The round brings Hakimo's total funding to $32M [^1][^2].
