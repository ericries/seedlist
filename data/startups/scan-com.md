## About

Scan.com operates a diagnostic-imaging API platform that connects payers, providers, and patients through a single contract and platform, routing referrals to independent imaging centers with AI-driven scheduling, transparent pricing, and subspecialty radiologist review typically within 48 hours [^1][^2]. Founded in the UK in 2017 by clinicians Dr Khalid Latief and Jasper Nissim together with Charlie Bullock (CEO), Oliver Knight (COO), and Joe Daniels, the company expanded into the U.S. in 2022 and now generates the majority of its revenue there [^3]. On August 31, 2026, Scan.com announced $220 million in combined equity and debt financing: a $90M Series C equity round led by Noteus Partners plus $130M in debt facilities from VerisFi Capital and Atempo Growth [^1][^2]. Revenue has doubled over the past year, surpassing a $165M annualized run rate, and the company has served more than 900,000 patients globally [^1][^2].
