## About

Skyfora is a Helsinki-based weather intelligence company that converts existing telecom infrastructure — GNSS receivers already installed at millions of mobile sites worldwide — into a real-time atmospheric sensing network without requiring new hardware [^1] [^2]. The company's software measures atmospheric delay in satellite signals and translates it into real-time profiles of precipitable water vapour, humidity, temperature, and pressure to improve AI-driven weather forecasts [^1] [^2]. Founded in 2019 by Svante Henriksson and Antti Pasila, Skyfora grew out of Henriksson's hurricane research at the Finnish Meteorological Institute, which won the Copernicus Masters Disaster Management award in 2018 [^3].
