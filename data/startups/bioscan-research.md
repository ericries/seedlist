## About

Bioscan Research is a Bengaluru-based medtech company developing affordable non-invasive medical devices for early detection of brain injuries using deep-tech in optics, electronics, mechanics, and software [^1] [^2]. Its flagship device, CEREBO, uses near-infrared spectroscopy paired with machine learning to detect intra-cranial haemorrhage at a pre-symptomatic phase [^1] [^3]. The company was founded in 2017 by Anupam Lavania and Shilpa Malik [^1] [^2] [^3]. Bioscan reported a fivefold increase in business over the trailing 12 months and plans further expansion in India and international markets [^2].
