## About

OpsMill is a Paris-headquartered (with a London office) infrastructure data management startup that builds Infrahub, an open-source platform giving AI agents and engineering teams a single trusted view of an enterprise's IT infrastructure [^1][^2]. The company raised an €11.9 million ($14 million) Series A on May 7, 2026, led by IRIS (Iris Capital) with participation from BGV and existing investors Serena and Partech [^1][^2]. Co-founder and CEO Damien Garros previously spent two decades on the operator side at Juniper, Roblox, and Network to Code; co-founder and COO Karen Gallantry built and scaled Infrahub alongside him [^1]. The Infrahub platform has been adopted by customers including TikTok and European cloud providers [^1].
