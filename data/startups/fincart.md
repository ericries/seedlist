## About

Fincart is a Cairo, Egypt-based e-commerce operations platform that connects merchants to more than 40 shipping providers and layers in AI-powered customer engagement tools and cash advance services based on merchant sales [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by Mustafa Masry (CEO) and Nihal Ali (COO) with a mission to consolidate the fragmented set of tools merchants use to run online storefronts in Africa and the Middle East [^1]. Fincart currently serves 450+ merchants across fashion, cosmetics, accessories, and electronics categories and has processed approximately USD 20M in gross merchandise value [^1].

The seed round was oversubscribed and co-led by Launch Africa and Antler MENAP, with participation from Yango Ventures, Five35 Ventures, Bluestream Capital, Hi2 Global, and Kalahari Venture Labs [^1][^2]. Fincart previously raised a pre-seed round led by Plus VC with participation from Orbit Ventures and Plug and Play [^1]. The company plans to use the new capital to scale its commercial and technology teams and expand into new markets in 2027 [^1].
