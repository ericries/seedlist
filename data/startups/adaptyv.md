## About

Adaptyv is a Lausanne, Switzerland–based biotech startup building an automated wet lab for AI-driven protein design [^1][^2][^3]. The company synthesises DNA in-house, expresses proteins cell-free, and measures binding using SPR with built-in quality control, all accessible through a web platform or API so AI agents can order their own experiments directly [^1]. Founded by Julian Englert and Daniel Nakhaee-Zadeh Gutierrez, Adaptyv counts over 100 customers including frontier AI labs, top pharma companies, and AI-native drug discovery startups [^1][^3]. The company plans to open a second lab and office in London in Q4 2026 [^1][^2].
