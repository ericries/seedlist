## About

MAKO (formerly MicroTau) is a Sydney, Australia-based aerospace startup developing Flightfilm, an adhesive film layer applied to the outer surface of an aircraft to improve aerodynamic performance [^1][^2]. The film is micro-engineered to mimic the skin of sharks, helping smooth airflow over fuselage surfaces to reduce drag and save fuel during flight [^1][^2]. MAKO asserts that Flightfilm can deliver up to 4% efficiency gains, translating to fuel savings and emissions reductions [^1]. Flightfilm can be installed without external modifications, although it must complete certification and obtain a Supplemental Type Certificate (STC) before commercial rollout [^1].
