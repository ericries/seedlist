## About

Merit is a Los Angeles-based color cosmetics brand founded in 2021, known for a minimalist, considered approach to beauty products aimed at delivering high-quality essentials without trend chasing [^1][^2]. In July 2026, SEMCAP Beauty & Wellness — the beauty-focused investment firm led by former Unilever Prestige founder Vasiliki Petrou — took a minority stake in the brand to support its ongoing global expansion [^1][^3]. As part of the deal, Petrou joined Merit's board [^1][^3]. Merit was recognized as a BeautyMatter 2024 FUTURE50 brand and had previously raised $20M in Series A funding in 2021 [^1].
