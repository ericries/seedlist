## About

CardNexus is a Bordeaux-based mobile-first marketplace and collection platform for trading card game (TCG) collectors, founded in 2025 [^1][^2]. The app combines a peer-to-peer marketplace, AI-powered card scanning, and collection management across 12+ trading card games [^1][^2]. As of the round announcement, CardNexus reported over 50,000 users, more than 30 million cards inventoried (10 million scanned via the mobile app), and active sellers across 30+ countries [^1].
