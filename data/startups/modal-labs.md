## About

Modal Labs is a New York-based serverless infrastructure platform for AI applications, providing on-demand GPU access for AI development and inference workloads [^1][^2][^3]. Revenue grew from $60M to $300M ARR in 8 months driven by demand for GPU access and AI coding tools [^1][^2]. The $355M Series C announced May 21, 2026 was co-led by General Catalyst and Redpoint with new investors Menlo Ventures, Bain Capital Ventures, and Accel joining; the round closed in two tranches with the first at $2.5B valuation and the second pushing valuation to $4.65B [^1][^2][^3].
