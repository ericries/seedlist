## About

Fifth Dimension AI is a London-based decision intelligence platform for real-assets investors and managers, founded in 2023 by Dr Kate Jarvis and Johnny Morris [^1][^2]. The platform unifies fragmented institutional and market data — from ERPs, deal platforms, spreadsheets, and document repositories — into a single intelligence layer with agentic AI that surfaces risk and prepares decisions proactively, integrating with existing client systems without requiring data migration [^2]. The €22M Series A announced May 11, 2026 was led by HV Capital with participation from Prudence, Mercia, MMC, and AFG, alongside continued support from existing investors Speedinvest, Seedcamp, and Anthemis [^1][^2]. Proceeds will fund US and Asia-Pacific expansion (including a new Singapore office) and deepen the platform's agentic AI capabilities for real assets investment decisions [^1][^2].
