## About

Basata is a Phoenix-based healthcare AI startup whose voice and document agents automate administrative workflows—referrals, intake, patient scheduling, and follow-up—that often still run on fax machines and phone calls [^1][^2]. The system extracts patient details from incoming faxes, creates EHR charts, and books appointments via AI voice calls within minutes [^1]. Basata has served more than 500,000 patients to date, including 100,000 in the past month, and counts large specialty groups in cardiology, urology, gastroenterology, and ophthalmology among its customers [^1]. The $21M Series A announced May 8, 2026 was led by Basis Set Ventures with participation from Cowboy Ventures, PHX Ventures, Zenda Capital, and Victoria Treyger; total funding now stands at $24.5M [^1][^2].
