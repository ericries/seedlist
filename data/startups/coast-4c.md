## About

Coast 4C is a Southeast Asia-focused regenerative aquaculture company operating a platform that connects smallholder seaweed farmers with global processors of carrageenan and other seaweed-derived ingredients [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded by Nick Hill and combines technical assistance, farmer inputs, and market access with digital traceability aimed at strengthening marine conservation while professionalizing the Southeast Asian seaweed supply chain [^1][^2][^3].

*Disclaimer: This is a lean profile built from initial round coverage and may be expanded as more information becomes available.*

Proceeds will be used to reach breakeven ahead of a Series A, expand the farmer network across coastal Southeast Asia, and complete the rollout of the digital farm management and traceability platform [^1][^2].
