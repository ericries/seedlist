## About

Wirestock is a Glendale, CA-based multi-modal data provider for AI labs, founded in 2020 by Mikayel Khachatryan [^1]. The company pivoted in 2023 from a stock photography distribution platform to supplying images, videos, design assets, and 3D content to foundation model labs, and now has 700,000+ artists and designers on its platform [^1]. Wirestock serves six major foundation model makers and reports $40M in annual run-rate revenue [^1]. The $23M Series A announced May 14, 2026 was led by Nava Ventures with participation from SBVP (co-founded by Sheryl Sandberg), Formula VC, and I2BF Ventures [^1].

## What Investors Say

Freddie Martignetti, Founder of Nava Ventures, stated: "I think Wirestock has a deep understanding of what foundational models and hyperscalers need in terms of multimodal data to start creating more human-like systems." [^1]

## What Founders Say

Mikayel Khachatryan, Co-Founder and CEO, said: "Initially, a lot of our deals were just selling what we had off the shelf, like our existing library. But then it turned into a lot of custom requests for content and data, and that created new opportunities for creators, and the platform just took off." [^1]
