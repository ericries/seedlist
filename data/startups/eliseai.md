## About

EliseAI is a New York-headquartered AI-agent platform serving the housing (proptech) and healthcare verticals — automating leasing, maintenance, and renewals for apartment operators, and intake calls, referrals, scheduling, insurance verification, and chart preparation for healthcare providers [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2017 by Minna Song and Stoyan Stoyanov, the company reports use in roughly 1 in 6 US apartments and $200M+ in annual recurring revenue after five consecutive years of 100% growth [^1][^2].
