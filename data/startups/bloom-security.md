## About

Bloom Security emerged from stealth on July 30, 2026 with a $20M seed round to secure enterprise endpoints in the AI era [^1][^2][^3]. The Tel Aviv-based company was founded in 2025 by Itay Keren (CEO), Ofir Balassiano (CPO), and Itay Frishman (CTO), who previously held senior engineering, product, and research roles at Palo Alto Networks following its acquisitions of Dig Security and Demisto [^1][^2][^3]. The company says it is already deployed at dozens of large enterprises across the United States and Europe [^1][^2].
