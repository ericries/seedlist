## About

Joyvié Health is a UK-based healthtech startup developing continence underwear designed from first principles to significantly reduce stool-to-skin contact, preserve dignity, and reduce caregiver burden [^1]. The company was founded by Zoe Robson following the death of her father in 2025 after a late-stage pancreatic cancer diagnosis, which exposed her to the realities of continence care [^1].

The product contains stool in a disposable pouch immediately after excretion. Early testing shows approximately 90% reduction in stool-to-skin contact and approximately 70% faster changes compared to standard incontinence products [^1].
