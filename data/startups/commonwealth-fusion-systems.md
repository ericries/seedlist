## About

Commonwealth Fusion Systems (CFS) is a Massachusetts-based fusion energy company spun out of MIT that is developing magnetic-confinement tokamak reactors using high-temperature superconducting magnets [^1][^2]. The company is building Sparc, a demonstration reactor targeting scientific breakeven in 2027, and Arc, its first commercial power plant planned for Virginia [^1][^2]. Major offtake commitments include a 200-megawatt power purchase agreement with Google and a $1 billion+ commitment with Eni [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-30 CFS announced $1B in new financing from pension funds, sovereign wealth funds, and infrastructure and industrial corporate partners, bringing its total funding to approximately $4B — making it the best-funded fusion startup in the world [^1][^2].
