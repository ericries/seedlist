## About

Terra Industries is a Nigeria-based defense technology company that develops autonomous defense systems, including long- and mid-range drones, interceptor drones, sentry towers, and unmanned ground vehicles, for governments and infrastructure operators [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by Nathan Nwachuku and Maxwell Maduka [^1][^2]. Terra operates manufacturing facilities in Ghana and Nigeria, including a 34,000-square-foot drone factory (Pax-2) in Accra, and announced its first office outside Africa in London [^1][^3].
