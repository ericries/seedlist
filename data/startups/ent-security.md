## About

Ent (legal name Athena Formation Inc.) is a San Francisco-based endpoint security startup that emerged from stealth on June 16, 2026 with $100M in seed funding — one of the largest seed rounds in cybersecurity history [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded by Elias Manousos (CEO) and Brandon Dixon, who previously founded RiskIQ (acquired by Microsoft in 2021 for a reported $500M+) and went on to help launch Microsoft Security Copilot [^1][^2][^3]. Ent's platform runs a lightweight agent on Windows, macOS, and Linux devices that evaluates the behavior of both human users and AI agents in real time, applying customer-defined security policies with just-in-time interventions before incidents occur — an approach the company calls "intent-aware prevention" [^1][^2][^3]. Ent says the product is already in use with Global 2000 companies in hospitality, financial services, and defense [^2].
