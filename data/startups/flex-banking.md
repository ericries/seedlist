## About

Flex is a Miami-based AI-native private banking platform for high-net-worth business owners that announced on 2026-07-14 a $70 million Series B1 at a $1.2 billion valuation, led by Halo Fund (co-founded by Qualtrics founder Ryan Smith and Accel general partner Ryan Sweeney) with participation from Portage, Wellington, Crosslink Capital, 53 Stations, Titanium Ventures, Spice, and Florida Funders [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2022 by Zaid Rahman, Flex combines banking, credit, payments, and wealth tools for middle-market business owners and their households, and is using the new capital to launch Flex Global, an international expansion of the product [^2][^3]. The Series B1 comes roughly seven months after a $60 million Series B in December 2025 and brings total equity funding to $180 million, alongside $300 million in debt facilities [^2][^3]. The company says it has reached $10 billion in annualized payment volume and plans to nearly double headcount from 110 to 200+ by year end [^2].
