## About

Judgment Labs is a San Francisco-based AI infrastructure company founded by Alex Shan (CEO), Andrew Li (Chief Scientist), and Joseph Camyre (CTO) that builds the continuous-improvement stack for AI agents [^1][^2]. The platform helps teams evaluate long reasoning traces, tool use, and memory, then turn production data into continuously improving agents [^1]. The combined $32M Seed + Series A announced May 12, 2026 was led by Lightspeed Venture Partners (which led both rounds, with the Series A coming less than six months after seed) with participation from Nova Global, SV Angel, Valor Equity Partners, and Dynamic [^1][^2].
