## About

City Therapeutics is a Cambridge, Massachusetts-based biotechnology company developing next-generation RNA interference (RNAi) therapeutics [^1][^2]. The company is advancing an RNAi engineering platform with multiple programs, including an ongoing Phase 1 clinical trial of CITY-FXI and additional pipeline programs (including CITY-RBP4 for Stargardt disease) progressing toward the clinic by year-end 2026 [^1][^2]. The company is led by CEO Andy Orth and was co-founded by John Maraganore (former Alnylam CEO), who serves as Executive Chairman [^1][^2].
