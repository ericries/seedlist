## About

BranchLab is an AI platform that helps pharmaceutical companies commercialize therapies by identifying, activating and optimizing high-intent patient and healthcare professional audiences in near real time, without relying on individual-level health information [^1][^2]. The company announced a $26 million Series A on May 13, 2026, led by McKesson Ventures with participation from FCA Venture Partners, Sanofi Ventures and AIX Ventures, bringing total funding to $35 million [^1][^2][^3]. BranchLab states its platform has delivered an average increase of nearly 70% in marketing efficacy across a diverse set of therapeutic areas, and plans to use the capital to expand enterprise deployments and deepen integrations across the healthcare and media ecosystem [^1][^2].
