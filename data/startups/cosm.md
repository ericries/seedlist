## About

Cosm operates "shared-reality" immersive dome venues built around 87-foot, 12K LED displays for screening sports, films and other entertainment [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2020 by Steve Winn and is led by President and CEO Jeb Terry; it currently operates venues in Los Angeles, Dallas and Atlanta, with announced expansion to Detroit and Cleveland [^1][^2][^4]. Cosm has partnerships across major sports properties — including the NFL, NBA and WWE — and has screened films from studios including Universal and Warner Bros. (e.g., "The Matrix" and "Harry Potter") in its dome format [^1][^4][^5]. On June 24, 2026, Sony Pictures Entertainment announced a $100 million strategic investment in Cosm as the lead of its Series C, taking a minority stake; Sony Pictures CEO Ravi Ahuja is joining the Cosm board [^1][^2][^3][^4]. Sony has signalled it will experiment with the format using its own franchises, including Spider-Man, Ghostbusters and anime titles [^1][^4].
