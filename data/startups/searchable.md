## About

Searchable is an AI search visibility platform helping brands measure and improve how they surface in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and other AI search engines [^1]. The company surpassed $100K MRR and 500+ paying customers within 60 days of launch — including American Express, Siemens, Pfizer, Tencent, Boston Consulting Group, DigitalOcean, and Havas [^1]. The $14M round announced May 18, 2026 at an $85M valuation was led by Headline; the round follows a $4M raise in late 2025 at a $40M valuation [^1].
