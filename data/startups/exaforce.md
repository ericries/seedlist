## About

Exaforce is a San Francisco-based AI security company building autonomous AI agents that triage and respond to security alerts in real time, helping security operations teams cut through hundreds of daily alerts to identify and stop attacks as they happen [^1]. The three-year-old startup announced a $125M Series B on May 12, 2026, valuing the company at $725 million and bringing total funding to roughly $200M [^1]. The round was joined by HarbourVest, Peak XV, Mayfield, Khosla Ventures, and Seligman Ventures; the company plans to use the capital to expand its agentic security platform across more enterprise customers [^1].
