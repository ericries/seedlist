## About

Axle Energy is a London-based climate-tech company that provides software connecting distributed home energy assets — EV chargers, heat pumps, solar panels, and home batteries — to wholesale electricity and flexibility markets, so those assets can automatically shift consumption to greener, cheaper periods and balance grid supply and demand [^1][^2]. Axle offers its platform via an API used by energy suppliers and hardware manufacturers [^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by Karl Bach and Archy de Berker [^2].
