## About

Flourish Health is a Richmond, Virginia-based mental health provider delivering in-home care for youth with serious, complex mental health needs through psychiatrist-led "Care Pods" and an AI-enabled care-delivery platform [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-27, the company announced a $26 million Series A led by B Capital, F-Prime Capital, and Cherryrock Capital, bringing the company's total funding since inception to $46 million (including $20 million in previously undisclosed funding) [^1][^2][^3]. The company's model serves children and adolescents with externalizing behaviors, suicide-related crises, and complex family situations, with reported outcomes including 70%–96% decreases in hospitalizations, 69%–90% reductions in residential stays, and 80%–95% sustained reductions in higher levels of post-discharge care [^1]. Capital will support the company's national expansion in partnership with the largest US health plans [^1][^3].
