## About

Aurenar is a St. Louis, MO-based medical device company developing a non-invasive, single-use neuromodulation platform to reduce life-threatening complications in intensive care unit (ICU) patients [^1][^2]. The company's V-Link™ platform, which recently received breakthrough device designation from the U.S. FDA, delivers transauricular vagus nerve stimulation (taVNS) to modulate the body's inflammatory response [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Aurenar announced the close of an oversubscribed $5.7 million seed financing round co-led by the Go Red for Women Venture Fund™ (part of American Heart Association Ventures™) and Solas BioVentures, with additional participation from BJC Health and Kaleida Capital [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds will fund continued advancement toward pivotal trials, including final device development, verification and validation (V&V) testing, and regulatory submissions to the FDA [^1][^2]. Aurenar is led by founder and CEO Dr. Eric Leuthardt [^1][^2].
