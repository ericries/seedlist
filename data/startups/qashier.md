## About

Qashier is a Singapore-based unified merchant operating system serving small and medium businesses across Southeast Asia [^1][^2]. The platform combines payments, point-of-sale, embedded financial services and AI-enabled workflow automation into a single stack, and now processes roughly US$1 billion in annualized payment volume for more than 20,000 merchants across Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, and the Philippines [^1][^2][^3]. Qashier says it has been profitable every month since December 2025 and grew annualized recurring revenue 61% over the year, having secured its Major Payment Institution licence in Singapore in February 2025 [^1][^3].
