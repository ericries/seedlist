## About

Horizon3.ai is a San Francisco-based cybersecurity company that builds autonomous, AI-powered penetration testing software for enterprises and government agencies [^1][^2]. The company's flagship platform, NodeZero, allows organizations to continuously pentest their production infrastructure without shutting down operations, replacing periodic manual assessments with continuous, autonomous testing [^1][^2]. Horizon3 was co-founded in 2019 by CEO Snehal Antani (former CTO of US Special Operations Command) and CTO Anthony Pillitiere [^1]. The company reports 120% year-over-year ARR growth, more than 7,000 organizations served, and 310,000+ tests safely executed in production [^1].

The Series E was co-led by NightDragon and NEA and valued Horizon3 at more than $2 billion [^1][^2]. Dave DeWalt (Founder/CEO of NightDragon, former CEO of FireEye and McAfee) and Morgan Kyauk (Managing Director at NightDragon) joined Horizon3's board of directors as part of the round [^1]. Proceeds will fund aggressive go-to-market scaling, international expansion into Singapore and Australia, and continued product innovation on autonomous blue-team remediation agents [^1][^2].
