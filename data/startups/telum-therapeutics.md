## About

Telum Therapeutics is a Spanish biotech company developing protein-based antimicrobial therapeutics for multidrug-resistant infections. Founded in 2019 and based in Pamplona (Navarra), Spain, Telum uses its proprietary APEX® platform — combining metagenomic datasets, synthetic biology, and generative AI — to design biologically derived antimicrobial candidates [^1][^2]. The lead program targets hospital-acquired and ventilator-associated bacterial pneumonia (HABP/VABP) caused by *Acinetobacter baumannii*, a multidrug-resistant Gram-negative pathogen [^1][^2][^3].
