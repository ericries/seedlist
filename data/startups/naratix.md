## About

Naratix is a Romanian eCommerce data startup founded in 2021 that develops AI-powered infrastructure for online retailers and marketplaces [^1]. The platform uses proprietary AI agents and advanced algorithms to transform raw product information into structured, standardized data covering automatic categorization, attribute enrichment, taxonomy mapping, and SEO-optimized content generation [^1][^2]. Naratix is already active in 30+ countries serving 10+ enterprise clients through integrations with VTEX, Mirakl, and Shopware [^1]. The company's stated objective is to index 1 billion products in global eCommerce [^2][^3].
