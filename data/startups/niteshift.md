## About

Niteshift, founded by former Datadog engineers Sajid Mehmood and Conor Branagan, is building a cloud platform for AI coding agents [^1][^2]. The company pitches a coding cloud that routes between models rather than locking customers into a single vendor, selling infrastructure on per-minute usage rates [^1]. On June 10, 2026, Niteshift announced a $7 million seed round led by Greylock partner Jerry Chen [^1][^2][^3]. Participants included Amplify Partners, BoxGroup, SV Angel, and angels Reid Hoffman, Olivier Pomel and Alexis Lê-Quôc (Datadog co-founders), Ankur Goyal (Braintrust), and Misha Laskin (Reflection AI) [^1][^2].
