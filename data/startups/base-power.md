## About

Base Power is an Austin, Texas-based energy company that installs residential battery systems in customers' backyards to store electricity and help stabilize the power grid [^1][^2]. The company operates in Texas and Illinois, installing approximately 100 batteries per day and has deployed more than 500 megawatt-hours of storage capacity to date [^1][^2]. Base offers batteries on a subscription basis: customers pay an installation fee plus a monthly rate and per-kilowatt-hour charges [^1]. The company manufactures its "Base Core" 39.2 kWh battery unit at an Austin factory [^1][^2].
