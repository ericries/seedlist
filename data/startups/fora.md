## About

Fora is a New York-based travel agency platform that combines a modern advisor marketplace with AI tools for trip planning [^1]. The company's AI assistant "Via" helps its network of independent travel advisors research destinations, build itineraries, and manage client communications, while Fora handles booking infrastructure and supplier relationships [^1]. Founded in 2021, Fora has grown its advisor network into the thousands and now hit unicorn valuation status with the Series D [^1].
