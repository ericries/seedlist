## About

Farther is a New York-based AI-enabled wealth management firm and registered investment advisor (RIA) with a proprietary Intelligent Wealth Platform, founded in 2019; the company has grown to $23B+ in recruited assets [^1][^2][^3]. The $150M Series D announced May 20, 2026 was led by General Atlantic with participation from CapitalG, Bessemer Venture Partners, Cota Capital, and MassMutual Ventures; the round granted Farther unicorn status with its first-time billion-dollar valuation [^1][^2][^3].
