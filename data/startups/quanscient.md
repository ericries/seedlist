## About

Quanscient is a Tampere, Finland-based deep-tech company building a cloud-based multiphysics simulation platform that combines classical, AI, and quantum algorithms for hardware engineering [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2021 by Juha Riippi, Alexandre Halbach, Asser Lähdemäki, and Valtteri Lahtinen [^1]. Its platform targets industrial customers in semiconductors, electric mobility, and other hardware sectors that depend on multiphysics simulation to evaluate large, complex design spaces [^1].
