## About

Geordie AI is a London-based AI-agent security and governance platform founded in early 2025 by CEO Henry Comfort (former Darktrace COO Americas), Chief AI/Product Officer Hanah Darley (former Darktrace director of security and AI strategy), and CTO Benji Weber (former Snyk senior director of engineering) [^1][^2][^3]. Geordie won the 2026 RSAC Innovation Sandbox contest in March 2026 (the same competition that previously surfaced Wiz and SentinelOne); the platform is deployed across ~30 customer environments including AlphaSense (covering tens of thousands of agents) and Owkin (hundreds of agents across 50+ petabytes of data), with a team of 37 employees [^1]. The $30M Series A announced May 28, 2026 was led by Balderton Capital at a ~$180M post-money valuation with new investor Crosspoint Capital and follow-on from existing backers General Catalyst and Ten Eleven Ventures — believed to be the largest Series A for a European cybersecurity startup to date; it follows the $6.5M seed in September 2025 and brings total funding to $36.5M [^1][^2][^3].
