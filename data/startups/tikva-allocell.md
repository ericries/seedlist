## About

Tikva Allocell is a Singapore-based biotechnology company developing engineered, donor-derived (allogeneic) cell therapies for solid tumors [^1][^2][^3]. Its lead candidate, TAVST01, targets B7-H3-positive solid tumors — including lung, breast, prostate, pancreatic, and pediatric cancers — by combining Epstein-Barr virus (EBV)-specific T cells with a B7-H3-targeting receptor and SerpinB9 protein engineering intended to help the cells resist immune rejection [^1][^2]. The technology platform is licensed exclusively from Baylor College of Medicine, with scientific advisors including Malcolm Brenner, Cliona Rooney, and Helen Heslop [^1][^2]. The company is led by CEO Ivan Horak [^1][^2].
