## About

UroMems is a Grenoble, France-based medical device company developing UroActive, the first smart active implant for stress urinary incontinence (SUI) [^1][^2]. The device uses a MyoElectroMechanical System (MEMS) placed around the urethral duct that automatically responds to patient activity without manual adjustments [^1][^2]. The $60M strategic investment announced May 13, 2026 was financed entirely by U.S.-based Ajax Health Fund I to fund the SOPHIA2 pivotal clinical trial across 21 US and French investigational centers in preparation for FDA and EU regulatory submissions [^1][^2]. Ajax Health partner David Beylik and Dr. Jeremy Durack joined the UroMems board [^1]. The round follows a $47M Series C completed in June 2024 led by Crédit Mutuel Innovation [^1].
