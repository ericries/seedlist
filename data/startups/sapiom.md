## About

Sapiom is a San Francisco–based AI infrastructure company that builds the platform teams use to ship, run, and scale AI agents, founded in 2025 by Ilan Zerbib [^1][^2][^3]. In the six months since launch, Sapiom has processed more than 270 million transactions and now powers over 100,000 agent runs per day [^1][^3]. The Series A was announced 11 months after the company was founded and six months after a $15.75M seed led by Accel [^1][^3].
