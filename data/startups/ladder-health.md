## About

Ladder Health is a virtual-first pediatric developmental care company delivering AI-powered developmental and therapeutic care for children and families, with a stated mission to improve outcomes, expand access, and reduce costs for families historically underserved by the healthcare system [^1]. The June 2026 seed funding supports expansion across North Carolina, Massachusetts, and Maryland, with planned entry into additional states and continued investment in its AI-enabled care platform and health system partnerships [^1].
