## About

Venus Aerospace is a Houston-based propulsion company developing Rotating Detonation Rocket Engines (RDRE), a class of high-efficiency rocket propulsion originally targeted at hypersonic passenger jets [^1]. After a successful flight test of its RDRE in 2025, the company pivoted toward defense and space applications, aiming to replace traditional solid rocket motors in missiles and to power high-speed space vehicles for military customers [^1]. Venus was co-founded in 2020 by Sassie and Andrew Duggleby [^1].
