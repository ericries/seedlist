## About

Adaptive is a New York-based agentic accounting platform for construction, automating job costing, accounts payable, billing, WIP, payments, and compliance workflows for construction finance teams [^1] [^2]. The company's AI agents integrate with existing systems like QuickBooks, Foundation, and Sage without requiring ERP migration [^2]. Adaptive is backed by Tidemark, Andreessen Horowitz, and Emergence Capital, which lists it as a portfolio company [^3] [^4].
