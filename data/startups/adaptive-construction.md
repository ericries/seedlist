## About

Adaptive builds an AI-native financial automation platform for construction companies, covering job costing, accounts payable, billing, and work-in-progress reporting for construction finance teams [^1][^2]. Tidemark describes the company's positioning as a "System of Action" for construction finance — software that acts on transactions and moves work through the accounting close rather than sitting alongside it [^1]. According to AlleyWatch's daily funding report, the September 2026 Series B brought Adaptive's total funding to date to $57M [^2].
