## About

Zazume is a Barcelona-based proptech that digitises the entire residential rental lifecycle through an AI-powered property-management platform; the company manages 3,500 properties and expects to reach more than 5,000 by year-end and 10,000 by 2027 [^1][^2][^3]. The €2.5M round announced June 2, 2026 was led by London-based Nordstar and Spanish family office GTV Capital (making its first entry into the cap table), and includes the conversion of prior capital from Sabadell Venture Capital and other family offices; total funding to date stands at €7.8M [^1][^2][^3]. Zazume plans to allocate more than 50% of the round to acquiring residential property-management portfolios in Spanish provincial capitals, with the remainder for marketing and sales [^1][^2].
