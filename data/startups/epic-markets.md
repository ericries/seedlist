## About

Epic Markets is a London-based online brokerage startup building what it describes as an institutional-grade multi-asset brokerage platform designed to deliver professional-level trading technology to retail investors, with an emphasis on execution speed, liquidity, and multi-asset access [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by Kevin Kimmel and Bryan Seegers, both former Citadel Securities executives [^1][^2]. Epic Markets is completing regulatory license applications and plans to launch later in 2026, targeting the US and UAE markets in addition to the UK [^2].
