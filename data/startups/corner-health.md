## About

Corner Health is a US healthcare technology company that operates an AI-powered platform enabling nurse practitioners (NPs) to run independent, solo primary care practices without traditional clinic support staff [^1][^2][^3]. The company's software handles scheduling, patient communication, lab orders, referrals, and other operational workflows so licensed NPs can focus on clinical care [^1][^2]. Corner Health reports a network of more than 70 provider-owned primary care practices, is described in press coverage as the fastest-growing primary care network in Arizona and Washington, and has enabled 35,000+ patient visits in the past year [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-09 Corner Health announced $32.5 million in combined Seed and Series A financing, comprising a $25 million Series A led by Oak HC/FT with participation from existing investors First Round Capital and Zigg Capital, on top of $7.5 million in prior seed and pre-seed funding [^1][^2][^3][^4].
