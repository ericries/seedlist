## About

P-1 AI is a San Mateo–based engineering AI startup building Archie, an agentic AI engineer designed for hardware engineering disciplines including mechanical, electrical, thermal, fluids, and systems design [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by CEO Paul Eremenko, previously CTO of Airbus [^1][^3]. P-1 AI has established design partnerships with industrial firms in data center cooling and critical power systems, and is launching Archie Solo, a lightweight version for individual engineers [^3].
