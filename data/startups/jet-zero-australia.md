## About

Jet Zero Australia is an Australian sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) developer working to build a domestic ethanol-to-SAF production facility that would supply low-carbon jet fuel to airlines operating from Australian airports [^1]. Its Project Ulysses in Townsville, Queensland is one of the country's most advanced planned SAF production sites [^1].

On September 7, 2026, techstartups.com reported that Jet Zero Australia had raised A$30 million in a strategic round with participation from Qantas, Airbus, and POSCO International, alongside undisclosed existing investors — bringing total funding to more than A$80 million [^1][^2].
