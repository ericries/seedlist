## About

encosa is a Munich-based EnergyTech startup providing battery storage solutions to German small and medium enterprises (SMEs) [^1]. Founded in 2024, the company offers an integrated end-to-end solution covering selection, planning, approvals, installation, and operation of battery storage systems [^1]. At the heart of the business model is a proprietary AI-supported software platform that optimizes battery storage economically, combining self-consumption savings with revenue from electricity market participation [^1].
