## About

StrongestLayer is a San Francisco-based AI-native email security company founded in 2024 [^1][^2]. Its platform uses AI reasoning to detect email threats by analyzing message intent rather than relying on pattern matching or reputation databases [^1]. The company reports that production deployments have grown more than 8x since its initial seed funding twelve months prior [^2]. StrongestLayer targets "AI-era" email attacks that evade legacy defenses [^2][^3].

The July 2026 round brings total seed funding to $9.3M [^2][^3]. Chris Key is the former Chief Product Officer of Mandiant [^1][^2]. Proceeds will fund go-to-market expansion and platform development ahead of a planned Series A [^1].
