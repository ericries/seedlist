## About

LightTable is a Denver-based AI-native pre-construction platform founded in 2025 by CEO and co-founder Paul Zeckser; the company has built purpose-built AI models that read and analyze construction drawings at the level of an experienced architect or engineer to identify uncoordinated design errors before they become costly change orders or jobsite delays [^1][^2][^3]. The $22M Series A announced May 27, 2026 was led by Innovation Endeavors with participation from Blackhorn Ventures, DivcoWest Ventures, 9Yards Capital, and existing investors Primary Venture Partners, MetaProp, and Banter Capital; capital will scale LightTable's flagship QA/QC product, expand the team across functional areas, and accelerate development of new tools for US and international markets [^1][^2][^3].
