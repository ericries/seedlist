## About

Hang Ten Systems is a Palo Alto-based enterprise AI services company building an AI-native operating model that helps large enterprises build, change, and run business software using agentic code generation and a reusable skills library [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded by Dr. Vishal Sikka, former CEO of Infosys and former Executive Board Member at SAP [^1][^2]. Jerry Yang, co-founder of Yahoo, serves on the company's board [^2]. Early customers include Fresenius and Siemens Energy [^2]. The seed round will fund team expansion and operations supporting global enterprise AI deployments [^1][^3].
