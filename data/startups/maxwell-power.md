## About

Maxwell Power, formerly known as HDM Renewable Finance, is a San Diego-based company that finances and deploys solar and battery storage systems for residential and small commercial customers, offering long-term power purchase contracts designed to lock in energy savings [^1][^2]. Since its 2018 founding, Maxwell has invested over $1 billion to help customers save an estimated $200 million on energy costs [^1][^2]. The company partners with sales and installation companies that serve consumers in markets where retail electricity prices have risen sharply — particularly the Mid-Atlantic, New England, and Pacific states, where prices increased 19% or more between 2022 and 2025 [^1].
