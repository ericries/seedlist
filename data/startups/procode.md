## About

Procode is a Los Angeles-based AI-powered revenue cycle management (RCM) and medical billing company serving private-practice surgeons across surgical specialties [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by Jeff Cripe, Dr. Kam Rezzadeh, and James Baez-Silva, and currently serves more than 350 plastic surgery and dermatology providers, in part through its acquisition of The Auctus Group [^1][^2]. Procode published a peer-reviewed comparative retrospective study in PRS Global Open demonstrating 86.7% overall CPT coding accuracy — outperforming human auditors (42.5%) and general-purpose LLMs (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude) [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Procode announced a $10 million Series A led by Health Velocity Capital, bringing total funding to $14 million [^1][^2].
