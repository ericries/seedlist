## About

Kotoba Technologies builds real-time speech AI models specialized for East Asian languages — Japanese, Korean, and Chinese — through its proprietary Koto model family [^1][^2]. The platform powers AI agents, simultaneous translation, voice interfaces for contact centers, wearables, and other devices, deployed through both B2B enterprise contracts and a direct-to-consumer translation application [^1][^2]. Kotoba was founded in 2023 by Noriyuki Kojima (Co-Founder & CEO) and Jungo Kasai (Co-Founder & CTO), both PhDs from Cornell and the University of Washington [^1][^3]. The company is dual-headquartered in San Francisco and Tokyo and says Koto has achieved sub-2-second latency in simultaneous translation in internal benchmarks [^2].
