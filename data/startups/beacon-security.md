## About

Beacon Security is a New York-based agentic security platform that provides a data foundation for AI-driven cyber defense — connecting, normalizing, and enriching telemetry across an organization so that both human and AI agents can act on trustworthy context [^1] [^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by Gal Tal-Hochberg (CEO, previously co-founder of HiredScore, which was acquired by Workday for a reported $520 million), Or Mattatia, and Iddo Israely [^1] [^2]. Beacon says it reached 300% ARR growth in the first half of 2026 as Fortune 500 teams across financial services, insurance, and technology adopted the platform [^1] [^3].
