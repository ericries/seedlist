## About

Twenty is an Arlington, Virginia-based defense-tech startup building AI-enabled offensive cyber warfare systems for the U.S. military and Intelligence Community [^1][^2]. The company describes itself as America's first venture-backed cyber warfare startup [^1].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|------|-------|--------|------|--------------|
| 2026-06-17 | Series B | $100M | Accel | Friends & Family Capital, Point72 Ventures, Caffeinated Capital [^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |

The Series B values the company at $1B post-money and brings total funding to approximately $138M [^2][^3][^4][^5].
