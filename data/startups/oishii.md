## About

Oishii is a Jersey City-based indoor vertical farming company that grows pesticide-free, Non-GMO strawberries year-round, combining robotics and automation with centuries-old Japanese farming techniques [^1][^2]. Founded in 2016 by Hiroki Koga and Brendan Somerville, the company has raised approximately $370 million in total funding since inception and operates what it describes as the world's largest indoor vertical strawberry farm [^1][^2]. On May 13, 2026, Oishii announced the first closing of a $150 million Series C round led by SPARX Asset Management, with participation from Nomura Real Estate Development, MISUMI Group, and Mizuho Bank, intended to expand production capacity, advance robotics integration, and broaden retail access across the United States and Japan [^1][^2].
