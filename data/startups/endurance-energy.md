## About

Endurance Energy is a geothermal energy startup developing offshore power plants to harness deep ocean geothermal resources near tectonic plate boundaries, particularly around the Pacific "Ring of Fire" [^1]. The company estimates approximately 6 terawatts of developable geothermal potential over the next 5-10 years, aiming to provide renewable baseload power for coastal cities and industrial applications [^1]. Founder and CEO Andrew Redd is a former SpaceX engineer who worked on the Dragon and Starship programs; as of the announcement, Endurance employed 25 people including 12 former SpaceX employees [^1].
