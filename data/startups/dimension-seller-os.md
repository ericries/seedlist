## About

Dimension is the maker of Seller OS, an agentic operating system for social commerce brands and agencies [^1][^2]. The AI-powered platform automates store management, affiliate program coordination, advertising, customer support, and creator relationship management, replacing native seller interfaces with a unified dashboard [^1][^2]. Founded by Ali Mirzaei, Dimension operated profitably throughout 2024 and 2025 before raising external capital [^1][^2].
