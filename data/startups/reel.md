## About

Reel is a Copenhagen-based energy tech company that operates a B2B renewable electricity platform built around Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs). The company aims to make renewable energy economics work for both corporate buyers — who want predictable pricing — and renewable energy producers — who want improved profitability through advanced energy trading and optimisation tools [^1][^2]. Reel was founded in 2020 by CEO Jon Sigvert, an environmental engineer by training who previously studied energy systems modeling at the Technical University of Denmark [^3].

The company already balances renewable energy plants for clients including Blue Elephant Energy and Greenwind, and it has expanded rapidly across European markets since 2023 [^4].
