## About

Ethos is a London-based AI-powered expert network platform that uses voice-based onboarding to capture detailed expertise across multiple domains beyond standard job titles, enabling sophisticated queries to match specialized professionals with companies seeking expert advice [^1]. The platform serves hedge funds, private equity firms, AI labs, and enterprise consulting firms, charging 30% or more per project [^1]. Ethos was co-founded by James Lo and Daniel Mankowitz [^1].
