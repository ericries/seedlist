## About

Alpen High Performance Products is a Louisville, Colorado-based U.S. manufacturer of high-performance residential and commercial windows, doors, and architectural glass, with core innovations in advanced thin-glass insulated glass units (IGUs), Winsert secondary glazing solutions, and high-performance fiberglass windows [^1][^2]. The company reports more than 50% growth over the two and a half years preceding the round [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Alpen announced the initial $6 million closing of a targeted $10 million Series B financing led by Inherent Capital and Arborview Capital, with proceeds earmarked for scaling manufacturing operations to meet market demand for advanced building materials [^1][^2][^3].
