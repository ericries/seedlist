## About

Numeral builds an AI-native sales-tax compliance platform for mid-market and enterprise businesses, automating the end-to-end lifecycle from nexus monitoring and business registration to tax calculations, filings, and remittance across 90+ countries [^1][^3]. The company was founded in 2021 by Sam Ross (CEO) and Matt Duvall (CTO) and contractually guarantees penalties-and-interest coverage — a differentiator against legacy incumbents that require manual human oversight [^1][^3].

## What Investors Say

Salesforce Ventures, describing its participation in the round, has stated that "AI is reshaping software category after category in real time, but tax remains stubbornly manual," positioning Numeral as an AI-native platform for a domain that legacy incumbents have automated only partially [^3].
