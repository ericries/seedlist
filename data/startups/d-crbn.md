## About

D-CRBN is a Belgian deeptech company spun out of the University of Antwerp that develops electrified plasma technology to convert CO₂-rich off-gases and hydrocarbons into carbon monoxide and syngas — feedstocks for sustainable fuels, chemicals, and materials [^1][^2]. The company is headquartered at Olieweg 95, 2020 Antwerp, Belgium [^1]. Prior to its Series A, D-CRBN received a €2.5 million grant from the European Innovation Council Accelerator Program in March 2024 [^3].
