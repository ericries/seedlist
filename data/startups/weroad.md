## About

WeRoad is a Milan-based group-travel marketplace founded in 2017 by Paolo De Nadai, Fabio Bin, and Erika De Santi that organizes small-group adventure trips for solo travelers — addressing the gap that arises when post-college peers settle down, have kids, or can no longer align travel schedules [^1][^2]. The platform pairs travelers with curated itineraries and local "coordinators" across destinations worldwide; the $58M (€49M) Series C announced May 27, 2026 was led by Airbnb and will fund WeRoad's US market expansion, bringing total funding to approximately $100M after the company's prior ~$42M across earlier rounds [^1][^2][^3].
