## About

Tenet Security emerged from stealth on June 17, 2026 with $6 million in seed funding to address the security risks posed by autonomous AI agents in enterprise environments [^1] [^2]. The company is built around a patent-pending technology called Agent-side Simulation, which predicts and simulates an AI agent's likely next actions before they execute against production systems [^1]. Co-founders Barak Sternberg (CEO) and Nevo Poran (CTO) are both Unit 8200 alumni who previously built Cisco's AI Defense research team together [^1].
