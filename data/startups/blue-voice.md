## About

Blue Voice is a Boston-based AI startup that gives police officers an AI copilot for real-time policy, protocol, and legal guidance — often described as a "Harvey for police officers" [^1][^2]. On August 31, 2026, the company emerged from stealth with a $6 million funding round led by SignalFire and Las Olas VC [^1][^2]. Founded by David Lawrence (Harvard Law dropout and CEO), Amit Patankar (Harvard MBA and former Google engineer), and Michael Gropman (retired Boston police deputy chief), Blue Voice reports that officers at 225 county agencies across 25 U.S. states use the tool daily, with the system answering approximately one officer question per minute about department protocol and legal requirements [^1][^2].
