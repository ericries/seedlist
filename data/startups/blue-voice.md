## About

Blue Voice is a Boston-based AI startup building a real-time policy-guidance assistant for police officers, likened by press coverage to Harvey (the AI legal assistant) but for law enforcement [^1]. The company emerged from stealth on August 31, 2026, with the announcement of its $6 million seed round [^1]. Blue Voice reports that officers at 225 county agencies across 25 states now use the tool daily to check that their actions align with department protocol [^1].

The company was co-founded by David Lawrence, a Harvard Law dropout; Amit Patankar, a Harvard MBA and former Google engineer; and Michael Gropman, a retired Boston police deputy chief [^1][^2].
