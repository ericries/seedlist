## About

Naked Energy is a Crawley-based renewable-heat technology company whose flagship "Virtu" modular solar collector heats water up to 120°C and, according to the company, saves three times more carbon per square metre than standard solar photovoltaic panels [^1][^2]. Existing UK installations of Virtu include the British Library and the All England Lawn Tennis Club (Wimbledon) [^1]. New capital funds a UK-based automated manufacturing facility expected to create 40 direct and roughly 100 indirect jobs [^1][^2][^3].
