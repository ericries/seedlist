## About

Enigma is a robotics research lab developing intuitive human-robot interfaces and proprietary AI models by studying how people naturally interact with machines [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-27, the company announced a seed round co-led by Index Ventures and Ribbit Capital, with participation from Sarah Guo's Conviction Partners and angel investors from OpenAI, Anthropic, Thinking Machines, xAI, and Cognition [^1][^3][^4]. The round amount is reported as $70 million by TechCrunch and TheNextWeb and $71 million by Israeli press (Calcalist, Value Add) — Enigma's own materials via Israeli coverage cite $71M [^1][^3][^4]. The company houses more than 100 proprietary AI robots in hangars in Israel and California and is launching a large-scale experiment that allows anyone in the world to interact online with the robots — which can perform tasks such as drawing pictures with a paintbrush, sword-fighting each other, and running simple chemistry experiments by picking up and mixing flasks [^2][^3]. Enigma was co-founded by Jonathan Jacobi (CEO, reported to be Microsoft's youngest-ever employee and a former Israeli intelligence operative) and Gal Niv (CTO) [^3][^4].
