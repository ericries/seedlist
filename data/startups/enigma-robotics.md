## About

Enigma is a robotics research lab developing intuitive human-robot interfaces and proprietary AI models by studying how people naturally interact with machines [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-27, the company announced a $70 million seed round co-led by Index Ventures and Ribbit Capital, with participation from Sarah Guo's Conviction Partners [^1][^2]. The company houses more than 100 proprietary AI robots in hangars in Israel and California and is launching a large-scale experiment that allows anyone in the world to interact online with the robots — which can perform tasks such as drawing pictures with a paintbrush, sword-fighting each other, and running simple chemistry experiments by picking up and mixing flasks [^2]. Enigma was co-founded by Jonathan Jacobi (reported to be Microsoft's youngest-ever employee) and longtime friend Gal Niv [^2].
