## About

Anomaly Insights is an AI-powered payer intelligence company whose platform analyzes billions of healthcare transactions in real time, surfacing payer behavior patterns, policy shifts, and adjudication deviations that determine what providers actually get paid [^1][^2]. Founded in 2020, the company serves diagnostic laboratories, outsourced RCM companies, and large provider organizations nationwide, and is deployed across 20+ health systems averaging $4B+ in annual patient revenue [^1][^2]. On May 13, 2026, Anomaly announced an additional $17M in funding led by Sound Ventures, with Alumni Ventures joining alongside existing investors Link Ventures, Redesign Health, and RRE Ventures, bringing total funding to $34M [^1][^2][^3].
