## About

Ultrahuman is a Bengaluru-based consumer health company best known for its Ring AIR and Ring Pro smart rings, which track sleep, glucose, temperature, and other biometrics [^1]. The company reports a $140M annual revenue run rate, roughly 800,000 rings sold to date, and about 45% of revenue coming from the US market [^1][^4]. TechCrunch reports demand for Ring Pro in the US is running "18x-20x available supply" [^1]. Ultrahuman is targeting a $200 million annual revenue run rate by January 2027 and has flagged 2028 as its earliest potential IPO window, contingent on eight quarters of profitability [^4]. Co-founder and CEO Mohit Kumar has framed the company's product strategy around turning the ring into a general-purpose on-body computer rather than a fitness tracker [^1].
