## About

Alchemab Therapeutics is a London-based biotechnology company founded in 2019 that develops therapeutic antibodies discovered by analysing the immune systems of patients whose bodies naturally resist otherwise progressive diseases [^1][^2][^4]. On 2026-07-08 the British Business Bank announced a £25 million investment into Alchemab as an extension to the company's earlier Series A, describing it as its largest direct investment in a life sciences company to date [^1][^2][^3][^4]. Alchemab's prior £60 million Series A (announced 2021) was led by SV Health Investors alongside British Patient Capital, and the company has developed a platform for identifying novel drug targets and therapeutics through analysis of patient antibodies [^4][^5]. CEO Jane Osbourn told Scrip the British Business Bank commitment is validation of the drug discovery platform's long-term potential and will allow Alchemab "to grow and thrive in its home country." [^2][^3]
