## About

Hivebotics is a Singapore-based robotics company that designs and operates Abluo, an autonomous cleaning and facilities-services robot deployed in commercial and industrial settings [^1][^3]. The company was founded in 2022 at the National University of Singapore by Rishab Patwari (CEO) and Nguyen Tuan Dung (COO) [^3][^4]. According to Vertex Ventures, the Abluo platform has accumulated close to 10,000 operating hours across 20+ deployment sites in Asia, Europe, the Middle East, and North America, including hospitals, airport terminals, and shopping malls [^3].

On September 4, 2026, techstartups.com reported that Hivebotics had closed a $6 million Series A round led by Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia and India, with participation from Fareast Land Development and Rigel [^1]. Vertex Holdings formally announced its lead on September 22, 2026 [^3]. The company's own blog previously disclosed a $2.5 million pre-seed round, bringing total disclosed funding to approximately $8.5 million [^1][^2][^3].
