## About

Hivebotics is a Singapore-based robotics company that designs and operates Abluo, an autonomous cleaning and facilities-services robot deployed in commercial and industrial settings [^1]. According to the company, the Abluo platform has accumulated close to 10,000 operating hours across 20+ deployment sites in Asia, Europe, the Middle East, and North America [^1].

On September 4, 2026, techstartups.com reported that Hivebotics had closed a $6 million Series A round led by Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia and India, with participation from Fareast Land Development and Rigel [^1]. The company's own blog previously disclosed a $2.5 million pre-seed round, bringing total disclosed funding to approximately $8.5 million [^1][^2].
