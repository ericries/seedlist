## About

American Growth Insurance (AGI) is an Atlanta-based AI-enabled insurance brokerage growth platform that acquires independent specialty insurance agencies and rebuilds their operations around custom-built AI technology [^1][^2]. The company launched publicly on July 16, 2026 with nearly $70 million in committed equity funding backed by Rockbridge Growth Equity and Atomic, the venture studio [^1][^2][^3]. Prior to launch, AGI spent roughly a year working with 10 partner agencies to develop and test its AI-first operating model, reporting that average agency profitability improved by more than 50% through a combination of revenue and productivity gains [^1][^3]. Brian Morgan is CEO [^1].
