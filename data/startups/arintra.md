## About

Arintra is a San Francisco-based enterprise AI company that autonomously assigns medical billing codes to patient charts and applies the resulting information across the revenue cycle, including clinical documentation, denial appeals, payer insights, and DRG validation [^1][^2]. The company says its platform processes billions of dollars in annual claims and that customers have seen a 5.1% increase in compliant revenue capture [^1]. Arintra raised a $25M Series B on August 26, 2026, bringing total funding to $51M [^1][^2][^3].
