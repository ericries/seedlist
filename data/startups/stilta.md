## About

Stilta is an AI platform automating intellectual property research and analysis: users input a patent number and relevant content, and AI agents search for conflicting patents, identify similar properties, and compile filing and court histories [^1]. The platform aims to lower costs in patent litigation, enabling companies to analyze and enforce patents previously shelved due to prohibitive expenses [^1]. The $10.5M Seed announced May 19, 2026 was led by Andreessen Horowitz with participation from Y Combinator and operator-investors from OpenAI, Legora, and Lovable [^1].

## What Founders Say

Oskar Block, CEO, said of Stilta's AI architecture: "They reason in parallel and converge the way a room full of specialists would, but at a scale no human team can match." [^1]

Block on the broader implications: "The question isn't really whether the legal system is prepared for AI. It's whether companies are prepared for what becomes possible when the analytical bottleneck disappears." [^1]
