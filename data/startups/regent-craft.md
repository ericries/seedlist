## About

REGENT is a North Kingstown, Rhode Island-based company building electric seagliders — wing-in-ground-effect vehicles that operate exclusively over water at aircraft speeds — for commercial coastal transport and defense logistics [^1][^2][^3]. The company is preparing for the first human flight of its Viceroy Seaglider prototype and has begun production at its 255,000-square-foot Seaglider Manufacturing Facility, backed by commercial orders and expanding work with the U.S. Marine Corps [^1][^2][^3].
