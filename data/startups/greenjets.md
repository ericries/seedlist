## About

Greenjets is a London-based aerospace technology company developing propulsion systems, aircraft platforms, and launch technologies for defense and next-generation flight applications [^1]. The company was co-founded by Anmol Manohar and Guido Monterzino [^1][^2]. In July 2026, the UK Ministry of Defence selected Greenjets — alongside Cambridge Aerospace and Frankenburg Technologies — under the Low-Cost Air Defence Effectors (LCADE) programme to develop British low-cost drone interceptors [^1].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-07-17 | Series A | £30M ($40M) | Blossom Capital | NATO Innovation Fund, UK National Security Strategic Investment Fund (NSSIF), Tanglin Ventures, NSFO Family Office [^1][^2][^3] |

The company plans to use the funding to transition from development to production, targeting delivery of thousands of systems over the next 12 months while expanding across Europe, the United States, the Middle East, and India [^1][^2].
