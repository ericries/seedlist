## About

PlayBlue is a Bengaluru-based omnichannel multi-brand sports retail platform building what it describes as India's first organised retail experience spanning athleisure, footwear, gear, fitness equipment, recovery, and nutrition [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by Satyam Trivedi (CEO), former CEO of GMR Sports, and Jayam Vora, and plans to open its first flagship store in Bengaluru before expanding to Mumbai and Delhi NCR with a goal of 150+ stores over the coming years [^1][^2][^3].
