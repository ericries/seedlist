## About

Emergent is an AI application-building platform headquartered in Bengaluru, India, with an additional office in San Francisco [^1][^2]. The company was founded in June 2025 by brothers Mukund Jha (CEO) and Madhav Jha (CTO), and publicly launched a year later [^1][^2]. Emergent lets non-technical users describe an app in natural language and generates production-grade software — handling deployment, hosting, testing, and debugging — a category the press has termed "vibe coding" [^1][^2][^3]. Mukund Jha describes the platform as "an engineering team in a box" for founders, small businesses, and internal enterprise builders [^1]. According to the company, more than 12 million applications have been created on the platform since public launch [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-15 Emergent announced a $130 million Series C at a $1.5 billion post-money valuation — a roughly fivefold jump from its previous round four months earlier — making the company a unicorn approximately a year after its public launch [^1][^2][^3]. The round was led by Creaegis, with participation from new investors MNI Ventures / Claypond and Sentinel Global, and returning backers Khosla Ventures, SoftBank Vision Fund 2, Lightspeed, and Y Combinator [^1][^2][^3]. Total funding to date is approximately $230 million [^1].
