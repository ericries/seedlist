## About

Superlight (legal entity Bristol Superlight Limited) is a UK-based commercial electric vehicle manufacturer purpose-building electric middle-mile logistics trucks using aerospace-derived design principles and a software-centric digital architecture [^1] [^2] [^4]. Its flagship OV-1 vehicle is described as offering 50% more cargo capacity at 50% less energy than competing 7.5-tonne trucks [^1] [^3] [^4]. The company was founded in 2016 by brothers Noamaan and Sheban Siddiqi and is headquartered in Yateley, UK with additional offices in Seattle, Washington [^2] [^4].
