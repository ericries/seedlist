## About

Shifters is a global technology company developing AI-native autonomous robotic teams for defense, national security, and hazardous commercial operations. The company is headquartered in Washington, D.C., with operations across the U.S., Middle East, and Europe [^1]. Shifters builds supervised autonomous ground robotic systems designed to enter dangerous environments before human personnel are exposed to risk, operating under a "Robots Go First" mission [^2].
