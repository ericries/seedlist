## About

Factorial is a Barcelona-based all-in-one HR, finance, and IT platform used by over 16,000 companies across 90+ countries [^1]. Founded in 2016 by Jordi Romero and Bernat Farrero (alongside co-founder Pau Ramon), the company achieved unicorn status in 2022 after its Series C round [^2]. Following ten years building one of Europe's largest systems of record for HR, finance, and IT, the company has rebuilt its product around AI [^1].
