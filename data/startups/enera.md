## About

Enera is a London- and Barcelona-based AI-native operational infrastructure platform for EV charging networks, founded in 2025 by Nicholas Marquardt and Arnaldo Vera [^1][^2]. The product is built around a "Control Room" data-ingestion engine and automated white-labeled helpline agents designed to troubleshoot live telemetry anomalies and resolve failed charging sessions in real time [^1]. The company is in early commercialization and intends to use the pre-seed proceeds to accelerate active pilot programs with United Kingdom Charge Point Operators (CPOs) and finance broader European geographical expansion [^1][^3].

This deal marked Lakehouse Ventures' first investment outside the United States [^1].
