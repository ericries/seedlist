## About

Panthalassa is a Portland, Oregon-based public-benefit corporation building autonomous, ocean-going AI compute nodes powered by wave energy and cooled by seawater [^1][^2]. Founded in 2016, the company integrates AI inference hardware with wave-power generation and seawater cooling into a single offshore unit—a "lollipop-shaped" buoyant head connected to a submerged vertical tube [^1]. Panthalassa plans to deploy its Ocean-3 pilot node series in the northern Pacific Ocean in 2026, with commercial deployments targeted for 2027 [^1]. The $140M Series B announced May 5, 2026 brings total funding to $210M [^1][^3].

## What Investors Say

Peter Thiel, who led the round, stated: "The future demands more compute than we can imagine. Extra-terrestrial solutions are no longer science fiction. Panthalassa has opened the ocean frontier." [^1]

John Doerr commented: "Panthalassa's autonomous wave power system is a game changer in addressing global energy needs and clean power generation." [^1]

## What Founders Say

Garth Sheldon-Coulson, Co-Founder and CEO, said: "There are three sources of energy on the planet with tens of terawatts of new capacity potential: solar, nuclear, and the open ocean." [^1]
