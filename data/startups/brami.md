## About

BRAMI is an Italian food brand bringing high-protein pasta to American kitchens [^1]. Founded in 2016 by first-generation Italian American Aaron Gatti, the company makes pasta using Italian durum wheat semolina blended with lupini bean flour to deliver a higher-protein, higher-fiber nutritional profile while preserving traditional Italian pasta taste and texture [^1] [^2]. BRAMI has been the fastest-growing national pasta brand in the United States for the past three years and is sold in more than 4,000 retail locations including Walmart, Target, Whole Foods, Safeway/Albertsons, Costco, and Sam's Club [^1].
