## About

Greyparrot is a London-based AI company whose computer-vision "Analyzer" cameras sit above sorting belts at waste-management facilities in more than 20 countries, identifying material, product, and brand of every item passing below in real time [^1][^2][^3]. Customers include WM, Veolia, Biffa, Circular Services, and FCC, and the platform has detected over one trillion waste objects to date [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Greyparrot announced a $27 million (£20M / €23M) Series B led by technology investor Omar Mir, bringing total funding to $60 million [^1][^2][^3]. In the UK, the Environment Agency has accepted Greyparrot AI-derived material composition data for statutory compliance reporting for customers Biffa and FCC [^1][^2].
