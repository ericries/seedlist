## About

TRIMTECH Therapeutics is a Cambridge, England biotechnology company developing novel small molecule therapies that target and degrade protein aggregates associated with neurodegenerative diseases [^1]. The company's TRIMTAC and TRIMGLUE degraders are designed to selectively degrade toxic protein aggregates and oligomers while leaving the functional monomeric forms of these proteins intact [^1]. CEO Dr. Nicki Thompson leads the company [^1]. Following the June 2026 financing, representatives from JJDC and BGF will join the TRIMTECH Therapeutics board as non-executive directors [^1].
