## About

Angle Health is an AI-native healthcare benefits platform that enables small businesses to offer customized health insurance plans to employees, providing end-to-end health plan administration to more than 5,000 employers across 47 states [^1][^2][^4]. On 2026-09-18, the company announced a $600 million financing package — comprising a $200 million Series C led by Vitruvian Partners and a $400 million tender offer, at a $2.7 billion valuation — with participation from new investor Town Hall Ventures and existing investors Blumberg Capital, Portage Ventures, PruVen Capital, and Y Combinator [^1][^2][^3][^4].
