## About

Joulent is a Houston-based technology-driven energy company developing multi-gigawatt firm, baseload power infrastructure engineered for AI data centers and other compute-intensive workloads [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded and is led by Chris James, and formalized a 50% ownership interest in its first project, Kilby, which will deliver approximately 2.67 gigawatts of generation capacity to a Microsoft-operated data center campus under a 20-year power purchase agreement [^1][^2][^3].
