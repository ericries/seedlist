## About

Photon Queue is a quantum hardware startup spun out of the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign that develops room-temperature quantum memory devices used to store and synchronize single photons in quantum computing and networking systems [^1][^2][^3]. Unlike most quantum-memory approaches, Photon Queue's devices operate without cryogenics, vacuum systems, or quantum transduction, and are designed to work across photonic, trapped-ion, and neutral-atom quantum platforms [^1][^3]. The company has deployed its first commercial systems at Sandia National Laboratories and the University of Maryland [^2][^3]. Photon Queue was founded in 2024 by Nathan Arnold (CEO) and Kelsey Ortiz (CTO) [^1].
