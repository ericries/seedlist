## About

Hive Autonomy (branded as "HIVE") is a London- and Norway-based physical-AI company building what it calls a "silicon brain" — a single intelligence platform that lets industrial machines in warehouses, production lines, and construction sites perceive, decide, and act autonomously across different machine types and tasks without new integrations or per-machine model training [^1][^2]. The company was founded in Norway in 2020 and is currently deployed across several Scandinavian sites while expanding into the US [^2]. On 2026-07-07, HIVE announced a €13.1 million ($15 million) pre-Series A led by UK-based SuperSeed, with participation from Veriten (US), Skyfall (Norway), and Nysnø (Norway), plus angel investors Børge Hald (founder of Medallia) and Jørn Lyseggen (founder of Meltwater) [^1][^2]. The company said the capital will accelerate development of its platform, expand the founding team with AI and robotics talent, and scale commercial deployments with existing and new industrial partners; it claims the platform reduces productive machine-hour cost by up to 80% when fully deployed [^2].
