## About

BRINC is a Seattle-based public-safety drone maker that builds purpose-built drones for police, fire, and emergency responders. The company's stated mission is to put a 911 response drone on the roof of every police and fire station in the United States, an addressable base of roughly 80,000 facilities [^1][^2].
