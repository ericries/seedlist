## About

Orbital is a Los Angeles-based startup developing orbital AI data centers designed to operate on solar power in low-Earth orbit [^1][^2][^3]. Founded by Euwyn Poon, who previously co-founded micromobility company Spin (acquired by Ford), the company plans to deploy satellite constellations containing clusters of NVIDIA-powered GPU servers [^1][^2]. Orbital emerged from a16z's Speedrun accelerator program in May 2026 and closed a USD 5M oversubscribed pre-seed round announced June 9, 2026 [^2][^3]. Its first test mission, Orbital-1, is scheduled to launch on a SpaceX Falcon 9 in April 2027 [^1][^2].
