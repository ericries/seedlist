## About

Saltroad is a UK-based clinician-led speech and language therapy provider that operates a network of over 1,000 NHS-qualified therapists across the UK [^1][^2]. In June 2026, the company raised £1.5 million and simultaneously acquired Ogma, an AI documentation platform designed for speech and language therapy, with plans to expand its therapist network by using AI to automate clinical documentation and reduce administrative burden [^1][^2]. The company had previously raised £575k in pre-seed funding in 2024 [^3].
