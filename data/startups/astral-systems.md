## About

Astral Systems is a Bristol-based fusion startup developing a compact "multi-state fusion" (MSF) reactor with the near-term goal of producing on-demand medical isotopes for cancer therapy. In partnership with McMaster University and Brazil's Institute for Energy and Nuclear Research, Astral targets commercial production of Actinium-225 and Lead-212 — isotopes used in targeted cancer therapies — by early 2027 [^1][^2].
