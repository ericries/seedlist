## About

Ollin Biosciences is a South San Francisco-based clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing therapies for vision-threatening retinal diseases [^1][^2][^3]. Its lead asset, OLN324 (also known as IBI324, an Innovent partnership program), is a VEGF/Ang2 bispecific antibody being advanced into Phase 3 trials in diabetic macular edema (DME) and wet age-related macular degeneration (AMD), with studies planned to start in the second half of 2026 [^1][^2][^3]. The company has reported Phase 2 ("JADE") data in which nearly 90% of DME patients on OLN324 achieved absence of disease at 12 weeks compared with 57% on faricimab, with numerically greater vision gains and zero intraocular inflammation [^1][^2]. The Series B was raised to fund the global Phase 3 program and was oversubscribed [^1][^2][^4].
