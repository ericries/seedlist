## About

Hark is an AI lab founded in late 2025 by Brett Adcock (also founder of Figure and Archer Aviation), building multimodal foundation models and custom hardware for a personal AI assistant intended as "a universal interface with the digital world" [^1][^2]. Adcock has said he personally seeded the company with $100M of his own capital before raising external funding [^1][^3]. The company plans to release its first multimodal models in summer 2026, followed by custom hardware devices [^1][^2].

The $700M+ Series A announced May 21, 2026 was led by Parkway Venture Capital at a $6B post-money valuation, with participation from Nvidia, Align Ventures, AMD Ventures, ARK Invest, Brookfield, Greycroft, Intel Capital, Prime Movers Lab, Qualcomm Ventures, Salesforce Ventures, and Tamarack Global [^1][^2][^3][^4].
