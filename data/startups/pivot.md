## About

Pivot is a Paris and New York-based AI operating system for enterprise procurement, operating in 25+ countries serving enterprises including DoorDash, Lemonade, and Flix, and processing $3B in invoices annually [^1][^2][^3]. The $40M Series B announced May 21, 2026 was co-led by Forestay Capital and Notion Capital with participation from Greyhound Capital, Hedosophia, Visionaries Club, and Emblem; total funding now stands at $70M [^1][^2][^3].
