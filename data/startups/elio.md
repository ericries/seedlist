## About

Elio is a San Mateo, California-based startup founded by former Meta AR/VR executives Nadav Grossinger and Nitay Romano to build sensors designed for AI systems rather than the human eye [^1][^2]. The company's technology embeds computation directly into the optical system, using dynamic optical layers and AI correction to extract signals that conventional lenses flatten or miss [^1][^2]. Elio is positioning its sensors for microscopy, semiconductor inspection, robotics, and defense applications [^2][^3].

## What Founders Say

> "Today's sensors capture one fixed view of the world, decided in advance, shaped by what the human eye can see rather than what a machine needs. Elio's sensors let AI decide what to capture, in real time, based on what it is trying to figure out, delivering exactly the information needed, when it is needed, instead of a fixed picture to interpret after the fact."
