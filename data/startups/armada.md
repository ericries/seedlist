## About

Armada is a San Francisco-based modular AI data center and edge computing company co-founded and led by CEO Dan Wright (with co-founder John Runyan), manufacturing rugged, deployable data-center modules designed for military operations, oil fields, mining sites, and other remote industrial locations, paired with a satellite-enabled edge platform [^1][^2][^3]. The $230M Series B announced May 19, 2026 was oversubscribed and co-led by Overmatch, BlackRock, and 8090 Industries at a $2B pre-money valuation, with new strategic investors Johnson Controls, NightDragon, Mitsui, and Singtel Innov8 joining existing backers Felicis, Marlinspike, Shield Capital, Lux Capital, Founders Fund, Silent Ventures, Veriten, and Gladebrook; the round brings Armada's total funding to nearly $500M [^1][^2][^3]. Armada also announced a framework agreement with Johnson Controls to manufacture modular data centers at Galleon Forge One, a planned 400,000-square-foot Arizona facility expected to create 500+ jobs and begin production in summer 2026 [^1][^3].
