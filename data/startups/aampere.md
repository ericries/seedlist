## About

Aampere is a German digital resale platform for used electric vehicles, streamlining transactions between dealers and buyers across Europe [^1][^3]. Founded in 2022 and based in Bad Liebenzell, Baden-Württemberg, the company reports selling several thousand EVs per year and grew revenue roughly fourfold in the year preceding its 2026 round [^1][^2].
