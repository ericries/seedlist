## About

Decade is a Brazil-based AI-native wealth advisory company founded by former Nubank CTO Vitor Olivier and Hyperplane founder Felipe Meneses (Brazil's first Thiel Fellow) [^1][^2][^3]. The platform pairs each client with a senior human advisor and an AI model that analyzes their financial situation, monitors investments, and provides tailored recommendations, aiming to bring wealth advisory services traditionally reserved for the ultra-rich to a broader Latin American audience [^1][^3]. Decade emerged from stealth on August 4, 2026 with what the company reports is the largest seed round ever raised for a Latin American startup [^1][^2].
