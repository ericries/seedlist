## About

Freight Hero is a Durham, North Carolina-based AI company that fully manages freight brokers' back-office operations, combining artificial intelligence with human operations teams and charging customers a flat fee per load while assuming responsibility for tasks from rate confirmation through proof of delivery [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-27, the company announced a $5 million seed round led by Field Ventures, with participation from Flybridge Capital, Tip Top VC, Front Porch Venture Partners, Dynamo Ventures, and AI Fund [^1][^2]. The round brings total funding to more than $6 million. Proceeds will support expansion of Freight Hero's sales organization, engineering team, and operations workforce while extending the platform into adjacent functions including billing, accounting, and carrier sales [^1]. The company was founded by logistics entrepreneur André Luis Martins Filho [^1].
