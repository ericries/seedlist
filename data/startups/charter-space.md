## About

Charter Space is a nationally licensed insurance brokerage that offers specialized insurance solutions for the commercial space industry [^1]. The company builds software that consolidates technical, manufacturing, and test data and feeds it into the underwriting process, bridging a gap where traditional insurers lack aerospace expertise [^1]. Charter Space currently serves more than 50 U.S. space and defense companies and is headquartered in El Segundo, California [^1].
