## About

Ocean Security builds AI agents to investigate and stop sophisticated email-based attacks (phishing, business email compromise, social engineering) on behalf of enterprise security teams [^1]. The $28M venture round announced May 19, 2026 was led by Lightspeed Venture Partners with participation from Picture Capital and Cerca Partners [^1].
