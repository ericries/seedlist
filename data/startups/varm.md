## About

VARM is a Berlin-based climate startup that recruits, trains, and equips local installers to insulate single-family homes in one day at a fixed ~€5,000 price (under €4,000 after German subsidies). The B Corp-certified company is targeting one million insulated buildings across Europe by 2035, addressing the labour bottleneck that has stalled home-insulation rollout despite ~14 million poorly insulated German homes [^1][^2].
