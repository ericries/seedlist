## About

Concentrate AI is an AI gateway that provides large language model access, routing, and spend management for enterprise teams [^1][^2][^3]. The company was co-founded in 2025 by Ari Jacoby (CEO) and Todd Lieberman [^1][^3]. Concentrate AI emerged from stealth on June 10, 2026 with a $5.1M pre-seed round co-led by True Ventures and RRE Ventures [^1][^2][^3].
