## About

Gritt is a robotics startup that combines AI control software with off-the-shelf hardware — including rented skidders and robotic arms — to automate labor-intensive outdoor construction work, beginning with utility-scale solar plant installation and expanding to tasks such as rebar tying and concrete work [^1]. The company was founded in 2024 by Carnegie Mellon-trained roboticists Puneet Puri (CEO) and Vishal Dugar (CTO), and plans to add new manipulation capabilities so its system can fasten solar panels, drill posts, and build the racks they sit on [^1]. On 2026-07-21, Gritt exited stealth with a $26 million Series A round led by Obvious Ventures with participation from Union Square Ventures and Active Impact Investments, bringing total funding to $34 million; the earlier seed round was backed by First Round Capital, Climactic, Congruent Ventures, and VSC Ventures [^1].
