## About

Poetic (formerly Forge) is a San Francisco-based AI startup that automates complex enterprise processes for regulated industries [^1] [^2]. The platform uses a proprietary programming language that translates operators' natural-language instructions into deterministic execution, designed to improve reliability and reduce token costs compared to traditional AI agents [^1] [^3]. The company reports 99% accuracy on processes companies have failed to automate for decades, and lists AIG, SoFi, and Chime among its customers [^2]. Poetic was founded in 2023 by Markie Wagner, a former Google and Waymo machine-learning engineer who previously ran AI consultancy Delphi Labs [^2].
