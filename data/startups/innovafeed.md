## About

Innovafeed is a Paris-based biotech company producing functional and sustainable insect-based ingredients for animal nutrition, pet food, and agriculture, founded in 2016 by Aude Guo, Bastien Oggeri, and Clément Ray [^1]. The company rears and processes the black soldier fly (Hermetia illucens) to produce protein, oil, and frass-based fertilizer marketed under the Hilucia brand, positioned as an alternative to fishmeal and vegetable oils in fish and animal feed [^1][^2]. Its flagship industrial facility is located in Nesle, France, which the company describes as the largest insect production site in the world [^2]. Over three years, Innovafeed has produced more than 15,000 tons of protein and oil, increased production volumes tenfold, and reduced production costs by a factor of seven [^2][^3].
