## About

Scapia is a Bengaluru-based travel fintech founded in 2022 by Anil Goteti (former Flipkart executive), operating a co-branded credit card, UPI-based payments, travel bookings, and commerce in one platform [^1][^2][^3]. Flight bookings have grown 5-6x year-over-year and hotel stays 8x; the company employs ~250 [^1]. The $63M Series C announced May 21, 2026 was led by General Catalyst with participation from existing investors Peak XV Partners and Z47 (formerly Matrix India); the all-equity round assigns a post-money valuation of more than $500M (up from ~$200M in April 2025) and brings total funding to $126M [^1][^2][^3].
