## About

Nexeon is an Oxfordshire-based developer of silicon-anode battery materials that enable higher-energy-density lithium-ion cells for electric vehicles and consumer electronics [^1][^2]. Founded in 2006, the company operates a UK-based pilot manufacturing facility for its proprietary silicon anode technology [^2]. On August 31, 2026, Nexeon announced a £100 million (approximately €116.7 million / $133 million) growth round anchored by the UK's National Wealth Fund, which contributed £52.6 million (approximately €61.4 million / $70 million) [^1][^2][^3].
