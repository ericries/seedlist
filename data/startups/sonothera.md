## About

SonoThera is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing ultrasound-mediated nonviral genetic medicines [^1] [^2]. The company's platform combines RIPPLE, a proprietary ultrasound-mediated delivery technology, with PORE, a payload engineering platform supporting DNA and RNA therapeutics, gene editing, and gene silencing [^1] [^3]. SonoThera's approach uses widely available, FDA-cleared diagnostic ultrasound systems for noninvasive outpatient procedures, with the goal of safe, targeted, and repeatable delivery of genetic medicines [^1]. Lead programs target Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD) and autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (ADPKD), with first clinical trials expected to begin in 2027 [^1] [^2].

## What Founders Say

CEO Kenneth Greenberg stated that the platform "could allow the application of gene therapy into chronic prevalent diseases with much greater populations than rare disease[s]" [^3]. On the payload capacity advantage over viral vectors, Greenberg said: "It's over a 13-kilobase payload, which is essentially about three times larger than you can fit into an AAV vector" [^3]. On the DMD program, Greenberg said: "We're not aware of any other company that's attempting to deliver full-length dystrophin" [^3].
