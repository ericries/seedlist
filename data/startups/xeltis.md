## About

Xeltis is an Eindhoven, Netherlands-based medtech company (with US operations) developing transformative bioabsorbable implants that enable the natural creation of living and long-lasting vessels and heart valves. The company's lead product, aXess™, is a vascular access conduit for hemodialysis patients; the platform also includes the XABG (coronary artery bypass) and XPAD (pediatric heart valve) programs [^1]. Eliane Schutte serves as Chief Executive Officer [^1].

This funding builds on the nearly €50M secured in 2025 from the European Investment Bank and existing shareholders [^1][^2]. Proceeds will fund the US pivotal trial and FDA submission for aXess™, European commercial rollout, and advancement of the XABG and XPAD clinical programs.
