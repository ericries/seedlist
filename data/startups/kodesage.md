## About

Kodesage is an enterprise software modernization startup based in London and Budapest, founded in 2024 by Gergely Dombi, Miklos Szurdi, and Gyorgy Szilagyi [^1][^2]. The company helps heavily regulated enterprises modernize decades-old software entirely on-premise, so sensitive code never reaches public cloud AI tools [^1][^2]. The new funding supports a go-to-market push in the United States and Europe and additional hiring in engineering and product [^1].
