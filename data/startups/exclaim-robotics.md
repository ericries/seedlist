## About

Exclaim Robotics is a Zurich, Switzerland-based robotics company building autonomous mobile robots that perform routine repair and maintenance tasks inside AI data centers, including cleaning and replacing optics, reconnecting copper cabling, and re-seating drives [^1][^2][^3]. The robots are designed to navigate the data hall autonomously, position themselves at a rack, and work the front face from the floor to the top rail without requiring changes to the building [^1][^3]. The company emerged from stealth on 2026-08-04 with a pre-seed round co-led by Founderful and Playfair [^1][^2][^3]. Exclaim Robotics was founded by Helen Oleynikova, an ETH Zurich PhD with prior research and engineering roles at NVIDIA, Google, and Microsoft [^1][^2][^3]. Beyond data centers, the company plans to deploy its technology in other environments where infrastructure maintenance is hazardous or remote, particularly in high-voltage energy systems [^1][^3].
