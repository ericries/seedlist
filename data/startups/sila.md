## About

Sila is a battery materials company producing silicon-carbon anode material that replaces the graphite traditionally used in lithium-ion batteries, delivering higher energy density [^1]. The company opened the first automotive-scale silicon-anode plant in the U.S. in Moses Lake, Washington, in late 2025, with initial production capacity of roughly 2 gigawatt-hours of anode material [^1]. The 2026 financing supports a planned expansion of the Moses Lake facility, which could increase capacity by more than 100x [^1].
