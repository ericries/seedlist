## About

Fish Audio is a Palo Alto-based AI voice generation startup founded by former Nvidia researcher Shijia Liao and CEO Rissa Cao [^1]. The company builds AI voice models with more than 15,000 natural-language controls, serving creators and enterprises across gaming, avatars, and voice agents [^1]. On 2026-07-28, Fish Audio announced a $52 million seed round co-led by Coreline Ventures and Capital Today, with participation from 359 Capital, Parable, Play Time, Alphalist Partners, Bayhouse Ventures, Carya Venture Partners, and HF0 [^1]. The company reports over 8 million users, $21 million in annual recurring revenue, and more than 31,000 GitHub stars across its open-source speech-generation models [^1]. Fish Audio's project began when Liao trained a voice-generation model on a single GPU and open-sourced it; the company has since released five models and open-sourced three [^1].
