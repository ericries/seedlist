## About

MicroLub is a Leeds-based deeptech ingredient company spun out of the University of Leeds, developing protein-based biolubricants that let food manufacturers reduce fat content by up to 75% while preserving taste and texture [^1] [^2]. The technology, invented by Professor Anwesha Sarkar of the School of Food Science and Nutrition, replicates the mouthfeel of fat by mimicking natural lubrication using water and plant proteins [^1].
