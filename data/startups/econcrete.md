## About

ECOncrete is a marine infrastructure tech company whose bio-enhancing concrete technology supports up to 2x more biodiversity, 7x more carbon storage, and 16x better water quality than traditional concrete, while delivering up to 10% greater compressive strength [^1][^2]. Over the prior 18 months, ECOncrete delivered its technology in more than 20 marine infrastructure projects globally, providing over 90,000 m² of habitat and achieving 3.4x revenue growth [^1]. The $14M round announced May 11, 2026 was led by Builders Vision with participation from Barclays Climate Ventures, the Prince Albert II of Monaco Foundation, Monaco Asset Management's ReOcean Fund, BDT & MSD Partners (via Oceans Catalyst), DCP, and a financing facility from Open Road Impact [^1][^2].
