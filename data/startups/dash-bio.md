## About

Dash Bio is a Waltham, Massachusetts-based bioanalysis company that operates a 24,000-square-foot GLP-compliant, fully automated laboratory serving pharmaceutical and biotechnology drug developers [^1][^2][^3]. The company pairs laboratory robotics with a proprietary custom laboratory operating system and productized assay workflows spanning ELISA, MSD, qPCR, droplet digital PCR (ddPCR), and LC-MS, delivering preclinical results in days rather than months with transparent pricing and digital traceability [^1][^3]. Dash Bio reports serving more than 30 customers within nine months of commercial launch, including four of the world's 15 largest pharmaceutical companies [^3]. The founding team previously architected digital and automation infrastructure at Moderna [^3]. Prior to the Series A, Dash Bio raised $11M in July 2025, bringing total funding to $17.5M before this round [^4].
