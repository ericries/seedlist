## About

NewOrbit is a UK-based space technology company building commercial satellites designed to operate in Very Low Earth Orbit (VLEO), the 200-300 kilometer altitude band that has been largely unused by commercial operators for six decades [^1][^2]. The company, headquartered in Reading, England, was co-founded by Anatolii Papulov (CEO) and Ruslan Rakhimov (CTO) [^1][^3]. NewOrbit positions VLEO as the next foundational shift in the space industry, with its first commercial satellite, NEO-1, planned to launch in 2028 [^1][^2]. The company aims to provide satellite imagery at significantly lower cost than conventional Earth observation satellites, with faster data downlink speeds [^1].

The startup plans to open a NEO Production Complex with capacity scaling from 10 satellites annually to several per week [^1]. NewOrbit's advisory board includes Jean-Jacques Dordain, former Director General of the European Space Agency, and Sir Chris Deverell, former UK Joint Forces Commander [^3].
