## About

xCures is an AI-driven healthcare technology company that extracts and standardizes clinical data from fragmented medical records to support clinical decision-making and research [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2018 as a spinout from Cancer Commons by Marty Tenenbaum and Jeff Shrager [^2][^3]. Mika Newton serves as CEO [^1][^3].

By the time of its 2026 Series B, xCures had processed more than 300 million medical records from over 550,000 healthcare locations, and counted enterprise customers including Exact Sciences, Caris Life Sciences, and Novocure [^1][^2]. The company reported 2025 ARR of approximately $10 million and projected 2026 ARR of $20 million or more [^1].
