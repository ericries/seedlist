## About

AegisAI is an email security company that builds its own large language models to defend enterprise inboxes against AI-driven spear phishing and social-engineering attacks [^1][^2]. Founded in 2024 by former Google security executives Cy Khormaee (CEO) and Ryan Luo, the company emerged from stealth less than a year before its Series A [^1][^2]. Its product, Vanguard, is an autonomous agent that hunts threats beyond the inbox [^2].

On 2026-07-23 AegisAI announced a $36M Series A led by Battery Ventures, with participation from existing investors Accel and Foundation Capital, bringing total funding to $49M [^1][^2][^3]. Capital will scale AegisAI's fleet of autonomous defense agents, accelerate general availability of Vanguard, and expand enterprise go-to-market [^2]. The round comes as AI-generated spear phishing grew from 2.8% to 13.9% of all observed phishing in 2025, per data cited in the announcement [^2].

## What Investors Say

Dharmesh Thakker, General Partner at Battery Ventures: "Email is where enterprise trust lives and generative AI just broke every assumption legacy email security was built on. Cy and Ryan spent a decade building exactly these systems at Google scale." [^2]

## What Founders Say

Cy Khormaee, Co-founder and CEO: "You cannot patch human trust. If your security program still relies on template-based phishing tests and awareness training, you are training your people to spot last year's threat, not a capable agent crafting a novel lure just for them." [^2]

Cy Khormaee on the market opportunity: "The core idea of building customized, highly advanced agents that can do investigations is going to [determine] who becomes the next dominant security company." [^1]
