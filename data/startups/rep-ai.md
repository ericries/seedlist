## About

Rep AI is an AI operating system for e-commerce led by CEO and co-founder Yoav Oz; the company builds a single agentic platform that runs the full customer journey for online retailers — behavioral-intent detection on arrival, personalized sales guidance, conversion optimization, and post-purchase support [^1][^2][^3]. The $6.2M strategic follow-on funding announced May 28, 2026 was led by Silicon Road Ventures with participation from Osage Venture Partners, Flashpoint Venture Capital, and strategic investor Zendesk; the round follows Rep AI's $8.2M Series A announced August 2024 and brings total funding to approximately $14.4M [^1][^2][^3].
