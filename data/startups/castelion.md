## About

Castelion is a Torrance, California-based defense technology company building low-cost, mass-producible hypersonic strike weapons, with manufacturing operations in New Mexico, Texas and California and offices in Washington, D.C. [^1]. The Series C financing will accelerate scaled production of Blackbeard, Castelion's first hypersonic strike missile, and expand the company's portfolio to longer-range strike weapons and defensive systems, targeting initial fielding in 2027 [^1][^2].
