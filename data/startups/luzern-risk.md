## About

Luzern Risk is an AI-native captive insurance platform that helps businesses design, form, and manage their own insurance programs, integrating risk expertise with technology across industries including real estate, healthcare, manufacturing, and transportation [^1][^2]. Founded in 2023 by Gabriel Weiss, Jonathan York, Rachel Jenkins, and Sam Espinosa, the company offers live financial dashboards, an integrated operations platform, and claims capability to form captives in under 30 days [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-21, Luzern Risk announced a $45 million Series B round led by Insight Partners with participation from Trust Ventures and Caffeinated Capital, bringing total equity raised to $58.5 million [^1][^2].
