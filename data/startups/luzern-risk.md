## About

Luzern Risk is a New York City-based full-service captive manager operating an AI-native technology platform that helps businesses design, form, and manage their own captive insurance programs across industries including real estate, healthcare, manufacturing, and transportation [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2023 by Gabriel Weiss (CEO), Jonathan York (CTO), Rachel Jenkins, and Sam Espinosa, the company offers live financial dashboards, an integrated operations platform, and claims capability designed to form captives in under 30 days [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-17, Luzern Risk announced a $45 million Series B round led by Insight Partners with participation from Trust Ventures and existing investor Caffeinated Capital, bringing total equity raised to $58.5 million [^1][^2][^3]. Caffeinated Capital previously led the company's $12 million Series A in April 2025 [^3].
