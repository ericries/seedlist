## About

Firecrawl builds developer infrastructure for turning web content into structured data for AI applications, and is now expanding into Alexandria — a specialized knowledge library for AI systems [^1]. The San Francisco company is a Y Combinator alumnus (Summer 2022 batch) founded by Caleb Peffer, Eric Ciarla, and Nicolas Silberstein Camara, and disclosed a Series B in September 2026 to fund further work on "the knowledge library for superintelligence" [^1][^2][^4].
