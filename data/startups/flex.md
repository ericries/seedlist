## About

Flex is an AI-native private banking platform serving high-net-worth business owners in the middle market. The company is building Flex Global, a stablecoin-powered banking platform for cross-border business owners across 100+ countries [^1][^2].

| 2026-07-14 | Series B1 | $70M | Halo Fund | Portage Ventures, Wellington Management, Crosslink Capital, 53 Stations, Titanium Ventures, Spice, Florida Funders [^1][^2][^3] |

The Series B1 valued Flex at $1.2B, roughly double its December 2025 Series B valuation [^2][^3].
