## About

Critical Materials Group (CMG) is an Austin, Texas–based defense manufacturing company founded in 2025 that is modernizing U.S. energetics production through modular, automation-ready manufacturing platforms [^1]. On September 2, 2026 the company announced a $10.3 million seed round led by Overmatch Ventures, with participation from Victory Six Advisors and Fulcrum Ventures [^1]. Morgan Hitzig of Overmatch Ventures highlighted the strategic importance of the sector, noting that energetics "sit upstream of nearly every U.S. kinetic capability" [^1]. CMG's founding team combines military and defense industry experience: CEO Kevin Capozzoli brings end-user and operational background, while co-founder Travis runs a private laboratory focused on formulating, testing, and qualifying energetic materials [^1]. Proceeds will accelerate platform development, expand engineering and operations teams, and advance regulatory compliance for government contracts [^1].
