## About

Critical Materials Group (CMG) is an Austin, Texas–based defense manufacturing company founded in 2025 that is modernizing U.S. energetics production through modular, automation-ready manufacturing platforms [^1]. On September 2, 2026 the company announced a $10.3 million seed round led by Overmatch Ventures, with participation from Victory Six Advisors and Fulcrum Ventures [^1]. Morgan Hitzig of Overmatch Ventures said CMG "sits at the intersection of geopolitics and deep tech," highlighting the founding team's combined military and defense industry expertise [^1]. Proceeds will accelerate platform development, expand engineering and operations teams, and advance regulatory compliance for government contracts [^1].
