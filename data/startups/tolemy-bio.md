## About

Tolemy Bio is a biotech startup with offices in Cambridge, UK and Barcelona, Spain, founded in 2024 by Alex Ward and Caelan Anderson, building Orbit — an AI-native virtual-cell intelligence platform that combines experimental data, agentic AI, and virtual-cell models to help advanced therapy and biologics teams make cell biology more predictable, interpretable and controllable [^1][^2]. The €1.4M Pre-Seed announced May 12, 2026 was led by Norrsken Evolve with participation from Big Sur Ventures, JME Ventures, Masia, and a new UK stealth fund; capital will fund data generation, ML and engineering hiring, and early customer deployments [^1][^2].
