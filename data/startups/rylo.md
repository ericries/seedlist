## About

Rylo (formerly Nagish) is an AI communication platform for the Deaf and hard-of-hearing community, founded in 2021 and headquartered with offices in New York and Tel Aviv [^1][^2]. The company's products include real-time speech-to-text and text-to-speech captioning, with planned expansion into sign language translation, workplace accessibility tools, sentiment analysis, and fraud detection [^1][^3]. The company rebranded from Nagish to Rylo in connection with the June 2026 financing [^1].
