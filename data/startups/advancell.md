## About

AdvanCell is a clinical-stage radiopharmaceutical company developing targeted alpha therapies for cancer, operating a vertically integrated platform that combines proprietary Lead-212 (²¹²Pb) technology with isotope supply, advanced manufacturing, and clinical development [^1][^2][^3]. The company is co-headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts, and Brisbane, Australia, and was founded by Andrew Adamovich, who serves as Managing Director for Australia; Philina Lee, PhD was appointed CEO effective January 1, 2026 to lead U.S. expansion [^1][^4]. Its lead clinical candidate, ADVC001 (²¹²Pb-ADVC001), is a PSMA-targeted alpha therapy in development for metastatic prostate cancer [^1][^2][^3].

On 2026-07-15 AdvanCell announced an oversubscribed and upsized $315 million Series D financing led by Ally Bridge Group and co-led by Alpha Wave [^1][^2][^3]. New investors included Bain Capital Life Sciences, Fidelity Management & Research Company, T. Rowe Price Associates, a leading sovereign wealth fund, Eventide Asset Management, and Velosity Capital [^1][^2][^3]. Existing investors Morningside, Eli Lilly and Company, SV Health Investors, Sanofi Ventures, Abingworth, SymBiosis, Tenmile, Brandon Capital, Piper Heartland, Catalio Capital Management, Proto Axiom, Time BioVentures, and other shareholders also participated [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds will advance ADVC001 toward Phase 3 development, expand the Lead-212 platform, and strengthen U.S. manufacturing infrastructure [^1][^2][^3].
