## About

Findigs is a New York City-based developer of an AI-native residential leasing decisioning and underwriting platform [^1][^2]. The company started as a tenant screening business and has expanded into what CEO Steve Carroll describes as an "autonomous decisioning platform" — automated screening software that processes rental applications to deliver instant, data-grounded underwriting decisions and provides contractual fraud guarantees [^1][^2]. The platform manages over 400,000 residential units as of the Series C announcement [^1][^2].

The company plans to use the new capital to advance its leasing decisioning platform, expand its affordable-housing capabilities for LIHTC and Section 8 workflows, and launch Rent Guarantee products designed to protect operator revenue [^1].

## What Investors Say

Marc Weiser, Managing Director at RPM Ventures, said of the investment: "Every rental application in the country runs through screening, underwriting and leasing decisions, tens of millions a year. Findigs is the only product we've seen that rebuilt the decision itself." [^2]

Hugh R. Frater, a Findigs board member, said: "The best way to manage a risk is usually by not taking it. Modern technology provides an alternative." [^2]

## What Founders Say

Co-founder and CEO Steve Carroll said of the company's evolution: "When I think about the evolution of the company, we used to be a tenant screening business. Over the last two years, what we've built is an autonomous decisioning platform." [^2]

Carroll added: "We are creating the category of leasing decisioning, and that category is an explicit service of better revenue quality for our customers." [^2]
