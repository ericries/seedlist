## About

Venice AI is a privacy-first AI platform that gives users access to more than 200 open- and closed-source AI models while providing encrypted, zero-retention inference [^1][^2]. Model responses stream directly to the user's browser and are not stored on Venice's servers; paid subscribers can access end-to-end encrypted modes on select models [^1]. The company was launched in 2024 by Erik Voorhees, an early bitcoin advocate who previously founded Satoshi Dice and cryptocurrency exchange ShapeShift [^1]. Venice reports more than 3 million active users, roughly 1.7 million average daily API calls, and $70M in annualized run-rate revenue at the time of its Series A [^1][^2].
