## About

Inspiren is a New York-based AI platform for senior living communities that combines ambient sensing and real-time behavioral monitoring with clinical data to detect emergencies, falls, and health changes before they escalate [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2016 by Michael Wang (a former Green Beret and nurse), Alex Hejnosz, Paul Coyne, and Vincent Cocito [^2]. Deployments at Aegis Living, Clearwater Living, and Solera Senior Living have reported outcomes including a 34% longer average length of stay, 22% fewer falls, and up to 54% fewer hospitalizations [^1].
