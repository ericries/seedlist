## About

Nomerra is a pre-seed fintech and AI startup automating operations for the asset servicers and asset managers that support private capital markets [^1][^2][^3]. Co-founded roughly six months prior to its announcement round by Johannes Gebendorfer (CEO) and Jakob Zacherl — both former founding employees of fund administrator bunch — the company has already lined up roughly a dozen design partners, each managing more than $10 billion in assets [^2][^3]. The company will use the funding to grow its engineering team and meet demand across enterprise asset servicers and managers in Europe and the United States [^1].
