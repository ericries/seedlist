## About

Alteon is a Bengaluru-based deep-tech startup building autonomous aircraft designed to stay airborne for extended periods by harvesting energy from wind gradients through dynamic soaring — a flight technique inspired by how albatrosses extract energy from wind patterns over ocean surfaces [^1]. Founded in 2024 by 20-year-old Samay Sanghvi, the company plans to first deploy the aircraft for maritime surveillance missions [^1]. Alteon operates from a 10,000-square-foot facility with a team of about 20, produces 4-5 aircraft per week, and has conducted over 200 test flights in the month preceding the funding announcement, including an autonomous flight over the Bay of Bengal that completed seven soaring cycles at 62+ mph within one meter of the water's surface [^1].

On August 31, 2026, TechCrunch reported that Alteon had closed a $2.5 million pre-seed round led by Stripe angel investor Lachy Groom, with participation from Together Fund, Emergent Ventures, and 1517 Fund [^1]. Axios Pro Rata also flagged the round in its August 31 deal listings [^2].
