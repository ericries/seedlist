## About

New Dawn Bio is a Dutch biotechnology startup based in Wageningen that harvests stem cells from trees, multiplies them in bioreactors, and guides them to thicken, harden, and adhere to one another to create an interconnected tissue [^1][^2]. The company claims its process can grow premium wood in days instead of years — approximately 10,000 times faster than conventional forestry — without requiring logging [^2]. New Dawn Bio has produced its first piece of wood and is participating in the EUROSTARS-funded BRANCH project, which aims to develop the first cultured wood prototype grown from tree plant cells in bioreactors [^2].
