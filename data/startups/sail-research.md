## About

Sail Research is a San Francisco-based infrastructure company building an optimized inference stack and stateful sandbox environments designed for long-horizon AI agents — workloads where agents run for hours or days and rack up disproportionate inference cost [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2026 by Neil Movva and Samir Menon, and emerged from stealth with $80 million in combined Seed and Series A funding at a $450 million valuation [^1][^2][^3].
