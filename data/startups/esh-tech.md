## About

Esh-Tech is an Israeli defense technology company that develops DroneLight, a pulsed-laser counter-drone system unveiled at Eurosatory 2026 in Paris [^1][^2][^3]. The company's laser effector uses short pulses of light to physically drill holes into incoming drones, running on roughly 4 kilowatts of input power and neutralizing targets in one to two seconds [^1][^3]. Esh-Tech estimates DroneLight can reduce the acquisition cost of laser effectors by up to 75% compared with traditional continuous-wave laser systems [^1]. The company is led by CEO Erez Riahi [^2][^3].
