## About

Forus (formerly Tandem) is a New York-based AI-powered healthcare network connecting doctors, pharmacies, payers, and biopharma, founded in 2023 by Sahir Jaggi [^1][^2]. The platform automates insurance authorization, financial assistance, and pharmacy routing — the administrative layer between clinical decisions and patient treatment initiation [^1][^2]. The company reports 10x year-over-year provider adoption for two consecutive years, annualized revenue surpassing $10M by end of 2025 and roughly 5x growth through 2026, plus support across nearly 80% of U.S. residential zip codes [^1][^2]. The $160M growth round announced May 12, 2026 at a $1B valuation was led by Thrive Capital with participation from General Catalyst, Accel, Bain Capital Ventures, Redpoint, BoxGroup, and Pear VC [^1][^2][^3].
