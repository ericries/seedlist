## About

Tequipy is a Polish-British startup that ships, services, and retrieves employee IT devices in over 180 countries through a network of more than 800 authorized resellers [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2022 by Tomek Stawarski (former Global Head of IT at Revolut), Bart Czerkies, and Albert Podraza, the company combines software with a network of several hundred local partners to purchase, configure, and retrieve devices on the ground [^1][^3]. Devices are pre-configured for client MDM systems such as Jamf, Intune, or JumpCloud with an average delivery time of 3 days [^3]. The platform serves 150+ companies worldwide, including Booksy, Connecteam, Gigs, ICEYE, RemoFirst, and Taptap Send [^3]. On May 25, 2026, Tequipy announced over €3 million in new funding led by Smedvig Ventures with participation from Manta Ray and Unfold.vc, bringing total raised to over €3.7M with the prior 2024 round of €700K led by Unfold.vc [^1][^2][^3][^4].

## What Founders Say

> Co-founder Tomek Stawarski has stated that during his tenure at Revolut he scaled IT operations from 100 employees in two offices to 5,000 across 17 countries, witnessing firsthand the breakdown of device logistics at international scale — the experience that motivated him to build Tequipy [^1][^3].
