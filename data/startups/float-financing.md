## About

Float is a Stockholm-headquartered AI-native financial operating platform that provides non-dilutive growth capital to European tech SMEs, including revenue-based financing, credit lines and working capital [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by CEO Cedric Notz and Jannis Koehn, the company says it has distributed more than EUR 100 million in funding to over 130 European tech businesses, including RoomPriceGenie and RedTrack, and plans to double its team and expand its UK presence with the new Series A [^1][^2]. Hamburg-based holding company CHAPTERS Group AG led the round and its CEO Jan-Hendrik Mohr joined Float's board [^2].
