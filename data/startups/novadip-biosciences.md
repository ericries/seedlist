## About

Novadip Biosciences is a Belgian late-stage clinical biotech company specializing in regenerative medicine [^1][^2][^3]. The company has developed a proprietary platform called 3M3 that uses adipose-derived stem cells within a three-dimensional extracellular matrix to create tissue for healing large bone defects, non-union fractures, and spinal fusion cases [^1][^2]. Novadip's lead candidate, NVD003, is an autologous graft designed for implantation within three months of cell collection, targeting congenital pseudarthrosis of the tibia and bone non-union — clinical results have shown 89% of patients achieved bone union at 24-month follow-up [^2]. Its second candidate, NVDX3, is an allogeneic bone grafting material [^1]. On 2026-09-21, Novadip announced a €10.4 million convertible financing led by New Science Ventures (US-based), with participation from Fund+, Wallonie Entreprendre, VIVES Fund, ORSA Tech, Sambrinvest, Noshaq, and Invest.BW; the round brings total convertible financing to €20 million when combined with a previous €9.6 million round, and unlocks the final tranche of an €18 million venture debt agreement signed in 2023 with the European Investment Bank (supported by the European Union's InvestEU initiative) [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds will support completion of the pivotal Phase 3 trial for NVD003 in congenital pseudarthrosis of the tibia, preparation of the US Biologics License Application, and advancement toward strategic partnerships [^3].
