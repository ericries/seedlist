## About

Novadip Biosciences is a Belgian late-stage clinical biotech company specializing in regenerative medicine [^1][^2]. The company has developed a proprietary platform called 3M3 that uses adipose-derived stem cells within a three-dimensional extracellular matrix to create tissue for healing large bone defects, non-union fractures, and spinal fusion cases [^1][^2]. Novadip's lead candidate, NVD003, is an autologous graft designed for implantation within three months of cell collection, targeting congenital pseudarthrosis of the tibia and bone non-union — clinical results have shown 89% of patients achieved bone union at 24-month follow-up [^2]. Its second candidate, NVDX3, is an allogeneic bone grafting material [^1]. On 2026-09-21, Novadip announced a €10.4 million convertible financing led by New Science Ventures (US-based), with participation from Fund+, Wallonie Entreprendre, VIVES Fund, ORSA Tech, Sambrinvest, Noshaq, and Invest.BW; the round unlocks access to an €18 million venture debt tranche from the European Investment Bank secured in 2023 [^1][^2].
