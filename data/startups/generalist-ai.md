## About

Generalist AI is a San Mateo, California-based robotics company building general-purpose AI foundation models for robots that operate in factories, warehouses, laboratories, and eventually homes [^1][^2]. Rather than programming task-specific behaviors, the company develops models intended to let robots figure out complex tasks across different environments and hardware configurations [^2].

The company was founded in 2024 by veterans of Google DeepMind and Boston Dynamics, including CEO Pete Florence, Chief Scientist Andy Zeng, and CTO Andrew Barry [^1][^2]. In April 2026, Generalist released GEN-1, its general-purpose robotics model. The company reported that GEN-1 improves average success rates to 99% on tasks where previous models achieved 64%, completes dexterous tasks roughly three times faster than prior approaches, and requires only one hour of robot data per task [^2].
