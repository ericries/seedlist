## About

Nexstrom is a Singapore-based deep-tech startup building equipment and processes to manufacture transition-metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) — extremely thin 2D semiconductor materials — directly on 300mm wafers [^1]. The company was incubated by Xora Innovation, Temasek's deep-tech venture-building arm, and aims to enable chipmakers to produce 2D materials at scale for the Angstrom era of logic and memory [^1][^3]. Its flagship platform, North Star, targets commercial readiness between 2030 and 2035 [^1].
