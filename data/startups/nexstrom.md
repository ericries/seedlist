## About

Nexstrom is a Singapore-based deep-tech startup building equipment and processes to manufacture transition-metal dichalcogenides (TMDs) — extremely thin 2D semiconductor materials — directly on 300mm wafers [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by Dr. Lance Li, a distinguished professor at the National University of Singapore and former TSMC director who has researched 2D material growth since 2012, and Dr. Phoebe Tan, a Partner at Xora Innovation; Xora — Temasek's deep-tech venture-building arm — serves as both incubator and lead investor, with Xora Managing Partner and CIO Phil Inagaki sitting on Nexstrom's board [^2]. The company's flagship North Star platform is a chemical-vapor-deposition system built for foundry-compatible automated wafer handling on 300mm wafers, and Nexstrom describes performance benchmarks of up to 100 times lower defect density, 75 percent lower switching power, and 2x smaller gate length capability relative to silicon in the Angstrom era of logic and memory [^2][^3]. Foothill Ventures categorizes Nexstrom as a seed-stage investment developing "sub-1nm 2D Transition Metal Dichalcogenide (TMD) materials and processes for Angstrom-era logic and memory" [^3].
