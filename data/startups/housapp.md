## About

HousApp is an Amsterdam-based proptech startup building an AI-native platform that automates paperwork, scheduling, and administrative workflows for real estate agencies [^1][^2]. Founded in 2025 by Sebastiaan Kloppenborg, HousApp evolved from a property viewing scheduler into a broader AI platform for estate agents [^1]. The company serves more than 2,200 Dutch real estate agents and expanded through the acquisition of proptech firm Friva earlier in 2026 [^2].
