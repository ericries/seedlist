## About

Island builds an Enterprise Browser — a Chromium-based browser purpose-built for organizations to run, secure, and govern how employees and AI agents interact with SaaS applications and internal data [^1][^3]. Founded in 2020 by Mike Fey and Dan Amiga and headquartered in Dallas, the company positions the browser as an alternative to layers of endpoint security, VDI, and DLP by pushing controls into the point of interaction [^1][^3].
