## About

Havoc is a Providence, Rhode Island–based collaborative autonomy startup (founded 2024, with offices in Austin and San Diego) building a multi-domain autonomy software stack that operates across air, surface, and ground platforms for defense applications [^1]. The company announced a $100M Series A on May 12, 2026 with new investors CCM Capital Markets, Clear Street, Cobalt Capital, Boardman Bay Capital Management, Meet Perry, Mute Ventures, Soren Ventures, SAIC, and JA Green joining returning backers Outlander VC, Scout VC, B Capital, Lockheed Martin, Taiwania Capital, UP.Partners, The Veteran Fund, and Vanderbilt University; total raised since founding is approximately $200M [^1].
