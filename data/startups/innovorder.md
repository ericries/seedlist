## About

Innovorder is a French restaurant technology company founded in 2014 that provides an omnichannel digital platform for commercial restaurants and collective catering operations [^1]. The platform unifies point-of-sale (POS), ordering kiosks, online ordering, click-and-collect, delivery, and QR-code table ordering through a single system [^1]. The company serves over 2,500 clients, split roughly 50/50 between quick-service restaurants and collective catering, including Big Fernand, Amorino, Bagel Corner, Elior, Sodexo, and the City of Paris (which uses Innovorder to manage 25 restaurants) [^2].

Innovorder reached profitability in 2024 and grew 40% in the prior year, with projected 2026 revenue of €15 million [^1][^2]. The company is co-founded and led by Jérôme Varnier (CEO), Romain Melloul (General Manager), and Olivier Loverde (CTO, who joined in 2016) [^2].

In conjunction with the June 2026 round, Innovorder launched Atlas, an AI-native platform that converts restaurant data into actionable decisions, including chatbot-based analytics and HR scheduling tools, with a data-security architecture that avoids sending data to external LLM providers [^2].
