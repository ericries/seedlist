## About

Furo is a Munich-based industrial battery-storage software company developing AI systems that help commercial and industrial customers reduce electricity costs by optimizing battery-storage operations [^1][^2]. The company was founded by three co-founders — Lena Sophia Voß, Leonie Wagner, and Simon Wittner — all age 28, who met through Munich's Center for Digital Technology and Management program and incorporated Furo as a Delaware C Corporation while operating out of Germany [^1]. On 2026-09-10, TechCrunch reported that Furo had raised a $4 million seed round led by TQ Ventures, with participation from Neo, Sandberg Bernthal Venture Partners (the fund co-founded by Sheryl Sandberg), and the Center for Digital Technology and Management (CDTM) [^1]. Wirtschaftswoche reported the round as approximately €3.44 million [^2].
