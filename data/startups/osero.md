## About

Osero is a Stablewatch-incubated stablecoin yield infrastructure project building what it describes as "the savings account for where your stablecoins already are," integrating with Sky Ecosystem's USDS protocol [^1][^2]. The $13.5M seed round announced May 12, 2026 was co-led by Sky Ecosystem and Tether's Plasma blockchain, with angel investors representing USDT0, Maple, Accountable, Four Pillars, RedStone, The Rollup, and Kairos Research participating; $10M of the total is designated to fund reserve requirements that protect both users and Sky Protocol from potential losses [^1][^2][^3].
