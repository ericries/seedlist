## About

Soource is a Bolzano-based startup offering AI-driven procurement solutions focused on sourcing and supplier selection [^1][^2]. The company has developed proprietary infrastructure that automates high-operational-intensity activities in supplier management and supports data-driven procurement decisions, with customers across the utilities, pharmaceutical, energy, insurance, food, industrial and construction sectors [^1].
