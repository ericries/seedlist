## About

Coram AI is a San Francisco-based physical security platform that uses AI agents to analyze video footage from existing security cameras [^1]. The company's "Deep Investigation" software allows enterprise customers to search across months of video, entry records, and visitor data spanning hundreds of cameras and sites, then automatically generate investigation reports [^1][^2]. AI models run locally on edge devices rather than in the cloud, addressing customer concerns about privacy and bandwidth [^1]. Co-founders Ashesh Jain (CEO) and Peter Ondruska previously worked on autonomous driving perception systems [^1]. More than 1,500 sites across the U.S. and Canada use the platform, including Fortune 500 customers and brands such as 1-800-GOT-JUNK?, Hershey's Ice Cream, and the World YMCA [^2].
