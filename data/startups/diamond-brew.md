## About

Diamond Brew is a Chicago-based consumer packaged goods startup that makes what it bills as "America's first shelf-stable, brewless coffee pod." The company's hexagon-shaped pods contain freeze-dried coffee crystals produced through a proprietary liquid nitrogen flash-freezing process; the crystals dissolve in hot or cold water and require no brewing machine [^1][^2][^3]. The company was launched in late 2024 by founder and CEO Douglas Yu, and operates primarily through direct-to-consumer channels and social media [^1][^3].
