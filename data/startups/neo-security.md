## About

Neo is a Boston-based cybersecurity company building an agentic software control platform for enterprises, giving security operations teams inventory, posture intelligence, attribution, and policy control over AI agents, AI-enabled applications, browsers, identities, and traditional software with newly introduced agentic capabilities [^1][^2]. The company emerged from stealth on 2026-07-20 with $100 million in combined funding and was founded by former SentinelOne, Wiz, and Palo Alto Networks executives, including CEO Nick Warner (previously President of Go-to-Market at SentinelOne) [^1][^2][^3].

Third-party reporting describes the financing as a $75M Series A stacked on top of a previously undisclosed $25M seed round completed in 2025; the company's own press release aggregates the two into "$100M in funding" [^3].
