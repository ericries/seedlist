## About

Manam Chocolate is a Hyderabad, India-based craft chocolate brand operating a vertically integrated, "farm-to-bar" model that sources Indian-grown cacao from Andhra Pradesh and runs its own fermentation, manufacturing, and retail experiences [^1][^2]. Founded in 2021 by Chaitanya Muppala, the company aims to showcase Indian cacao through bean-to-bar chocolate products [^2][^3]. As of the June 2026 raise, Manam operated three retail outlets across Hyderabad and the Delhi-NCR region, with plans to expand to 18 stores over the next two years [^1][^3].
