## About

Attention is a New York-based AI sales platform founded in 2021 by CEO Anis Bennaceur and CTO Matthias Wickenburg that records and transcribes customer conversations across meetings, emails, calls, and CRMs and uses AI to automate follow-ups, next steps, and CRM updates [^1][^2][^4]. Attention raised a $3.1M seed round led by Eniac Ventures on January 9, 2023 [^2], followed by a $14M Series A led by Alven on October 21, 2024 with participation from Eniac Ventures, Frst, Liquid 2 Ventures, 645 Ventures, and Aglaé Ventures [^3]. A partial Series B closing of $21.6M out of a $37.5M offering was disclosed via SEC Form D reported on April 13, 2026 [^1]; the final round was announced on June 24, 2026 at $30M led by RTP Global with participation from returning investors Aglaé Ventures, Eniac, and Alven, new investor Linea, and angel investors from the customer base including Kirill Bigai (Preply co-founder and CEO) and Sam Jacobs (Pavilion CEO) [^5][^6][^7].
