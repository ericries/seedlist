## About

Airbound is a Bengaluru-based drone delivery startup building blended-wing-body tail-sitter aircraft that take off vertically and cruise on fixed wings [^1]. The company has completed more than 13,000 autonomous flights and operates a commercial corridor for Narayana Health, India's fourth-largest hospital network [^1]. Founded in 2023, the company designs vertical-flight drones with payload-to-aircraft ratios roughly three times better than the industry norm [^1].
