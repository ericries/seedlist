## About

Leyden Labs is a Dutch biotech developing intranasal antibody sprays designed to block respiratory viruses at the point of entry, leveraging its Mucosal Protection Platform to protect against influenza, coronaviruses, and other respiratory viruses [^1][^2]. The company's lead program is PanFlu, based on CR9114, a broadly neutralising anti-influenza antibody targeting a conserved region of the influenza haemagglutinin [^2]. Leyden Labs was selected for the European Innovation Council's STEP Scale Up programme [^1].
