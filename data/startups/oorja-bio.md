## About

Oorja Bio is a Houston, TX-based clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing ORJ-001 (a first-in-class therapeutic for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF)) and a broader pipeline targeting fibrotic diseases and cardiopulmonary function [^1][^2][^3]. The company is led by CEO Sujay Kango and CMO Janethe Pena, both former Acceleron Pharma executives (Acceleron was acquired by Merck in 2021 for $11.5B) [^2][^3]. The $30M Series A announced May 19-20, 2026 was led by Westlake BioPartners (founding investor), with Beth Seidenberg (Founding Managing Director, Westlake BioPartners) leading the investment; the round funded Oorja's stealth-exit launch with capital to advance ORJ-001 into Phase 2 trials [^1][^2][^3].
