## About

Walden Robotics is a physical AI company that develops general-purpose robots for manufacturing and logistics, spun out of Toyota Research Institute (TRI) in January 2026 [^1][^2][^3]. Co-founder and CEO Russ Tedrake is an MIT professor who previously served as senior vice president of large behavior models at TRI, where he led work on AI models built for physical tasks rather than language [^1][^2][^3]. Walden's approach centers on "large behavior models" — AI systems that let robots learn new work through real-world repetition rather than being manually reprogrammed per task [^1][^3]. The company reports that its robots reached live production inside a North American Toyota factory within roughly two months of first pilot, handling car-part transport, machinery cleaning, assembly kitting, machine tending, tool setting, and parts preparation [^2].

On 2026-07-15 Walden emerged from stealth with a $300 million seed round at a $1.1 billion post-money valuation, co-led by Toyota (Toyota Motor Corp., Toyota Invention Partners, and Toyota Ventures) and Deviation Capital [^1][^2][^3]. Additional participants include NVIDIA, Boeing, Samsung NEXT, Prologis Ventures, CoreWeave Ventures, AE Industrial Partners, Calibrate Ventures, Colle Capital, Shine Capital, NextView Ventures, Squarepoint Capital, One Madison Group, KAS Venture Partners, and Menlo Ventures [^1][^3].
