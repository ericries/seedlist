## About

Hippo Harvest is a Pescadero, California-based controlled environment agriculture company that grows USDA-certified organic leafy greens using autonomous mobile robots inside greenhouses [^1][^2]. The company says its robotics- and machine-learning-powered growing system uses 92% less water, 55% less fertilizer, and 94% less land compared to traditional agriculture while maintaining organic certification [^1]. Products are distributed through retailers across Northern California and the Pacific Northwest, including Sprouts, Haggen (an Albertsons banner), and Gus's Community Markets in San Francisco [^1]. Hippo Harvest launched its Butter Lettuce line at retail in early 2026 and is preparing to commercialize indoor-grown Spinach [^1].

On 2026-07-09 Hippo Harvest announced a $30M Series C round led by Cox Farms — a subsidiary of Cox Enterprises — with participation from existing investors Congruent Ventures, Hawthorne Food Ventures, Collaborative Fund, and Fresh Investment Club [^1][^2][^3]. The company said proceeds will fund a 30-acre expansion at a new facility in Hollister, California and development of its next-generation robotic growing system [^1].
