## About

Grace Investment Machine (GIM) is an AI-native investment technology company that builds agentic AI systems for capital markets, headquartered in Hong Kong with additional operations in Beijing and Shanghai [^1][^2][^3]. The company describes its approach as a "Visionary Machine": AI systems that generate, test, and refine investment hypotheses through market data, feedback loops, and coordinated agents, advancing on two fronts — foundation models tailored to capital-market environments, and multi-agent systems that generate, validate, and evolve investment signals across coordinated reasoning layers [^1][^2]. GIM's flagship research paper, CogAlpha, features a seven-layer agent architecture and was accepted to the ACL 2026 main conference with an Oral recommendation [^1][^3].

On 2026-07-09 GIM announced the close of a $20M Series A financing co-led by Hony Capital and an unnamed leading US venture capital firm, with participation from IDG Capital and existing investor Monolith Capital [^1][^2][^3]. The company said the round marks its third funding round within its first year of operations and that it is bringing AI-driven strategies and investment products into live validation across multiple asset classes and markets [^1][^2].
