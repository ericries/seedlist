## About

BrainChild Bio is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing CAR T cell therapies for tumors of the central nervous system, with dual headquarters in Seattle and Cambridge, MA [^1]. The company's lead program, BCB-276, is an autologous B7-H3-targeted CAR T cell therapy being evaluated in the ILLUMINATE Phase 2 registrational trial for diffuse intrinsic pontine glioma (DIPG), a fatal pediatric brainstem cancer with median survival of ~11 months [^1]. Its pipeline is built on technology exclusively licensed from Seattle Children's, and includes BCB-214, a triple-targeting CAR T therapy advancing toward initial clinical testing in glioblastoma [^1].
