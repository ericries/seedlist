## About

Regatta Data is building RegattaDB, a distributed SQL database that unifies OLTP, OLAP, and vector workloads at scale. It launched RegattaDB into general availability on July 15, 2026, targeting AI agent systems that require transactional, analytical, and vector data access in a single engine [^1][^2].
