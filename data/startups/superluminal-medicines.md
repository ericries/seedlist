## About

Superluminal Medicines is a Boston-based biotechnology company using AI, machine learning, and structural biology to accelerate drug discovery for G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) [^1][^2]. Its proprietary Hyperloop platform combines computational and experimental methods to develop small-molecule therapeutics, with an initial pipeline focused on cardiometabolic diseases and rare genetic forms of obesity [^1][^2]. The company is led by CEO Cony D'Cruz and CSO Ajay Yekkirala, and operates out of Lilly Gateway Labs at 15 Necco St. in Boston [^1][^3].
