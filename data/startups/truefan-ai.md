## About

TrueFan AI is a Gurugram-based artificial intelligence video platform for enterprises. The company has developed a foundational AI video model that lets businesses generate personalised video content at scale, removing the need for traditional production cycles [^1] [^2]. The platform serves more than 100 enterprises across banking, healthcare, consumer goods, and media, and reports scaling from 5 million to more than 20 million videos produced annually within a year, alongside 11-fold revenue growth over the past 20 months [^1] [^3]. The company is led by co-founder and CEO Nimish Goel [^1].
