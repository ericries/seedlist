## About

Quantum Bridge Technologies is a Toronto-based quantum-safe cybersecurity company spun out of one of the world's leading quantum security research groups, founded in 2019 by Mattia Montagna and Hoi-Kwong Lo [^1][^2][^3]. The company's patented Distributed Symmetric Key Establishment (DSKE) protocol enables organizations to generate, distribute, and manage symmetric keys through a decentralized architecture, removing single points of failure [^1][^2]. The $8M Series A announced May 20, 2026 was led by Primo Capital SGR with participation from Wayra (Telefónica), Cadenza VC, Club degli Investitori angels, HPE, and Bacchus Venture Capital; combined with prior financing from Alumni Ventures and the University of Toronto, total funding now stands at $16M [^1][^2][^3].
