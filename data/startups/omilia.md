## About

Omilia is an Athens, Greece-based provider of conversational AI for enterprise customer support, operating across voice, chat, and messaging channels [^1][^2]. Founded in 2002 by Dimitris Vassos, the company builds self-learning agentic AI for contact centers using a multi-tool architecture rather than relying solely on generative models, and supports compliance standards including FedRAMP, PCI-DSS, SOC 2, HIPAA, and GDPR for regulated industries [^1][^3]. Omilia serves enterprises including Capital One, Discover, RBC, Taco Bell, DWP, and PSEG, and its annual recurring revenue has grown more than 10x since the 2020 Series A to over $60 million, with headcount at roughly 500 and expected to reach 600 by year-end [^2][^3].
