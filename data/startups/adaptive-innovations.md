## About

Adaptive Innovations is an AI-native healthcare provider, starting in home health, based in New York City with operations in Dallas, Texas [^1][^2]. The company combines an AI operating system for home health operations with in-person clinical services, automating medical intake, scheduling, charting, and compliance billing to reduce administrative overhead [^2][^3]. Since launching in 2025, Adaptive reports an 80% reduction in clinician documentation time and has delivered more than 100,000 care visits through partnerships with 500+ healthcare organizations [^1][^4].

The company is led by Co-CEOs Alex Wendland and Logan Stinson, CTO Ryan Tolsma, and COO Hunter Stinson [^1][^2].
