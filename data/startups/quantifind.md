## About

Quantifind develops Graphyte, an AI-native risk intelligence platform that combines internal and external data with purpose-built language models to power agentic middleware for entity resolution and risk discovery in financial crime prevention. The company serves six of the world's top 10 Tier 1 financial institutions and supports tens of thousands of financial crime, compliance, and national security professionals. CEO Ari Tuchman is a co-founder [^1][^2][^3].
