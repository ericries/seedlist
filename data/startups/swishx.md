## About

SwishX is a Bengaluru, India-based developer of what it describes as the world's first agentic AI platform built exclusively for pharmaceutical and medtech companies, aimed at modernising commercial operations across sales, marketing, tenders, contracts, and distribution [^1][^2]. Co-founded by Dushyant Sapre (ex-Google) and Jai Anand (ex-Amazon), the platform is organized around four hubs — Tender IQ, Contract IQ, Marketing IQ, and Channel IQ — each with specialized AI agents that target up to a 30% improvement in revenue outcomes [^1][^2]. On May 12, 2026, SwishX announced a $2.2M seed round with participation from Powerhouse Ventures, Blume Ventures, Sadev Ventures, and Atrium Ventures, with the company targeting $5M ARR and 100+ enterprise customers by FY2026–27 [^1][^2][^3].
