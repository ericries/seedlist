## About

Street Group is a Manchester-based vertical SaaS and AI company serving the UK residential property sector [^1]. The company operates an integrated operating system for estate and letting agents spanning its core CRM (Street.co.uk), the prospecting and lead-generation platform Spectre, and a suite of AI-native products including Cortex, which lets agents build and orchestrate their own AI agents [^1][^2]. Co-founded by siblings Tom and Heather Staff, who continue as majority shareholders and co-CEOs after the Hg investment, Street employs approximately 200 staff at its Manchester headquarters [^1][^3].
