## About

Rightway is a New York-headquartered healthcare navigation and pharmacy benefits company that operates as a pass-through Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM) and clinical care navigation platform for employers and health plans [^1][^3]. The company serves 45 Fortune 500 clients and markets itself as an independent PBM using a transparent single-fee model rather than the spread-pricing model of legacy PBMs, pairing pharmacy services with member-facing clinical guides [^1][^3].
