## About

Rightway is a healthcare navigation and pharmacy benefits company that operates as a pass-through Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM) and clinical care navigation platform for employers and health plans [^3]. The company markets itself as an independent PBM using a transparent single-fee model rather than the spread-pricing model of legacy PBMs, and pairs pharmacy services with member-facing clinical guides [^3].
