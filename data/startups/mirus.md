## About

MiRus is an Atlanta-based medical device company founded and led by CEO Jay Yadav, M.D., developing proprietary biomaterials, implants, and procedural solutions for cardiovascular and orthopedic disease — notably the SIEGEL Balloon Expandable Transcatheter Aortic Valve Replacement (TAVR) system, the first nickel-free balloon-expandable TAVR valve, built on a proprietary rhenium alloy [^1][^2][^3]. MiRus recently initiated the STAR pivotal trial evaluating SIEGEL across three valve sizes in up to 1,025 patients with severe symptomatic aortic stenosis [^1]. The $1.5B strategic equity investment announced May 18, 2026 from Boston Scientific acquired an approximately 34% stake in MiRus, and includes an exclusive option for Boston Scientific to acquire the MiRus TAVR business outright through up to $3B in additional cash payments contingent on clinical and regulatory milestones [^1][^2][^3].
