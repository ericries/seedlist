## About

TYLsemi is a San Jose-based semiconductor company delivering a full-stack chiplet platform for custom AI silicon [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Mohit Gupta (CEO) and Sunil Bhardwaj, both of whom previously led engineering and operations teams at Alphawave Semi [^1][^2]. TYLsemi's portfolio spans production-ready chiplets for connectivity (TYL.IO), power delivery (TYL.Power), and memory (TYL.Mem), along with TYL.Forge, an end-to-end platform for custom silicon implementation [^1][^2]. TYLsemi says the platform can reduce custom-chip development time and cost by up to 50%, and that TYL.IO and TYL.Power chiplet samples will be available to qualified customers in 2027 in partnership with TSMC [^1][^2].
