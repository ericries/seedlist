## About

Neon (Neon Commerce, Inc.) is a San Francisco-based platform that helps game studios sell games and in-game items directly to players via web-based direct-to-consumer (D2C) storefronts, sidestepping app-store fees [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by CEO Chris Faught, the company provides commerce and loyalty infrastructure that some partner studios now route 50–70% of gross revenue through, up from a prior industry ceiling of ~25–30% [^1]. Neon reports 200% year-over-year growth [^2].
