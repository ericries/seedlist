## About

Premier Lacrosse League (PLL) is a professional men's outdoor lacrosse league co-founded in 2018 by Paul and Mike Rabil, operating as a tour-based media and sports business with a stated goal to establish lacrosse as a mainstream US sport [^1][^2]. The league acquired Major League Lacrosse in 2020 to consolidate the men's professional field lacrosse market and launched the Women's Lacrosse League (WLL) as an affiliated property [^1][^2]. As part of the June 2026 Series E, ESPN took a minority equity position in the league and PLL announced plans to expand to Texas with the first PLL and WLL franchises based in the state [^1][^2].
