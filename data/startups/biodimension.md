## About

Biodimension is a Bengaluru, India-based life sciences startup specializing in New Approach Methodologies (NAMs) and tissue engineering [^1]. The company develops lab-grown human tissue and organoid models as ethical alternatives to animal testing, providing pharmaceutical, biotech, and cosmetic customers with more human-relevant data for research and product safety evaluations [^1]. Biodimension was co-founded by Manojkumar S. (CEO), Ranjith Kumar Velusamy, and Pradeep Arunachalam [^1].
