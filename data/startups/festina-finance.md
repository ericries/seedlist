## About

Festina Finance is a Copenhagen-based fintech company that builds cloud-native, modular technology platforms for pensions, life insurance, and financial planning [^1][^2]. Founded in 2007 by Morten Schantz, the company operates in Denmark, the Netherlands, the UK, and Norway, and employs more than 200 people [^1][^3]. Its platforms are used by customers responsible for approximately 10 million pension policies and 3 million banking customers across Europe [^1][^3].

On June 19, 2026, Festina Finance announced over €25 million in growth investment led by UK-based Birchway Capital, at a valuation of approximately €200 million [^1][^2][^3]. The capital will be used to continue developing the company's life and pensions platform and to expand its UK presence [^1][^3].

## What Founders Say

Morten Schantz, founder of Festina Finance, said the investment enables acceleration of work to "scale our platform across Europe as demand for modern pensions infrastructure continues to grow." [^2]

Schantz also stated: "The pensions and life insurance industry is undergoing a major technology transformation, driven by the need to replace legacy infrastructure with more modern, flexible platforms." [^3]
