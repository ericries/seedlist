## About

Layup Parts is a Huntington Beach-based composites manufacturing startup founded by ex-Anduril engineer Zack Eakin; the company aims to simplify and accelerate the ordering and production of custom carbon-fiber and fiberglass parts, reducing turnaround time from weeks to hours in some cases, and serves customers in motorsports, aerospace, defense, and other industries [^1][^2]. The $42M Series A announced June 2, 2026 was led by Marlinspike with participation from Cerberus Ventures, Pinegrove Venture Partners, Founders Fund, and Lux Capital [^1][^2].
