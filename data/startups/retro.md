## About

Retro — operated by Lone Palm Labs — is a friend-focused photo-sharing app built by co-founders Nathan Sharp and Ryan Olson, both former Instagram product engineers; launched in 2023 as a personal photo journal, it has expanded to let users privately share weekly photos with friends, create shared albums, view recaps, and "rewind" through past memories [^1][^2]. The company's $21M+ Series A was disclosed via an SEC Form D filing dated August 19, 2026 (with the round closed in December 2025), and was co-led by Thrive Capital and Field Ventures (Dylan Field); PitchBook estimates the post-money valuation at over $100 million and the app has been downloaded approximately 7 million times [^1][^2].
