## About

Retro — operated by Lone Palm Labs — is a friend-focused photo-sharing app built by former Instagram employees; launched in 2023 as a personal photo journal, it has expanded to let users privately share weekly photos with friends, create shared albums, view recaps, and "rewind" through past memories [^1][^2]. The company's $21M+ Series A was disclosed via an SEC Form D filing dated August 19, 2026, with the round closed in December of the prior period; PitchBook estimates the current valuation at over $100 million [^1][^2].
