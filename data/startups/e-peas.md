## About

e-peas is a Belgian fabless semiconductor company based in Louvain-la-Neuve, Wallonia, founded in 2014 as a spinout from UCLouvain [^1][^2]. The company designs ultra-low-power management integrated circuits (PMICs) and energy-harvesting chips that let electronic devices operate autonomously from ambient energy sources such as light, radio frequency, vibration, and heat — reducing or eliminating the need for batteries [^1][^2][^3]. e-peas positions itself as a pioneer of the "Ambient IoT" concept, and its chips are used in customer products ranging from remote controls, solar keyboards, and electronic shelf labels to industrial and geolocation sensors, with Samsung among its listed customers [^2][^3].
