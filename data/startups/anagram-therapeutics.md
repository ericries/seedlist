## About

Anagram Therapeutics is a clinical-stage private biopharmaceutical company headquartered in Natick, Massachusetts, dedicated to improving the lives of people living with exocrine pancreatic insufficiency (EPI) due to cystic fibrosis, pancreatic cancer, and related disorders [^1]. The company's lead candidate, ANG003, is a novel orally delivered recombinant enzyme replacement therapy designed to reduce the daily pill burden — patients currently take up to 40 capsules per day, while ANG003 is intended to require only one tablet per meal [^1] [^2]. The company is led by President and CEO Robert Gallotto [^1].

ANG003 has demonstrated positive clinical data in people with EPI due to cystic fibrosis, and Anagram is initiating an international Phase II trial following its earlier ANG003-22-101 study [^1] [^3]. Prior to the 2026 Blackstone investment, the Cystic Fibrosis Foundation contributed more than $30 million in non-dilutive funding to support clinical and development work on ANG003 [^1] [^2].
