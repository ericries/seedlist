## About

kausable is a German DeepTech AI startup building reasoning-first frontier AI that adapts on its own to changing context, without requiring retraining [^1][^2]. The company is backed by private angel investors from Black Forest Labs, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Noxtua, and the European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems (ELLIS) [^1][^2]. It plans to use the seed round to expand its nine-person team and advance its rapid-learning frontier model [^2].
