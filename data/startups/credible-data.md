## About

Credible Data is a Boulder, Colorado-based startup building a "trusted business context engine" for enterprise data — a semantic layer that tells AI agents what the data in a warehouse actually means [^1][^2]. Its platform creates governed semantic models defining business metrics, entities, relationships, policies, and access controls, aiming to let AI systems generate more consistent and reliable responses [^1][^2]. The company cites its own survey of 101 enterprises in June 2026 in which 57% traced a confidently wrong agent answer back to missing or inconsistent business context in the prior six months [^2]. On 2026-07-28, Credible Data announced a $10 million seed round led by Gradient, SignalFire, and K5 Global, with participation from SV Angel and angels including Godard Abel (G2 CEO), Wes McKinney (pandas creator), and Alex Dean (Snowplow Analytics CEO) [^1][^2]. The company was founded by former Google Cloud executive Kyle Nesbit [^1][^2].
