## About

Andera is a San Francisco-based AI-native platform that automates internal audit, handling control testing end-to-end from user access provisioning to complex financial controls like bad debt reserves [^1][^2]. The platform processes hundreds of millions of tokens of financial evidence across Excel workbooks, PDFs, system screenshots, and journal entries to identify relevant documentation and render audit judgments [^1]. Co-founder and CEO Aryo Patel previously worked at Microsoft on Azure infrastructure scaling and on the trading desk at Jane Street; co-founder and CTO Tinah Hong previously built machine learning systems at Stripe and was a software engineer at Microsoft [^2]. The company was founded in 2024 [^2].
