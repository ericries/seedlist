## About

Circular Materials is an Italian climate-tech scale-up, founded in 2019, that develops processes to recover critical raw materials — including dissolved metals such as copper, nickel, and cobalt — from industrial wastewater [^1][^2][^3]. The company's proprietary SWaP (Supercritical Water Precipitation) technology aims to recover more than 99% of the dissolved metals in wastewater streams, and its facility in Padua, Italy has received "end-of-waste" status under the European Waste Framework Directive [^2]. In 2025, its RECOVER-IT project was selected by the European Commission as a Strategic Project under the Critical Raw Materials Act (CRMA) [^4]. On 2026-07-21, Circular Materials announced a SAFE financing round totaling €11.8 million with participation from CDP Venture Capital, the European Innovation Council Fund, EIT RawMaterials, 360 Capital, Corbites, and Lumar S.r.l., to be used to expand operational capacity, develop the SWaP technology further, and build an additional industrial recovery hub in Ferrara, Italy [^1][^2][^3].
