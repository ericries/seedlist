## About

Willow is an agentic access platform that gives enterprises identity, access, and governance controls for AI agents [^1]. Based in Herzliya, Israel, the company emerged from stealth on June 4, 2026 [^1][^2]. The platform assigns verifiable identities to AI agents, enforces least-privilege access controls, monitors shadow AI usage, and integrates with identity providers such as Okta and Entra [^3]. It works across commercial models including Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT as well as custom models, with deployment via SaaS, dedicated cloud, or self-hosted [^3].

Willow was founded by former Wix engineers Eyal Ben Ezra (CEO), Shalev Shalit (CTO), and Idan Chetrit (VP Platform) [^1][^2]. The platform has been deployed across more than 5,000 Wix employees and is expanding to enterprise customers in cybersecurity, real estate, and fintech [^1].
