## About

Fuse Energy is a London-based vertically integrated energy company that combines renewable generation, wholesale trading, and direct-to-consumer electricity and gas supply [^1]. It was founded in 2022 by Alan Chang, the former Chief Revenue Officer at Revolut, and Charles Orr, an early Revolut employee [^1]. The company owns and operates solar farms in Southern England and a wind farm in Scotland, runs an in-house energy trading desk, and supplies electricity and gas to UK households through its app and website [^1].

By December 2025, Fuse supplied roughly 200,000 UK households, having launched publicly in July 2023 [^1]. The company reports being EBITDA-positive every month since December 2025 [^2].
