## About

Foundational Industries is a manufacturing startup building factories designed from the ground up to be operated entirely by AI software rather than retrofitting traditional assembly lines with robotic components [^1]. The company is led by CEO Jonathan Winer, formerly at Alphabet's Sidewalk Infrastructure Partners. Its initial focus targets data-center hardware manufacturers and custom rack enclosures for new AI silicon systems [^1].

## What Founders Say

"The factory has to be designed for AI from the ground up. Every process, every work cell, every material movement, every inspection step, every scheduling decision, every quality loop has to be built around a common intelligence layer." — Jonathan Winer, CEO & Co-Founder, quoted in The AI Insider [^2].

"This is how America leapfrogs. Not by recreating the physical world China already dominates. Not by trying to win the last war on labor cost, scale, and legacy supply chains." — Jonathan Winer, CEO & Co-Founder, quoted in The AI Insider [^2].
