## About

Foundational Industries is a manufacturing startup building factories designed from the ground up to be operated entirely by AI software rather than retrofitting traditional assembly lines with robotic components [^1]. The company is led by CEO Jonathan Winer, formerly at Alphabet's Sidewalk Infrastructure Partners. Its initial focus targets data-center hardware manufacturers and custom rack enclosures for new AI silicon systems [^1].
