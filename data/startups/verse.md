## About

Verse (also branded Verse Enterprises) is a San Francisco-based energy management software startup focused on accelerating AI data center deployment by orchestrating on-site solar, battery storage, and other distributed energy resources alongside existing grid infrastructure [^1][^2][^3]. The company says its software helps data centers bypass multi-year utility interconnection queues, with a goal of managing more than 100 data center sites within the next year [^1][^3].

On June 18, 2026, Verse announced an oversubscribed $54 million Series B and launched a new product called Dispatch Intelligence in partnership with Calibrant Energy [^1][^3].
