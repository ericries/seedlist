## About

Oak emerged from stealth on July 15, 2026 with $60 million in seed funding to build what it describes as an "AI-Native Identity Operating System" — a unified control plane that governs every identity across an organization, whether human, machine, or AI agent [^1][^2][^3]. The company positions its platform as a replacement for the fragmented stack of legacy identity governance and identity security tools that CISOs typically deploy [^1][^3].

Oak is co-headquartered in Tel Aviv, Israel and San Francisco [^1]. The company was co-founded by Shai Morag (CEO) and Tal Marom (Chief Product Officer) [^1][^2]. Morag previously co-founded Secdo, sold to Palo Alto Networks in 2018, and cloud identity security startup Ermetic, sold to Tenable for $265 million in 2023 [^2][^3]. According to TechCrunch, the founding team spent months consulting with roughly 100 CISOs and IAM leaders before building the product [^2].

Oak's platform uses an AI connector framework to map access permissions to actual application usage and removes unnecessary permissions in real time rather than during periodic access reviews [^2][^3]. The product is generally available and is already deployed by enterprise customers at the time of the announcement [^1][^2].

## What Investors Say

Andrei Brasoveanu, Partner at Accel: "Backing Shai a second time was one of the easiest decisions we've ever made. He and his team have spent their careers solving the hardest problems in enterprise security, and identity is the biggest one left standing." [^1]

## What Founders Say

Shai Morag, Oak CEO and Co-Founder: "I've built several companies in this space, so I understand why identity has stayed broken for so long. The tools were never built to work as one, and adding more of them was never going to fix it." [^3]

Shai Morag, on the company's ambition: "The market has reached a breaking point, and I had the chance to bring together the people who understand identity, security, and AI best." [^1]
