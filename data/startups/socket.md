## About

Socket is a San Francisco-based software supply chain security platform that catches malicious code in open-source dependencies in real time, protecting enterprises as they adopt AI-accelerated software development [^1][^2][^3]. The $60M Series C announced May 20, 2026 was led by Thrive Capital at a $1B valuation, with participation from Andreessen Horowitz, Abstract Ventures, and Capital One Ventures [^1][^2][^3].
