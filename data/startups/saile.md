## About

Saile is a New York-based, physician-founded healthcare workforce platform that centralizes credential management for doctors and connects them to clinical shift opportunities across hospitals and other facilities [^1]. Co-founded by Dr. Marc Ayoub and Taylor Hakes, the platform stores and tracks medical credentials, automates credentialing and staffing workflows, and surfaces compliance alerts — pitched as the "infrastructure layer beneath staffing" for an underutilized physician workforce that struggles to move between health systems [^1]. The company announced a $2.2M pre-seed on May 13, 2026, led by Matchstick Ventures with participation from Headwater Ventures [^1].
