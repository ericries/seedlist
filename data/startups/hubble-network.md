## About

Hubble Network operates a low-Earth-orbit satellite constellation that extends Bluetooth Low Energy coverage to standard consumer and industrial devices, connecting Bluetooth chips directly to space without added hardware [^1]. The company has six satellites in orbit with a target of 60 by 2030, and reports over 100 million terrestrial gateways in 170 countries plus 500,000+ active devices on the network [^1]. Hubble is led by co-founder and CEO Alex Haro and is based in Seattle [^1][^2].
