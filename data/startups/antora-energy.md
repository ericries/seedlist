## About

Antora Energy develops thermal battery storage systems that store intermittent renewable electricity as high-temperature heat in solid carbon blocks for heavy industrial applications and data centers [^1][^2]. The company operates a battery gigafactory in San Jose, California, and is deploying a 5 GWh thermal storage project at a POET ethanol facility in South Dakota that, when completed, will be the largest thermal energy storage project of its kind in the world [^1][^2]. Antora was founded in 2017 by Andrew Ponec and has raised $770M to date [^1][^3].
