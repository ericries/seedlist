## About

Regatta Data is a New York-based database company founded in 2020 by Boaz Palgi (CEO), Eran Borovik, and Erez Webman, whose prior data-infrastructure companies include Topio (acquired by NetApp), XtremIO (acquired by EMC), Storwize (acquired by IBM), and ScaleIO (acquired by EMC, today Dell PowerFlex) [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-15, the company announced the general availability of RegattaDB — a distributed SQL database that unifies transactional (OLTP), analytical (OLAP), and vector-search workloads on a single engine — positioned as the data foundation for AI-agent systems [^1][^2]. RegattaDB is available as a managed cloud service (Regatta Cloud) or self-hosted [^2].
