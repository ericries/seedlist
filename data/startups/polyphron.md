## About

Polyphron is a New York–based biotech developing artificial human biology by combining physical tissue manufacturing with AI simulation, producing donor-diverse human tissues that serve as a verification substrate for testing biological interventions at scale [^1][^2]. The company's stated aim is to close the gap between AI-generated interventions and the slower biology required to validate them [^1]. Its founding team comes out of Google DeepMind, the Broad Institute and Harvard Medical School, Ginkgo Bioworks, and prior AI startup exits [^1].
