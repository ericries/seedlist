## About

Patronus AI is a San Francisco-based AI evaluation, simulation, and reliability testing company founded in 2023 by Anand Kannappan and Rebecca Qian, both formerly Meta AI researchers [^1][^2]. The company builds infrastructure for stress-testing AI agents, and at the time of its Series B unveiled "Digital World Models" — large-scale simulation environments that let developers train, evaluate, and improve AI agents across complex digital workflows before production deployment [^1][^2][^3]. Patronus AI reports revenue growth of more than 15x over the past year and counts virtually every frontier AI lab and many emerging AI startups among its customers [^1][^2].
