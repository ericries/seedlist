## About

LeapXpert is a New York-based enterprise communications and governance company that captures, archives, and now extracts intelligence from business conversations on consumer messaging channels including WhatsApp, WeChat, iMessage, SMS, Telegram, LINE, and Signal [^1][^2]. The company serves regulated industries — beginning with financial services and now expanding into government and the Forbes Global 2000 — where employees must communicate on the apps clients prefer without exposing the firm to recordkeeping and supervisory failures [^1][^2]. With its June 2026 $180M growth round, LeapXpert is investing in AI features that go beyond compliance archiving to actively surface deal context, client signals, and risk events from message logs [^1][^2]. The company is headquartered at 230 Park Avenue in New York [^2].
