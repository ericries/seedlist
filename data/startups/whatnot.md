## About

Whatnot is a Los Angeles-based live shopping marketplace where sellers auction collectibles, apparel, and other goods to buyers through video streams. Founded in 2019, the company holds roughly 60% of the US live commerce market by its own account and reported over 650,000 new signups per week around the time of its Series G. The August 2026 round nearly doubled its valuation from $11.5B (Series F, October 2025) to $20B.
