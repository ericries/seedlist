## About

Cargofy is a logistics AI platform that builds "digital workers" — AI agents — for freight and logistics companies across Europe, the United States, and the Caspian region [^1][^2]. The platform integrates with more than 70 logistics tools and automates carrier communication, document processing, and dispatch coordination across multiple languages [^1]. The funding will support international expansion, including new operational hubs in Germany, the Netherlands, France, Spain, and additional U.S. regions, and the extension of automation from customer-facing processes to back-office functions such as billing, compliance, and carrier coordination [^2][^3].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-06-18 | Series A | $6M primary + $5M secondary ($11M total) | u.ventures, Toloka.vc, Movens Capital | Des Traynor (Intercom co-founder) [^1][^2][^3][^4] |

The Series A consisted of $6 million in primary capital and $5 million in secondary transactions, totaling $11 million (approximately €9.5M) [^2][^3][^4]. Des Traynor (co-founder of Intercom) participated as an angel investor [^1][^4]. One of Cargofy's earliest backers fully exited through the secondary component, achieving a >50x return on their initial investment [^4]. Prior seed backers include JKR Investment Group and Flyer One Ventures [^4]. Cumulative funding to date: $13.5M [^4].
