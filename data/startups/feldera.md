## About

Feldera is a developer of an Incremental View Maintenance (IVM) engine powered by DBSP, which observes how data changes and keeps arbitrarily complex SQL views fresh at lakehouse scale — the company claims its platform reduces warehouse compute expenses by 95%+ while achieving real-time data freshness measured in seconds [^1][^2]. Feldera was co-founded by Lalith Suresh (CEO) and is targeting AI/data workloads where continuous freshness at reduced compute cost is a bottleneck [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-21, Feldera announced $21.5 million in combined Seed and Series A funding — the Series A led by Inovia Capital with participation from Costanoa Ventures and Battery Ventures, and the Seed led by Costanoa Ventures with participation from Databricks and Anyscale co-founder Ion Stoica [^1][^2][^3].
