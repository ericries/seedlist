## About

Reverion is a Munich-area climate-tech company (headquartered in Eresing, Bavaria) that builds reversible solid-oxide fuel-cell power plants capable of generating electricity from biogas, hydrogen, or natural gas, and reversing to produce hydrogen or methane from excess electricity [^1][^2]. The company reports up to ~74% electrical efficiency — roughly double a conventional generator — and the plants can run carbon-negative when fueled by biogas with integrated CO2 capture [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-28, Reverion announced a $175M Series B — one of the largest European climate-tech rounds of the quarter — to fund a new German megafactory with ~250 MW of annual manufacturing capacity and scale production tenfold [^1][^2][^3].
