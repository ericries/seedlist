## About

Fever is a live-entertainment discovery and ticketing platform that operates across more than 55 countries and reports having inspired over 300 million people last year to attend exhibitions, sports events, theatrical performances, museums, concerts, and festivals [^1][^3]. The company was co-founded by Ignacio Bachiller, Francisco Hein, and Alexandre Pérez, and produces its own "Fever Originals" experiences (including Candlelight concerts and Harry Potter: A Forbidden Forest Experience) alongside third-party ticketing partnerships [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-09-17, Fever announced a $250 million round led by EQT with participation from Point72 Private Investments and Baillie Gifford, along with existing investors; the company said revenue has more than tripled over the past three years and that it is operating with positive EBITDA [^1][^2].
