## About

Orthogonal is a San Francisco-based AI infrastructure company building a discovery, orchestration, and payments layer for autonomous AI agents on the open internet [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by CEO Christian Pickett and Bera Sogut, with a founding team drawn from Coinbase, Vercel, Google, and Amazon Robotics [^1][^2]. The platform integrates 35+ APIs (including Apollo, People Data Labs, Coresignal, Olostep, and Linkup) and provides a multi-chain transaction routing framework so that agents can find services, coordinate work, and execute payments without a human in the loop [^1][^2][^3].
