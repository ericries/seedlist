## About

Telepatia is a Colombia-based AI clinical platform serving hospitals across Latin America, offering real-time consultation transcription, clinical decision support, drug-interaction flagging, and "AI healthcare employees" such as AI doctor, nurse, and auditor agents [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2025 by Nicolás Abad, the platform launched publicly in July 2025 and reports deployment at 25+ hospital systems reaching more than 14 million patients across Brazil, Colombia, Mexico, Chile, and Argentina [^2][^3]. On 2026-06-17, Telepatia announced a $33M Series A led by Andreessen Horowitz Bio + Health (a16z Bio + Health), bringing total funding to $42M following a $9M seed round in October 2025 [^1][^2][^3][^4].
