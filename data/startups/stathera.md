## About

Stathera is a Montreal-based fabless semiconductor company that develops MEMS-based silicon oscillators engineered to replace legacy quartz-crystal timing components in data-center compute, connected systems, and AI infrastructure [^1]. The company's proprietary DualMode architecture is designed to deliver timing components that are up to 85% smaller than quartz alternatives, more power-efficient, and more resilient to thermal shock and vibration [^1].
