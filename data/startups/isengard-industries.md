## About

Isengard Industries is a San Francisco-based defense technology company building AI-enabled precision strike systems and counter-drone (counter-UAS) platforms designed for scalable, sovereign manufacturing by allied nations [^1][^2][^3]. The company frames its approach as "Weapons of Mass Production" — enabling partner nations to manufacture precision-strike and counter-drone systems domestically at a fraction of legacy prime contractor costs [^1][^3]. It was founded in 2026 by Francisco Serra-Martins (previously co-founder and CEO of Terminal Autonomy, which scaled to $60M revenue serving U.S. Army, NATO, and Ukrainian armed forces) and James Tennant (former Australian Army Officer and defense-tech private credit investor) [^1][^3]. Isengard is part of Y Combinator's Summer 2026 batch and employs approximately 30 people [^1].
