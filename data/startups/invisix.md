## About

Invisix is an Eindhoven-based ASML spin-out founded in 2025 by ASML alumni and PhD physicists Christina Porter (CEO) and Sietse van der Post (CTO) to commercialise a decade of soft x-ray metrology R&D incubated within ASML since 2015 [^1][^2]. The company develops soft x-ray measurement systems for advanced logic and memory semiconductor manufacturing, using short-wavelength broadband soft x-ray scatterometry to reconstruct the 3D structure of next-generation chip devices at high throughput [^1][^2].
