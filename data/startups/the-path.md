## About

The Path is a global AI therapy platform designed to support long-term mental wellness, co-founded by Tony Robbins along with Calm alumni Anson Whitmer (a founding Calm team member who led data science and AI) and Tyler Sheaffer (who founded Calm's engineering team) [^1][^2]. Previously known as Mental, the company post-trains open-source model architectures rather than relying on mainstream commercial LLMs, in order to retain control over safety features and conversational parameters; its specialized mental health model achieved a score of 95 on the Vera-MH safety benchmark, compared with an average of 65 for general-purpose consumer AI [^1][^2]. The platform draws on clinical frameworks including cognitive-behavioral therapy, acceptance and commitment therapy, dialectical behavior therapy, and solution-focused brief therapy [^1].
