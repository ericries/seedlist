## About

Synthetic is a fully autonomous AI bookkeeping service founded by Ian Crosby, who previously co-founded Bench Accounting, North America's largest small-business bookkeeping service [^1][^2]. The platform connects directly to a customer's banks, payroll systems, billing systems, and inboxes, asks clarifying questions to learn the business context, and returns a clean set of accrual-basis books ready for a tax preparer — without employing any human bookkeepers or accountants [^1][^2]. Pricing starts at $49 per month, roughly a quarter of the cost of a human-staffed service [^1][^2]. The $10M seed round announced on May 14, 2026 was led by Khosla Ventures with participation from Basis Set Ventures and operator-investors including Tobi Lütke (CEO of Shopify) and Kaz Nejatian (CEO of Opendoor) [^1][^2][^3].
