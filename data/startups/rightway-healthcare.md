## About

Rightway Healthcare is a healthcare benefits platform providing pharmacy benefit management (PBM) and clinical care navigation services to employers [^1][^2]. The company operates a transparent, "neutral PBM" model built by pharmacists with 100% pass-through revenue and a single transparent fee rather than traditional rebate-based profits [^2]. Rightway counts 45 Fortune 500 companies as clients, including Instacart and Tyson Foods, and reports a member Net Promoter Score of 70, 15% healthcare and pharmacy savings, and 40% average member engagement [^2][^4]. The company was founded in 2017 by Jordan Feldman [^1][^4].
