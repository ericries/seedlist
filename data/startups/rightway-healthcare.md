## About

Rightway Healthcare is a healthcare benefits platform providing pharmacy benefit management (PBM) and clinical care navigation services to employers [^1][^2]. The company operates a transparent, "neutral PBM" model built by pharmacists with 100% pass-through revenue and a single transparent fee rather than traditional rebate-based profits [^2]. Clients include Instacart and Tyson Foods [^2].
