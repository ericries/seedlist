## About

Prior Labs is a German frontier AI laboratory building Tabular Foundation Models (TFMs) — foundation models designed for structured data like spreadsheets and enterprise databases [^1][^2][^3]. Its flagship technology, TabPFN, enables AI to solve prediction tasks such as financial forecasting and supply chain risk analysis directly on tabular business data [^2][^4]. The company was founded in late 2024 by Frank Hutter, Noah Hollmann, and Sauraj Gambhir, and raised a €9 million pre-seed round in February 2025 led by Balderton Capital [^1][^3]. On 2026-07-17, SAP completed its acquisition of Prior Labs and committed more than €1 billion over four years to scale the company into a European frontier AI lab focused on structured data [^4][^5]. Prior Labs continues to operate as an independent entity within SAP, retaining its brand, leadership team, and research agenda [^4][^5].
