## About

Aflabox is an Italian agritech startup building a portable UV-imaging and AI platform that detects mycotoxins (including aflatoxins) and assesses the commercial quality of cereals along agricultural supply chains [^1][^2][^3]. The company combines optical hardware, deep-learning models, and geolocalized supply-chain data to make food-safety testing faster and more accessible for producers in Africa and Europe [^1][^2]. CEO and co-founder Luca Alinovi previously spent more than 25 years in food safety and international development, including as an FAO Representative and aflatoxin specialist; co-founder Fabrizio Cardillo brings three decades of international business development experience across African markets [^1][^2].

| 2026-08-03 | Seed | €1.35M | CDP Venture Capital | Eatable Adventures (FoodSeed), Farming Future, ToSeed & Partners [^1][^2][^3] |

CDP Venture Capital led the round through the FoodSeed program managed by Eatable Adventures, with additional backing from Farming Future (the Italian National Technology Transfer Pole for Agrifood Tech) and ToSeed & Partners [^1][^2]. Proceeds will fund device validation and certification, commercial expansion in Kenya, Nigeria, and Italy, buildout of a dedicated micro-factory, and continued AI platform development [^1][^2].
