## About

Oxford Quantum Circuits (OQC) is a UK-based quantum computing company building and deploying superconducting quantum computers, with a focus on quantum-AI data-centre platform deployments [^1][^2]. Headquartered in Reading, England, OQC was spun out of the University of Oxford in 2017 [^2].
