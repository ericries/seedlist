## About

Binti is an Oakland-based software company that builds case-management and decisioning tools for child-welfare agencies, helping caseworkers spend less time on paperwork and more time on family preservation, foster care, and adoption work [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2013 by Felicia Curcuru and Gabe Kopley and serves more than 550 child-welfare agencies across the United States, including state-level systems in Utah and Rhode Island and large county jurisdictions such as Los Angeles County [^1]. In early June 2026, SJF Ventures led a $6 million round, with proceeds earmarked for building AI-powered modules on top of Binti's existing platform [^1]. Previous backers include Kapor Capital, First Round Capital, Founders Fund, and Melinda French Gates' Pivotal Ventures [^1].
