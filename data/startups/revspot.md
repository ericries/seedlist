## About

Revspot is a Bengaluru, India-based AI-native platform that automates qualified pipeline generation for high-ticket business-to-consumer (B2C) companies [^1][^2]. The platform serves more than 50 customers across sectors including real estate, education, automotive, and wealth management, using AI to automate lead generation and qualification [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-29, Revspot announced a $4.8 million Series A round co-led by Inflexor Ventures and Info Edge Ventures, with participation from Pentathlon Ventures, Silicon Road Ventures, and existing investor Titan Capital, bringing total disclosed funding to $6.2 million [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2024 by Darshan Subash, Chirag Wadhera, and Varun Garg, and previously raised a $1.4 million seed round in late 2024 led by Info Edge Ventures and Titan Capital with participation from angel investor Kunal Shah [^1][^2].
