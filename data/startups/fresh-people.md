## About

Fresh People is a Valencia-based people and leadership technology company that is rebranding to Booster — an AI-powered leadership system designed to act as a strategic copilot for managers, helping them with decision-making, team dynamics and translating strategy into operational execution [^1][^2]. Booster has been adopted by more than 2,000 managers, with notable clients including Acciona, LaLiga and Kave Home [^1].
