## About

Arato Software Ltd. is an Israel-based startup founded in 2024 that builds tools for developers to test and evaluate their AI applications before deployment [^1][^2]. The platform simulates how users might interact with AI applications — using text, image, voice and business data — so organizations can spot potential failures, identify risks and highlight areas for improvement before systems reach production [^1][^2]. The company has 23 employees and is led by three co-founders — CEO Shahar Erez, CTO Hilik Paz, and VP of R&D Tal Salmona — who previously worked together at Mercury Interactive and VMware [^2]. Erez and Paz also co-founded workforce management platform Stoke, which Fiverr acquired in 2021 for $95 million [^1][^2].
