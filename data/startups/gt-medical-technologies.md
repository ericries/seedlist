## About

GT Medical Technologies is a Tempe, Arizona-based medical device company commercializing GammaTile, an FDA-cleared bioresorbable implant placed at the time of brain tumor removal surgery to deliver immediate radiation to the resection site [^1][^2]. At the American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO) 2026 meeting, GT Medical presented data from its ROADS randomized controlled trial showing that GammaTile reduced the risk of tumor recurrence and death at 12 months by 93% and 41%, respectively, compared with standard of care [^1]. Per Langoe serves as Chief Executive Officer and James Leech as Chief Financial & Strategy Officer [^1]. Proceeds from the round will support the BRIDGES randomized controlled trial in patients with newly diagnosed glioblastomas, as well as commercial and operational expansion [^1].
