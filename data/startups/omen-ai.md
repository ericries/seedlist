## About

Omen AI develops a compact spectrometer that monitors fluid health in real time inside data center liquid cooling systems, spotting bacterial growth and chemistry changes before they cause expensive downtime [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2024 by Zach Laberge, who had previously built his first business at age 14 integrating sensors with construction machinery [^1]. Omen says it works with about a dozen data center customers, including TensorWave, an AI compute cloud built on AMD chips [^1][^2]. The company has raised $40 million in total since its founding [^1].
