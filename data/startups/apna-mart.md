## About

Apna Mart is a Gurugram-based franchise-led omnichannel quick commerce platform focused on delivering groceries and FMCG products through a neighborhood store-led model in Tier II and Tier III cities across India [^1][^2]. Founded by Abhishek Singh and Chetan Garg, the company announced a ₹120 crore (~$12.7M) Series C round on 2026-07-30, co-led by existing investors Accel India and Fundamentum (each investing ₹55 crore) with participation from Peak XV Partners (₹10 crore) [^1][^2]. The round valued Apna Mart at approximately ₹1,470 crore, roughly double its previous valuation of ₹738 crore [^1]. The company reports operating revenue grew 2.5x to ₹500 crore in FY26 (compared with ₹185 crore in FY25) [^1][^2]. Proceeds will strengthen working capital and accelerate expansion into more markets across India [^1].
