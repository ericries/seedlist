## About

Orderful is a San Francisco-based AI-native electronic data interchange (EDI) infrastructure platform that connects suppliers, retailers, manufacturers, and logistics providers across global supply chains [^1][^2][^3]. Founded by Erik Kiser, Orderful says it has processed more than 6 billion EDI transactions across more than 50,000 unique trading partner requirements over the past nine years [^1]. In December 2025 the company launched Mosaic, its AI-native EDI platform, which the company says compresses partner onboarding from months to hours or days by eliminating manual data mapping [^1][^2]. The June 2026 Series C brings total funding to $85 million [^1][^2].
