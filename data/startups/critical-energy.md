## About

Critical Energy is a Los Angeles-based startup developing modular 2.5-5 MW geothermal turbines designed to be manufactured on assembly lines, shipped via standard trucking containers, and assembled rapidly at project sites [^1][^2]. The company aims to commercialize factory-built geothermal power plants targeting AI data center operators and hyperscale cloud providers seeking reliable baseload power [^2][^3]. Critical Energy was founded by Spencer Jackson, a former SpaceX engineer, who serves as chief executive officer [^1][^2].
