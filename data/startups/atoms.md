## About

Atoms is a robotics and industrial automation company founded by Uber co-founder Travis Kalanick, built upon the assets of City Storage Systems (CloudKitchens), which Kalanick founded in 2016 [^1][^2]. The company is focused on robotics deployed across food, mining, and transportation industries, and is expected to absorb Pronto AI, an autonomous driving startup [^2]. Atoms brought its eight-year stealth venture into the open in early 2026 [^2].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|------|-------|--------|------|--------------|
| 2026-07-22 | Late-stage private | $1.7B | Andreessen Horowitz | Bain Capital, Fifth Wall, Uber, Chemistry, K5 Global, SV Angel, Alpha Square Group, A*, Abstract [^1][^3][^4] |

Ben Horowitz will join Atoms' board following the investment [^1][^3][^4]. Debt financing partners on the round include Bank of America, Goldman Sachs, Wells Fargo, JPMorgan, and Barclays [^4].

## What Investors Say

Ben Horowitz, Andreessen Horowitz: "I think the most valuable thing someone could do with AI and robotics is to repeat the same thing Uber did for transportation, or that computers did for the digital world: to make everything and everyone more productive" [^1].

## What Founders Say

Travis Kalanick, Founder & CEO: "On many levels, this round is a bit of unfinished business. Fuel to complete the bits-to-atoms story arc we started at Uber, continued at CloudKitchens and will now finish at Atoms" [^1].

Kalanick, on the mission: "16 years ago, I started a journey to digitize the physical world. Understand, predict and control the physical world with software" [^1].
