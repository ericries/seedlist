## About

Moonlight AI is a Swiss diagnostics startup founded in 2022 and headquartered in Courroux, in the Swiss canton of Jura [^1][^2]. The company applies computer vision to routine hematology and cytology smear images — the same slides labs already prepare in their core workflows — to detect therapeutically relevant genomic biomarkers without traditional laboratory sequencing [^1][^3]. Initial disease focus areas include myelodysplastic syndrome (MDS), non-small cell lung cancer, and chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) [^3][^4]. The company was co-founded by Christian Ruiz (CEO), Nicole H. Romano (CTO), and Dr. Stefan Habringer (CMO), and validated its early platform in collaboration with University Hospital Basel [^1][^2].

The seed round (reported as €2.8M / approximately $3.3M USD) was co-led by Lotus One Investment (Singapore), VP Venture Partners (Switzerland), and MEDIN Fund (Tunisia), with participation from N&V Capital (Liechtenstein) and existing investor QAI Ventures (Switzerland) [^3][^4]. The capital is earmarked for team expansion, accelerating commercialization and regulatory pathways, and expanding the company's proprietary data library linking whole-slide imaging with genomic data [^3].
