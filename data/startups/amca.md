## About

Amca (Advanced Manufacturing Company of America) is an El Segundo, California-based aerospace and defense manufacturing platform founded in 2024 by CEO Jai Malik and COO Eli Giovanetti that combines engineering, qualification testing, technical data development, and certified manufacturing capacity into a single integrated platform for critical aerospace and defense components [^1][^2][^3]. The company operates six factories across California, Iowa, and New York spanning over 123,000 sq ft — including BC Systems, Electro-Mech Components, and Cal-Draulics (acquired 2025) — and supplies BAE Systems, Airbus, Textron, Bombardier, Honeywell, GE Aerospace, Lockheed Martin, Boeing, Northrop Grumman, Embraer, and Raytheon [^1][^2]. The $300M Series B announced May 20, 2026 closed at a $1B+ valuation 18 months after founding, led by Caffeinated Capital (Series A lead) with major participation from Lightspeed Venture Partners and continued support from Andreessen Horowitz, Lux Capital, Construct Capital, and House Capital; proceeds will fund factory expansion, acquisitions, and deployment of Amca's "Rapid" AI manufacturing platform [^1][^2][^3].
