## About

Qureight is a Cambridge, UK-based healthcare technology company that develops AI-powered medical imaging solutions to accelerate clinical trials for lung and heart diseases, offering an end-to-end platform for managing, analysing, and interpreting imaging data to generate precise trial endpoints [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-29, Qureight closed a $20 million Series B financing led by Molten Ventures, with participation from Hargreave Hale AIM VCT, XTX Ventures, Guinness Ventures, Meltwind, and Ascension [^1][^2][^3]. The company will use the capital to build an AI imaging laboratory housing its 3D chest imaging foundation model and expand into asthma, pulmonary hypertension, bronchiectasis, and drug-induced lung toxicity [^1][^2]. Dr Inga Deakin (Molten Ventures) and Anna Salim (Hargreave Hale) joined the board [^2].
