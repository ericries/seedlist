## About

A Security is an Israel-based cybersecurity startup that emerged from stealth on June 8, 2026 with $37 million in total funding to build an autonomous offensive security and remediation platform designed to counter weaponized, AI-driven attackers [^1][^2][^3]. The platform deploys offensive AI agents that continuously search for vulnerabilities and chain them into real-world exploit paths, while defensive agents drive remediation across enterprise environments [^1][^3]. The company was founded by CEO Yossi Torati, CPO Omer Gull, and CTO Yuval Itzchakov [^1][^2].
