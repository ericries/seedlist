## About

Chptr is a New York-based media infrastructure startup that distributes time-sensitive community stories — starting with end-of-life memorial content — across television, radio, and digital channels [^1][^2]. The company is live in 132 U.S. television markets and uses human-reviewed AI tools to transform photos and family stories into broadcast-ready segments, with direct integrations into iHeartMedia, Sinclair, and Hearst networks [^1][^2]. Chptr was founded in 2021 and is led by founder and CEO Rehan Choudhry [^1][^3].
