## About

Weave is a San Francisco-based engineering-intelligence platform that measures the business impact of AI-assisted software development, aiming to eliminate what the company calls "tokenmaxxing" — the practice of optimizing AI coding tools for token volume rather than meaningful engineering outcomes [^1][^2]. Founded by Adam Cohen (CEO) and Andrew Churchill (CTO), the platform normalizes human and AI code contributions across metrics like pull requests and deployments so leadership teams can assess ROI on AI tooling investments [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Weave announced a $13.5 million Series A led by Standard Capital [^1][^2].
