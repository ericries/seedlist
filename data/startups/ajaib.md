## About

Ajaib is a Jakarta-based multi-asset investment platform that offers Indonesian retail and institutional investors access to stocks, bonds, funds, crypto assets, stablecoins, commodities, and foreign exchange [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2019 by Stanford MBA classmates Anderson Sumarli and Yada Piyajomkwan and has grown to more than seven million users [^1][^2]. Ajaib also offers OTC stablecoin settlement services for corporate and institutional clients in Indonesia [^1].
