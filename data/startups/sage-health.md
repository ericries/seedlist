## About

Sage Health is a full-risk primary care and wellness provider for Medicare-eligible seniors, founded in 2022 by CEO John Haskell, operating advanced neighborhood health centers in primary-care shortage areas across Alabama, Arkansas, Maryland, and Mississippi where physicians manage no more than 500 patients each [^1][^2][^3]. The $50M growth capital announced March 27, 2026 was a debt term loan from Trinity Capital (Armentum Partners served as debt capital advisor) and brings Sage Health's total capital raised to $170M to date; the proceeds will fund geographic expansion into additional medically underserved markets [^1][^2][^3].
