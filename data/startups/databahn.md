## About

DataBahn is a Dallas-headquartered agentic data control plane for the enterprise, ingesting, normalizing, enriching, governing, and routing telemetry from more than 600 sources across any destination or model [^1][^2]. The company serves Fortune 500 enterprises and reports 400% year-over-year revenue growth, 180% net revenue retention, zero customer churn, and a 97% proof-of-concept win rate [^1][^2]. DataBahn was co-founded by CEO Nanda Santhana [^1].
