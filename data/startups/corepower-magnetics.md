## About

CorePower Magnetics is a Pittsburgh-based hardware company manufacturing advanced magnetic components for power electronics — developing nanocrystalline alloys and streamlined production processes to create smaller, lighter, and more efficient inductors and transformers used in AI infrastructure, data centers, grid modernization, and industrial electrification [^1]. On 2026-09-17, the company announced $10.6 million in equity financing co-led by Engine Ventures and Material Impact, with participation from Evergreen Climate Innovations, Carnegie Mellon Catalyst Fund, and Baruch Future Ventures [^1][^2].
