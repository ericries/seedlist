## About

Convey is a San Francisco-based enterprise AI platform that lets non-technical operators build and manage autonomous "digital teammates" to automate repetitive enterprise work [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2025 by CEO Rohan Chopra, and its customer list includes NBCUniversal, Samsara, TelevisaUnivision, Unity, Faire, and ChargePoint [^1][^2].
