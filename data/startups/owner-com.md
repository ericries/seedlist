## About

Owner is a San Francisco–based vertical SaaS company providing independent restaurants with an AI-native platform for websites, online ordering, mobile apps, CRM, POS, customer support, and AI phone ordering [^1][^2]. Founded in 2020 by Adam Guild and Dean Bloembergen, Owner has surpassed $100M in ARR, and independent restaurant owners are expected to drive over $1B in 2026 sales through the platform [^1][^2].
