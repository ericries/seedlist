## About

Corgi is a San Francisco-based AI-native, full-stack insurance carrier built for startups, founded in 2024 by Nico Laqua (CEO) and Emily Yuan (COO) [^1]. The company was part of Y Combinator's Spring 2024 batch [^2]. Corgi offers business insurance products including directors and officers liability, errors and omissions, cyber, commercial general liability, hired and non-owned auto, fiduciary liability, and AI liability coverage [^1]. Corgi received full regulatory approval to operate as an insurance carrier in July 2025, and reported annual recurring revenue surpassing $40M by January 2026 [^1]. Notable customers include Deel and Artisan [^2].
