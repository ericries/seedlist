## About

syte is a Münster-based proptech company operating an AI-powered land and real estate analytics platform that maps more than 62 million parcels across Germany [^1] [^2]. The platform serves over 200 customers and helps developers, brokers, and municipalities identify site potential and automate pre-construction planning; the company reports doubling annual recurring revenue year-on-year [^2].
