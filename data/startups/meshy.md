## About

Meshy builds foundation models for AI-powered 3D content generation, enabling users to create usable 3D assets from text descriptions or images in roughly one minute [^1][^2]. The company, founded by MIT PhD Ethan Hu, reports 12 million registered users and over 100 million models created as of July 2026, with 12x year-over-year annual recurring revenue growth [^1]. Meshy targets creators, developers, and studios in gaming, industrial design, and immersive media [^1][^2].

The Series B valued Meshy at $1.5 billion (approximately 10 billion yuan) and is described in the announcement as the largest funding round to date for a company built specifically for AI 3D generation [^1]. Existing investors Granite Asia, Sequoia China, BAI Capital, and Source Code Capital all participated in the oversubscribed round [^2].
