## About

Sent is a New York-based messaging infrastructure startup founded in 2024 by Daniel Vataj (CEO) and Betim Drenica (CTO) [^1][^4]. The company provides a unified API that lets businesses reach any user over SMS, WhatsApp, or RCS through a single integration, automatically routing each message to the channel most likely to deliver [^1][^2]. Sent operates over 75 direct carrier relationships reaching more than one billion unique phone numbers and is designated as a licensed U.S. telecom carrier [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Sent announced a $12 million Series A led by Companyon Ventures, with participation from Bessemer Venture Partners, Urban Innovation Fund (UIF), and CP Overture [^1][^2][^3]. The round follows a $3.55 million Seed in May 2025 led by Companyon with the same syndicate participating [^1][^5].
