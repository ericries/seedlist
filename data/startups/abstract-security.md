## About

Abstract Security is a San Francisco-based cybersecurity company building a composable, streaming-first security operations platform, positioned as an alternative to traditional monolithic SIEM architectures [^1][^2]. Founded in 2023, the company reports 380% ARR growth, 264% net revenue retention, and a 3x expansion in customer base over the past year, alongside 40 strategic hires [^1]. Founders and early leaders include veterans from ArcSight, Bank of America, Mandiant, and Palo Alto Networks [^1].
