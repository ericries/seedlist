## About

Alva Industries is a Trondheim, Norway-based deep-tech company developing ultra-compact, high-performance electric motors using its patented FiberPrinting manufacturing process [^1][^2]. The company serves customers across robotics, defense, aerospace, medical technology, industrial automation, and autonomous systems [^1][^2]. Alva is led by CEO Oliver Skisland [^1][^2].
