## About

NewCore is a workforce identity security company building a security-first identity platform designed to govern and secure both human employees and autonomous AI agents within a single architecture [^1][^2]. The company emerged from stealth on June 15, 2026 with $66M in total funding at a $300M valuation [^2][^3].

NewCore was founded in 2025 by CEO Zohar Alon (previously sold Dome9 to Check Point Software), CTO Amihai Neiderman, and CCO Erez Yarkoni [^1][^3]. The company has 50+ employees split between Tel Aviv and San Francisco and integrates with AI coding agents including Anthropic's Claude Code, OpenAI's Codex, and Cursor [^2].
