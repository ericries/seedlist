## About

Harmoney is a Ghent, Belgium-based software platform for counterparty risk management, founded in 2016, serving 70+ financial institutions across 7 countries (including Belfius, Baloise, and Ayvens) and monitoring millions of counterparties daily [^1][^2][^3]. The €10M strategic minority growth investment announced May 22, 2026 came from Smile Sail (an evergreen private equity fund focused on European software and AI leaders with ~€1B AUM); proceeds will scale international go-to-market, deepen client relationships, and accelerate AI-driven product innovation in counterparty risk management [^1][^2][^3].
