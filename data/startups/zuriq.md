## About

ZuriQ is a Zürich-based quantum computing startup building utility-scale quantum computers using a native two-dimensional trapped-ion architecture. The company was spun out of ETH Zürich in 2024 by Dr. Pavel Hrmo, Dr. Tobias Sägesser, and Dr. Shreyans Jain from Professor Jonathan Home's lab. The 2D architecture aims to overcome the scaling barrier that limits conventional 1D trapped-ion designs used by IonQ and Quantinuum [^1][^2].
