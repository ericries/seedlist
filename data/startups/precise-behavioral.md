## About

Precise Behavioral is a physician-led behavioral health services and technology company that delivers virtual and in-person psychiatric and behavioral care in partnership with health systems and manages the full revenue cycle for those services [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by Dr. Nitin Nanda, the company reports reaching profitability in under three years, delivering more than 100,000 patient encounters, and licensing its platform to several large health systems [^1][^3]. On 2026-07-29, Precise Behavioral announced $14.2 million in venture funding led by A1 Health Ventures, with participation from Ziegler Link-Age Fund, Converge Capital Partners, and Granite Financial Holdings (an affiliate of Blue Cross of Idaho); proceeds will scale operations, digital SaaS capabilities, and the company's AI roadmap [^1][^2][^3].
