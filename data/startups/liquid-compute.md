## About

Liquid Compute is developing a CFTC-regulated exchange for computational resources, providing verification, clearing, settlement, and price discovery services for neoclouds, AI companies, and traders [^2][^3]. Chemistry describes the product as "the regulated exchange for compute" [^4]. On 2026-09-15, Liquid Compute announced a $15M Seed round co-led by FirstMark and Chemistry with participation from K8 Capital, Night Capital, TrueBridge, Brainchild Holdings, UFO Holdings, and Dmitry Balyasny [^1][^5]. Both co-leads list Liquid Compute among their current portfolio companies [^3][^4]. FirstMark's investment was led by partner Adam Nelson [^5].
