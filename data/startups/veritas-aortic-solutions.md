## About

Veritas Aortic Solutions is a Costa Mesa, California medical device company developing the Veritas Transcatheter Valved Aortic Root Conduit (TVARC) system, a catheter-based alternative to open-heart surgery for treating root and ascending aortic diseases, including dissections and aneurysms [^1]. The company is a spinout of inQB8 Medical Technologies and is co-founded by Arshad Quadri, MD (Executive Chairman and Chief Medical Officer) and J. Brent Ratz (CEO) [^1].
