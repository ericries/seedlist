## About

Tomo is a personal AI service accessible via text message that provides coaching, accountability, and reminders to help users work toward long-term goals [^1][^2][^3]. Co-founders Justin Quan (CEO) and Raymond Chen (CTO) emerged from stealth in June 2026, reporting more than 10,000 paying subscribers acquired via word-of-mouth and a recently launched companion app [^1][^3]. The product is positioned as a "personal champion" — a phone number users text that learns what they want to become, remembers what matters, and helps them make small daily decisions aligned to long-term aspirations [^1][^3].
