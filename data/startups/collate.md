## About

Collate is a San Francisco-based AI platform that automates documentation for life sciences companies, generating and maintaining the regulatory filings, clinical trial paperwork, and product-development records that diagnostics, medical device, and drug development companies must produce from concept through market launch [^1][^2][^3]. The company was co-founded by Surbhi Sarna (former Y Combinator partner and founder of nVision Medical, acquired by Boston Scientific for $275M in 2018), Nate Smith (co-founder of recruiting platform Lever and former YC visiting partner), and Jigish Patel [^2][^4]. Collate emerged from stealth in January 2025 with a $30M seed round and, roughly 17 months later, raised a $95M Series B led by Redpoint Ventures at a valuation approaching $1B, bringing total funding to $125M [^1][^2]. The company reports 50+ customers including large pharmaceutical and medical device companies [^3].
