## About

Pronto is a Bengaluru-based on-demand home services platform founded in 2025 by Anjali Sardana that connects urban households with trained, background-verified workers for tasks such as cleaning, laundry, utensil washing, meal preparation, gardening, and car washing [^1][^2]. The company expanded its network of service workers to 6,500 (up from 1,440 in January 2026) and handles approximately 24,000–26,000 orders daily, claiming roughly 20% market share in instant home services [^1][^2]. The $20M Series B extension announced May 6, 2026 was led by solo investor Lachy Groom with participation from Glade Brook Capital, doubling Pronto's valuation to $200M post-money from $100M two months earlier [^1][^2][^3]. The earlier Series B base round of $25M was led by Epiq Capital in early March 2026 [^1].
