## About

Clear Robotics is a Hong Kong-based maritime technology company developing integrated, all-electric unmanned vessel technology and autonomous retrofit solutions [^1]. The company operates a fleet of 26 all-electric, AI-optimized unmanned surface vessels (USVs) and is expanding into Singapore, Malaysia, and the Philippines [^1] [^2]. It won the Pier71 Smart Port Challenge in 2024 and the Captain's Table in 2022 [^2].
