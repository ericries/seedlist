## About

Applied Compute is a San Francisco-based AI infrastructure startup building what it calls "Specific Intelligence" — a category of enterprise AI that enables organizations to build proprietary in-house agent workforces powered by their own domain expertise and data [^1][^2]. The company was founded by former OpenAI researchers who worked on key advances in reinforcement learning and agentic systems [^1].
