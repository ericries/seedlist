## About

SOMAREALITY is a Vienna-based DeepTech company that develops algorithms which draw on eye-tracking data to infer cognitive states such as cognitive load, attention, perception, fatigue and overall performance in real time [^1][^2]. Founded in Vienna in 2019, the company applies its technology to aviation, healthcare, and sports [^1][^3]. SOMAREALITY has generated over €2 million in B2B revenue since 2024 and more than doubled its 2025 revenue compared to 2024 [^3].
