## About

Sensie is a Ghent-based agtech startup (operating company Agrimentis B.V.) bringing real-time plant intelligence to professional greenhouse growers [^1]. The company offers affordable wireless plant sensors that combine climate, irrigation, and substrate data with direct physiological feedback from the crop itself, giving growers a live view of plant condition rather than relying solely on environmental proxies [^1].

Sensie's Sensie Omni product was named as a nominee for the GreenTech Amsterdam 2026 Innovation Awards [^2].
