## About

Status AI is a gamified social media platform where users create personas and enter user-generated worlds — users can "become a celebrity with millions of followers, step inside their favorite show or book, run for president, or go viral" [^1]. The company reports 13M generated worlds and 5M character profiles, with early adoption concentrated among young women [^1]. The $17M combined seed and Series A announced May 19, 2026 was led by General Catalyst with participation from Y Combinator, LightShed Partners, and Abstract [^1].
