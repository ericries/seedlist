## About

Volund Manufacturing is a Huntington Beach, California-based defense propulsion startup founded in 2025 by Eric Hostetler (CEO), Steven Stanley (CTO), Ken Spaulding (Chief Manufacturing Officer), and James Sonntag (COO) [^1][^2][^3]. The company designs and manufactures low-cost gas turbine engines for attritable defense systems such as cruise missiles and unmanned aerial systems, using a vertically integrated factory model that pairs proprietary digital manufacturing systems with in-house CAD-to-floor automation [^1][^3]. The team includes veterans from Anduril, Apple, Aerojet Rocketdyne, Blue Origin, Yeti, and Oakley [^1][^3]. Volund is working with integrators including Anduril, Raytheon, and Lockheed Martin, and aims to produce hundreds of engines per year by end-2027 and thousands per year by 2028 [^3].
