## About

iGii is a Stirling, Scotland-based deep-tech advanced materials company commercialising Gii, a proprietary three-dimensional porous carbon nanomaterial grown at room temperature and scalable to industrial volumes [^1][^2]. The material is targeted at applications including multi-analyte sensing, microheaters, and flexible printed battery electrodes [^1][^2].

On September 16, 2026, iGii announced a £22.7 million (approximately €26.49 million) Series B package consisting of £11.7 million in equity investment led by the Scottish National Investment Bank with participation from PXN Ventures and Archangels, and £11 million in support from Scottish Enterprise [^1][^2]. Proceeds are earmarked for deepening manufacturer engagements, advancing technology development, progressing customer relationships toward long-term supply agreements, and scaling the team to approximately 70 employees [^2].
