## About

Berlin-based Almetra (formerly Deltia) builds an intelligence layer for factories that combines video, machine data, IT systems, and operator knowledge into a single source of operational intelligence [^1][^2]. AI-powered cameras installed above production lines process video locally and convert it into structured production data such as cycle times, output rates, and equipment utilization, without requiring IT integration [^1]. Almetra counts Bosch, Siemens Energy, and ABB among its customers, with ABB reporting a 15% improvement in productivity [^1][^2]. The company has been accepted into Google DeepMind's Robotics Accelerator and the Physical AI Fellowship run by AWS, NVIDIA, and MassRobotics [^1].
