## About

Petra Labs is an answer engine optimization (AEO) platform for enterprise brands, founded in 2025 by Sami Akkawi, Rishi Penmetcha, and Ramie Khoury [^1][^2]. The company helps brands measure and improve how frequently they appear in answers generated by AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, pairing a proprietary measurement platform with an embedded team of AEO experts running strategy and execution [^1][^2]. Petra builds custom last-mile attribution models that tie AI-referred visibility to website traffic, customer acquisition, and revenue [^1][^2].
