## About

Ditto Care is a Rotterdam-based healthtech startup founded in 2025 by Tobias Polak, Bart Voorn, and Merlijn van Breugel that uses AI to record doctor consultations and translate them into plain language for patients [^1][^2]. The app has reached 100,000 downloads and plans expansion into German-speaking markets (DACH region) in 2026 [^1]. The €7.6M Seed round announced May 12, 2026 was led by Heal Capital (the investment vehicle of the German private health insurance industry) [^1][^2].
