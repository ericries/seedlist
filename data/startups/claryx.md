## About

Claryx is a New York-based genomic intelligence company that helps hospitals detect and stop infectious disease outbreaks before they spread [^1][^2][^3]. The company's CloneLink platform sequences the genomes of pathogens from confirmed patient infections and analyzes whether apparently separate cases are linked, and also creates a digital twin of the hospital environment using samples from air, water and surfaces to identify potential contamination sources [^1][^2]. Co-founder Dirk Hackenberger developed chemistry during his PhD that reduced the most expensive sequencing step by more than twentyfold, allowing hospitals to look for outbreaks before anyone knows there's one to find [^1]. In a two-year study at UPMC Presbyterian, the platform identified 172 otherwise undetected outbreaks involving 476 patients, with 96% of traced outbreaks not spreading further [^1].
