## About

Veridion is a Bucharest-based business intelligence startup building an AI-powered, continuously updated digital replica of the global business landscape — tracking 642 million (per press) to ~693 million companies (per company website) across 249+ countries with 461 attributes per company, and providing real-time commercial risk assessment and market intelligence [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-16, the company announced a €17.5M ($20M) Series A led by Hoxton Ventures with participation from Underline Ventures, OTB Ventures, Gapminder, Day One Capital, and Launchub [^1]. Previous rounds include €5.19M in a Series A extension (February 2023) and €1.29M in seed funding (2020) [^1].
