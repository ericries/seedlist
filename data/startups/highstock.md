## About

Highstock operates an AI-powered B2B marketplace that connects consumer brands with excess inventory to a vetted network of global wholesale buyers, handling matching, logistics, compliance, and payments [^1]. The platform lists more than $1 billion in inventory and works with over 100 major brands, and has begun expanding beyond beauty and personal care into apparel and broader consumer goods [^1][^2].
