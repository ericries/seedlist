## About

Pearl Health is a New York-based healthcare technology company that helps primary care providers manage risk and deliver value-based care to Medicare patients [^1][^2]. The company's platform combines AI-driven clinical intelligence with population health tools to support providers participating in Medicare risk-based programs, and reports it manages approximately $3.6 billion in annualized medical spend for 250,000+ Medicare beneficiaries [^1]. Pearl was co-founded in 2020 by Michael Kopko, Ankit Patel, Jeff De Flavio, and Kevin Ryan [^2].

On 2026-07-08, Pearl Health announced a $110 million capital raise consisting of $50 million in Series C equity and a $60 million debt facility [^1][^2][^3]. The equity round was led by Andreessen Horowitz with participation from Viking Global Investors, AlleyCorp, and Ulysses Capital; Trinity Capital provided the debt facility [^1][^2][^3]. The company said it will use the capital to expand its AI platform, accelerate enterprise health system and payer partnerships, expand into Medicare Advantage, and launch new risk offerings [^1][^3].
