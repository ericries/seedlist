## About

Konvy is a Bangkok-based omnichannel beauty and personal care distributor and retailer founded in 2011 by Qinggui Huang and Leon Huang that operates across e-commerce, marketplaces, social commerce, and offline retail in Southeast Asia [^1]. The company has built a leadership position in Thailand's beauty market and is now scaling regionally [^1]. The USD 22 million Series B announced May 12, 2026 was led by Cool Japan Fund with participation from existing investor Insignia Ventures Partners; capital will fund Southeast Asia expansion and deepening of Japanese-brand partnerships [^1].
