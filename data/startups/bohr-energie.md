## About

Bohr Energie is a Toulouse-based energy-tech scaleup building an AI-powered aggregation and market-access platform for independent renewable-energy producers [^1][^2]. The software uses AI to forecast production and route generated power to the best available price on European wholesale markets, giving small and mid-sized producers access to opportunities that were previously the preserve of large utilities [^1]. The company currently manages more than 170 renewable assets and expects to reach roughly one gigawatt under management by year-end 2026 [^1]. Bohr Energie was co-founded by Julien Haure (CEO), Julien Chollet, Luis Urday, and Jean-Pierre Mader [^3].
