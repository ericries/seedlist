## About

BatX Energies is a Gurugram-based cleantech startup that recycles end-of-life batteries and manufacturing scrap to recover critical minerals — including lithium, cobalt, nickel, and graphite — and refines them for reuse in lithium-ion battery manufacturing [^1][^2][^3]. Co-founded by Utkarsh Singh and Vikrant Singh, the company is building capacity to produce Cathode Active Materials (CAM) domestically as part of India's effort to establish an independent battery-materials supply chain [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds from the Series A will be used to expand recycling and refining capacity, strengthen R&D, and accelerate the development of a domestic supply chain for battery materials [^1][^2][^3].
