## About

Candid Health is a San Francisco-based healthcare technology company that automates revenue cycle management — the process by which providers submit medical claims, receive reimbursement, and reconcile payments [^1][^2]. Founded in 2019 by former Palantir engineers Nick Perry and Doug Proctor, the company combines clinical, provider, and billing data into a unified model and uses AI to automate tasks traditionally handled by manual, often offshore labor [^1][^2]. Candid serves more than 200 healthcare organizations and reports 190% year-over-year contracted revenue growth [^2][^3].
