## About

Cosmico is a Milan-based Talent-as-a-Service platform connecting companies with vetted digital professionals across Europe [^1]. In May 2026, alongside its €12M Series B announcement, Cosmico finalised the acquisition of 100% of Flatmates — a creator agency co-founded by Michele Pagani and Marcello Ascani that applies the Talent-as-a-Service model to the creator economy across Italy, Spain and the USA. Cosmico had bought a majority stake in 2024 and accelerated the remaining purchase two years ahead of plan [^1][^2]. The group's ecosystem is now organised around four verticals: Cosmico (Talent-as-a-Service for digital professionals), Flatmates (Talent-as-a-Service for the creator economy), Creative Harbour (Community-as-a-Service for employee engagement), and Play New (Talent Process Design for building teams of "SuperAgents" — AI-augmented professionals) [^1]. The company reported closing 2025 with €24M in revenue, up 109% year-over-year [^1].
