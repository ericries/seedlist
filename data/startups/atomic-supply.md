## About

Atomic is a Boston-based supply chain planning platform that uses agentic AI to help physical-goods companies automate inventory, purchasing, and allocation decisions across their operations [^1] [^3]. The founding team includes veterans from Tesla's planning organization who first developed an early version of the system during Tesla's 2018 Model 3 production ramp [^1] [^2]. Customers include DoorDash (DashMart), HelloFresh (Good Chop), Starface World, OOFOS, LMNT, and Vincero [^1] [^3].
