## About

Contraline is a Charlottesville, Virginia-based clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing novel male contraceptives [^1][^2]. The company is advancing both hormonal and non-hormonal approaches to expand contraceptive options for men and couples globally [^1]. Its lead program, NES/T Gel, is an investigational daily hormonal reversible male contraceptive, while ADAM is an investigational long-lasting non-hormonal reversible contraceptive currently in clinical development [^1][^2].

The NES/T program recently completed a global Phase 2b trial enrolling 462 couples, with Phase 3 expected to begin in 2027 [^1].
