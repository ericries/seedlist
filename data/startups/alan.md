## About

Alan is a Paris-based health insurance and prevention platform founded in 2016 by Jean-Charles Samuelian-Werve [^1][^2]. The company positions itself as "the first company to bring insurance, prevention and care together in a single experience" and operates across France, Spain, Belgium, and Canada [^1]. As of Q1 2026, Alan reported serving more than 1.1 million members and reaching over €800 million in Annual Recurring Revenue, representing 53% year-on-year growth, and is profitable in France [^1][^2]. On June 25, 2026, Alan announced a €480 million Series G financing round valuing the company at €5.5 billion [^1][^2][^3].
