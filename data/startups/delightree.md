## About

Delightree is a Sunnyvale-based software company building an "agentic operating system" for franchise and multi-unit brands. The platform handles training, compliance, daily operations, and performance execution across locations in restaurants, fitness, wellness, retail, healthcare, and home services [^1][^2]. Since launching its AI-powered operating system just over two years before this round, Delightree reports that revenue has grown nearly 20x, with the platform now used across more than 6,000 locations nationwide [^1].
