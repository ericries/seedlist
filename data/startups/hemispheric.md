## About

Hemispheric is an Israeli NeuroAI company building Descartes, a six-billion-parameter foundation model trained to interpret non-invasive electrical brain activity for clinical diagnostics [^1][^2]. The company emerged from stealth on 2026-07-15 after roughly six years of development, disclosing $52 million in early-stage funding and 112 employees [^1][^2]. Descartes was trained on more than 250,000 hours of multimodal EEG and behavioral recordings from over 100,000 participants, and is being applied to PTSD, depression, anxiety, mild brain injury, schizophrenia and Alzheimer's disease via a wearable dry-electrode EEG headset paired with tablet-based tasks [^1][^2][^3]. Co-founder Gidi Littwin previously co-founded RealFace, which was acquired by Apple and integrated into FaceID [^1][^3].
