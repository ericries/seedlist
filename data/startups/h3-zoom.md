## About

H3 Zoom is a Singapore-based deep-tech startup that builds AI-powered inspection and asset-intelligence software for buildings, infrastructure, and critical assets [^1][^2]. The platform combines computer vision, proprietary vision-language models, robotics-enabled data capture, and enterprise inspection workflows to convert raw inspection data into structured intelligence for the built environment lifecycle [^1][^2]. The company is led by founder and CEO Shaun Koo [^1][^2].
