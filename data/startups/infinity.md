## About

Infinity is an AI infrastructure startup building a CUDA-alternative kernel layer that lets AI models run efficiently on any accelerator chip, not just NVIDIA GPUs [^1]. The company positions its software as a portability layer for inference workloads, aiming to reduce lock-in and lower per-token costs as demand for inference scales [^1]. The seed round was led by Touring Capital with participation from Principal VC and individual researchers from OpenAI and Anthropic [^1].
