## About

Physical Superintelligence (PSI) is an AI physics lab building "Emmy," a platform of virtual physicists that runs large-scale simulations, tests scientific hypotheses, and designs physical systems [^1]. The company's initial commercial focus is optimizing data center infrastructure by modeling power, cooling, networking, and computing interactions; it also serves as the founding technical partner for the Fermi Explorer Mission, described as the first AI-planned interstellar mission to Alpha Centauri [^1]. CEO Matt Pines has stated PSI "aims to industrialize the discovery of new physics" [^1].
