## About

Enveda is a Boulder, Colorado-based biotech company using artificial intelligence to discover therapeutic small molecules derived from natural sources — plants, fungi, and microbes — with clinical programs in severe skin conditions and post-GLP-1 weight-loss maintenance [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2019 by Viswa Colluru, previously at Recursion Pharmaceuticals, and operates from Boulder with an additional office in Hyderabad, India [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-23, Enveda announced a $311 million Series E led by Catalio Capital Management with participation from ICONIQ, Lightspeed, Durable Capital Partners, Surveyor Capital (a Citadel company), T. Rowe Price Investment Management, Digitalis Ventures, Alderline Group, and an unnamed sovereign wealth fund as new investors, plus existing investors Baillie Gifford, Premji Invest, FPV Ventures, True Ventures, Kinnevik, Dimension, Lifeforce Capital, and Lux Capital [^4]. The round doubled the company's valuation to $2 billion versus its 2025 Series D round [^1][^4], bringing total capital raised to more than $845 million since inception [^4].
