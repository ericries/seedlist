## About

Enveda is a Boulder, Colorado-based biotech company using artificial intelligence to discover therapeutic small molecules derived from natural sources — plants, fungi, and microbes — with clinical programs in severe skin conditions and post-GLP-1 weight-loss maintenance [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2019 by Viswa Colluru, previously at Recursion Pharmaceuticals, and operates from Boulder with an additional office in Hyderabad, India [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-23, Enveda announced a $311 million Series E led by Catalio Capital Management with participation from Iconiq, doubling the company's valuation to $2 billion versus its 2025 Series D round [^1][^3].
