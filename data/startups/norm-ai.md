## About

Norm Ai is a New York-based legal AI company that embeds legal reasoning into AI agents to handle high-stakes regulated work for enterprise clients [^1][^2]. The company operates Norm Law, an AI-native law firm in which proprietary AI agents are supervised by human attorneys and billed on outcome-based pricing rather than hourly rates [^1][^2]. Norm says its platform serves institutional clients representing more than $30 trillion in assets under management [^2]. The company was founded in mid-2023 by John Nay [^1][^3].
