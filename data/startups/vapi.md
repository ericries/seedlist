## About

Vapi is a San Francisco-based developer platform providing API-native infrastructure for enterprise voice AI — letting companies build, deploy, and manage voice agents for customer support, lead qualification, scheduling, and outbound sales [^1][^2]. Co-founders Jordan Dearsley and Nikhil Gupta, who met at the University of Waterloo and previously built a Y Combinator-backed calendar product, launched Vapi publicly on Product Hunt in March 2024 [^1]. On May 12, 2026, Vapi announced a $50M Series B led by Peak XV with participation from M12 (Microsoft's Venture Fund), Kleiner Perkins, and Bessemer Venture Partners, bringing total funding to $72M at a ~$500M post-money valuation; the company said voice agents on its platform have handled more than one billion calls, with over one million developers and 2.7 million unique agents built on Vapi to date [^1][^2]. Named enterprise customers include Amazon Ring, Kavak, Instawork, New York Life, UnityAI, Cherry, and Intuit [^1].
