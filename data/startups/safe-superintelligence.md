## About

Safe Superintelligence (SSI) is an AI research company co-founded in 2024 by former OpenAI Chief Scientist Ilya Sutskever, together with Daniel Gross and Daniel Levy [^1][^2]. The company has publicly said it does not plan to commercialize AI products in the near term and is instead focused on research aimed at building a safe superintelligence [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-27 Nvidia announced a $5 billion investment in SSI, coupled with access to Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin compute platform, in what has been described as one of Nvidia's largest AI investments to date [^1][^2][^3]. The financing brings SSI's total raised to approximately $7 billion at a $32 billion valuation (valuation last reported February 2025) [^2][^3].
