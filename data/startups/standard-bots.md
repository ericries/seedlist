## About

Standard Bots is a Glen Cove, New York-based industrial robotics company building AI-powered robotic arms for US manufacturing [^1][^2]. The company aims to deliver 10% of new US industrial robot deployments in 2026 and is expanding its Glen Cove manufacturing facility to 70,000 square feet to scale domestic production [^1][^2].
