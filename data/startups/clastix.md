## About

Clastix is a Naples-based infrastructure software company building tools to operate Kubernetes at fleet scale [^1]. Its commercial offering, kMetal, packages the open-source projects Capsule (multi-tenancy) and Kamaji (hosted control planes) into a platform aimed at AI labs, telecom, cloud, and public-sector operators, with customers including Fastweb, NVIDIA, and Mistral [^1]. The company plans to keep Kamaji open source under Apache 2.0 and use the round to expand product development, customer success, and go-to-market [^1].
