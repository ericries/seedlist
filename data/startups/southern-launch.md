## About

Southern Launch is a South Australia-based rocket launch and re-entry services company that operates the Koonibba Test Range near Ceduna — the site of the world's first commercial spacecraft re-entry in February 2025 — and is developing the Whalers Way Orbital Launch Complex near Port Lincoln [^1][^2][^3]. The company closed a A$25M Series A on July 1, 2026, led by national security investment firm Brindabella and Company, with a A$10M direct equity investment from the Australian federal government's National Reconstruction Fund alongside former Macquarie Group CEO Nicholas Moore, former CFO Alex Harvey, and Coupland Asset Management [^1][^2][^3]. Southern Launch currently employs 35 people and plans to grow to 185 full-time jobs as it scales its Southern Hemisphere launch infrastructure [^1][^2].
