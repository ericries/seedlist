## About

Nivasa Finance is an Indian housing-credit startup building distribution and fulfilment infrastructure for affordable home loans (₹5–35 lakh) targeting underserved borrowers in non-metro India [^1][^2]. The company supports borrowers across the full loan journey — initial assessment, documentation readiness, lender matching, and disbursal — using a hybrid model that combines technology-led workflows with on-ground field execution, and works with more than ten lending partners including banks, SFBs, HFCs and NBFCs [^1]. Co-founder Samit Shetty previously founded and scaled Chaitanya India Fin Credit (later acquired by Navi Technologies) and served as the first CEO of Navi Finserve [^1]. On May 13, 2026, Nivasa announced ₹25 Cr in seed funding from Prime Venture Partners, Blume Ventures, Whiteboard Capital, and angel investors [^1][^2].
