## About

Aylight is a Zurich-based photonics startup building chip-scale multiwavelength lasers for AI data-centre optical interconnects and high-precision frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) sensing. Founded in 2025 by Bahareh Marzban (CEO) and Dmitry Kazakov (CTO) following research at ETH Zürich, Aylight's architecture generates multiple precisely spaced wavelengths from a single chip using a frequency-modulated comb, reducing the need for arrays of discrete lasers in optical communication systems. The design targets manufacturability in existing semiconductor photonics foundries [^1][^2][^3].
