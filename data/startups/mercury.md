## About

Mercury is a San Francisco-based digital banking platform serving 300,000+ business customers including a third of early-stage startups; the company has been profitable for the past four years and hit $650M annualized revenue in Q3 2026 [^1][^2][^3]. The $200M Series D announced May 20, 2026 at a $5.2B valuation (49% up from the $3.5B March 2025 Series C) was led by TCV with participation from existing backers Andreessen Horowitz, Coatue, CRV, Sapphire Ventures, Sequoia Capital, and Spark Capital [^1][^2][^3]. Mercury plans to pursue a federal bank charter [^1][^3].
