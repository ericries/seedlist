## About

Bundle is an Abu Dhabi-based Web3 rewards platform that lets brands combine incentive budgets into shared reward pools, using blockchain and stablecoins to distribute rewards across markets [^1][^2][^3]. Rather than offering discounts, the platform focuses on customer acquisition and retention through larger, networked rewards funded jointly by participating brands [^2][^3]. Bundle plans to launch initially across Singapore, Vietnam, and the Philippines with more than 50 founding brands [^2][^3].
