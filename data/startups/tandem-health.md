## About

Tandem Health is a Stockholm-based clinical AI company building what it calls an "AI-native operating system" for European healthcare, offering an AI medical scribe, clinical decision support, and coding assistant now used across 14 European markets [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 and has expanded through partnerships with NHS trusts in the UK, Capio and Doktor.se in the Nordics, Humanitas in Italy, and Recoletas in Spain, and became the first AI medical scribe in Europe certified as EU MDR Class IIa [^2]. On 2026-09-14, Tandem Health announced a $100M Series B led by the Scaleup Europe Fund, managed by EQT, with participation from Kinnevik, Northzone, Amino Collective, and Visionaries — bringing total funding to approximately $160M following a $50M Series A in 2025 [^1][^2][^3].
