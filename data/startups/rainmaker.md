## About

Rainmaker Technology Corporation develops drone-based cloud seeding technology intended to increase precipitation from existing cloud systems, targeting agriculture, hydropower, and drought-hit regions [^1]. The company is based in El Segundo, California and was founded by Augustus Doricko [^2]. Recent field work includes glaciogenic cloud seeding trials in Alaska's Kenai Peninsula and validation studies of "cavum" signatures from Oregon operations [^1].
