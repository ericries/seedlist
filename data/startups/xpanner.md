## About

Xpanner is a Santa Fe Springs, California-based robotics and physical AI company founded in 2020 that retrofits existing construction equipment with autonomous capabilities through its X1 Kit platform, offered as an Automation-as-a-Service (AaaS) subscription [^1]. The company's approach lets customers turn equipment they already own into automated assets through hardware and software updates rather than fleet replacement [^1]. On May 14, 2026, Xpanner announced an $18M Series B bridge round led by Korea Investment Partners (KIP) with KB Investment Co. (KBIC) participating, bringing total funding to $38M since inception [^1]. Revenue grew from $3M in 2023 to $7M in 2024 to $21M in 2025, with $8M in Q1 2026 revenue and $1M EBIT [^1].
