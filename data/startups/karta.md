## About

Karta is a Miami-based fintech offering premium US-issued Visa credit cards to internationally mobile consumers who lack US credit history [^1]. The product targets affluent global users — credit lines up to $200,000, zero foreign exchange fees, and an AI-powered 24/7 concierge service delivered via WhatsApp [^1][^2]. Karta has partnered with more than 80 private banks and wealth managers globally to source qualified clients [^2]. The company says it grew 10x in 2025, with revenue and payment volume up 4x quarter-over-quarter in Q1 2026 [^1][^2].
