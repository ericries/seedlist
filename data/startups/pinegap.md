## About

Pinegap is an AI-driven equity research platform headquartered in New York with a technology center in Bengaluru, founded in 2024 by Ankit Varmani and Deepak Sharma [^1][^2][^3]. The company builds custom AI agents that work directly with buy-side fund teams — hedge funds and mutual funds — automating research workflows tuned to each fund's investment style, private data, and output formats [^1][^2]. As of the Series A, Pinegap reported having deployed over 1,000 agents across 100+ institutional clients, generating upwards of 50,000 research reports per month [^1][^2]. Cumulative funding stands at $10.5M following the Series A [^1][^2].
