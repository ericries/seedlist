## About

Raghu Vamsi Aerospace Group (RVAG) is a Hyderabad-based precision aerospace manufacturing and deeptech company [^1][^2][^3]. The group operates more than 10 manufacturing facilities across India, the UK, and the US, and employs over 1,200 people [^1][^2][^3]. RVAG supplies precision components and assemblies to global aerospace OEMs including GE Aerospace, Pratt & Whitney, Honeywell, Safran, and Collins Aerospace, and has an order book exceeding ₹2,500 crore as of the July 2026 announcement [^2][^3]. The group's operating businesses span precision manufacturing, mission systems, deeptech, and autonomous technologies [^1][^2][^3].
