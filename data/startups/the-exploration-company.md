## About

The Exploration Company is a European space company developing Nyx, a reusable orbital capsule for cargo and crew transportation, and Storm, a high-thrust reusable rocket engine using full-flow staged combustion [^1][^2]. The company is headquartered in Munich, Germany, with additional operations in France, Luxembourg, Spain, and Italy, and is led by Co-Founder and CEO Hélène Huby [^2]. On 2026-09-08, The Exploration Company announced a $450 million Series C round — described by the company as "the largest-ever Series C by a European space company" — with capital allocated to bringing Nyx to the International Space Station (with a planned docking by November 2028) and accelerating development of Storm [^1][^2][^3]. The Series C brought total funding to approximately $680 million [^1]. The company said 10 Nyx missions are booked, representing more than $2 billion in contracts and commitments [^2].
