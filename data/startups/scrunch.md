## About

Scrunch (legal name Scrunch AI) is an AI customer experience platform that helps brands monitor and improve how they appear in AI-generated search results from large language models such as ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and Perplexity [^1][^2]. Founded in 2023 by Chris Andrew and Robert MacCloy and headquartered in Park City, Utah, the company emerged from stealth in late 2024 [^3][^4]. CEO Chris Andrew was previously the first employee and Chief Product Officer at Hearsay Systems (later acquired by Yext) [^4].

The company's Agent Experience Platform (AXP) delivers machine-readable content optimized for AI crawlers alongside human-facing websites, and provides analysis of brand visibility, citation gaps, and competitive positioning across AI answer engines [^1][^5]. Scrunch reported serving 500+ brands and agencies, including Lenovo, Skims, Headspace, and Penn State University [^5][^6].

On June 3, 2026, Sitecore announced an agreement to acquire Scrunch for approximately $225 million, citing the shift of buyer discovery from traditional search to AI-generated answers [^5][^6][^7].
