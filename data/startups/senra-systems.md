## About

Senra Systems is a Cypress, California-based software-driven wire harness manufacturer serving the U.S. aerospace and defense industrial base [^1][^2][^3]. Founded by CEO Jordan Black (a SpaceX veteran) and co-founder Benjamin Shanahan, the company combines a proprietary software platform (Amp) with automation and skilled technicians to standardize what has historically been a manual, paper-driven process across the industry [^1][^2]. Senra currently produces around 1,000 wire harnesses per month across two factories and has publicly stated a goal of reaching 10,000 per month by 2027 [^1]. Customers include aerospace and defense programs where wire harnesses serve as the electrical cabling backbone of rockets, aircraft, and vehicles [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-15 Senra announced a $65 million Series B co-led by Lowercarbon Capital and Interlagos, with participation from General Catalyst, Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, Founders Fund, Dylan Field, CIV, 8VC, The Friedkin Group, Jaws Estates Capital, Sozo Ventures, and Alumni Ventures [^2][^3]. The round brings total funding to over $112 million and will underwrite the company's third manufacturing facility [^2][^3]. In connection with the round Senra also announced Ken Venner as CTO [^2].
