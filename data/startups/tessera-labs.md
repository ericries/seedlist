## About

Tessera Labs is a San Jose-based vendor-agnostic, multi-agent AI platform for ERP modernization, founded in 2025 by CEO Kabir Nagrecha and COO Ming Chang [^1][^2]. The company's platform is pre-trained on thousands of organizational landscapes and aims to compress enterprise transformation timelines from years to weeks while reducing costs by more than 50% [^1][^2]. The $60M Series A announced May 6, 2026 was led by Andreessen Horowitz with participation from Foundation Capital, Myriad Venture Partners, and Osage University Partners; a16z partner Seema Amble joined the board [^2].
