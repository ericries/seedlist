## About

Catch is a Tel Aviv-based AI executive assistant startup that emerged from stealth on September 2, 2026 with a $5 million seed round [^1][^2]. The product is positioned as an always-on AI assistant for business leaders — automating scheduling, email, travel booking, and day-to-day administrative work across text, email, Slack, and phone, without users having to build workflows or write prompts [^1][^2]. Catch reports having scheduled over 12,000 meetings, delegated more than 20,000 tasks, and processed over 200,000 emails to date, and maintains SOC 2 compliance [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by Nir Sabato (CEO) and Yoav Ramon (CTO) [^1][^2].

## What Investors Say

Avi Eyal, Entrée Capital: "Catch solves the admin function completely for executives without requiring code or workflow setup, potentially redefining how modern leadership operates" [^2].

## What Founders Say

Nir Sabato, Co-Founder & CEO: "Executives don't want to build their own AI agents... they want it done right, in a way they can trust. Catch works alongside business leaders the way a great human assistant would, but 24/7, with no dip in attention and fully secure" [^1].

Nir Sabato, Co-Founder & CEO: "Executives want their AI assistant to execute admin decisions autonomously the way a great human assistant would, but 24/7 and fully secure" [^2].

Yoav Ramon, Co-Founder & CTO, on Catch's "splinter agents" architecture: "The 'splinter agents' architecture restricts individual agents to specific tasks with limited data access, improving quality and reducing cost and security risk" [^2].

The company's public landing page (catch.ai) confirms Catch is in "waitlist opens soon" pre-launch mode with the tagline "Put down the apps" [^3].
