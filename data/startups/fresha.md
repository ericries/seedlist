## About

Fresha is a London-based AI-powered booking and payments platform for the beauty and wellness industry (salons, spas), founded in 2015 and serving 140,000+ businesses with 35M+ appointments booked monthly [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports a $140M+ revenue run-rate, is profitable, and growing 60%+ annually [^1][^2]. The $80M growth investment announced May 21, 2026 came from KKR's Next Generation Technology Growth fund at a $1B unicorn valuation, bringing total funding since 2015 to $285M; proceeds will fund US, Europe, Africa, and Southeast Asia expansion plus AI feature investment across booking, marketing, accounting, and workforce management [^1][^2][^3].
