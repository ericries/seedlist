## About

Twenty is an Arlington, Virginia-based defense technology company building AI-enabled offensive cyber warfare systems for the United States military and its allies [^1][^2]. The company describes its product as an end-to-end platform that combines AI and automation with controlled deployment, evaluation, and mission alignment in order to industrialize offensive cyber capabilities [^1]. Twenty was founded in 2024 and is led by co-founder and CEO Joe Lin [^1][^2].

## Funding History

| 2026-06-17 | Series B | $100M | Accel | Friends & Family Capital, Point72 Ventures, Caffeinated Capital [^1][^2][^3] |

The Series B valued Twenty at $1 billion and brought total disclosed funding to $138 million [^1][^2][^3]. The company said it will direct the capital toward research and engineering to scale its AI-enabled cyber capabilities [^2].
