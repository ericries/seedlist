## About

Pave Finance is an AI-powered portfolio management platform that enables registered investment advisors to customize and automate portfolio construction at scale [^1][^2]. The company reports that firms using its platform manage $130B in assets across more than 30,000 accounts, with integrations across 170+ custodians; Pave Investment Advisors, LLC is registered with the SEC and securities are offered through Pave Securities, LLC (FINRA/SIPC) [^2].
