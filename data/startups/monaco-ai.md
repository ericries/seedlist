## About

Monaco is a San Francisco-based AI-native sales platform founded by Sam Blond that automates the "grunt work" of sales for startups [^1]. The $50M Series B announced May 12, 2026 was led by Benchmark with Founders Fund (returning investor "tripling down"), Human Capital, Alt Cap, Mantis, Saga VC, and angels Garry Tan, Neil Mehta, John Collison, and Patrick Collison participating; total funding now exceeds $85M [^1]. Jack Altman, General Partner at Benchmark, led the investment and provided a term sheet two hours after a product preview [^1].
