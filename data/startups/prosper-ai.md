## About

Prosper AI is a New York-based AI-native healthcare operations platform that automates the patient journey, including appointment scheduling, insurance eligibility and benefits verification, and patient billing [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2023 by Xavier de Gracia and Josep Mingot [^2]. The platform handles voice and text interactions across patients, providers, payors, pharmacies, and pharmaceutical companies, integrating with EHR systems including athenahealth, ModMed, Veradigm, ECW, and ImagineSoftware [^1][^3].
