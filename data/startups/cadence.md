## About

Cadence is a New York-based clinical AI company that builds technology and care delivery infrastructure to manage chronic conditions for older adults at scale, partnering with health systems to deploy its AI-powered care model [^1] [^2]. The company reports tripling annual recurring revenue in 2025 and serving more than 100,000 active patients across 20+ health systems [^1].
