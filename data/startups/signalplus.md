## About

SignalPlus is a Hong Kong-based crypto derivatives trading platform [^1][^2][^3]. The $40M strategic investment announced May 20, 2026 came from HashKey Capital ($20M cash contributed by HashKey Group as part of the total); the funding will expand SignalPlus's crypto derivatives infrastructure and trading tools [^1][^2][^3].
