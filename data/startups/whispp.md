## About

Whispp is a Leiden-based voice AI company building on-device audio-to-audio technology that reconstructs a person's natural voice in real time from whispered or impaired speech [^1]. The company targets integration with mobile and PC OEMs to make its voice-reconstruction models available at the device level rather than as a standalone consumer app [^1]. Whispp was founded by Joris Castermans, with Akash Raj Komarlu serving as CTO [^1].
