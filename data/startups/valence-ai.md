## About

Valence AI develops emotional intelligence infrastructure for voice AI systems, using a patented signal processing pipeline and Pulse Emotion models that identify emotional states from live speech during real-time conversations [^1][^2]. The company holds two U.S. patents on its proprietary audio signal processing pipeline — among the only patents ever issued on identifying emotional state from live speech in real-time conversation using deep learning — with one covering the core pipeline and the other extending the method to include live haptic feedback [^1]. In production deployments, Valence AI reports handle-time reductions of 30% alongside gains in customer satisfaction scores, close rates, and time to close [^1]. The company was founded by Chloe Duckworth (CEO) and Shannon Brownlee [^1][^2].
