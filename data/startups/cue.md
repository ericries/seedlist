## About

Cue is a Cape Town–based customer service software company that builds AI agents capable of managing customer queries from start to finish [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2015 and its platform serves more than 500 companies across automotive, retail, insurance, financial services, and education sectors in the UK and South Africa [^2][^3]. Cue's first-generation agents autonomously resolve more than 60% of customer conversations, and the platform processes over 500 million customer messages and conversations annually [^1][^2]. Average recurring revenue grew by more than 160% year-on-year in Cue's latest financial year [^1][^3].
