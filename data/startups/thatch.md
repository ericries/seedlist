## About

Thatch is a US-based health benefits platform that gives employees a "health budget" they can use to choose the coverage that fits their life, replacing the traditional group-insurance selection model with an employer-funded allowance that employees allocate across plans and services [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-15, Thatch announced a $108M Series C at a $1B valuation, led by The General Partnership (TheGP), with Index Ventures, Scale Venture Partners, General Catalyst, Andreessen Horowitz, Avid Ventures, Quiet Capital, SemperVirens, QuantumLight, Eli Lilly and Company, ADP Ventures, and Paychex participating [^2]. Cumulative funding is reported at $192M [^1]. Co-founder and CEO Chris Ellis has led the company since founding around 2021 [^2][^3].
