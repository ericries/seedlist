## About

Thatch is a US-based health benefits platform that gives employees a "health budget" they can use to choose the coverage that fits their life, replacing the traditional group-insurance selection model with an employer-funded allowance that employees allocate across plans and services [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-15, Thatch announced a $108M Series C at a $1B valuation to expand its "healthcare system people love" [^2], with Andreessen Horowitz named as the round's lead in the AlleyWatch weekly notable startup funding report for the week ending 2026-09-21 [^1].
