## About

Harken Sweets is a New York City-based developer of better-for-you chocolate candy bars, founded by Katie Lefkowitz [^1][^2]. The company's products are sweetened with whole-food dates instead of refined sugar or synthetic sweeteners, and contain up to 14 grams of fiber per serving [^2]. Harken's products are distributed across Sprouts Farmers Market, Whole Foods Market, Kroger, Costco, Walmart, Albertsons, and Wegmans, in addition to direct-to-consumer sales [^2].
