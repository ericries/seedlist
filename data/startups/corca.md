## About

Corca is a New York-based startup building an AI-native collaborative workspace for mathematics, sometimes described as a "Cursor for math" [^1] [^2]. Founded in 2023 by Anton Gladkoborodov and Oleg Shevlyagin, Corca's browser-based platform enables users to write mathematical expressions naturally without specialized syntax like LaTeX, perform real-time symbolic and numerical computation, and collaborate within a single shared workspace [^2] [^3]. The platform targets engineering, physics, finance, scientific research, and AI development workflows that have historically relied on fragmented tools like Mathematica, MATLAB, Jupyter notebooks, and shared documents [^2].
