## About

Crusoe is a Denver-based AI infrastructure company that builds and operates purpose-built data centers for large-scale AI training and inference workloads, counting Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI among its customers [^1][^2]. In early September 2026 the company reportedly closed a $3 billion Series F led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners with participation from Mubadala Capital, valuing the company at $30 billion — roughly triple its ~$10 billion valuation from ten months prior — and bringing total funding to approximately $7.2 billion [^1][^2]. The round follows a recently signed $13 billion five-year cloud contract with Jane Street [^1].
