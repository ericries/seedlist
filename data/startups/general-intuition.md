## About

General Intuition is building "embodied AI" foundation models — systems that can physically navigate and interact with the real world — trained on hundreds of millions of hours of gameplay footage from Medal, the video game clip-sharing platform from which the company spun out in 2025. The company was founded by CEO Pim de Witte alongside Eloi Alonso, Adam Jelley, and Vincent Micheli, and demonstrates technology that transfers learned behaviors from gameplay to physical embodiments like quadrupedal robots [^1][^2].
