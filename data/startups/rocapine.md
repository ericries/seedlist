## About

Rocapine is a Paris-based AI-native wellness venture studio that builds and scales consumer wellness apps, applying mobile gaming's rapid-testing methodology to health applications [^1][^2]. The company describes its approach as building apps that "hold instead of hook" — focused on durable engagement rather than dark patterns. Rocapine reached $6M in annual recurring revenue within nine months of operating, and one of its apps (Unchaind) hit $1M ARR within 16 days of launch [^2]. The company plans to test approximately 400 apps in the year following its Series A [^1]. Rocapine's stated goal is to improve the lives of at least 40 million people in the next five years [^1].
