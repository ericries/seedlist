## About

Warren is a Ghent, Belgium-based fintech that operates a licensed workplace pension fund (Warren Pension Fund OFP) and an AI-powered financial coaching app for employees [^1][^2]. Warren obtained an IBP (Institution for Occupational Retirement Provision) licence in June 2025 and operates its own no-fee pension fund supervised by Belgium's FSMA, investing employee contributions through a diversified portfolio of equity and bond ETFs [^1][^2]. The coaching layer integrates employee compensation data, national pension records, and bank transactions to let employees model retirement scenarios and access video consultations with financial specialists [^1][^2]. The company reports signing about 100 Belgian companies in its first year and previously raised a €3M pre-seed in early 2025 [^1][^2]. Warren was founded by CEO Cedric De Vleeschauwer and CCO David Du Pré [^1].
