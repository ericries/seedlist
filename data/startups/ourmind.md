## About

OurMind is an Amsterdam-based startup founded in 2024 by Marco Ferraz, Paul Koning, Renan Sales Barros, and Fredrik Gustafsson [^1]. The company builds a personal AI platform aimed at reducing administrative burden and burnout for healthcare providers, with applications for consultation preparation, administrative support, and patient communication [^1][^2]. More than 300 general practices and 14 hospitals use the platform [^1].
