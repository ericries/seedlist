## About

Centrical is a Performance Intelligence platform for frontline teams across sales, service, and operations [^1]. The platform connects performance data, coaching, learning, practice, and recognition to guide employees and managers toward improved outcomes [^1]. Founded in 2013 by Gal Rimon, the company serves Fortune 500 enterprises across 150 countries in 60 languages with offices in New York, Tel Aviv, and London [^1]. Customers include Deutsche Telekom, Samsung, DHL, and IHG Hotels & Resorts [^1][^2].

Centrical has raised approximately $100 million since founding, with the prior capital raise in 2021 [^2]. During the interim period, the company narrowed its focus to large enterprise customers, reduced headcount from 180 to approximately 100 employees, and repositioned its platform for the AI era [^2].
