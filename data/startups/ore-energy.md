## About

Ore Energy is a Dutch multi-day energy storage startup developing grid-scale iron-air batteries designed to store renewable electricity for up to 100 hours [^1][^2]. The company positions its technology as a low-cost baseload solution to help utilities and energy-intensive industries (including AI data centres) rely on renewable power around the clock [^1][^3]. Ore Energy was co-founded by CEO Aytac Yilmaz and spun out of TU Delft research on iron-based electrochemistry [^2][^3].
