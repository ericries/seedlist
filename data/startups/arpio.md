## About

Arpio is a Durham, North Carolina-based AI-native recovery and resilience platform for cloud environments, founded in 2020 by Doug Neumann and Shaw Terwilliger [^1][^2]. The platform enables enterprises to recover critical systems from ransomware, outages, and other disruptive events in minutes rather than days, and validate resilience before incidents occur [^1][^2][^3].
