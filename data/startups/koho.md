## About

KOHO is a Canadian challenger bank founded in 2014, headquartered in Vancouver with operations in Toronto [^1][^3]. The company offers a prepaid Mastercard, lines of credit, international money transfers, credit-building products, tenant insurance, and cryptocurrency services [^1]. KOHO is actively pursuing a Schedule 1 federal banking licence in Canada [^1][^2]. The company reports 2.5 million customers (up from one million in late 2023), approximately C$250M in annual revenue, 50% year-over-year growth, and 250 employees [^1][^2].
