## About

Cyclops is a Miami-based stablecoin infrastructure company built exclusively for the payments industry. Its platform lets payments companies onboard with a single partner to unlock stablecoin settlement, pay-ins, pay-outs, and treasury optimization across global corridors through one API [^1][^2]. Co-founders Alex Wilson, Pat Duffy, and David Johnson previously worked together at The Giving Block, a crypto donations platform [^3]. The company also maintains an office in Vienna, Austria, and counts Shift4 Payments and Mastercard among its early clients [^3]. Cyclops reports 350% month-over-month volume growth and a merchant network that has expanded to roughly 300,000 [^1].

On 2026-07-15, Cyclops announced a $20 million Series A led by Nava Ventures, with participation from Castle Island Ventures, Coinbase Ventures, Circle, Lasagna Ventures, and Global PayTech Ventures [^1][^2][^3]. The round follows an $8 million seed raised in March 2026 [^3]. Nava Ventures' Kevin Chenault joined the Cyclops board as part of the round [^1].
