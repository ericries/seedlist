## About

Harmony is an AI-native enterprise service management (ESM) platform that resolves employee requests across IT, HR, finance, procurement, and legal by deploying conversational AI agents embedded in workplace tools like Slack and Microsoft Teams [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2025 by Nitzan Shapira and Ran Ribenzaft, who previously co-founded cloud observability company Epsagon (acquired by Cisco for $500 million in 2021) [^2][^3]. Harmony aims to build a broader ESM platform spanning infrastructure and operations, enterprise applications, cybersecurity, and ERP [^1]. On 2026-07-28, Harmony announced a $34 million seed round led by Lightspeed Venture Partners [^1][^2][^4].
