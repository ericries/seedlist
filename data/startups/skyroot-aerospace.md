## About

Skyroot Aerospace is a Hyderabad-based private launch services company founded in 2018 by former ISRO scientists Pawan Kumar Chandana and Naga Bharath Daka [^1][^2]. The company is preparing for the maiden orbital launch of its Vikram-1 rocket in the coming weeks and is developing Vikram-2, a heavier-lift launch vehicle expected to debut in 2027 [^1][^2]. The $60M round announced May 7, 2026 ($50M primary equity co-led by Sherpalo Ventures and GIC, plus $10M structured debt from BlackRock-affiliated funds) values the company at $1.1B pre-money, making Skyroot India's first space-tech unicorn—more than double its $500M pre-money valuation from 2023 [^1][^2][^3]. Sherpalo Ventures founder and Alphabet board member Ram Shriram will join Skyroot's board [^2][^3].
