## About

Davis is a Paris-based AI-native real estate company that aims to compress early-stage development timelines by automating feasibility studies and architectural design [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Entrepreneurs First alumni Mehdi Rais (CEO, from a family of architects) and Amine Chraibi (AI researcher from École Polytechnique focused on generative models for structured data) [^1][^2]. Alongside the funding, Davis introduced Gaudi-1, its proprietary model for generating architectural designs under real-world regulatory constraints [^1][^2]. The $5.5M pre-seed announced May 6, 2026 was co-led by Heartcore Capital and Balderton Capital with participation from Yellow, Evantic, Entrepreneurs First, and angel investors from SpaceMaker, Black Forest Labs, Hugging Face, Supabase, and Spore.bio founding teams [^1][^2].
