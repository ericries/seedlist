## About

Optura builds an enterprise platform that helps healthcare organizations assess AI business value and viability, prioritize AI investments, and provide real-time visibility into the enterprise impact and return of each initiative [^1][^2]. The company developed a measurement framework — Return on AI Investment, or "ROAI" — to enable enterprises to project and track value creation for technology initiatives [^1][^3]. More than $2B in AI initiatives are loaded into the Optura platform, with $120M in tracked value at a reported 700% ROAI on in-flight initiatives and over 250 new use cases identified across customers such as Independence Blue Cross, Prime Therapeutics, and Ardent Health [^1][^2]. The $17.5M Series A announced on May 14, 2026 was led by Salesforce Ventures with participation from Echo Health Ventures and continued investment from Susa Ventures, Matrix Partners, and HC9 Ventures, bringing total funding to over $25M [^1][^2][^3].
