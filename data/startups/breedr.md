## About

Breedr is a livestock-management and marketplace platform that gives every animal a digital record — weight, health, and family history — updated across the three or four farms and ranches a beef animal typically passes through during its lifespan [^1][^2]. Producers can trade animals directly on Breedr's marketplace instead of selling at auction, with the digital record travelling with the animal [^1]. Founded in the UK in 2018 by fourth-generation Australian rancher Ian Wheal, the company is now headquartered in Texas [^1][^2]. On August 26, 2026, Breedr announced a $27 million (approximately €23 million) Series B led by Partech's impact fund, taking total funding to $46.6 million (approximately €39.9 million) [^1][^2][^3].
