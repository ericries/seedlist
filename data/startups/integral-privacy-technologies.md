## About

Integral Privacy Technologies (which operates as "Integral") is a San Francisco-based company building what it calls an "Independent Privacy Layer" for the real-world data economy — a software-automated, expert-led privacy layer that plugs directly into sensitive real-world datasets to execute entity-preserving remediation and continuously measure re-identification risk [^1][^2][^3]. The company's "Forward Deployed Privacy Services" transform real-world data into AI-ready assets for training, labeling, and enterprise AI applications, with an initial focus on healthcare data [^1][^2]. Co-founder and CEO Shubh Sinha has said the team spent four years working through the problem in healthcare before broadening the platform to other real-world data domains [^3]. Proceeds from the July 2026 announcement will be used to expand Integral's privacy engineering and statistical methodology divisions, invest in continuous risk-assessment linkage infrastructure, and accelerate go-to-market operations across global AI development labs [^1][^2].
