## About

Observable Space is a Los Angeles-based vertically integrated space-domain-awareness and laser-communications company formed in February 2025 by the merger of telescope-maker PlaneWave Instruments and space-data-application company OurSky; co-founder and CEO Dan Roelker leads the combined entity, which builds software-defined telescopes and optical systems used for satellite tracking, deep-space observation, and laser communications ("lasercom") [^1][^2][^3]. The $90M Series A announced May 28, 2026 was led by Lux Capital with co-leads RTX Ventures, Upfront Ventures, Detroit Venture Partners, and Island Green Capital, plus participation from BRV Capital, Fathom Fund, and Venrex; Observable Space simultaneously announced a $94M U.S. Space Force contract (with $22M in early task orders) to deploy mobile off-grid optical sensing stations for space domain awareness [^1][^2][^3].
