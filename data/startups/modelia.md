## About

Modelia is a Madrid-based AI platform that generates and manages visual assets for the fashion industry, founded in 2024 by Iván Rodríguez and René Haas [^1][^2]. The platform is used by brands including Desigual, AWWG, and Fútbol Emotion to generate and adapt product visuals across commercial channels [^1][^2]. Modelia was an official partner of Mercedes-Benz Fashion Week Madrid for its March 2026 edition and is scheduled to return for the event's 40th anniversary in September 2026 [^1].
