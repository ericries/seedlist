## About

Airspeed is a London-based agent-native platform for go-to-market execution that deploys autonomous AI agents acting on commercial context across calls, emails, tickets, and CRM systems — automatically updating records, flagging risks, and generating follow-ups without waiting for a human to trigger them [^1][^2]. The company was formerly known as Glyphic and rebranded on May 20, 2026 [^1]. It was founded by two former DeepMind research scientists, including Cambridge alumnus Devang Agrawal [^1]. Airspeed reports revenue grew 4x over the past twelve months, with monthly run volume nearly tripling from January to April 2026, and now serves 200 customers across 20 countries [^1].
