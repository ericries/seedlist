## About

Sekai is a San Francisco Bay Area-based consumer platform where users build software by describing what they want, generating "mini-apps" that can be created, played, and remixed [^1]. The platform has hosted more than 15 million user-created mini-apps, with roughly 200,000 new apps being created every day, and users spending over an hour a day on average [^1]. Sekai was founded in 2024 by Lucky Zhang, for whom Sekai is his fourth company [^1]. The company has a creator partnership with Creative Artists Agency (CAA) [^1].
