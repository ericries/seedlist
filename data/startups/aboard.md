## About

Aboard is an Orange, California-based mobility company building an extended-range electric (EREV) travel trailer that integrates energy, mobility, and living into a single platform [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Jiangtao Lyu, with a team rooted in automotive engineering, to bring automotive-grade design and engineering to the travel trailer category [^1]. The configurable EREV trailer starts at $80,000 and is designed to operate without hookups or traditional RV systems [^1]. Aboard plans its formal public launch on May 29, 2026 at Outside Days in Denver, Colorado [^1]. On May 21, 2026, Aboard announced the close of a Pre-Series A round bringing its total raised to $13 million, co-led by Ondine Capital and Llama Ventures [^1][^2].
