## About

9amHealth is a San Diego-based virtual specialty care platform providing AI-driven clinical management for chronic cardiometabolic conditions including diabetes, obesity, hypertension and cholesterol, founded in 2021 and led by CEO Frank Westermann [^1][^2]. The company announced a $26 million Series B round on May 13, 2026, led by Define Ventures with participation from SemperVirens VC, Catalio Capital Management, and NewHealth Ventures; the capital will fund expansion to additional high-cost specialty chronic conditions that collectively account for up to 70% of employer pharmacy spend [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports that its platform has generated more than $50 million in projected medical and pharmacy savings across two Fortune 100 employers [^2][^3].
