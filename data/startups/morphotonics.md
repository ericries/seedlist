## About

Morphotonics is a Dutch deeptech company developing nanoimprint lithography (NIL) technology for large-area manufacturing with sub-100nm precision [^1][^2]. The Veldhoven-based firm produces "stamps" applied to photosensitive materials to make waveguides and other optics for augmented reality glasses, advanced displays, and next-generation consumer electronics, with hardware sales driving roughly 90% of revenue and 10-15 systems deployed globally as of September 2026 [^1][^2]. Customers include smart-glasses manufacturers such as Meta Ray-Ban, Even Reality, and Magic Leap, and the company is expanding into data-center optical components based on Photonic Integrated Circuits; its next-generation Cypris platform is expected to produce 6+ million waveguides annually and ship in early 2027 [^1][^2]. Revenue tripled in 2025 compared to 2024, and headcount grew from roughly 30 in September 2024 to about 60 by the Series B close, expected to stabilise near 70-75 [^1][^2]. Hugo da Silva has led the company as CEO since September 2024 [^1].
