## About

Morphotonics is a Dutch deeptech company developing nanoimprint lithography (NIL) technology for large-area manufacturing with sub-100nm precision [^1][^2]. The Veldhoven-based firm produces "stamps" applied to photosensitive materials to make waveguides and other optics for augmented reality glasses, advanced displays, and next-generation consumer electronics, with hardware sales driving roughly 90% of revenue and 10-15 systems deployed globally as of September 2026 [^1][^2].
