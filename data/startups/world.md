## About

World (formerly Worldcoin) is a biometric identity project co-founded by Sam Altman that uses iris-scanning hardware (the "Orb") to create cryptographic proof-of-human identifiers on-chain [^1]. The project pairs the identity layer with the WLD token, and its stated goal is to distinguish humans from AI agents online as generative AI proliferates [^1]. Tools for Humanity, led by CEO Alex Blania, is the developer arm behind the network [^1].
