## About

Chamelio is an Israeli legal intelligence platform (operating as Innvo AI Inc.) building AI agents for in-house legal teams — automating routine contract workflows such as NDA turnaround, redlining, playbook generation, contract metadata extraction, and repository search, while escalating complex matters to human attorneys [^1][^3]. The company was co-founded in 2023 by Alex Zilberman (CEO, formerly COO and co-founder of AnyVision, prior leadership roles at Pagaya), Gil Banyas (COO, 15 years of legal experience including general counsel at AnyVision), and Gal Lellouche (CTO, previously founded an AI company acquired by Sage) [^1]. Reported customers include Wiz, monday.com, Socure, AppsFlyer, Bizzabo, Lightricks, Radware, and Wonderful [^1][^3].
