## About

Mimir is an Oslo-based AI-native customer support platform for B2C e-commerce companies, founded in 2024 by Jørgen Vartdal Halse, Jens Kristoffersen, and Øyvind Monsen [^1]. The platform manages roughly 250,000 customer conversations per month for around 60 brands across 5 countries, including VILLOID, HiFi Klubben, and Holzweiler [^1]. The company reports approximately 7x growth over the past year and reached profitability without external funding before raising its pre-seed round [^1].
