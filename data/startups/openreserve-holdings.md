## About

OpenReserve Holdings is a New York-based financial technology company building a national bank that integrates blockchain infrastructure into traditional banking products and services, targeting demand for regulated banking from digital-asset-native clients [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2025 by Diwakar Choubey (co-founder of MoneyLion) and Richard Correia [^1][^2]. OpenReserve has applied to the OCC to charter OpenReserve Bank, National Association, as a full-service national bank [^3].
