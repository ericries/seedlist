## About

OpenReserve Holdings is a New York-based financial technology company building a national bank that integrates blockchain infrastructure into traditional banking products and services, targeting demand for regulated banking from digital-asset-native clients [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2025 by Diwakar Choubey (co-founder of MoneyLion) and Richard Correia [^1][^2]. OpenReserve has applied to the OCC to charter OpenReserve Bank, National Association, as a full-service national bank [^3]. The company is headquartered at 260 5th Avenue, 9th Floor, New York, NY 10001 [^4].

OpenReserve raised $24,005,501 in a Regulation D Rule 506(b) exempt offering, composed of $15,005,501.94 in Series Seed Preferred Stock and $9,000,000 in SAFEs, with a target of $25,000,000 [^4][^5]. Per the Form D, the first sale date was April 13, 2026; the filing was submitted to the SEC on June 10, 2026 and reported publicly by AlleyWatch on June 11, 2026 [^1][^4]. Eleven accredited investors participated; no lead investor or individual participant names were disclosed in the filing or in any subsequent press coverage [^4].
