## About

Empirical Security is a Chicago-based cybersecurity company building custom AI models for risk-based vulnerability management and exposure management [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2024 by Ed Bellis (CEO), Michael Roytman (CTO), and Jay Jacobs (Chief Data Scientist) — all veterans of Kenna Security, which they co-founded and Cisco acquired in 2021 [^2][^3]. Michael Roytman previously served as Chief Data Scientist at Kenna and Jay Jacobs led the Exploit Prediction Scoring System (EPSS) [^1][^3]. Empirical's core thesis is that generic AI models cannot reliably prioritize the specific vulnerabilities that matter to a given customer, so the company trains customer-tailored mathematical models on security data [^1]. On 2026-07-20, the company announced a $25 million Series A led by Brightmind Partners [^2][^4].
