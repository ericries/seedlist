## About

Alta Ares is a French defense technology startup, founded in 2024 by Hadrien Canter, Stanislas Walch, Théo Bondarec, and Alain Henry, that builds edge-AI systems running directly on drones and battlefield hardware. The platform processes live optical, infrared, and multisensor data to detect, track, and engage aerial and ground targets without requiring cloud connectivity [^1][^2]. The company has developed two flagship counter-drone interceptors: X-Lock, a short-range interceptor for autonomous drone threats, and Black Bird, a medium-range system engineered to counter high-speed cruise missiles and glide bombs [^1]. Alta Ares operates from France and Ukraine and reports multi-million-euro signed contracts and active deployments across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia [^2].
