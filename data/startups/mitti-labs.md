## About

Mitti Labs is a climate-tech company headquartered in New York with a subsidiary in Bengaluru, India, that combines satellite imagery, AI, and field operations to help farmers cut water use and methane emissions in rice cultivation [^1][^2]. The company's GeoAI platform uses high-resolution synthetic aperture radar (SAR) data with field-collected ground truth and physical models to generate digital twins of Asia's rice fields at the resolution of individual plots [^1][^2]. Mitti Labs has grown from working with about 8,000 farmers in its first season in 2024 to more than 100,000 farmers across several Indian states this season [^1].
