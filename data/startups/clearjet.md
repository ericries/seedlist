## About

ClearJet is an AI-enabled logistics platform that connects e-commerce shippers with unused belly-cargo capacity on commercial passenger flights, marketed as a "SuperCarrier network" spanning 95 US airports [^1][^2][^3]. The company is profitable, has more than tripled revenue year over year, and is approaching nine figures in top-line revenue [^1][^2].

On August 12, 2026, ClearJet announced a $25 million Series B growth investment led by Edison Partners, with participation from returning investors Venture53, Origin Ventures, Salt VC, and SpringTime Ventures [^1][^2][^3]. Early backers Sky VC (formerly JetBlue Ventures) and Tandem Ventures also remain in the cap table [^1][^2]. The round brings ClearJet's total capital raised to more than $40 million [^1][^2][^3].
