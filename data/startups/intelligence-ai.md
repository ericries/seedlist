## About

Intelligence is a San Francisco-based startup operating Design Arena, a crowdsourced benchmarking platform for AI-generated visual media [^1][^2][^3]. Co-founded in 2025 by Grace Li and a group of college friends before their graduation, the company runs "A vs. B" head-to-head comparisons in which humans pick between two AI-generated outputs (websites, images, and other visual media), then packages those votes as training feedback for frontier AI labs [^1][^2]. As of the seed round, Design Arena had 5.3 million users globally and $60M in annualized revenue [^1][^2][^3].
