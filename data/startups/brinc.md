## About

BRINC is a Seattle-based public safety drone maker that announced on 2026-07-14 a $125 million round led by Motorola Solutions, with participation from Index Ventures and Figma founder/CEO Dylan Field, bringing total capital raised to more than $250 million [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2018 by Blake Resnick, the company builds drones designed to be launched from police and fire stations to arrive on the scene of 911 calls before first responders, giving dispatchers and officers real-time video of the situation [^1][^2]. Prior backers include OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, Scale AI founder Alexandr Wang, Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar, former LinkedIn CEO Jeff Weiner, former acting Defense Secretary Patrick Shanahan, and former FCC chairman Julius Genachowski [^1]. BRINC says revenue more than tripled and monthly production capacity quintupled in 2025, and the company will use the new funding to expand manufacturing at its Seattle facility and grow the team [^1][^2].
