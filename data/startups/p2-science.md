## About

P2 Science is a Woodbridge, Connecticut-based green chemistry company co-founded by Professor Paul Anastas (the "father of green chemistry") and Dr. Patrick Foley out of Yale's Center for Green Chemistry and Green Engineering, transforming sustainable feedstocks into high-performance ingredients for beauty, personal care, and adjacent industries; CEO Oihana Elizalde leads the company [^1][^2][^3]. P2 Science was named to Fast Company's World's Most Innovative Companies list in both 2025 and 2026 and was selected for L'Oréal's €100M L'AcceleratOR sustainable-innovation accelerator [^1]. The oversubscribed $23M growth round announced May 26, 2026 was led by Sofinnova Partners — its first up round — with new investors Emerald Technology Ventures and GS Futures joining returning backers Lewis & Clark Partners, DSM-Firmenich Ventures, Connecticut Innovations, Elm Street Ventures, Chanel, BASF, and Safer Made; capital will fund expansion into aroma technologies, performance polymers, home care, coatings, and crop care [^1][^2][^3].
