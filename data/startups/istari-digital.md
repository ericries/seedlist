## About

Istari Digital is an Arlington, Virginia-based defense software company building digital engineering infrastructure — a "working data layer" that lets defense contractors and government engineers share live engineering models across security boundaries without handing over the underlying files [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded in 2022 by former Air Force acquisition chief Will Roper [^1][^3]. On 2026-07-27, Washington Business Journal reported that Istari had raised $22.6 million toward a $40 million Series B goal, disclosed via a July 24 Form D SEC filing, bringing total capital to approximately $62.7 million [^1][^2][^3]. The company's biggest current proof point is Industry Øne, a shared digital engineering program connecting Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, and 13 other organizations, backed by an $8.6 million Air Force contract announced in May 2026 [^1][^4].
