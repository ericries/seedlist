## About

Fleek is a London-based B2B marketplace that connects secondhand-clothing wholesalers — primarily in India, Pakistan, and Dubai — with vintage-clothing retailers across more than 100 countries [^1][^2]. Co-founded in 2022 by Abhi Arora and Sanket Agarwal, the company operates roughly 2,000 verified wholesale suppliers and more than 50,000 vintage retailers on its platform and has built Fleek Sort, a proprietary computer vision–language model that identifies garment details, flags defects, and estimates pricing and sale timelines to standardize a historically opaque supply chain [^1][^2].
