## About

Fleek is a London-based B2B marketplace that connects secondhand-clothing wholesalers — primarily in India, Pakistan, and Dubai — with vintage-clothing retailers across more than 100 countries [^1][^2]. Co-founded by Abhi Arora and Sanket Agarwal, the company went through Y Combinator's Winter 2022 (W22) batch [^5] and operates roughly 2,000 verified wholesale suppliers and more than 50,000 vintage retailers on its platform, having built Fleek Sort, a proprietary computer vision–language model that identifies garment details, flags defects, and estimates pricing and sale timelines to standardize a historically opaque supply chain [^1][^2].
