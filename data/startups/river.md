## About

River is a Bengaluru-based Indian electric two-wheeler startup founded in 2021 by Aravind Mani (CEO) and Vipin George [^1][^2]. The company focuses on utility-oriented electric mopeds and has scaled primarily on the strength of a single model, the Indie, which launched in 2023 and is priced at approximately ₹155,000 (~$1,630) [^1]. River currently sells roughly 6,000 vehicles per month and has distributed more than 50,000 units to date, targeting self-employed customers aged 28–35 [^1]. The company competes with newer entrants Ather Energy and Ola Electric as well as legacy manufacturers Bajaj Auto and TVS Motor [^1].
