## About

Eddy Grid is a Utrecht-based EnergyTech company founded in 2023 by CEO Sam Rohn and co-founders that develops algorithms to optimize renewable-energy assets and operates the Netherlands' largest independent energy optimization platform, managing more than 500 MW of capacity [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports nearly 900% revenue growth in 2025, projects another ~1,000% growth in 2026, and is cash-flow positive [^1][^2]. The €7.5M round announced May 28, 2026 was funded almost entirely internally by existing investors Graduate Ventures and Volve Capital — valuing the three-year-old company at more than 20× its valuation from two years prior — and included nearly €1M in secondary transactions for existing shareholders [^1][^2][^3].
