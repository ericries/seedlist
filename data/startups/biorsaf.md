## About

Biorsaf is a Castell'Azzara, Tuscany-based food-safety and workplace-compliance platform founded in 2021 by CEO Marco Papalini [^1][^2]. Its BS-Safe platform automates HACCP, workplace-safety, and water-quality compliance for food companies, and on September 10, 2026 Biorsaf announced a €5.2 million Series A round alongside the acquisition of Bologna-based Cooki, whose food-safety system, 35,000-product database, and 1,000+ active users combine with Biorsaf's own 10,000-user base to form what the companies describe as Italy's leading Food RegTech platform [^1][^2][^3].
