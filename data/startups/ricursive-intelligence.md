## About

Ricursive Intelligence is an AI lab focused on AI-driven semiconductor design, co-founded by Dr. Anna Goldie (CEO) and Dr. Azalia Mirhoseini (CTO), both former Google researchers whose reinforcement-learning work on chip layout design ("AlphaChip") was integrated into four generations of Google's TPU chips [^1][^2]. The Palo Alto, California-based company emerged from stealth in late 2025 and raised a $300M Series A at a $4B post-money valuation roughly two months after its launch [^1][^2][^3].
