## About

Develo is a Los Angeles-based healthcare software startup founded in 2022 by Aaron Sin (CEO) and Han Ke that builds an AI-native, FHIR-conformant electronic medical records (EMR) platform purpose-built for independent pediatric practices [^1][^2]. The platform manages scheduling, charting, billing, and family engagement, with automated bi-directional outreach to capture patient information [^2]. Develo publicly announced a $14M Series A on May 11, 2026, led by Blueprint Equity, with Villain Capital, z21 Ventures, Bienville Capital, and healthcare-tech angels Paul Black, Chris Barton, and Chris Sullens participating [^4][^5][^6]. The Series A first-sale date was recorded as March 4, 2026 in a SEC Form D filing for $13,999,989 [^3]. Prior backers include Fractal Software, z21 Ventures, and StartX [^1][^2].
