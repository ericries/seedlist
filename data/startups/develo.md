## About

Develo is a Los Angeles-based healthcare software startup founded in 2022 by Aaron Sin (CEO) and Han Ke that builds an AI-native, FHIR-conformant electronic medical records (EMR) platform purpose-built for independent pediatric practices [^1][^2]. The platform manages scheduling, charting, billing, and family engagement, with automated bi-directional outreach to capture patient information [^2]. According to a SEC Form D filing dated March 4, 2026, Develo raised $13,999,989 in an exempt offering, with Aaron Sin listed as Chief Executive Officer [^3]. Tracxn classifies the round as Develo's Series A, dated February 4, 2026 [^2]. Prior backers include Fractal Software, z21 Ventures, and StartX [^1][^2].
