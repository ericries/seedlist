## About

Claros Technologies is a Minneapolis-based cleantech company focused on destruction and analytical solutions for PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances, commonly known as "forever chemicals") [^1][^2]. The company develops the proprietary ClarosTechUV PFAS Destruction System and operates ClarosLabs analytical services for industrial manufacturers, remediation firms, utilities, and government agencies [^1][^2]. In December 2025, Claros — in partnership with Daikin America — completed one of the world's largest in-field PFAS destruction demonstrations, achieving greater than 99.99% destruction of long-, short-, and ultra-short chain PFAS compounds in industrial process water [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Claros announced a $55 million Series B led by Treehouse Family Capital, with participation from Daikin America, Veralto, Bush Foundation, Nord Asset Management, Saint Paul & Minnesota Foundation, and continued support from existing investors [^1][^2][^3].
