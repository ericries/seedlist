## About

Fair Warning is a members-only mobile auction platform founded in June 2020 by former Christie's co-chairman Loic Gouzer, releasing one verified artwork per week for pre-screened collectors to bid on [^2][^3]. The platform charges a 15% buyer's premium on hammer prices and screens users to prevent resale for profit [^3]. Notable transactions have included a Basquiat sold for US$10.8M and a Banksy work valued in the tens of millions [^3]. On 2026-08-04, Fair Warning disclosed a $2.5M equity raise per SEC filing with two investors participating; the raise brings the company's cumulative reported equity funding to approximately $5.6M [^1].
