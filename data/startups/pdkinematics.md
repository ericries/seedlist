## About

PDKINEMATICS is a Lithuanian defence engineering company building precision guidance systems for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) [^1]. The company announced the close of a €2 million seed round on June 17, 2026, bringing total funding to €3 million [^1] [^2]. The new capital will be used to scale manufacturing and expand deployments of the company's main product, Gannet — a precision targeting system designed for UAV platforms that allows bomber-class UAVs to fly at up to 700 metres while maintaining strike accuracy, roughly six times the altitude achievable with unguided munitions [^1]. PDKINEMATICS has signed a strategic partnership with Bavovna.ai, one of Ukraine's largest bomber-class UAV manufacturers, with its systems now deployed in battlefield conditions [^1].
