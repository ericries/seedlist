## About

Active Technology (主动科技) is a Shanghai-based brain-computer interface (BCI) startup developing implantable neural interfaces to restore motor and sensory function for patients with severe neurological impairments such as spinal-cord injury or stroke-related paralysis [^1][^2][^3]. The company was established in February 2026 and operates a 2,500-square-meter facility, with plans to expand its headquarters to over 10,000 square meters by end of 2026 [^2]. Its product roadmap spans motor, visual, and emotional BCIs [^2].

The round is reported as the largest angel-stage financing on record in China's BCI sector [^2][^3]. Proceeds will expand facilities, upgrade equipment, accelerate clinical trials, and enlarge the team to drive clinical translation and industrial rollout of the company's invasive BCI systems [^1][^2].
