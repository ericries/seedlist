## About

Diameter Pay is a Jersey City–based fintech infrastructure company that provides banks, fintechs, and digital asset exchanges access to U.S. dollar accounts and payment systems through a single API — including virtual accounts, domestic and international payments, stablecoin on- and off-ramps, and compliance controls across multiple banking partners [^1]. On September 3, 2026 the company announced a $10 million Series A co-led by CMT Digital and Lightspeed Faction, with participation from SixThirty Ventures, Stellar Development Foundation, Tech Council Ventures, Onigiri Capital, and BitRock Capital [^1]. The company reported having processed over $10 billion year-to-date in 2026 at the time of the announcement, and is led by founder and CEO David Lighton with a team of veterans from Deutsche Bank, Fidelity Investments, and the World Bank [^1].
