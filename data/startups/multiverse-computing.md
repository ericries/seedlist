## About

Multiverse Computing is a San Sebastián, Spain-based deep-tech company applying tensor network methods from quantum physics to compress large language models [^1][^2]. Its CompactifAI platform reduces model size by 80–95% while preserving accuracy, enabling AI deployment across cloud, on-premises, and edge devices [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-27, the company announced a Series C targeting up to €500M ($570M) at a €1.5B ($1.7B) pre-money valuation, a 5x step-up from its Series B [^1][^2][^3]. Revenue grew 96x year-over-year in Q1 2026 [^2]. JPMorgan and Santander CIB advised on the deal [^1].
