## About

Cyclops is a stablecoin infrastructure company built specifically for the payments industry. Its API-based platform bundles crypto and stablecoin services — settlement, payouts, payment acceptance, and treasury — letting payment companies offer faster settlement and cross-border transactions without building the tooling themselves [^1][^2].

The company reports having processed over $2B in transaction volume, supporting more than 400 digital assets, operating in 150+ countries, and holding 100+ global licenses [^1].
