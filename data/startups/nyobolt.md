## About

Nyobolt is a Cambridge, England-based battery technology company founded in 2019 by Dr. Sai Shivareddy and Professor Dame Clare Grey as a spinout from the Yusuf Hamied Department of Chemistry at the University of Cambridge [^3]. The company develops ultra-fast charging, high-power lithium-ion battery systems using specialized anode materials, advanced cell design, and integrated power electronics, targeting autonomous robots, humanoid systems, and AI data center infrastructure [^1][^2]. On May 6, 2026, Nyobolt announced a $60M Series C round at a valuation exceeding $1 billion, bringing total funding to approximately $160M [^1][^2].
