## About

Upriver is an AI-native data engineering platform that autonomously manages the data layer enterprises rely on to operate AI applications [^1][^2]. The platform connects directly to an organization's data environment, resolves quality issues, maintains pipelines, and executes data engineering workflows end-to-end [^1]. Ido Bronstein is co-founder and CEO [^1]. Existing customers include Unity and DMGT, with platform partnerships covering Databricks and Snowflake [^1][^2]. The company cites Gartner's January 2026 finding that at least 50% of generative AI projects are abandoned after proof of concept, with poor data quality among the leading causes [^1].
