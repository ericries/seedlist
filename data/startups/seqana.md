## About

Seqana is a Berlin-based digital MRV (monitoring, reporting, and verification) company that quantifies soil health using satellite imagery and machine learning [^1]. Customers include Danone, eAgronom, Klim, and Bayer, and the company has assessed millions of hectares of agricultural land to date [^1]. Seqana's products help carbon market project developers and agrifood corporates measure soil carbon and broader soil health indicators at scale [^2].
