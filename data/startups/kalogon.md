## About

Kalogon is a Melbourne, Florida-based smart seating company founded in 2019 by engineers from SpaceX to solve pressure injuries for wheelchair users [^1][^2]. Its first product was Orbiter, a smart wheelchair cushion clinically validated to improve blood flow and comfort; the company has since expanded into a full seating system with custom Medicare-coded products that address postural support alongside pressure management [^1]. Kalogon more than tripled medical revenue year-over-year ahead of this round and moved into a dedicated manufacturing facility in Melbourne, Florida [^1]. Markets served include healthcare, aviation, and military applications [^1].
