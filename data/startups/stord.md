## About

Stord is an Atlanta-based logistics and fulfillment company that operates a network of physical warehouses combined with inventory management software for e-commerce brands. Founded in 2015 by Sean Henry (CEO) and Jacob Boudreau (CTO), the company positions itself as an "anti-Amazon" alternative — offering brands fulfillment speed comparable to Amazon while allowing them to retain direct customer relationships [^1].
