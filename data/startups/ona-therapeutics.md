## About

Ona Therapeutics is a Barcelona-based biopharmaceutical company developing first-in-class antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs) for treatment-resistant cancers [^1] [^2]. The company is based at the Barcelona Science Park and was spun out of IRB Barcelona and ICREA [^1]. Its patient-driven discovery platform identifies novel tumor-specific antigens associated with resistance biology.

Series B proceeds will advance ONA-255, a first-in-class ADC initially focused on breast cancer, into clinical development, and progress ONA-389, a second first-in-class ADC targeting colorectal cancer. The company is also expanding indications into gastric and liver cancer [^1] [^2].
