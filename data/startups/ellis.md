## About

Ellis is an AI-native operations platform for private credit managers, providing a reconciled, source-verifiable data foundation and purpose-built AI agents for workflows including reconciliation, LP reporting, SBIC compliance, and document intelligence [^1][^2]. The company emerged from stealth on July 30, 2026 [^1]. Ellis was founded by Ryan Williams, previously co-founder of Cadre, a technology-enabled institutional alternatives platform that facilitated approximately $6 billion in transaction value before being sold to Yieldstreet in 2024 [^2][^3].
