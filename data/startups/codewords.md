## About

CodeWords is a London-based AI automation platform that builds proactive AI agents for businesses [^1]. The company was originally founded as Agemo, an AI research lab focused on neurosymbolic reasoning, and later pivoted toward a practical product aimed at simplifying automation for non-technical business users [^1]. Its flagship agent, Cody, learns about a company's tools, goals and workflows, identifies repetitive tasks, and executes them automatically [^1]. CodeWords reports running more than 500,000 workflows per month for operators, marketers, finance teams and agencies [^1].

The company was co-founded by Aymeric Zhuo and Osman Ramadan [^1][^2].
