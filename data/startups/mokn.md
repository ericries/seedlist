## About

MokN is a French cybersecurity startup founded in 2024 by CEO Gautier Bugeon along with Antoine Coudoux, Alexis Georges, and Adrien Casteleiro, building a novel "phish-back" technology that traps attackers and recovers compromised credentials before they are used or exposed on the dark web [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports protecting more than 1 million users across large corporations and mid-size companies, including Fortune Global 500 customers [^1]. The €12.9M (~$15M) Series A announced May 28, 2026 was led by GV (Google Ventures) — its first investment in a French startup — with participation from Datadog and existing European backers Moonfire and OVNI Capital plus angels; the round follows MokN's €2.6M pre-seed of October 2025 and brings total funding to approximately $17.6M [^1][^2][^3].
