## About

TytoCare is a New York- and Netanya, Israel-based digital-health company that develops FDA-cleared handheld devices and AI-powered software algorithms for remote physical examinations, capturing clinical-grade heart, lung, ear, skin, throat, and abdominal data [^1][^2][^3]. The platform is used by health systems, payers, and employer health programs to deliver primary care, cardiopulmonary care, and oncology-related monitoring outside of in-person visits [^2][^3]. On 2026-07-15 TytoCare announced a growth round of more than $25 million led by Insight Partners, and appointed Adam Pellegrini as chief executive officer and Greg Orr as chief operating officer [^1][^2][^3]. The company said the funds will deepen integrations with health systems and payers, with a particular focus on congestive heart failure, COPD, post-surgical recovery, and oncology treatment monitoring [^2][^3].
