## About

Frame Security emerged from stealth on May 11, 2026 with a $50 million funding round to build a human risk management platform — covering simulated phishing attacks, employee training, and threat triage to address what the company calls "the largest attack vector in cybersecurity: people" [^1][^2][^3]. Frame was founded by CEO Tal Shlomo, an early employee of cloud security company Wiz, and CTO Sharon Shmueli, formerly CTO of the global venture group Team8 [^1][^2]. The company already counts enterprise customers including Louis Dreyfus Company, AlphaSense, and Rockefeller Capital Management [^1]. Proceeds will fund expansion of engineering, AI research, and go-to-market across the US and globally [^1][^2].
