## About

groundcover is a Tel Aviv-based cloud-native observability platform that allows engineering teams to monitor applications, cloud infrastructure, and AI workloads from within customers' own cloud environments [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2021 and led by CEO Shahar Azulay, the company reports having tripled annual recurring revenue and doubled its global headcount in the year prior to the raise [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-29, groundcover announced a $100 million Series C led by One Peak at a reported $500 million valuation, bringing total funding to $160 million [^1][^2][^3]. The capital supports go-to-market expansion in North America and deeper AI capabilities in the product roadmap [^1][^2].
