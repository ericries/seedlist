## About

Mariana Minerals is a San Francisco-based, software-first vertically integrated critical minerals company that designs, constructs, and operates mines and refineries using its proprietary MarianaOS platform [^1][^2]. The system integrates capital project execution, refinery operations, and mine management through AI and machine learning, targeting a roughly 50% reduction in typical 5-10 year mining project timelines [^1]. The company operates Copper One in Utah (restarted 2025, targeting 50,000 metric tons annual production) and Lithium One in Texas (commercial production expected 2027), with a stated goal of developing 10 mineral projects over 10 years [^2][^3]. Founder Turner Caldwell previously led Tesla's metals and minerals team; CTO Juan Lozano scaled ML systems at Affirm and Kite; CFO Baker Tilney founded and scaled an integrated energy company [^3].
