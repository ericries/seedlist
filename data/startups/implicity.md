## About

Implicity is a Paris-based digital health company that builds an AI-driven cardiac remote monitoring platform, delivered as software as a medical device [^1][^2]. The universal platform aggregates and analyzes data across cardiac rhythm management and heart failure monitoring, independent of the underlying device manufacturer, and is used daily at more than 250 medical centers monitoring 120,000+ patients [^1]. The company was founded in 2016 by electrophysiologist Dr. Arnaud Rosier and has operated a US subsidiary from Cambridge, Massachusetts since 2022 [^1].

On September 9, 2026, Implicity announced a €35 million funding round led by IRIS with participation from Five Arrows, the alternative assets arm of Rothschild & Co [^1]. The company also disclosed a peer-reviewed study reporting a 26% reduction in mortality and a 4% reduction in hospitalizations among monitored patients [^1][^2].
