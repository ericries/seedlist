## About

Nof1 is an AI research lab training frontier models focused on financial markets [^1][^2][^3]. Its flagship experiment, Alpha Arena, pits frontier AI models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and xAI against each other in autonomous trading competitions using real capital [^1][^3]. The leadership team brings together entrepreneurs and researchers with backgrounds at DeepMind and Renaissance Technologies, among others [^1]. The $15M funding round announced May 15, 2026 was co-led by SUI Group Holdings (NASDAQ: SUIG, $3M check) and London-based Karatage Opportunities, a proprietary hedge fund focused on digital assets and emerging technology [^1][^2][^3].
