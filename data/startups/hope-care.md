## About

Hope Care is a Portuguese digital health company that develops regulated remote patient monitoring technology for chronic conditions [^1][^2]. Its OpMA platform enables healthcare organisations to design and deploy digital care programmes delivered through centralised remote care and telehealth centres [^1][^2]. The medical device received Class IIb certification under EU Regulation 2017/745 in October 2023 and the company holds ISO 13485:2016 and ISO 9001:2015 certifications [^2]. Hope Care partners with multiple Portuguese Local Health Units (ULS network), pharmaceutical companies, and international healthcare institutions across Europe [^1][^2].

On September 8, 2026, Hope Care closed a €6 million Series A round led by Iberis Capital with participation from Vieira de Matos – VDM Capital and existing shareholder Buenavista Equity Partners [^1][^2]. Proceeds are earmarked for expansion into Germany, France, Switzerland, and Romania [^1].
