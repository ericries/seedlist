## About

Peregrine Technologies is a San Francisco-based data platform serving state and local governments, with a focus on public safety and public works [^1]. The company's platform unifies data across agency systems and is used by more than 400 agencies and organizations across North America, serving over 125 million people [^1]. Customers include cities from San Francisco to Atlanta; in Kansas City, Peregrine has supported SAVE KC, a data-driven focused deterrence initiative credited with helping drive an 18% decline in violent crime [^1]. The platform has also been deployed for major events and emergency response, including coordination for the Super Bowl, Grammy Awards, and hurricane response operations [^1]. Peregrine was co-founded by Nick Noone (CEO) and Ben Rudolph (CTO) [^1].
