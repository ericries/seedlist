## About

Quaise Energy is a Houston-based deep-tech company developing millimeter-wave drilling technology to unlock superhot geothermal energy from depths in excess of a mile below the Earth's surface, where rock temperatures range from 400 to 1000 degrees centigrade [^1][^2]. The company's flagship demonstration, "Project Obsidian," is planned in Oregon and targets first grid power by 2030, with a hyperscaler already committed to 50 megawatts of the project output [^1][^2]. Quaise was spun out of MIT technology and is led by CEO Carlos Araque [^1][^2].
