## About

Alcolase is a Copenhagen-based biotech developing an enzyme-based supplement to mitigate symptoms of alcohol intolerance (flushing, increased heart rate, dizziness, nausea, headaches) caused by aldehyde dehydrogenase (ALDH2) deficiency, common among East Asian populations [^1][^2][^3]. The €1.5M seed announced May 18, 2026 was backed by Ada Ventures, Delphinus Venture Capital, Antler, and Manigoff Invest, alongside a group of business angels; proceeds will fund in vivo studies, technology development, intellectual property work, and early commercial partnerships starting in Singapore [^1][^2][^3]. The company has also established a UK therapeutic subsidiary with Ada Ventures' support [^2].
