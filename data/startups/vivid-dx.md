## About

Vivid Dx is an Oxford, UK-based precision diagnostics company developing a direct-from-blood platform that uses Raman spectroscopy and AI to detect pathogens and support phenotypic antimicrobial susceptibility testing (AST), with the goal of reducing diagnostic timelines from days to hours [^1][^2]. Its work is focused on improving treatment decisions for life-threatening infections such as sepsis while helping address antimicrobial resistance. On 2026-07-28, the company announced $15 million in seed financing co-led by Oxford Science Enterprises and Cedars-Sinai Intellectual Property Co., with participation from Jameel Investment Management Company and additional strategic investors [^1][^2]. Concurrent with the raise, Vivid appointed diagnostics executive Keith O'Neill as chief executive officer [^1][^2].
