## About

QuantumDiamonds is a Munich-based deep-tech company spun out of the Technical University of Munich in 2022 that applies quantum-sensing technology — specifically Quantum Diamond Microscopy (QDM), which uses nitrogen-vacancy centers in synthetic diamond — to inspect advanced semiconductor chips non-destructively across all chip layers [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-08 the company announced the close of a €91 million financing package combining a €15 million equity round led by climate-tech investor World Fund with €76 million in non-dilutive funding approved at EU level under the European Chips Act, jointly provided by the German Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs and Energy and the Free State of Bavaria [^1][^2][^3]. According to the company and TechCrunch, QuantumDiamonds is the first startup approved for manufacturing funding under the European Chips Act, joining industrial incumbents such as GlobalFoundries and Carl Zeiss [^1][^2]. The company says it is already engaged with nine of the world's top ten semiconductor manufacturers, opened a Taiwan hub in March 2026, and installed its first U.S. system at Eurofins EAG Laboratories in Sunnyvale, California, in April 2026 [^1].
