## About

Keyfactor is an Atlanta-headquartered enterprise trust infrastructure company that issues and manages machine identities and digital certificates for cybersecurity, cryptography, and post-quantum cryptography use cases [^1][^2]. According to the company, it supports more than 2,500 customers globally, including 50% of the largest banks in the U.S. and Europe, 80% of leading U.S. retailers, and over 40% of Fortune 100 companies [^1][^2]. Keyfactor is led by CEO Jordan Rackie and maintains a co-headquarters in Stockholm, Sweden [^1].
