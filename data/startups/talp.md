## About

Talp is a US-incorporated (Wilmington, Delaware) AI startup with Turkish roots that builds behavior simulation infrastructure — AI personas grounded in behavioral patterns and cognitive traits that can be deployed against a website, ad campaign, product page, or pricing test to predict what real customers will do before launch [^1][^2][^3]. The company positions its platform as an alternative to surveys and focus groups, targeting the "say-do gap" between what consumers claim they want and what they actually do [^1][^3]. Talp says it processes hundreds of campaigns per month across multiple sectors and was co-founded by Baran Ataş and Samet Alan [^2][^3]. The pre-seed round arrives in a category where rival AI-persona startup Aaru raised at a reported $1B valuation months earlier [^1].
