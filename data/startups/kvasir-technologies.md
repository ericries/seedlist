## About

Kvasir Technologies is a Copenhagen-based climate tech startup that produces a climate-neutral marine biofuel from non-edible lignin-based agricultural and forestry residues [^1]. The company is a spin-out of the Technical University of Denmark (DTU) and operates a test facility in Fredericia capable of producing up to two tonnes of biofuel per day [^1]. Its patented conversion technology yields a drop-in marine fuel compatible with existing ship engines and bunker infrastructure [^2].
