## About

Quobly is a French quantum computing startup industrializing silicon-based quantum computers. Spun out of CEA-Leti and CNRS, the company is headquartered in Grenoble and aims to bring its first commercial product to market by end of 2026 via its Alloy product line, beginning with Alloy Pioneer for HPC and research environments [^1][^2].
