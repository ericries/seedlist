## About

Alta is a Tel Aviv, Israel-based enterprise software company, founded in 2023 by Stav Levi-Neumark (CEO), Tom Hoffen, and Mor Shabtai, that builds an AI-native operating layer for revenue teams [^1][^2][^3]. The platform consolidates prospecting, research, outbound campaigns, inbound qualification, AI calling, and continuous optimisation into coordinated AI agents that share a single "Company Brain," integrating with 50+ data sources and 60+ go-to-market tools [^1][^2]. Alta says its customers include Snowflake, Deel, Atlassian, and Atoms [^1][^2].
