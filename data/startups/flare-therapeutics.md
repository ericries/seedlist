## About

Flare Therapeutics is a Cambridge, MA-based oncology biotech developing small-molecule degraders and modulators targeting transcription factors, a class historically considered undruggable [^1]. The company's lead program, FX-111, is a degrader of the androgen receptor (AR) targeting prostate cancer; the company received IND clearance and plans to initiate Phase 1A clinical development in Q3 2026 [^1]. Flare was founded by Abbie Celniker, Partner at Third Rock Ventures [^1].
