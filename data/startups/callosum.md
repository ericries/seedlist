## About

Callosum is a London-based AI infrastructure startup building software that routes AI workloads across heterogeneous chips and models, matching tasks to hardware based on cost, speed and energy efficiency [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2025 by Cambridge neuroscientists Danyal Akarca and Jascha Achterberg, the company frames its work as a counter-thesis to the "monoculture" bet that superintelligence will come from a single frontier model on identical chips [^1][^2].

On August 20, 2026, Callosum announced a $100 million (approximately €85.4 million) seed round led by Atomico, with participation from Plural, DCVC, the UK Sovereign AI Fund and angel investors [^1][^2][^3]. The round was widely characterised as one of the largest seed rounds ever raised in Europe [^1][^3]. It follows a $10.25 million seed round in February 2026 when the company came out of stealth, bringing total capital raised to over $110 million [^2][^3].
