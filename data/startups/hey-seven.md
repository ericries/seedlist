## About

Hey Seven is an AI-native player development platform built for casino and integrated resort operators [^1]. The system combines autonomous AI with operator-defined guardrails to help land-based, online, and omnichannel gaming operators identify premium and emerging premium players earlier and deliver personalized attention at scale [^1][^2]. Bettor Capital, a venture firm focused on the real-money online gaming sector, was the company's first institutional investor [^1][^3].
