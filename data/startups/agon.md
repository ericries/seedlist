## About

Agon is a London and Berlin-based defensetech company building a platform for designing, training, and iterating AI systems in synthetic battlefields, with an initial focus on training autonomous weapons systems including counter-drone systems to practice combat scenarios against adversaries [^1][^2]. The company launched from stealth on 2026-07-29 with $30 million in cumulative pre-seed and seed funding — a $7 million pre-seed tranche followed by a $23 million seed round backed by XYZ Venture Capital, Lux Capital, and Northzone, with existing pre-seed investors Lakestar, 201 Ventures, D3, and angel investor David Helgason (Unity Technologies founder, who chairs the company) participating [^1][^2]. Klaus Hommels of Lakestar joined the company's board as part of the round [^2]. Agon was co-founded by Tristam Constant (former British Army officer and Anduril director) and Junaid Hussain (co-founder of Cambridge Aerospace and Iconic) [^1].
