## About

Qorelo is a Berlin-based AI startup building an automation engine for enterprise resource planning (ERP) delivery, specifically targeting the SAP S/4HANA migration crunch ahead of SAP's 2027 deadline for corporate users [^1][^2][^3]. The company automates the functional delivery workstream of large ERP programmes, claiming to reduce project delivery timelines by up to 45% [^1][^2]. Qorelo was founded in late 2025 and counts a major German automotive enterprise (reported as Mercedes-Benz) among its early customers [^2][^3]. The global SAP application services market for S/4HANA transformation was valued at roughly €37.8 billion in 2025 and is projected to grow to €60.2 billion by 2030 [^1].
