## About

Pocket builds a $129 credit card-shaped device that attaches to the back of a smartphone and records and transcribes real-life conversations offline [^1]. The paid plan ($200/year) adds AI summaries and advanced features [^1]. The startup says it has sold over 130,000 units since launching in 2025 [^1]. Co-founder Akshay Narisetti was previously a founding member of AI wearable startup Omi; co-founder Gabriel Dymowski previously founded a blockchain document management startup [^1].
