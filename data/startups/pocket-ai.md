## About

Pocket builds a $129 credit card-shaped device that attaches to the back of a smartphone and records and transcribes real-life conversations offline [^1][^2]. The paid plan ($200/year) adds AI summaries and advanced features [^1]. The startup says it has sold over 130,000 units since launching in 2025 [^1], and Y Combinator's company page identifies it as part of the Winter 2026 batch [^3]. By March 2026, the company had shipped more than 35,000 units and reached a $27 million annualized revenue run rate, with monthly growth topping 50% in some months [^2]. Co-founder Akshay Narisetti was previously a founding member of AI wearable startup Omi; co-founder Gabriel Dymowski previously founded a blockchain document management startup [^1][^3].
