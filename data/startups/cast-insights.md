## About

Cast Insights is a real-time spoken intelligence platform that ingests public speech from television, radio, podcasts, and livestreams and converts it into structured, searchable data [^1]. Customers — hedge funds, policy teams, strategy groups, and newsrooms — use the platform to track emerging narratives, compare statements across time and sources, and receive alerts when meaningful statements are made [^1][^2].
