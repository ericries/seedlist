## About

Syntetica is a French materials-recycling startup building a chemical process that can break down both Nylon 6 and Nylon 6,6 from mixed textile waste and re-polymerise them into virgin-grade pellets for yarn manufacturing [^1]. Co-founders Marco Bertone (CEO, previously in fashion and second-hand e-commerce), chemistry researcher Louis Monsigny and CTO Ash Ward (former Northvolt) started the company to address nylon's status as one of the least-recycled synthetic fibres in the apparel supply chain [^1]. Syntetica has partnerships with Lululemon, Victoria's Secret, Etam and Michelin's Centre for Sustainable Materials, and plans to scale its process from lab to pre-industrial output using the Series A capital [^1].
