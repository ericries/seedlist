## About

Gyver is a European AI-powered workforce platform for industrial and energy sectors, focused on recruiting and upskilling skilled blue-collar trades — starting with electricians — using conversational AI to match workers with employers [^1]. The company is positioning around the shortage of skilled trades workers needed for Europe's electrification and renewable-energy buildout [^1]. On May 13, 2026, Gyver announced a €1.4M pre-seed round led by Brighteye with participation from āltitude, Vento Ventures, Zanichelli Venture, existing investor Antler, and several business angels [^1].
