## About

Zero is a Helsinki-based go-to-market operating system built for the AI era, positioned as a replacement for traditional CRMs and adjacent sales tooling [^1][^2]. The platform organises GTM work into four modules — Prospect (lead discovery from buying signals), Close (deal management), Grow (retention and expansion), and Scale (AI-agent automation) — and integrates with tools such as HubSpot and Slack [^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by Tuomo Riekki, a co-founder of Smartly.io, and Santtu Koivumäki, who previously led go-to-market at Smartly.io [^1].

On September 15, 2026, Zero announced a €8.93 million (approximately $10.3 million) seed round led by Primary Venture Partners [^1][^2][^3]. Participants included Inception Fund, Defiant, Greens, the founders of Lovable, Supercell, Langdock, Silo AI, and Depict, and earlier backers 20VC and James Hawkins (PostHog co-founder), plus operator funds backed by executives from Spotify, Wolt, Shopify, Dropbox, Meta, and OpenAI [^1][^3]. Individual angels named in the round include Fabian Hedin, Ilkka Paananen (Supercell), and Peter Sarlin (Silo AI) [^3]. The round follows a €2.34 million ($2.7 million) pre-seed in December 2024 led by 20VC [^1].
