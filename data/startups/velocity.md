## About

Velocity is a London-based enterprise stablecoin platform for corporate treasury and settlement operations, founded in May 2025 by payments industry veterans Eric Queathem (CEO) and Tom Greenwood [^1][^2]. The platform enables CFOs and treasury teams to hold, move, and settle funds using stablecoin infrastructure combined with traditional banking rails, compliance, custody, and liquidity management, routing international payments over local bank rails and blockchain networks to enable instant settlement outside of traditional banking hours [^1][^2][^3].

On 2026-07-14 Velocity announced a $38 million Series A co-led by Dragonfly Capital and FirstMark, with participation from Activant Capital and new investors Capital One Ventures, QED Investors, Coinbase Ventures, Wintermute Ventures, and Ripple [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports it has now raised nearly $50 million since launch, including a prior $10 million pre-seed round and an undisclosed strategic investment from Dragonfly in October [^2]. Proceeds will fund global expansion of the banking and payments network, product development, and regulatory capabilities [^1][^2][^3].
