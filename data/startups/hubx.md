## About

HubX is a Türkiye-based AI-native consumer app studio that operates more than 40 mobile and web products spanning generative AI, education, and health and fitness — collectively generating over 600 million downloads across 190+ countries since its 2022 founding [^1][^2][^3]. On August 28, 2026, HubX announced up to $75 million in financing from Point72 Private Investments — a $50 million initial commitment with an option for an additional $25 million — at a $1.2 billion pre-money valuation, its first external round and the transaction that made it Türkiye's eighth unicorn and first AI-native consumer applications unicorn [^1][^2][^3]. The company has grown to more than 370 team members across Izmir and Istanbul and will deploy the proceeds to accelerate its existing portfolio and pursue global acquisitions of promising mobile and web products [^1][^2].
