## About

Spectro Cloud is a San Jose, California-based AI infrastructure management company whose PaletteAI platform helps platform teams and cloud providers build, govern, operate, and scale AI infrastructure across GPU clusters, AI factories, and distributed inference environments spanning enterprise, public sector, neocloud, and sovereign cloud settings [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2019 and led by CEO Tenry Fu, the company provides Kubernetes-based tooling that supports multi-silicon and multi-model portability across heterogeneous environments [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-15 Spectro Cloud announced an oversubscribed $100 million Series D led by Growth Equity at Goldman Sachs Alternatives, with strategic participation from AMD Ventures, Ericsson, LG Technology Ventures, and Maximus [^1][^2][^3]. The round brings total capital raised to approximately $260 million [^1][^2]. Proceeds will fund product development, expansion into sovereign and neocloud markets, and support for enterprise customers moving AI workloads into production [^1][^2][^3].
