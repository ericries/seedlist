## About

Traysar is an Austin, Texas-based defense technology company building autonomous "subterra" platforms for the subterranean domain [^1][^2]. The company emerged from stealth on June 16, 2026 at the 2026 Reindustrialize Summit in Detroit, describing itself as the world's first subterranean defense tech company [^1][^3]. Traysar is developing two initial systems: an excavator-class autonomous tunnel breaching platform designed to navigate and map underground networks, and a high-speed autonomous burrowing platform for creating subsurface access points and payload delivery [^1][^2]. The engineering team includes early SpaceX and The Boring Company engineers, and the company was co-founded by CEO Yadin Soffer alongside Asher Katz and Gilad Adin [^1][^3].
