## About

Enigma is a physical AI company building foundation models and intuitive interfaces to make robots easier to control and deploy across hardware [^1][^2]. The company emerged from stealth on 2026-07-27 with a $71 million seed round co-led by Index Ventures and Ribbit Capital, with participation from Conviction Partners and angel investors including leaders from OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepMind, xAI, Cognition, and Wiz [^1][^2][^3]. Founded less than a year prior by Jonathan Jacobi and Gal Niv, Enigma operates robot hangars in Israel and California and launched robots.online as the first public interactive AI robotics experience, where users can remotely control robots to perform tasks like drawing, sword fighting, and chemistry experiments [^1][^2][^3]. The company already partners with organizations in healthcare, logistics, and entertainment [^1].
