## About

ProducePay operates a digital produce platform connecting growers, distributors, and retailers, providing financing and market access across the fresh produce supply chain [^1][^2]. The Los Angeles-based company is led by CEO Patrick McCullough and was founded by Pablo Borquez Schwarzbeck [^1][^3]. In its September 2026 round, ProducePay announced a strategic shift from a capital-intensive model toward a data platform, coupling $140M+ in new equity with a new lending fund that could ultimately reach $300M — a structure that allows the company to advance roughly $1B to growers annually while retaining minimal capital exposure [^1][^2].
