## About

ProducePay operates a digital produce platform connecting growers, distributors, and retailers, providing financing and market access across the fresh produce supply chain [^1]. The Los Angeles-based company is led by CEO Patrick McCullough and was founded by Pablo Borquez Schwarzbeck [^1][^2]. In its September 2026 round, ProducePay announced a strategic shift from a capital-intensive model toward a data platform, with a facility structure designed to advance roughly $1 billion to growers annually while retaining minimal capital exposure [^1].
