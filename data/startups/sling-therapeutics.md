## About

Sling Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing linsitinib, an oral small-molecule IGF-1R inhibitor being advanced through the Phase 3 ORBIT pivotal trial for adults with moderate-to-severe active thyroid eye disease (TED) [^1][^2]. The company's September 2026 Series C financing is intended to advance Phase 3 development [^1][^2]. Linsitinib has received FDA Fast Track Designation and demonstrated efficacy on eye bulging (proptosis) in prior Phase 2b/3 work [^1].
