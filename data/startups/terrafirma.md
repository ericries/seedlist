## About

TerraFirma is an Austin, Texas-based, vertically integrated construction technology company building semi-autonomous heavy equipment systems for robotic earthworks and site operations [^1][^2][^3]. The company was co-founded by CEO Noah Schochet and CTO Noah McGuinness, both former SpaceX engineers [^1][^2]. TerraFirma's platform combines proprietary hardware, software, and AI to enable a small team of remote operators to run fleets of construction machines across projects spanning housing, energy, transportation, manufacturing, and education [^2][^3].
