## About

Benji is a Chicago- and New York-based universal API for loyalty partnerships, enabling brands across travel, hospitality, retail, food, and fintech to connect their loyalty programs through a single integration [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Nick Anastasiades, Jon Elron, and Arik Gaisler; its growing network includes JetBlue's loyalty program along with Cook Unity, 1-800-Flowers, and Chip City, representing more than 50 million combined active members [^1][^2].
