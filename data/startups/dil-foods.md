## About

Dil Foods is a Bengaluru-based full-stack virtual restaurant enabling platform founded in 2022 by Arpita Aditi to support small and mid-sized food businesses through a shared kitchen, technology and brand-portfolio model [^1][^2]. The company currently operates nine regional food brands across six cities and covers 340 pincodes, and announced a ₹72 crore Series B round on May 13, 2026, led by Bikaji Foods Family Office with participation from V3 Ventures, MJV Ventures and Alteria Capital, bringing total funding to over ₹113 crore [^1][^2][^3]. The company intends to use the capital to scale to 600 locations by FY28 and reach an annualized revenue of ₹500 crore [^1][^3].
