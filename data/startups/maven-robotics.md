## About

Maven Robotics is a Santa Clara, California-based robotics company developing autonomous industrial robots designed to take on end-to-end tasks rather than solving isolated problems, co-founded by brothers Hamza Derbas (CEO) and Khalid Derbas (CFO) [^1]. On 2026-09-10, TechCrunch reported that Maven had raised a $100 million Series A round led by RoboStrategy, with participation from LocalGlobe, Vine Ventures, and XTX Markets Ventures [^1].
