## About

Proxy Foods AI is a Washington, DC-based food and beverage R&D platform built around an agentic AI stack that integrates ingredient, nutritional, sensory, regulatory, and cost data with natural language interfaces to help food and beverage companies rapidly create, reformulate, and scale recipes [^1][^2][^3]. The company launched its enterprise platform to accelerate product development cycles across food science, regulatory, and commercialization workflows [^2][^3].
