## About

Gimlet Labs is an AI infrastructure company building what it calls "the first multisilicon cloud, built from the ground up for inference performance" — a serverless inference platform that distributes workloads across heterogeneous accelerator hardware (GPUs, dataflow architectures, CPUs) to optimize AI inference throughput and latency [^1]. The company's Gimlet Cloud runs "everything from simple agents to complex multi-agent systems," and its kforge system "autonomously generates optimized low-level kernels from PyTorch" across CUDA, ROCm, and Metal backends [^2]. Gimlet reports it has scaled to "hundreds of megawatts in managed capacity" and added "billions in contracted revenue" since its Series A five months prior [^1].
