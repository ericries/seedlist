## About

Gimlet Labs is a San Francisco-based AI infrastructure startup building tooling and platforms for AI systems. In early September 2026 the company announced a $300 million Series B led by Andreessen Horowitz at a reported $3 billion valuation, bringing total funding to approximately $392 million [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by Zain Asgar, Michelle Nguyen, Omid Azizi, James Bartlett, and Natalie Serrino, and is headquartered in San Francisco [^2].
