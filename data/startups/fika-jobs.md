## About

Fika Jobs is a Stockholm-based video-first hiring platform where AI agents (powered by Google Gemini) conduct interviews with candidates and produce short-form video profiles employers browse. The model charges job seekers nothing and takes 10% of successful hires' first-year salaries from employers [^1].
