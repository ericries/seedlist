## About

Quantum Systems is a Munich-area (Gilching) developer of AI-enabled autonomous aerial systems for defence and security applications, best known for its Vector, Trinity, Reliant and Twister uncrewed platforms and the MOSAIC UXS software ecosystem that connects them [^1][^2][^3]. Co-founded by Florian Seibel and Sven Kruck, the company has grown into one of Europe's largest defence-tech businesses by revenue [^1][^2]. With the Series D, Quantum Systems said it will accelerate the transition from individual uncrewed platforms to an interoperable family of systems, expand production capacity, strengthen supply-chain resilience, scale delivery across allied markets, and continue investing in software and AI capabilities [^1][^2][^3][^4].
