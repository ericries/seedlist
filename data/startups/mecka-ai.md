## About

Mecka AI is a New York City-based embodied AI startup that trains robots using human motion data collected through body sensors and iPhones [^1]. Rather than relying on teleoperation (humans manually controlling robots), Mecka gathers physical motion data from humans — including hand gestures, walking gaits, and other movements — to develop embodied AI models [^1]. The company was founded in 2024 by CEO Josh Gao alongside co-founders Mogen Cheng, Jason Chong, and Duy Nguyen [^1]. Mecka projects an annual run rate of $100 million based on existing contracts and employs roughly 40 people [^1].
