## About

Clarion Health is a conversational AI platform for healthcare practices that automates scheduling, referrals, prescription refills, and patient communications, describing itself as "the front office that runs itself" [^1][^2]. Founded in 2024 by Ryan Gallagher and Jeffrey Lamothe, the company reports metrics including a 71% reduction in no-shows and 60% of calls resolved end-to-end, and emphasizes its status as a physician-led company in its category [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-21, Clarion Health announced a $10 million seed round led by Accel with participation from Y Combinator [^1].
