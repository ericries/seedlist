## About

Clarion Health (a Y Combinator W24 company; corporate entity name Anaphero) is a New York City-based conversational-AI platform for healthcare practices that automates scheduling, referrals, prescription refills, and patient communications, describing itself as "the AI front office for healthcare" [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2024 by Ryan Gallagher, MD (CEO), a Stanford/Harvard-trained physician previously on the founding teams of Two Chairs and Ophelia (YC W20), and Jeffrey Lamothe (CTO), who previously led voice AI work at Amazon Alexa and AI/ML teams at Salesforce, the company positions itself as physician-led and reports serving "tens of thousands of patients monthly" across virtual care providers, health systems, and major insurers [^2][^3]. Reported product metrics include a 71% reduction in no-shows and 60% of inbound calls resolved end-to-end [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-21, Clarion Health announced a $10 million seed round led by Accel with participation from Y Combinator; per Clarion's own site, additional backers include Sequoia (scout) and operator angels from Ophelia, Medallion, and Counsel Health [^1][^2][^3].
