## About

Biotia is a clinical diagnostics company that combines metagenomic sequencing with AI to identify pathogens in recurrent and complicated infections [^1][^2]. The company offers BIOTIA-ID (metagenomic diagnostic for urogenital and joint infections), BIOTIA-DX (software for isolate identification and outbreak detection), and GeoSeeq (a cloud-based genomics platform used by 100+ organizations globally) [^2]. Biotia collaborates with institutions including The Rockefeller Foundation, Cornell Tech, Mayo Clinic Platform, Weill Cornell Medicine, and SUNY Downstate [^2].
