## About

Tangos AI is a Tel Aviv-based startup building autonomous AI agents that carry out end-to-end financial-crime investigations, producing evidence-backed, regulator-ready case files across sanctions, fraud, and compliance workflows [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2025 by Eyal Azoulay, a serial entrepreneur whose prior three companies exited (including one acquired by BNY); the team includes former U.S. Treasury Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) officials, veterans of Israel's intelligence and security community, and AI specialists who previously led large-scale autonomous systems projects [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-07, Tangos announced a $20 million Seed round at a $100 million valuation, led by Red Dot Capital Partners, with participation from Leaders Fund, Clarim, Venture Israel, SignalFire, Clutch VC, and Selah Ventures, plus a strategic investment from Bright Data [^1][^2][^3][^4].
