## About

Karoo Health is an AI-native cardiovascular technology platform functioning as an operating system for cardiac care [^1][^2]. The platform connects clinical, claims, pharmacy, and patient data across payers, providers, and patients through unified data and workflow infrastructure, enabling earlier risk detection, care coordination, and improved cardiovascular disease outcomes [^1][^2]. Karoo supports more than 600 cardiology providers across 11 states [^2]. The company previously raised a $3.4M seed round in 2023 [^3].

Proceeds will further develop Karoo's AI-native cardiovascular platform — including predictive models and clinical signal detection — and expand engineering, clinical teams, and payer and provider deployments [^1][^2]. As part of the financing, Lee Shapiro (Managing Partner, 7wire Ventures) and Jeff Stolte (Managing Partner, Allumia Ventures) joined Karoo's Board of Directors [^1][^2].
