## About

Scout Space is a Northern Virginia-based space domain awareness (SDA) company building optical and sensor hardware and software for tracking and characterizing objects in geostationary orbit and beyond [^1][^2][^3]. The company's flagship Owl sensor will fly on the inaugural mission of Blue Origin's Blue Ring spacecraft for payload hosting and on-orbit services [^1][^2]. The $18M Series A announced May 6, 2026 was led by Washington Harbour Partners with participation from existing investors Noblis Ventures, Fusion Fund, Decisive Point, and the Virginia Innovation Partnership Corporation; capital will fund a new 2,600-square-foot manufacturing facility in Northern Virginia and grow the team from ~30 to 50+ over the next 18 months [^1][^2][^3].
