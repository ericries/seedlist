## About

OuterSignal is a New York-based agentic customer intelligence platform that enriches consumer brand customer data with demographic, occupational, and behavioral insights to help brands personalize marketing and detect VIP buyers [^1] [^3]. The platform integrates with Shopify, Klaviyo, Meta, and other ecommerce tools, and serves hundreds of consumer brands including AG1, HexClad, Jones Road Beauty, True Classic, Jomashop, Lucy, Gratsi, and Magic Mind [^1] [^3]. The company was co-founded by Zach Zelner (CEO) and Noah Friedman (President) and launched its product in January 2026 [^1].
