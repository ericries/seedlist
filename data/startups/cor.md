## About

COR is an AI-powered project profitability platform built for agencies and professional services firms — combining project management, time tracking, resource planning, budget monitoring, and profitability analysis in a single system [^1][^2]. Founded in 2017 by Santi Bibiloni, Jose Gettas, and Gabriel Marin, COR currently serves teams across 38 countries, including marketing agencies, consultancies, law firms, accounting firms, and software companies [^1][^2].
