## About

Exein is a Rome-based cybersecurity company building what it calls a Physical AI security platform, protecting intelligent machines — robots, drones, autonomous vehicles, and industrial systems that use AI to perceive, decide, and act in the physical world [^1][^2][^3]. The company operates across industrial automation, automotive, energy, healthcare, semiconductors, aerospace, and robotics; H1 2026 ARR grew roughly 4x year-over-year, and its network sees approximately 5,000 new non-repetitive attacks weekly [^1]. Exein was founded by Gianni Cuozzo (CEO), Gerardo Gagliardo (CFO), and Giovanni Alberto Falcione (CTO) [^2].
