## About

Respond.io is a Kuala Lumpur-based customer conversation management platform for mid-market B2C businesses. The platform unifies messaging and calling channels (WhatsApp, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Telegram, Line, WeChat, voice calls, and live chat) and layers AI agents on top to handle high-volume customer inquiries, qualify leads, and close sales [^1][^2]. The company serves over 10,000 businesses across 180+ countries and processes more than 2 billion messages quarterly [^2]. Respond.io targets verticals where customer conversations precede purchases, including healthcare, automotive, retail, education, and travel [^1]. The company reported $35M ARR with 169% year-over-year growth and a 30% profit margin at the time of its Series B [^1].
