## About

OLIX is a London-based AI chip startup building infrastructure for frontier AI inference [^1][^2]. Its first product, the DX-1, is a decode accelerator designed for AI inference workloads and is part of OLIX's X-1 platform, which distributes AI models across multiple chips to improve performance and energy efficiency [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by James Dacombe and plans to bring its first AI systems to customers by H2 2027 [^1][^2].
