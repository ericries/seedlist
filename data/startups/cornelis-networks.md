## About

Cornelis Networks builds Active Compute Fabric, an open interconnect for AI and HPC clusters that targets the fact that much GPU time is wasted waiting for data to arrive [^1][^2]. Spun out of Intel's high-performance interconnect business in 2020 as the successor to the Omni-Path lineage, the company positions its architecture as GPU- and accelerator-agnostic, competing with Nvidia's networking stack [^1][^2].
