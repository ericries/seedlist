## About

Inner Logic is a Baltimore-based medical technology company (formerly Semaphor) building AI-powered simulation infrastructure for procedural medical devices [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2026 by CEO Tito Porras, MD, CTO Mathias Unberath, PhD, and Chief Robotics Officer Axel Krieger, PhD, the platform generates virtual patients from real clinical data, allowing device manufacturers to test and validate surgical systems in virtual environments before clinical use [^1][^3]. The founders draw an explicit parallel to the simulation infrastructure that enabled autonomous vehicles, arguing that autonomous surgery requires similar tooling to move development and testing from expensive physical trials into simulation [^1][^3].
