## About

Nuclear Turbines is a Manchester-based deeptech startup developing compact, cost-effective advanced nuclear power systems that replace conventional steam turbines at the heart of a nuclear plant with high-temperature turbine technology borrowed from jet engines and gas-fired power stations [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-27, the company announced a £15 million (~€17.5 million) foundational round led by IQ Capital, with participation from Rhapsody Venture Partners, Zero Carbon Capital, and Empirical Ventures [^1][^2][^3]. Nuclear Turbines was co-founded by Jeremy Owston, a former BAE Systems Principal Engineer, and Tim Abram, a nuclear engineer and professor at the University of Manchester, in partnership with Empirical Ventures' venture studio [^1][^2]. The company claims its approach could generate electricity at one-fifth the cost of today's nuclear plants — bringing nuclear prices below those of fossil fuels [^1]. Capital will be used to advance and validate the reactor design, build large-scale test rigs, and expand the team as the company prepares to manufacture its first fuel elements [^1].
