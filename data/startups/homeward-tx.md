## About

Homeward is an Austin, Texas-based proptech company that helps homeowners buy a new home before selling their existing one, or obtain cash offers for their current property [^1]. The company offers a bundled suite of Homeward Offer (cash home buying), Homeward Mortgage, and Homeward Title services [^2]. Founded in 2018 by Tim Heyl, Homeward operates as a fully remote company serving customers coast-to-coast across the contiguous United States [^2].
