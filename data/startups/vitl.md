## About

VITL is a Nashville-based e-prescribing platform launched in late 2024 by founder and CEO Charlie Jordan that connects cash-pay medical clinics to a network of verified 503A and 503B compounding pharmacies — enabling real-time price comparison, multi-pharmacy ordering, and patient tracking for prescriptions including GLP-1 medications [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports more than 630 clinics and 1,000+ prescribers on its platform, eight-figure ARR within two years of launch, and 90%+ quarter-over-quarter order growth [^2][^3]. The $7.5M Series A announced March 26, 2026 was led by SignalFire — which sourced the deal through its data-driven sourcing platform without an outbound pitch — and will fund pharmacy network expansion, product development, team scaling, and interoperability work [^1][^2][^3].
