## About

Pearl Diagnostics is a Baltimore-based biotech and Johns Hopkins University spinout founded in 2014 (previously operating as MycoMed Technologies, LLC and MycoMedDx Inc.), developing noninvasive fungal infection tests including a urine-based diagnostic for aspergillosis [^1][^2]. CEO Eric Gornstein — who joined in fall 2025 — noted "It's extraordinarily rare for a company at the Series A level to have a commercial product," highlighting the company's advanced commercial stage [^1]. The Series A proceeds will fund the US commercial launch following recent FDA clearance and support expansion into Europe, where Pearl has secured CE approval [^1]. The company is headquartered at 1812 Ashland Avenue, Suite G32, Baltimore, MD 21205 [^2]. Its board of directors includes Kieren Marr, Brad Spellberg, John Cammack, and Eric Gornstein [^2].

Pearl Diagnostics raised $11,150,000 (reported by Technical.ly as $11.2M) in a Regulation D Rule 506(b) exempt equity offering [^1][^2]. Per the Form D, the first sale date was March 19, 2026; the filing was signed by CEO Eric Gornstein on April 2, 2026 and submitted to the SEC on April 21, 2026, with public coverage by Technical.ly on April 24, 2026 [^1][^2]. The round was fully subscribed at the $11.15M target with 21 accredited investors and a $56,544 minimum investment per investor [^2]. No lead investor or participating firms were publicly disclosed in the filing or in press coverage [^1][^2].
