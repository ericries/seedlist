## About

Scope is a London-based AI workflow platform transforming inspections for the testing, inspection, and certification (TIC) industry, founded in 2024 by Jonathan Low and Jakob Cassiman [^1][^2][^3]. Inspectors from 6 of the top 10 global inspection companies use the platform; ARR has grown 9x since launch in July 2025 with a 100% pilot conversion rate [^1][^2]. The €17.3M (~$20M) Series A announced May 22, 2026 was led by Index Ventures with participation from Susa Ventures, Entrepreneurs First, Syndicate 1, and angels Mehdi Ghissassi (Ai71, ex-DeepMind), Cedric Pech (MongoDB), Naren Shaam (Omio), and Andy Szybalski (Microsoft AI) [^1][^2][^3].
