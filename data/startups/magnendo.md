## About

Magnendo is a Newton, Massachusetts-based MIT spinout developing magnetic robotic navigation technology for neuro-endovascular intervention [^1][^2][^3]. Founded by Yoonho Kim, Ph.D., the company is building a robotic system that combines magnetically steerable guidewires with robotic control, advanced medical imaging, and intelligent navigation software to improve endovascular access [^1][^2]. The platform is designed to help physicians navigate complex vascular anatomy more quickly and reliably while remaining compatible with existing clinical workflows and commercially available devices [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-24 Magnendo announced the closing of an $18M Series A led by Penumbra, Inc., with participation from a syndicate of new and existing institutional investors [^1][^2][^3]. The financing will support productization of Magnendo's robotic platform and preparation for first-in-human clinical studies [^1][^2]. The technology addresses a significant bottleneck in stroke treatment: while mechanical thrombectomy has transformed treatment of acute ischemic stroke, navigating from the vascular access site to the clot through complex patient anatomy contributes to prolonged procedure times and poorer outcomes [^1][^2].
