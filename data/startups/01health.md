## About

01Health is a London-based healthtech company founded in 2022 by Dr. Sonia Szamocki, a former NHS doctor and BCG consultant, building an infrastructure platform that lets specialist healthcare services be delivered through local clinics rather than concentrated in hospitals and major healthcare centres [^1][^2]. The company describes its model as bringing "Harley Street to the high street" [^1][^2].
