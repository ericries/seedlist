## About

4AM is a New York-based skincare startup founded by college friends Jade Beguelin (former Wall Street derivatives trader) and Sabrina Sade (Georgetown-trained MD) [^1][^2]. Its hero product Clean Sheets is a credit-card-sized, individually wrapped facial wipe in a micellar base with centella asiatica, calendula, vitamin B5, and glycerin, marketed as a single-step cleanser/toner/treatment [^1]. The oversubscribed $4M+ Seed round announced May 11, 2026 was led by CAVU Consumer Partners with participation from B4 Capital, Type Capital, and strategic angels including Joe Cloyes (Youth to the People), Cassandra Grey (Violet Grey), Brian Bordainick (Starface), Dr. Muneeb Shah (Remedy Science), Dr. Joyce Park (Kerativ), Maggie Sellers Reum, and Michelle Hu [^1][^2]. Capital will fund the launch into 1,745 Target stores nationwide and operational scaling [^1][^2].
