## About

Eight Sleep is a New York-based consumer sleep technology and predictive-health company founded in 2014 by Matteo Franceschetti (CEO), Massimo Andreasi Bassi (CTO), and Alex Zatarain — best known for the Pod, a temperature-regulating mattress cover that tracks sleep and adjusts in real time [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports free-cash-flow positivity in 2025 after launching three new products (Pod 5, Pod Pillow Cover, Thermal Blanket), expanding to 34 countries, and publishing two peer-reviewed studies showing the Pod reduces menopausal hot flashes by 56% [^1][^2]. The $50M strategic round announced March 4, 2026 was led solely by Tether Investments at a $1.5B valuation, following a $100M growth round in August 2025 (from HSG, Valor Equity Partners, Founders Fund, and Y Combinator) at a $1B valuation; the new capital will fund expansion into predictive AI-driven health, FDA approval work for sleep-apnea detection, and additional international markets [^1][^2][^3].
