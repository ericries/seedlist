## About

SambaNova Systems is a Palo Alto-based AI chip and systems company building purpose-built inference infrastructure for enterprise deployment of large-scale AI models [^1]. Its SN40L accelerator and next-generation SN50 (announced February 2026) are designed for on-premises AI inference at institutional scale [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded in 2017 by CEO Rodrigo Liang together with Stanford professors Kunle Olukotun and Christopher Ré [^2].

The Series F values SambaNova at $11 billion post-money and represents a first close, with additional investors expected to participate in a second close [^1]. Intel has backed the company since its Series C and now co-develops products with SambaNova [^1]. JPMorgan Chase was named alongside the round as an inference-infrastructure partner deploying SambaNova's SN40L and SN50 systems for on-premises AI inference [^1].
