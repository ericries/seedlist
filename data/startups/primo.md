## About

Primo is a Paris-based AI platform that unifies device management, SaaS applications, identity, and IT support, deploying autonomous AI agents to execute end-to-end IT operations such as employee onboarding, offboarding, and access management [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by CEO Martin Pannier, the company reports more than 400 customers across 10 countries, including ASL Airlines and Attio, and about €3.4M in annual recurring revenue with 4x year-over-year growth [^1]. On 2026-09-22, Primo announced a €6.9M ($8M) round led by Headline and Global Founders Capital, with participation from Arthur Waller (Pennylane co-founder/CEO) and Romain Niccoli (Pigment co-founder/CEO) [^1][^2].
