## About

Armadin is an AI-powered cybersecurity startup founded by Kevin Mandia (previously founder of Mandiant, acquired by Google for $5.4B in 2022) along with co-founders Travis Lanham, Evan Pena, and David Slater [^1][^3]. The company deploys "always-on agentic swarms" that continuously chain together vulnerabilities across external assets, cloud infrastructure, identity systems, internal networks, and applications — a shift from traditional point-in-time penetration testing [^1][^3]. The product, Armadin Red, delivers "validated kill chains" that show vulnerability reachability and exploitability with remediation guidance [^3]. In one controlled engagement, 26,000 agents performed 17 million actions across three days, uncovering 38 valid attack paths [^2].
