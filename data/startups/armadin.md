## About

Armadin is an AI-powered cybersecurity startup founded by Kevin Mandia (previously founder of Mandiant, acquired by Google for $5.4B in 2022). The company deploys "always-on agentic swarms" that continuously chain together vulnerabilities to test enterprise defenses — a shift from traditional point-in-time penetration testing [^1]. Armadin's goal is to help organizations identify and remediate security weaknesses before malicious actors or rogue AI agents can exploit them [^1].
