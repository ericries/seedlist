## About

UNIT AI is a San Francisco-based physical AI company building modular, AI-powered warehouse automation for retailers and third-party logistics (3PL) providers. Its platform is designed to automate direct-to-consumer inventory management and returns without requiring companies to redesign existing facilities, operating in spaces as small as 1,000 square feet [^1][^2]. The company was founded by warehouse automation veterans Guy Glass (previously founder of Caja Robotics, acquired by Fives) and Avihou Barkay (President of Plus One Robotics and GM of Caja Robotics) [^1].
