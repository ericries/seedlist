## About

WhiteHawk Minerals (NYSE: WHK), formerly known as WhiteHawk Income, is a Philadelphia-based natural gas mineral and royalty company. It owns natural gas mineral and royalty interests in the Marcellus and Haynesville Shales in the Appalachian and Haynesville Basins, with an economic interest in approximately 13% of all natural gas produced in the United States [^1][^2]. The company holds positions across approximately 3.4 million gross drilling spacing unit acres [^2].

WhiteHawk was founded by Daniel Herz, who previously served as Founder, President and CEO of Falcon Minerals Corporation (NASDAQ: FLMN) from August 2018 to June 2021 [^3]. PhiCap Advisors served as co-founders and equity partners; Jeffrey Slotterback, a PhiCap partner, is CFO and co-founder of WhiteHawk [^3][^4]. For the year ended December 31, 2025, the company reported revenue of $67.6 million and a net loss of $3.59 million [^5].

The upsized IPO priced 7,700,000 shares of Class A common stock at $26.00 (the midpoint of the $25-$27 range), with underwriters granted a 30-day option for up to an additional 1,155,000 shares [^1][^6]. Shares began trading on the NYSE under the ticker "WHK" on June 9, 2026 [^1]. Cornerstone investors Horizon Kinetics and T. Rowe Price indicated on $74 million of the IPO, representing approximately 37% of the deal [^2]. Additional bookrunners included Capital One Securities and Stephens Inc., with Tuohy Brothers Investment Research as co-manager [^6].
