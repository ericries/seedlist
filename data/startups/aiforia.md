## About

Aiforia is a Helsinki-based life-science technology company that develops deep-learning AI software for digital pathology, helping pathologists analyze ultra-high-resolution biopsy images to detect tumors and diagnose disease [^1][^2]. The company is the leading European provider of CE-IVD marked AI-powered solutions for digital pathology and operates subsidiaries in the United States and France [^1]. Aiforia's platform accelerates and standardizes tissue analysis workflows used in clinical diagnostics, drug discovery, and pre-clinical research [^1].

| 2026-08-03 | Venture Debt | €20M | European Investment Bank | — [^1][^2] |

The financing is structured as venture debt under the European Commission's InvestEU programme [^1][^2]. According to disclosure filings, the €20M facility is drawn in three tranches subject to revenue milestones within 36 months of the binding agreement, with a three-year grace period, seven-year maturity, and synthetic warrants granted to the EIB [^3]. Proceeds will accelerate Aiforia's product development for AI-powered cancer detection and expand its commercial operations [^1][^2].
