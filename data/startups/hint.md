## About

Hint is a New York-based AI-native home management platform co-founded by Martha Stewart, Yih-Han Ma and Kyle Rush, designed as an always-on, trusted interface for homeowners to manage and optimize every facet of the home, from maintenance and contractor coordination to preventive care [^1][^2][^3]. The company announced a $10 million seed round on May 13, 2026, led by Slow Ventures with participation from Montauk Capital, Tusk Venture Partners, Amplo, Energy Impact Partners, Hannah Grey VC, Brian Kelly and others [^1][^2][^3]. Hint plans to publicly launch in summer 2026 [^2][^3].
