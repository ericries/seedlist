## About

Poindexter Labs is a UK-based AI data company that produces expert-level training data for advanced AI models [^1][^2]. The company operates a proprietary platform enabling collaborative workflows where subject-matter experts — including mathematicians, scientists, lawyers, doctors, finance professionals, and engineers — generate and validate training data for frontier AI labs and enterprise customers [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Jocelyn D'Arcy, formerly a mathematics teacher, who bootstrapped the business to £1.6M in revenue before raising venture capital [^3]. Poindexter Labs officially launched at Maths World in London on June 3, 2026 [^2].
