## About

ENGO is a Grenoble, France-based sports technology company founded in 2019 that designs and manufactures ultra-lightweight augmented reality (AR) smart eyewear for endurance athletes, including runners, cyclists, and triathletes [^1][^2][^3]. The company is led by CEO Éric Marcellin-Dibon and COO Fabrice Berger Duquene, and reports that it already generates 90% of its revenue outside France, with roughly half of that coming from the United States [^1][^2].
