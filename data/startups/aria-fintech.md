## About

Aria is a Paris-based embedded invoice financing platform for B2B marketplaces, ERP systems, and vertical SaaS platforms [^1]. The company's technology connects suppliers seeking rapid payment with buyers who want extended terms — suppliers receive their money instantly while buyers keep their standard 60-day terms [^1]. Aria's platform underpins upwards of 70 of Europe's biggest B2B marketplaces and freelancer platforms, including Malt and Job&Talent [^2].

Since its 2020 launch, Aria has financed more than €1.5 billion worth of invoices while maintaining a default rate under 0.1% [^2]. The company advanced 1.7 million invoices during 2025 and had already surpassed 1.1 million in the first half of 2026 [^2].
