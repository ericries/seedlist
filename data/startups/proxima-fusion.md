## About

Proxima Fusion is a Munich-based fusion energy company developing a stellarator-based commercial fusion power plant, spun out of the Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics in 2023 [^1][^2]. The company operates offices in Munich, Zurich, and Oxford with approximately 200 employees, and is building "Alpha," a net-energy demonstrator near Munich in partnership with Bavaria, targeting the early 2030s, followed by "Stellaris," its planned commercial plant [^1][^2]. In June 2025, Proxima raised a €130M Series A co-led by Cherry Ventures and Balderton Capital to advance its stellarator design [^3]. On 2026-07-07, the company announced a €411M growth financing at a €2.4B post-money valuation, co-led by XTX Ventures and East X Ventures, with Google making its first European fusion investment and German energy utility RWE contributing €25M as a strategic investor [^1][^2][^4][^5]. Total funding since inception exceeds €650M, including €95M in public grants [^1].
