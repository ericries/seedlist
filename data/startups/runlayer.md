## About

Runlayer is an AI enablement and control platform that functions as a secure control layer for AI tools across enterprise environments, monitoring access and usage while identifying and blocking threats including prompt injections, tool poisoning, and data exfiltration [^1][^2]. The platform serves as a corporate app store and control room where employees access pre-approved AI tools with company data already connected and security guardrails in place, and identifies "shadow AI" usage [^2]. Customers include Instacart, Gusto, Opendoor, and dbt Labs [^2].
