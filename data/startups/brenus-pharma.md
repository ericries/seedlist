## About

Brenus Pharma is a Lyon, France-based clinical-stage biotech developing an off-the-shelf platform for novel immuno-oncology modalities that mimic tumor protein expression to enable multi-specific in vivo immune responses against hard-to-treat solid tumors [^1][^2][^3]. The company's lead candidate STC-1010 is a Phase I in-vivo immunotherapy for metastatic MSS colorectal cancer (mCRC) [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-20 Brenus announced an €11 million ($12.6M) Series A extension bringing total capital raised since inception to €38 million ($43.5M) [^1][^2][^3]. The extension was supported by follow-on participation from existing investors Angelor, UI Investissement (managing FRAI), Crédit Agricole (CACE Création, CACF Capital Innovation), Noshaq, Orsa (formerly Investsud), BIO JAG, and Bpifrance (through non-dilutive funding), and welcomed two new international investors: Sambrinvest (strengthening the company's European shareholder base) and Korea Omega Investment Corp, marking Brenus' first institutional investment from the Asia-Pacific region [^1][^2][^3].
