## About

KelAI is a New York-based startup building an autonomous AI research engine for hedge funds and institutional investors [^1][^2]. The platform automates the full investment research loop within a single agentic system: idea generation, backtesting, signal validation, live monitoring, and feedback [^1]. Founder and CEO Jeremie Cohen previously worked as a systematic portfolio manager at WorldQuant [^2]. KelAI is part of Y Combinator's Spring 2026 batch [^3]. The company reports that its signals have been running with an institutional investor since October 2025 [^2].
