## About

Moove is a mobility fintech company founded in Nigeria in 2020 that provides revenue-based vehicle financing to mobility entrepreneurs across markets in Africa, Europe, the Middle East, and North America [^1][^2]. The company is scaling into autonomous vehicle fleet ownership and operating "Nests" — robotics-first depot infrastructure where autonomous fleets are charged, serviced, maintained, and orchestrated for continuous operation [^1][^2]. Moove has partnerships with Waymo and Uber in the U.S. autonomous mobility market [^2].
