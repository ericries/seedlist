## About

Confido is an AI-native financial and operations platform for consumer packaged goods (CPG) brands, unifying trade promotion management, sales forecasting, demand planning, cash application, deduction management, and supply chain workflows into a single system [^1][^3]. The company was founded in 2020 by Justin Hunter (CEO, previously at Capital One Corporate Strategy) and Kara Holinski (previously Associate Program Manager at Schmidt Futures, MIT engineering background), and went through Y Combinator in Summer 2021 [^3]. Confido is based in New York City and reports serving CPG customers including Mars, Baskin-Robbins, Feastables, Dude Wipes, and Idahoan Foods, with integrations across 70+ retailers, distributors, and accounting systems [^1][^3].
