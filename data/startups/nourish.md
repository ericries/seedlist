## About

Nourish is a New York-based dietitian-led virtual metabolic health clinic pairing 10,000+ Registered Dietitians with AI agents to deliver insurance-covered personalized care for chronic disease prevention and reversal [^1][^2][^3]. Reported clinical outcomes include 8% weight loss, 1.3-point A1C reduction, 31-point LDL cholesterol reduction, and 23-point systolic BP reduction — averaging $2,000+ per patient in annual savings for health plans [^1]. The $100M Series C announced May 19, 2026 was led by Menlo Ventures (J.P. Sanday joining the board) with participation from Thrive Capital, Index Ventures, J.P. Morgan Growth Equity Partners, Maverick Ventures, Y Combinator, BoxGroup, Atomico, Daybreak, and Operator Partners; total funding now stands at $215M [^1][^2][^3].
