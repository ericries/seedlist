## About

SiFly Aviation is a Santa Clara, California-based startup that develops long-endurance electric drones, most notably the Q12, which the company says flies four to five times longer and up to ten times farther than leading enterprise drones [^1][^2]. Founder and CEO Brian Hinman has said the Series A financing provides roughly 18 months of runway to bring the Q12 into U.S. production and to continue development of the company's DronePort infrastructure [^1][^2]. SiFly is negotiating a lease for a 15,000-square-foot manufacturing facility in Sunnyvale and expects to ship roughly 1,000 Q12s in 2027 [^2].
