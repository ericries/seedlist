## About

Kaapi Machines is a Bengaluru-based commercial coffee equipment and services company serving India's cafés, hotels, restaurants, and quick-service chains [^1][^2]. Founded in 2007 by Abhinav Mathur, the company is the exclusive India distributor for premium coffee equipment brands including La Marzocco, Rancilio, WMF, Mahlkönig, Probat, Carimali, and Modbar, and provides espresso machines, grinders, roasters, barista training, and after-sales service [^1][^3]. Named customers include Starbucks, Blue Tokai, McDonald's, Araku, Subko, Burger King, and Boojee [^2][^3].

The August 2026 round is Kaapi Machines' first external fundraise and is described as a strategic partnership with Sedna HoReCa, a PE-backed B2B hospitality platform [^1][^2]. Sedna HoReCa was the sole investor. Advay Capital advised Kaapi on the transaction, with ALMT Legal and K Law serving as counsel [^1][^3]. Proceeds are earmarked for product portfolio expansion, manufacturing capabilities, technology, warehousing, and service infrastructure [^1][^2].
