## About

Rivo is a consumer fintech building autonomous cash management on top of users' existing bank accounts [^1][^2][^3]. The platform automatically moves idle household cash into higher-yielding short-term U.S. Treasury securities through banking partner Jiko and returns funds before bills come due, without requiring users to switch banks or move money manually [^1][^2][^3]. Rivo emerged from beta on August 4, 2026 alongside the announcement of a $2.7M seed round and total funding of $3.1M [^1][^2]. The company frames its target problem as the "Inertia Tax" — the gap between minimal interest earned on checking balances and what banks earn on those deposits [^1][^2].
