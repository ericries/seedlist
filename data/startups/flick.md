## About

Flick is a San Francisco-based AI-native filmmaking platform co-founded by filmmaker Zoey Zhang and Ray Wang, a founding engineer on Instagram Stories who helped scale the product to 400M+ daily active users [^1][^2]. The platform is designed to give professional filmmakers cinematic control over an AI-native creative workflow oriented toward storytelling, iteration, and artistic direction rather than one-click video generation [^1][^2]. The company also launched the Flick Filmmaker Residency, debuting with 10+ short AI-native films at Cinequest Film Festival, MIT, and the Omni AI film festival [^1]. The $6M seed announced May 14, 2026 was led by True Ventures with participation from GV, Y Combinator, Lightspeed, Formosa Capital, Pioneer Fund, Olive Tree Capital, N1, and angel investors [^1][^2].
