## About

Wispr is a San Francisco-based AI voice-to-text company founded in 2021 by Stanford classmates Tanay Kothari and Sahaj Garg [^3]. Its flagship product, Wispr Flow, converts speech into text for writing, messaging and workplace tasks, and the company says users have generated more than 60 billion words through its software across nearly all Fortune 500 companies and more than 10,000 enterprises [^1][^2]. Alongside the Series B, the company previewed Canto, its first proprietary speech-recognition model [^1][^2].
