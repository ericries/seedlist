## About

Moonshot AI is a Beijing-based artificial intelligence lab founded in 2023 by Yang Zhilin, a former Meta AI and Google Brain researcher [^1]. The company develops the Kimi series of open-weight large language models, including Kimi K2.5 and K2.6, which have made Moonshot one of China's most prominent AI labs [^1]. The company's annualized recurring revenue topped $200 million in April 2026, driven by paid subscriptions and API usage of the Kimi chatbot and AI model services [^1][^2]. Moonshot is exploring a Hong Kong IPO under Beijing's new listing rules for overseas-registered entities [^3].
