## About

FirstClub is a Bengaluru-based curated online grocery and quick commerce platform founded in 2025 by Ayyappan R, a former Flipkart executive and former Cleartrip CEO [^1][^3]. The company positions itself around a "quality-first" model — vetting fresh produce, lab-testing select staples, and barring more than 200 ingredients from products listed on the platform — rather than competing on delivery speed alone [^1][^2]. FirstClub offers roughly 4,000 products across food and beverage, grocery, health supplements, and home and kitchen, with delivery windows of 20-25 minutes [^1][^3].

As of the Series B announcement, FirstClub had crossed 1 million orders within its first year, acquired 170,000 households, reached a $50M annualized GMV run rate, and operated 21 stores in Bengaluru and 3 in Hyderabad [^1][^3].
