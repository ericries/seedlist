## About

Quartermaster develops weather-hardened ocean surveillance sensors and a "SmartMast" hardware system that integrates cameras and radios onto ship masts to provide real-time maritime data to governments, shipping companies, and insurers beyond standard AIS location pings [^1]. The company is headquartered in Arlington, Virginia and is led by founder and CEO Neil Sobin; more than 800 SmartMast units have shipped and over 650 vessels are equipped across 25 countries [^1].
