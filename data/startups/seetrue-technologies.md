## About

SeeTrue Technologies is a Joensuu, Finland–based deeptech company developing ultra-low-power, ultra-miniature eye-tracking hardware and computer-vision software for medical optical systems, vision care, and smart wearables such as XR and AI glasses [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2018 by Piotr Bartczak and Roman Bednarik, the company delivers real-time high-FPS gaze tracking with minimal energy use, positioning its architecture for embedded use in next-generation consumer and professional devices [^1][^2].
