## About

Dextr AI builds autonomous AI agents for the hospitality industry, coordinating reservations, guest communications, check-ins, and staff operations across hotels [^1]. The platform includes a voice reservations agent called Daisy, a guest management agent called Alfred, and a staff interface called Doss, and integrates with major property management systems [^1]. The company is based in San Francisco and led by co-founders Sajid Shariff (CEO) and Scott Arnold (CTO) [^1].
