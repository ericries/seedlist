## About

ChemT Biotechnology is a Singapore-based AI-driven biotech company building CelMo, a virtual-cell platform that simulates how cells respond to manufacturing conditions and genetic changes in order to optimize biomanufacturing production processes [^1][^2]. ChemT was founded in 2024 by Jie Sun (Co-Founder & CEO) and Dr. Ling Wu, and says it has built commercial partnerships with more than 40 pharmaceutical, biotech, and CDMO companies globally within roughly 18 months of launch [^1][^2]. The June 2026 seed brings total funding to roughly $5 million, including $1 million in prior angel investment [^1][^2].
