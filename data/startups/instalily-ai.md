## About

InstaLILY AI is a New York-based vertical AI platform that builds domain-trained AI "teammates" — called InstaWorkers — for enterprise workflows in distribution, industrial services, logistics, and healthcare [^1][^2][^3]. On July 14, 2026, the company introduced Lily, described in its press materials as "the world's first AI Forward Deployed Engineer," a product that ingests a customer's operations, builds and ships tooling, and goes live in days [^2][^3]. Customers named in the Series B announcement include SRS Distribution (The Home Depot family), United Rentals, ShipStation Global, PartsTown, Radwell International, Henry Schein, PartsSource, and Kedrion Biopharma [^2][^3]. Its hybrid architecture — built in collaboration with Google DeepMind — pairs a large model for reasoning with a small model deployed on InstaLILY's Small Data Center running on NVIDIA hardware for latency-sensitive tasks [^2]. The company was co-founded by former McKinsey executive Amit Shah [^4].
