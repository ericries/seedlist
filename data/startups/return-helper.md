## About

Return Helper is a Taipei, Taiwan-based provider of e-commerce returns solutions for cross-border merchants [^1]. The company offers a SaaS management platform including a returns portal and buyer portal (integrated with Shopify), a proprietary warehouse management system with mobile apps, and reverse logistics services [^1]. Return Helper achieved over 60% year-on-year revenue growth in 2025 and reached profitability in the second half of that year [^1][^2].

In the European market, the company's revenue grew 215% in 2025, with Q1 2026 on pace for 230% year-over-year growth, served by a network of approximately 100 European merchant partners [^2].
