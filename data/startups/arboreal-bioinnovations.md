## About

Arboreal Bioinnovations is an India-based specialty food and nutraceutical ingredients technology company [^1][^2]. Founded in 2018 by Swati Pandey and Manish Chauhan, the startup develops ingredients and supply-chain solutions for food, beverage, and nutraceutical businesses [^1][^2]. Its portfolio includes next-generation proteins, sugar-reduction solutions, cocoa-based ingredients, natural zero-calorie sweeteners, functional fibres, and nutraceutical actives [^2].

The round is described as one of the largest Series A transactions in India's specialty food ingredients sector [^2]. Proceeds will fund manufacturing capacity expansion, R&D, and commercialisation of next-generation functional ingredients [^1][^2].
