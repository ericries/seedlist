## About

Wordsmith is an Edinburgh-based legal AI company founded in 2023 by former-lawyer-turned-engineer Ross McNairn (CEO), Volodymyr Giginiak (CTO), and Robbie Falkenthal (COO); the company builds an AI platform for in-house legal teams that captures, triages, resolves, and records legal requests coming in across channels such as email, Slack, Salesforce, and Microsoft Teams, so routine work is handled by AI agents while complex matters are escalated to lawyers [^1][^2][^3]. The €60.2 million Series B announced June 3, 2026 was led by Highland Europe with participation from existing investor Index Ventures, bringing total funding to approximately €86 million; the round will fund product development, US expansion, and scaling headcount to 300 employees globally by year-end, on top of more than 500 enterprise customers including BT, Financial Times, Canva, Trip.com, Safelite, Sage, and Starling Bank [^1][^2][^3].
