## About

XCaliber Health is an Andover, Massachusetts-based agentic AI platform that aims to replace healthcare's fragmented point solutions with an operating system orchestrating data, workflows, and autonomous action in real time [^1][^2]. The platform delivers pre-built agents for interoperability, analytics, scheduling, and prescription refills, processing more than 8M chart updates and 160,000 EHR updates daily across over 700,000 unique patients [^1]. The $6.5M Seed round announced May 5, 2026 was led by ManchesterStory with participation from Benhamou Global Ventures and Arka Venture Labs [^1][^2].
