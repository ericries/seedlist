## About

Cathedral is a military-focused AI cybersecurity startup founded in 2026 by four former staffers of the U.S. Department of Government Efficiency (DOGE): Gavin Kliger, Luke Farritor, Marko Elez, and Jack Stein [^1][^2]. The company aims to leverage artificial intelligence to strengthen U.S. military cyber operations, pursuing government contracts for both offensive and defensive cyber capabilities [^1]. Cathedral is seeking dedicated computing infrastructure through data center acquisition or partnerships to support its work, and the co-founders' prior backgrounds include roles at SpaceX and Databricks [^1].
