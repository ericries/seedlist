## About

Superleap is a Bengaluru-based enterprise software company building an AI-native CRM platform for sales and revenue teams, positioning itself as an alternative to legacy CRMs [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by former Unacademy operators Subham Kumar Boundia, Alok Maurya, and Saurabh Maheshwari [^1][^2]. Superleap's platform combines revenue data, customer intelligence, and AI agents to automate tasks across the sales cycle, and it reports 10x growth over the past year, serving customers such as Razorpay, Aakash Education, MediBuddy, HCL GUVI, Cars24, PagarBook, Plum, Qube Cinema, and Superhealth across BFSI, SaaS, education, healthcare, travel, hospitality, and media [^1][^2][^3].
