## About

Gauntlet is a New York-based on-chain asset management and risk platform for decentralized finance, founded in 2018 by CEO Tarun Chitra [^1][^2][^3]. The company operates institutional on-chain vaults that manage capital across DeFi markets and provides risk management, optimization, and analytics services to more than 150 fintechs and institutional clients, with over $1.5 billion in assets under its curation [^1][^3]. On 2026-07-09, Gauntlet announced a $125 million Series C round in which SBI Holdings, Inc. (through its U.S. subsidiary SBI Holdings USA, Inc.) was the sole investor, marking the largest capital raise in Gauntlet's history [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds will fund development of new on-chain products for institutional users, including AI-powered operations and broader stablecoin coverage — with planned support for the Japanese yen and Mexican peso — alongside continued expansion of Gauntlet's institutional vault products [^1][^2].
