## About

Arcos is a Munich-based civil-security infrastructure company building and operating an integrated platform ("Trident") that processes signals from alarm, video-surveillance, and emergency-response systems for critical infrastructure, commercial, and residential customers across Europe [^1] [^2]. The company markets itself around European data sovereignty and locally operated infrastructure [^2].
