## About

Fora is a New York-based AI-powered travel agency platform that lets people become travel advisors and provides infrastructure for client communication, itinerary planning, and bookings [^1]. Founded in 2021, the company operates a two-sided platform: advisors use Fora's tools (including an AI assistant called Via) to run their travel businesses, while travelers connect with advisors to plan trips [^2]. Since launch, Fora advisors have booked more than $3 billion in travel, with the company reaching successive $1 billion in-lifetime-bookings milestones in progressively shorter windows [^3].
