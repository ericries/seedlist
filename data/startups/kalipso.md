## About

Kalipso is a Barcelona-based AI-driven regulatory technology startup building a compliance operating system for regulated businesses [^1][^2]. The platform analyzes regulatory developments, identifies applicable obligations, and generates implementation-ready recommendations with source traceability [^1]. Kalipso already tracks more than 100 regulatory sources across more than 40 jurisdictions, processing over 3,000 regulatory updates a day [^2]. The company was founded in 2025 by Pierre Ferran (CEO) and Virginia Debernardi (COO) [^1][^2].
