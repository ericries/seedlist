## About

BoomerangHR is a New York-based AI-powered alumni management and offboarding platform that turns departing employees into a network of potential rehires, referrals, and sales leads [^1][^2]. Its three products are Alumni Management (one live profile per former employee, auto-synced skills and rehire eligibility), Offboarding (self-serve portal covering benefits, documents, and exit interviews), and Boomerang Sourcing (AI-matched alumni ranked against open roles) [^2]. On 2026-09-15, BoomerangHR announced a $12.7M Seed round led by Kalos Ventures and Album Ventures with participation from Monta Vista Capital, Capital Eleven, and Strada Education Foundation [^1]. Notable customers listed include Cloudflare, Medline, Golden State Warriors, Hilton Grand Vacations, SpaceX, Priceline, and McDonald's [^2].
