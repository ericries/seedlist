## About

Taxwire is a New York-based sales tax automation platform that combines frontier AI with human tax experts to handle nexus monitoring, registration, calculation, filing, and remittance for businesses operating across the United States and over 100 other countries [^1][^2]. The company natively integrates with major billing and accounting platforms including QuickBooks, Xero, and NetSuite [^1]. Taxwire was founded by Andrew Rea (CEO) and Steven Schmatz (CTO), and announced $25 million in combined Seed and Series A funding on June 30, 2026 [^1][^2].
