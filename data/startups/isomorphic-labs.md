## About

Isomorphic Labs is a London-based AI drug discovery company spun out of Google DeepMind in 2021 by Demis Hassabis [^1][^2]. The company builds its discovery platform on AlphaFold-derived technology to predict protein structures and model molecular interactions, and is advancing drug-design programs across multiple therapeutic areas through its IsoDDE engine [^1]. The company has indicated it expects its first clinical trials by the end of 2026 [^1][^2]. The $2.1B Series B announced May 12, 2026 was led by Thrive Capital with existing backers Alphabet and GV continuing, and new investors MGX, Temasek, CapitalG, and the UK Sovereign AI Fund joining [^1][^2][^3].
