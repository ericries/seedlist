## About

SWISSto12 is a Renens, Switzerland–based space company that designs and manufactures satellites and radio-frequency payload systems, with a focus on small geostationary (GEO) satellites [^1][^2]. Its flagship product, HummingSat, is a washing-machine-sized GEO satellite platform that the company positions as a lower-cost alternative to traditional multi-tonne geostationary spacecraft [^1][^2]. As of the Series C announcement, the company reports seven contracts for HummingSat with global satellite operators including SES and Viasat [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-16 SWISSto12 announced the close of a $70 million Series C round (approximately €61 million in European coverage) [^1][^2][^3]. Individual investors in the round and post-money valuation were not disclosed; the company confirmed only that both new and returning investors participated [^1][^3]. The round follows an $84.8 million ESA award to the HummingSat ARTES partnership project earlier in 2026 [^1]. The company reports 2025 revenue of $140 million, contract values exceeding $500 million, and positive EBITDA in 2026 [^2][^3].

## What Founders Say

Emile de Rijk, CEO and founder of SWISSto12: "Space is increasingly recognized as essential infrastructure for the global economy. In this expanding market, our solutions across payload and satellite lines are creating significant new opportunities for customers." [^3]

Emile de Rijk on the small-GEO market opportunity: "Small GEO is increasingly viewed as a solution for multi-orbit, disaggregated constellations. There is huge interest here, both for targeted commercial missions and for governments seeking a sovereign strategic backbone for satellite communications." [^2]

Fredrik Gustavsson, Chief Financial and Strategy Officer at SWISSto12: "The financial picture at SWISSto12 is robust and primed for global growth. $140 million in revenue for 2025, more than $500 million in customer contracts, and a 110% compound annual growth rate since 2022." [^3]
