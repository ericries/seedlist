## About

Bespoke Labs is a Mountain View–based AI research company that builds reinforcement-learning environments and data-curation tooling for training reliable AI agents [^1][^2]. Its simulated environments replicate real enterprise workspaces — codebases, Slack chatrooms, emails, and system logs — so agents can learn to execute complex, long-horizon workflows before being deployed inside real companies [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by Alex Dimakis and Mahesh Sathiamoorthy [^1][^3].
