## About

Fluidstack is an AI infrastructure company that provides GPU cloud capacity for frontier AI developers, positioning itself as a "neocloud" competitor to hyperscalers. The company claims its technology can deliver gigawatts of compute capacity within six months, versus the 18–24 months typically required by the industry [^1]. Fluidstack relocated its global headquarters to New York City in late 2025 [^2] and has signed a memorandum of understanding with the French government to build one of the world's largest decarbonized AI supercomputers [^3].

The Series A closed in January 2026 at a $7.5 billion post-money valuation and was publicly announced on July 20, 2026 [^1]. Situational Awareness is the AI-focused investment firm founded by former OpenAI researcher Leopold Aschenbrenner and is backed by Stripe's founders [^5][^6]. Fluidstack has not publicly named any of the other participants, describing them only as "world-leading investors" [^5][^6][^7].
