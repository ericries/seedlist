## About

Cellares is a Burlingame, California-based biotech company that develops automated "Cell Manufacturing Centers" for producing CAR-T and engineered cell therapies [^1]. The company aims to address manufacturing bottlenecks in personalized medicine, with total funding reaching approximately $632M following its Series D [^1].
