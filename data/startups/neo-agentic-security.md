## About

Neo is a Boston-headquartered agentic software control platform that helps enterprise security teams manage and secure AI agents, AI-enabled applications, and traditional software across their environments [^1][^2][^3]. The platform provides an inventory of AI-enabled assets, identifies excessive access privileges and misconfigurations, delivers real-time attribution of actions to users, agents, and applications, and enforces granular policies on tool calls, data movement, agentic workflows, and API access [^1][^2]. Neo was co-founded by Nick Warner (previously COO at SentinelOne, with prior senior roles at Cylance, McAfee, and Forcepoint), Shlomi Salem (formerly detection engineering lead at SentinelOne), and Eran Shirazi (previously co-founder of EasySend and a vulnerability research lead in Israel's IDF Unit 8200) [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-20, Neo announced its emergence from stealth with $100 million in combined seed and Series A financing led by Andreessen Horowitz and Bessemer Venture Partners, with participation from Craft Ventures and Merlin Ventures; the company did not disclose the separate sizes of the seed and Series A rounds or its post-money valuation [^1][^2][^3].
