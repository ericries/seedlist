## About

CAIS is a New York-based alternative investment platform for independent financial advisors, providing pre-trade, trade, and post-trade lifecycle services for alternatives [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Matt Brown, who serves as CEO [^1]. During the first half of 2026, transaction volume increased 53% year over year while total platform assets rose 55%, with a 3-year organic revenue CAGR of 37% [^1][^2].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|------|-------|--------|------|--------------|
| 2026-07-29 | Series D | $170M | Vista Equity Partners | AllianceBernstein, Blue Owl Capital, Carlyle, Fortress Investment Group, Golub Capital, Lord Abbett, Royal Bank of Canada [^1][^2][^3] |

The Series D valued CAIS at over $2 billion and brings total funding to nearly $600 million [^1][^2]. As part of the deal, Vista Equity Partners President David Breach will join the CAIS board of directors, with representatives from Blue Owl, Lord Abbett, Fortress, and Carlyle serving as board observers [^2].
