## About

Notes.fm is a music royalty collection platform that unifies royalty discovery, distribution, and publishing administration for independent artists, allowing musicians to release music, review earnings, and get paid across 50+ platforms in one place [^1][^2]. Founded by Tim Luckow and Derek Davies, the company highlights that over $1 billion in music royalties goes unclaimed each year due to fragmented registration systems [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-17, Notes.fm announced a $5 million venture round backed by artists and industry figures including Benny Blanco, Zach Bryan, Stefan Max, Danny Kang, Tainy, Blake Slatkin, Matt Quinn, Ari Emanuel, Sam Hendel, and Julie Greenwald, along with multiple management firms [^1].
