## About

Allium is a New York-based blockchain data infrastructure company that aggregates and standardizes transaction data from more than 150 blockchains for institutional clients [^1][^2][^3]. The company's customers include Visa, Coinbase, Andreessen Horowitz's crypto division, the Federal Reserve, and Stanford [^1][^3]. Co-founded by CEO Ethan Chan and Cheng Han Lee, Allium reports its revenue grew more than tenfold over the two years preceding the Series B, on a team of roughly 50 employees [^1][^3]. The June 2026 Series B follows a $16.5 million Series A led by Theory Ventures in 2024 and brings total disclosed funding to just over $56 million [^1].
