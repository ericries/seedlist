## About

DesignVerse is a Bucharest-based AI-native enterprise software startup founded by Andrei Manolache (former Oracle product design lead) and Robert Dragutoiu that helps organizations modernize complex legacy systems—including air traffic management, banking, cybersecurity, and government infrastructure [^1][^2]. The platform was adopted by EUROCONTROL, which manages European air traffic; DesignVerse modernized a 15-year-old EUROCONTROL application in just over a month versus an estimated six months using conventional methods [^2]. The €4.6M ($5.5M) Seed round announced May 13, 2026 included Begin Capital, Gapminder VC, Underline Ventures, and strategic angel investors from Adobe, LSEG, and UiPath; capital will fund engineering hiring and US/EU enterprise expansion [^1][^2].
