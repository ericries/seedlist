## About

Bunkerhill Health is a San Francisco-based healthcare AI company founded in 2019 that builds Carebricks, an agentic AI platform for health systems [^1][^2]. The company partners with major health systems including Cleveland Clinic, Mayo Clinic, the University of Texas Medical Branch, and Intermountain Health to deploy AI agents across hospital operations [^1][^3]. On July 16, 2026, Bunkerhill announced a $25 million Series B round led by Khosla Ventures, bringing total funding to $55 million across its Seed, Series A, and Series B rounds [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports 20-fold revenue growth over the past year and has signed more than a dozen health systems [^1]. Bunkerhill was co-founded by Nishith Khandwala (CEO) and David Eng [^1].
