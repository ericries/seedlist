## About

TYBR Health is a Houston-based regenerative medicine and medical device company focused on improving post-surgical soft-tissue healing [^1][^2]. The company's flagship product, the B3 GEL System, is an FDA-cleared flowable extracellular matrix hydrogel designed to protect tendons, ligaments, muscles, and surrounding tissues during recovery from orthopedic procedures [^1][^2]. The B3 GEL System received FDA clearance in June 2025 and launched commercially in the U.S. in January 2026 [^2].
