## About

Apolownia is a Paris-based mission-driven company founded in 2024 that develops high-integrity coastal ecosystem restoration and blue carbon projects [^1][^2]. Its model combines scientific and technological expertise — satellite remote sensing, geospatial analysis, modelling, drones and 3D modelling, advanced MRV, digital tools and AI — with on-the-ground field expertise to restore natural carbon sinks and regenerate biodiversity [^1][^2]. Apolownia's flagship initiative, BlueRizon, is a mangrove restoration project in Java, Indonesia covering more than 3,200 hectares (with potential extension to 4,000+ hectares) that is targeted for official launch in Q3 2026 [^1][^2].
