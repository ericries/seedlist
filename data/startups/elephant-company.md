## About

Elephant Company is a Berlin-based AI-powered training and communication platform for frontline and blue-collar workers, founded in 2022 [^1][^2]. The platform provides a Field App, AI assistant, AI Coursebuilder, and Elephant Hub to manage content, knowledge, teams, and learners directly in the field [^1][^2]. The €5M+ funding round announced May 15, 2026 was co-led by EnBW New Ventures and Wepa, with participation from business angels from Flix, home24 SE, SB21, Ventic Ventures, and topi; proceeds will fund team expansion and platform scale-up [^1][^2].
