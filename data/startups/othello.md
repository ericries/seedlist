## About

Othello is a New York-based AI sales coaching platform providing real-time in-call guidance and automation for sales teams [^1][^2]. Founded in 2025 by Jared Zelman, the company positions its product as a real-time partner for sales professionals — guiding, automating, and coaching them at every stage of the sales process [^2]. Othello reports it has grown to over 5,000 users within six months of launch, including customers from multiple Fortune 500 companies [^2].
