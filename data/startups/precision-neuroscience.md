## About

Precision Neuroscience develops a brain-computer interface (BCI) intended to restore communication and movement for people with paralysis and neurological disease [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2021 by Benjamin Rapoport, Michael Mager, Demetrios Papageorgiou, and Mark Hettick and is headquartered in New York; its Layer 7 cortical interface has received FDA 510(k) clearance and has been used in clinical studies with over 95 implanted patients across 15+ hospital partners [^1][^3][^4].
