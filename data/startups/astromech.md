## About

Astromech is a Dallas-based AI-biotech company spun out of Colossal Biosciences, building a predictive engine for biology that aims to forecast evolution, disease risk, and system vulnerabilities before they happen [^1]. Co-founded by Ben Lamm and Harvard geneticist George Church, the company initially raised $30 million in seed funding announced September 23, 2025 (per SEC Form D filed August 12, 2025) [^2][^3][^4]. A $10.5 million seed extension closed March 12, 2026 (per SEC Form D filed March 26, 2026), bringing total funding to $40.5 million, with the company reaching a reported $2 billion valuation just nine months after spin-out [^1][^4].

The seed round investors are not publicly disclosed; Astromech remains in stealth mode and the SEC Form D filings list only Ben Lamm, George Church, and Brian Beard as related persons (officers/directors), with no investor identities revealed [^4].
