## About

Natural is a San Francisco-based agentic payments company building infrastructure that enables AI agents to autonomously move and store funds, make payments, collect money, and transact with both humans and other agents [^1][^2][^3]. The startup was founded in 2024 by CEO Kahlil Lalji, Eric Wang, and Walt Leung [^1][^2]. Natural is redesigning payment infrastructure from the ground up for autonomous AI transactions, arguing that traditional financial systems were built for human-initiated payments and cannot support agent-to-agent commerce at scale [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-20, Natural announced a $30 million Series A led by Forerunner (Kirsten Green), bringing total funding to more than $40 million [^1][^2][^3][^4].
