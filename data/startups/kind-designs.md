## About

Kind Designs is a Miami-based climate resilience company that uses large-scale 3D printing and ecological engineering to produce "Living Seawalls," Living Seawall Tiles, Artificial Reefs, and Living Shorelines designed to protect coastal communities from flooding and storm surge while restoring marine ecosystems [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded and is led by CEO Anya Freeman [^1][^2][^3].

On 2026-07-16 Kind Designs announced the close of a $10 million Pre-Series A round at a $70 million valuation, bringing total capital raised to approximately $21.5 million [^1][^2][^3]. The round was originally targeted at $5 million but was upsized after receiving close to $20 million in investor interest [^1][^2]. Participants included returning backers Mark Cuban and former Washington, D.C. mayor Adrian Fenty, along with new investor Kyle Kuzma of the NBA [^1][^2][^3].
