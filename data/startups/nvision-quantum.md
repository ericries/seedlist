## About

NVision is a quantum healthcare company applying quantum sensing and quantum computing to medical imaging and drug discovery [^1][^2]. The company's POLARIS platform enhances MRI signal detection of sugar-based imaging agents by orders of magnitude, enabling real-time metabolism measurement on standard MRI systems [^1][^2]. NVision is expanding from quantum sensing into quantum computing with its new PIQC photonic platform using organic-molecule-based qubits integrated onto photonic chips for drug design applications [^1]. The $55M Series B announced May 13, 2026 was anchored by Abbott as sole strategic diagnostic investor, with participation from CDP Venture Capital, Playground Global, Matterwave/b2ventures, and Entrée Capital, plus a $17M venture loan from the European Investment Bank; total funding now stands at $120M [^1][^2][^3].
