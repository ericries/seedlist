## About

Silicon Microgravity (SMG) is a Cambridge, UK-based deep-tech company developing MEMS (microelectromechanical systems) sensor and inertial-navigation technology for extreme operating environments, with applications spanning launch vehicles, lunar and planetary exploration, satellite communications, GPS-denied navigation and autonomous operations [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-17 the company announced a £6M (approximately €7.08M) investment round to expand UK manufacturing capacity, grow its engineering team and accelerate commercial partnerships across Europe and the United States [^1][^2][^3][^4]. CEO Francis Neill is quoted in coverage of the round [^2][^4].
