## About

Stoa is a London-based FinTech platform that turns idle consumer and business cash deposits into upfront, everyday perks and rewards from partner brands rather than relying on traditional interest models [^1][^2]. Customers place funds into fixed-term "Stoa Pots" and receive lifestyle or business benefits — including subscriptions like Netflix and ChatGPT, travel credits, and merchant rewards — in exchange for the deposit commitment [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022, the platform is live in the UK for both consumers and businesses, and Stoa says it is in early partnership discussions in the United States as it prepares for planned expansion [^1][^2].
