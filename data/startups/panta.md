## About

PANTA is a London-based SaaS fintech that provides an end-to-end digital platform for the financial index industry, automating index design, calculation, rebalancing, reporting and compliance workflows to replace legacy index management systems [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Tobias Sproehnle (previously at Markit, Thomson Reuters, London Stock Exchange and Morningstar) and Mark Pralle (previously at Markit and Morningstar) [^1][^2]. PANTA plans to raise a larger Series A in early 2027 [^2].
