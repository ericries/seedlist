## About

Prometheus is an industrial AI startup founded in late 2025 and publicly launched in November 2025, led by Amazon founder Jeff Bezos and former Google X executive Vik Bajaj [^1][^2]. The company is building AI tools to help engineers design and manufacture physical products, with the stated ambition of creating an "artificial general engineer" — a system envisioned as capable of taking a complex physical product like a jet engine from concept through production [^3]. The startup employs roughly 150 people and operates GPU infrastructure for AI model development [^1].

Bezos described the company's mission as compressing lengthy manufacturing cycles: "The cycle from dream, to manufacturing at rate, to having it out in the world can be very long" [^2].
