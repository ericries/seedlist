## About

H1 is a New York-based AI-powered healthcare-information platform that helps pharmaceutical, health-plan, health-system, and technology customers identify and engage the right physicians across critical clinical and commercial workflows, co-founded and led by CEO Ariel Katz [^1][^2][^3]. H1 reports 85% of the top 20 pharma companies and 9 of the top 10 health plans as customers; recent CVS Health collaborations include a new AI model that substantially improved healthcare-provider directory accuracy [^1][^2]. The $40M round announced May 28, 2026 was led by CVS Health Ventures (Partner Justin Brock); other participants were not disclosed and the round follows multiple operational collaborations between H1 and CVS Health [^1][^2][^3].
