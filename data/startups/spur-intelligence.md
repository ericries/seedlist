## About

Spur Intelligence is an IP intelligence platform that helps fraud and security teams detect anonymized, proxied, or otherwise obscured internet traffic in real time [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2017 by former Defense Department engineers Riley Kilmer and Ethan Smith and is led by CEO Kevin Hickey [^1][^2]. Its platform is used by enterprises to distinguish legitimate human users from bot traffic and AI agents [^2][^3].
