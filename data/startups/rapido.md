## About

Rapido is a Bengaluru, India-based ride-hailing platform founded in 2015, operating bike taxis, auto-rickshaws, and cabs across 400+ cities at price points below Uber and Ola [^1][^2]. The company announced a $240M primary round on May 15, 2026 led by Prosus, with existing investors WestBridge Capital and Accel participating, taking total financing to $730M and valuing the company at $3B post-money — nearly triple its September 2024 valuation of $1.1B [^1][^2]. Rapido reports 31.8M monthly active users, behind Uber India (33.6M) and ahead of Ola (28.6M), and led all three platforms with 33M new app downloads in 2024 [^2].
