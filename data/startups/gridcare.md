## About

GridCARE is a Menlo Park, CA-based power acceleration platform for AI data centers, building software that identifies and unlocks idle grid capacity so hyperscalers can deploy compute without waiting years for new generation [^1][^2][^3]. The company has completed a demonstration project with Portland General Electric that freed 80MW of incremental data center capacity in 2026, part of more than 400MW expected to be energized by 2029 [^1][^2]. The $64M oversubscribed Series A announced May 14, 2026 was led by Sutter Hill Ventures (John Doerr as lead partner), with participation from National Grid Partners, Future Energy Ventures, Emerson Collective, Stanford University, and existing investors Xora, Aina Ventures, Overture, Acclimate Ventures, and Clearvision Ventures [^1][^2][^3].
