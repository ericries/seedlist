## About

Worldmodeldata is a Cambridge, UK-based AI data infrastructure startup building a training-data library sourced from modern video games for world models, vision-language-action (VLA) systems, and physical AI. The company argues that gameplay footage captures rich, dynamic human-in-the-loop interaction data at a scale unavailable in real-world video, and is aggregating and structuring these datasets under commercial agreements with game studios. Founder and CEO Rhea Loucas, a serial entrepreneur, has set a target of accumulating one million hours of curated gameplay data by the end of 2026 — the current largest comparable database contains roughly 40,000 hours. Lord Richard Allan, former Meta VP of Public Policy, joined the board as chairman on emergence from stealth [^1][^2][^3].
