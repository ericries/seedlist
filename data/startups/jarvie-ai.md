## About

Jarvie AI is a San Francisco-based startup building an AI assistant designed for group messaging platforms, initially launching on iMessage with plans to expand to other messaging services [^1][^3]. The platform allows users to add an AI assistant directly into existing group conversations, where it helps coordinate plans, organize trips, summarize discussions, surface relevant information, and assist with daily tasks [^1][^3]. The company says Jarvie has long-term memory, understands social relationships, and is continuously learning new capabilities [^3]. Jarvie AI was founded by Ken Chong (Founder & CEO) and Matt Sawchuk (Co-Founder) [^3]. The company says it will use the seed capital to accelerate product development and expand the platform's capabilities [^1].
