## About

Metaview builds an agentic recruiting platform that uses AI agents to source, engage, screen, and interview candidates end-to-end [^1]. The company plans to grow from 80 to 250 employees by the end of 2027 and will use the proceeds to expand its agentic recruiting coworker, "fillmore," which handles autonomous sourcing and outreach [^1]. Metaview is headquartered in San Francisco with offices in London and plans to open a New York location [^1].
