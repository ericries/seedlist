## About

ChipAgents is a Santa Clara-based agentic AI platform for semiconductor design and verification, using AI agents to accelerate RTL code verification, debugging, and design optimization [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by William Wang and reports deployments at more than 120 semiconductor companies, including MediaTek and Micron; ARR grew 6x in the first half of 2026 [^1][^2]. The $60M Series A2 extension came roughly six months after the original $74M Series A close in February 2026 [^1][^3].
