## About

Westmag (short for Western Magnetics Company) is a South San Francisco-based manufacturer of drone motors and robot actuators designed and produced in the United States [^1]. The company designs and builds motors and actuators on a shared architecture, vertically integrating design, manufacturing, and supply chain to onshore production of components previously imported from Shenzhen [^2]. Westmag is ramping production at Factory 01, its launch manufacturing facility and headquarters in South San Francisco, to fulfill committed customer orders for hundreds of thousands of units [^1].

The company was co-founded by David Hansen (CEO) and Jordan Sanders (COO) and emerged from stealth on June 2, 2026 with an $11 million seed round [^1][^3].
