## About

Redo is a Draper, Utah-based SaaS platform that helps brands manage post-purchase customer interactions, including returns, exchanges, order tracking, package protection, fulfillment support, customer service, and marketing [^1][^2][^3]. The company says more than 4,100 brands use its platform, with more than 1,750 merchants using multiple Redo offerings [^1][^3]. The June 2026 Series B values Redo at a $1.25 billion post-money valuation, pushing the company into unicorn territory [^1][^2][^3]. As part of the round, Redo announced the acquisition of ReturnBear, a provider of international returns and fulfillment operations across more than 100 countries [^1][^3].
