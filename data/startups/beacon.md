## About

Beacon is an AI-native business operating system company that selectively acquires mission-critical "everyday economy" businesses and integrates them into a shared operating platform powered by AI. The company is dual-headquartered in Toronto and San Francisco and was founded by Nilam Ganenthiran. Beacon has been compared to Constellation Software as an "anti-PE" AI roll-up, acquiring approximately one business per week [^1] [^2].
