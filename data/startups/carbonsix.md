## About

CarbonSix is a South Korea-based Physical AI company developing deployment-ready robotic intelligence software and hardware for global manufacturing operations [^1][^2]. The company's platform automates complex, variable manufacturing tasks and improves performance using real-world operational data, and is designed for immediate integration into live production lines [^1][^2]. CarbonSix reports commercial contracts and scaling revenue at the time of its Series A [^1][^2].
