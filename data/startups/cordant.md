## About

Cordant emerged from stealth in July 2026 with an $8M seed round to build what it calls a "command center" for financial infrastructure. The platform ingests signals from payment rails, bank accounts, ledgers, and compliance systems to provide a unified operational view across fragmented payments stacks used by banks and fintechs [^1][^2][^3]. It was founded in 2026 by former Rapyd leaders Eric Rosenthal, Lior Levitt, Sagi Ittah, and Juan Jose Huezo [^1][^2]. Bitso and Paxos are also serving as design partners [^1][^3].
