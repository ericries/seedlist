## About

Lightbringer is a Malmö-based legaltech company building an AI-powered patent platform for startups and small-to-medium-sized businesses, positioning itself as an alternative to traditional patent law firms ("Big Law") [^1][^2]. The company develops software for patent drafting, filing, and portfolio management [^2]. Lightbringer reports 300% year-over-year revenue growth in Q2 2026 and says it serves more than 200 deep tech companies across 17 countries [^2]. Co-founder and CEO Dominic Davies is a patent attorney with 20 years of experience; co-founder Markus Andreasso is a software engineer with 25+ years at Sony and Voyado and 50+ patents; co-founder Ola Wassvik is former co-founder and CTO of FlatFrog and has managed 300+ patent families [^2].
