## About

Focused Energy is a German-American laser-fusion company founded in 2021 as a TU Darmstadt spinout by CEO Thomas Forner and CSO Prof. Markus Roth, with offices in Darmstadt, Berlin, Austin, and San Francisco; the company is building what it claims will be the world's first commercial laser-fusion power plant on RWE's former Biblis site in Hesse, Germany [^1][^2][^3]. The $240M Series A announced May 27, 2026 was led by RWE (which contributed $60M and will develop the Biblis site using its power-plant expertise) with participation from SPRIND (the German Federal Agency for Breakthrough Innovation), the European Innovation Council Fund, returning lead Prime Movers Lab, Beteiligungs-Managementgesellschaft Hessen, and Futury Capital; the company describes the round as the largest fully-secured Series A in the global fusion industry to date and the round makes Focused Energy the most valuable fusion company in Europe [^1][^2][^3].
