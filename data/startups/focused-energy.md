## About

Focused Energy is a German-American laser-fusion company founded in 2021 as a TU Darmstadt spinout by CEO Thomas Forner and CSO Prof. Markus Roth, with offices in Darmstadt, Berlin, Austin, and San Francisco; the company is building what it claims will be the world's first commercial laser-fusion power plant on RWE's former Biblis site in Hesse, Germany [^1][^2][^3][^4]. The $240M (€206M) Series A announced May 27, 2026 was led by RWE — which expanded its initial October 2025 investment by an additional €60 million and will make the Biblis site available for fusion use as part of decommissioning — with participation from SPRIND (the German Federal Agency for Breakthrough Innovation), the European Innovation Council Fund, returning lead Prime Movers Lab, Beteiligungs-Managementgesellschaft Hessen, and Futury Capital, plus investors from Europe, the US, Asia, and the Gulf region [^1][^2][^3][^4][^5]. The company describes the round as the largest fully-secured Series A in the global fusion industry to date [^1].
