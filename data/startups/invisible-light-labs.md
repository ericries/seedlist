## About

Invisible-Light Labs is a TU Wien spin-off based in Vienna developing nanoelectromechanical Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy (NEMS-FTIR) technology for chemical identification of sub-micron particles, aerosols, and nanomaterials [^1][^2]. Its flagship product EMILIE enables analysis of samples in extremely small quantities, with applications in pharmaceuticals (nanoparticle drug development), environmental monitoring, and nanotechnology research [^1][^2]. The company has a co-marketing partnership with Bruker, a market leader in scientific instruments [^1].
