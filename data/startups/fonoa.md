## About

Fonoa is a Dublin-based AI tax-operating system founded in 2019 by three Uber alumni — Davor Tremac (CEO), Filip Sturman, and Ivan Ivankovi — that automates global tax compliance, VAT/GST determination, e-invoicing, and reporting for cross-border enterprises [^1][^2][^3]. Customers include Canva, Uber, Netflix, Nebius, and Booking.com, with some reporting up to 90% faster tax calculation [^1][^2]. The $110M (€94.4M) Series C announced May 28, 2026 was led by Headline with new investors Eurazeo and Forestay Capital joining existing backers Index Ventures, OMERS, Coatue, and Dawn Capital; Fonoa simultaneously acquired Indirect Tax Edge (Edge), PwC's global indirect-tax compliance product, bringing the entire indirect-tax lifecycle into a single data model from tax-ID validation through real-time determination, e-invoicing, and return submission [^1][^2][^3].
