## About

American Perpetuals Exchange Corporation (APEC) is a U.S.-based derivatives trading platform building a regulated venue for perpetual futures on equities and stock indices -- not cryptocurrencies [^1][^2]. The company plans to apply for licensing from the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) and is pursuing a dual CFTC/SEC oversight model [^2][^3]. Founder and CEO Theodore Gillibrand, son of U.S. Senator Kirsten Gillibrand, graduated from Stanford University in June 2026; he previously was a fellow at Paradigm and an intern at Andreessen Horowitz [^1].
