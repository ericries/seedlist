## About

Clouted is a Los Angeles-based AI startup building a "Virality Engine" for consumer and entertainment brands; its AI agents ingest a brand's strategy, content library, and digital presence to autonomously run campaigns across clipping, UGC, fan pages, influencer seeding, performance ads, and viral stunts [^1][^2]. With the funding announcement, Clouted publicly launched its Distribution Intelligence platform, positioned as an AI infrastructure layer that makes viral marketing repeatable and measurable [^1][^2].
