## About

Hulp is a Delhi NCR–based AI-powered personal-assistance platform that lets urban Indian households delegate everyday tasks — spanning kitchen management, administrative filings, travel bookings, and restaurant reservations across 15+ service categories — via WhatsApp or a mobile app, using AI workflows backed by human assistants [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in April 2024 by Tarun Mathur (ex-Policybazaar Chief Business Officer), Neha Kulwal (ex-Mitgo India MD), and Vishal Singh Khutel, and currently operates in Delhi, Gurugram, and Noida with a 50-person operations team [^1][^2][^3].
