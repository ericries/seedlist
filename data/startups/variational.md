## About

Variational is a peer-to-peer on-chain derivatives trading protocol on Arbitrum that has processed $200B+ in trading volume and powers Omni, a zero-fee trading platform aggregating liquidity from on-chain and traditional markets [^1][^2][^3]. The ~$50M Series A announced May 20, 2026 was led by Dragonfly with participation from Bain Capital Crypto, Coinbase Ventures, and other strategic investors; the round coincided with the launch of the protocol's first Real-World Asset (RWA) markets [^1][^2][^3].
