## About

Intropy is a London-based AI-native platform automating inventory, pricing, and other critical decisions for spare parts businesses [^1][^2]. The company reports that its technology has processed more than €8.7 billion in parts demand and that its customers have achieved returns on investment exceeding 10x [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-30, Intropy announced a €9.5 million seed round (approximately $11 million) led by Felix Capital, with participation from Quiet Capital and existing investors General Catalyst and firstminute capital [^1][^2]. Intropy plans to use the capital to accelerate product development, grow its engineering and machine learning teams, and open a New York office [^1][^2].
