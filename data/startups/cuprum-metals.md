## About

Cuprum Metals Inc. is a Vancouver-based copper extraction technology company founded in 2024 by a team of chemical professionals who approached copper leaching from a chemistry and chemical engineering perspective rather than traditional mining or metallurgy [^1]. The company has developed a patented water-based chemical leach process that can also operate using sea water and is designed to achieve high recovery rates at significantly faster processing times than conventional methods [^1]. The technology targets chalcopyrite and other minerals that have historically been difficult to leach, while reducing both operating and capital costs [^1].
