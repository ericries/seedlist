## About

ProphetX operates a federally regulated, peer-to-peer sports prediction market. The company received CFTC approval to operate as both a Designated Contract Market (DCM) and a Derivatives Clearing Organization (DCO), launching its exchange for sports event contracts in June 2026 [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, ProphetX announced a $35 million funding round co-led by Parlay Capital and Data Point Capital, with participation from FDJ Ventures, Greenwave Ventures, Connexa Capital, Impellent Ventures, The Operating Group, Sharp Alpha Advisors, Chicago Trading Company Ventures, Belvedere Trading, principals at White Swan Data and Consolidated Trading, and Ematiq [^1][^2]. Proceeds will fund liquidity, B2B partnerships, and platform scaling [^1][^2].
