## About

Undo is a Cambridge, UK-based software company providing runtime context and AI-powered root-cause analysis for debugging mission-critical software [^1]. The platform captures complete execution history into recordings, enabling AI agents and engineers to diagnose complex software issues across development, testing, and production environments [^1][^2]. Undo's customers include networking, database, semiconductor, and financial services companies [^1].

The company was founded by Greg Law, who continues to serve as CEO [^1].
