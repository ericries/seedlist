## About

Aria is a Paris-based fintech providing pan-European embedded invoice-financing infrastructure for B2B marketplaces, ERP systems, and vertical SaaS platforms [^1][^2]. Its API-based product lets suppliers get paid immediately while buyers retain standard 60-day payment terms, and Aria handles identity verification, credit assessment, collections, insurance, and payment execution across Europe [^2].

On 2026-07-09, Aria announced a €7M Series A extension led by 115K — the venture arm of French bank La Banque Postale — with existing backer 13books Capital participating [^1][^2]. The company also secured a €240M securitisation debt facility to fund invoice financing, arranged by Nomura with participation from Fost, Sienna, and Montpensier Arbevel [^2]. The equity extension brings Aria's total Series A funding to €22M [^1][^2].
