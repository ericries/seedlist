## About

Spott is an AI-native applicant tracking system (ATS) and CRM platform built for recruitment agencies. The Leuven-based startup, founded in 2024, consolidates fragmented recruitment tools by turning emails, CVs, and pipeline data into searchable intelligence so recruiters can focus on relationship-building while automation handles administrative tasks [^1][^2]. Spott went through Y Combinator's Winter 2025 batch and now serves hundreds of agencies across Europe, the US, and APAC, with offices in Leuven, New York, and Sydney [^2][^3].
