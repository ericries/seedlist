## About

Trevel is a Gurugram-based electric vehicle mobility startup operating across India's Delhi NCR region. The company provides premium EV cab-hailing services including airport transfers, city rides, and hourly rentals [^1][^2]. Founded in 2025 by Mishu Ahluwalia, Rishi Raj Kochhar, and Sahil Jindal, Trevel positions itself as a premium electric alternative to traditional ride-hailing services [^1][^3].

The company plans to deploy the new capital toward expanding its electric vehicle fleet, enhancing its technology platform, increasing marketing efforts, and strengthening operational capabilities. As part of its growth roadmap, Trevel plans to add approximately 25 new vehicles every month, with the goal of expanding its fleet to 500 electric vehicles by March 2027 [^1][^2].
