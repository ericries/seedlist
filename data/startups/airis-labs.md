## About

Airis Labs is a Tel Aviv-based AI defense-technology company founded in 2023 by CEO Noam Friedman, CPO Rotem Abeles, and GM US Amos Lahav that transforms unstructured visual data from smartphones, social media, digital forensics, security cameras, body cameras, and drones into machine-readable intelligence for government and defense organizations — a category the company calls "User-Generated Field Intelligence" [^1][^2][^3]. The $31M Series B announced May 27, 2026 was led by PSG Equity with participation from prior investors TLV Partners, Stepstone Group, Redseed Ventures, and angel investors Eyal Waldman, Jeff Horing, Yasmin Lukatz, and David Chinn; the round brings total funding to $60M [^1][^2][^3].
