## About

Kohort is a London-based mobile gaming analytics, forecasting and user-acquisition (UA) optimisation company. Its ML-based platform — anchored by its Ktrl product — is built for decision-makers in mobile gaming and consumer apps, helping studios, finance teams and investors optimise UA spend and run cohort-based forecasting [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2018 (originally under the name Ramp) by Dan Marcus (CEO) and Jan Pickard (COO); CTO Greig Rapley leads engineering [^3][^4].

Kohort says its predictive models are trained on roughly $6 billion (€5.1B) of historical UA spend, and that nearly $1 billion (€850M) in annual UA spend currently flows through the platform; the company reports campaign-level prediction accuracy of 95% [^1][^2].

The Series A capital is earmarked for the launch of Kohort's full suite of "UA Agents," focused on three capabilities: campaign optimisation (an extension of the Ktrl product), on-demand UA research benchmarked against historical spend on the platform, and automated reporting [^1][^2].
