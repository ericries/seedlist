## About

CloudNC is a London-based manufacturing software company building AI-powered software for CNC (computer numerical control) machining, founded in 2015 by Theo Saville and Chris Emery [^1]. Its flagship product, CAM Assist, integrates with traditional CAM systems and automates the first-pass programming decisions machinists make — tool selection, approach directions, cutting parameters, and machine code generation [^1]. On 2026-09-08, TechCrunch reported CloudNC had raised a $20 million Series B extension led by Nimble Ventures, with participation from Calculus Venture Capital, Entrepreneurs First, and LM Capital (Lockheed Martin's venture arm), bringing the company's lifetime funding to $128 million [^1][^2]. The company employs approximately 80 people and reports serving more than 1,000 machine shops globally, roughly 80% of them in the United States [^1].
