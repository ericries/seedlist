## About

Humanoid is a UK-based AI and robotics company building industrial humanoid robots for logistics, manufacturing, retail, and adjacent sectors [^1]. Founded in London in May 2024 by Artem Sokolov, the company plans to roll out Beta version robots in Q4 2026 followed by mass manufacturing of wheel-based humanoid robots [^2]. Humanoid has secured partnerships with Fortune 500 companies including SAP, NVIDIA, Bosch, and Siemens, and signed the industry's largest publicly announced commercial agreement with Schaeffler for large-scale deployment of humanoid robots in manufacturing environments [^2].

The July 2026 Series A made Humanoid Europe's first pure-play humanoid robotics unicorn [^1][^2].
