## About

RemotePass is a London-headquartered global employment and fintech platform that helps companies hire, pay, and support workers across borders where local entity setup, compliance, and banking infrastructure remain hard [^1][^2]. Founded in 2021 by Kamal Reggad and Karim Nadi, the platform now serves more than 35,000 workers across 150+ countries, has processed over $800M in cross-border payroll, and counts Logitech, Tata Group, InDrive, and Careem among its customers [^1][^2]. RemotePass achieved profitability in early 2025 and has since reinvested in growth; in late 2025 it launched SpendCards (embedded corporate expense cards) and rolled out AI agents that automate onboarding, compliance, and support workflows [^1][^2]. The $17.4M Series B announced May 20, 2026 will fund commercial expansion, financial infrastructure for distributed teams, and its AI roadmap [^1][^2].
