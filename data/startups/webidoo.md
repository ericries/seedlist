## About

Webidoo is an AI technology company headquartered in Chicago with Milan roots, building an "AI operating layer" for small and medium-sized businesses through proprietary platforms including Jooice, Groow, and Welpy that execute and automate core business processes [^1][^2]. Founded in 2017, the company announced a $25M funding round on May 12, 2026 led by IXC3, part of the Azimut Group, with participation from prior backers 8a+ (part of Banca Generali) and TIM Ventures; total funding now exceeds $30M including a 2021 round [^1][^2]. Capital will fund expansion of Webidoo's AI platforms, scaled SMB adoption of agentic AI, and strategic acquisitions of SaaS and marketing companies across the United States [^1]. In 2025, Webidoo reported over $18M in revenue and over $3M in EBITDA [^1].
