## About

Yardstik is a Minneapolis-based Human Trust Platform that helps employers monitor workforce risk beyond hiring — combining background checks with continuous monitoring of motor-vehicle reports, license and certification expirations, and OIG exclusions [^1][^2][^3]. On August 27, 2026, the company announced a $30M Series B led by Harbert Growth Partners, bringing total capital raised to $65M; the company cited 149% YoY revenue growth and a 98% account-retention rate over the past three years [^1][^2][^3].
