## About

Grid.online is a Prague-based logistics startup building shared infrastructure for first- and last-mile parcel delivery [^1]. Founded in 2025 by Ondřej Krátký and Patrik Raš as an independent spin-off from Czech mobility platform Liftago, the company offers a single API through which parcel carriers can access on-demand delivery capacity from a network of flexible couriers [^2].

The company reports scaling parcel volumes more than 10× in its first year of operation and surpassing 1 million deliveries through the network, with between 1,000 and 2,000 active couriers and several thousand more on a waiting list [^1] [^2].
