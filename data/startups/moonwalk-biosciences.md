## About

Moonwalk Biosciences is a biotechnology company developing adipose-targeted siRNA (RNAi) therapeutics for obesity and cardiometabolic diseases [^1][^2]. The company's lead program, MW101, is targeted at first-in-human studies planned for late 2027, and its approach combines an exclusive license to adipose-targeting chemistry from Suzhou Siran Biotechnology with human genetics and multi-omics discovery [^2].
