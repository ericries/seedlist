## About

PEX is a New York-based corporate card and spend management platform that has powered more than $11.7 billion in spend since inception [^1][^2]. The company announced on 2026-07-28 that it had secured $160 million in debt and equity financing led by Bluff Point Associates, a Westport, Connecticut-based private equity firm, with the charge card program funded by a credit facility provided by Clear Haven Capital Management [^1][^2]. PEX plans to use the capital to accelerate growth of its charge card program, expand product innovation including AI-powered capabilities that automate routine financial tasks, and invest in sales, partnerships, and strategic growth initiatives [^1][^2].
