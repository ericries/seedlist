## About

Capsa AI is a London- and New York-based startup building an AI operating system for private capital firms. Its platform indexes scattered institutional data — CRMs, email, SharePoint, deal memos, spreadsheets — into a searchable layer and overlays agentic AI for deal sourcing, due diligence, portfolio monitoring, and back-office operations [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by Danyal Oezduezenciler (CEO) and Callum Downie (CTO) [^2][^3]. Capsa reports 14x year-over-year ARR growth, a 100% customer renewal rate, and net dollar retention above 122% [^1][^4].
