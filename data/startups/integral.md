## About

Integral is a Berlin-based AI-native accounting, tax, and payroll services firm for SMEs, deploying AI agents to reconcile transactions, create account postings, and prepare payroll and tax filings, with licensed professionals reviewing and signing all work [^1][^2]. Founded in 2024 by CEO Lukas Zörner and Anil Can Baykal, the company has raised more than €30M in total since inception [^1][^3][^4]. On 2026-09-16, Integral announced an €18M Series A co-led by Mosaic Ventures and Reid Hoffman, with participation from Cherry Ventures, General Catalyst, and Puzzle Ventures [^1][^2][^3][^4]. Proceeds will expand machine learning research and backend systems engineering teams, scale the network of certified human supervisors, and accelerate market penetration across Europe [^3]. Prior funding included a €6.3M raise in February 2025 and acquisition funding in November 2025 to acquire cleverlohn, a payroll and HR provider [^1].
