## About

ZeroDrift is an AI compliance-firewall company founded in 2026 by repeat enterprise-AI entrepreneur Kumesh Aroomoogan (former co-founder and CEO of Accern, the no-code NLP platform that raised $60M+ and was acquired in 2025); the platform sits between an enterprise's AI systems and the outside world, checking every AI-generated message, voice call, and video against SEC, FINRA, MiFID II, GDPR, HIPAA, and other regulations in real time [^1][^2][^3]. The founding team includes senior leaders from Goldman Sachs (former Global Head of Engineering), Microsoft (Bing Search and AdCenter core systems), and Google (Chrome OS Enterprise) [^2]. The $10M seed announced June 2, 2026 was led by a16z Speedrun with participation from Reign Ventures, PitchDrive Ventures, U&I Ventures, Active Capital, Geek Ventures, Converge Ventures, Atlas SGR, and Founders Future; the round closed within three weeks and was oversubscribed 3x [^1][^2][^3].
