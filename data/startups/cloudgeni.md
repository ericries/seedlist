## About

Cloudgeni is an Oslo-based startup founded in 2024 by Davlet Dzhakishev and Iuliia Petryshyn Thuen, both former Microsoft and McKinsey operators, building specialized AI agents that design, deploy, monitor, and operate enterprise cloud infrastructure [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports early traction with Norwegian industrial group Hydro and shipping company Havila, and has entered a partnership with IBM [^1][^3]. The €858K (~$1M) pre-seed announced May 29, 2026 will scale the team further across the Nordics and the US [^1][^2].
