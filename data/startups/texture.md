## About

Texture is a grid software platform giving utilities a single view of every device and data source on their network, with first-party connections to 50+ OEMs including Tesla, FranklinWH, Honeywell, Ecobee, and SolarEdge [^1][^2]. The platform is operational at utility cooperatives and energy companies, providing the coherent data infrastructure needed to operate the grid in real time [^1]. The $12.5M Series A announced May 20, 2026 was co-led by VoLo Earth Ventures and Equal Ventures with participation from Lerer Hippeau and Abstract Ventures, bringing total funding to approximately $23M [^1][^2][^3].
