## About

Grand Games is an Istanbul, Turkey-based mobile gaming company founded in 2024 that specializes in hybrid casual puzzle games designed for brief, daily play sessions [^1][^2]. The studio operates five autonomous internal studios with roughly 75 employees, has shipped six live games, and has surpassed 50 million total downloads across 200+ countries [^1]. The $70M Series B announced May 11, 2026 was led by Balderton Capital's Growth Fund with participation from Bek Ventures, Laton Ventures, and angel investor Mert Gür, bringing total funding to $103M [^1][^2]. The company reports 5x year-over-year revenue growth.
