## About

Nopan is an Amsterdam-based performance layer for account-to-account and wallet payments, founded by former Netflix payments leaders Konstantin Surkov and Nick Ryabov [^1][^2]. The company describes its infrastructure as an optimisation layer that helps digital businesses and payment service providers lift conversion rates and reduce cost on non-card payment methods so they can compete with card networks on reliability [^1][^2]. Nopan received its De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB) licence in Q4 2025 and became a Principal Member of the European Payments Initiative (EPI), the consortium behind the Wero wallet [^1]. The company said it has raised EUR 7.2 million in total funding, led by Newion with participation from Crane and Seedcamp plus angel investors [^1][^2].
