## About

Paper is a San Francisco–based design platform for teams shipping software with AI and coding agents [^1][^2]. The product combines a design environment with agent-native workflows so designers and engineers can collaborate through a shared language [^1][^2]. Customers include Ramp, Lovable, Vercel, PostHog, Quartr, and Y Combinator [^1]. The company was founded by Stephen Haney [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-23 Paper announced a $34M Series A led by Accel and ICONIQ, with participation from Designer Fund, Michael Grinich, Anton Osika, and engineers and designers from Anthropic and OpenAI [^1][^2][^3]. Daniel Levine is the Accel partner leading the deal [^3]. The capital will accelerate product development, expand the team, and deepen investment in AI-native workflows for designers [^1][^2].
