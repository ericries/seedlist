## About

Rem3dy Health is a Birmingham, UK-based health technology company behind the Nourished brand, which produces personalized nutrition products powered by AI and 3D printing [^1] [^2]. The June 2026 round valued the company at £84 million and was raised to support international expansion and continued development of its AI platform and manufacturing capabilities [^1].
