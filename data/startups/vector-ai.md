## About

Vector is a Boston-based provider of an AI ad platform for B2B marketers, building contact-level advertising and intent-based targeting designed to amplify marketing teams rather than replace them [^1][^2]. The company announced a $10M Series A on May 13, 2026, led by SignalFire and HubSpot Ventures, intended to accelerate development of an AI ad automation platform built to multiply the impact of B2B marketers [^1][^2][^3].
