## About

Decart is an Israeli vertically-integrated frontier AI lab co-founded in late 2023 by CEO Dean Leitersdorf and CPO Moshe Shalev, building real-time video and world models alongside an ultra-optimized inference and training stack (DOS) designed to make agents and reasoning models run smarter and faster [^1][^2][^3]. The company's model lineup includes Lucy (a world model for immersive experiences) and Oasis (a world model for physical AI), with Amazon as a strategic customer running Decart's real-time world model on its chips [^1][^2]. The $300M round announced May 18, 2026 was led by Radical Ventures at a $4B valuation, with new investors Nvidia, Atreides Management, Valor Equity Partners, Adobe Ventures, Toyota Ventures, and eBay Ventures joining returning backers Sequoia Capital, Benchmark, and Zeev Ventures; angel participants include Andrej Karpathy, Michael Eisner, the Nintendo family, and Moritz Baier-Lentz [^1][^2][^3]. The round brings total funding to over $450M.
