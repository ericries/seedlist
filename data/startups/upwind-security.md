## About

Upwind Security is an Israeli cloud security company led by CEO Amiram Shachar that builds a runtime-powered cloud security platform for detecting and remediating threats across cloud environments. In early September 2026 Upwind announced a $300 million funding round co-led by Bessemer Venture Partners and TCV, more than doubling its valuation to $3.8 billion in five months and bringing total funding to approximately $730 million [^1][^2]. Salesforce Ventures, Greylock, Craft Ventures, and Cyberstarts also participated in the round [^1].
