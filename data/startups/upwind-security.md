## About

Upwind Security is an Israeli cloud security company led by CEO Amiram Shachar that builds a runtime-powered cloud security platform for detecting and remediating threats across cloud environments [^3]. Upwind was founded in 2022 by Amiram Shachar and co-founders from Spot.io (acquired by NetApp in 2020 for $450M) [^3]. In early September 2026 Upwind announced a $300 million funding round co-led by Bessemer Venture Partners and TCV, more than doubling its valuation to $3.8 billion in roughly five months and bringing total funding to approximately $730 million [^1][^3]. Salesforce Ventures, Greylock, Craft Ventures, Cyberstarts, Leaders Fund, and Alta Park Capital also participated in the round; existing investor Penny Jar Capital also participated [^3]. The company previously raised $250 million in January 2026 at a $1.5 billion valuation [^2][^3].
