## About

imagi is a Stockholm- and New York-based edtech company building an AI literacy platform for K-12 students and teachers [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2018 by Dora Palfi and Beatrice Ionascu, the platform provides students with supervised access to AI tools through curriculum-aligned lessons, while offering teachers training, safety controls, and educational resources to guide classroom AI adoption [^1][^3]. imagi has worked with more than 700,000 students across 140 countries since launch and recently partnered with Lovable to let teachers create custom "vibe-coding" lessons with controlled student access [^2].
