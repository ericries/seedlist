## About

Sightera Biosciences is an Antwerp-based AI drug discovery startup that spun out of the University of Antwerp (UA) and Antwerp University Hospital (UZA) in January 2026 [^1][^2]. Founded in January 2025 by Hendrik Vercammen (CEO), Maxim Le Compte (CSO), Lars Vanlommel (CBO), and Christophe Deben (CDO), the company trains its AI on proprietary data from patient-derived biological material — focusing on advanced, therapy-resistant, or end-stage disease biology (so-called "extreme biology") — with initial applications in oncology and fibrosis [^1][^2][^3].

The funding will support team expansion, platform build-out, advancement of Sightera's lead molecular glue oncology programme toward preclinical candidate selection, and strategic partnerships with pharma and biotech [^1][^2].
