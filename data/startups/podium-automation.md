## About

Podium Automation is a Brooklyn-based manufacturer that designs and fabricates industrial control panels using software-enabled, automated processes [^1]. The company was founded in 2024 by Jamie Niu Serota (CEO) and Jacob Buser (CTO) and targets quick-turn delivery of UL 508A-certified control panels in under four weeks [^1][^2]. Customers range from scale-ups to Fortune 500 manufacturers [^1].

The Series A brings Podium Automation's total reported equity funding to more than $23 million [^1][^2]. As part of the financing, Dayna Grayson, Co-Founder and Managing Partner at Construct Capital, joined Podium's Board of Directors [^1][^3].
