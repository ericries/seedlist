## About

Integral is a Berlin-based AI-powered services firm delivering automated accounting, taxation, and payroll for SMEs — reconciling invoices, creating postings, and preparing filings via AI agents while licensed professionals maintain oversight. Integral Tax operates as a licensed audit firm under German law with exclusive European data processing [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-16, the company announced an €18M Series A led by Mosaic Ventures and Reid Hoffman with participation from Cherry Ventures, General Catalyst, and Puzzle Ventures [^1]. Founded in 2024, Integral has raised over €30M since inception and acquired payroll/HR provider cleverlohn in November 2025; a prior €6.3M round closed in February 2025 [^1].
