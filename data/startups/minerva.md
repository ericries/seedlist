## About

Minerva is a Brooklyn-based AI platform for consumer marketing teams that unifies fragmented first-party customer data, enriches it with proprietary consumer context, and runs end-to-end marketing workflows with AI agents [^1][^2]. The company launched publicly on June 11, 2026, alongside a $20M financing and a strategic collaboration with OpenAI on frontier models including GPT-5.5 [^1][^2]. Minerva says it had signed 36 customers at launch — including the NBA, Juicebox, Luxury Presence, Trust & Will, and Wander — with early deployments showing 3.4x paid-media ROAS and 2.5x direct-mail MQL improvements [^1].
