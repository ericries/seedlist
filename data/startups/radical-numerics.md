## About

Radical Numerics is an AI research lab building general biological intelligence — multimodal AI models that read, write, and reason in the language of biology, including DNA, RNA, proteins, and other molecules [^1][^2]. The company is the team behind Evo and Evo 2, AI models that generate DNA sequences trained on genomes from over 100,000 species [^2].

The company was founded by Eric Nguyen (CEO, PhD Stanford Bioengineering & AI), Michael Poli (Chief AI Scientist, PhD Stanford, Liquid AI founding team), Stefano Massaroli (President, postdoc with Yoshua Bengio, Liquid AI founding team), and Armin Thomas (CTO, postdoc at Stanford with Chris Ré, Liquid AI) [^1].

Scientific advisors include Eric Horvitz (Chief Scientific Officer, Microsoft), Chris Ré (Stanford), George Church (Harvard), and Andrew Weber (former U.S. Assistant Secretary of Defense for Nuclear, Chemical, and Biological Defense Programs) [^1].

The company targets the AI drug discovery market and has two early commercial partnerships in cancer detection and pathogen characterization [^2].
