## About

GenerativeX is a Tokyo-based agentic AI company that deploys "Forward Deployed Engineer" (FDE) teams to design, implement, and operationalize AI agent systems for large enterprises across financial services, health sciences, manufacturing, and telecommunications [^1][^2]. Founded in June 2023 by Rei Araki, the company says it has grown to serve more than 80 enterprise clients with operations in San Francisco, New York, and Tokyo [^1][^2]. GenerativeX plans to use the Series A capital to scale its FDE framework in North America, hire senior engineers and consultants, and pursue strategic acquisitions and partnerships [^1][^2].
