## About

Flow Engineering is a San Francisco–based company building AI agents that automate and verify hardware engineering workflows [^1]. Its platform ingests CAD drawings, product requirements, simulation results, and test data, then uses AI to align them so engineers can catch inconsistencies and iterate faster [^1]. Customers include Anduril, Rivian, Joby Aviation, General Motors PPU, RV Tech, and Stoke Space [^1].
