## About

TensorWave is a Manhattan Beach-based AI hardware startup founded by ex-Lockheed Martin Skunk Works engineers that builds AMD-based AI accelerator clusters as an alternative to NVIDIA's GPU dominance [^1]. The company develops "TE1 chips" for large-model workloads, offering enterprises an alternative to vendor lock-in [^1]. Following its Series B, the company is valued at approximately $1.55B [^1].
