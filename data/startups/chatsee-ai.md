## About

ChatSee.ai is a San Francisco–based startup building a "failure intelligence" platform for autonomous AI systems. The platform monitors, detects, and structures behavioral failures across enterprise AI agents, creating an organizational memory of recurring failure patterns to help both humans and AI systems continuously improve [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by Sekhar Sarukkai (previously involved with Skyhigh Networks, Securent, and Confluent Software) and Sanjay Agrawal, who has worked on large-scale distributed systems and enterprise AI infrastructure [^1].
