## About

Nectar Social is an AI-powered marketing operating system that unifies social intelligence, community management, creator workflows, and conversational commerce in a single platform, with official data partnerships across Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, and X [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by sisters Misbah and Farah Uraizee — both former Meta leaders (Misbah in product, Farah in engineering) — and is now headquartered in Palo Alto, CA after relocating from Seattle [^3][^4]. Less than a year out of stealth, Nectar already powers more than 10 million conversations per week across pre-purchase, post-purchase, and creator workflows, growing 5x in the three months leading up to its Series A; customers include e.l.f. Beauty, Babylist, Figma, Graza, and Liquid Death [^1][^2]. The $30M Series A announced on May 14, 2026 was led by Menlo Ventures and its Anthology Fund (created in partnership with Anthropic), with participation from True Ventures, GV, and Gwyneth Paltrow's Kinship Ventures [^1][^2].
