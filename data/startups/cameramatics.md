## About

CameraMatics is a Dublin-based fleet intelligence company founded in 2016 by Mervyn O'Callaghan and Simon Murray [^1]. The platform combines AI-driven video intelligence, advanced driver assistance systems, and real-time operational analytics to improve fleet safety and operations [^1][^2]. The company employs more than 150 people across offices in Dublin, Waterford, London, Darlington, Amsterdam, Barcelona, and the US, and serves nearly 1,000 fleet customers including Royal Mail, Wolseley, and Installed Building Products [^1][^2]. CameraMatics is on track to exceed €30 million in contracted recurring revenue in 2026 [^1].
