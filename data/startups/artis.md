## About

Artis is an Austin-based brand partner for residential architects, interior designers, and custom home builders, providing branding, social media management, websites, and AI-search/generative engine optimization for high-end residential design firms [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by Zach Rubin (CEO), Sahar Afrakhan, and David Rubin, the company currently serves more than 60 firms, including McALPINE, Kligerman AD, Schafer & Company, Cravotta Interiors, and Foursquare Builders [^1][^2]. Artis pairs an AI-powered platform with hands-on creative services to handle the work that pulls designers away from their craft [^1][^2].
