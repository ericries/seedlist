## About

Kraken Technology Group is a UK-based maritime defence company headquartered in Fareham, Hampshire, that designs and manufactures uncrewed surface vessels (USVs) and associated payloads for NATO and allied defence customers [^1][^2][^3]. Founder and CEO Mal Crease previously led Vector Racing, a world-championship offshore powerboat programme, and the company draws engineering expertise from offshore powerboat racing, automotive innovation, and military operations to build fast, mission-configurable USVs [^4]. Kraken's platforms have already been deployed in operational trials with allied navies and were selected in early 2026 for the Royal Navy's Project Beehive, under which 20 uncrewed surface vessels are to be delivered for training and development [^5][^6].

The Series B was announced on 2026-07-09 at a $1 billion valuation, making Kraken Europe's newest maritime-defence unicorn [^1][^2][^3]. In addition to the new capital, existing backers NATO Innovation Fund, the UK National Security Strategic Investment Fund (NSSIF), SmartCap, Notion Capital, and Speedinvest converted prior instruments into Series B equity [^1][^2].
