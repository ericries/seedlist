## About

Expedition Therapeutics is a biopharmaceutical company launched in 2024 that is developing next-generation therapies for respiratory disease, led by CEO Yi Larson [^1][^3]. Its lead program, EXPD-101, is a next-generation DPP1 inhibitor being evaluated in a Phase 2 clinical trial for chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), designed to target neutrophilic inflammation [^1][^2]. The company operates via a China NewCo model that in-licenses drug candidates originally developed in China [^3].
