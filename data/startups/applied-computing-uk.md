## About

Applied Computing is a London-based startup building a foundation AI model for the oil, gas, refining and petrochemical industry [^1][^2]. Founded in 2023, the company targets industrial facilities where a single plant can carry thousands of sensors measuring temperature, pressure, velocity, and viscosity [^1]. Its flagship platform, Orbital, is described by the company as the first foundation model built specifically for energy operations, combining physics-grounded intelligence with models across chemical engineering, time-series forecasting and language [^1][^2]. The company operates from London (headquarters), Bengaluru (operations), and — following this round — Houston, Texas (US energy hub) [^1][^2]. Applied Computing also appointed former Shell AI leader Dan Jeavons as President in connection with the round [^2].
