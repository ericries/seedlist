## About

Etched is an AI chip startup, founded in 2022 by three Harvard dropouts (Gavin Uberti, Robert Wachen, and Chris Zhu), that builds transformer-specialized inference hardware [^1][^2]. Its architecture hard-codes the transformer model into silicon, sacrificing generality for order-of-magnitude gains in inference throughput and cost [^1]. The company is backed by Sequoia Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, SK Hynix, Jane Street, Diffusion Capital, Peter Thiel, Andrej Karpathy, Dylan Field, and Amjad Masad [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-23 Etched announced a $300M Series C at a $10.3B valuation, less than a month after emerging from stealth with its production hardware [^1][^2][^3]. The round was led by Sequoia Capital with participation from Andreessen Horowitz, Jane Street, Diffusion Capital, and SK Hynix; TechCrunch reported it as the highest-valuation Sequoia-led Series C on record [^1][^2]. The valuation roughly doubled from a $5B mark set in a December 2025 round [^1]. Etched has opened an 80,000-square-foot facility housing an NPI lab and in-house SMT line in addition to its Taiwan production footprint [^2].
