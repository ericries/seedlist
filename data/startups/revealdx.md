## About

RevealDx is a Seattle-based medical AI company that develops RevealAI-Lung, software that generates a Malignancy Similarity Index (mSI) score for lung nodules to help radiologists distinguish benign nodules from early-stage lung cancers [^1]. The mSI is derived from real-world reference data from the National Lung Screening Trial, stratifying risk across more than 1,500 patient cohorts [^1]. RevealAI-Lung holds FDA clearance, European MDR certification, and Australian TGA approval, and is covered under U.S. Medicare CPT codes 0721T and 0722T [^1].
