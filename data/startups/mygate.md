## About

Mygate is a Bengaluru-headquartered technology platform for gated residential communities, founded in 2016 [^1][^2]. The platform provides resident welfare associations and residents with tools to manage gate security and visitor access, society finances and accounting, amenities booking, internal communications, and operations [^1][^2]. It also operates a brand engagement and advertising platform for consumer brands targeting community households [^1].

As of June 2026, Mygate serves more than 27,000 residential communities accounting for 5.7 million homes across India [^1][^2]. The company's operating revenue grew 80% to ₹173.5 crore in FY25 from ₹96.2 crore in FY24, while net loss narrowed 61% to ₹15.4 crore [^1].

On June 10, 2026, Mygate announced ₹225 crore in growth funding from Dharana Capital, which acquired a 12-14% stake through a mix of primary capital infusion and secondary share sales [^1][^2][^3]. The round was Mygate's first major fundraise in more than three years, following a ₹100 crore round in November 2022 [^1]. The company plans to expand to 10 million homes [^2].
