## About

Speakeasy is a New York–based unified operations and intelligence platform for live experiences and the "IRL economy," combining ticketing, reservations, table management, premium booking, payments, POS integrations, CRM, and analytics into a single system used by nightlife venues, sports and entertainment facilities, festivals, and membership clubs [^1] [^2]. Customers include TAO Group Hospitality, Fontainebleau Development, E11EVEN, Barstool, Cipriani, and Breakaway Music Festival [^1] [^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by Alex Manavi (CEO), Tamas An, and Paul Stacek, and reports it had bootstrapped to profitability with 500% year-over-year growth prior to closing this financing [^1] [^2].
