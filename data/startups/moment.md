## About

Moment is a New York-based AI operating system for investment management, founded by former quants and traders from Citadel Securities [^1][^2][^3]. The platform powers investment management for firms collectively managing $10T+ in client assets (up from $300B 18 months ago) including Edward Jones, LPL Financial, and Hightower Advisors [^1][^2]. The $78M Series C announced May 19, 2026 was led by Index Ventures with participation from Andreessen Horowitz, Avra, and other existing investors — coming less than 10 months after Moment's $36M Series B (also Index-led) and bringing total reported equity funding to $134M [^1][^2][^3].
