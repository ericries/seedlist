## About

NinjaOne is an Austin-based endpoint management platform that enables IT teams to manage and secure millions of devices through a unified dashboard [^1]. The company serves approximately 24,000 customers and has grown rapidly in valuation from $1.9B to $5B to $12.3B over the prior 18 months [^1]. NinjaOne recently acquired Dropsuite for $262M [^1].
