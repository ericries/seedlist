## About

Metris Energy is a London-based climate tech startup building an AI-native platform that unifies fragmented renewable energy asset data to help operators manage portfolios efficiently and access new revenue streams through automated workflows and intelligent systems [^1]. On 2026-09-21, the company announced a €4.35 million ($5 million) seed round led by PT1 Ventures with participation from Octopus Ventures, AENU, Blackfinch Ventures, Plug and Play, and Love Ventures [^1].
