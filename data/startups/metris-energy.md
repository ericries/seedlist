## About

Metris Energy is a London-based climate tech startup building an AI-native platform that unifies fragmented renewable energy asset data — consolidating information from inverters, meters, SCADA systems, CRMs, and other fragmented tools used by power producers — to help operators manage portfolios efficiently and access new revenue streams through automated workflows and intelligent systems [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded by Natasha Jones (CEO), launched publicly in 2025, and currently manages over 10,000 solar plants representing approximately 500MW of capacity, with reported eightfold year-on-year revenue growth [^2]. On 2026-09-21, Metris Energy announced a €4.35 million ($5 million) seed round led by PT1 Ventures with participation from Octopus Ventures, AENU, Blackfinch Ventures, Plug and Play, and Love Ventures, bringing total funding to date to $7.5 million [^1][^2][^3].
