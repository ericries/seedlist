## About

Avant Genomics is a Charlottesville, Virginia-based biotech spun out of the University of Virginia by Rachelle Turiello (CEO) and Renna Nouwairi (COO) that is developing a fully automated platform for liquid biopsy sample preparation in precision cancer detection [^1][^2][^3]. The company's Avant Source platform reduces sample preparation from over 50 manual steps to two steps, cuts processing time in half, and improves DNA yield by up to 70% compared to semi-automated tools — addressing a key bottleneck that currently limits clinical adoption of liquid biopsy [^1][^2]. On May 13, 2026, Avant Genomics announced more than $3M in funding from TitletownTech, Halyard Ventures, Virginia Venture Partners, CAV Angels, and Global Impact Fund, with proceeds supporting R&D, manufacturing, and hiring [^1][^2][^3].
