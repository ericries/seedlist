## About

Dopl Technologies is a Bothell, Washington-based medical technology company developing a telerobotic ultrasound platform to expand access to diagnostic ultrasound for patients regardless of location [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by former Nvidia medical AI engineer Ryan James alongside practicing cardiologists and radiologists, Dopl's product ecosystem includes Traverse (a remote robotic ultrasound system), Dopl Connect (a secure connectivity and data platform), and SonoFlex (a distributed clinical workforce and workflow management solution) [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-27, the company announced a $6.3 million seed round led by SpringTide Ventures, with participation from WRF Capital, Tacoma Venture Fund (previous pre-seed lead), HeartX, Transform Health Ventures, Precursor Ventures, and additional early-stage institutional investors [^1][^2]. Proceeds will support the company's pursuit of FDA clearance for its product ecosystem. The round brings total funding above $8 million [^1].
