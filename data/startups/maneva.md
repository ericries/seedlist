## About

Maneva is a Palo Alto-based industrial AI company that builds a "video-to-action" AI platform for manufacturing, transforming existing factory cameras into real-time operational decision-making tools using deep learning computer vision and agentic AI [^1] [^2] [^3]. The company was co-founded in late 2021 by CEO Rae Jeong, a former DeepMind engineer, and CTO Kelvin Chan, a former Magna International engineer [^1] [^2]. Maneva's platform monitors production lines and helps manufacturers detect defects, optimize throughput, and automate factory operations without requiring new camera infrastructure [^2] [^3].
