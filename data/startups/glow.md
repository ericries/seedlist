## About

Glow is a Palo Alto-based AI-native endpoint security company that emerged from stealth on 2026-07-22 with a $180 million Series A round at a $1.2 billion valuation — becoming a unicorn on day one [^1][^2][^3]. The round was co-led by Sequoia Capital, Cyberstarts, Greenoaks Capital, and Redpoint Ventures, with participation from Index Ventures, Lux Capital, Swish Ventures, Operator Collective, and Holly Ventures [^1][^2][^3]. Glow's platform uses specialized AI agents to monitor and control software, AI agents, and developer tools on employee devices, assessing risk in real time and enforcing security policy [^1][^2]. Founders Roi Tiger (former VP of Engineering at Meta), Omer Singer (former head of cybersecurity strategy at Snowflake), and Ophir Arie (former VP of R&D at Claroty) started the company in 2025 [^1][^3]. Emily Heath, former CISO at United Airlines and Docusign, joined as COO [^1]. Roughly 100 employees, 70% based in Israel; announced customers span healthcare, retail, and financial services [^1].
