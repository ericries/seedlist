## About

Hoola Health (formerly BabyMD) is a Bengaluru-based paediatric healthcare startup operating an integrated child healthcare model across India [^1][^2][^3]. The company runs neighborhood clinics that bundle routine consultations, vaccinations, advanced diagnostics, and developmental therapies for children, with kid-friendly clinic environments featuring play zones and therapy spaces [^1][^3]. The company was founded in 2024 by Deeksha Senguttuvan and currently operates five clinics across Bengaluru, having served more than 20,000 families within its first 18 months of operations [^1][^3].

On June 10, 2026, Hoola Health announced it had raised $5 million (approximately ₹47.6 Cr) in a funding round led by Peak XV Partners' seed platform Surge [^1][^2][^3]. Existing institutional investor W Health Ventures participated, alongside angel investors Ashish Gupta (MFV Partners), Abhishek Goyal (Tracxn co-founder), and Bijou Kurien (chairman of the Retail Association of India) [^1][^2][^3]. With this capital, Hoola Health plans to expand its physical footprint with 30 additional clinics across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Delhi NCR over the next two years [^1][^3].
