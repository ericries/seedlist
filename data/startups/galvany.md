## About

GALVANY is a Berlin-based heat pump platform integrating the acquisition, installation, and management of heat pump systems for German households and property owners [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by Raik Belka, the company has installed more than 2,500 heat pump systems and reported €20.1 million in revenue in 2025 — a sevenfold year-over-year increase — while ending the year profitable on an EBIT basis [^1][^2][^3]. Profitability at the seed stage is unusual in European climate tech.

At the core of GALVANY's offering is a connected energy platform built around the proprietary GALVANY Cube hardware, produced in partnership with Panasonic in Pilsen [^1][^2]. The platform combines heat pumps, battery storage, and GALVANY Fusion energy management software. The company plans to use the new capital to focus on the multi-family-home existing-building market, expand its housing-company customer base, and develop GALVANY Fusion further as an AI-powered "Agentic Energy OS" [^1][^2].
