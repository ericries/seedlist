## About

UrgentIQ is an AI-native electronic medical record (EMR) and clinical operating platform purpose-built for urgent care centers, spanning patient intake, clinical documentation, and billing [^1][^3]. The company markets itself as helping clinics see "8+ More Patients Per Day" with "50+ Fewer Clicks Per Patient," and maintains HIPAA and EPCS (electronic prescription for controlled substances) certifications [^3].
