## About

Stitch is a Riyadh-headquartered financial technology provider that builds a unified, cloud-native operating system for modern financial institutions, spanning lending, cards, payments, and ledgers [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2022 by Mohamed Oueida and aims to replace banks' fragmented, legacy core systems with a single AI-ready system of record that lets institutions launch new products up to 80% faster than traditional methods [^2][^3]. In the six months leading up to its May 2026 raise, more than $5 billion has been transacted on the Stitch platform; customer count grew 10x in 2025 and revenue grew 20x in the same period [^1]. The $25M Series A announced on May 14, 2026 was led by Andreessen Horowitz — the firm's first investment in the GCC — with continued participation from existing investors Arbor Ventures, COTU Ventures, Raed Ventures, and SVC, bringing total funding to $35M [^1][^2][^3].
