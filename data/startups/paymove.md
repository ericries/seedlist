## About

paymove is a Polish FinTech founded in 2022 that builds payment infrastructure, including occasional payments, e-commerce and mobile payments, and agentic payments driven by AI models [^1][^2]. The company operates across more than 2,000 locations in Poland, reaching over 600,000 users and processing hundreds of thousands of transactions annually [^1]. paymove aims to develop a Payment-as-a-Service platform and is pursuing international expansion, with advanced contract negotiations underway in Spain, Portugal, and Italy [^1][^2].
