## About

BUSINESSNEXT is a Noida, India-based enterprise software company that builds an autonomous operating platform for banks and financial institutions [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2007 and its software is used by more than one million bankers across over 75,000 branches globally, including HDFC Bank, Axis Bank, and Kotak Mahindra Bank [^2][^3]. On 2026-07-23 BUSINESSNEXT announced a $40 million Series C strategic investment from ServiceNow Ventures at a $700 million post-money valuation [^1][^2][^3]. The transaction gives ServiceNow approximately a 5% stake and pairs BUSINESSNEXT's customer-facing banking capabilities with ServiceNow's Financial Services Operations platform to expand autonomous banking across Asia-Pacific [^1][^2][^3].
