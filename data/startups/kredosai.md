## About

KredosAi is a Bellevue, Washington-based AI-powered enterprise collections and revenue recovery platform. The company was founded in 2021 by Balaji Sridharan (CEO) and Dave Thoms, both former T-Mobile executives [^1][^2][^3]. KredosAi describes its approach as "behavioural intelligence" — using AI to help enterprises recover late payments while preserving customer relationships. The company reports that its platform has cut write-offs by 11.5% and lifted customer lifetime value by 13.6% versus conventional collections across its customer base [^2].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|------|-------|--------|------|--------------|
| 2026-07-02 | Series A | $7M | BMW i Ventures | Motley Fool Ventures, Walter Ventures, Okapi Venture Capital, StartFast Ventures, SaaS Ventures, Stout Street Capital [^1][^2][^3][^4] |

The round was oversubscribed. New investors Motley Fool Ventures and Walter Ventures joined, while existing shareholders Okapi Venture Capital, StartFast Ventures, SaaS Ventures and Stout Street Capital participated in the follow-on [^2][^3]. Total funding raised to date is slightly over $10 million [^2]. Proceeds will fund go-to-market expansion into financial services and auto lending, product development (including a multi-agent framework with voice-agent capabilities), and roughly doubling headcount over the next year [^2][^3].

## What Investors Say

CEO Balaji Sridharan told GeekWire that the BMW i Ventures investment is "purely financial" and not tied to BMW's auto-lending business [^1].
