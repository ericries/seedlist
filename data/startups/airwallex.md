## About

Airwallex is a global financial platform providing payments, foreign-exchange, and embedded-finance infrastructure for businesses operating across borders [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2015 in Melbourne and now operates from Singapore as its global headquarters, serving more than 200,000 businesses worldwide [^1][^2]. In March 2026, Airwallex reported $1.3 billion in annualized revenue (up 74% year-over-year) and $287 billion in annualized transaction volume [^1][^2].
