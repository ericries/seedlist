## About

Trase is an AI operating system company building agentic infrastructure for highly regulated industries such as healthcare and defense [^1]. The company was incubated by Red Cell Partners and publicly launched in late 2025 [^2]. Its core platform, Trase Origin, deploys specialized agents inside customer environments — for example, Duke University Health System's Division of Cardiology, where Trase agents automate the more than 5,000 faxes the clinic receives each month, work previously handled by hand by medical assistants and nurses [^2]. The company has offices in Seattle and the Washington, D.C. metro area, with a team of 55 [^2].
