## About

Primo is a Paris-area AI platform that unifies device management, SaaS applications, identity, and IT support into a single system with autonomous AI agents that execute complex IT workflows end-to-end — such as employee onboarding and offboarding [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022, the company serves 400+ customers across ten countries with roughly €3.4M in annual recurring revenue as of the funding announcement [^1][^3].
