## About

Cato is a Milan-based enterprise AI company (legal entity AZMB Srl) that helps companies find, qualify, and win public-sector tender opportunities in Italy and across Europe [^1][^2][^3]. The platform ingests procurement notices, matches them to a customer's capabilities, and automates the drafting of compliant bid documents [^1]. The company was founded in 2025 by Andrea Zorzetto (CEO) and Matteo Bossolini, and reports serving more than 100 customers within its first six months of operation [^3][^4].

On September 7, 2026, Cato raised a €6 million seed round led by Keen Venture Partners, with participation from Vento, Heartfelt, Moonstone, BHeroes, Alecla7, Nova Venture, and angel investors from Lexroom, Sibill, and Pillar — bringing total funding to date to €7.6 million [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds are earmarked for platform development, commercial expansion, team growth, and international expansion beyond Italy [^3].
