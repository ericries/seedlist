## About

ULOOK builds autonomous satellite swarms that use radio-frequency (RF) sensing and AI to detect, classify, geolocate, and track radio signals from ships, aircraft, and ground-based transmitters for defence, maritime surveillance, and environmental monitoring [^1][^3]. Founded in 2024 and headquartered in Bengaluru, the company's founders have collectively worked on 12+ satellite missions [^1][^3]. ULOOK joined Peak XV's Surge 12 cohort in September 2026 [^1][^2][^3].
