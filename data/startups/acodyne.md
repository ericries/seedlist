## About

Acodyne is a Copenhagen-based startup developing a family of electric, autonomous heavy-lift cargo drones for defence, offshore energy, and remote logistics applications [^1][^2]. Its E100 prototype is designed to carry approximately 100 kg at speeds up to 450 km/h, with initial flight testing planned before the end of 2026 [^1]. The company positions its aircraft as an alternative to helicopters for time-critical and hard-to-reach logistics missions [^2].
