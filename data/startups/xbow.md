## About

XBOW is a Seattle-based autonomous offensive security company founded in January 2024 by Oege de Moor, the creator of GitHub Copilot and GitHub Advanced Security [^2][^3]. The company's platform combines AI reasoning with offensive security workflows to deliver expert-level penetration testing at machine speed, serving more than 100 enterprise customers globally including Moderna and Seznam [^1][^2]. On May 6, 2026, XBOW announced a $35M Series C extension from strategic investors, building on its previously announced $120M Series C round led by DFJ Growth and Northzone, and bringing cumulative funding above $270M at a valuation over $1 billion [^1][^2].
