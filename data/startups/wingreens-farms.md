## About

Wingreens Farms is a Gurgaon-based packaged foods company founded in 2011 by Anju Srivastava and Arjun Srivastava that produces healthy snacks, sauces, dips, breakfast cereals, non-dairy milk, and protein shakes [^1][^2]. The company also operates the cold-pressed beverage brand Raw Pressery [^1]. The ₹120 crore (~$12.6M) Series D announced May 11, 2026 was led by Ashish Kacholia with participation from Alchemy Fund, alongside the 100% acquisition of sustainable food company Safe Harvest through a share-swap transaction [^1][^2][^3]. Wingreens has reached EBITDA profitability in FY26 and is targeting a public listing within two years [^1][^2]. Capital will support product portfolio expansion, distribution growth, supply chain integration, and investments in innovation and farmer partnerships [^1][^2].
