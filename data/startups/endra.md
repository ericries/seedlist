## About

Endra is a Stockholm-based AI platform that automates the design work of mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) consultants — the engineers who design the systems running modern buildings [^1][^2][^3]. The company announced a $50 million Series A on June 1, 2026, bringing its total raised to $75 million in under two years [^1][^2]. Endra was founded by childhood friends Niklas Lindgren and Anton Juric, who sold a prior company in an adjacent space, alongside David Rydberg and Gustav Hammarlund, both former low-latency trading engineers at Goldman Sachs in Stockholm [^2][^3]. Endra works with global engineering consultancies including AtkinsRealis, Buro Happold, Hoare Lea, Ramboll, and AFRY [^2][^3].
