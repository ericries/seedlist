## About

Dessn is a London-based AI-native product design and development startup founded in 2024 by Nim Cheema and Gabriella Hachem [^1][^2]. The company built a visual design environment operated by conversational AI prompts and connected directly to users' codebase, enabling non-technical product teams and designers to create and iterate products in real-time code [^1]. The €5M round announced May 12, 2026 combines a Seed round and a previously unannounced pre-Seed; the larger Seed tranche was led by Connect Ventures with participation from Betaworks, N49P, and others [^1][^2].
