## About

Resect Artificial Intelligence (Resect AI) is a Seattle-based enterprise AI company building "the accountability layer for artificial intelligence by removing or 'resecting' hallucinations and improving the factual accuracy of large language models" [^1]. The company also operates an office in the greater Portland area and targets customers in publishing, finance, healthcare, research, and education [^1]. On September 3, 2026 Resect AI launched out of stealth with $25 million in funding from undisclosed private equity investors [^1]. The company is led by CEO Kevin Owens and Chief AI Officer Tim Walton, alongside co-founders Tyler Gerber (COO) and Tommy Lofgren (CPMO), with Mark Curcio serving as Chief Responsible AI Officer and board member [^2]. According to the announcement, Resect AI's patented technology "operates in-stream — looking deep inside large language models in real time to observe, detect, interpret, and modify model behavior before a hallucination can" occur [^3].
