## About

Resect Artificial Intelligence (Resect AI) is a Seattle-based enterprise AI company building "the accountability layer for artificial intelligence by removing or 'resecting' hallucinations and improving the factual accuracy of large language models" [^1]. The company also operates an office in the greater Portland area and targets customers in publishing, finance, healthcare, research, and education [^1]. On September 3, 2026 Resect AI launched out of stealth with $25 million in funding from undisclosed private equity investors [^1]. The company is led by CEO Kevin Owens and Chief AI Officer Tim Walton, and focuses on detecting hallucinations, modifying model behavior, and addressing enterprise AI governance and compliance challenges [^1].
