## About

Oak Hill Bio is a Cambridge, Massachusetts-based biotechnology company advancing rugonersen, an investigational antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) therapy for the treatment of Angelman syndrome [^1][^2]. Rugonersen was originally developed at Roche; several former members of the program joined Oak Hill Bio to lead its continued development [^1]. Proceeds from the Series A will be used to advance rugonersen into a pivotal Phase 3 clinical study, expected to begin in mid-2026 [^1][^2].
