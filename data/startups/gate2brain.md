## About

Gate2Brain is a University of Barcelona spin-off founded in 2020 from IRB Barcelona, the University of Barcelona, and Hospital Sant Joan de Déu Barcelona, advancing a proprietary peptide-shuttle platform for transporting therapies across the blood-brain barrier to address paediatric and adult brain tumours and other central nervous system diseases [^1][^2][^3]. The company's lead candidate, G2B-002, targets diffuse intrinsic pontine glioma (DIPG) and pediatric glioblastoma and has received Orphan Drug Designation from both the U.S. FDA and the European Medicines Agency [^1][^2].
