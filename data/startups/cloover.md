## About

Cloover is a Berlin-based AI-native energy platform that provides independent installers with an operating system to sell residential solar, heat pumps, and home electrification products with embedded point-of-sale financing [^1][^2][^3]. The company positions itself as an "AI-native neo-utility," letting installers keep their brand, customers, and choice of hardware while Cloover handles the financing, back-office, and software layer [^1][^2].

On September 1, 2026, Cloover announced an additional €86.2 million (approximately $100 million) financing facility, bringing its total financing capacity to more than €1.12 billion ($1.3 billion), backed by a €350 million guarantee from the European Investment Fund [^1][^2][^3]. The company reported reaching profitability three years after launch, with a revenue run rate exceeding €301.7 million (approximately $350 million) [^1][^2].
