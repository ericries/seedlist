## About

Monumental is an Amsterdam-based construction-robotics company building autonomous bricklaying robots and an AI software platform, Atrium, to address labor shortages in the building industry [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2021 by Salar al Khafaji (CEO) and Sebastiaan Visser (CTO), who previously co-founded data-visualization startup Silk before it was acquired by Palantir in 2016 [^2][^3]. Monumental currently operates a fleet of more than 150 robots on construction projects across the Netherlands and the United Kingdom, and says its robots have helped build over 100 homes plus a school, community center, hotel, and canal walls [^1][^2]. The company charges contractors on an outcome-based model — paying for completed walls rather than purchasing equipment — and plans to expand across Europe and enter the United States later in 2026 [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-15 Monumental announced a $32 million Series B led by Khosla Ventures, with participation from existing investors Plural and Hummingbird [^1][^2][^3].
