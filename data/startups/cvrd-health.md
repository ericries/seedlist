## About

CVRD Health (pronounced "covered") is a Miami-based technology platform modernizing how federal government contractors manage health and welfare benefits under the Service Contract Act and Davis-Bacon Act [^1][^2][^3]. The platform offers contractors real-time visibility into benefit obligations, spend, and reimbursement status, paired with full benefits administration through an Individual Coverage Health Reimbursement Arrangement (ICHRA) that provides each employee tax-free funds to choose coverage [^1][^2]. The $5M seed round announced May 21, 2026 was led by Upfront Ventures, with participation from Waterline Ventures and Distributed Ventures; capital will accelerate platform development, expand compliance and member advocacy teams, and bring the solution to federal contractors of all sizes nationwide [^1][^2][^3].
