## About

Algorithmiq is a quantum software company founded in 2020 by Sabrina Maniscalco (CEO), Guillermo García-Pérez (CSO), Matteo Rossi (CTO), and Boris Sokolov [^1][^2]. The company develops quantum software for breakthroughs in chemistry, materials science, and life sciences via physically meaningful, energy-efficient quantum computation [^1][^2]. The €18M round announced May 11, 2026 was led by United Ventures with participation from CDP Venture Capital and Inventure VC, bringing total funding to €36M and reportedly representing Italy's largest-ever VC investment in a quantum startup; Algorithmiq is simultaneously relocating its global headquarters from Helsinki to Milan while maintaining significant Finnish operations [^1][^2][^3].
