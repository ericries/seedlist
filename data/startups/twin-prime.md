## About

Twin Prime is a London-based frontier AI lab founded in 2025 by George Lentzas, Stephane Sezer, Drew Calcagno, and Michael Leite-Garcia, building AI models designed to process diverse sensor data for real-time decision-making in defense and national security applications [^1][^2]. The $10M pre-seed announced May 14, 2026 was led by Expeditions Fund with participation from THEON International, Quorum, and a group of American and European venture investors; the round was paired with a planned joint venture with defense prime THEON to integrate Twin Prime's AI across THEON's product portfolio [^1][^2][^3].
