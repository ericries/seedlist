## About

ORiS is a Turin, Italy-based DeepTech startup developing laser-based wireless power transmission (WPT) systems for space and dual-use applications, with the goal of enabling satellites to recharge one another in orbit without relying solely on solar panels [^1][^2][^3]. The company's terrestrial prototype platform, LOONA, has demonstrated wireless power transfer to a hovering drone at distances of more than 100 metres, with drone testing conducted through NATO's DIANA defence accelerator [^1][^2]. ORiS plans to have a flight model ready by the end of July 2026 ahead of a planned in-orbit demonstration in 2027 with mission partner DCUBED, beaming power over a distance of 10 metres [^1][^2]. The company is incubated at I3P (Politecnico di Torino's incubator) and ESA BIC Turin [^3].

On 2026-07-21, ORiS announced a €5 million pre-seed round — €4.5 million in equity co-led by Earlybird and Pitchdrive, plus a €500k grant from the Piedmont Region's "Consolidamento patrimoniale e crescita delle start up innovative" programme [^1][^2][^3].
