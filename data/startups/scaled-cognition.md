## About

Scaled Cognition is a Mountain View AI model lab building what it calls "Super-Reliable Intelligence" for enterprise use [^1][^2]. Its flagship model, APT (Agentic Pretrained Transformer), is designed to deliver conversational quality while eliminating hallucinations and enforcing policy adherence for high-stakes enterprise applications in financial services, healthcare, telecom, and insurance [^1][^2]. CEO Dan Roth previously built and sold an agentic AI company to Microsoft; CTO Dan Klein is a UC Berkeley AI professor and NLP researcher [^2]. Genesys, a cloud contact-center provider serving more than 8,000 organizations in over 100 countries, uses APT inside the Genesys Cloud platform for agentic virtual agent capabilities and is also an investor in the company [^1].
