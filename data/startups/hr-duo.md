## About

HR Duo is a Dunshaughlin, Ireland-based HR and workforce-management SaaS platform for SMEs with 100–1,000 employees in shift-based and frontline-heavy industries [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports more than 40 employees and 27,000+ daily users across Ireland, the UK and Romania, and says it has grown ARR roughly sixfold since 2020 while maintaining ~85% gross margins [^1][^2]. HR Duo plans to use its 2026 funding to grow a UK sales team and strengthen SaaS infrastructure, with more than 70% of its current sales pipeline in the UK [^1][^2][^3].
