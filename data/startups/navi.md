## About

Navi is a Bengaluru-based digital financial services company offering UPI payments, lending through NBFC arm Navi Finserv, insurance, and mutual funds [^1][^2]. It was founded in 2018 by Flipkart co-founder Sachin Bansal after his departure following Flipkart's acquisition by Walmart [^1][^2].
