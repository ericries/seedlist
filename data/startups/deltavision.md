## About

deltaVision is a Munich, Germany-based developer of fluidic systems — including valves, tanks, and propellant-management components — for launch vehicles, satellites, lunar landers, and in-orbit servicing spacecraft [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2022 as a Technical University of Munich spinout, the company has grown to approximately 134 employees and describes itself as profitable prior to raising external institutional capital, and plans to launch its first integrated refuelling payload into orbit in March 2027 [^2][^3]. On 2026-07-21, deltaVision announced its first equity funding round of €10.2 million, led by KT Ventures and Valemount Capital (which had each previously provided convertible loans) with participation from Futury Capital and several family offices [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds will fund an increase in component production to 5,000 units per year, establishment of a French subsidiary (deltaVision SASU) in Nouvelle-Aquitaine focused on sensing and intelligence products, and continued R&D on integrated propellant-transfer systems for in-orbit refuelling [^2][^3].
