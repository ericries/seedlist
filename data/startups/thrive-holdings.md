## About

Thrive Holdings is an OpenAI-backed operating company that acquires and modernizes traditional service businesses using AI. Founded by Anuj Mehndiratta and Kareem Zaki, the company aims to bring AI-enabled workflows to legacy industries including infrastructure, professional services, and enterprise operations.
