## About

Proception is a robotics startup developing high-dexterity robotic hands designed to mimic the capabilities of human hands [^1][^2]. The company's robotic hand features 22 degrees of freedom and multiple joints per finger to enable a wide range of dexterous motions [^1]. To train its models, Proception developed a sensor-laden glove that captures human hand interaction data without requiring a robot in the loop [^1]. The company was founded by Zhongjie 'Jay' Li, a former technical lead on Tesla's Optimus humanoid robot program; Li and Tesla reached a settlement of a trade secret lawsuit dismissed shortly before the funding announcement [^1][^2]. Proception is a Y Combinator-backed company and is shipping its first batch of robotic hands to researchers and robotics companies [^1].
