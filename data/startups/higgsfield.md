## About

Higgsfield is an AI media generation platform founded in 2023 by Alex Mashrabov, a former Snap executive [^1]. The company builds tools for creating AI-generated images and video, including Cinema Studio for filmmakers and Marketing Studio for marketing teams [^1]. As of the August 2026 Series B, Higgsfield reported approximately $700M in annualized revenue, 30 million users across 200 countries, and 390 Fortune 500 enterprise clients [^1].
