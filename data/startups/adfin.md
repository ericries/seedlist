## About

Adfin is a London-based agentic finance platform founded in 2024 by Tom Pope (formerly Worldpay and Tink) and Ciprian Diaconasu (founding engineer at Mambu) that automates revenue collection and money-movement workflows for SMEs and finance teams by owning both the underlying financial infrastructure and the agentic workflows on top [^1][^2]. The €15.3M Series A announced May 12, 2026 was led by Index Ventures with participation from Visionaries Club, Stéphane Kurgan (former COO of King), and Andrey Khusid (founder of Miro); the round brings total funding to $30M in under two years [^1][^2]. Capital will fund engineering and sales hiring plus international expansion [^1].
