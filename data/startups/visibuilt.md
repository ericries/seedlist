## About

Visibuilt is a Danish biotech developing visiBINDER, a mycelium-based biobased binder designed to replace carbon-intensive materials such as bitumen and cement in paving and infrastructure applications [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2022 and closed a €3.34 million Seed round on 2026-07-13 to move its technology from lab pilots toward market validation and industry testing [^1][^2].
