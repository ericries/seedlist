## About

Current is a New York-based consumer fintech platform offering banking, payments, liquidity, and credit products [^1]. On June 11, 2026, the company announced an $80 million Series E equity financing led by Springcoast Partners at a $1.5 billion valuation [^1][^2][^3]. CEO Stuart Sopp said the company has posted three consecutive years of more than 70% growth and is crossing over to profitability [^1]. Andreessen Horowitz, Tiger Global Management, Avenir, Foundation Capital, Wellington Management, Sapphire Ventures, General Catalyst, Cross River, and QED Investors participated [^1][^3].
