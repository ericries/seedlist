## About

Cylake is a cybersecurity company building AI-native, agentic security architecture on a single, sovereign data foundation for organizations that require total data sovereignty and cannot use products tied to the public cloud [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Nir Zuk (also founder of Palo Alto Networks), Wilson Xu, and Ehud (Udi) Shamir [^1]. Cylake launched from stealth on March 5, 2026 and is currently working with design partners; product availability is anticipated in early 2027 [^1].

## What Investors Say

Asheem Chandna, Partner at Greylock Partners: "The next generation of cybersecurity will be AI-native, agentic, and built on holistic data and context. Cylake is focused on a segment of the market where security must operate under full control to meet regulatory and operational reality." [^1]

## What Founders Say

Nir Zuk, Founder and CEO of Cylake: "Cybersecurity is constantly evolving, and sometimes new challenges demand completely new approaches. Cylake is for institutions where maintaining full control over data and operations is not optional." [^1][^2]
