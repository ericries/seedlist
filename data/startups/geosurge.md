## About

geoSurge is a London-based DeepTech AI company that helps brands understand and shape how they are represented inside generative AI systems such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Claude [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2025 by Francisco Vigo and describes its focus as "Corpus Engineering" — targeting the underlying data models AI systems train on rather than surface-level citation tracking [^1][^2][^3]. geoSurge says it works with customers across four continents in Financial Services, Education, and Hospitality [^1][^2].
