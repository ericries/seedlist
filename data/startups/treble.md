## About

Treble is a Reykjavík-based sound and acoustics simulation platform that generates synthetic acoustic training data for voice AI, wearables, robotics and other "physical AI" systems, based on wave-based physics simulation rather than recorded audio [^1][^2][^3]. The company's platform is used by customers including Amazon (Alexa) and Logitech to model virtual acoustic environments and stress-test voice products under reverberation and noise [^1][^2].

**Finnur Pind, Co-Founder & CEO, quoted in TechCrunch:**
