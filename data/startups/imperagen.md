## About

Imperagen is a Manchester, UK-based biotech spun out of the Manchester Institute of Biotechnology in 2021 by Andrew Currin, Tim Eyes, and Andy Almond [^1]. The company develops enzyme engineering technology that combines quantum physics-based simulation with AI models to replace traditional trial-and-error lab processes with computational prediction of enzyme variants [^1]. The £5M (~$6.7M) Seed announced May 20, 2026 was led by PXN Ventures with participation from IQ Capital and Northern Gritstone [^1].
