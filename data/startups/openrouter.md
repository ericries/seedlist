## About

OpenRouter is a New York-based AI model exchange that lets developers and enterprises access, route, and optimize across hundreds of AI models through a single API [^1]. Founded in 2023 by Alex Atallah (a co-founder of OpenSea), the platform sits between application developers and the major model providers, abstracting away differences in pricing, capacity, and capability [^1][^2].

By May 2026, OpenRouter was processing roughly 25 trillion tokens per week — about 100 trillion per month — representing a 5x increase from six months prior [^1][^3].
