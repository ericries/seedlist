## About

Redpine is a Stockholm-based AI infrastructure company building a licensed-data API for AI agents, founded in 2024 by Anders Hammarbäck and David Österdahl [^1][^2]. The platform lets enterprises securely connect agentic AI workflows to licensed, high-quality, multimodal datasets that aren't otherwise reachable on the public web [^1][^2]. The September 2025 pre-seed round was angel-led, with backers including Colin M. Evans (OpenAI), Feng Hong (Xiaomi co-founder), Anna Nordell Westling (Sana co-founder), Daniel Langkilde (Kognic founder), Gustav Lindqvist (Perplexity), and a network of Spotify alumni [^3][^4][^5].
