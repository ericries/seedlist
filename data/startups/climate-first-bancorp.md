## About

Climate First Bancorp is a St. Petersburg, Florida-based bank holding company founded in 2021 by Ken LaRoe [^1][^2]. It operates Climate First Bank, an FDIC-insured, values-based digital community bank positioned as the world's first founded specifically to combat the climate crisis, and OneEthos, a Federal Reserve Bank-regulated fintech arm focused on solar lending [^2][^3]. The bank is a Certified B Corp, a 1% for the Planet member, and has been operationally net-zero since opening in June 2021 [^2][^3].

As of the June 2026 funding announcement, Climate First Bank had grown to nearly $2 billion in assets over five years and generated more than $11.5 million in net profit in 2025 [^2]. OneEthos has originated over $450 million in solar loans across more than 9,300 individual solar projects, serving a network of over 8,000 solar sales representatives nationwide [^2][^3].
