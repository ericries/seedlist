## About

Ambrook is a New York-based accounting and financial-management software company originally built for farmers, ranchers, and rural businesses and now expanding into trucking, construction, and real estate [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports 8,000 farms and businesses on the platform, up from 2,500 a year ago [^1]. Ambrook has raised $59M in cumulative funding [^2][^3].

Proceeds will fund product expansion beyond agriculture into other "real economy" verticals and continued platform investment [^1][^2].
