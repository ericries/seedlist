## About

Allen Control Systems (ACS) is an Austin, Texas-based defense technology company developing autonomous precision weapon systems for U.S. and allied militaries [^1]. Its flagship product, Bullfrog, is an AI-powered robotic weapon station that mounts an M240 machine gun and uses computer vision and precision robotics to detect and defeat small unmanned aerial system (drone) threats [^1][^2]. Bullfrog has been deployed by the U.S. Army and demonstrated a 100% success rate during Technology Readiness Experiment 2026 (T-REX 26-1), a Department of Defense counter-drone testing event [^1][^3]. The company was co-founded by Steve Simoni (President) and Mike Wior (CEO) [^3].
