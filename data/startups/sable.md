## About

Sable is a San Francisco–based AI company building autonomous "AI employees" that can navigate software interfaces, run product demonstrations, and conduct customer-facing conversations [^1][^2]. Its flagship product, Aidan, combines computer use, vision, and voice to lead live product experiences, answer buyer questions, and gather feedback in real time [^2]. The company, less than a year old, counts Notion and Decagon among its production customers [^1][^2]. It was co-founded by Nim Ravid (CEO), Leon Chen, Linda He, and Itamar Rocha [^2].
