## About

Skapion is a defense technology company developing a mobile counter-swarm system designed to detect, engage, and neutralize coordinated drone attacks at scale [^1]. The company is headquartered in Washington, D.C. and operates a research and development center in Ramat Gan, Israel [^2]. The system is being designed to support hundreds of simultaneous engagements while significantly reducing interception costs compared with conventional air defense solutions [^1].

Co-founder Pini Yungman previously served as general manager of Rafael Advanced Defense Systems' Air and Missile Defense Systems division, where he worked on programs including David's Sling and Iron Dome [^2].
