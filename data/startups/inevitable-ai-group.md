## About

Inevitable AI Group (IAIG) is a Tel Aviv-based AI-native venture studio that builds and launches software companies designed to capitalize on the changing economics of AI-driven software development [^1][^2]. Founded in 2026 by Nimrod Lehavi (CEO) and Ofer Bar-Or (COO), the studio partners with entrepreneurs to launch AI-native products in weeks in proven software markets, providing them with the tools to grow rapidly [^1]. The thesis: advances in AI let small teams build, launch, and scale software products faster and more efficiently than ever before [^1].
