## About

Ploy is a San Francisco-based AI growth platform that automates marketing operations and turns company websites into autonomous, always-on growth surfaces [^1]. The company emerged from stealth on June 17, 2026, with a $27 million seed round [^1][^2]. Founder Bryant Chou is the former co-founder and CTO of Webflow [^1][^2]. Ploy's platform is designed to run a marketing team's full system — campaign planning, content production, website optimization, and reporting — under one autonomous AI layer aimed at marketing teams and agencies [^2][^3].
