## About

TidalSense is a Cambridge-based medical device company that builds an AI-driven handheld diagnostic — N-Tidal — that detects lung function changes to diagnose chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and asthma in under five minutes at the point of care [^1]. Formerly Cambridge Respiratory Innovations, the company was founded in 2013 and its patented sensor technology relies on relaxed breathing rather than forced spirometry, reducing technique-dependent misdiagnosis [^1]. COPD is the third most common cause of death in England, killing about 30,000 people a year and costing the NHS £1.9 billion [^2].
