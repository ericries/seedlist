## About

CellCentric is a Cambridge, UK-based clinical-stage biotech (with an office in Boston) developing inobrodib, an oral small-molecule p300/CBP inhibitor for advanced blood cancers, including multiple myeloma [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2003 by Will West (CEO) and Cambridge geneticist Azim Surani [^4]. On May 6, 2026, CellCentric announced an oversubscribed $220M Series D led by Venrock Healthcare Capital Partners — Europe's largest private biotech fundraise of 2026 to date [^1][^3]. Proceeds will fund the Phase 2 DOMMINO-1 study and the launch of the global Phase 3 DOMMINO-2 trial in the second half of 2026 [^1].
