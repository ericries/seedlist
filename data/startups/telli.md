## About

telli, a Berlin-based startup founded in 2024 by Seb Hapte-Selassie, Philipp Baumanns, and Finn zur Muehlen, builds AI that runs customer-facing operations for B2C companies. The platform lets businesses build and deploy AI agents across channels, manage conversations, review calls, improve performance, and automate the operational work behind sales, service, and support — replacing legacy stacks of CRMs, call centres, tickets, queues, and dashboards [^1][^2].
