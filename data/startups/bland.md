## About

Bland is a San Francisco-based voice AI company building a platform for complex, high-stakes enterprise conversations [^1]. Founded in 2023, the company has raised more than $100M in under three years [^1][^2]. CEO Isaiah Granet has stated the company is "focused on the calls that are hardest to automate" [^1].
