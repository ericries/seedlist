## About

Highland Electric Fleets, founded in 2019 and headquartered in Beverly, Massachusetts, provides Electrification-as-a-Service (EaaS) to school districts, municipalities, and fleet operators — supplying electric school buses, charging infrastructure, software, and operational support through long-term service agreements [^1][^2]. The company has projects in 30 states; its electric school buses have logged more than 10 million miles in operation, and it is contracted to supply 500 electric buses for the upcoming Los Angeles Olympic Games [^1][^2].

This $75M closes an additional preferred equity commitment from Galvanize and affiliates, bringing total preferred equity commitments to $150M [^1]. The investment is the first deployment from Galvanize's Credit and Capital Solutions fund, which raised $1.3B in 2025 to provide tailored financing for clean energy companies and projects [^2].
