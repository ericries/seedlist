## About

SkyPilot is a Berkeley-based AI compute platform that unifies GPU access across hyperscale clouds, specialized "neoclouds," Kubernetes clusters, and mixed GPU generations, allowing AI teams to launch, manage, and scale workloads across providers without rebuilding applications for each environment [^1][^2][^3]. The company emerged from stealth on 2026-07-21 with a $20 million seed round led by Lux Capital, with participation from Coatue, Amplify Partners, Foundation Capital, Race Capital, and The House Fund [^1][^2][^3]. SkyPilot grew out of an open-source project incubated at UC Berkeley's Sky Computing Lab (also the birthplace of Apache Spark and Ray) and was co-founded by Databricks and Anyscale co-founder Ion Stoica, Berkeley professor Scott Shenker, CEO Zongheng Yang, and Berkeley researchers Zhanghao Wu and Romil Bhardwaj [^1][^2][^3]. Notable technology-operator angels also participated: Databricks CEO Ali Ghodsi, Google Chief Scientist Jeff Dean, Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch, Replit CEO Amjad Masad, Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue, and dbt Labs CEO Tristan Handy [^1][^3].
