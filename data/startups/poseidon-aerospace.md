## About

Poseidon Aerospace is an Alameda, California-based startup developing autonomous, unmanned cargo aircraft for commercial and defense logistics, with its first vehicle called Egret [^1]. The company was co-founded by David Zagaynov (CEO) and Parker Tenney [^1]. Poseidon focuses on improving cargo movement rather than pursuing flashy autonomy features, with Zagaynov describing the approach as: "logistics is a commodity, and so if you sell a commodity and do it better, faster, cheaper, the demand is all yours" [^1].
