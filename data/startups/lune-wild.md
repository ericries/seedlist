## About

Lune & Wild is a London-based premium baby and children's food brand founded in 2021 by cousins Lara Rodgers and Nadia Simonds; the company makes chef-developed meals and snacks for babies and young children, positioned in the premium nutrition segment with a hands-on production model and a recent listing on Ocado.com [^1][^2][^3]. The £2 million Series A announced June 1, 2026 (reported as €2.3 million in continental press) was led by Guinness Ventures with participation from NHS GP and "The Doctor's Kitchen" founder Dr. Rupy Aujla and former Ella's Kitchen managing director Doug Struthers (now Lune & Wild chairman), alongside returning angels Charlie Gardiner (formerly Little Houses Group), Siân Parry Jones (co-founder, Archer Street Cocktail Bar), and Harry Bloice (former commercial director, Butternut Box); proceeds will fund production capacity, retail expansion, team growth, and marketing as the business reports doubling revenue annually and selling more than one million meals over the past 12 months [^1][^2][^3].
