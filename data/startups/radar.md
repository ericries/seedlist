## About

RADAR is a New York-based AI-powered retail intelligence platform combining proprietary overhead sensors with software and an analytics layer into an end-to-end system — ceiling-mounted sensors read every tagged item continuously, tracking precise location and movement across the sales floor, stockroom, and fitting rooms [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2018 by CEO Spencer Hewett, the $170M Series B announced May 20, 2026 was led by Gideon Strategic Partners and Nimble Partners with participation from Align Ventures, taking the company to a $1B unicorn valuation [^1][^2][^3].
