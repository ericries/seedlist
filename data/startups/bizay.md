## About

Bizay is a Lisbon-based technology platform for customized products, operating across 21 countries in Europe and the Americas. Founded in 2013 by CEO Sérgio Vieira and co-founders, the company claims the world's largest catalogue of customisable products, spanning merchandising, packaging, business essentials, store decorations, and staff uniforms. Its supply chain is anchored by production hubs in Iberia, Brazil, and Dallas (US), with over 200 employees and 50,000+ SKU variants serving SMB customers [^1][^2].
