## About

Lucis is a Paris-based preventive health platform that combines blood biomarker analysis with AI to deliver personalised, science-based health recommendations [^1][^2]. The platform analyses more than 110 blood biomarkers across metabolic health, hormones, cardiovascular risk, inflammation, and nutrient levels [^1].

Founded in 2025 by Maxime Berthelot and Baptiste Debever, Lucis reached 10,000 users in under a year [^1][^2]. Among users who completed a six-month follow-up, the company reports that 75% improved at least three biomarkers without medication [^1].
