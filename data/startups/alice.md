## About

Alice, formerly known as ActiveFence, is an AI trust, safety, and security company headquartered in New York and Tel Aviv [^1]. Founded in 2018, the company offers a platform called WonderSuite for automated red-teaming, runtime guardrails, and continuous monitoring of AI systems [^1]. Alice's customers include Anthropic, Google, and Nvidia, and the company reports approaching $100M in annual recurring revenue [^1][^2].
