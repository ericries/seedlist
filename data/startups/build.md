## About

Build is a New York-based AI infrastructure startup automating due diligence for large real estate and infrastructure projects, a category it calls "agentic real estate" [^1][^2]. The platform analyzes information from more than 1,600 data sources and evaluates planning, environmental, power, and political constraints in parallel rather than sequence, helping customers identify risks earlier and focus resources on the highest-potential opportunities [^1][^2]. Build was founded in 2024 by James Stirrat-Ellis, a former architect who worked on Singapore's Changi Airport expansion before leaving a Harvard master's program to start the company, and co-founder Ben McClusky [^1][^2]. The company reports that its platform has been deployed across more than 100 projects in 15 countries for governments, Fortune 500 companies, and institutional real estate groups including Tishman Speyer, and that it recently signed its first hyperscaler client [^1][^2].
