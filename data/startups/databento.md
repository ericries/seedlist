## About

Databento is a Salt Lake City–based market data platform that provides institutional-grade financial data on demand to trading firms [^1]. The company writes its own feed handlers for more than 70 exchanges and colocates servers at exchanges to capture data directly from the source [^2]. Databento serves over 3,000 firms and says revenue grew 6.65x year over year, with enterprise retention above 97% since inception [^2]. The company reports it has been profitable every month while operating with 24 employees [^3].

CEO Christina Qi previously ran a hedge fund trading $7 billion a day before founding Databento with the goal of unbundling and modernizing the market data stack that has historically been dominated by Bloomberg and LSEG [^3].
