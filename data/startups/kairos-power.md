## About

Kairos Power is an advanced nuclear energy company headquartered in Alameda, California, developing the KP-FHR (fluoride salt-cooled high-temperature reactor) technology, with its Hermes 2 Demonstration Plant under construction in Oak Ridge, Tennessee [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-21, Kairos Power and Samsung C&T (the construction and trading arm of Samsung Group) announced a strategic investment and collaboration of up to $100 million to support KP-FHR deployment, structured as approximately $70 million in direct equity investment plus $30 million of in-kind engineering services, subject to regulatory approvals [^1][^2][^3]. The partnership complements Kairos Power's multi-plant development agreement with Google to deliver reactors capable of generating about 500 megawatts of electricity by 2035 [^1].
