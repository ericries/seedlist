## About

Kairos Power is an advanced nuclear energy company headquartered in Alameda, California, developing the KP-FHR (fluoride salt-cooled high-temperature reactor) technology, with its Hermes 2 Demonstration Plant under construction in Oak Ridge, Tennessee [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-21, Kairos Power and Samsung C&T (the engineering and construction arm of Samsung Group) announced a strategic investment and collaboration of up to $100 million to support KP-FHR deployment, structured as $70 million in direct equity investment plus $30 million of in-kind engineering services, subject to regulatory approvals [^1][^2][^3]. Under the collaboration, Samsung C&T — which has built or helped build about a dozen nuclear reactors globally — will serve as an engineering and construction partner for KP-FHR deployment [^1]. The investment complements Kairos Power's multi-plant development agreement with Google to deliver about half a gigawatt (~500 MW) of clean electricity by 2035, with the initial 50 MW Hermes 2 reactor at Oak Ridge counting toward that broader commitment; the Nuclear Regulatory Commission approved Kairos Power's construction plans for two Oak Ridge reactors in late 2024 [^1].
