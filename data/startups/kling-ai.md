## About

Kling AI is a Beijing-based generative video model developer being carved out from Chinese short-video platform Kuaishou as an independent entity, with plans for a Hong Kong listing within roughly 12 months [^1][^2][^3]. The company's model produces short films and clips from text and image prompts and is used by filmmakers, advertisers, and creative studios [^2][^3].
