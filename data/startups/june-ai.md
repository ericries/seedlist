## About

June AI is an enterprise AI-deployment platform that helps organizations implement AI agents by scanning existing systems to identify business processes and bottlenecks, then automatically generating implementation roadmaps [^1][^2]. The platform is designed to reduce reliance on forward-deployed engineers by guiding customers through the steps required to deploy AI agents across complex enterprise environments [^1]. June was founded by Efrat Rapoport, Ohad Hen, Barak Goldstein, and Idan Tsitiat — all previously worked on AI initiatives at Salesforce [^2][^3]. The company emerged from stealth on August 3, 2026 [^1][^3].
