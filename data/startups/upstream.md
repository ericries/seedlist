## About

Upstream is a Paris-based startup building an AI-native email inbox designed for collaboration between humans and AI agents [^1]. Co-founded by Louis Lecat (CEO), the company is rebuilding email infrastructure so AI agents can read, write, organize, and act on behalf of users — handling AI-powered prioritization, automated drafting in the user's writing style, meeting scheduling, information retrieval, and team collaboration around email threads [^1].
