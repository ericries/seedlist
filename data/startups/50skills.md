## About

Reykjavík-based 50skills builds an AI-powered operating system for HR workflows, combining a no-code visual workflow builder with custom AI agents and human oversight [^1][^3]. The platform ships two products — Hire (an applicant tracking system) and Journeys (a workflow automation platform) — and reports customers including Icelandair, Blue Lagoon, and Domino's across the Nordics and UK, with more than four million actions completed on the platform in the last year [^1]. Proceeds will be used to expand 50skills' AI HR workflow automation platform [^1][^2].
