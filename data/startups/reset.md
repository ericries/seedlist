## About

Reset is a Menlo Park, California-based provider of an embedded earned wage access (EWA) platform built exclusively for credit unions and community banks [^1][^2]. The platform lets members access their earned income fee-free through their financial institution's own card, while the institution captures deposit growth and interchange revenue rather than ceding those flows to neobanks [^1][^3]. In early 2026, Reset was selected as one of six fintechs nationwide for the Filene Research Institute's FiLab program; it is currently piloting with six credit unions representing 1.2 million members across five states and $17 billion in combined assets [^1][^2].
