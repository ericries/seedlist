## About

Shelfmark is a Pittsburgh-based physical AI startup that builds autonomous visual inspection systems for continuous-flow manufacturing lines — the reels, rolls, and webs used to make products like flooring, paper, and specialty materials [^1][^2][^3]. Industrial cameras paired with vision models detect defects in real time and feed process-improvement signals back to plant operations [^1][^2]. Founded by CEO Pat O'Donnell, the company plans to hire roughly six people over 18 months and expand into European markets while keeping operations centered in Pittsburgh [^2].
