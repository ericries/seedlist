## About

AI Infrastructure Capital AG is a Swiss startup that purchases enterprise NVIDIA GPU servers outright and rents them to enterprises under long-term contracts, siting the hardware in Iceland to combine renewable power with naturally cold outside air for cooling [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in June 2026 by Cédric Waldburger and Roald Parmentier and is based in Pfäffikon, Canton Schwyz [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-30, AI Infrastructure Capital announced a €16 million (~$16M) launch round anchored by Rapperswil-based Valyou-Anlagestiftung (an investment foundation), with Valyou's managing director Yonten Wagma and Rapperswil entrepreneur Fabian Villiger joining the board [^1][^2][^3]. The capital funds wholesale acquisitions of high-density NVIDIA GPU servers and expansion of clean-room server facility allocations [^2][^3].
