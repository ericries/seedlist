## About

Mirendil is a San Francisco-based AI startup founded by former Anthropic researchers Behnam Neyshabur and Harsh Mehta, building self-improving AI systems that automate AI research itself — including experimental design, hyperparameter search, model evaluation, and iterative training [^1][^2]. The 20-person founding team includes researchers and engineers from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and xAI [^1].
