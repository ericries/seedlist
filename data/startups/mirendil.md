## About

Mirendil is a San Francisco-based AI startup founded by former Anthropic researchers Behnam Neyshabur and Harsh Mehta, building self-improving AI systems that automate AI research itself — including experimental design, hyperparameter search, model evaluation, and iterative training [^1][^2]. The 20-person founding team includes researchers and engineers from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and xAI [^1].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-06-24 | Seed | $200M | Andreessen Horowitz, Kleiner Perkins | NVIDIA [^1][^2][^3][^4][^5][^6] |

The seed round valued Mirendil at approximately $1 billion post-money — one of the largest seed rounds in AI history [^1][^2][^4][^5][^6]. Andreessen Horowitz officially announced its lead role on June 24, 2026 [^4].

## What Investors Say

Matt Bornstein and Malika Aubakirova, Andreessen Horowitz: "They are working on one of the biggest possible problems in AI, and they are one of the few teams who have experience and strong priors about how to make the end-to-end system work" [^4].
