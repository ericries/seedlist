## About

Genius AI is a New York-headquartered vertical-SaaS company that provides an AI-driven management platform for in-person service businesses, particularly in beauty, wellness, and health [^1][^2]. The company automates administrative workflows including scheduling, payments, marketing, and client management, centered around the GlossGenius flagship product [^1][^2]. Founded in 2016 by twin sisters Danielle and Leah Cohen-Shohet, the company rebranded from GlossGenius to Genius AI on July 21, 2026, alongside its Series D financing [^1][^2]. Genius AI is approaching $200 million in annual recurring revenue and serves more than 125,000 customers [^1].
