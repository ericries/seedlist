## About

Epitel develops the REMI Remote EEG Monitoring System, a wireless EEG platform that enables extended-duration monitoring of brain activity at home, integrated with AI-driven event detection to flag potential seizures [^1][^2][^3]. The platform is cleared for neurological monitoring in patients as young as one year old, with AI-assisted event detection available for patients aged six and older [^1]. Steve Pacelli serves as President & CEO [^1].
