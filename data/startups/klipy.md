## About

KLIPY is a San Francisco-based provider of an API platform for expressive media — GIFs, memes, stickers, clips, and AI-generated media — that developers can integrate into social and creative products [^1][^2][^3]. The company says its customers include BeReal, Baidu, Canva, Figma, and Microsoft SwiftKey, and its tools also give creators, brands, and rights holders ways to distribute, manage, and control content [^1][^2].

On June 17, 2026, KLIPY announced a $3.8 million round and disclosed that it had joined Google's AI Futures Fund [^1][^2][^3].
