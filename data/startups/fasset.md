## About

Fasset is a Los Angeles-based stablecoin-powered digital banking and investment platform focused on financial inclusion in emerging markets, founded in 2019 [^1][^2]. The company has secured regulatory approvals in the UAE, Indonesia, Malaysia, the EU, Türkiye, and Pakistan and processes more than $32B in annualized transaction volume across 125 countries, serving over 1,000 SMBs [^1][^2]. The $51M Series B announced May 14, 2026 was led by SBI Group with participation from Arz Portföy, Investcorp, and a group of strategic family offices; proceeds will fund expansion across Asia, Africa, and the Americas plus new SME working capital and trade financing products [^1][^2].
