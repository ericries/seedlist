## About

Volta Infra Holdings is an AI cloud infrastructure company founded in 2026 by former Brookfield executives Ricard Boada and Sofia Gumuzio [^1][^3]. Volta positions itself as a "neocloud" provider targeting mid-stage AI startups, treating compute as a new infrastructure asset class [^1][^3]. The company launched with a $10 billion, six-year cloud compute contract with a leading AI developer, to be hosted at Bitdeer's 133-megawatt data center in Norway, and a $5 billion AI infrastructure financing pool structured with Azora [^1][^2][^3].
