## About

Skello is a Paris-based HR SaaS company building AI-powered scheduling, attendance, and labor-compliance tools for frontline teams in hospitality, retail, healthcare, and other service industries. Operating from Paris, Lille, and Barcelona, the company employs more than 400 people, reached profitability in 2025, and exceeded €50 million in annual recurring revenue in 2026. In 2025 Skello launched Skello Assistant, an AI agent that turns operational data into tailored HR insights and automates follow-up actions for frontline managers [^1][^2].
