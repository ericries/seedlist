## About

Goldenrod Therapeutics is a Houston-based biotechnology company developing 11h, a next-generation orally bioavailable, brain-penetrant PDE4 inhibitor with potential as a best-in-class therapy for Friedreich's Ataxia and a foundation for multiple neurodegenerative and neuroinflammatory disorders [^1][^2]. The 11h technology was in-licensed from the University of Nebraska Medical Center (UNMC), where it was developed by Dr. Corey Hopkins [^1][^2]. Proceeds from the seed round will fund manufacturing, formulation optimization, IND-enabling studies, and a Phase I study in Friedreich's Ataxia [^1].
