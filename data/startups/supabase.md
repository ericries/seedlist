## About

Supabase is an open-source Postgres development platform headquartered in San Francisco, founded in 2020 by Paul Copplestone and Ant Wilson [^1]. The company provides a backend-as-a-service offering built on PostgreSQL, with hosted authentication, storage, real-time, edge functions, and vector embeddings. As of June 2026, Supabase reports databases doubling year-over-year and Claude Code as its largest contributor since the start of the year, with AI agents now deploying the majority of databases on the platform [^1] [^2].

Alongside the Series F announcement, Supabase released a preview of Multigres, a horizontal scaling tool designed to enable Supabase-built applications to scale "up to the size of OpenAI or even larger" [^1].
