## About

Polar is an AI-first web browser designed for knowledge workers, enabling workflow automation, prompt saving, and agent-based task assignment for professionals in sales, recruitment, marketing, research, and business operations [^1]. The company was founded by CEO Kevin Jiang, who previously worked at Perplexity on Comet, and operates on a freemium model with paid plans starting at $20/month [^1]. On 2026-07-29, Polar announced a $5.7 million seed round led by Madrona with participation from Thomas Dohmke (ex-GitHub CEO), Phoebe Gates (Phia founder), Erik Bernhardsson (Modal CEO), Benjamin Spector (Flapping Airplanes co-founder), Rob Wachen (Etched co-founder), and Robert Yang (Fundamental Research Labs co-founder/CEO) [^1].
