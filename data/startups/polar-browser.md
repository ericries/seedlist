## About

Polar is an AI-first web browser designed for knowledge workers, enabling workflow automation, prompt saving, and agent-based task assignment for professionals in sales, recruitment, marketing, research, and business operations [^1]. The company was founded in 2026 by CEO Kevin Jiang, Vishaal Ram, and Howard Zhong; Jiang previously worked at Perplexity on Comet, and the team has backgrounds from MIT, Perplexity, and leading quantitative funds [^1][^2][^3]. Polar operates on a freemium model with paid plans starting at $20/month [^1]. On 2026-07-29, Polar announced a $5.7 million seed round led by Madrona [^1][^2][^3]. Per TechCrunch, individual angels in the round include Thomas Dohmke (ex-GitHub CEO), Phoebe Gates (Phia founder), Erik Bernhardsson (Modal CEO), Benjamin Spector (Flapping Airplanes co-founder), Rob Wachen (Etched co-founder), and Robert Yang (Fundamental Research Labs co-founder/CEO) [^1]. The company's press release names only Thomas Dohmke and Erik Bernhardsson explicitly [^3].
