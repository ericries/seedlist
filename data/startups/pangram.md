## About

Pangram is a New York-based AI detection startup building software to identify AI-generated text and images across the open web and enterprise workflows [^1]. The company was founded by Stanford AI and machine learning graduates Max Spero and Bradley Emi and launched publicly about two years before the 2026 round [^1]. On 2026-07-29, Pangram announced a $9 million funding round led by Menlo Ventures with participation from Haystack, ScOp Venture Capital, Script Capital, and Cadenza [^1][^2][^3]. The company did not publicly designate the 2026 round as a Series A [^2]. Alongside the round, the company introduced Pangram 4 (its next-generation text detection model) and Pangram Image (an AI image detection model in research preview) [^1][^2]. The 2026 round follows a $3.98 million seed (including a $2.7 million extension announced 2025-06-24) led by ScOp with participation from Script Capital, Cadenza, and prior pre-seed lead Haystack [^4][^5].
