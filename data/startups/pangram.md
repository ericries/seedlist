## About

Pangram is a New York-based AI detection startup building software to identify AI-generated text and images across the open web and enterprise workflows [^1]. The company was founded by Stanford AI and machine learning graduates Max Spero and Bradley Emi and launched publicly about two years before the 2026 round [^1]. On 2026-07-29, Pangram announced a $9 million round led by Menlo Ventures with participation from Haystack, ScOp, Script Capital, and Cadenza [^1]. Alongside the round, the company introduced Pangram 4 (its next-generation text detection model) and Pangram Image (an AI image detection model in research preview) [^1].
