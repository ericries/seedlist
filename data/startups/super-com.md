## About

Super.com is a Toronto-based consumer savings app that bundles hotel and prescription discounts, cashback, cash advances and credit-building tools into a $15/month "Super+" membership positioned as a savings super app for lower-income Americans [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2016 by Hussein Fazal and Henry Shi and became profitable in 2025 while surpassing $200M in net revenue and growing more than 50% year-over-year [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-07 the company announced a $65M Series D financing led by TPG at a $1.2B post-money valuation, bringing total capital raised to more than $150M since founding [^1][^2][^3]. Super+ membership approached one million members at the time of the round, and cumulative customer savings had passed $1B since the app launched [^1][^2].
