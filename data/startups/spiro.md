## About

Spiro is an African electric-vehicle platform operating the continent's largest battery-swapping network for electric two-wheelers [^1][^2]. The company is headquartered in Dubai and grew out of the Equitane investment platform under chairman Gagan Gupta [^2]. By June 2026, Spiro had more than 100,000 electric motorcycles on the road, over 2,500 battery-swap stations, and had completed more than 30 million battery swaps across seven African markets: Kenya, Rwanda, Uganda, Togo, Benin, Nigeria, and Cameroon [^1][^2][^3].

The June 2026 financing closed an equity round that ultimately totaled $270 million, with Chinese growth investor NewTrails Capital providing an additional $55 million on top of prior commitments from Impact Fund Denmark, Equitane, and Afreximbank's Fund for Export Development in Africa (FEDA) [^1][^2][^3].
