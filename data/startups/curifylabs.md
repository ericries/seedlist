## About

CurifyLabs is a Helsinki-based healthtech company automating the preparation of personalized medications for hospital and compounding pharmacies. Its Compounding System Solution (CSS) combines proprietary software, GMP-manufactured excipient bases, and 3D printing hardware — most recently the PharmaPrinter Aurum — to produce patient-specific doses on demand. The company reports that pharmacies in 21 U.S. states and across Europe use CurifyLabs technology to compound and dispense thousands of doses daily. CurifyLabs' platform is ISO 13485 certified and designed in compliance with FDA 503A/503B non-sterile compounding standards [^1][^2].
