## About

Smash Foods is a better-for-you food company producing low-sugar chia-fortified fruit spreads and Jammy Protein Bites — nut butter snacks filled with the company's signature fruit spreads [^1][^2]. Co-founded by Anna Peck and Steven Ford, the company has expanded to more than 10,000 retail locations nationwide, tripled its business over the past year, and reached profitability [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-23, Smash Foods announced an $18 million growth investment led by L Catterton, with participation from The Family Fund and Eclair Partners (also reported as Eclair Ventures) [^1][^2][^3]. The capital will be used to scale manufacturing for the Jammy Protein Bites line, develop additional products, and further establish the brand in American grocery [^1][^2].
