## About

Probook is a New York City-based developer of an AI-powered operating system for home service businesses, built around a centralized dispatch engine [^1][^2]. The platform consolidates customer intake, automated data cleaning, SMS messaging, and outbound scheduling into a single context layer so dispatchers can route the right technician to the right job at the right time [^2][^3]. Founders George Eliadis (CEO), Ben Cervantez, and Lewis Zhang (CTO) launched the company drawing on Eliadis's background as a tradesman [^1][^3]. Early customers include Summers Plumbing, Heating & Cooling — which booked 2,542 jobs in its first month on Probook with zero human intervention across 14 locations and 260 technicians — and Del-Air, which reported dispatcher productivity doubling from 10 to 22 technicians per dispatcher [^2][^3].
