## About

Ethereal Machines is a Bengaluru-based deeptech precision manufacturing startup that designs, builds, and operates proprietary multi-axis CNC machines combined with AI-powered factory software [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2014 by Kaushik Mudda (CEO) and Navin Jain, the company serves global clients in aerospace, defense, healthcare, semiconductors, and consumer electronics through a Machining-as-a-Service (MaaS) model from its smart factory in Peenya, Bengaluru [^1][^2][^3]. On June 11, 2026, Ethereal Machines announced a $28.5M Series B led by Avataar Ventures (₹199.55 crore / ~$21.5M), with participation from Peak XV Partners and Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan, among others [^1][^2][^3]. The company plans to use the capital to build a 300,000 sq ft mega factory near Bengaluru, develop India's first homegrown CNC controller, expand its AI-powered factory software platform Vesper, and build out teams in the US and Europe [^1][^2][^3].
