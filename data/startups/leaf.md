## About

Leaf is a San Francisco-based data infrastructure company that helps food and agriculture companies connect and analyze their farm data, including machinery output, soil samples, weather, imagery, and farm management data [^1][^2]. Founded in 2021, the company processes data from over 20% of the global acres annually and counts Bayer, BASF, Growmark, and Syngenta among its customers [^1][^2]. CEO G. Bailey Stockdale describes the wedge as: "Farm data is really valuable, but it's also nearly impossible to work with" given the hundreds of proprietary file formats produced by agricultural hardware [^2].

Leaf recently rebuilt its platform using a data lake model to enable faster queries and AI-friendly analysis [^2].
