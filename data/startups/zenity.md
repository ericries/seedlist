## About

Zenity is a Tel Aviv-based cybersecurity company that provides security and governance for AI agents deployed in enterprise environments [^1][^2]. The platform monitors AI agents in real time, detecting deviations from their intended purpose and blocking or altering actions that pose risks to the organization, its clients, or its data [^2]. Zenity was founded in 2021 by Ben Kliger (CEO) and Michael Bargury (CTO), both veterans of Israel's Unit 8200 [^3]. The company reports 230 employees and targets regulated sectors including financial services, telecommunications, healthcare, pharmaceuticals, and energy [^2].

Zenity plans to use the proceeds to accelerate product development, expand globally, and address growing enterprise demand for security purpose-built for autonomous AI agents [^1][^3].
