## About

Dream is an Israeli cybersecurity and sovereign AI company that builds AI and cyber defense platforms for governments and critical infrastructure operators [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by Shalev Hulio (former NSO Group CEO), Sebastian Kurz (former Chancellor of Austria), and Gil Dolev [^1][^2]. Dream is headquartered in Tel Aviv with additional offices in Abu Dhabi and Vienna [^1].

The company began commercial operations in late 2024 and has secured nearly $300 million in total contract value since [^1]. Its platforms enable governments to operate advanced AI systems "under their own control" for national-scale cyber defense against state-level threats [^1][^3].
