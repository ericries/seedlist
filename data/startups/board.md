## About

Board is a New York-based connected-screen gaming startup founded in 2023 by Brynn Putnam (founder of Mirror, sold to Lululemon for $500M); the flagship product is a 24-inch wood-finished touchscreen that uses proprietary technology to recognize physical game pieces, combining the tactile feel of board games with the interactivity of video games [^1][^2][^3]. Board devices are deployed in tens of thousands of homes, schools, hospitals, and restaurants across all 50 states, with 85% of customers averaging 30+ play sessions per month [^1][^2]. The $20M Series A announced June 2, 2026 was led by Union Square Ventures — representing the first investment from General Partner Michael Mignano since he joined USV (Mignano joining Board's board) — with angel participation from Biz Stone, Tim Ferriss, and Scott Belsky [^1][^2][^3].
