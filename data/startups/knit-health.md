## About

Knit Health is a UC Berkeley spinout founded in 2025 that develops Large Clinical Behavior Models (LCBM)—AI systems trained on real clinical decision patterns from electronic medical record (EMR) data spanning over 130M patients across 30 US health systems [^1][^2]. The platform uses behavioral cloning and causal inference to automate care routing, triage, and discharge predictions, addressing limitations of healthcare LLMs trained primarily on published literature [^1][^2]. The $11.6M Seed round announced May 12, 2026 was co-led by Uncork Capital and Frist Cressey Ventures, with the pre-seed previously led by Moxxie Ventures (Coalition Operators participated) [^1][^2].
