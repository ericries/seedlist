## About

Zero is a Helsinki-based AI-powered CRM and revenue platform that consolidates prospecting, deal management, customer growth, and revenue operations into a single system, aiming to replace legacy CRM stacks for scaleups [^1][^2]. The product spans four modules — Prospect (buying-signal lead identification), Close (pipeline management), Grow (customer retention and expansion), and Scale (AI agents for GTM automation) [^2]. On 2026-09-15, Zero announced a €8.93M ($10.3M) Seed round led by Primary Venture Partners with participation from Inception Fund, Defiant, Greens, and the founders of Lovable, Supercell, Langdock, and Silo AI [^1]. This follows a €2.34M pre-seed round in December 2024 led by 20VC, with backing from PostHog co-founder James Hawkins and operator funds tied to executives at Spotify, Wolt, Shopify, Dropbox, Meta, and OpenAI [^1]. Customers listed on the company site include Watchdog, Emfas, and Sumary; the platform integrates with HubSpot [^2].
