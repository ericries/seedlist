## About

Inocras is a San Diego-based bioinformatics firm specializing in whole-genome sequencing (WGS) and automated analytics for precision oncology [^1][^2][^3]. Its CLIA/CAP-certified platforms include CancerVision (tumor profiling) and MRDVision (molecular residual disease detection), already deployed across 100+ cancer institutions in Asia [^1][^2]. The company is expanding U.S. commercial operations following the August 2026 financing [^1][^2][^3].

The oversubscribed round brings Inocras's cumulative funding to approximately $100M and coincides with a joint research agreement with AimedBio to integrate WGS into AimedBio's antibody-drug conjugate clinical programs [^1][^2].
