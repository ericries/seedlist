## About

Unastella is a Seoul-based rocket startup developing small launch vehicles and engines using kerosene and liquid oxygen propulsion [^1]. The company successfully launched its UNA EXPRESS-I rocket from South Korean soil in May 2025, becoming one of a small group of private companies to launch a rocket domestically in Korea [^1]. The 22-person team handles design, manufacturing, ground operations, and flight data in-house, with plans for small satellite launch services and crewed suborbital spaceflight [^1].
