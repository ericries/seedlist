## About

Sofab Inks is a Louisville, Kentucky-based specialty materials company developing electron transport layer (ETL) materials for perovskite solar cells [^1][^2][^3]. The company was spun out of the University of Louisville's Conn Center for Renewable Energy Research with support from the U.S. Department of Energy [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-21, Sofab Inks announced a $6 million seed round led by Cloudberry Ventures [^1][^2][^3]. The company claims commercial engagement or late-stage discussions with approximately 90% of the perovskite industry, and the round arrives alongside a manufacturing reliability breakthrough for perovskite solar at scale [^1][^3]. The funds will be used to expand the engineering team and transition into high-velocity production of the company's perovskite ETL materials [^1][^2].
