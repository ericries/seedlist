## About

Muybridge is an Oslo, Norway-based software-defined imaging platform that replaces traditional broadcast camera infrastructure with arrays of 4K sensors and GPU-powered systems — creating virtual camera perspectives digitally to enable broadcasters to capture and generate new viewing angles in real time [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2019 by Håkon Espeland and Anders Tomren [^1][^3]. The $16M oversubscribed Series A announced May 21, 2026 was co-led by Investinor, Fairpoint, Idekapital, and RunwayFBU, with participation from Nordic technology founders and operators; capital will fund international expansion across Europe and the US [^1][^2][^3].
