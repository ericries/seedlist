## About

Transfyr is a Cambridge, Massachusetts–based physical-AI platform for scientific laboratories, using integrated sensors and multimodal AI models to build an "observability layer" for physical laboratory science — capturing what scientists actually do at the lab bench and translating hands-on experimental activity into machine-readable data suitable for closed-loop, AI-driven automation [^1][^2][^3][^4]. The company was co-founded by Anna Marie Wagner (previously Head of AI and Corporate Development at Ginkgo Bioworks) as CEO and Dr. Renee Wegrzyn (founding Director of ARPA-H) as CIO, and launched publicly with a $25M seed round on August 26, 2026 [^1][^2][^3][^4].

## What Founders Say

Co-founder and CEO Anna Marie Wagner on the gap Transfyr is addressing: "Science is missing a critical layer of infrastructure that's necessary for efficient reproducibility, translation, scaling, and automation." [^2]

Co-founder Dr. Renee Wegrzyn on the translation bottleneck in science: "The real bottleneck to revolutionary science isn't a lack of big ideas, it's the massive friction of translating those ideas into reliable, scalable reality." [^2]
