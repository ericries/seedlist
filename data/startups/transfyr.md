## About

Transfyr is a physical-AI platform for scientific laboratories, using multimodal sensors and AI models to capture what scientists actually do at the lab bench and translate hands-on experimental activity into machine-readable data suitable for closed-loop, AI-driven automation [^1][^2][^3]. The company was co-founded by Anna Marie Wagner (previously Head of AI and Corporate Development at Ginkgo Bioworks) and Dr. Renee Wegrzyn (founding Director of ARPA-H), and launched publicly with a $25M seed round on August 26, 2026 [^1][^2][^3].
