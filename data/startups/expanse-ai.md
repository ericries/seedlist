## About

Expanse is a U.S. and U.K.-based AI infrastructure company that predicts compute requirements for AI workloads before execution, helping organizations reduce over-allocation, prevent failed jobs, and optimize GPU infrastructure utilization [^1]. The platform analyzes workloads directly within customer environments while maintaining data sovereignty [^1]. On 2026-09-16, the company announced a €4.5M ($5.3M) seed round led by Crane Venture Partners with participation from PXN Ventures, AIP Seed, and angel investors including former DeepMind researchers [^1][^2].
