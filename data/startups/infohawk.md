## About

InfoHawk is an Austin-based AI platform built to detect, analyze, and neutralize online deception at internet scale [^1]. The company focuses on detecting AI-driven misinformation and scams, and was founded by Rob Leathern (former Meta ads integrity executive), Ben Poiesz, and Jamie McCrindle [^1][^2].
