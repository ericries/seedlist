## About

Easy Aerial is a New York-based manufacturer of tethered and free-flight aerial intelligence platforms for defense, security, and critical infrastructure applications [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2015 by Ido Gur and Ivan Stamatovski, and its systems — including military-grade tethered UAS and drone-in-a-box solutions — are used by the U.S. Air Force and other defense customers; the company is ISO 9001 / AS9100 certified, NDAA compliant, and listed on the DIU's Blue Clear List [^1][^2]. On August 31, 2026, Easy Aerial announced the completion of a $20M Series B round led by Insight Partners, with participation from Entrée Capital and private U.S. investors [^3][^4][^5][^6]. The company said the capital will fund global expansion, increased production capacity, international defense programs, and continued investment in autonomous aerial technologies, and follows recent contract wins across U.S. and international markets [^4][^6].
