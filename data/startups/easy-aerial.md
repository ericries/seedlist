## About

Easy Aerial is a New York-based manufacturer of tethered and free-flight aerial intelligence platforms for defense, security, and critical infrastructure applications [^1]. The company was founded in 2015 by Ido Gur and Ivan Stamatovski, and its systems — including military-grade tethered UAS and drone-in-a-box solutions — are used by the U.S. Air Force and other defense customers; the company is ISO 9001 / AS9100 certified, NDAA compliant, and listed on the DIU's Blue Clear List [^1][^2]. On September 1, 2026, Easy Aerial announced a $20M Series B round led by Insight Partners, with participation from Entree Capital and private U.S. investors [^1].
