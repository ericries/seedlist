## About

Geni is a screen-free toy company that makes AI-powered audio storytelling devices for children [^1]. The platform combines large language models and physical play, letting children choose physical tiles representing characters, objects, or emotions, which Geni transforms into short screen-free audio adventures [^2]. The company was co-founded by Kevin Tang and Marcelo Coelho in 2025 [^1][^2]. Coelho is a researcher at the MIT Media Lab, and Geni was developed through the MIT designX venture program [^3].
