## About

Eliyan is a Santa Clara, California-based semiconductor infrastructure startup developing die-to-die, chip-to-chip, and rack-to-rack chiplet interconnect technologies for next-generation AI, cloud, and high-performance computing systems [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2021 by Ramin Farjadrad (CEO, former Marvell Semiconductor CTO and VP of networking), Patrick Soheili, and Syrus Ziai [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-29, Eliyan announced an oversubscribed $145 million Series C at a $1 billion valuation, achieving unicorn status [^1][^2][^3]. The round was led by Seligman Ventures, with new strategic investors Cisco Investments and Lumentum joining alongside existing investors [^1][^2]. Umesh Padval, Managing Partner at Seligman Ventures, joined Eliyan's Board of Directors as part of the round [^1]. The company expects to begin initial chiplet shipments in 2026 and forecasts hundreds of millions of dollars in sales by the end of 2027 [^1].
