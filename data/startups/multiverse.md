## About

Multiverse is a London-based EdTech company founded in 2016 by Euan Blair and Sophie Adelman. The company started as a digital apprenticeships provider and has grown into an enterprise upskilling platform focused on AI and tech adoption, working with more than 1,500 organisations globally to close skills gaps in data, AI and technology [^1][^2]. In January 2026, Multiverse acquired Berlin-based data and AI training company StackFuel to accelerate European expansion [^1][^3].
