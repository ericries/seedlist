## About

4baseCare is a Bengaluru-based precision oncology startup using genomics, bioinformatics, and AI to deliver personalised cancer care [^1][^2]. Its flagship platform, OncoTwin, helps oncologists derive treatment insights from clinico-genomic and real-world patient data [^1][^2]. The company operates a network of hospital-linked genomics laboratories and conducts roughly 1,500 genomic tests per month, with operations spanning India, the Middle East, Southeast Asia, Latin America, and Central Asia [^2]. 4baseCare was co-founded by Hitesh Goswami and Kshitij Rishi [^2].
