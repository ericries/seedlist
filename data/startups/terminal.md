## About

Terminal provides a unified API that normalizes telematics data from more than 325 providers into a single, consistent format for insurance companies, fleet operators, logistics firms, and financial services companies [^1][^2]. Founded in 2023 by CEO Raghav Midha and CTO Connor Giles, the platform handles data storage, security, compliance, and standardization for commercial-vehicle data including location, speed, fuel consumption, and maintenance [^1][^2]. The company is based in Toronto [^1].
