## About

PhysicsX is a UK-based AI-for-engineering company developing an AI-native engineering platform that uses physics AI models to predict physical behavior in seconds rather than hours or days [^1]. The platform enables engineering teams to evaluate orders of magnitude more design variants and carry physics insight across the full product lifecycle, serving industries including aerospace, defense, automotive, semiconductors, energy, and materials [^1][^2]. PhysicsX is headquartered in London with offices in New York, the Bay Area, and Singapore, and has grown its team to more than 300 people, doubling in the last 12 months [^1][^3]. The company was co-founded by Jacomo Corbo (CEO) and Robin Tuluie (Chairman) [^1].
