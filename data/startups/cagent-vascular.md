## About

Cagent Vascular is a Pennsylvania-based medical device company developing the Serranator Serration Remodeling Therapy (SRT) platform and the Serranator SONIC Intravascular Lithotripsy (IVL) System, which transmits ultrasonic acoustic waves through a balloon and its serrated metal elements directly into calcified plaque to improve vessel compliance at low inflation pressures [^1][^2]. On May 19, 2026, the company announced the closing of an oversubscribed $41M Series D financing and reported the first human use of the Serranator SONIC IVL System, performed in Uzbekistan as part of the REMODEL I prospective study [^1][^2]. Proceeds will support completion of the pivotal REMODEL II trial and commercial activities surrounding SONIC IVL [^1][^2].
