## About

Pit is a Stockholm-based AI-native software company that builds production-grade custom software for enterprise operations, positioning itself as an "AI product team as a service" [^1][^2]. The company exited stealth on May 7, 2026 with a €13.6 million ($16 million) launch round led by Andreessen Horowitz [^1][^2]. Pit's founding team came out of Voi, Klarna, and iZettle, where they built AI-powered systems to replace manual operational workflows at scale [^1]. CEO and co-founder Adam Jafer was previously a co-founder of Swedish e-scooter company Voi [^1]. Early customers include Voi, Tre, Stena Recycling, and Kry, spanning logistics, telecom, e-commerce, and healthcare [^1].
