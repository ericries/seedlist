## About

Didit is an AI-native infrastructure provider for identity verification and fraud prevention, building programmable identity infrastructure that lets developers verify people, businesses, and digital actions at global scale [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by twin brothers and AI engineers Alberto and Alejandro Rosas [^1][^2].

Didit reports growing more than 30% month over month and now serves more than 2,000 companies across every region in the world [^2]. The company joined Y Combinator's Winter 2026 batch [^3].
