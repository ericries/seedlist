## About

Turnout is a San Diego-based AI-powered consumer advocacy company that helps Americans navigate government bureaucracy — starting with tax debt relief and Social Security Disability claims [^1][^2]. The company pairs expert human advocates with "Jake," its suite of AI agents that automate the manual, operational, and administrative work of dealing with government agencies (waiting on the phone, searching for documents, filling out forms, tracking deadlines) [^1][^2]. Founded in 2024 by CEO Itai Hirsch, Turnout previously raised a $21M Seed in September 2025 co-led by Shine Capital and LGVP [^3]. On June 11, 2026, the company announced a $35M Series A led by HighPost Capital at a $400M valuation, with the round expanding its AI agent platform and scaling its human advocate workforce nationwide [^1][^2].
