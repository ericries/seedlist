## About

Genspark is an agentic AI workplace platform developed by MainFunc, Inc., a startup headquartered in Palo Alto, California with an office in Singapore [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by former Baidu executives Eric Jing and Kay Zhu, alongside Wen Sang (Co-Founder & COO, PhD from MIT, previously founded YC-backed Smarking) and Jiakai Justin Liu [^2][^3]. Genspark first launched publicly in June 2024 as an AI-powered search engine generating "Sparkpages," then pivoted in April 2025 to release the Genspark Super Agent, an autonomous general-purpose agent that coordinates multiple LLMs and tools to complete multi-step tasks [^2].
