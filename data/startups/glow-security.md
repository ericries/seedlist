## About

Glow is an AI-native endpoint security platform headquartered in Palo Alto, California, that emerged from stealth on July 22, 2026 [^1][^2]. The company builds AI-agent-driven software that helps enterprises monitor and control the applications, AI agents, and developer tools running on employee devices, deploying specialized AI agents that continuously map environments and analyze risk in real time [^1][^2]. Founded in 2025, Glow is led by CEO Roi Tiger, a former VP of Engineering at Meta, alongside co-founders Omer Singer (formerly head of cybersecurity strategy at Snowflake), Ophir Arie (formerly VP of R&D at Claroty), and Arnon Joseph [^1][^2]. The company has nearly 100 employees, with roughly 70% based in Israel and the remainder in the United States, and already serves paying customers in healthcare, retail, and financial services [^2].
