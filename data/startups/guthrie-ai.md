## About

Guthrie AI is a Philadelphia-based construction technology company that recruits, trains, and places Virtual Bid Assistants (VBAs) for commercial glazing contractors [^1][^2]. The platform automates bid management tasks including file organization, scope identification, quote preparation, and vendor routing, and integrates with estimator tools such as Bluebeam, Outlook, and Excel [^1][^2]. Guthrie AI was founded by Ted Baumgardner (Founder and President) and Tuneer De (Co-Founder and Head of Product) [^1][^2]. According to the company, the platform cuts bid turnaround times from weeks to days, letting glazing contractors bid on up to 70% more work without adding internal headcount [^1].
