## About

Healia is a Columbus, Ohio-based healthcare benefits platform that helps employers reduce healthcare costs for dual-income families [^1][^2]. The company creates health reimbursement arrangements (HRAs) that allow employers to cover employee healthcare expenses and premiums when workers enroll in a spouse's plan, offering an alternative to duplicative traditional employer coverage [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Healia announced $18 million in total funding, including a $14 million Series A round led by 111° West Capital with participation from Y Combinator, First Round Capital, Pioneer Fund, GoAhead Ventures, and North Coast Ventures [^1][^2][^3][^4]. The company was founded by CEO Priyang Shah, a Y Combinator W24 alumnus who previously helped scale Root Insurance from 15 employees to a $6.5B IPO [^4][^5]. The company was founded in 2022 [^5].
