## About

EQON is a Norwegian climate technology company based in Stavanger that develops a patented dimming solution and cloud-based software layer for industrial heating cables [^1][^2]. EQON replaces traditional on/off heating cable management with intelligent, real-time power regulation, delivering the precise amount of heat required at any moment and cutting energy consumption and CO₂ emissions by up to 80 percent [^1][^2][^3]. Applications include process heating for food and chemical production, frost protection on ships, rigs and offshore assets, railway points heating, and other resistive heating loads [^1][^2]. In a pilot in Olavskleivå for the municipality of Stavanger, the company reported an 84% annual electricity reduction [^1][^3].
