## About

Trooly is an AI-native user research platform that runs 45-minute, in-depth voice interviews with consumers across global markets [^1]. The system pairs a multimodal voice agent with an intelligent follow-up engine to conduct real-time interviews in multiple languages, aiming to deliver research insights faster and at lower cost than manual methods [^2][^3]. The company launched its public beta in North America alongside its seed funding announcement in July 2026 [^1].
