## About

Thalia Therapeutics plc is a London-based clinical-stage biotechnology company developing RNA-based therapeutics and delivery technologies in oncology and cardiovascular disease [^1][^2]. On 24 June 2026, the company announced the acquisition of Sanmirna Therapeutics, Inc. — a US corporation holding an exclusive licence to miRisten, an anti-microRNA-126 therapy for Acute Myeloid Leukaemia — for initial consideration of £3.675 million with deferred milestone payments of up to £13 million [^1][^2]. The acquisition transitions Thalia into a clinical-stage RNA therapeutics business [^3].
