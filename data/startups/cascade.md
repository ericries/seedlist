## About

Cascade is an AI-powered predictive platform for architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) firms that helps them identify and win projects earlier in the bidding process [^1]. The company was co-founded in 2026 by Hannia Zia (CEO) and Joana Ferreira (CTO), both former operators at Google and Amazon [^2][^3]. Cascade operates out of New York City and London [^3].

The platform analyzes diverse data sources — including bond filings, permits, and capital plans — to surface upcoming construction opportunities across U.S. state agencies, local districts, private contracts, and federal agencies, and uses machine learning to predict which developers are most likely to win specific bids [^1][^2]. The company reports that customers have identified over $10 billion in project opportunities through the platform [^2].
