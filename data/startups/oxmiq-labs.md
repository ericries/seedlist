## About

OXMIQ Labs is a unified GPU and AI architecture company founded by Raja Koduri, the former Chief Architect and Executive Vice President of Intel's architecture, graphics and software division and a longtime GPU architect at AMD [^1][^2][^3]. OXMIQ develops OxCore, a licensable GPU intellectual-property architecture designed to let semiconductor companies and AI system builders create custom AI silicon without running a full chip program from scratch [^1][^2][^4]. Alongside the Series A, Jim Keller, CEO of Tenstorrent and a widely recognized chip architect, joined OXMIQ's board of directors; retired Intel Fellow Dr. Valluri Rao joined as an advisor [^1][^4].
