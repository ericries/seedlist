## About

Taktile is a New York-headquartered provider of an "Agentic Decision Platform" that enables financial institutions to combine AI agents, rules, contextual data, and human oversight to automate and optimize high-stakes decisions such as customer approvals, claims reimbursement, fraud prevention, and business loan underwriting [^1][^2]. The company reports outcomes including 95% automation in B2B underwriting and 75% fewer anti-money laundering false positives among customers [^1]. Taktile was co-founded by CEO Maik Taro Wehmeyer [^1][^2].
