## About

Rosella is a technology-enabled commercial insurance brokerage for U.S. small and mid-sized businesses, combining human brokers with software to automate risk analysis, policy placement, and servicing [^1] [^3]. The Sydney-headquartered company is a member of Peak XV's Surge 12 cohort, announced September 28, 2026 [^1] [^2] [^3].
