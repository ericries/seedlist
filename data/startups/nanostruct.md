## About

NanoStruct is a Würzburg, Germany-based deep tech startup founded in 2021 that develops nanostructured sensor chips for the rapid identification of dangerous pathogens in food [^1][^2]. The platform combines optical measurement technology, nanotechnology, biotechnology, and machine learning to reduce detection time for pathogens such as Listeria and Salmonella from 2–3 days to just a few hours [^1][^2]. The €2.6M Seed round announced May 11, 2026 was led by High-Tech Gründerfonds (HTGF) with participation from Bayern Kapital and AUXXO Female Catalyst Fund; proceeds will validate the system through pilot projects in the food analysis market and build a structured sales organization [^1][^2].
