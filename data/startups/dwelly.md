## About

Dwelly is a London-based AI-native lettings and property management platform that acquires independent UK letting agencies and integrates them into its operating system, using AI to automate lettings, property management, rent collection, compliance, and maintenance [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2024 by Ilia Drozdov (CEO), Dan Lifshits, and Dmitry Khanukov, the company has completed six acquisitions in 2026 and describes itself as "AI-first by default" [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Dwelly announced a $170 million Series B (£128m) consisting of $95 million (£71.5m) in equity and a $75 million (£56.4m) debt facility, bringing total funding to over $260 million within roughly a year [^1][^2][^3]. The equity round was led by EQT Growth with participation from General Catalyst, s16vc, Begin Capital, and DVC; debt was provided by Trinity Capital [^1][^2][^3]. Additional angel investors include the CEOs of Legora, Synthesia, and ElevenLabs, and Philipp Freise, partner and co-head of European private equity at KKR [^1][^2].
