## About

CORE Biomedicine is a biotechnology company pioneering lineage-based precision oncology therapeutics with operations in Boston, Tokyo, and Suzhou [^1][^2]. The company's lineage-based approach is designed to identify and target core oncology drivers with precision while expanding the potential patient populations that may benefit from these novel therapies [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, CORE Biomedicine announced a $21 million Series A co-led by UTokyo Innovation Platform and Elikon Venture, with participation from InnoPinnacle Fund, Mitsubishi UFJ Capital, Suzhou Capital Group, CD Capital, YuanBio Venture Capital, Vision Incubate, and Root Venture Partners [^1][^2]. Alongside the financing, the company's wholly owned subsidiary CORE Biomedicine Japan received a grant through the Strengthening Program for Pharmaceutical Startup Ecosystem run by the Japan Agency for Medical Research and Development (AMED) [^1][^2].
