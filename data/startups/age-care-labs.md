## About

Age Care Labs is a Gurugram-based eldercare company operating two brands — Emoha, an at-home and app-based platform providing geriatric care, emergency assistance, and health monitoring, and Epoch Elder Care, which runs assisted living and dementia care homes [^1][^2]. Co-founded in 2019 by Saumyajit Roy (Co-Founder & CEO) and Rohit Bhayana (Managing Partner at Lumis Partners), the two brands serve more than 60,000 seniors across 120 cities in India [^1][^2]. The Series B1 tranche of ₹85 Cr is the first tranche of a larger Series B round of Rs 250 crore ($30 million), expected to be launched in the first quarter of 2027 [^1][^2]. As part of the announcement, Age Care Labs also entered into a joint venture with Shrem Group called Shremoha, a premium senior independent living platform combining Shrem's real estate and hospitality expertise with Emoha's geriatric care experience [^2].
