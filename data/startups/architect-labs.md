## About

Architect Labs is a Palo Alto-based AI startup building end-to-end systems for automated custom chip design and verification [^1][^2][^3]. The company emerged from stealth in June 2026 and positions its platform as a way to convert workload requirements into production-ready silicon without requiring large in-house chip-design teams — aiming to democratize access to custom semiconductor development [^1][^2]. It was co-founded by Ebrahim Hussain and Aaditya Subedi [^1][^2].
