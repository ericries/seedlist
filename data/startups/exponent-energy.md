## About

Exponent Energy is a Bengaluru-based electric vehicle rapid-charging startup founded in 2020 by former Ather Energy executives Arun Vinayak and Sanjay Byalal Jagannath [^1][^2]. The company develops a full-stack platform combining proprietary battery packs (e^packs), charging pumps (e^pumps), and connectors (e^plugs) that together allow commercial EVs to fully charge in roughly 15 minutes using standard lithium-ion cells [^1][^2].

On June 10, 2026, Exponent Energy announced ₹200 crore (approximately $21.1 million) in a Series B2 round co-led by 360 ONE Asset and TDK Ventures, with first-time participation from Hitachi Ventures and follow-on from existing investors Eight Roads Ventures, Lightspeed, 3one4 Capital, and AdvantEdge VC; YourNest contributed $4 million through its Continuum Fund [^1][^2]. The round brings total funding raised to approximately $66 million [^1]. Capital will fund R&D, expansion into additional commercial vehicle segments, and geographic expansion into new countries [^1][^2]. The company recently launched an EV financing subsidiary, Exponent One, which raised $2 million separately [^1].
