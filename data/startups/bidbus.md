## About

Bidbus is an Irvine, California-based online automotive marketplace that lets consumers list their vehicles for competitive bidding from licensed dealerships rather than negotiating a single trade-in or instant-buy offer [^1][^2]. Sellers complete a guided virtual vehicle inspection, set an expected price, and receive live bids from qualified dealer buyers [^1][^2]. Since launch the company has served more than 10,000 vehicle sellers and reached approximately $300M in annualized gross merchandise value [^1]. Bidbus announced a $15M Series A on 2026-07-31 led by Ibex Investors [^1][^2].
