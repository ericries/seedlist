## About

Vori is a San Francisco-based company building what its founders describe as "the self-driving operating system for supermarkets" [^1]. The platform manages a store's entire operations end-to-end, including checkout and payments, shelf-level inventory tracking, wholesaler ordering, pricing, and promotions [^1][^2]. Since launching in January 2024, Vori says it has processed more than $500M in payments across 55 cities and served more than 1M consumers [^1][^2].

Vori was founded in 2019 by Brandon Hill, Tre Kirkman, and Robert Pinkerton — all Stanford graduates [^3]. CEO Brandon Hill is a third-generation grocer; his grandparents were in the industry, his parents met in a supermarket, and his mother works at Vori today [^1].
