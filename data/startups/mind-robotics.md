## About

Mind Robotics is a Rivian-spinoff industrial robotics company developing robots with "human-like skills" intended to automate factory operations and broader industrial work [^1]. The company announced a $400M round on May 13, 2026 led by Kleiner Perkins, with participation from Volkswagen Group's venture arm and Salesforce's venture arm; the new financing follows a $500M Series A in March 2026 (and $115M from Eclipse in 2025), bringing total funding above $1B at a valuation of more than $3B [^1]. Rivian CEO RJ Scaringe, who serves as Chairman of Mind Robotics, has said he started the company because he felt other startups were not fully equipped to automate industrial work [^1].
