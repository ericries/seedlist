## About

Fin.com is a New York-based financial infrastructure company building a unified network for global cross-border money movement, with white-label infrastructure that lets businesses convert stablecoins into local bank account deposits [^1] [^2]. The company serves financial services firms, consumer platforms, and prediction markets, and reports that its clients collectively serve over 800 million users [^1]. Fin.com maintains offices in New York, Las Vegas, Dubai, Dhaka, Bangalore, and Lahore [^1].

## What Investors Say

Vitor Lourenço, Founding Partner at Expa, on why cross-border payments resonate with the firm: "All of the Expa partners are immigrants. We grew up in different countries, so the opportunity of cross-border payments was very clear for us." [^1]

## What Founders Say

Nabeel Alamgir, co-founder and CEO, on the company's mission: "We believe that money movement is like a plane taking off from one airport, but it has to land somewhere else. We want to solve the last mile delivery problem." [^1]
