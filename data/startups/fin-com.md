## About

Fin.com is building an operating system for cross-border payments — a single API through which companies can collect, convert, hold, and pay out money around the world, using stablecoins for settlement and local payment rails at the edges [^3]. Contemporaneous coverage frames the same product as a unified network for global cross-border money movement that connects to major exchanges to handle the fiat leg of institutional client flows [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-15, Fin.com announced a $20M Seed round led by Expa with participation from Coinbase Ventures, Tenet Fund, Second Sight Ventures, and Bam Azizi [^1][^3].
