## About

Fin.com is a New York-based financial infrastructure company building a unified network for global cross-border money movement, with white-label infrastructure that lets businesses convert stablecoins into local bank account deposits [^1] [^2]. The company serves financial services firms, consumer platforms, and prediction markets, and reports that its clients collectively serve over 800 million users [^1]. Fin.com maintains offices in New York, Las Vegas, Dubai, Dhaka, Bangalore, and Lahore [^1].
