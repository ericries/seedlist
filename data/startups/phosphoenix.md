## About

Phosphoenix is an Amsterdam-based neurotechnology company developing a neuroprosthetic system that directly stimulates the brain's visual pathway to restore functional vision in people with profound blindness [^1][^2]. The company's approach centers on the Fountain Probe, a neural interface that targets the lateral geniculate nucleus (LGN) to elicit visual perceptions called phosphenes [^2]. Phosphoenix was co-founded by Pieter Roelfsema, Xing Chen, and Bert Monna, and is a spin-out that emerged from research at the Netherlands Institute for Neuroscience [^1].
