## About

Wultra is a Czech cybersecurity company providing post-quantum authentication and digital identity solutions for banks and fintech companies [^1][^2]. The company helps financial institutions replace legacy authentication methods with phishing-resistant and quantum-resistant technology, and supports identity proofing, transaction authorization, and electronic signatures [^1][^2]. Wultra was founded in 2014 by CEO Petr Dvorak, and supports more than 70 clients across 25 countries [^1][^2]. The company will use the new financing to scale its digital identity platform, support the EUDI Wallet ecosystem rollout, and expand operations into the Middle East and the United States [^1][^2].
