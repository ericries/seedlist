## About

GenePlanet is a Ljubljana, Slovenia-based genomics and laboratory platform that delivers clinical, preventive, and research genetic testing and next-generation sequencing (NGS) services across more than 50 markets [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2008 by Marko Bitenc [^2][^3]. In February 2026, GenePlanet acquired Budapest-based genetic testing company New Era Genetics, and its Zagreb laboratory serves as the central hub of a certified Pan-European laboratory platform that also includes hubs in Sweden and Hungary [^1]. On 2026-07-30, GenePlanet announced a strategic investment from Paris- and San Francisco-based healthtech investor Lauxera Capital Partners to accelerate its global expansion, with existing investor BlackPeak Capital partially exiting while retaining a meaningful shareholding [^1][^2][^4].
