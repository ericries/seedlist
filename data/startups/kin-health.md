## About

Kin Health is an AI-powered application that records doctor visits and generates summaries with next steps, lets users document questions for upcoming appointments, and supports sharing notes with family and friends; the company plans to integrate data from electronic health records and other medical sources [^1]. The $9M Seed announced May 18, 2026 was led by Maveron with participation from Town Hall Ventures, Eniac Ventures, Flex Capital, Foundry Square Capital, Pear VC, and The Family Fund [^1].
