## About

Greenpixie is a London-based climate-tech startup, founded in 2021 by CEO John Ridd and CTO William Tinney, that builds usage-based sustainability data tooling for companies running workloads on hyperscaler cloud platforms including Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud [^2]. The company's "GreenOps" platform measures and helps reduce the carbon emissions of cloud computing and AI workloads, with customers including Mastercard [^1]. Earlier funding includes a £250K pre-seed round in 2022 led by Ascension and a £50K Innovate UK grant in 2023 [^2].
