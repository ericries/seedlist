## About

Beelzebub is a Milan-based cybersecurity startup developing an AI-native platform that uses AI-powered honeypots ("hacker traps") to protect organisations from AI-driven cyberattacks [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-27, the company announced a €3 million seed round led exclusively by United Ventures, following a €300,000 pre-seed round backed by strategic investors and advisors — bringing total funding to €3.3 million [^1][^2]. The project began as Mario Candela's open-source research project in Milan in 2021 and was incorporated as a company in July 2025, gaining traction after Microsoft, AWS, and Akamai began using it in their operations [^1][^2]. Capital will fund expansion of the research team, the opening of new commercial offices in Rome and San Francisco by the end of the year, and client acquisition across Europe — with a particular emphasis on NIS2-regulated organisations [^1][^2].
