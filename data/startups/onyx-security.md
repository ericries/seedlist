## About

Onyx Security operates the Secure AI Control Plane, a platform that helps enterprises govern autonomous AI agents deployed inside their organizations [^1][^2]. Co-headquartered in New York and Tel Aviv-Yafo, the company was founded in 2024 by Maxim Bar Kogan (CEO) and Gil Elbaz [^1]. Onyx reports securing more than 1.1 million agents across enterprise customers and inspecting more than 66 million AI sessions in real time [^1]. In June 2026, Anthropic announced an integration with Onyx to secure enterprise AI adoption [^2].
