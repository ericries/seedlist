## About

Hush Security is a Tel Aviv-based cybersecurity company building identity infrastructure for AI agents and other non-human identities [^1][^2]. The company's founders previously worked together at Meta Networks, a Zero Trust platform acquired by Proofpoint in 2019 for $120 million [^1][^2]. Hush's platform aims to eliminate standing credentials for autonomous software and provide governance controls for enterprise AI agents [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Hush Security announced a $30 million Series A with Akamai Technologies joining as a strategic investor alongside existing investors Battery Ventures and YL Ventures, bringing total capital raised to $41 million [^1][^2]. The company was less than one year out of stealth at the time of the round [^1].
