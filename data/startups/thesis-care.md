## About

Thesis Care (formerly Trovo Health) is a New York-based AI clinical capacity platform founded in 2024 by Niren Gandra, M.D. (CEO) and Aditya Pandyaram (CPO), both former executives at patient financial engagement company Cedar [^1][^2][^3]. The platform combines AI agents with clinical experts-in-the-loop to take on care management and clinical operations workflows for healthcare organizations [^1][^2]. The $45M Series A announced March 25, 2026 was led by Oak HC/FT with participation from CRV, Black Opal Ventures, and C-level healthcare technology angels; the company also unveiled its rebrand from Trovo Health to Thesis Care to reflect the platform's evolution, bringing cumulative funding to $60M [^1][^2][^3].
