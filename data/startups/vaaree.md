## About

Vaaree is a Bengaluru-based curated online marketplace for home products across décor, furnishings, bed and bath, kitchen, and lighting categories, founded in 2022 by Garima and Varun Vohra [^1][^2][^3]. The platform lists more than 150,000 products and operates VibeCheck, an AI-powered discovery tool that allows shoppers to build moodboards, visualize combinations, and purchase coordinated looks [^1][^2]. Following the Series A, Vaaree plans to expand its fulfillment network for next-day delivery across major Indian cities and further invest in AI-driven product discovery [^1][^2].
