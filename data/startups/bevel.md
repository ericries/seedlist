## About

Bevel is an AI-native private risk management platform that pairs software with concierge-style advisors to help homeowners craft personal insurance and coverage plans across home, auto, and personal umbrella lines [^1][^2]. The company operates across offices in New York, Austin, and San Francisco [^2].
