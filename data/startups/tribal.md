## About

Tribal is an enterprise-native AI platform founded by Salesforce, Wix, and Spot.io veterans, building context-aware AI agents that learn the complete metadata graph of an enterprise system to build with full organizational context [^1][^2][^3]. The $10M Seed announced May 20, 2026 was led by Team8 with participation from DYDX Capital and a cohort of angel investors including prominent founders from the Salesforce ecosystem [^1][^2][^3].
