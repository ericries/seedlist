## About

Rejuvenate Bio is a San Diego-based biotechnology company developing gene therapies for chronic and age-related diseases [^1]. The company's platform is initially being applied to companion animal health through a strategic R&D collaboration with Merck Animal Health, with longer-term ambitions to translate gene therapy approaches into treatments for age-related conditions [^1][^2].
