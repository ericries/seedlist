## About

Form Energy is a Somerville, Massachusetts–based energy storage company developing iron-air batteries designed to store electricity for up to 100 hours, targeting long-duration grid applications [^1][^2]. The company operates a battery factory in Weirton, West Virginia and was founded in 2017 [^2][^3].
