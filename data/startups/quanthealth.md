## About

QuantHealth is a Tel Aviv-based digital-health startup that uses AI to simulate clinical trials before pharmaceutical and biotech companies commit to enrolling patients. Its platform models how a specific trial design would likely play out — testing different protocols, spotting efficacy and safety risks, and helping teams optimize before spending capital [^1][^2]. The company reports that 12 of the top 20 pharmaceutical companies use its platform, with up to 90% claimed simulation accuracy [^1]. QuantHealth's simulations cover more than 600 virtual trials at present [^1].
