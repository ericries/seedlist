## About

Hyperion Robotics is an Espoo, Finland–based robotics company that builds robotic microfactories for large-scale 3D concrete printing of infrastructure components such as bridges, substations, and water systems [^1][^2]. The company positions itself as bringing "physical AI" to European construction and infrastructure, and previously raised €3M in a 2022 seed round led by Lifeline Ventures [^1][^2][^3].

On 2026-07-16 Hyperion Robotics announced a €6.4 million funding round (approximately $7.4 million in USD coverage) [^1][^2][^3]. The round was co-led by Course Corrected and the European Innovation Council Fund, with participation from RE Ventures, Lifeline Ventures, Übermorgen Ventures, and PC Rettig Impact & Co [^1][^3]. Proceeds will fund Forge I, the company's first large factory in the UK, to be built in Flixborough, near Scunthorpe, in partnership with LKAB [^1][^3].
