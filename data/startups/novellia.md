## About

Novellia is a New York-based patient-controlled health data platform that helps individuals unify up to 20 years of medical records from disparate hospital systems, doctors' offices, and labs in under 30 seconds, then share anonymized data with pharmaceutical companies and researchers [^1][^2]. The company is founded by Shashi Shankar (CEO), who came from a pharma background, and Elliot Katz (CTO) [^3]. Its customers include several of the top 10 pharmaceutical companies and early-stage diagnostics firms [^1].

Alongside the Series A, Novellia launched a patient-facing mobile app extending its online platform that allows individuals to securely access their complete health history [^1].
