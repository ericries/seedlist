## About

Beacon Software is an AI-native holding company that acquires profitable, founder-led vertical software businesses serving "Main Street" markets — including youth sports leagues, campgrounds, schools, construction firms, and manufacturers — and rebuilds them on a shared AI platform [^1][^2]. Headquartered in Toronto with offices in San Francisco and New York, the company was founded in 2024 by Nilam Ganenthiran (former President of Instacart) and Divya Gupta [^2][^3].

Beacon describes itself as an "anti-private-equity" roll-up: rather than cutting costs, it applies an in-house AI and operational playbook to grow acquired businesses, reporting more than 50% EBITDA growth across its portfolio over the past year and acquiring a new business roughly once a week [^1][^2]. With the Series C, total capital raised reached approximately $550 million within two years of launch [^1][^2].

In conjunction with the Series C, Beacon hired Mark Schaaf (former CTO of Instacart and Superhuman) as Chief Operating and Product Officer, and Goutham Buchi (former CTO of AngelList) as Chief Technology Officer [^1][^2].

## What Investors Say

Lightspeed Venture Partners, writing about their investment in Beacon, highlighted "the rare combination of proven scaling expertise and deep technical innovation" that founders Nilam Ganenthiran and Divya Gupta bring, noting the opportunity aligns with their Growth Private Equity strategy and heritage backing enterprise software companies [^3].

General Catalyst, announcing its decision to double down on Beacon, stated that Beacon has helped acquired companies improve their Rule of 40 metric by an average of 1,000 basis points within one year "not through cost-cutting, but by building with their applied AI and operational playbook" [^6].
