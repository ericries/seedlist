## About

Yellow Card is an Atlanta, Georgia-based stablecoin infrastructure company that operates cross-border payment rails across more than 50 countries, primarily focused on Africa, Latin America, and Asia-Pacific [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded in 2016 by CEO Chris Maurice and CTO Justin Poiroux and previously operated a retail crypto exchange before pivoting to stablecoin-based payments infrastructure for enterprises [^2][^3]. Its flagship product, Global USD Accounts, enables businesses to hold dollars, swap stablecoins, manage treasury, and move local currency on domestic rails; existing users include Visa and Western Union [^1]. Yellow Card reports having processed more than $6B in volume across 35+ countries via 106+ Tier 1 banking and liquidity partners [^2].

The round is described as strategic rather than a traditional Series designation and marks the first participation from a traditional bank's corporate venture arm (SC Ventures, Standard Chartered) and a consumer-electronics conglomerate's investment fund (Sony Innovation Fund) alongside Yellow Card's existing crypto-native backers [^1]. Proceeds will fund scaling of Global USD Accounts and deeper reach in Latin America and Asia-Pacific [^1][^2].
