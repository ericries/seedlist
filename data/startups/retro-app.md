## About

Retro is a friend-focused photo-sharing app built by former Instagram employees Nathan Sharp and Ryan Olson, operating under the corporate name Lone Palm Labs [^1]. Launched in 2023 as a personal photo journal, the app has expanded to let users privately share photos of their week with friends, create shared albums, and view recap features [^1]. Retro has generated more than 7 million downloads since launch, and in-app spending grew over 460% in the 180 days prior to the funding disclosure [^1].
