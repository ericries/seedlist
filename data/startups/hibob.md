## About

HiBob is the company behind "Bob," a people-management platform that connects HR, payroll, and finance for mid-market and enterprise organizations [^1]. On September 1, 2026, the company announced a funding round led by Salesforce with participation from Farallon Capital Management, positioning HiBob as an "organizational intelligence" layer for AI-powered enterprises [^1][^2]. Multiple sources reported the round at $166M (per company/press-release coverage in Israeli media) [^1][^2], while Bloomberg reported the size as $160M [^3]. The financing values HiBob at approximately $3.2 billion, up from a $2.7 billion valuation in its previous 2023 round [^2][^3]. HiBob reports more than 5,500 customers globally [^1].
