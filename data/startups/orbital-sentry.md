## About

Orbital Sentry is a deep-tech startup building persistent infrared imaging systems for geosynchronous orbit (GEO), targeting real-time missile launch warning and battlespace awareness [^1][^2][^3]. Its planned commercial overhead persistent infrared (OPIR) platform is designed to deliver continuous, high-sensitivity infrared monitoring from GEO [^1][^2].
