## About

CuspAI is a Cambridge, UK-based AI startup founded in 2024 by Chad Edwards (CEO) and Max Welling (CTO), building an AI platform to accelerate materials discovery for semiconductors, manufacturing, clean energy, and computing applications [^1][^2][^3]. The platform generates candidate material designs and tests them in simulations. Geoffrey Hinton and Yann LeCun serve as advisers, and the early customer roster reportedly includes ASML, Meta Platforms, and Hyundai Motor [^1][^3]. The company open-sourced a materials simulation toolkit called kUPS in April 2026 [^2].

On 2026-07-20, CuspAI announced a $450 million (€393.2 million) Series B at a $2.6 billion (€2.2 billion) valuation, and simultaneously launched the AI Materials Foundry — a coalition of more than 45 organisations including NVIDIA, Meta, Samsung, Hyundai Motor Group, Henkel, Applied Materials, Tokyo Electron and Lam Research — pooling computing and scientific resources to accelerate materials design [^4][^5][^6]. The valuation grew from $520 million in September 2025 [^5].
