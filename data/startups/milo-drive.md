## About

Milo Drive is a Gurugram-based electric-vehicle mobility platform serving India's ride-hailing and urban transportation ecosystem [^1][^2]. Co-founded by Monil Jayeshkumar Khatri and Vishal Jewrajka, the company operates an asset-light, technology-led model that combines vehicle access, charging intelligence, demand aggregation across ride-hailing channels, and operator tools to improve driver earnings and fleet reliability [^1][^3]. The platform has powered more than one million rides to date [^1].

On 2026-07-08, Milo Drive announced a $2.4M seed round co-led by Caret Capital and Antler, with participation from Alteria Capital, IAN Capital, Climate Angels, and Aureolis Ventures [^1][^2][^3]. The company said the capital will be used to accelerate EV adoption on its platform and create more formal employment opportunities for India's driver workforce [^2]. India's EV ride-hailing market is projected to grow from $0.24B in 2025 to $4.2B by 2030 [^2].
