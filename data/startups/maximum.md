## About

Maximum is a Miami-based fintech infrastructure startup building an AI-native operating system for banks, designed to replace the fragmented, decades-old core infrastructure that powers most of the banking industry [^1][^2][^3]. Founder Randy Fernando is a repeat fintech entrepreneur who previously founded and sold Vault to Acorns in 2017 and Power to Marqeta in 2023 [^3]. Maximum's platform aims to give banks a real-time system with AI at the foundational level, allowing them to build and deploy custom agents for operational automation [^1][^3].
