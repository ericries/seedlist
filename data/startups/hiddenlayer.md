## About

HiddenLayer is an Austin-based AI security company founded in 2022 that builds a platform to protect agentic, generative, and predictive AI applications across their lifecycle — from model discovery and supply-chain validation to attack simulation and runtime defense [^1][^2]. On September 2, 2026, the company announced a $100 million Series B led by Delta-v Capital, with participation from Ten Eleven Ventures, Morgan Stanley, Microsoft's M12, and Booz Allen Ventures [^1][^2]. HiddenLayer reported more than 10x annual recurring revenue growth over the prior year, 50+ new platform customers, 39 granted patents (65 pending), and cited support for a frontier model provider serving 700+ million weekly users [^2]. Proceeds are earmarked for expanding Agentic Runtime Security and introducing Agent Harness Security for autonomous coding agents [^2].
