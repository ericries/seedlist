## About

Proaction is an AI-native fleet management technology and services company positioning itself as a modern alternative to legacy commercial-fleet outsourcing [^1] [^2]. Rather than requiring operators to choose between disconnected software and expensive managed services, Proaction combines its services platform with AI agents that replace traditional call centers, letting fleets manage work themselves on top of Proaction's infrastructure [^1] [^2]. The company plans to use the new capital to accelerate product development, expand AI-enabled operations, and grow its engineering, operations, sales, and customer success teams [^1] [^3].
