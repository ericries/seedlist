## About

Able Made is a soccer-inspired sustainable apparel and accessories company that sells off-pitch, soccer-style clothing, founded by Suzanne McKenzie in 2012 [^1][^2]. The brand's products are marketed as responsibly produced and have appeared on athletes across the NBA, WNBA, NFL, and MLB [^2]. The seed round was backed by a group of professional-sports investors that includes Oregon Sports Angels — an angel network that invests in sports and technology startups — along with named individual angels from the sports world [^1][^2][^3].
