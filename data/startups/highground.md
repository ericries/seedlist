## About

HighGround, a Washington, DC-based data and intelligence layer for defense capital markets, raised $6.5 million in seed funding announced June 17, 2026 [^1]. The company's platform ingests budget, procurement, and vendor signals from more than 500 federal sources to help defense contractors, investors, and operators track contracting data, legislative trends, and procurement activity [^1]. The new capital will be used to expand the platform and enter the federal health and pharmaceutical sectors [^1].
