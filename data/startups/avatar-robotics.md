## About

Avatar Robotics is a San Francisco–based industrial robotics company that launched publicly in December 2025 and combines robotic hardware, remote human operators, and AI-driven autonomy to address labor shortages in warehousing, logistics, and manufacturing [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Colin Webb (CEO), Nenye Anagbogu, and Hamid Farzaneh [^1].
