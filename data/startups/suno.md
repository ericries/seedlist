## About

Suno is a Cambridge, Massachusetts-based generative-AI music company founded in 2024 by CEO Mikey Shulman and three co-founders who were previously working at Kensho (the AI/financial-data firm), building consumer and pro tools that let users generate full songs with vocals and instrumentation from text prompts [^1][^2][^3]. The company has previously announced an upcoming music model developed in collaboration with the music industry establishment, while also facing several active copyright lawsuits [^1][^2]. The $400M Series D announced June 3, 2026 was led by Bond Capital at a $5.4B valuation, joined by IVP, Forerunner Ventures, Union Square Ventures, Alkeon, and Quiet Capital, with returning investors Matrix Partners, Lightspeed Venture Partners, Menlo Ventures, and Schroders Capital — coming ~7 months after Suno's $250M November 2025 round at $2.45B [^1][^2][^3].
