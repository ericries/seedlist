## About

Enzo Health is a Lehi, Utah-based AI-powered platform for home health agencies founded in 2024 that combines an intake tool, AI scribe, and quality assurance tool built specifically for home health teams [^1][^2]. The company's mission is to remove the administrative burden driving clinician burnout and turnover in home-care delivery [^1]. The $20M Series A announced May 4, 2026 was led by N47 with participation from existing investors Gradient, Tandem Ventures, and Rigby Watts; total funding now stands at $26M [^1][^2]. Proceeds will expand Enzo's home health offerings and diversify into skilled nursing and hospice [^1][^2].
