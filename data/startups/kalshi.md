## About

Kalshi is a New York-based CFTC-regulated prediction markets exchange where users trade event contracts on real-world outcomes [^1][^2]. The platform reported that institutional trading volume increased 800% over the six months preceding the May 2026 raise, while annualized trading volume tripled from $52B to $178B [^1]. Kalshi reports it controls over 90% of U.S. prediction market activity [^1]. The $1B Series F announced May 7, 2026 was led by Coatue at a $22B valuation, doubling its $11B valuation from five months earlier [^1][^2][^3].
