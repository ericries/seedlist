## About

Throne Science (also known as Throne) is a digital health startup building an AI-powered toilet-mounted camera system that uses computer vision to analyze stool and urine, tracking gut health and hydration metrics. The platform includes a Gut Health AI coach powered by large language models that provides personalized feedback based on health data. The company was founded in 2023; John Capodilupo was previously co-founder and CTO of Whoop [^1].

Validation studies of the platform are underway in partnership with Harvard, Stanford, and the University of Chicago [^1].
