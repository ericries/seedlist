## About

Adjuvia Therapeutics is a preclinical biotechnology company developing oral therapeutics designed to repair mitochondrial damage by simultaneously addressing oxidative stress, chronic inflammation, and organ degeneration [^1][^2]. Its lead candidate, ATI-105, is a blood-brain-barrier-penetrant lipid nanoparticle formulation of a novel astaxanthin molecule targeting mitochondrial dysfunction [^1][^3]. On 2026-07-28, Adjuvia closed an $8 million Series Seed financing led by JLO Ventures with participation from Portfolia Ventures and biopharma industry leaders; proceeds will fund an IND filing for ATI-105 later in 2026 and a Phase 1 healthy-volunteer trial in Fall 2026, followed by a Phase 1/2 study in Friedreich's ataxia patients in early 2027 [^1][^2][^3].
