## About

Stark Defence is a Berlin-based defense technology company developing one-way attack drones, also known as loitering munitions, for European militaries [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by Florian Seibel, who previously built Munich-based surveillance drone maker Quantum Systems, alongside Johannes Schaback [^1][^2][^3]. Uwe Horstmann, a founding partner of Berlin venture firm Project A, was appointed CEO in October 2025 [^1][^3]. Stark's lead product is the Virtus loitering munition, and Germany awarded Stark and Munich rival Helsing initial contracts worth roughly €269 million each in February 2026 to equip the Bundeswehr's 45th Armoured Brigade in Lithuania [^1][^3]. The company has committed more than 80% of its new capital to research, manufacturing, and scaling production toward thousands of systems per month [^1][^2][^3].
