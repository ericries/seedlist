## About

Stoke Space is a Kent, Washington launch vehicle company developing Nova, a fully and rapidly reusable orbital rocket architecture in which both the first and second stages return to Earth for reflight [^1][^2]. Co-founded in 2019 by Andy Lapsa and Tom Feldman, the company is targeting Nova Pathfinder's first orbital launch in early 2027 and a 15-ton-payload Nova Block 2 by 2029 [^2]. On September 8, 2026, Stoke announced an initial $1 billion closing of a Series E co-led by Point72 Ventures and Spark Capital, bringing total funding to approximately $2.3 billion [^1][^2][^3].

## What Investors Say

Chris Morales, Partner at Point72 Ventures: "Stoke is pursuing what we see as one of the most important opportunities in space transportation: making launch fully reusable, reliable, and scalable" [^2].

Clay Fisher, General Partner at Spark Capital: "Full reusability is the inevitable end state of the market, providing an order-of-magnitude cost advantage" [^2].

## What Founders Say

Andy Lapsa, Co-Founder and CEO: "Every mature transportation system is built around fully reusable vehicles. It's the only way to reach the cost floor while scaling availability" [^2].

Andy Lapsa on the Series E: "This round is really to scale. To lay the infrastructure, to scale in production and flight frequency, and importantly to fund the development of the second generation vehicle" [^1].
