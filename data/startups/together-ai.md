## About

Together AI is a San Francisco-based AI acceleration cloud that provides training and inference infrastructure for open-source frontier models, including DeepSeek, Nemotron, MiniMax, and Kimi [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2022 by Vipul Ved Prakash (CEO), Percy Liang, and Ce Zhang (CTO) [^2][^3]. As of the Series C, the company reported annual bookings of $1.15 billion in the most recent quarter and said it expects to grow its infrastructure footprint roughly 50-fold over the next five years [^2][^3].
