## About

Atomic One is a Málaga-based technology company that has developed an AI operating system for e-commerce brands, designed to automate up to 80% of the operational tasks involved in running an online store [^1][^2]. The platform deploys AI agents that collect and process large volumes of data to execute decisions that have traditionally required human intervention [^1][^2]. The company currently employs 13 professionals across artificial intelligence, software engineering, data science, product management, and marketing [^1][^2].
