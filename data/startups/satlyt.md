## About

Satlyt is a space-infrastructure startup building software that runs AI models directly on satellites, enabling onboard inference across multiple spacecraft operators [^1]. The company is dual-headquartered in Sunnyvale, California and Nairobi, Kenya, and is led by founder Rama Afullo, a Kenyan American engineer who previously worked at Google's cloud computing division and SpaceX's Starlink division [^1][^2].
