## About

Rime is a San Francisco-based enterprise voice AI company that develops speech models trained on proprietary conversational data, announced on 2026-07-15 that it raised a $24 million Series A led by M13 Ventures, with participation from Twilio Ventures, Corazon Capital, Unusual Ventures, Cadenza Ventures, and existing investors [^1][^2][^3]. Founded by CEO Lily Clifford (former Stanford PhD student), Brooke Larson (ex-Amazon Alexa engineer), and Ares Geovanos (Stanford engineer), Rime uses a phoneme-based architecture that optimizes pronunciation of brand names and industry-specific terms, reducing customization needs for customers in food service, healthcare, airlines, and fintech [^1][^3]. The company says it powers close to 100 million phone calls a month for customers such as Mayo Clinic, Dialpad, Upstart, and Asurion [^2][^3]. The Series A follows a $5.5 million seed announced in May 2024 and brings total funding to approximately $29.5 million [^2]. As part of the round, M13 partner Morgan Blumberg joined the board and Rafael Valle (previously audio understanding lead at Meta Superintelligence Labs) joined as Chief Scientist [^2][^3].
