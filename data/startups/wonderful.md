## About

Wonderful is an Israeli-Dutch AI agent startup founded in early 2025 by CEO Bar Winkler and CTO Roey Lalazar that builds an AI operating system platform coordinating AI agents, workflows, and applications across enterprise data and systems [^1][^2][^3]. Initially launched as a customer service AI agent platform for non-English-speaking markets across telecom, finance, healthcare, and manufacturing, the company now operates across 35+ countries using forward-deployed engineers to integrate solutions into client operations [^1][^2][^3]. On September 2, 2026, Wonderful announced a $550M Series C plus a $170M secondary transaction led by Insight Partners with participation from Salesforce (its first investment in the company), Index Ventures, IVP, Vine Ventures, 9Yards, and Bessemer Venture Partners, valuing the business at $5B — more than double the $2B Series B valuation set just six months earlier [^2][^3]. Wonderful reported approximately 650 employees (half based in Israel) and a $70M annual revenue run rate projected to exceed $100M by year-end 2026 [^3].
