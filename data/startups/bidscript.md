## About

BidScript is a UK-based (Cheshire) AI-native tender management platform that helps organisations find, qualify, manage, write and submit bids for public and private sector contracts across sectors including construction, engineering, IT and education [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded by childhood friends Henry Brogan and Tyler McCarthy while they were still at university [^1][^2]. BidScript reports that customers using the platform see up to 50% higher tender win rates, and says it will use the follow-on pre-seed funding to grow its team, expand product capabilities and pursue expansion in the UK, US and Middle East [^1][^2].
