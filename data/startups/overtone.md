## About

Overtone is a voice- and audio-forward AI dating service founded by Hinge founder Justin McLeod [^1][^2][^3]. Rather than a swipe-based social platform with photos and profiles, the service is designed around AI-mediated one-to-one voice conversations that build a deep understanding of each user before making curated introductions [^1][^3]. The company frames the product as an alternative to feed-based dating apps: "We get to know each person deeply, learning about them in their own voice… and we make only the introductions that are worth making, grounded in relationship science and thoughtful reflection" [^1]. Overtone is not yet open to the public and plans to launch in select markets by end of 2026 [^1]. Advisors and board members include psychotherapist Esther Perel, coach Diana Chapman, and Match Group CEO Spencer Rascoff, with McLeod as Chairman [^1].
