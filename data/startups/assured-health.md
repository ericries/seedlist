## About

Assured Health, based in New York, uses AI agents to automate provider credentialing, licensing, payer enrollment, and network management for healthcare organizations, reducing processes that traditionally take months down to days [^1][^2][^3]. Its AI agents verify provider information across roughly 2,000 primary sources [^3]. The platform serves more than 100 healthcare providers, including Houston Methodist and Tono Health [^1].
