## About

Vinyl Equity is a Chicago-based financial technology company building modern infrastructure for capital markets and corporate transactions. The company operates as an SEC-registered transfer agent, supporting public and private companies with IPOs, mergers and acquisitions, equity plan administration, and shareholder recordkeeping [^1] [^2]. CEO Rob Schoder describes the legacy systems the company is replacing as "built for a different era," with workarounds that "no longer scale" [^1].
