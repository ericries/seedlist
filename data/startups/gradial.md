## About

Gradial is a Seattle-based startup that builds AI agents to automate enterprise marketing workflows [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023, shortly after the debut of ChatGPT, by four co-founders who met at Dartmouth: Doug Tallmadge (CEO), Deip Kumar (CTO), Anish Chadalavada (Chief Growth Officer), and Anup Chamrajnagar (COO) [^2]. Tallmadge and Kumar both previously worked as engineers at SpaceX's Starlink, while Chadalavada and Chamrajnagar had prior experience at Point72 Ventures [^2].
