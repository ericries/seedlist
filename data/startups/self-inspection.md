## About

Self Inspection is a San Diego-based automotive AI company founded in 2021 that provides a smartphone-based vehicle damage-assessment platform used by rental fleets, automotive finance companies, auctions, and marketplaces [^1][^2]. The platform guides users through image capture with a smartphone camera and compares captured data against a database of vehicle damage patterns to estimate repair costs and identify required parts [^1]. The company reports having completed more than one million vehicle inspections, saving customers over $80 million in costs and 300,000 operational hours; Stellantis's financial services arm uses the platform for corporate-owned vehicles and lease-end inspections [^1][^2]. On July 16, 2026, Self Inspection announced a $10 million Series A round led by Sandberg Bernthal Venture Partners, the family office of Sheryl Sandberg [^1][^2]. Constantine Yaremtso is CEO [^1].
