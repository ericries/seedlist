## About

Moss is a Berlin-based AI finance platform for mid-sized companies, founded in 2019 by Ferdinand Meyer, Anton Rummel, Stephan Haslebacher, and Ante Spittler (CEO) [^1][^2]. The company offers corporate credit cards and spend-management software and is used by more than 5,000 companies across Germany, the UK, the Netherlands, and Austria [^1]. As of the Series C, Moss reported over €70 million in annual recurring revenue, 65% year-over-year revenue growth, and more than 350 employees [^1]. Fresh capital is earmarked primarily for AI agents that further automate financial processes for mid-market customers [^1][^2].

The Series C valued Moss at €1 billion, making it Europe's newest fintech unicorn [^1][^2][^3]. Portage, the fintech arm of Canadian investment firm Sagard, led the round; existing backers (previously including Cherry Ventures, Global Founders Capital, and Valar Ventures) participated [^2][^3].
