## About

Partly builds Interpreter, a foundation model purpose-built for the automotive parts and repair supply chain, trained on five years of human feedback and synthetic data, more than 50 manufacturer agreements, and continuous live data. Tested across 50 Toyota repair jobs, Interpreter achieved 60% F1 (a combined measure of precision and recall) versus 1-5% F1 for general AI models without catalogue data. Founded in Christchurch, New Zealand in 2020 by Levi Fawcett (an ex-Rocket Lab engineer) alongside co-founders Nathan Taylor and Taylor Howes, the company is now headquartered in Austin, Texas with a second office in San Francisco and employs 160 people across 20+ countries [^1][^2][^3].
