## About

Partly builds Interpreter, a foundation model purpose-built for the automotive parts and repair supply chain, trained on five years of human feedback and synthetic data, more than 50 manufacturer agreements, and continuous live data. Tested across 50 Toyota repair jobs, Interpreter achieved 60% F1 (a combined measure of precision and recall) versus 1-5% F1 for general AI models without catalogue data. Founded in Christchurch, New Zealand in 2020 by Levi Fawcett (an ex-Rocket Lab engineer) alongside co-founders Nathan Taylor and Taylor Howes, the company is now headquartered in Austin, Texas with a second office in San Francisco and employs 160 people across 20+ countries [^1][^2][^4].

The Series A in late 2022 was led by Octopus Ventures and valued Partly at $180M [^4][^5]. The June 2026 Series B was a partial close at a $500M post-money valuation; the company has stated it does not anticipate seeking additional funding until 2027 [^4]. Total capital raised to date is approximately $92.4M [^4]. Series B proceeds will fund compute for training version seven of Interpreter, team expansion across Europe, and US sales and marketing [^1][^2].
