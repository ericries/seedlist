## About

Odyssey Therapeutics is a Boston-based clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company founded in 2021 by Gary D. Glick, Ph.D., a serial biotech entrepreneur whose prior companies include IFM Therapeutics, Scorpion Therapeutics, and Lycera Corporation [^1][^4]. The company develops small-molecule medicines for autoimmune and inflammatory diseases, with a lead program (OD-001) targeting RIPK2 for inflammatory bowel disease and ulcerative colitis [^2][^3]. On May 8, 2026, Odyssey priced an upsized IPO of 15.5 million shares at $18, raising approximately $279M in IPO proceeds, plus a concurrent $25M private placement with TPG Life Sciences Innovations, for combined gross proceeds of approximately $304M [^2][^3]. Shares began trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market under the ticker "ODTX" on May 8, 2026 [^2][^3]. The company has raised more than $700M across prior private rounds since its 2021 launch, including a $218M Series A (December 2021) led by OrbiMed Advisors and co-led by SR One, and an oversubscribed $213M Series D (September 10, 2025) led by SR One [^4][^5][^6].
