## About

Amber Electric is a Melbourne-based energy retailer and software company whose platform automates the charging and discharging of home batteries and electric vehicles in response to real-time wholesale electricity prices, letting households buy, store, and sell electricity back to the grid [^1][^2]. The company says it manages more than 50% of Australia's automated home batteries [^1][^2], and is expanding into Europe through a partnership with E.ON that underpins the "Next Optimize" smart tariff launched in the UK in March 2026 [^2].

On 2026-09-21, Amber announced an A$78.5 million Series E led by 1GT — Morgan Stanley Investment Management's climate private equity strategy — with participation from E.ON, Environmental Technologies Fund (ETF Partners), and Innovation Victoria [^1][^2][^3]. The company described the round as "nearly twice oversubscribed" [^1]. ETF Partners had previously led a €26M investment in Amber (initial investment June 2025) [^3].
