## About

Xapien is an AI-native due diligence platform used by compliance, legal, and procurement teams to research counterparties, third parties, and individuals in minutes rather than hours [^1]. Founded in 2018 as Digital Insight Technologies (later rebranded to Xapien) by Dan Secretan and Shaun O'Mahony — both formerly of BAE Systems' financial crime and national security divisions — the company serves ~350 clients across 15 countries and reports 350%+ ARR growth over 24 months [^2]. Chris Green joined as CEO in 2022; the company is UK-headquartered and expanding its Boston office to serve the US market, where roughly 50% of revenue is now generated [^2].
