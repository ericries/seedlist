## About

LinqAlpha is a New York-based AI research platform building what the company calls an "Alpha Intelligence Layer" for global public markets — a multi-agent system that surfaces market-moving signals across equities, macro, credit, and multi-asset strategies [^1][^2]. The company was founded by former Goldman Sachs analysts and MIT computer science PhDs Jacob Choi, Subeen Pang, Jin Kim, and Hojun Choi [^1][^2]. LinqAlpha's platform is used by more than 70 financial institutions across the United States, Europe, and Asia, including sell-side sales, trading, and research teams at investment banks and buy-side clients including Causeway Capital Management and Schonfeld Strategic Advisors; buy-side clients collectively manage more than $5 trillion in assets [^1][^2].
