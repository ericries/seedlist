## About

M1X Global is a New York-based sovereign financial infrastructure company that helps governments issue and manage sovereign debt on public blockchains [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2026 by CEO Mark Lurie and President/COO Jordan Goldman, the company partnered with the Republic of the Marshall Islands to issue USDM1, a tokenized U.S. dollar-denominated sovereign debt instrument backed one-to-one by U.S. Treasuries and issued natively by a sovereign on a public blockchain [^1][^2]. USDM1 has been evaluated in institutional working groups involving Bank of America, Citadel Securities, Virtu Financial, Tradeweb, and DTCC as a mechanism to improve collateral mobility and settlement in 24/7 markets [^1][^2].
