## About

PageMind is a Spanish AI startup founded by Jaume Portell that helps e-commerce businesses optimise product content for how products are discovered, understood and recommended across digital channels and AI-powered search engines [^1]. The platform analyses consumer behaviour and search intent to generate product descriptions, buying guides, comparison pages and FAQs, and deploys conversational AI assistants to improve product visibility on AI search platforms [^1].
