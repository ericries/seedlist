## About

Singularity is a Los Angeles-based defense startup developing low-cost, missile-based air-defense interceptors designed to shoot down cheap drones and munitions at a fraction of the cost of legacy systems [^1][^2][^3][^4]. The company built all of the interceptor's subsystems in-house — including the rocket motor — and is applying automotive-scale manufacturing methods to production, drawing engineers from Tesla and Toyota to run the assembly line [^1][^2]. Co-founder and CEO Jack Oswald and COO Shail Giroux lead a team of about 65 employees pulled from SpaceX, Tesla, Anduril, and Lockheed Martin, alongside operators with combat air-defense experience [^1][^2][^3]. The company flight-tests its interceptor multiple times a month and says it plans to deploy to two active conflict zones [^2].

On 2026-07-14 Singularity emerged from two years of stealth with an $80 million Series A at a $400 million valuation, co-led by Khosla Ventures and Felicis [^1][^2][^3][^4]. Participating investors include seed backers AE Ventures and NEA, along with Long Journey, Harpoon, Menlo Ventures, Y Combinator, Decisive Point, New Vista, Sunflower, and Soma [^1][^2][^4].
