## About

Beagle Labs is a New York–based insurtech that operates an AI platform delivering property inspection data to commercial insurance underwriters [^1]. The company was founded in 2022 by Dwight Neptune, Josie Madingo, Keelan Gayle, and Luis Guzman [^1].
