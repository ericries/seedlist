## About

Unframe is a Cupertino-based managed AI delivery platform with offices in Israel and Berlin, founded in 2024 by CEO Shay Levi, COO Larissa Schneider, and VP R&D Adi Azarya; the company productizes the operational layer that takes enterprise AI use cases from pilot to production [^1][^2][^3]. Since its April 2025 public launch with a $50M seed, Unframe booked more than $100M in total contract value within 12 months and reports 400% net revenue retention [^1][^2]. The $50M Series B announced May 19, 2026 was led by Highland Europe with participation from existing investors Bessemer Venture Partners, Craft Ventures, TLV Partners, Third Point Ventures, Cerca Partners, and Vintage Investment Partners; the round brings total funding to $100M [^1][^2][^3].
