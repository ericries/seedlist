## About

Augmodo is a Seattle-based spatial-AI company that builds a wearable "Smartbadge" — a lightweight device equipped with dual cameras that employees wear on the job [^1][^2]. The device continuously scans the physical environment using computer vision, 3D mapping, and spatial computing, and constructs real-time digital maps called "Realograms" that log inventory and asset locations without interrupting workers [^1][^2]. Founded around 2020 and led by CEO Ross Finman, Augmodo originally focused on grocery retail and is now expanding into warehouses, factories, hospitals, and automotive facilities [^1][^2]. The company says it maps more than 186 million square feet of retail space monthly and grew revenue 10x over the past year [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-13, Augmodo announced a $21 million funding round at a $350 million post-money valuation, led by existing backer TQ Ventures [^1][^2].
