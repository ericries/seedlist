## About

CereVasc is a Boston-based clinical-stage medical device company developing minimally invasive treatments for neurological diseases [^1]. Its lead product, the eShunt System, is designed to treat Normal Pressure Hydrocephalus (NPH) by draining excess cerebrospinal fluid from the brain into the venous system through a small implant placed in the cerebellopontine angle, avoiding traditional invasive shunt surgery [^1] [^2].

The Series C proceeds will fund completion of the ongoing STRIDE pivotal trial in NPH patients, operational scale-up, regulatory preparation for a future Premarket Approval (PMA) submission to the FDA, and US commercialization readiness [^1].

In conjunction with the financing, the board was strengthened with the addition of Kevin Conroy as lead independent director, Christopher Geyen as independent director and audit committee chair, and Tom Schnettler of Piper Sandler Merchant Banking representing the new Series C investors [^1].
