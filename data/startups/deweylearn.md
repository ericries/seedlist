## About

DeweyLearn is a New York-based multimodal AI company that assesses real-world skill performance and delivers expert-level feedback across clinical healthcare, higher education, workforce development, and K-12 settings, in both physical and remote environments [^1][^2]. The platform combines audio, video, and learning data to evaluate competencies rather than relying on quizzes or click-tracking alone [^1][^3]. Co-founders Luyen Chou (CEO) and Dirk Liebich (CTO) frame the product as a "world model of learning" that understands what learners do, not just what they click [^2][^3].

On 2026-07-16, DeweyLearn announced an oversubscribed $5 million Series A led by SJF Ventures, with participation from Catalysis Capital, Morningside, and Owl Ventures [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds will be used to scale the multimodal AI platform and expand across clinical healthcare, higher education, workforce development, and K-12 markets [^1].
