## About

Helion Energy is a fusion energy company headquartered in Everett, Washington, working to commercialize fusion power generation [^1]. The company signed an agreement with Microsoft in 2023 to deliver electricity from a Central Washington fusion power plant by 2028 — the most ambitious commercial timeline in the fusion sector [^1][^2]. Helion is led by co-founder and CEO David Kirtley, who has stated the company is best positioned to generate electricity from fusion this decade [^1][^3].
