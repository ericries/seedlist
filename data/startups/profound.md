## About

Profound is an AI visibility platform focused on "answer engine optimization" (AEO) — helping brands measure and improve how they appear across AI assistants and answer engines such as ChatGPT, Google's AI Overviews, and Perplexity [^1][^3]. The company reports more than 1,000 enterprise customers, including Comcast, The Estée Lauder Companies, and Walmart, and reached a $1.8B valuation with its Series D roughly seven months after its Series C [^1][^2]. TechCrunch's contemporaneous coverage also notes the company grew revenue roughly 3x over the six months preceding the round [^1].
