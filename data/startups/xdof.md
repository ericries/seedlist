## About

XDOF emerged from stealth in June 2026 with $70 million in funding to build infrastructure for robot foundation models, including datasets, robotic systems, and software tools designed to support physical AI development [^1][^2]. The company works with robotics labs and commercial developers on hardware, operations, and model training, focusing on the underlying infrastructure needed to build more capable general-purpose robots [^1]. XDOF has approximately 60 employees and 20 active customers, including several frontier AI labs [^1].

The company was co-founded by Philipp Wu (CEO), Fred Shentu (CTO), and Nemo Jin (COO), with roots in the GELLO teleoperation project at UC Berkeley [^1]. At launch, XDOF released ABC-130K, which it describes as the largest open-source bimanual robot manipulation dataset, containing more than 130,000 demonstrations across 195 tasks, developed in collaboration with researchers from UC Berkeley, Carnegie Mellon, MIT, and Amazon [^1][^2].
