## About

HappyRobot is a San Francisco-based startup that builds an "agentic AI" platform for enterprise operations, initially focused on logistics and supply chain automation via voice AI agents that plug into existing enterprise systems [^1][^2]. The company works with more than 150 enterprise customers, including DHL, Kuehne + Nagel, Naturgy, Repsol, and Uber, and has grown 5x since raising its Series B in late 2025 [^1][^3]. HappyRobot is a Y Combinator alumnus that pivoted from geospatial AI to supply chain automation [^4].

The Series C values HappyRobot at $1.2 billion post-money, making it a unicorn [^1][^4]. The round is structured in two tranches: $95M (C-1) and $31M (C-2) per some reporting [^4].
