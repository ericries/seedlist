## About

Nernst Electric is an Irish startup based in Bray, south of Dublin, developing on-site oxygen generation for fish farms and heavy industry. The company generates high-purity oxygen — exceeding 99 percent purity — directly on site using ceramic membranes to separate oxygen from ambient air, giving fish farms a reliable supply at lower cost and higher energy efficiency, with applications across aquaculture, mining, metallurgy, and steelmaking [^1][^2]. Nernst first joined the Hatch Blue portfolio in July 2025, receiving pre-seed funding from Hatch Accelerator Fund II and joining Hatch Blue's accelerator programme [^1][^3].
