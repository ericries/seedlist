## About

Tiny Health uses shotgun metagenomic sequencing to analyze infant and adult gut microbiomes, offering direct-to-consumer testing and a "Powered by Tiny" B2B division [^1] [^2]. The Austin-based company has collected nearly 200,000 microbiome samples and serves over 6,000 healthcare practitioners [^1] [^2]. Series B proceeds include a $5M commitment to the Tiny Health Microbiome Research Program supporting researchers and clinical partners, plus product initiatives including TinyAI (trained on the ~200K sample corpus), Gut Microbiome Age, and Gut Resilience metrics, with research expansion into IBD, menopause, GLP-1 response, and oncology [^2].
