## About

Tiny Health uses shotgun metagenomic sequencing to analyze infant and adult gut microbiomes, offering direct-to-consumer testing and a "Powered by Tiny" B2B division [^1]. The Austin-based company has collected nearly 200,000 microbiome samples and serves over 6,000 practitioners [^1].
