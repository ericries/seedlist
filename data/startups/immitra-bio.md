## About

Immitra Bio is a Swiss biotech developing a platform for scalable, one-time curative in-vivo gene editing therapies based on a mutation-agnostic gene editing technology paired with a digital target identification pipeline [^1][^2][^3]. Unlike conventional ex-vivo approaches, the platform is designed to avoid stem cell manipulation and patient conditioning [^1][^3]. Its lead candidate, IB-003, is being developed as a potential one-time curative treatment for an inherited anemia [^1][^2]. The company is headquartered in Plan-les-Ouates in the Geneva canton and is led by CEO Jan Nelis [^1][^2].
