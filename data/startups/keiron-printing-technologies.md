## About

Keiron Printing Technologies is an Eindhoven-based deep-tech company developing Laser-Induced Forward Transfer (LiFT) precision solder paste printing for high-reliability electronics manufacturing [^1][^2]. On July 21, 2026 the company announced a €20.7M Series A to ramp production and expand commercial operations across Europe, North America, and Asia-Pacific [^1][^2][^3].

## What Founders Say

Paul Rooimans, CEO of Keiron, said: "Solder paste printing has been the industry's most expensive open secret for decades – a failure driver on a market worth well over €600 billion a year that everyone lived with because no one could fix it" [^1].
