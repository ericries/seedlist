## About

Unit1 Studio is a London-based entertainment technology company developing immersive avatar concerts that recreate live performances, enabling artists to connect with fans across multiple venues and geographies simultaneously — moving beyond the single-venue model pioneered by ABBA Voyage [^1][^2][^3]. The company was co-founded by Barney Wragg (CEO), Jonathan Butterell (Creative Director), and Pavle Mihajlovic (Technical Director) [^1][^3]. On 2026-09-21, Unit1 Studio announced a £20 million (€23.3 million / $20 million) financing package combining equity investment with production financing, led by Balderton Capital with participation from Mercuri, Gilston Music, and Paul McGuinness (former U2 manager) [^1][^2][^3].
