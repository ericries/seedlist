## About

Unit1 Studio is a London-based entertainment technology company developing immersive avatar concerts that recreate live performances, enabling artists to connect with fans across multiple venues and geographies simultaneously — moving beyond the single-venue model pioneered by ABBA Voyage [^1]. On 2026-09-21, the company announced a €23.3 million (£20 million) financing package combining equity investment with production financing, led by Balderton Capital with participation from Mercuri, Gilston Music, and Paul McGuinness (former U2 manager) [^1].
