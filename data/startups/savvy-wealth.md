## About

Savvy Wealth is a New York-based AI-native registered investment advisor (RIA) platform that gives independent financial advisors modern software — a proprietary "Savvy Intelligence" AI layer, portfolio tools, and back-office automation — to grow their practices [^1][^3]. Founded in 2021 by CEO Ritik Malhotra, the firm manages roughly $9 billion in client assets, has 150 advisors on its platform (double the prior year), and recruited $4 billion in assets in 2026 alone [^3].
