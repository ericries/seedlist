## About

INXM is a Berlin-based startup developing an AI process execution engine for enterprise and Mittelstand operations [^1][^2]. The company emerged from stealth on June 3, 2026 with a €5.7 million pre-Seed round [^1][^2]. The INXM Orchestrator turns user intent into executable Plans, coordinating work across systems, people, and processes to produce repeatable, auditable outcomes through deterministic executions [^1]. INXM was founded in 2025 by Alex Oelling (CEO), Matthias Kainer (CTO), Jesper Bylund, and Kamil Klüber [^1][^3]. Oelling previously served as Chief Digital Officer at both Isar Aerospace and Volocopter, where he built organisational and digital infrastructure for European aerospace programmes; Kainer worked alongside him at both companies on mission control systems and launch orchestration [^1][^3].
