## About

AusperBio Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing next-generation antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) therapies for chronic hepatitis B (CHB) and other liver diseases, headquartered in San Francisco with R&D operations in China [^1][^2]. Its lead candidate, AHB-137, is an unconjugated ASO designed to suppress hepatitis B surface antigen (HBsAg) production, inhibit viral DNA replication, and promote immune reactivation, with the stated goal of achieving a functional cure for CHB [^1]. On August 27, 2026, the company closed a $120 million Series C financing, bringing its total capital raised since 2024 to approximately $360 million [^1][^2][^3].
