## About

Samepage is a Singapore-based AI continuous intelligence and monitoring platform for product management teams, building AI products that help product leaders stay informed, aligned, and effective in fast-moving organizations [^1]. Its flagship product is Samepage Signals [^1]. The company was co-founded by Sahil Jain, Jason Wu, and Paul Wicker [^1].
