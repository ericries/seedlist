## About

Auger is an AI-powered autonomous operating system for global supply chains, founded in 2024 by Dave Clark, the former CEO of Flexport and a longtime senior leader at Amazon where he architected the company's logistics operations [^1][^2]. The company is headquartered in Bellevue, Washington and aims to replace the patchwork of legacy ERP, spreadsheet, and bolted-on AI tooling that midsize-to-large shippers and manufacturers use to run their supply chains [^1][^3]. Rather than layering AI on top of existing systems, Auger is building a unified, machine-readable data foundation that delivers end-to-end visibility and automates decision-making across procurement, planning, and fulfillment [^2][^3].
