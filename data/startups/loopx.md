## About

LoopX is a Toronto- and Sudbury-based Physical AI company building machine intelligence for uncontrolled mining environments where visibility, terrain, dust, and lighting change rapidly [^1][^2][^3]. The company's platform combines thermal-vision AI, edge computing, and proprietary models trained on real-world mining data, powering applications including the LoopX Collision Avoidance System (CAS) for mobile equipment, LoadSight for automated payload measurement and material tracking, and BoomGuard for active safety around boom machines [^1][^2].
