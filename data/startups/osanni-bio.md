## About

Osanni Bio is a San Francisco-based therapeutics hub-and-spoke company founded by serial biotech entrepreneur Michael Ackermann, Ph.D. — whose prior companies include Tarsus Pharmaceuticals, Oyster Point Pharma (acquired by Viatris), Oculeve (acquired by Allergan) and Presidio Medical [^1][^2][^3]. Osanni operates a discovery engine that designs novel drug concepts, advances them to a meaningful proof-of-concept, and then graduates de-risked programs into an ecosystem of affiliate companies; its current pipeline spans ophthalmology, cardiology and oncology, with a dry AMD candidate among its programs [^1][^2][^3][^4]. The company was founded roughly three years ago with a $27 million Series A, and the June 2026 Series B was raised to scale the discovery platform and advance multiple programs [^1][^3][^4].
