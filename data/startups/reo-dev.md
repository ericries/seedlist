## About

Reo.Dev is an AI-native go-to-market platform designed for software companies selling into engineering and technical decision-makers [^1]. The company's Developer Knowledge Graph covers more than 100 million engineer profiles and powers AI agents used by more than 200 companies, including NVIDIA, LangChain, ElevenLabs, Couchbase, Nebius, n8n, and Temporal [^1][^2]. Founded in 2023, Reo.Dev is dual-headquartered in San Francisco and Bengaluru [^2].
