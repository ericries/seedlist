## About

Iceotope is a Sheffield, UK-based provider of precision liquid cooling solutions for data centers and edge infrastructure, founded in 2005 [^1][^2]. The company has evolved from a research-driven green computing venture into a specialist in precision liquid cooling for AI infrastructure [^1][^2]. The $26M Series B announced May 14, 2026 was co-led by Two Seas Capital and Barclays Climate Ventures with participation from existing investors Edinv, ABC Impact, Northern Gritstone, and British Business Bank; capital will fund product engineering, patent portfolio expansion, and ecosystem partnerships [^1][^2].
