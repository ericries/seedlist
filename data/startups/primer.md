## About

Primer is a London-based payments infrastructure platform helping merchants consolidate their payments stack with no-code orchestration and AI-driven routing, founded in 2020 by former PayPal executives [^1][^2][^3]. The $100M Series C announced May 20, 2026 was led by Sofina with new investor Peak XV Partners joining existing backers Balderton Capital, Accel, ICONIQ, Tencent, and Speedinvest; the round was oversubscribed and brings total funding to $170M, supporting AI capabilities expansion and US growth [^1][^2][^3].
