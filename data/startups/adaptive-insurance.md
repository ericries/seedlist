## About

Adaptive Insurance is an Austin, Texas-based climate-resilience company that develops AI-driven specialty insurance and technology products designed to fill the coverage gaps created by climate volatility and shrinking availability of traditional insurance [^1][^2]. Co-founder and CEO Mike Gulla previously spent 20 years across Nationwide, Allstate, and Hippo (where he was Hippo's first insurance hire) before starting Adaptive in 2024 [^3]. The company launched GridProtect in 2025 — the first-of-its-kind parametric insurance product for short-duration power outages that triggers predefined payouts based on verified outages using real-time third-party data — and has since added Wind and Hail Deductible Buy-Back, residential flood insurance, and Commercial Equipment Breakdown coverage to its product suite [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-07, Adaptive announced an additional $5 million in financing, bringing total funding to $10 million; new investors include IAG Firemark Ventures, Sunna Ventures, Room & Pillar, and Connecticut Innovations, alongside existing backers Congruent Ventures, Seraphim Space, and Montauk Capital [^1][^2].
