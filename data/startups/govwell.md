## About

GovWell is an AI operating system for local government founded in 2023 that replaces legacy software for municipalities and counties, automating permits, licenses, and public service workflows—cutting processing time by up to 95% [^1][^2]. The platform serves over 130 municipalities and counties across 34 US states [^1]. The $25M Series A announced May 14, 2026 was led by Insight Partners with participation from existing investors Work-Bench and Bienville Capital, plus GovTech industry angels David Reeves (ex-OpenGov), Andreas Huber (First Due), and Chris Bullock (ClearGov) [^1][^2]. Total funding now stands at $34.5M [^1].
