## About

Runpod is a Newark, New Jersey-based AI developer cloud that gives builders a single self-serve platform to experiment with, train, fine-tune, run inference on, and scale multi-node workloads against GPU compute, billed at per-second rates [^1]. The platform reports more than one million developers building on it and supplies pre-configured templates and a model library to shorten the path from prototype to production [^1]. The company says it crossed $120 million ARR within roughly 18 months of closing its $20 million seed round in May 2024, then doubled again before closing the $100M growth investment in June 2026 [^1].

The growth round valued Runpod at $1.0 billion and Summit Partners' Michael Medici joined the board; J.P. Morgan Securities LLC served as sole placement agent [^1][^2]. Runpod reportedly turned down acquisition offers north of $500 million ahead of the round [^3]. The company's primary press release and contemporaneous trade press both call this a "growth investment," not a Series A — Runpod has not used Series letter designations publicly [^1][^2][^4].

## What Investors Say

Michael Medici, Managing Director at Summit Partners and incoming board member: "Runpod is positioned to become a defining infrastructure platform for the next generation of AI developers" [^1].

## What Founders Say

Zhen Lu, CEO and co-founder: "Builders need one place to take an idea from first experiment to production traffic, without stitching together multiple tools" [^1].
