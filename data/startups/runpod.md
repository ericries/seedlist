## About

Runpod is a Newark, New Jersey-based AI developer cloud that gives builders a single self-serve platform to experiment with, train, fine-tune, run inference on, and scale multi-node workloads against GPU compute, billed at per-second rates [^1]. The platform reports more than one million developers building on it and supplies pre-configured templates and a model library to shorten the path from prototype to production [^1]. The company says it crossed $120 million ARR within roughly 18 months of closing its $20 million seed round in May 2024, then doubled again before closing the Series A [^1].
