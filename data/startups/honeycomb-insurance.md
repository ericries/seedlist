## About

Honeycomb Insurance is an AI-native insurer specializing in apartment buildings and condo associations [^1]. The company's platform analyzes hundreds of data points per property — including geospatial datasets, aerial imagery, and building history — to assess risk and price coverage individually without requiring physical inspections [^1]. Honeycomb was co-founded by CEO Itai Ben-Zaken (a Wharton MBA and Israeli military intelligence veteran) and CTO Nimrod Sadot [^1].

By the end of 2025, the company had reached $275 million in gross written premium, coverage across 22 states, and over $100 billion in total insured value, while operating profitably and cash-flow positive [^1][^2].
