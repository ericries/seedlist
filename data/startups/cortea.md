## About

Cortea is a Berlin-based AI company building a quality layer for audit firms, founded in 2024 by Valentin Neumann and Philipp Hövelmann [^1][^2]. The company develops "Audit Quality Agents" designed to help firms improve audit quality, consistency, and professional oversight while increasing capacity across the audit process [^1][^3]. Cortea reports its agents reviewed more than 4,000 audit reports in a single season and flagged issues in every one, performing in minutes work that typically takes auditors several days at a mid-sized firm [^1][^3].

The June 2026 seed round brought Cortea's total funding to €15.1M [^1]. Angel participant Larry Bradley is the former Global Head of Audit at KPMG [^1][^3].

## What Investors Say

Dan Chaplin, partner at Dawn Capital, said Cortea will be "at the forefront of this transformation, helping their partners not only to meet demand and scale capacity, but to improve quality at every step" [^3].

Larry Bradley, former Global Head of Audit at KPMG, said: "In audit and assurance, trust is everything. If AI cannot improve quality, transparency and professional judgement, it has no place in the audit process" [^3].

## What Founders Say

Co-founder and CEO Valentin Neumann said: "The audit profession is about to undergo one of the biggest shifts in its history. AI will transform how audits are executed, but it will also raise the bar for quality, consistency, and professional oversight" [^3].

Neumann added: "Every audit firm is exploring how AI can improve the way audits are performed. The firms that succeed will be those that can combine the efficiency of AI with the rigour and trust that the profession demands" [^3].
