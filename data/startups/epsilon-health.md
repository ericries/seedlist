## About

Epsilon Health is an AI-native radiology practice that integrates artificial intelligence directly with board-certified radiologists rather than developing isolated AI models [^1]. The company processes over 2,500 imaging studies per day and, in less than 10 months, has served over 250,000 patients — handling more than half the imaging volume for one of the country's largest outpatient imaging providers [^1]. Epsilon aims to address radiologist shortages by improving clinical workflows while generating proprietary real-world datasets [^1].
