## About

BigHat Biosciences uses a machine-learning platform combined with a wet lab to design antibody therapeutics, aiming to iterate on protein candidates faster than traditional biology-first drug discovery [^1]. The company was founded in 2019 by Mark DePristo, Peyton Greenside, and Theresa Tribble, and is based in San Mateo, California [^1][^2]. Its lead program BHB810 is advancing toward the clinic alongside a broader pipeline of AI-designed antibodies [^1]. Total funding to date is $223 million [^1].
