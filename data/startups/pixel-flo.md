## About

Pixel-Flo is a University of Sheffield spin-out, incorporated in 2025 and based at the Innovation Centre, 217 Portobello in Sheffield, UK, that is commercialising a next-generation "Continuous-Flo" fluidic mass-transfer process for microLED displays [^1][^3]. The technology uses a slot-die-based approach and meniscus forces to continuously guide microLED die into place, replacing the mechanical pick-and-place methods that have historically bottlenecked microLED display manufacturing [^1][^3]. On 2026-07-09 the company announced a £5.25 million Seed round led by Northern Gritstone, with participation from SCVC, the Parkwalk Northern Universities Venture Fund, and Germany's HTGF (High-Tech Gründerfonds), to expand its team and demonstrate the process on a commercial coating system [^2][^4].
