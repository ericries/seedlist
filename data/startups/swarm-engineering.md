## About

SWARM Engineering is a decision intelligence company serving agrifood and manufacturing sectors [^1] [^2]. The platform combines domain-trained intelligent agents and optimization algorithms to help enterprise operations teams make faster, higher-quality decisions across supply chain, workforce, and logistics [^1] [^2]. SWARM's technology is built on industry-specific operational ontologies rather than generic AI systems, with founding team experience from Microsoft, Palantir, Google, and UiPath [^2] [^3].
