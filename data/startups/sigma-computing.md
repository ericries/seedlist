## About

Sigma Computing is a San Francisco-based AI apps and analytics platform built for cloud data warehouses, letting organizations create and control AI applications directly on their data [^1][^2][^3]. The company crossed $200M in ARR by April 2026 and serves 2,000+ customers including Fortune 10 enterprises and AI-driven companies [^1][^2]. The $80M Series E announced May 18, 2026 at a $3B valuation (double the prior round) was led by Princeville Capital with new investors Databricks Ventures, ServiceNow Ventures, and Workday Ventures joining existing backers Altimeter Capital, Avenir Growth Capital, D1 Capital Partners, K5 Global, NewView Capital, Spark Capital, Sutter Hill Ventures, and XN [^1][^2][^3].
