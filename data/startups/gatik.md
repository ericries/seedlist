## About

Gatik is a Mountain View, California-based autonomous trucking company focused on the middle-mile — short-haul B2B freight between distribution centers and retail outlets — for Fortune 50 retail, grocery, and CPG customers [^1][^2][^3]. The company operates driverless 26- and 30-foot trucks in Dallas-Fort Worth, Phoenix, northwest Arkansas, and Toronto, with more than 85,000 fully driverless orders completed and 99% on-time delivery [^1][^3]. Gatik has more than $600M in contracted revenue and plans to expand its driverless fleet past 100 trucks by end of 2026 [^1][^3].
