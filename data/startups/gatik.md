## About

Gatik is a Mountain View, California-based autonomous trucking company focused on the middle-mile — short-haul B2B freight between distribution centers and retail outlets — for Fortune 50 retail, grocery, and CPG customers [^1][^2][^4]. The company was co-founded in 2017 by CEO Gautam Narang, CTO Arjun Narang, and Chief Engineer Apeksha Kumavat [^1][^4]. Gatik operates driverless 26- and 30-foot trucks across Texas, Arizona, Arkansas, and Ontario, Canada, with more than 85,000 fully driverless orders completed and 99% on-time delivery [^1][^4][^5]. The company has more than $600M in contracted revenue and plans to expand its driverless fleet past 100 trucks by the end of 2026 [^1][^4][^5].

A multiyear commercial agreement with PepsiCo — signed roughly two months before the Series D closed — put 41 driverless Gatik box trucks into service delivering Frito-Lay products (including Cheetos and Doritos) across Dallas-Fort Worth, Phoenix, and northwest Arkansas [^2]. Gatik has raised approximately $500M in total since exiting stealth in 2019 [^2].
