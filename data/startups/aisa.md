## About

AIsa is a San Francisco-based startup building a unified transaction layer that enables AI agents to autonomously discover, access, and pay for digital resources such as data and APIs [^1]. The company, founded in 2025, describes its platform as programmable infrastructure that provides usage-based billing with settlement options in fiat or stablecoins [^1]. Co-founder Jordan Liu has described the vision as "We are building the Amazon for agents" [^1]. By mid-2026, AIsa had onboarded more than 20,000 registered agents without paid marketing [^1][^2].
