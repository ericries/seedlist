## About

8Layers is a Madrid-based cybersecurity company focused on protecting digital identities, which the company describes as a leading vector in modern cyberattacks [^1][^2]. Its platform, commercially available since May 2026, integrates Identity Security Posture Management (ISPM), Identity Threat Detection and Response (ITDR), and automated compliance for European regulations (ENS, NIS2, ISO 27001) into a single product [^1][^3]. The company was founded in 2024 by Daniel García Morán, Pedro Palao, Pablo Carretero, and Iago Salgado, a team with more than 20 years of combined experience in scalable systems and cybersecurity [^1].
