## About

Function Health is an Austin-based preventive-health platform that combines lab testing, imaging, and personalized AI-driven intelligence to help members monitor and manage lifelong health [^1][^2]. The company launched in beta in 2023 and reports more than 500,000 members and 100 million completed lab tests to date [^1][^2]. Recent expansion includes acquisitions of Getlabs (nationwide at-home blood draw network), Ezra (full-body MRI), and SuppCo (supplement platform) [^2][^3]. On 2026-07-30, Function announced $450M in growth financing from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund, structured as non-dilutive capital tied to customer acquisition [^1][^2][^3].
