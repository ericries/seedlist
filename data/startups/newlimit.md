## About

NewLimit is a South San Francisco-based longevity biotech developing epigenetic reprogramming medicines designed to restore youthful function in old cells and treat age-related diseases, founded in 2021 by Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong, former GV partner and bioengineer Blake Byers, and stem-cell biologist Jacob Kimmel, who serves as CEO and President [^1][^2]. The company uses an AI-driven genomics platform to discover combinations of transcription factors that reverse cellular aging, and plans to bring its first aging-reprogramming medicine — a therapy targeting old human liver cells — into human clinical trials in 2027 [^1][^3]. On June 2, 2026, NewLimit announced a $435 million Series C round led by Founders Fund that valued the company at approximately $3.1 billion post-money, roughly tripling its valuation from the prior year [^1][^2][^3].
