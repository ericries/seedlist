## About

PixVerse is a Singapore-headquartered AI video-generation company founded in 2023 by Wang Changhu and Jaden Xie [^1][^2]. Its consumer product lets users create short-form AI video from text and image prompts, and the company reports over 150 million registered users and more than 15 million monthly active users worldwide [^1][^2]. PixVerse operates offices in Singapore, Beijing, and Shanghai with roughly 150 employees [^1]. In January 2026 the company launched R1, which it describes as the world's first real-time interactive world model — moving video generation from a fixed output into a continuous stream that responds to user input [^2][^3].

On 2026-07-13 PixVerse announced the close of a Series C extension that brings the total Series C to $439 million and pushes its valuation past $2 billion [^1][^2][^3]. The initial Series C, closed in March 2026 at roughly $300 million, was led by CDH Investments [^1][^2]. New participants in the extension include Alibaba, Lollapalooza Capital, Ivy Capital, Grand Mount Capital, Eastern Bell Capital, Mirae Asset, BlueFocus, and CloudAlpha, joining returning backers iGlobe Partners and OCBC's Lion X Ventures [^1][^2][^3]. The company says the capital will be used to expand its world model and interactive entertainment offerings and to grow internationally [^2][^3].
