## About

Kopa.ai is a Vilnius-based agentic-AI operating system for e-commerce teams, founded in 2023 by Donatas Benaitis and Vytautas Krutulis, that deploys autonomous AI agents to run advertising, creative production, budgeting, inventory coordination, pricing, analytics, and storefront operations for Shopify brands and other e-commerce operators [^1][^2][^3]. Kopa.ai launched its public product in December 2025 and reached €1M ARR by May 2026 with 2,000+ customers across Europe [^1][^2]. The €2M seed announced May 28, 2026 was co-led by XTX Ventures (the venture arm of XTX Markets) and Vilnius-based Practica Capital, with participation from Inovia Capital and London-based angel Etan Ilfeld (Remagine Ventures partner); capital will fund AI infrastructure expansion, engineering hires, and European go-to-market acceleration [^1][^2][^3].
