## About

QubeHealth is a Mumbai, India-based healthcare-focused fintech that operates QubeHealth-Pay, a payments platform enabling users to pay for medical expenses across hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, diagnostic centres, and doctors without network restrictions [^1][^2]. The platform layers cashback rewards and healthcare financing options on top of insurance gaps [^1]. According to the company, QubeHealth-Pay processed approximately ₹100 crore in healthcare payments during FY26 with over 700,000 individual users, and is targeting ₹240 crore in FY27 with a longer-term goal of $1 billion in annual payment volume by FY30 [^1][^2]. The company's client roster includes Walmart India, Flipkart, Tata Teleservices, Equitas Small Finance Bank, and Omnicom Group [^1].
