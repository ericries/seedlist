## About

Fixxly is a Bengaluru-based AI-powered quick commerce platform for construction and home improvement materials, offering products including plywood, paint, electrical equipment, lighting, plumbing materials, and hardware to contractors, electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, interior designers, and homeowners, with some orders deliverable in as little as 30 minutes [^1][^2]. Founded in 2026 by Shezan Bhojani and Sachith Varma, the company announced a $5.5 million seed round on 2026-07-29 led by Accel, with participation from Fireside Ventures and Lightspeed India Partners [^1][^2][^3]. Fixxly plans to begin commercial operations in Bengaluru on 2026-09-01, using the fresh capital to strengthen its technology platform, expand operations, and prepare for launch [^1][^2].
