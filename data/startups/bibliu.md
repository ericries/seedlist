## About

BibliU is a Greenville, Texas-based integrated academic and campus store solutions provider serving colleges and universities [^1][^2]. The company combines managed retail operations with a proprietary digital learning platform, providing course materials delivery and bookstore operations across more than 170 institutions in over 30 U.S. states [^1][^2]. Its acquisition of Texas Book Company enabled the shift from a digital-only platform to a hybrid model [^2]. On June 2, 2026, BibliU announced over $55 million in new funding led by BlackRock with participation from existing investor Stonehage Fleming, as the company surpassed $100 million in annual recurring revenue and reported over 100% compound annual growth over the previous two years [^1][^2][^3].
