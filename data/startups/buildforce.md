## About

Buildforce is a tech-enabled staffing platform for electricians and electrical contractors, connecting workers with commercial and industrial construction projects through a mobile app for individual electricians and a web application for contractors [^1][^2]. Founded in 2019, the company describes itself as the largest tech-enabled electrician staffing business in the US [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-28, Buildforce announced a $10 million Series A led by Saepio Capital with participation from new investor Blue Heron Capital and existing investors Revolution's Rise of the Rest Seed Fund, S3 Ventures, and Chicago Ventures; proceeds will fuel national expansion and technology development [^1][^2].
