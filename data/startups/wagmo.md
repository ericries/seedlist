## About

Wagmo is a modern pet healthcare platform providing pet insurance, wellness reimbursements, and 24/7 virtual veterinary care, delivered primarily through employer benefits programs and reaching more than 5 million employees across 19,000+ employer groups as of the July 2026 announcement [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-14, Wagmo and Curql — a collective of more than 160 credit unions that jointly invest in fintech — announced a strategic investment by Curql in Wagmo intended to open a new distribution channel for pet insurance, wellness reimbursements, and virtual vet care to credit unions and their members nationwide [^1][^2][^3]. Financial terms of the investment were not disclosed [^1][^2].
