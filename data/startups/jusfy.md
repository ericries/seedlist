## About

Jusfy is a São Paulo-based legal technology company building an AI-native legal operating system for Brazilian law firms and legal departments [^1]. The platform combines client intake, legal research, case management and practice management with embedded legal fintech services [^1]. Founded in 2021 by former attorney Rafael Saccol Bagolin (previously at Bagolin e Bastianello) and CTO Juliano Lima, Jusfy has assembled a proprietary corpus of more than 350 million Brazilian legal documents and developed a suite of AI agents branded as JusGPT [^2][^3].
