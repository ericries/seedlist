## About

Mirage (formerly Captions) is a New York-based AI lab and consumer video platform led by co-founder and CEO Gaurav Misra; the company rebranded from Captions to Mirage in September 2025 to reflect an expanded focus on foundational AI models for short-form video while continuing to power its Captions app [^1][^2][^3]. The Captions platform serves over 20 million global users — including small businesses, creators, and enterprise customers such as HubSpot, CoreWeave, and King — and users have created more than 250 million videos to date [^2]. The $75M growth financing announced March 24, 2026 came from General Catalyst's Customer Value Fund (CVF), bringing total funding to more than $175M; capital will fund agentic video-editing development, mobile/web platform integration, and international expansion focused on Asia [^1][^2][^3].
