## About

Gravity Gardens is a Nijmegen-based agtech company founded in 2024 that develops a dry, chemical-free seed activation process aimed at improving crop establishment without water, chemical coatings, or microplastics [^1][^2]. The company announced a €2.6 million seed round on September 9, 2026, taking total funding above €3 million and moving the company toward industrial-scale equipment development as EU regulations phase out microplastic seed coatings [^1][^2][^3].

## What Investors Say

Erica van Eeghen, VP Capital: "The most impactful innovations often improve a system's foundations. Gravity Gardens' technology could make stronger crop establishment accessible to many farmers while using fewer natural resources." [^1]

Marcel Zijp, Brightlands Venture Partners: "Gravity Gardens' platform stimulates the plant's natural power, and its cost competitive technology can be applied in a wide variety of crops." [^1]
