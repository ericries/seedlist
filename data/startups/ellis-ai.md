## About

Ellis is a New York-based AI-native operations platform for private credit firms, launched from stealth on July 30, 2026 with more than $10 million in seed funding [^1][^2][^3]. The company automates operational workflows across fund administration, accounting, and legal systems — reconciling positions and cash flows, identifying anomalies, and tracing exceptions — connecting to the systems and documents a firm already uses rather than requiring rip-and-replace [^1][^2]. Ellis was founded by Ryan Williams, who previously co-founded institutional alternatives investment platform Cadre, which facilitated approximately $6bn of transaction value [^1][^3].

## What Founders Say

Ryan Williams described the market opportunity in a TechCrunch interview: "Even as the front end of private markets became more modern and accessible, the operating infrastructure underneath it remained fragmented." [^1]

On product philosophy, Williams said: "Ellis connects to the systems and documents a firm already uses rather than forcing it to rip everything out and start over." [^1]

On AI's role: "I expect the human loop to become narrower, but not disappear. Our goal is not to replace human judgment; it's to help people cut through the noise and make educated decisions faster." [^1]
