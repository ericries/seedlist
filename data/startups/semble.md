## About

Semble is a London-based healthcare management SaaS platform serving outpatient providers across the UK and France. The platform consolidates fragmented clinical software into a single system covering scheduling, billing, clinical records, reporting, prescriptions, and patient communications, with integrations to over 1,200 external tools [^1][^2]. The company is used by more than 1,700 healthcare businesses across 80 specialties, with 16,000 daily professional users and 10 million patients on the platform [^1][^2]. Semble was named by TIME as one of the World's Top HealthTech Companies for 2025 [^2].

Semble was co-founded by Christoph Lippuner (CEO) and Mikael Landau [^1][^2]. The company previously operated under the name Heydoc before rebranding to Semble [^3].
