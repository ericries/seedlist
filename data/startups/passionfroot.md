## About

Passionfroot is an AI-powered marketplace connecting B2B creators — newsletter writers, podcasters, and technical content creators — with brands running creator marketing campaigns [^1][^2]. Founded in Berlin in 2022 by Jen Phan and Lorenzo De Nobili, the platform now serves more than 150 B2B brands including ElevenLabs, Figma, Replit, Framer, and Gamma across AI, developer tools, enterprise productivity, marketing, financial services, and design technology [^1][^2]. The company reported 13x revenue growth over the prior year while remaining profitable with a team of 15 employees, and has paid at least $10 million to creators over the past 18 months [^1][^2]. Passionfroot's AI agent, Zest, automates campaign creation and creator discovery [^1].
