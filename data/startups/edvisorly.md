## About

EdVisorly is a Los Angeles-based education technology company that helps community college students transfer into four-year universities by automating unofficial transcript evaluations, matching completed classes against target-university degree requirements, and surfacing time-to-degree and cost projections for students and families [^1][^2]. The platform serves more than 100 colleges, universities, and higher-education systems as institutional customers, including Carnegie Mellon University, the University of Connecticut, the University of Massachusetts, and California State Polytechnic University, Pomona [^1][^2]. EdVisorly was founded by Manny Smith, a former Air Force Academy graduate whose personal experience with community-college transfer inspired the company [^1].
