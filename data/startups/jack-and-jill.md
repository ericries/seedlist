## About

Jack & Jill is a London-based AI recruitment platform that deploys two conversational agents — "Jack" for job seekers and "Jill" for employers — arranging introductions only when both agents confirm mutual fit [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-15, the company (registered as Tinker Tailor Talent Ltd.) announced a €34.68M ($40M) Series A led by Air Street Capital with participation from Madrona, Antler, Creandum, Ada Ventures, Entrepreneurs First, Expedite Capital, Repeat.vc, Dig Ventures, Firedrop, Episode 1, and Playfair [^1][^2]. As of the raise, the platform reports more than 385,000 candidate users and 5,350+ companies using Jill, with roughly 5,000 interviews arranged per month [^2][^3].
