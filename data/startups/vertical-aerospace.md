## About

Vertical Aerospace is a Bristol-based electric aerospace company developing the Valo (formerly VX4), a piloted four-passenger eVTOL aircraft designed for zero-operating-emission urban and regional air mobility [^1][^2]. Publicly traded on the NYSE under the ticker EVTL, the company is one of a small group of Western eVTOL developers pursuing type certification with the UK Civil Aviation Authority and the FAA [^1][^2][^3].

On August 11, 2026, Vertical Aerospace announced a €86.6 million (approximately $100 million) financing package composed of €56.2 million (approximately $65 million) in convertible note draws with Mudrick Capital Management plus a €30.3 million (approximately $35 million) equity investment from a group of existing and new investors [^1][^2][^3]. The Mudrick component includes a recently closed €4.3 million (approximately $5 million) draw and a planned €30.3 million (approximately $35 million) accelerated draw under the company's existing €43.3 million (approximately $50 million) convertible note facility [^1][^3].
