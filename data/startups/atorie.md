## About

Atorie is a San Francisco–based AI-powered fashion brand that connects luxury manufacturers directly with consumers, offering items made from the same materials and factories as high-end labels at significantly lower prices [^1]. The company was founded by Redouane Ramdani and Luis Angulo [^1].
