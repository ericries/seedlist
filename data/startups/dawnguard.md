## About

Dawnguard is an Amsterdam-based cybersecurity startup that is building an AI-native platform for security architecture automation, helping organisations design, build, and continuously validate secure cloud-native systems from the earliest stages of development [^1][^2][^3]. The platform turns secure architecture into deployable infrastructure — validating designs before deployment, generating production-ready Infrastructure as Code, and continuously checking that live cloud environments remain aligned with the approved security design [^1][^2]. Co-founded in 2025 by CEO Mahdi Abdulrazak and CTO Kim van Lavieren, whose backgrounds span IBM, Microsoft, Amazon, and military cyber operations, the company took its platform to general availability alongside the July 2026 funding announcement and opened a New York City office to support US expansion [^1][^2][^3].
