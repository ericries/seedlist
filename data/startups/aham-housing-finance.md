## About

Aham Housing Finance is a Chennai-based technology-led affordable housing finance company serving self-employed and first-time homebuyers across India [^1][^2][^3]. The company focuses on data-driven underwriting, localized distribution, and risk management to reach borrowers who often face challenges securing loans from large banks [^2][^3]. On 2026-07-30, Aham announced a ₹100 crore follow-on funding round led by The Sanmar Group, which acquired a 43% controlling stake, with participation from existing investor Negen Capital [^1][^2][^3]. The capital will fund lending capacity expansion, entry into new geographies, and further investment in technology and underwriting infrastructure [^1][^2].
