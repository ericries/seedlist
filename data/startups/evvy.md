## About

Evvy is an AI-driven precision medicine platform for women's health, built around at-home CLIA and CLEP-certified vaginal microbiome sequencing tests analyzed via mail-in swabs, with clinical interpretation delivered through its clinician network and an AI product ("EvvyAI") for personalized guidance [^1][^2]. The company reports serving more than 100,000 patients, working with 3,000 practitioners, and having published 15 peer-reviewed papers; it is expanding into fertility research and applying its microbiome dataset to close the gender health gap in clinical research [^1][^2].
