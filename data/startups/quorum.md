## About

Quorum is an AI-powered government affairs platform used by more than 2,000 organizations, including over half of the Fortune 100, integrating legislative tracking, stakeholder engagement, grassroots advocacy, PAC management, and AI-powered intelligence for government affairs teams [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-20 Enlightenment Capital — a Washington, DC-area investment firm focused on aerospace, defense, government, and technology — announced a strategic investment in Quorum to accelerate the company's product roadmap and expand its agentic AI capabilities [^1][^2][^3]. Financial terms of the transaction were not disclosed [^1][^3]. Quorum was co-founded in 2014 and is led by CEO Alex Wirth [^1][^2].
