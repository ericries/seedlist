## About

Katalyze AI is an agentic-AI platform for pharmaceutical companies, letting scientists, engineers, and analysts in biopharma organizations build teams of AI agents that take on engineering, scientific, and manufacturing work [^1]. The company is dual-headquartered in San Francisco and Toronto and reports that its platform is already used by five of the world's 20 largest pharmaceutical companies, including Sanofi [^1][^2]. Katalyze was co-founded by Reza Farahani (CEO), Shreyas Becker (COO), Hannes Bretschneider (Chief AI Officer), and Matt Cruz (Founding Engineer), with backgrounds spanning AI, enterprise software, and life sciences [^1][^2].

## What Founders Say

CEO Reza Farahani framed the company's ambition around pharmaceutical timelines: he cited the "eight to twelve years and more than two billion dollars" typical drug-development cost and said Katalyze aims to "cut that in half by 2030" through agentic tooling for operational and manufacturing processes [^2].
