## About

Exponent is a New York-based financial operating system for multi-location franchise operators, unifying capital, data, and software in one platform [^1][^2]. The $40M combined funding announced May 14, 2026 comprises a $7.5M Series A equity round led by Chailease (with Era, Inauguration Capital, K8 Capital, and NBA player Kyle Kuzma co-investing) plus more than $30M in committed credit facilities including a previously announced $20M revolving credit facility from Jovian Capital Management [^1][^2][^3].
