## About

Neko Health is a Swedish preventive-health company that operates a proprietary full-body scanning platform combining medical imaging, sensor data, and blood analysis with in-clinic physician review [^1][^2]. It was founded in Stockholm in February 2023 by Spotify co-founder Daniel Ek and Hjalmar Nilsonne, who serves as CEO [^1][^2][^3]. The company runs clinics in Sweden and the UK — including multiple London locations, Manchester, and Birmingham — and reports that more than 100,000 members have completed a scan and more than 350,000 have registered on the waitlist [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-15 Neko Health announced a $700 million Series C at a valuation of approximately $7 billion — a roughly fourfold jump from its January 2025 Series B [^1][^2][^3]. The round was led by Lightspeed Venture Partners and co-led by O.G. Venture Partners, with participation from existing investors Atomico, General Catalyst, and Lakestar and new investors Liberty City Ventures, Positive Sum, and BDT & MSD [^1][^2][^3]. Individual investors in the round include Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan, Tim Ferriss, Maria Sharapova, and will.i.am [^1][^2]. The company plans to use the capital to open its first US clinics — starting in New York — and to expand research and technology investment in preventive care [^1][^2][^3].
