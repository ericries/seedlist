## About

F2 is a New York-based AI platform for private credit and private markets investors, founded in 2025 by Don Muir and Emre Kazdagli [^1][^2]. The platform provides end-to-end agentic workflow automation for lenders — covering deal screening, underwriting, and portfolio monitoring — and is customized for each enterprise customer [^1]. As of the funding announcement, F2 reports it is deployed across more than 100 private credit funds and banks, with customers including Bain Capital and Live Oak Bank, and has announced a partnership with Google Gemini Enterprise [^2].

On June 10, 2026, F2 announced a $14 million seed round led by HighlandX, with participation from existing investors Left Lane Capital, NFX, Y Combinator, and Torch Capital [^1][^2][^3]. The round brought F2's total equity raised to $24 million [^2]. NFX, an existing investor, has separately published its rationale for backing the company, citing customer time savings of up to 45 hours per week from automating analysis of Excel, PDF, and PowerPoint files in private-market data rooms [^3].
