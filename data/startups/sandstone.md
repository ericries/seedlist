## About

Sandstone is a Legal Relationship Management platform designed for in-house legal teams at small and mid-sized businesses. The platform helps legal departments manage intake from multiple channels (Slack, email, Jira), route and triage work using AI agents, and build custom workflows for drafting, reviewing, and legal analysis [^1] [^2]. CEO Nick Fleisher previously worked as a corporate legal consultant at McKinsey & Co, and co-founder Jarryd Strydom is a former in-house attorney [^3]. The company reports having onboarded customers including Wayfair, Grindr, Mercury, Cox Media, and ElevenLabs, with revenue growing more than 40x in the 90 days prior to the Series A [^1].
