## About

Companion.energy is a Ghent, Belgium-based energy software platform helping large industrial and commercial enterprises optimize energy procurement and manage distributed assets in real time [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by Thomas Vyncke and Jonas Verstraeten, the company's AI-driven platform manages more than 2 TWh of annual energy consumption and production, representing roughly €500M in combined energy spend and revenues [^2]. The company reports tenfold revenue growth in the year to mid-2026, surpassing €1M in annual recurring revenue [^2].

The platform is positioned for a structural shift in European energy markets, where electricity prices have become significantly more volatile and operationally complex. Companion.energy plans to use the new funding to deepen the platform's multi-asset, multi-market optimisation capabilities and expand commercially across Europe, beginning with Germany and Spain [^1][^2].
