## About

Blink is a Boston-based AI-first employee experience platform for frontline workforces, founded and led by CEO Sean Nolan, providing communications, workforce management, and engagement tools designed to keep distributed and shift-based workers informed and connected [^1][^2][^3]. The company added over 700,000 hospitality users in 2025 [^1]. The $17M growth round announced May 18, 2026 was funded by Enlightened Hospitality Investments (EHI) — the VC arm of restaurateur Danny Meyer — and accompanied a new global partnership with Shake Shack, where Blink will support both Support Center and Shack-location teams; proceeds will fund product, engineering, and go-to-market expansion across the AI-first platform [^1][^2][^3].
