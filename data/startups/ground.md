## About

Ground is a San Francisco-based infrastructure company building an API that lets fintechs, neobanks, wealth managers, exchanges, and asset managers integrate onchain yield products without building their own blockchain infrastructure [^1][^2][^3]. The platform supports lending protocols, structured products, and liquid staking tokens across Ethereum, Solana, and Layer 2 networks, including integrations with Aave, Morpho, Maple, and Kamino [^2]. Ground was co-founded by Reid Cuming — a co-founder of tokenized asset manager Superstate — and Sam Yoon, who previously held engineering leadership roles at Braid and HiFi [^1][^2][^3]. The company emerged from stealth on June 24, 2026 [^1][^2].
