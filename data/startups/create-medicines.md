## About

CREATE Medicines is a Cambridge, MA-based clinical-stage biotechnology company pioneering in vivo immune programming, with a proprietary mRNA-LNP platform that directly engineers T cells, NK cells, and myeloid cells inside the body to enable scalable, repeat-dose, off-the-shelf immunotherapies [^1][^2]. The company has dosed more than 50 patients across its in vivo CAR clinical programs, which it describes as the largest dataset in the emerging field [^1][^2]. The $122M Series B announced May 14, 2026 was co-led by existing investors Newpath Partners, ARCH Venture Partners, and Hatteras Venture Partners, with participation from Alexandria Venture Investments and other current syndicate members; proceeds will advance CRT-402 (CD19-targeted in vivo CAR-T for autoimmune disease) into clinical trials and expand the dual CD19 x BCMA program [^1][^2]. Ron Philip joined as Executive Chairman; Brian Cuneo (ARCH) and Tom Thomas (Newpath) joined the board [^1].
