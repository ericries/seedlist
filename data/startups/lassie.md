## About

Lassie is a San Francisco-based AI company building autonomous systems that run small businesses end-to-end, starting with dental practices [^1][^2]. The platform enters insurance portals, pulls reimbursements, reconciles them, updates systems of record, and verifies funds in the bank [^1]. Lassie currently operates in over 700 practices across 49 states and provides business owners with more than 250,000 hours of labor annually [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by Steijn Pelle (CEO, previously an early product manager at Robinhood and Coinbase) and Frédéric Renken (the first product hire at Superhuman) [^2][^3].
