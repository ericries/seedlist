## About

Terminal is a Toronto-based data infrastructure company providing a single integration layer that aggregates and normalizes real-time and historical data from more than 325 telematics service providers, covering GPS location, safety events, vehicle diagnostics, fuel usage, and dash camera media [^1]. Founded in 2023, the company sells to Fortune 500 customers across insurance, fleet management, and logistics, enabling them to access unified telematics data without building and maintaining hundreds of one-off integrations [^2].
