## About

BetHog is a UK-based cryptocurrency online casino and sportsbook platform founded in 2024 by FanDuel co-founders Nigel Eccles (CEO) and Rob Jones, along with Ryan Anderson [^1][^2]. The company offers proprietary games and sports betting with an emphasis on "provably fair" gameplay, and in April 2026 launched Sentient Studios, a B2B platform that lets casino operators deploy BetHog's AI-powered live dealers (branded "Sunny") across their own live online-casino offerings [^3][^4][^6]. The $10M (~€8.5M) Series A announced April 22, 2026 was co-led by Will Ventures and RockawayX, with participation from PCV, 6MV (6th Man Ventures), Bullpen Capital, and Advancit Capital; the round brings total funding to $16M and will fund AI-dealer expansion on BetHog's own platform plus global Sentient Studios adoption [^4][^5][^6].
