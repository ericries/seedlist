## About

Naïve is a Palo Alto–based AI infrastructure company building the unified API that lets AI agents provision and run entire businesses — including payments, email, phone numbers, cloud infrastructure, storage, and company incorporation — from a single interface [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded by Sean Dorje and Dennis Zax, 20-year-old Berkeley dropouts who had previously built and sold ezML together as teenagers before going through Y Combinator [^2]. As of the Series A, cumulative funding stands at approximately $32M [^1].
