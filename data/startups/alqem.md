## About

alqem is a Munich-based deep-tech company using AI-driven materials discovery to hunt for rare-earth-free permanent magnets and other next-generation materials. The company was founded in 2026 by Dr. Hanh Nguyen (CEO, formerly of McKinsey, Unilever, and OCI Global), Dr. Tiago Cerqueira (CTO, co-developer of the Alexandria materials database), and Prof. Milan Allan (CSO, Chair of Experimental Physics, Quantum Metrology and Sensing at LMU Munich). alqem's platform screens hundreds of millions of candidate compounds computationally and synthesises the most promising, targeting permanent magnets — a market currently dominated ~94% by China — as its first application [^1][^2][^3].
