## About

Cashea is a Caracas-based buy-now-pay-later platform that enables Venezuelan consumers to pay for purchases in interest-free installments without a credit card [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by Pedro Vallenilla, the company has partnered with more than 3,500 merchants across more than 25 Venezuelan cities and now processes more than one BNPL transaction per second [^1]. Approximately 35% of Venezuelan adults are active on the platform, and transaction volume represents more than 3% of the country's GDP [^1].
