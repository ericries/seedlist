## About

Trace Finance is a financial infrastructure provider for cross-border payments, banking connectivity, and FX that bridges stablecoin rails and traditional banking across the U.S., Brazil, Latin America, and other emerging markets [^1][^2]. The company has processed more than $10 billion in cross-border volume and serves major payments companies, fintechs, exchanges, and platforms operating in regulated markets [^1][^3].
