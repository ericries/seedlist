## About

Veriqus Group is a Mumbai-based wealthtech firm that provides technology and wealth management solutions for financial advisors and high-net-worth clients across India [^1] [^2]. The company was co-founded by Ashish Gumashta, formerly chairman and CEO of Julius Baer India, and Roshi Jain, formerly a senior fund manager at HDFC Asset Management [^2] [^3]. Veriqus currently partners with more than 500 wealth creators who collectively manage assets exceeding ₹1.2 lakh crore [^1] [^2]. Its integrated platform combines wealth management, asset management, business advisory, and lending services [^3].
