## About

8090 Labs is an AI-native software development company building an enterprise AI coding platform called Software Factory, which helps corporate engineering teams move from prototypes to production-ready applications with enterprise controls, including audit trails [^1][^2]. Chamath Palihapitiya originally announced 8090 in January 2024 as a self-funded incubator aimed at rebuilding enterprise software with AI tooling, promising 80% of original features at 90% lower cost [^3]. With the $135 million Series A in June 2026, Palihapitiya transitioned from board member to CEO, marking his return to a full-time operating role for the first time since leaving Facebook in 2011 [^1][^2]. 8090 targets customers in heavily regulated industries including healthcare, insurance, life sciences, aerospace, energy, manufacturing, financial services, and the United States government [^1].
