## About

Jaipur Robotics is a Swiss deep-tech company building an AI operating system for waste-to-energy and industrial plants, combining computer vision and robotic manipulation to identify and sort mixed waste streams at high throughput [^1][^2]. The company is headquartered in Manno, in the Italian-speaking Swiss canton of Ticino, and deploys its systems in waste-to-energy and materials-recovery facilities across Europe [^1].

On September 7, 2026, techstartups.com and eu-startups.com reported that Jaipur Robotics had raised €4.3 million in seed funding co-led by EquityPitcher Ventures and High-Tech Gründerfonds (HTGF), bringing total disclosed funding to more than €5 million [^1][^2].
