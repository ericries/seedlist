## About

Glacis Labs is a crypto infrastructure company building ZeroDelta, a multichain clearinghouse for digital assets that matches, nets, and settles token flows with non-custodial, atomic delivery and a cryptographic receipt on every transfer [^1] [^2]. The company says ZeroDelta has settled over $1 billion in digital-asset volume and operates at a $1.5 billion annualized run rate across more than 40 chains [^1] [^2]. Glacis plans to extend ZeroDelta beyond stablecoins into tokenized securities and foreign exchange [^1] [^3].
