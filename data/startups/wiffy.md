## About

Wiffy is a Mumbai-based field services platform founded in 2019 by Vikram Sharma and Deepanshu Goyal. The company provides post-sales installation, maintenance and warranty services for home improvement and consumer durables brands, operating across 100+ cities in India through a network of 5,000+ certified technicians [^1] [^2]. Wiffy partners with major brands including IKEA, Panasonic, Godrej, Hettich and Amazon, and has served more than one million homes since inception [^1].

In July 2024, Wiffy raised Rs 25 crore in a funding round led by Mount Judi Ventures and Capria Ventures, with Blume Founders Fund and other existing investors also participating [^1].
