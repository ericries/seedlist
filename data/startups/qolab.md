## About

Qolab is a Santa Barbara-based superconducting quantum computing company co-founded by Dr. John Martinis, 2025 Nobel Prize in Physics laureate and former lead of Google's Quantum AI hardware team, working to build fault-tolerant quantum computers manufactured at semiconductor scale [^1][^2]. The company collaborates with Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory through the Quantum Systems Accelerator and with UC Santa Barbara researchers on next-generation quantum algorithms [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-02 Qolab announced $54.2M in Series B Preferred Stock financing and commitments, led by UC Investments, with participation from existing investors WARF (Wisconsin Alumni Research Foundation), Octave Ventures, and Phoenix Venture Partners; the round comprises $32.6M in new Series B, $12.6M in converted securities, and $10M in committed convertibles, and will fund semiconductor collaborations, technology development, and manufacturing scale-up [^1][^2][^3].
