## About

Perceptic is building an end-to-end AI operating system for drug development, spanning drug discovery to clinical trial design [^1][^2]. The company was founded by a trio of former Palantir executives who helped spearhead that company's Life Sciences practice; CEO Tilman Flock is a bioscience researcher who spent nearly seven years at Palantir building its commercial AI platform and helping life sciences companies deploy it [^1].

Rather than focusing on a single step in drug development as many AI biotech startups have, Perceptic pitches itself as the "connective tissue" between discrete AI tools and the proprietary internal and external data that pharmaceutical companies use to make decisions [^1]. Its software is already used by multiple top-tier pharmaceutical companies; the company was authorized to disclose Australian biotechnology firm CSL as a customer [^1].
