## About

INLEAP Photonics is a German deep-tech defense company that builds laser-based counter-drone systems, including the FASTLIGHT SHIELD (an eye-safe stationary drone defence system) and FASTLIGHT HELD (a mobile laser-based counter-UAS platform) [^1][^2][^3]. The company collaborates with the German Federal Ministry of Defense and the Bundeswehr's Cyber Innovation Hub, and maintains a strategic partnership with STARK Defence [^1][^2].

On September 1, 2026, INLEAP Photonics announced a €20 million seed round led by UVC Partners, with participation from HTGF (Germany), Balnord (Poland), Sentris (Estonia), and Scale Capital (Denmark) [^1][^2][^3]. The capital will be used to expand manufacturing and testing capacity, accelerate the transition to series production, and grow European sales and service operations [^1][^3].
