## About

Brev.dev was a San Francisco developer-experience startup that unified GPU and cloud-compute access for AI developers across AWS, Google Cloud, and other providers, letting teams spin up ready-to-use environments without managing infrastructure directly [^1][^2]. Co-founders Nader Khalil (CEO) and Alec Fong pivoted into Brev from their earlier YC-backed rideshare-advertising startup Paneau (YC W20) during the COVID-19 downturn, driven by their own developer-environment pain points [^3][^4].

NVIDIA acquired Brev.dev in July 2024; Khalil announced the deal publicly on July 17, 2024 [^5]. The team joined NVIDIA to build "NVIDIA Brev," now integrated into NVIDIA's developer platform [^6].

## What Investors Say

Mohammed Alzubi of Nama Ventures, describing the founding team's approach in the firm's Brev.dev retrospective: "if we are facing this problem and we are good coders, imagine how many engineering teams are facing this exact problem" [^3].

## What Founders Say

Nader Khalil, on Brev's design philosophy, from a June 2021 Forbes profile: "We built Brev such that you can stay abstracted from the infrastructure and build, but freely peel back the abstraction if you want" [^1].

Nader Khalil, announcing the NVIDIA acquisition on LinkedIn, July 17, 2024: "We started Brev in my parents garage with the goal of building the best damn developer experience possible. We've been working closely with NVIDIA since August, meeting brilliant folks like Michael Balint, Manuvir Das, Manish Harsh, and Julio C. Tapia behind their software suite and we couldn't be more excited to team up" [^5].
