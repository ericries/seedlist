## About

RapidPulse is a Miami, Florida-based medical device company developing a next-generation aspiration platform for treating acute ischemic stroke through mechanical thrombectomy [^1][^2][^3]. The company's proprietary technology uses "controlled cyclic aspiration" — a pulsed approach intended to improve clot engagement and removal compared with conventional aspiration methods [^1][^2]. RapidPulse was spun out of medical device incubator Syntheon [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-16, the company announced an oversubscribed $48 million Series B co-led by Medtronic, TechWald Next S.p.A., and S3 Ventures, with proceeds funding completion of the TURBO clinical trial (Thrombectomy Using Pulsed Aspiration for Reperfusion of Brain Occlusions) [^1][^2][^4].
