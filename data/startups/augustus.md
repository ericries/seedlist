## About

Augustus is building an AI-native, federally chartered clearing bank designed to give international fintechs and banks direct access to the US dollar — including stablecoin rails alongside traditional payment infrastructure [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2022 by Ferdinand Dabitz, Joshua Becker, Simon Wimmer, and Peter Lieck, the company positions itself as a "Global Dollar Bank" that already processes billions in volume for customers including Kraken, and targets fintechs and banks in Latin America, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Africa [^1][^2].

## What Founders Say

Ferdinand Dabitz, CEO and Co-Founder: "The Dollar is the greatest product in the world but its distribution is fundamentally broken" [^1][^2].

Dabitz, on the clearing bank layer: "We think distribution breaks at the clearing bank layer. Legacy clearing systems are slow, unavailable, take two days to settle and close on the weekends" [^3].

Dabitz, on stablecoin adoption: "We think in 10 years from now all clearing banks will offer stablecoin rails like they offer Fedwire" [^3].

Dabitz, on AI and programmable money: "If AI agents should interact with the bank in a meaningful way, they will need programmable money" [^3].
