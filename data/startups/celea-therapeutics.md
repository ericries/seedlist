## About

Celea Therapeutics is a PureTech Health founded entity spun out in 2025 to advance deupirfenidone (LYT-100), a next-generation antifibrotic being developed as a potential new standard of care for idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-02 Celea announced completion of a $180M financing to fund the Phase 3 SURPASS-IPF trial, with an initial $75M closed on 2026-06-29 in Series Seed Preferred stock at $1.93 per share and the remainder committed by additional investors [^1][^2]. Participants include RA Capital Management, Leaps by Bayer, PureTech Health (which contributed $30M and retains 35.4% of fully diluted share capital), an unnamed large U.S. healthcare-focused fund, and a leading sovereign wealth fund [^1][^2][^3].
