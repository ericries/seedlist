## About

Transcarent is a Palo Alto-headquartered consumer-directed health and care platform for self-insured employers, hatched by Glen Tullman and Lee Shapiro of 7wireVentures alongside Hemant Taneja of General Catalyst — the same team behind Livongo [^1][^2][^3]. The company launched publicly in March 2021 after closing its Series A financing in October 2020, which was announced concurrently with the acquisition of BridgeHealth to serve as the foundation of the platform [^1][^2][^3]. Transcarent achieved unicorn status in 2022 and reached a $2.2 billion valuation with its 2024 Series D [^4][^5].
