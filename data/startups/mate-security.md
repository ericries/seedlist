## About

Mate Security is an Israel-based cybersecurity startup building an AI-native operating system for security operations, targeting the workflows human SOC analysts and AI security agents perform inside enterprises [^1][^2]. The founding team includes veterans of Wiz, Microsoft, Meta, and other technology companies [^1]. On 2026-07-28, Mate announced a $35 million Series A led by Canaan Partners, with participation from Microsoft's M12, Insight Partners, and Team8, bringing total funding to more than $50 million eight months after emerging from stealth with a $15.5 million seed round [^1][^2]. The company reports more than 500% revenue growth since Q3 2025 and employs around 50 people [^1].
