## About

Gray Swan is a Pittsburgh-based AI-security company spun out of Carnegie Mellon University's AI safety research group, co-founded by CEO Matt Fredrikson and Chief Scientist Zico Kolter; the company stress-tests AI models and agents for frontier labs and enterprise customers — its products are used by Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta — to identify unintended behaviors and provide guardrails that prevent them [^1][^2][^3]. The $40M Series A announced May 28, 2026 was co-led by Wing Venture Capital and Madrona at a $200M valuation with participation from Obvious Ventures, Snowflake Ventures, Hudson River Trading, Samsung Next, and returning investor Magarac Venture Partners; capital will accelerate go-to-market operations and scale the team serving the world's leading AI organizations [^1][^2][^3].
