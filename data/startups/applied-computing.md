## About

Applied Computing is a London-based Imperial College spinout building Orbital, a foundation AI model purpose-built for energy operations that combines physics-grounded intelligence with models spanning chemical engineering, time-series forecasting, and language, aimed at helping oil, gas, and refinery operators improve efficiency and reduce emissions [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by Callum Adamson (CEO) and Dr. Sam Tukra, a former AI research lead at Shell [^1][^3]. On 2026-07-16, Applied Computing announced a €17.4M (£15M / $20M) Series A led by KBR — accompanied by a multi-year exclusive AI product agreement — with participation from Databricks Ventures [^1][^2][^3].
