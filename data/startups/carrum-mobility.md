## About

Carrum Mobility is a Bengaluru-based B2B fleet management platform that owns and operates CNG and electric vehicles for ride-hailing platforms in India [^1][^2]. Founded in 2024 by Karan Jain, the company manages approximately 5,100 vehicles across Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, and Kolkata, and has onboarded more than 18,000 drivers [^1]. The company reports it has been profitable since launch and is operating at an annualised revenue run rate of approximately ₹400 crore [^2].
