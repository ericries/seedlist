## About

Genius AI (formerly GlossGenius) is a New York-based technology platform for in-person service businesses spanning beauty, wellness, fitness, and health verticals [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-21, the company announced a $44 million Series D financing at a $1.15 billion valuation, led by Lux Capital with participation from Bessemer Venture Partners, Imaginary Ventures, L Catterton Growth, 2048 Ventures, StepStone Private Ventures, and other existing investors — bringing total capital raised to more than $125 million [^1][^2][^3]. The company rebranded from GlossGenius to Genius AI to reflect its expansion into agentic AI products that execute business operations autonomously — scheduling, payments, marketing, staff management, and client communications [^1][^2]. The platform powers over 125,000 businesses, approaches a $200 million annual revenue run rate, and has facilitated $7 billion in annualized sales [^1][^2][^3]. Founders Danielle and Leah Cohen-Shohet are twin sisters who previously worked at Goldman Sachs and studied at Princeton [^3].
