## About

Materna Medical is a Mountain View, California-based OBGYN platform company developing medical devices for women's health, led by CEO Tracy MacNeal [^1][^2][^3]. Its flagship commercial product, Milli, is an FDA-cleared (2023) vaginal dilator for patients with vaginismus and related conditions causing painful sex [^2][^3]. Its lead pipeline product, the Ellora Obstetrical System, is an investigational device designed to reduce pelvic floor muscle injuries during vaginal delivery for first-time mothers [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds from the B3 round will support the readout of the Ellora EASE pivotal trial, expand Milli channel access, and build out commercial manufacturing capabilities ahead of the anticipated Ellora launch [^1][^2][^3][^4].
