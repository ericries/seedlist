## About

Baz is a San Francisco-based agentic coding platform that uses purpose-built agents to review code, detect bugs and vulnerabilities, enforce engineering standards, and help development teams ship high-quality software in the AI era [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Guy Eisenkot and a team formerly from Palo Alto Networks' Cloud AppSec business [^1]. Alongside the funding announcement at the AI Engineer World's Fair on June 29, 2026, Baz introduced Baz Planner — a system that intervenes during the planning stage of code work to eliminate entire classes of issues before code is authored [^1][^2].
