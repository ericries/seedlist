## About

Neura Robotics is a German humanoid robotics company building "cognitive robots" combining AI perception, manipulation, and locomotion [^1][^2]. Founder and CEO David Reger has stated the company is focused on machines that can "see, hear, touch, and learn" [^1]. With its June 2026 Series C, Neura claims the largest funding round ever for a full-stack robotics company and becomes Europe's most-funded humanoid-robot maker [^1][^2]. The company plans to use the capital to scale serial production toward several million robots by 2030, and to expand its "NEURA Gyms," training environments for cognitive robots in real-world deployment scenarios [^1].
