## About

Definic (formerly Nordics) is a Košice, Slovakia-based vendor intelligence platform for enterprise IT procurement. The company helps enterprises improve IT vendor selection using delivery track records, case studies, and live market pricing. Enterprise clients using Definic have reportedly shortened tender cycles from months to weeks and decreased IT vendor expenses by as much as 20% [^1]. The company rebranded from Nordics to Definic in conjunction with its seed round, marking a shift from a regional IT marketplace into a global vendor intelligence platform [^1].
