## About

Dolfin is a Barcelona-based AI-native platform for sales compensation management, founded in 2023 by Daniel Seror and Antoni Bardina to replace spreadsheets and bespoke consulting setups that RevOps, finance, and compensation teams use to design and run commission plans [^1][^2]. The company announced a €2.1M Seed round on May 13, 2026, led by Swanlaab with participation from Archipelago Next, Inveready, and Dozen; the capital will fund product development and expansion across Europe and the United States [^1][^2].
