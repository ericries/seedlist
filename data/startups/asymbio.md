## About

AsymBio is a biologics-focused Contract Development and Manufacturing Organization (CDMO) headquartered in China and a subsidiary of Asymchem Group [^1][^2][^3]. The company provides end-to-end services spanning early-stage and process development, toxicology, clinical manufacturing, and large-scale commercial manufacturing, with particular expertise in antibody-drug conjugates and related biologics [^1][^2]. AsymBio generated approximately US$70 million in revenue in 2025, with Q1 2026 revenue exceeding US$21 million [^1][^3].

| 2026-08-04 | Capital Injection | $184M | Asymchem Group | Hillhouse Qirui [^1][^2][^3] |

The capital injection totaled RMB 1.2397 billion (approximately US$184 million), with parent Asymchem Group contributing RMB 1.05 billion (~US$156 million) and Hillhouse Qirui contributing RMB 177 million (~US$26 million); other unnamed investors also participated [^1][^2][^3]. Following the transaction, Asymchem Group's ownership stake in AsymBio increases to 83.4965% [^1][^2]. Proceeds will support capacity ramp-up, R&D platform investments, GMP manufacturing lines, and high-containment facilities [^1][^2][^3].
