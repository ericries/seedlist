## About

Sorted (Sorted Wallet) is a Hong Kong-based crypto wallet purpose-built for feature phones and entry-level smartphones, founded in 2022 by Stephen Browne [^1][^2][^3]. At 10MB it is the lightest crypto wallet on app stores, has been downloaded 500,000+ times, and is growing fastest in Nigeria, Kenya, Tanzania, Bangladesh, and Madagascar [^1][^2]. The $4.4M seed round announced May 20, 2026 includes $3.4M in equity from Tether and Gnosis (co-leads) with participation from Movement, Angel Invest Group, and angels including the founders of RWA.io, plus $1M in strategic support from Vox Solutions for mobile-operator integrations [^1][^2][^3]. Tether previously backed Sorted's $1.5M pre-seed in September 2024 [^2]. Proceeds will expand coverage in Sub-Saharan Africa and South Asia and deepen telecom integrations [^1][^2].
