## About

PhoenixAI (formerly CelerData) is a Menlo Park, California-based developer of an AI-native analytical database designed for agentic AI workloads [^1][^2][^3]. The platform gives autonomous AI agents sub-second access to live enterprise data at massive scale, emphasizing real-time ingestion and high concurrency so thousands of agents can query, reason and act on petabytes of live data simultaneously [^2][^3]. The company rebranded from CelerData in 2026 under the leadership of President Rick Underwood, a former Snowflake executive [^2][^3]. On June 11, 2026, PhoenixAI announced an $80M Series B led by Sky9 Capital, with participation from Atypical Ventures, Olive Technology Ventures, and previous investors. The capital will accelerate development of the AI-native database, expand go-to-market initiatives, and deepen governance for regulated industries [^1][^2][^3].
