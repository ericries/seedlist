## About

Zaro is a London-based enterprise AI startup that emerged from stealth in June 2026 with a $5.1M pre-seed round [^1]. The company is building a shared context layer that unifies enterprise AI tools, workflows, and data within a single adaptive workspace, with the goal of letting agents, applications, and workflows operate on a common substrate of company data, decisions, and operational history [^1] [^2]. Several members of Zaro's founding team previously worked at AI startup Convergence and contributed to the development of Salesforce's Agentforce platform following Salesforce's acquisition of Convergence [^2] [^3].
