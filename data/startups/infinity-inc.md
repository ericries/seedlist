## About

Infinity is an AI infrastructure startup building CUDA-alternative kernel software designed to run AI models across a wide range of hardware, including SRAM, GPUs, phone chips, and systolic arrays [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Jeremy Nixon, a former Google Brain researcher and creator of the AGI House community [^1]. Its core product, an AI research agent called Ignition, writes, tests, debugs, and rewrites low-level code for AI inference on Nvidia-alternative chips, aiming to reduce dependence on Nvidia's CUDA software stack that has anchored the dominant AI framework ecosystem (PyTorch, TensorFlow) to Nvidia GPUs [^1]. Named customers include AI chip maker D-Matrix, and the company reports discussions with additional chip and cloud partners [^1]. Infinity has 26 employees as of the round announcement [^2]. On 2026-07-20, Infinity announced a $15 million round at a $100 million valuation [^1][^2].
