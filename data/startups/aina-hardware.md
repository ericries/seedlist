## About

Aina is a consumer hardware company building physical interfaces for the AI-agent era — devices meant to command and control AI agents rather than passively record the user [^1][^2]. Founder and CEO Apoorv Shankar previously served as Vice President of Hardware at smart-ring maker Ultrahuman, where he led hardware for the Ultrahuman Ring [^1]. Incorporated in May 2025, the company operated as a Human-Computer Interaction lab under the codename "Project Mirage" before rebranding to Aina [^1][^2]. Its first product, Dune, is a context-aware three-key macropad for Mac whose functions automatically adapt to whichever application is in the foreground [^1].

On 2026-07-16, Aina announced a $5.5 million seed round co-led by Redstart Labs (the venture arm of Info Edge India) and 360 ONE, with participation from MIXI Global Investments, Antler, and Blume Founders Fund [^1][^2][^3]. The round also drew individual investors including Kunal Shah (CRED founder), Razorpay co-founders Harshil Mathur and Shashank Kumar, and Scribd founder Tikhon Bernstam [^1][^3]. Aina operates from both Bengaluru and San Francisco [^1].
