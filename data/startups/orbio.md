## About

Orbio is an AI-powered workforce management startup founded in 2025 by Sergi Bastardas, Nacho Travesí, and Antonio Melé that helps businesses recruit, onboard, manage, and retain frontline employees using AI agents [^1][^2]. The platform deploys AI agents named Maria, Daniel, and Claire that conduct candidate interviews, assess fit, monitor employee output, and handle daily check-ins across the employee lifecycle [^1][^2]. Customers include Poke, YUM! Brands (Pizza Hut, Taco Bell, KFC), and The Stepping Stones Group [^1].
