## About

Uniti is a New York-based agentic AI platform for real estate operators that automates sales, leasing, tenant support, maintenance, collections, payments, and reviews across residential and commercial properties [^1][^2][^3]. The company's AI agents run across voice, SMS, email, chat, and WhatsApp, integrating with more than 15 property management and CRM systems used by real estate operators [^2][^3]. Uniti was co-founded in 2024 by Francesco Decamilli (CEO) and Emre Altinok, and counts Regus, StorQuest, RHP Properties, Storage King USA, and Fora among its early customers [^2][^3]. On 2026-07-21, Uniti announced a $12 million Series A led by Pathlight, with participation from MetaProp, existing investors Prudence, Flex Capital, Alate Partners, and RE | VC, plus individual angels including Romain Huet (OpenAI), Lenny Rachitsky, and Gokul Rajaram [^1][^2][^3]. The round brings Uniti's total reported equity funding to approximately $16 million following a $4 million seed round announced in March 2025 [^2][^3].
