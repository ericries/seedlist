## About

Ornn is building a marketplace for GPU compute designed to function like a commodities exchange, letting AI companies, data center operators, and investors trade and hedge against GPU price swings [^1][^2]. The company's core product is the Ornn Compute Price Index (OCPI), a benchmark built from live GPU spot transactions covering H100, H200, B200, and RTX 5090 hardware, which it says is distributed on Bloomberg Terminal [^1]. In May 2026, the Intercontinental Exchange announced a partnership with Ornn to launch cash-settled, U.S. dollar-denominated GPU compute futures contracts referenced against the OCPI, pending regulatory approval [^1]. Co-founders Kush Bavaria and Wayne Nelms are MIT graduates [^1].
