## About

Tilt is a London-based live-auction commerce app founded in 2021 by Abhi Thanendran and Neil Shah, two early employees of Revolut [^1][^2]. The platform enables sellers to broadcast live video streams to buyers, using AI tools — including its "Snap" feature that creates a product listing from video in under a second — to power listing automation, real-time pricing, natural-language search, and content repurposing [^1][^2]. The company charges buyers (not sellers), is live across the UK, Italy, Spain, and Poland, and says it has grown 8x since its 2024 Series A [^2][^3]. On June 2, 2026, Tilt announced a $26 million round with Vinted Ventures joining as a new investor alongside existing backers TQ Ventures, Balderton Capital, Earlybird, and Seedcamp, bringing total funding to more than $50 million [^1][^2][^3].
