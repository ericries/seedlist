## About

Coralogix is a Boston-headquartered (Israeli-founded) AI-native observability and software-monitoring company led by co-founder and CEO Ariel Assaraf; the platform serves more than 5,000 customers including IBM, Tradeweb, and JFrog, monitoring logs, metrics, traces, and AI-agent behavior at scale [^1][^2][^3]. The $200M Series F announced June 3, 2026 was led by Advent and the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board (CPPIB) at a $1.6B post-money valuation, with participation from Greenfield Partners and Brighton Park Capital; the round comes only 11 months after Coralogix's $115M Series E and brings total funding to $550M, with proceeds focused on three areas: AI-native observability, telemetry-data infrastructure, and global enterprise expansion [^1][^2][^3].
