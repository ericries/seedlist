## About

TwoStep Therapeutics is a Stanford spinout developing tumor-targeted therapies with broad applicability across solid tumors, built on a versatile peptide that selectively binds multiple tumor-associated targets [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by CEO Caitlyn Miller (Stanford Bioengineering PhD) alongside faculty co-founders Jennifer Cochran, Nobel Laureate Carolyn Bertozzi, and Ronald Levy [^2]. In September 2026 it announced $62.5M in oversubscribed Series A financing alongside FDA clearance of the IND for its first-in-class peptide-drug conjugate [^1][^3].
