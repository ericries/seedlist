## About

Baseten is an AI inference platform that helps companies deploy, scale, and serve machine learning models in production [^1][^2]. The company says it processes more than one billion inference calls per day across 87 global clusters on 18 cloud providers, and reports approximately 20x year-over-year revenue growth [^1][^2].

The Series F was structured in two tranches at $13 billion and $11 billion valuations, reflecting staggered timing of investor commitments [^1][^2].
