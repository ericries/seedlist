## About

Cowboy Space Corporation (formerly Aetherflux) is a San Carlos, CA-based space infrastructure company building vertically integrated infrastructure to power and run AI compute in orbit — spanning launch vehicles, space-based power, and in-orbit compute [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2024 by Robinhood co-founder Baiju Bhatt, the company unveiled its new "Cowboy Space" name alongside the Series B announcement and plans to launch its first satellite later in 2026 to demonstrate space-to-Earth power beaming [^1][^2]. The $275M Series B announced May 11, 2026 at a $2B valuation was led by Index Ventures, with new investors IVP, Blossom Capital, and SAIC joining existing backers Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Construct Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, NEA, Interlagos, and founder Baiju Bhatt [^1][^2][^3].
