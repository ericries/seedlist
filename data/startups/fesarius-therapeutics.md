## About

Fesarius Therapeutics is a New York-based commercial-stage medical technology company focused on dermal repair [^1][^2]. Its flagship product, DermiSphere, is the first hydrogel Dermal Regeneration Template (hDRT) cleared by the FDA, with 2025 clearance for the management of multiple wound types [^1][^2]. On June 10, 2026, the company announced the closing of an oversubscribed $20 million Series A led by Jefferson Life Sciences, with participation from Johnson & Johnson Innovation – JJDC, Empire State Development's NY Ventures, and the American Society of Plastic Surgeons (ASPS) [^1][^2][^3]. The capital will support commercial expansion of DermiSphere, pursuit of Breakthrough Device Designation, and advancement of clinical studies for its OneStep procedure [^1][^3].
