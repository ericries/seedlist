## About

Exa Labs is a San Francisco-based AI search infrastructure company building a search engine purpose-built for AI applications, founded in 2021 [^1][^2][^3]. The platform powers search for Cursor, Cognition, HubSpot, OpenRouter, Monday.com, and 400,000+ developers [^1][^2]. The $250M Series C announced May 20-21, 2026 was led by Andreessen Horowitz at a $2.2B valuation [^1][^2][^3].
