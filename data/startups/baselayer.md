## About

Baselayer is a New York-based B2B risk assessment and fraud prevention platform serving financial institutions and government agencies [^1][^2]. The company uses proprietary Graph AI technology to automate Know Your Business (KYB) compliance and entity verification for banks, fintechs, and government agencies, and reports serving more than 2,000 U.S. financial institutions [^2][^3]. Baselayer was founded in 2023 by Jonathan Awad, Timothy Hyde, and William Slessman [^1][^2][^4]. On 2026-09-22, Baselayer announced a $35 million Series A extension led by M13 with participation from Picus Capital, Torch Capital, Afore Capital, and Matt Thompson (formerly of Socure); proceeds will fund the launch of an "Agentic Identity Suite," anchored by a "Know Your Agent" (KYA) verification system designed to issue credentials for AI agents that open accounts, make purchases, and move money on users' behalf, with partners including FIS, Prove, and Socure [^3]. M13 categorizes Baselayer as an enterprise Series A portfolio investment; Afore Capital lists Baselayer as a pre-seed investment covering "Business Credit Bureau & all related aspects (B2B Fraud Prevention, KYB, Credit Score)" with Torch Capital as a follow-on [^4][^5].
