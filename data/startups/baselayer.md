## About

Baselayer is a New York-based B2B risk assessment and fraud prevention platform serving financial institutions and government agencies [^1][^2]. The company uses proprietary Graph AI technology to automate Know Your Business (KYB) compliance and entity verification for banks, fintechs, and government agencies, and reports serving more than 1,900 institutions [^2]. Baselayer was founded in 2023 by Jonathan Awad, Timothy Hyde, and William Slessman [^1][^2].
