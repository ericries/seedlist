## About

Polysense is a Ghent-based AI company that builds real-time quality control and process optimization software for food manufacturers [^1][^2]. The platform combines continuous in-line inspection, real-time imaging data, and synthetic data models with automated process control, using three integrated products — Polysense Qualify (real-time inspection), Polysense Platform (data consolidation), and Polysense AutoControl (automated machine adjustments) — to detect quality deviations and automatically adjust production parameters [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2022 by Yarne De Munck, Lucas Van Dijck, and Jarne Bogaert, and initially raised $2.2M in an earlier round before narrowing its focus to food manufacturing [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-08 Polysense announced a $10.7M oversubscribed seed round led by Felix Capital, with participation from Fortino Ventures, Syndicate One, 100IN, and additional angel investors [^1][^2]. The company reports live commercial deployments with major European food producers including Agristo, Darta, and Poppies Bakeries, and has expanded from Europe into the United States and Middle East across vegetable, potato, bakery, confectionery, and packaging production lines [^1][^2]. Proceeds will fund international deployment acceleration and growth of engineering, sales, and customer success teams [^1][^2].
