## About

Lightspring is a Besançon, France-based deeptech company designing and manufacturing optical interfaces for photonic integrated circuits. The company uses 3D lithography (two-photon polymerization) to fabricate optical coupling structures directly onto photonic chips, targeting fiber-to-chip coupling losses below 0.5 dB and eliminating active alignment requirements [^1][^2][^3]. Optical packaging accounts for roughly 50% of photonic chip costs today, a bottleneck Lightspring is targeting for AI infrastructure and datacenter applications [^3]. It was co-founded by Grégoire Bonnat (CEO), Adrià Grabulosa (CTO), and Daniel Brunner (Scientific Director) and is based at TEMIS Innovation in Besançon [^2]. Lightspring was named a laureate of i-Lab, France's national deeptech innovation competition, receiving a €350,000 grant [^1][^3].
