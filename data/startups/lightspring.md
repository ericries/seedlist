## About

Lightspring is a Besançon, France-based deeptech company designing and manufacturing optical interfaces for photonic integrated circuits. The company develops 3D-fabricated optical interconnection technology intended to reduce packaging costs and complexity for photonic chips used in AI infrastructure [^1][^2]. It was co-founded by Grégoire Bonnat (CEO), Adrià Grabulosa (CTO), and Daniel Brunner (Scientific Director) and is based at TEMIS Innovation in Besançon [^2].
