## About

PLD Space is a Spanish aerospace company headquartered in Elche that develops reusable small-satellite launch vehicles, most notably MIURA 5, a two-stage rocket intended for commercial satellite launches [^1][^2][^3]. The company is led by Executive President Ezequiel Sánchez [^1][^3].

On September 1, 2026, PLD Space announced a €108 million extension of its Series C round, once again led by Mitsubishi Electric Corporation [^1][^2][^3]. The extension brings the Series C total to €288 million and PLD Space's aggregate funding to date to €488 million [^1][^2]. Participants in the extension include Spanish public investment firm COFIDES (co-investing again after the initial Series C), Endeavor Catalyst (Endeavor's global venture fund), and Spain Oman Private Equity Fund (SOPEF), managed by MCH Private Equity [^1][^2][^3]. The extension follows a €158.9 million award from the European Space Agency under the European Launcher Challenge [^1][^2].
