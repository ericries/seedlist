## About

AIR (air.security) is a security startup that helps companies vet the skills, capabilities, and add-ons that AI agents use — treating agent extensions and third-party tools as a supply-chain risk surface. The company came out of stealth in September 2026 disclosing $50M raised across two seed rounds: a $10M first round led by Sequoia Capital and a $40M second round led by Greenoaks Capital [^1].
