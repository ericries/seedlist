## About

AIR (air.security) is an Israel-based security startup that helps companies vet the skills, capabilities, and add-ons that AI agents use — treating agent extensions and third-party tools as a supply-chain risk surface. The company came out of stealth on September 1, 2026 disclosing $50M raised across two seed rounds: a $10M first round led by Sequoia Capital and a $40M second round led by Greenoaks Capital [^1][^3][^4]. Co-founders Yair Saban (CEO) and Niv Hoffman (CTO) met roughly a decade earlier while serving in Israel's Unit 8200 with backgrounds spanning offensive cybersecurity, enterprise infrastructure, and AI security research [^1][^4]. The company operates with ~40 employees in Israel [^4] and recently added Ryan Knisley — a two-time Fortune 50 CISO (Costco, Walt Disney) — as Chief Strategy Officer [^2][^3]. Prior to the launch, AIR's research team published a series of disclosures on supply-chain vulnerabilities in the agent ecosystem, including "MCPJacking" (155 hijackable MCPs, August 27, 2026) and "SkillJacking" (925 hijacked skills, July 2, 2026) [^2].
