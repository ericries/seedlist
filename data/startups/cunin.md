## About

CUNIN is a culture-led lifestyle brand entering India's fragrance market with what it describes as "carryable perfume accessories" — a category of fragrance products designed to be worn, clipped, and carried [^1][^2][^3]. The debut collection, The World of Antonyms, weaves a creative theme across its products, packaging, and branding, with the company positioning itself less as another perfume line and more as an identity-and-community-first lifestyle brand [^1][^3]. Proceeds from the pre-seed round will accelerate product development, expand the team, invest in brand building, and support an offline retail push [^1][^2][^3]. The round was co-led by All In Capital and Huddle Ventures [^1][^2].
