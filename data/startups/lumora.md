## About

Lumora is South Korea's first industrial-space independent power producer (IPP), specializing in underutilized industrial space — factory rooftops, parking structures, and idle land [^1][^2][^3]. Unlike fee-based developers, Lumora directly owns and operates solar assets under a full-stack IPP model, securing long-term generation revenue while delivering energy savings to customers [^1][^2]. As of the pre-seed close, the company had assembled a 43.6 MW nationwide pipeline across Korea and earned recognition through the 2026 Eco-Startup Growth Program (Ministry of Climate, Energy and Environment) and the 19th Startup NEST cohort (Korea Credit Guarantee Fund) [^1][^2][^3]. Lumora targets South Korea's estimated $145B industrial solar market [^1][^2].
