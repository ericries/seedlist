## About

Cellply is a Bologna, Italy-based deep tech company founded in 2013 by Massimo Bocchi and Roberto Guerrieri that develops analytical tools for the deep characterization of cancer immunotherapies, cell therapies, and cell-based biotherapeutics [^1][^2]. The company's flagship product, VivaCyte, combines microfluidics with AI-supported image analysis to enable fully automated characterization of immune cells at high resolution and scale [^1][^2]. The €7.15M round announced May 11, 2026 was led by ENEA Tech e Biomedical with participation from CDP Venture Capital (via the Fondo Rilancio fund); the capital will fund international expansion of VivaCyte and development of automated quality control procedures for cell therapies [^1][^2]. Miro Venturi joined the Board as part of the round [^2].
