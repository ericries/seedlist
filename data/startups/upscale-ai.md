## About

Upscale AI builds full-stack AI networking infrastructure — spanning silicon, systems, and software — designed to connect accelerators, memory, and storage into a unified high-performance fabric for large-scale AI training and inference workloads [^1][^2][^3]. The Santa Clara-based company is co-founded by CEO Barun Kar and Executive Chairman Rajiv Khemani, both veterans of networking semiconductors [^1][^2]. Upscale AI closed a $200M Series A in January 2026 and added a $190M Series A-1 extension on June 22, 2026, reaching $500M in total funding at a $2 billion valuation in under six months [^1][^2][^3].

## What Investors Say

Sandesh Patnam, Premji Invest, on Upscale AI's approach: the team has a "differentiated architectural approach" addressing "one of the most critical bottlenecks in AI infrastructure" [^2].

Emily Zhao, Salesforce Ventures: "Open-standard fabrics will become critical" as AI clusters expand across varied environments [^2].

## What Founders Say

Barun Kar, CEO, Upscale AI: "Networking is one of the most critical bottlenecks" in AI infrastructure; Upscale AI is building solutions "purpose-built for large-scale, synchronized workloads" [^2].

> "The world in the future is going to be a variety of heterogeneous AI systems. It's not an Nvidia or our solution." — Rajiv Khemani, Co-Founder & Executive Chairman, Upscale AI, Fortune interview, June 22, 2026 [^4]
