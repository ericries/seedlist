## About

Mekr Technologies is a New Delhi-based full-stack consumer appliance design and manufacturing platform founded in 2022 by Anand Yadav and Gaurang Kuchhal that provides product design, prototyping, tooling, certification, and mass manufacturing for consumer appliance brands [^1][^2]. The company has developed over 100 SKUs and worked with more than 40 brands including Croma, Wipro, Amazon Basics, and Flipkart [^1][^2]. The Rs 67 Cr Series A announced May 12, 2026 was led by Avaana Capital with participation from Titan Capital Winners Fund; capital will fund R&D, manufacturing automation, supplier localisation, quality systems, and export readiness [^1][^2].
