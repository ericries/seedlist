## About

Town is a San Francisco-based startup building a personalized AI assistant that learns how individuals work across the tools they already use — email, calendar, Slack, documents, and messaging platforms — and acts more like a chief of staff than a chatbot [^1][^2]. The product is designed to adapt to a user's communication style, priorities, and routines without requiring extensive prompting or setup [^2][^3].

The company was co-founded by Jean-Denis Greze (formerly of Dropbox and Plaid, where he served as CTO) and Tony Vincent (formerly in AI and product roles at Google) [^1][^2].

## What Investors Say

Alex Rampell, General Partner at Andreessen Horowitz, said of the investment: "Most AI assistants are essentially better search boxes. Jean-Denis and Tony are building something categorically different." [^3]

Kirsten Green, Founding Partner at Forerunner Ventures, said: "What this team understands deeply is that the next wave of AI adoption will not come from people opening another tool." [^3]
