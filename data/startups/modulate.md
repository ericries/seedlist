## About

Modulate is a Boston-based voice AI company building audio-native machine learning models for voice understanding, moderation, and analysis [^1][^2]. Founded in 2017 by MIT physics undergraduates Mike Pappas and Carter Huffman, the company is best known for ToxMod, its voice moderation platform used by online games and enterprise platforms to detect harmful speech, and is expanding into broader conversational intelligence [^1]. On September 28, 2026, Modulate announced a $25M funding round led by Future Ventures to scale its lead in audio-native AI [^1][^2][^3].
