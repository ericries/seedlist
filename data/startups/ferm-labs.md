## About

Ferm Labs is a Swiss-Italian food-tech startup headquartered in Zug, Switzerland with a production facility in Bolzano, Italy, that develops bioprocessing-based B2B flavour ingredients using fermentation technology [^1]. The platform accelerates fermentation from weeks to hours, converting low-value byproducts into clean-label ingredient pastes and powders; its flagship product, Kokumesan, targets industrial cheese flavouring [^1]. On 2026-09-14, the company announced a €3M growth round led by CDP Venture Capital (through the Green Transition Fund financed by NextGenerationEU), with participation from Fund F, Redstone (with Euregio+ and Alpine VC), and Loacker [^1].
