## About

Fortell is an AI-powered hearing aid startup founded by Matthew de Jonge [^1]. The company develops hearing aids that use AI to "understand the sounds around you in real time, figure out what's important and turn down the rest" rather than amplifying all audio simultaneously as conventional devices do [^1]. Fortell is targeting an industry dominated by five companies that control roughly 97% of the hearing aid market, where insurance typically does not cover costs and product innovation has been limited [^1]. On 2026-09-16, Fortell announced $163 million in funding with participation from Founders Fund, Thrive Capital, and Valor Equity Partners (Antonio Gracias) [^1].
