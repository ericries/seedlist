## About

Fortell is an AI-powered prescription hearing aid startup founded by Matthew de Jonge, who spent six years building the company before its public launch [^1][^2]. The company develops hearing aids that use AI to "understand the sounds around you in real time, figure out what's important and turn down the rest" rather than amplifying all audio simultaneously as conventional devices do [^1]. Fortell claims its "AI 2.0" technology delivers 10x more words understood versus leading AI hearing aids in challenging noise, up to 98% background noise elimination, and 95% preference over five competitors in clinical trials [^2]. The company is targeting an industry dominated by five companies that control roughly 97% of the hearing aid market, where insurance typically does not cover costs and product innovation has been limited [^1]. Fortell is available in New York, Greenwich, Boston, and Palm Beach at launch [^2]. On 2026-09-16, Fortell announced $163 million in funding with participation from Founders Fund, Thrive Capital, and Valor Equity Partners (Antonio Gracias) [^1][^3].
