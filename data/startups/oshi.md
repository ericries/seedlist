## About

Oshi (formerly Plantish) is a U.S. plant-based seafood startup that uses 3D printing to turn soy protein and mycoprotein into whole-cut fish alternatives, featuring 18g of protein and algal omega-3 fatty acids per serving [^1][^2]. On May 21, 2026, the company announced $3M in strategic venture funding from a Latin American seafood manufacturer (described by the company as a regional seafood giant) with additional backing from Unovis Asset Management, Alumni Ventures, and individual investors tied to Oatly, Impossible Foods, and Beyond Meat; the strategic partner brings supply-chain and distribution capabilities across Latin America [^1][^2]. The capital will support the launch of Oshi's plant-based whitefish line and expansion into 686 retail doors through distributors KeHE and UNFI; the company reports fourfold year-over-year revenue growth and presence in more than 100 restaurants [^1][^2].
