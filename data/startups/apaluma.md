## About

Apaluma is an Albuquerque, New Mexico-based AI data platform that digitizes and organizes fragmented government records, with an initial focus on environmental datasets spanning air, water, energy, and waste [^1][^2]. The company transforms regulatory records—permits, filings, enforcement actions, and other documents that typically live in PDFs, scanned files, and incompatible databases—into a connected ecosystem that is searchable, location-mapped, and governed by each agency's own rules [^1][^2]. Founder and CEO Alicia J. Keyes previously ran economic development for the state of New Mexico [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-16, Apaluma announced the close of a $5.55 million seed round co-led by Crosslink Capital and Kickstart, co-led by Haven Ventures and Overwater Ventures, with additional participation from Orange DAO and NM Vintage Fund [^1][^2].
