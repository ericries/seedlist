## About

Quercus Biosolutions is an ag-biotech startup applying AI protein-design tools originally developed for drug discovery to create a new category of crop protection products based on "designer mini proteins" that bind validated biological targets in weeds and other pests [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by Jon Lightner, PhD, and Matt Crisp, and licenses AI protein-design technology from partner Ordaos Bio [^1][^2]. Quercus reports five active programs and multiple field trials underway [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-29 the company announced an oversubscribed $5M seed round led by Serra Ventures [^1][^2].
