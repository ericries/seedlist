## About

Air Space Intelligence (ASI) is an AI startup building software for aviation and defense operations [^1][^2]. The company's flagship product, Flyways, is used by flight dispatchers to plan routes for aircraft, factoring in air traffic, weather, and airport conditions [^2]. Alaska Airlines and the US Department of Defense are among its customers [^2][^3]. In fall 2023 the company won two contracts with the US Air Force totaling $2.7M and qualified to compete for a third valued at up to $900M [^1].
