## About

CopySight is a Los Angeles-based AI IP-governance company that builds a copyright and likeness risk-scoring platform for AI-generated content [^1][^2][^3]. The company launched CopyScore V2 alongside its seed round, extending its risk scoring from AI-generated images into video, and reports processing more than 87,000 IP risk assessments since January 2026 — a 25x increase in usage over that period [^2][^3]. On 2026-07-30 CopySight announced a $3M seed round led by Mucker Capital [^1][^2][^3].
