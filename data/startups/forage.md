## About

Forage is a mission-driven payments company that enables merchants to accept SNAP EBT (Electronic Benefits Transfer) payments online and in-store, helping over 42 million low-income Americans use food stamp benefits at grocery platforms and retailers [^1]. Founded in 2020 and headquartered in San Francisco, the company processes EBT payments at over 100,000 stores in all 50 states [^2]. Forage's customers include Dollar General, Albertsons, Uber Eats, DoorDash, and Toast [^1][^3]. In late 2025, Forage launched a consumer app that lets users check their EBT balance, find savings, and earn rewards on everyday purchases — surpassing 100,000 downloads since launch [^2].
