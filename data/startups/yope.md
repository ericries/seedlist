## About

Yope is a private social networking app centered on "micro communities," where friends and family share photos, videos, messages, and games without algorithmic feeds or advertising [^1]. Accounts are private by default and the product monetizes through a subscription for power users [^1]. The company was founded in 2021 by CEO Bahram Ismailau and CTO Paul Rudkouski, and the current version of the app — a friends-only photo-messaging experience — was released in September 2024 after two prior product pivots [^1][^2][^3]. Ismailau is based in London and the company plans to open a U.S. office; the team is roughly 35 people [^1]. Yope reports nearly 15 million registered users, with over half opening the app at least five days per week, and users collectively sharing 10–20 million pieces of content weekly [^1].
