## About

ClearOps is a Munich, Germany-based enterprise SaaS company building an AI-driven operating system for industrial after-sales, including service and spare-parts processes for OEMs [^1][^2]. The company is headquartered in Munich with additional offices in Lisbon, Atlanta, and San José, and employs approximately 60 people globally [^1][^2]. Customers include industrial manufacturers AGCO, Terex, Jungheinrich, and Lippert [^1][^2]. ClearOps reports it has increased parts availability by up to 40%, driven 5-15% growth in parts sales, and reduced repair times by up to two days across customer networks [^1][^2].

The €8.6M Series A announced May 21, 2026 was led by Hitachi Ventures with participation from Schoeller Group and Barkawi Group; the company describes it as its first institutional capital raise [^1][^2].
