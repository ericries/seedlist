## About

ICEYE is a Finnish space technology company headquartered in Espoo that operates the world's largest synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite constellation, providing persistent monitoring capabilities to governments, defense, intelligence, insurance, and environmental monitoring customers globally [^1][^2]. Founded in 2014 by Rafal Modrzewski (CEO) and Pekka Laurila, ICEYE pioneered small SAR satellites capable of imaging the Earth through clouds and darkness [^1]. As of the 2026 Series F, seven European governments had procured sovereign satellite systems from ICEYE [^1][^2]. The company reported crossing over €250 million in revenue and over €100 million in EBITDA in 2025, with a contracted backlog exceeding €1.5 billion [^1][^2].
