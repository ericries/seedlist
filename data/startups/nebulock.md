## About

Nebulock is a San Francisco-based, AI-native contextual security analytics platform that helps enterprise security teams hunt, convict, and attribute threats across endpoint, identity, cloud, network, and SaaS environments [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Damien Lewke, who previously held product leadership roles at CrowdStrike and Arctic Wolf [^2]. Since emerging from stealth in mid-2025, Nebulock reports running over 300 million agentic investigations and surfacing 4,000+ high-confidence findings, with named customers including Cribl, Bain Capital, and HealthEdge [^1][^3].
