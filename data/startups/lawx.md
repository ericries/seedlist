## About

LawX is a Berlin-based legaltech company building an AI-native operating system to replace the fragmented legacy tools used by German law firms and notaries [^1]. The platform automates case management, document handling, billing, and communication, and integrates with Outlook, Word, and German legal registers [^1]. Since launching its product in November 2025, the company reports having generated more than €1 million in contracted recurring revenue [^1].
