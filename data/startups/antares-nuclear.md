## About

Antares is a nuclear fission energy company developing compact small modular microreactors (SMRs) for defense and space applications [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-27, the company announced a $470 million Series C round consisting of $370 million in equity and $100 million in debt, co-led by Paradigm and Caffeinated Capital, with participation from Point72 Ventures, Shine Capital, and Industrious Ventures [^1][^2][^3]. The financing brings total funding to over $604 million and follows the company's Series B of $96 million in December 2025 [^2][^3]. Antares' Mark-0 demonstration microreactor achieved criticality on June 4, 2026 at Idaho National Laboratory under the DOE Reactor Pilot Program — the first private advanced reactor criticality in decades [^1][^2]. The reactors use TRISO fuel (uranium encapsulated in carbon and ceramic shells) cooled by gases such as helium or molten salts and produce 100 kilowatts to 1 megawatt of electricity [^1]. Antares is one of three Pentagon finalists for the Advanced Nuclear Power for Installations program, targeting first electricity production in 2027 and initial military deployment by 2028 [^1][^2].
