## About

Omio is a Berlin-based multi-modal travel booking platform, founded in 2013 by Naren Shaam under the original name GoEuro, that offers train, bus, flight, and ferry booking across more than 3,000 transportation providers globally [^1][^2][^3]. According to Nikkei Asia, the company has raised approximately $480 million from investors including New Enterprise Associates, Goldman Sachs, and Temasek, and reached a $1 billion valuation prior to the July 2026 announcement [^1]. On 2026-07-21, Omio announced a $10 million strategic investment from Granite-Integral — a Japan-Singapore joint venture launched in March 2025 between Singapore-based Granite Asia and Japanese private equity firm Integral — to accelerate expansion in Japan and Southeast Asia, coinciding with the announcement of an agreement to acquire Rail Europe [^1][^2][^3]. Omio expanded into Japan in March 2026 and has active operations in six Southeast Asian countries and Brazil [^2].
