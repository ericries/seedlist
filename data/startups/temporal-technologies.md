## About

Temporal Technologies builds a durable execution platform used to orchestrate reliable, long-running workflows across distributed systems and AI infrastructure [^1]. The company positions its Series E as being scaled to meet demand from AI workloads where reliability is increasingly the bottleneck [^1][^2].
