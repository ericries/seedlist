## About

InvestiFi is a Dover, Delaware-based fintech providing embedded investing infrastructure to credit unions and community banks [^1][^2]. Its platform enables financial institutions to offer digital investing directly from checking or savings accounts via a patent-pending flow of funds branded as "Investing from Checking," aiming to help community institutions compete with larger brokerages and neobanks without asking account holders to leave their trusted banking relationship [^1][^2]. InvestiFi reports scaling from 4 clients in 2024 to more than 60 signed financial institutions by July 2026 [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-29, InvestiFi announced a $20 million funding round led by Vibe Credit Union [^1][^2].
