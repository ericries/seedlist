## About

Hypefy AI is a Croatia-based AI platform that automates end-to-end influencer marketing campaign execution — including creator discovery, outreach, pricing, contracting, content review, performance tracking, reporting and payments — while brands maintain control over briefs, budgets and approvals [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2021 by Stjepan Zelić, Davor Šibenik, and Filip Špiranec, and reports work with brands including NIVEA, Unilever, Philips, PepsiCo and Samsung, supporting thousands of campaigns across 43 countries [^1][^3].
