## About

Yulu is a Bengaluru-based electric mobility startup that operates a fleet of shared electric two-wheelers used primarily for last-mile deliveries and short urban trips [^1][^2][^3]. The company powers more than 750,000 deliveries per day and logs roughly 1.6 million zero-emission kilometers per week across a fleet of about 50,000 vehicles [^1][^2]. Yulu maintains strategic partnerships with Bajaj Auto (which manufactures its Wynn EV) and Magna International (its battery-swapping partner) [^2][^3].

On August 12, 2026, Yulu announced a $93 million Series C round led by GEF Capital Partners, composed of $63 million in equity and $30 million in debt, valuing the company at approximately $170 million post-money [^1][^2][^3][^4]. Existing strategic investors Bajaj Auto and Magna International waived their pre-emptive rights so GEF could take its target stake [^1][^2]. Approximately $5.5 million of the equity component was used to purchase shares from seed investors whose funds were nearing the end of their investment life [^1].

The Series C is expected to be Yulu's final equity raise before an eventual public listing; future fleet expansion is planned to be financed primarily through debt and lease structures [^1][^2]. The company plans to grow its fleet to 200,000 electric two-wheelers over the next two years and launch faster models for new logistics use cases [^1][^2][^3].

## What Founders Say

Co-Founder & CEO Amit Gupta on Yulu's operating thesis: "Yulu was built on the conviction that hyper-local mobility requires purpose-built vehicles along with suitable infrastructure, deep technology integration, and uncompromising unit economics." [^4]

Gupta has also described Yulu's positioning as "the AWS of mobility," supplying the vehicle, battery-swapping, and software infrastructure that lets delivery workers operate independently [^1].
