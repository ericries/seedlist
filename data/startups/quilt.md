## About

Quilt is an Oakland, California-based climate hardware startup founded in 2022 by three former Google employees. The company designs residential heat pump systems with integrated software for room-by-room climate control, replacing traditional furnaces and ductwork [^1]. Nest co-founder Matt Rogers backed the company through Incite Ventures from the seed round forward [^1].
