## About

Array Labs is a Redwood City, California-based space and defense startup building a coordinated constellation of radar satellites designed to deliver continuously updated intelligence on terrain, infrastructure, and moving activity worldwide [^1][^2]. Unlike optical satellites, its synthetic aperture radar (SAR) systems can operate through cloud cover and at night. On 2026-07-28, Array announced a $21 million oversubscribed strategic investment round anchored by Mitsubishi Electric Corporation, with continued participation from existing investors Catapult Ventures, Kompas VC, and Y Combinator [^1][^2]. The financing accompanies a strategic partnership with Mitsubishi Electric to develop and commercialize satellite-based maritime and aircraft tracking services for defense and security organizations across Asia-Pacific [^1][^2]. Array Labs has raised approximately $56 million disclosed to date [^1].
