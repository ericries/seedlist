## About

Sodex Innovations is an Austrian construction- and mining-technology company that combines hardware-mounted sensors on heavy machinery with AI software to automatically map terrain, track material movement and generate real-time site-progress analytics [^1][^2][^3]. The company says it serves more than 100 customers in the U.S., Australia and Europe, positioning its platform as a data-first alternative to manual site surveys [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-13 Sodex Innovations announced a €4M round led by Capmont Technology, with participation from new investors Bloomhaus, Look AI Ventures and Superangels alongside follow-on backing from SOSV, OMA (via ProGlove's founders) and 12 Rounds Capital (Katharina Klausberger and Armin Strbac) [^1][^2][^3][^4]. Proceeds will fund team expansion, product development and international scaling [^1][^2].
