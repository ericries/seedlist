## About

District is an AI-powered platform enabling independent sellers and entrepreneurs to build community-driven online marketplaces without technical expertise [^1]. The platform supports live selling, transactions, and community engagement in niche markets [^1]. The company was founded in 2022 by three Snapchat alumni: Eddie Koai, Patrick Mandia, and Khoi Tran [^1]. The $14.7M seed round announced May 6, 2026 was co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and Kindred Ventures with participation from Greylock Partners, SV Angel, and 20VC; angel investors included Maria Raga (ex-Depop CEO), Gokul Rajaram, Peter Sellis, and Imran Khan [^1].
