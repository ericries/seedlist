## About

Adapter is an Austin, Texas-based AI infrastructure startup founded by Adam Ghetti (CEO) and David Bader (a computer science professor at the New Jersey Institute of Technology, formerly of Georgia Tech) [^1]. After three-and-a-half years in stealth, the company launched publicly in July 2026 with what it calls "Cognition as a Service" — an infrastructure layer designed to give people and enterprises control over how their personal and work data is used by AI agents and applications [^1]. The company has 17 employees at launch [^1].
