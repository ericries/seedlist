## About

JUPUS is a Cologne-based legaltech company that has built what it describes as Europe's first AI secretarial service specifically for law firms, automating administrative and operational tasks such as answering client calls, structuring inquiries, preparing cases and drafting legal documents [^1][^2]. The company reports that more than 2,000 lawyers use the platform and over 2,000 new legal cases are processed through its AI each day, and that it quadrupled annual recurring revenue and tripled headcount in 2025 [^1][^2].
