## About

GrailPay is a New York-based provider of a risk and data network for bank-to-bank business payments [^1][^2][^3]. The company offers a composable risk decisioning engine paired with a modern payments platform, designed to enable money to move instantly between agents and across digital assets [^1][^2]. The Series A funding will go toward data analytics and modeling research, go-to-market expansion into agentic and instant payments use cases, and expansion of the existing Payments Identity Network to deepen coverage for banking and banktech infrastructure, payment processors, embedded fintech platforms, AR/AP systems, and risk platforms [^1][^2]. The round brings GrailPay's total funding to date to $17.2 million [^1].
