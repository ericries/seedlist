## About

Relativity Networks develops hollow-core optical fiber technology designed to transmit data significantly faster than conventional fiber, targeting data-center and hyperscaler deployments for AI workloads [^1][^2][^3]. Alongside its funding, the company disclosed a $40 million follow-on order from an unnamed hyperscaler customer [^2][^3].
