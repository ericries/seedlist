## About

Agave is a San Francisco-based AI platform for construction financials that integrates with contractors' existing ERP and project management systems to automate accounting and back-office workflows [^1][^2]. The company reports working with more than 500 general and specialty contractors and being used across more than 80,000 construction projects in the U.S. and Canada, representing more than $100 billion in project volume [^1][^2]. Agave was founded in late 2021 by Tom Reno, John Zucchi, and Pooria Azimi and has been profitable for over two years, tripling revenue year-over-year [^1][^2].
