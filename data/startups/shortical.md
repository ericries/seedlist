## About

Shortical is a Tel Aviv-based mobile-first microdrama platform that produces original short-form scripted series designed for mobile consumption, combining AI-driven video production, data science, and storytelling [^1][^2][^3]. The company reports more than 20 million episodes of its shows are watched every month in the United States, and its data-driven, AI-first approach has propelled Shortical into the top 10 highest-grossing apps in its category in the U.S. within roughly a year of launch [^1][^3]. Shortical is led by CEO and Co-Founder Guy Shimoni [^1][^2][^3].
