## About

Pie is an AI-powered growth platform for small and local businesses, helping them appear in AI search results, manage growth campaigns, and respond to inbound calls through an AI front desk [^1][^2]. The company is betting that small businesses now need help showing up in generative AI search the way they once needed help showing up on Google search [^1][^2]. Pie was founded by Syed Ali and Akhil Mantiprigada, both formerly engineers at Toast, where they recognized the gap while building AI products for restaurant operators [^1][^2]. On June 30, 2026, Pie emerged from stealth with $19.5 million in Series A funding led by Lightspeed Venture Partners, bringing total funding to $23.7 million [^1][^2].
