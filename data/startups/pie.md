## About

Pie is an AI-powered growth platform for small and local businesses, helping them appear in AI search results, manage growth campaigns, and respond to inbound calls through an AI front desk [^1][^2][^3]. The company is betting that small businesses now need help showing up in generative AI search the way they once needed help showing up on Google search [^1][^2][^3]. Pie was founded by Syed Ali (CEO) and Akhil Mantripragada (CTO), both former Square and Toast operators who most recently spun up new products inside Toast's New Venture Labs before founding Pie [^1][^2][^3]. On June 30, 2026, Pie emerged from stealth with $19.5 million in Series A funding led by Lightspeed Venture Partners, bringing total funding to $23.7 million [^1][^2][^3].
