## About

Beren Therapeutics P.B.C. is a Thousand Oaks, California-based public benefit company developing cyclodextrin-based therapeutics for cholesterol trafficking disorders [^1][^2]. The company is the parent of Mandos LLC and is led by Founder and CEO Jason Camm [^1]. Its lead candidate is adrabetadex for infantile-onset Niemann-Pick disease Type C (I-NPC), a rare and ultimately fatal lysosomal storage disorder; the FDA accepted Beren's New Drug Application for adrabetadex in February 2026 under Priority Review, with a target action decision on November 17, 2026 [^1][^3].

The $300M financing comprises a $135M equity round led by Wellington Partners, JIC Venture Growth Investments, Founders Fund, Narya Capital, and Eisai Co., alongside up to $165M in a strategic financing facility from Hercules Capital (comprising $110M senior-secured debt and $55M royalty financing) [^1][^2]. Capital supports preparation for potential commercialization of adrabetadex and long-term care initiatives for the rare disease community [^1].
