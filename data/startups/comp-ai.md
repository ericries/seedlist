## About

Comp AI is an open-source, AI-driven security & compliance platform that automates evidence collection, policy generation, and continuous monitoring across frameworks including SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, GDPR and FedRAMP [^2]. The platform advertises 580+ integrations, a 24/7 device monitoring agent, automated penetration testing, cloud infrastructure scanning, and live trust-center portals; the company reports it now serves over 1,000 companies from startups to enterprise, with auditable code on GitHub as a differentiator [^2]. On 2026-09-17, TechCrunch reported that Comp AI raised a $34M Series A led by Roo Capital and Grand Ventures, bringing total funding to $37.5M [^1]. The company has offices in Aventura, Florida and New York, NY [^2].
