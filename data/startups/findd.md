## About

Findd is an AI-native workforce management platform purpose-built for frontline workers, based in Provo, Utah [^1]. The company's core product centers on "DiDi," a proprietary AI agent layer that lets workers and managers interact with the software in natural language across SMS, voice, and email [^1] [^2]. Findd targets facility services and specialty contractor end-markets where hourly, distributed labor has historically been underserved by legacy HR software [^1].
