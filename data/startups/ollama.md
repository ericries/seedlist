## About

Ollama is an open-source developer platform for running large language models locally on personal computers, founded in 2023 by Jeffrey Morgan and Michael Chiang [^1][^2]. The company describes its mission as extending the "personal computer moment" to AI — making open models runnable on developer hardware with a focus on ownership, affordability, and privacy [^2]. As of July 2026, Ollama says the platform serves 8.9 million monthly active developers, has been starred ~176,000 times on GitHub, and is used by 85% of Fortune 500 companies [^1][^2]. The company remains lean, with roughly 14 employees [^1].

On 2026-07-09 Ollama announced a $65M Series B round led by Theory Ventures, bringing total funding to $88M across all rounds [^1][^2]. Existing investor Benchmark (which led the $15M Series A in 2024) participated, and 8VC also joined [^1][^2]. Ollama's own announcement additionally names Solomon Hykes (Docker founder / Dagger CEO), Aaron Katz (ClickHouse CEO), Spencer Kimball (Cockroach Labs co-founder), Quinn Slack (Amp CEO), Marianna Tessel, and Michael Montano as participating angels, and lists Y Combinator, Garage Capital, Pace Capital, 49 Palms, and GTMFund among backers to date [^2].
