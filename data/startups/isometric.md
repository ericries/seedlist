## About

Isometric is a London-based developer of an AI-powered industrial certification and compliance validation platform [^1]. Founded in 2022 by Eamon Jubbawy, who previously co-founded identity verification company Onfido (acquired by Entrust in 2024 for a reported $650M), the company first established itself as a carbon removal registry before expanding into the broader industrial certification market [^2].

Isometric's flagship product, Certify, is described as an agentic AI platform that autonomously ingests, parses, and cross-checks multi-modal data points — including satellite imagery, IoT sensor feeds, supply chain manifests, and lab results — to validate industrial sustainability, regulatory, and safety claims [^3]. The company claims Certify reduces industrial certification timelines from approximately 12 months to a matter of hours [^1].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |

| 2026-06-22 | Series A | $40M | AVP (AXA Venture Partners) | Lowercarbon Capital, Plural, John Doerr, Walter Kortschak [^1][^2][^3][^4][^5] |

AVP — formerly AXA Venture Partners, now independent following a 2024–2025 management buyout — led the round with Managing Partner François Robinet as the named partner [^4]. The round is Isometric's first publicly disclosed Series A; the company had previously operated primarily as a carbon-removal registry serving enterprise buyers including Microsoft, Anglo American, JPMorgan Chase, and Boeing [^4][^5].

## What Investors Say

François Robinet, AVP Managing Partner: "Certification has always forced a choice between speed and rigor. Isometric has eliminated that compromise" [^4].

## What Founders Say

Founder and CEO Eamon Jubbawy on the certification industry: "the certification industry faced a trade off between speed and rigour" — adding that industrial companies "can get both" through Isometric's platform [^2].
