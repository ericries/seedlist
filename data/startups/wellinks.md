## About

Wellinks is a New Haven, Connecticut-based cardiopulmonary care company that combines virtual chronic disease management with remote patient monitoring through its FDA-cleared Spire Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) System [^1][^2]. The platform uses proprietary Health Tags for passive monitoring aimed at identifying early physiologic changes associated with clinical deterioration — targeting detection roughly a week before symptom onset in patients with chronic respiratory disease [^1][^2]. On 2026-08-04 Wellinks announced the first close of a $10M Series B led by UMass Memorial Health alongside inside investors, building on a multiyear commercial relationship [^1][^3].
