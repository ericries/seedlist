## About

Furo (originally Lumera Energy, rebranded May 2026) is a Munich-based software platform for optimizing commercial and industrial battery storage systems, forecasting electricity prices and weather up to 48 hours ahead to enable real-time storage operations and monetize unused capacity in energy trading [^1]. The company claims customers can reduce electricity costs by up to 40%, and reports more than 800 companies using the platform across 6,000+ sites in Germany and Europe [^1]. On 2026-09-11, Furo announced a €3.44M ($4M) round led by TQ Ventures with participation from Sandberg Bernthal Venture Partners, Neo, and CDTM Venture Capital [^1].
