## About

Asuene is a Tokyo-based enterprise sustainability platform providing GHG measurement, reduction, and reporting software to more than 50,000 clients worldwide [^1][^2]. The company operates three products: its flagship ASUENE carbon accounting platform, NZero (an AI energy optimization platform acquired from a U.S. startup in 2025), and Carbon EX (a carbon credit trading platform) [^1]. Asuene has completed eight acquisitions to date, most recently UK-based supply-chain carbon platform Secaro, announced concurrently with its Series D [^3].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|------|-------|--------|------|--------------|
| 2026-07-23 | Series D | ¥13.5B (~$87M) | Decarbonization Partners | Twin Towers Ventures (Petronas), Sparks Asset Management, Incubate Fund, Environmental Energy Investment, Daikin Industries, RICOH Innovation Fund, GLIN Impact Capital, Hirotaka Sugita, SMBC (debt) [^1][^2][^4] |

The Series D consisted of ¥6.8B in new primary equity, ¥3.7B in secondary transactions covering employee stock options, and debt financing from Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation (SMBC) — totaling approximately ¥13.5 billion (~$87M) [^4]. The financing marked Decarbonization Partners' first investment in a Japanese company; the fund is a joint venture between BlackRock and Temasek [^1][^2][^4]. Cumulative funding to date is approximately ¥25 billion [^4]. Proceeds will support M&A, business expansion, and AI/product development [^1][^4].
