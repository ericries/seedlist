## About

Mantle8 is a Grenoble, France-based deep-tech startup pioneering natural (geologic) hydrogen exploration and drilling, with a proprietary technology platform for identifying commercial-scale hydrogen systems [^1][^2]. The €31M Series A announced May 13, 2026 was led by Sandwater with participation from Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Ecotechnologies 2 fund (managed by Bpifrance on behalf of the French government), IP Group, Wind Capital, and Calderion (an Audacia-backed investment platform); the round brings total funding to €37M ($44M) [^1][^2]. Capital will fund the world's most advanced natural hydrogen exploration and drilling campaign over the next two years [^1].
