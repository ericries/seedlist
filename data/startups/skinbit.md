## About

SkinBit is an AI-driven skin-cancer screening company building a proprietary imaging hardware platform combined with a longitudinal dermatology data platform, aimed at making 20-minute full-body screenings widely available in clinics across the United States [^1]. The company's model creates historical imaging records so physicians can make objective comparisons across visits rather than relying on visual examinations and patient recall [^1]. As of the funding announcement, SkinBit reported a waitlist exceeding 5,000 prospective users generated without paid marketing and was in discussions for more than 20 clinical locations [^1].
