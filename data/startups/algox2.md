## About

AlgoX2 is a New York-based data streaming platform that unifies ingestion, ordering, fan-out, transformation, and durable storage, delivering ultra-low latency and high throughput for AI-native environments [^1][^2]. The team previously built the trading infrastructure at NYSE, and the platform supports Kafka, NATS, Redis, and WebSockets natively; Polymarket uses AlgoX2 for exchange infrastructure [^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by Alexei Lebedev, Vladimir Parizhsky, and George Levin [^1].
