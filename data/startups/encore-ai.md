## About

Encore AI (originally founded as Insait IO in 2022) is an enterprise AI platform that analyzes customer conversations — calls, emails, and text messages — alongside CRM data to identify sales and support techniques that drive successful outcomes, then trains AI agents to replicate those approaches [^1]. The platform can deploy agents to work autonomously or assist human employees directly, and reportedly serves more than 40 enterprise customers, primarily financial institutions [^1]. On 2026-07-29, Encore AI announced a $30 million Series A led by Team8 with participation from Planven, Lukatz, Garage, and several unnamed banks and insurers; the company reports ARR has grown roughly 5x since its seed round approximately 18 months earlier [^1].
