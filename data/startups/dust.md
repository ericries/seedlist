## About

Dust is a Paris-based "multiplayer" agentic AI platform for the enterprise, allowing teams to collaboratively build, share, and operate AI agents across an organization [^1][^2][^3]. The platform is used by 3,000+ organizations with 300,000+ AI agents deployed [^1][^2]. The $40M Series B announced May 18, 2026 was co-led by Abstract and Sequoia Capital with participation from Snowflake Ventures and Datadog; total funding now exceeds $60M [^1][^2][^3].
