## About

Utilidata is an Ann Arbor, Michigan-based energy-AI infrastructure company whose power-orchestration platform Karman — developed in partnership with NVIDIA — unlocks stranded grid capacity with real-time visibility and control of power utilization, enabling more data-center compute to be deployed against existing grid resources [^1][^2][^3]. The $40M Series C extension announced May 28, 2026 was led by existing investor Renown Capital Partners with participation from Keyframe Capital and completes Utilidata's $100M Series C, which began with a $60.3M first close in April 2025 led by Renown with Keyframe, Quanta Services, and NVIDIA participating [^1][^2][^3].
