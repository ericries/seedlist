## About

Infrawatch is a London-based cybersecurity startup building an "Internet Infrastructure Intelligence Layer" to help organisations identify and monitor the infrastructure underpinning cyberattacks, phishing campaigns, fraud, and online abuse [^1][^2]. The platform classifies tens of billions of events each day across IP addresses, domains, proxies, VPNs, and command-and-control servers [^2]. Founder Lloyd Davies and his team include former employees of CrowdStrike, Recorded Future, and Intel 471 [^2]. The May 20, 2026 £2.2M pre-seed round was co-led by Outward VC and Triple Point Ventures with participation from Portfolio Ventures and fintech and cyber angel investors; proceeds will fund engineering and research hiring, platform development ahead of a public launch later this year, early enterprise deployments, and US expansion [^1][^2].
