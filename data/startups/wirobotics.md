## About

WIRobotics is a Seoul-based robotics company founded in 2021 that develops wearable and humanoid robotic systems based on human movement data [^1]. The company's flagship wearable walking-assist product, WIM, has surpassed 3,000 cumulative units sold and is sold in Europe, China, Türkiye, and Japan [^1]. Revenue grew from KRW 560 million in 2023 to KRW 1.3 billion in 2024 and KRW 2.79 billion in 2025, with Q1 2026 revenue already exceeding full-year 2024 [^1]. WIRobotics is also developing "ALLEX," a humanoid robotics platform, and was selected for NVIDIA's "Physical AI Fellowship" global robotics and AI development initiative [^1].
