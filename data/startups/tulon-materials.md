## About

Tulon Materials is a deep-tech specialty chemicals company that develops sustainable industrial-grade resins from plastic waste, using AI-assisted chemical simulation and materials validation to compress product development timelines. The company is DPIIT-recognized and headquartered in Navi Mumbai with resin manufacturing in Visakhapatnam [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2022 by Asesh Sarkar, Dr. Rabindranath Mandal, and Harsh Bhatt (CEO), the founding team brings a combined eight decades of experience in the chemical industry [^2][^3]. Tulon targets paints and coatings, printing inks, and adhesives markets, and is positioning its "Made in India" specialty chemicals for the European Union, with multiple products under technical validation by large multinational enterprises [^2][^3].
