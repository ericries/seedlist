## About

Prolo is a London-based AI procurement platform for small and medium-sized construction contractors, founded in 2024 by James Morris-Manuel, previously founder of PropTech company Virtual Walkthrough (acquired by Matterport) [^1][^2]. The service lets site managers place orders via WhatsApp, email or phone; Prolo combines Large Language Models, natural-language processing and voice transcription with a human trade desk to source bulk materials and specialist plant hire from a network of over 185 suppliers at rates historically reserved for Tier 1 contractors [^1][^2]. The oversubscribed seed round was led by Triple Point Ventures with participation from Anamcara Capital, Concrete VC, Foundation Ventures, Haatch, Koro Capital, Love Ventures and Portfolio Ventures [^1][^2].
