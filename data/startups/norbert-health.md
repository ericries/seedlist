## About

Norbert Health is a New York–based healthtech company building AI-powered robotic assistants that help nursing staff capture patient vital signs and health data at the bedside [^1][^2]. The company positions its technology as a "robotic nursing assistant" designed to relieve documentation and monitoring burden in hospitals and long-term care facilities [^1].

On September 1, 2026, Norbert Health announced a $14 million Series A led by Cardinal Capital Group, with participation from EXOR N.V., Careit, and angel investors including Alexis Le-Quoc (co-founder of Datadog), Olivier Pomel (co-founder & CEO of Datadog), Saeju Jeong (co-founder of Noom), and Thomas Clozel (co-founder & CEO of Owkin) [^1][^2].
