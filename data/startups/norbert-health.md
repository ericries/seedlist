## About

Norbert Health is a Brooklyn, NY–based physical AI company (with an additional office in Paris, France) building autonomous robotic nursing assistants that measure patient vital signs contactlessly, assess mobility, and share clinical insights with care teams in skilled nursing facilities and hospitals [^1][^3]. The company describes itself as "the AI module for autonomous care in the real world," combining a physical AI control system with contactless sensing and clinical protocol execution [^1][^3]. Its bedside robot, which uses a blue teddy-bear avatar to engage patients, has been deployed in skilled nursing facilities monitoring hundreds of patients daily; the company reports patient engagement rising from 34% to 48% after non-verbal communication upgrades, and monitoring compliance improving from 55% to 82% [^3]. Norbert graduated from NYU Stern's Endless Frontier Labs 2025-26 cohort (Digital Health track) [^3].

On September 1, 2026, Norbert Health announced a $14 million Series A led by Cardinal Capital Group, with participation from EXOR N.V., Careit, and angel investors including Alexis Le-Quoc (co-founder of Datadog), Olivier Pomel (co-founder & CEO of Datadog), Saeju Jeong (co-founder of Noom), and Thomas Clozel (co-founder & CEO of Owkin) [^1][^2]. The round brings the company's total funding to approximately $19M [^1].
