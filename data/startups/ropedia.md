## About

Ropedia is a Singapore-based data infrastructure company building the training data pipeline for physical AI and robotics [^1]. The company also maintains an office in Mountain View, California [^1]. Its flagship product HOMIE is a wearable, head-mounted device that simultaneously records first-person video, audio, depth, hand movements, gaze, body motion and camera position, with each data stream timestamped so developers can correlate perception with movement in real time [^2]. Ropedia operates a closed-loop pipeline combining data capture, quality assurance, annotation, and dataset preparation for robotics training [^1].

Co-founder and CEO Zhaoxi Chen has a background in 3D computer vision and multimodal AI. Co-founder Fangzhou Hong was previously a researcher at Meta working on egocentric multimodal intelligence. Chief Scientist Ziwei Liu is an Associate Professor at Nanyang Technological University [^1].
