## About

Ridgeline is an AI-native, cloud platform serving the investment management industry, offering an integrated front-to-back system covering trading, portfolio management, accounting, and operations [^1][^2]. The company was founded by Dave Duffield, whose prior enterprise software companies PeopleSoft and Workday both grew into multi-billion-dollar businesses [^1][^2]. Ridgeline says its September 2026 round values it at approximately $1.425 billion — Duffield's third billion-dollar enterprise software company [^1][^2].
