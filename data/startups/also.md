## About

Also is a Palo Alto-based company spun out of Rivian that develops autonomous delivery vehicles [^1]. The company has partnered with DoorDash in a multi-year commercial agreement to develop and deploy autonomous last-mile delivery vehicles at scale [^1][^2]. The Series C round values the company at $1 billion [^2].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|------|-------|--------|------|--------------|
| 2026-08-19 | Series D | $150M | Prysm Capital | Eclipse, Greenoaks, MVP Ventures [^3][^4] |
| 2026-03-31 | Series C | $200M | Greenoaks | DoorDash, Prysm Capital [^1][^2] |

DoorDash Co-Founder Stanley Tang joined Also's board as an observer as part of the March 2026 Series C [^1]. The August 2026 Series D brings total funding to approximately $455 million and supports Also's expansion beyond pedal-assist e-bikes and commercial cargo quads into autonomous delivery vehicles [^3].
