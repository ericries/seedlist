## About

Netris develops network automation software that helps AI neocloud operators accelerate time-to-market by automating data center setup, configuration, and multi-tenant operations through hardware-accelerated networking [^1]. The company's platform operates across more than 35 GPU clusters globally and serves customers including Lightning AI, Foxconn, and Visionbay [^1].
