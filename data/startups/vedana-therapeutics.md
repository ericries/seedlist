## About

Vedana Therapeutics is a Seattle-based biotech company developing next-generation antibody therapies for migraine prevention [^1][^2]. The company emerged from stealth on June 17, 2026 with $46 million in Series A financing [^1]. Vedana's lead program is a subcutaneously delivered next-generation anti-PACAP antibody for migraine prevention, and its second program targets both PACAP and CGRP pathways [^2][^3]. The company is led by co-founder and CEO Anurag Agrawal, Ph.D., and co-founder and Chief Scientific Officer Leon Garcia, Ph.D., with executive chair Rob Lenz, M.D., Ph.D., and Marcelo Bigal, M.D., Ph.D. on the board [^1].
