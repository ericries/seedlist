## About

Kapture CX is a Bengaluru-based verticalized agentic AI platform for enterprise customer experience and CRM operations [^1][^2]. The company describes its product as a full-stack agentic operating system that consolidates the AI model layer, agent layer, and user interface into a single platform so enterprises can deploy AI across complex workflows without stitching together multiple third-party tools [^1][^3]. Kapture reports it serves more than 1,000 enterprises across 18 countries, with customers including Bajaj Finance, Tata Group, and Reliance Group companies [^1][^3]. The company says it has been profitable since its 2023 Series A and grown revenue roughly 4x since then [^1][^3].

On 2026-07-09, Kapture CX announced a $10M Pre-Series B round led by Bajaj Finserv Ventures (BFSV), with participation from Cactus Venture Partners and India Alternatives [^1][^2][^3]. The company said proceeds will be used to accelerate global expansion and continued investment in product development [^1][^2]. BFSV described the investment as its first bet in the AI landscape [^2].
