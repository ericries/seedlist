## About

BirdsView is a Leipzig-based AI martech company that builds Avys, an autonomous email marketing agent for e-commerce SMEs [^1][^2]. Avys reads a retailer's product catalog, decides what to send each customer and when, and writes personalized emails in the merchant's brand voice, replacing manually rule-based CRM workflows [^1]. The company reports its product spans 1.2 million labeled products across 60 industries and processes roughly 52 million email interactions a month, with brand customers seeing meaningfully higher click and revenue per email [^1]. BirdsView was founded in 2023 by data scientists Tim Kuesters and Ozan Brochwitz-Tuerker [^1][^2].
