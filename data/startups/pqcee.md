## About

pQCee is a Singapore-based cybersecurity company building post-quantum cryptography solutions designed to protect enterprise data from "harvest-now-decrypt-later" attacks that anticipate future quantum decryption capabilities [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by Dr. Teik Guan Tan, the company provides crypto-agility deployment tooling that lets enterprises replace vulnerable public-key infrastructure with quantum-safe alternatives [^1][^3].
