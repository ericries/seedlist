## About

Arkenstone Defense is a Menlo Park–based operations and compliance platform that helps commercial technology companies sell to the U.S. Department of Defense and other federal agencies [^1][^2][^3]. The company emerged from stealth on 2026-07-07 with a $35M seed round and offers two products — Foundation, which handles FedRAMP, ATO and CMMC compliance and contract management, and Cohort, which provides payroll, benefits, HR and workforce operations tailored to cleared personnel [^2][^3]. Arkenstone was co-founded by Peter Dixon (previously founder of defense software company Second Front Systems), William Treseder and Stanford PhD Rachel Olney, and operates with roughly 60 employees and three dozen initial customers [^2][^3]. The company also acquired long-running compliance software firm Anitian as part of building the platform [^3].
