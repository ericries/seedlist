## About

Turbo Law is a US-based legal technology startup that builds an AI-powered litigation case management platform for defense law firms and insurers handling complex casework such as medical malpractice, insurance disputes, and tort cases [^1][^2][^3]. The company says its software transforms large volumes of legal records into structured, real-time case insights. Turbo Law was founded in 2025 by CEO Jay Sarmaz and CTO Ozgur Bora Gevrek [^2][^3].

On June 18, 2026, Turbo Law announced a $3.8 million seed round to expand its engineering, product, customer success, and go-to-market teams [^1][^2][^3].
