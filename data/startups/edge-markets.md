## About

EDGE Markets is a New York-based financial services company that builds banking and payment infrastructure for the gaming, crypto, and prediction markets ecosystem [^1]. Founded in 2020 by Seni Thomas, the company is launching two new products with its Series A: EDGE Pro, high-throughput deposit accounts for market makers that enable real-time deposits into CFTC-regulated exchanges, and EDGE Connect, a purpose-built Real Time Payments (RTP) rail allowing operators to push funds into user accounts 24/7/365 [^1][^2].
