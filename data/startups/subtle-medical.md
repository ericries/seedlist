## About

Subtle Medical is a Menlo Park-based AI medical imaging startup that develops vendor-neutral software to improve image quality and accelerate scans across MRI, PET, and CT workflows on existing imaging systems, helping providers increase throughput without buying new hardware [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by Enhao Gong, PhD and Greg Zaharchuk, MD, PhD [^1].

Alongside the Series C announcement, Subtle Medical appointed medical imaging executive Ohad Arazi (formerly of Change Healthcare, Zebra Medical Vision, and Clarius Mobile Health) as Chief Executive Officer; co-founder Enhao Gong transitioned to Chief Science Officer [^1][^2].
