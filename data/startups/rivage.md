## About

Rivage is a Paris-based proptech startup founded in 2025 that builds AI-powered rental management software for French real estate agencies [^1]. The platform automates billing, service-charge calls, bank reconciliation, and client communications, and ships with an open API plus model-context protocol connections so agencies can integrate their existing tools with LLMs like Claude or ChatGPT rather than replace their stack [^1]. Rivage was founded by Alex Chauvin (CEO), Antoine Barranco (COO), and Grégory Prève (CTPO), and reports current customers in the "hundreds of agencies" with €200K ARR and a team of 11 growing to a targeted 22 by end-2026 and about 35 by end-2027 [^1].
