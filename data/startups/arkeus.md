## About

Arkeus is a Sydney, Australia-based defense technology company building AI-powered sensing systems that serve as the "eyes and brains" of autonomous platforms operating in contested military environments [^1][^2][^3]. The technology is already deployed with the U.S. Department of War and Australian Department of Defence and integrated with major drone manufacturers including AeroVironment, Textron, Tekever, and Boeing subsidiary Insitu [^1][^2]. The $18M Series A announced May 15, 2026 was led by QIC Ventures with participation from new investors R+VC, Folklore Ventures, and DYNE Ventures, alongside existing investors Main Sequence Ventures, Salus Ventures, and Beaten Zone; capital will scale manufacturing across the US, Australia, and Europe [^1][^2][^3].
