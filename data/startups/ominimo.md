## About

Ominimo is a Budapest, Hungary-based InsurTech founded in 2024 by Dusan Komar, Dennis Weinbender, and Laslo Horvath, specializing in AI-powered motor insurance across European markets using a data-heavy pricing model [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-27, the company announced a €20.1 million Series B led by the venture capital arm of the European Bank for Reconstruction and Development (EBRD), reaching a €1.4 billion / $1.6 billion valuation — making the Serbian-Hungarian company the Balkans' newest insurtech unicorn just two years after launch [^1][^2][^3]. Cumulative external funding stands at approximately €30 million, following a Series A that included Zurich Insurance Group [^2]. Ominimo operates as a managing general agent (MGA) rather than taking insurance risk onto its own balance sheet, with underwriting partnerships including Signal Iduna and DA Direkt [^3]. The company reports approximately one million customers and annualized gross written premium of ~€307 million (up from €26.3 million in 2024), with roughly 130 employees across Hungary, Poland, the Netherlands, and Sweden — with expansion planned into Belgium, Romania, Spain, Italy, France, and the US by 2027 [^3].
