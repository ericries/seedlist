## About

Vikk AI is a Long Beach, California-based AI-powered legal discovery platform enabling consumers to ask legal questions in plain language, analyze legal documents, generate basic legal documents, and identify attorneys relevant to their legal needs [^1][^2][^3]. The platform also operates a sponsored lawyer advertising marketplace designed for the AI-first search era [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-21, Vikk AI announced $4.2 million in combined pre-seed and seed financing, consisting of a $700,000 pre-seed round and a $3.5 million seed round [^1][^2][^3]. Participating investors included Cooper Collins (Managing Member of MagnaSci Ventures), tax attorney Ronson Shamoun, injury attorney Joe Samo, healthcare executive Brad Jowell, and other individual investors [^1][^2]. Vikk AI was co-founded by Walid Romaya, who serves as CEO [^1][^2]. The capital will be used to accelerate product development, expand the platform, and scale a new category of sponsored lawyer advertising for the AI era [^1][^2].
