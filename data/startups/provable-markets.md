## About

Provable Markets is a fintech platform that provides securities lending and equity finance trading infrastructure through an SEC-registered alternative trading system, founded in 2020 by Matthew Cohen and Ruben de Vries [^1]. The company operates in the U.S. capital-markets infrastructure space, offering technology for participants in the securities lending market [^1].
