## About

BrandRank.AI is a Cincinnati, Ohio-based provider of Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) tools that help companies measure, protect, and grow brand trust within AI-driven search platforms including ChatGPT and Claude [^1][^2]. The company was co-founded by Pete Blackshaw and Hank Hudepohl and has raised $4.2M in total equity funding to date [^2].
