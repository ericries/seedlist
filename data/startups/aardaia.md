## About

Aardaia is a Wageningen, Netherlands-based agricultural biotechnology startup that domesticates wild plants into commercial crops using genomic prediction combined with in-depth phenotyping [^1][^2]. Its flagship candidate is the aardaker (Lathyrus tuberosus), a protein-rich tuber the company markets as a "protein potato" that pairs the productivity of a root crop with the nitrogen-fixing biology of a legume [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 [^1].
