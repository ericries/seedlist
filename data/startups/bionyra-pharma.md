## About

Bionyra Pharma is a France-based clinical-stage biotech launched in 2026 to develop next-generation biologics for immune-mediated inflammatory diseases [^1]. The company was co-founded by Frédéric Marrache, formerly head of immunology R&D at Sanofi, together with Sofinnova Partners [^2]. Its pipeline is built around BYN-002 and additional licensed monoclonal and bispecific antibody assets [^2].
