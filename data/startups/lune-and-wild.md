## About

Lune & Wild is a London-based baby and children's food brand founded in 2021 by cousins Lara Rodgers and Nadia Simonds [^1][^2]. The company offers chef-crafted recipes paired with specialist paediatric nutrition and sourced ingredients [^1]. The startup reports doubling revenue every year since launch and selling over one million meals in the trailing 12 months prior to the funding announcement [^1][^2].
