## About

Fireworks AI is a Redwood City-based enterprise AI inference platform that helps companies run and customize open-source and specialized generative AI models. Founded in 2022 by former Meta PyTorch leader Lin Qiao and other Meta engineers, Fireworks positions itself as an infrastructure layer for "specialized intelligence" — helping enterprises fine-tune and deploy models trained on their own data [^1] [^2].
