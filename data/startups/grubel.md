## About

Grubel is a German AI research lab building systems that adapt to individual complex legal cases, automating what it calls the "AI specialisation loop": identifying relevant information, curating it into training data and task environments, adapting a model to the specific matter, and testing whether the model's output meets required standards [^1][^2]. On 2026-09-10, Grubel announced a €3M Pre-Seed round led by Point Nine with participation from a group of AI researchers and legal-tech operators including Jeff Dean (Google), Chris Ré (Stanford / Together AI), Ion Stoica (UC Berkeley / Databricks), Gabe Pereyra (Harvey co-founder), and Winston Weinberg (Harvey co-founder) [^1].

**Reinhard Heckel, Co-Founder, quoted in EU-Startups:**
