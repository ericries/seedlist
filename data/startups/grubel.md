## About

Grubel is a German AI research lab building matter-specific AI systems for complex legal work, framed by the company as "Every matter builds its own AI, one that knows its world and does the best possible work within it" [^2]. The team automates what it calls the "AI specialisation loop": identifying relevant information, curating it into training data and task environments, adapting a model to the specific matter, and testing whether the model's output meets required standards [^1][^2]. Co-founder Moritz Hardt is a Director at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems and a co-inventor of test-time training; co-founder Reinhard Heckel is a Professor at TU Munich and a co-creator of OpenThoughts and DataComp [^2]. On 2026-09-10, Grubel announced a €3M Pre-Seed round led by Point Nine with participation from a group of AI researchers and legal-tech operators including Jeff Dean (Google), Chris Ré (Stanford / Together AI), Ion Stoica (UC Berkeley / Databricks), Gabe Pereyra (Harvey co-founder), and Winston Weinberg (Harvey co-founder) [^1][^2].

## What Investors Say

**Louis Coppey (Partner, Point Nine), quoted in EU-Startups:**

> "Over the past two to three years, we've seen many legal AI companies emerge, but few are pushing the boundaries of AI research in the legal field as ambitiously as Reinhard, Moritz and the Grubel team." [^1]

## What Founders Say

**Reinhard Heckel, Co-Founder, quoted in EU-Startups:**

> "Complex legal matters do not come with ready-made training data, task environments, or clear tests of success. We are automating the AI specialisation loop that identifies the right information, curates it into training data and environments, adapts the system to the matter, and tests whether its work meets the required standard." [^1]
