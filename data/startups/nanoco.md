## About

NanoCo is the company behind NanoClaw, a security-focused alternative to OpenClaw that runs AI coding agents in sandboxed containers rather than directly on a user's computer with full access to local services and credentials [^1]. Co-founders Gavriel Cohen and Lazer Cohen turned down acquisition offers of approximately $20 million to instead raise a $12 million Seed round and build the product into an independent company [^1].
