## About

Darkhive is a San Antonio-based defense tech startup developing autonomous robotics and unmanned aircraft systems with secure software delivery for defense and public safety customers [^1][^2]. In March 2026, Darkhive was selected by the Pentagon for a $49.7M APFIT program—the largest contract award in that program's history [^1]. The $30M Series B announced May 7, 2026 was led by RTX Ventures with participation from new investors Draper Associates and Bison Capital, alongside existing backers 1011 Ventures, Crosslink Capital, Alamo Angels, and Stellar Ventures [^1][^2].
