## About

HaloBraid is a hair-tech startup building a robotic braiding assistant for professional stylists [^1]. A stylist starts the braiding and then hands off the process to HaloBraid, which finishes the rest of the braid in seconds [^1]. The device is designed to be gentle on the hair and works for both knotless and box braids [^1]. A single braiding session can last six to twelve hours, limiting stylists to one or two clients per day; HaloBraid's device aims to automate the most labor-intensive portion of each braid, completing it roughly five times faster than a human hand [^1]. The product is slated to launch later in 2026 [^1]. Founder Yinka Ogunbiyi holds an MS in engineering and an MBA from Harvard [^1].
