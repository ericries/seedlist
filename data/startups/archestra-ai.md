## About

Archestra.AI is a London-based startup building an open-source platform that lets enterprises securely connect sensitive data to AI agents [^1]. The company was founded in 2025 by Matvey Kukuy [^1]. Its platform sits between corporate data systems and AI agents, providing guardrails so organizations can deploy agents without leaking confidential information [^1].
