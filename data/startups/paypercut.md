## About

Paypercut is a Sofia, Bulgaria-based fintech building a unified payments platform for online merchants across Central and Eastern Europe (CEE) [^1]. The company started as a buy-now-pay-later aggregator and has evolved into a full-stack payments platform serving more than 200 online merchants across eight CEE markets through a single integration [^2]. Paypercut's founding team — Stoil Vasilev (CEO), Emil Savov, and Gareth Walsh — includes former SumUp and Payhawk executives [^3].

The company has submitted its own EMI application under the Central Bank of Ireland, with authorisation expected in Q4 2026, a milestone that will enable Paypercut to hold customer balances directly [^1].
