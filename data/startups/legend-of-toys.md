## About

Legend of Toys is an India-based premium toy brand founded in 2024 by ISB alumni Afshaan Siddiqui and Vinay Jaisingh [^1]. The company targets the mass-premium and remote-control (RC) toy market with home-grown manufacturing and a D2C model [^1][^2]. Legend of Toys reports ₹30 Cr ARR within 18 months of launch, with month-on-month growth of around 20% [^1].
