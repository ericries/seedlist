## About

Unplugs Hearing is a consumer hardware company that makes mechanical earplugs with a transparency mode, allowing wearers to toggle between full hearing protection and ambient sound without electronics [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by Tom Worcester and previously ran a Kickstarter campaign for its click-style mechanical earplugs before scaling into direct-to-consumer retail [^2][^3]. On 2026-08-04, Unplugs Hearing reported a $3.4 million equity close per a SEC filing, one tranche of a $6.6 million total offering that included 43 investors [^1].
