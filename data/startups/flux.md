## About

Flux is a Boston-based code-first engineering intelligence platform that analyzes source code directly to give engineering leaders ground-truth visibility into the work happening across complex, AI-accelerated codebases [^1][^2]. Rather than relying on tickets and dashboards, the platform reveals what teams are actually doing, surfaces risk and technical debt, and connects engineering activity to business outcomes including cost, quality, and security [^1][^3].

The company was founded by CEO Ted Julian [^2][^3]. Flux plans to use the new capital to accelerate product development and go-to-market efforts as AI reshapes software development, including deepening AI-powered analysis of complex codebases and expanding the platform's ability to link AI transformation to business metrics [^1].
