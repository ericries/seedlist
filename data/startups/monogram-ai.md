## About

Monogram is an AI company building a visual interface for AI, launched publicly on July 7, 2026 alongside its $40M seed round announcement [^1][^2][^3]. The company's technology generates full user interfaces on the fly in response to AI queries, so users receive visual, interactive responses instead of text-only chat output [^1][^2]. Monogram was co-founded by Eren Bali (previously co-founder of Udemy and Carbon Health), Edouard Tabet, and Murat Akbal [^1][^2]. Its first product is an iOS app [^2][^3].
