## About

Konko AI is a Latin America-focused healthcare AI startup founded in 2023 that automates patient-journey management for clinics and hospitals via an AI assistant called "Kora" [^1]. Kora communicates with patients through WhatsApp 24/7, handles appointment scheduling, coordinates operational workflows, and provides analytics on patient conversion and retention [^1]. The product is oriented toward Latin American clinics and highlights compliance with Mexican data-protection law (LFPDPPP) and integration with Brazil's public health data system (Conecte SUS) [^1]. Konko was co-founded by Jean Marc Goguikian (CEO) and Michael Haddad; Goguikian has described the founding motivation as seeing Dr. Juliana Vallejo of Clínica Vallejo struggle with clinic operations management [^1][^3].

In its first year of operations, Konko reports processing over 2 million patient interactions across roughly 90 healthcare institutions and 60 medical specialties in Mexico, Colombia, and Costa Rica, with the platform autonomously handling ~75% of patient interactions [^3].

On September 2, 2026, Konko AI announced a $6 million venture round led by Hi Ventures with participation from LifeX Ventures, SquareOne Capital, GroundUp Ventures, and Phoenix Fund, bringing total funding to date to approximately $7.1 million [^2][^3].
