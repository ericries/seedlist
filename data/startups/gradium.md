## About

Gradium is a Paris-based voice AI company developing audio foundation models with a focus on ultra-low-latency speech synthesis and understanding [^1][^2]. The company was spun out of Kyutai, a non-profit AI research lab in Paris that was launched in 2023 with a €300M endowment from Xavier Niel, Rodolphe Saadé, and Eric Schmidt [^2][^3]. Gradium is co-founded and led by Neil Zeghidour, a researcher formerly at Google Brain, DeepMind, and Facebook (Meta) [^1][^2]. The company's product line spans text-to-speech, speech-to-text, translation, and voice cloning aimed at developers [^1].

On 2026-07-09 Gradium announced that Nvidia had led a $30M extension to its seed round, bringing the total seed to over $100M and making it one of the largest seed rounds ever raised by a European AI startup [^1][^2]. Gradium plans to use the capital to open a Bay Area office and compete for AI talent in the U.S. [^2][^3]. The company has already landed enterprise customers including French auto manufacturer Renault [^2].
