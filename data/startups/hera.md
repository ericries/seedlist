## About

Hera is a New York-based AI-powered senior care coordination platform that pairs trained care experts (called "Heroes") with an AI system named "Juno" to help families manage medication management, insurance navigation, and other non-clinical care needs for older adults. The company was founded by CEO Jenny Lee with two Palantir alumni co-founders and leverages 2024 Medicare reforms allowing reimbursement for care coordination services [^1][^2].
