## About

Lyte is a Sunnyvale, California-based robotics perception company building custom silicon, 4D sensing, and AI software that let robots perceive their surroundings and act safely in shared spaces [^1][^2]. The company's platform combines two integrated offerings — LyteGalaxy (a unified spatial intelligence stack of sensors, compute, and algorithms) and LyteVision (vertically integrated sensing blocks fusing coherent 4D vision, RGB, and IMU at the edge) — and targets autonomous mobile robots, robot arms, last-mile delivery systems, quadrupeds, robotaxis, and humanoid robots [^2]. Early customers operate primarily in warehousing and manufacturing [^1]. Lyte was founded in 2021 by Alexander Shpunt (CEO), Arman Hajati, and Yuval Gerson — three former Apple engineers who previously worked on advanced sensing and perception technologies; Shpunt earlier co-founded PrimeSense, whose 3D-sensing technology powered Microsoft Kinect before Apple acquired the company in 2013 [^1].

On September 2, 2026, Lyte announced a $165 million Series C at a $1.6 billion post-money valuation, led by Maverick Silicon, with participation from Fidelity Management & Research Company (which led the prior Series B), Atreides Management, Key1 Capital, and Ora Global (formerly Exor Ventures), alongside additional new and existing investors [^1][^3]. Andrew Homan, Managing Partner at Maverick Silicon, joined the company's board [^3]. The round brings total capital raised to $272 million since founding, and proceeds will fund production scaling, AI advancement, customer deployments, and hiring across engineering and commercial roles [^3].
