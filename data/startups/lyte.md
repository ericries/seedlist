## About

Lyte is a Sunnyvale, CA-based physical AI startup founded in 2021 by former Apple engineers who previously worked on sensing and perception technologies [^1][^2]. The company builds custom silicon, multimodal sensors (4D coherent vision, RGB, motion), and spatial AI software that lets robots perceive their environment; its LyteVision platform won CES 2026 Best of Innovation in Robotics [^2]. Co-founder and CEO Alexander Shpunt previously co-founded PrimeSense, whose 3D-sensing technology powered Microsoft Kinect before Apple's 2013 acquisition [^1]. Initial customers are in warehousing and manufacturing [^1].
