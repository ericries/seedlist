## About

ThreatLocker is an Orlando, Florida-based Zero Trust cybersecurity company that has enabled more than 70,000 organizations worldwide to enforce application allowlisting and endpoint controls [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-29, ThreatLocker announced a $190 million Series F led by Elephant, with continued support from D. E. Shaw Ventures and Arthur Ventures, and a new investment from Koch Disruptive Technologies [^1][^2][^3]. The round brings total funding across all rounds to nearly $500 million [^1][^2]. Capital will support development of AI-related security controls, further improvements to the Zero Trust Platform, and international expansion beginning with a Reading, U.K. office [^1][^2].
