## About

Devplan is a Seattle-based startup building an intelligence layer for AI-native product development, emerging from stealth in June 2026 with a $2.5 million seed round [^1][^2][^3]. Its product intelligence engine, "Weaver," connects tools like GitHub, Jira, Linear, Slack, Notion, Google Workspace, meeting notes, and customer feedback into a shared organizational knowledge graph that engineers and product managers can query via chat, a Slack bot, or directly from AI coding tools [^1][^2][^3]. Co-founders Chris Bee and Anton Safonov previously worked at Uber, Amazon, Snap, and Facebook/Meta; the company operates out of AI House on Seattle's Pier 70 and reports dozens of paying business customers on annual contracts [^1][^2].
