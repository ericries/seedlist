## About

HALO X-ray Technologies is a Nottingham, UK-based deep-tech company developing X-ray diffraction (XRD) detection technology that identifies materials at a molecular/structural level for security screening applications, aiming to help operators at airports, borders, and other high-volume screening environments make faster and more accurate threat-detection decisions than conventional X-ray systems allow [^1][^2][^3]. On 2026-07-20 HALO announced a strategic follow-on investment led by Agilent Technologies, with additional follow-on participation from UK Innovation Science Seed Fund (UKI2S) and Midlands Engine Investment Fund (MEIF) [^1][^2][^3]. The round amount was not publicly disclosed and has been described in press coverage as "multimillion-dollar" [^2]. The capital is intended to complete regulatory approval of HALO's advanced security screening technology ahead of production and commercial scale-up [^1][^3].
