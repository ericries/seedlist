## About

NavigateAI is a San Francisco-based AI company founded in 2025 by Eric Wu (Opendoor co-founder) building a field-grade copilot for the physical-world labor market — a real-time coaching, scoping, quality-control, and technical-information layer designed for construction, real estate, and infrastructure field workers [^1][^2][^3]. The founding team includes builders and researchers from Opendoor, Stripe, DeepMind, Stanford, and Google [^1]. The $25M seed announced May 26, 2026 was led by Elad Gil with participation from Khosla Ventures and Fifth Wall; strategic partners Lennar, Tishman Speyer, and Helix Electric joined alongside angels Zach Frankel (Ramp), Dallas Tanner (Invitation Homes), Marcus Ridgway (Invitation Homes), Winston Weinberg (Harvey.ai), Gary Beasley (Roofstock), Jesse Zhang (Decagon), Apoorva Mehta (Instacart), Tony Xu (DoorDash), Logan Green (Lyft), and Brian Armstrong [^1][^2].
