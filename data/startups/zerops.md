## About

Zerops is a Prague-based platform-as-a-service company founded by Aleš Rechtorík that redesigns cloud architecture to eliminate the separation between development and production environments [^1][^2]. The platform connects AI coding agents (Claude, Codex, Gemini) to real cloud infrastructure via its new Zerops Control Panel (ZCP) feature [^1][^2]. The €1.7M ($2M) Seed announced May 14, 2026 was led by Gi21 Capital; capital will fund global infrastructure expansion in the US and Asia, product development, and team growth [^1][^2].
