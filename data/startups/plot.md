## About

Plot is a New York-based social video intelligence platform that uses AI to identify brand mentions, products, objects, audio, and sentiment within consumer-generated social video — including content that does not tag the brand directly [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2022 by Megan Duong, previously a social marketing lead at Apple and a digital producer at Gap, after she grew frustrated with text-based social listening tools that could not make sense of short-form or untagged video content [^3]. Plot's enterprise customers include Visa, Mastercard, PepsiCo, Fenty Beauty, Benefit Cosmetics, CAVA, Intuit, and Warby Parker [^1][^2].
