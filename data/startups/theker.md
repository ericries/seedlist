## About

THEKER is a Barcelona-based AI robotics company founded in 2022 by Carla Gómez Cano and Jiaqiang Ye Zhu [^1][^2]. The company builds AI-native generalist robots designed to operate autonomously in industrial environments, adapting in real time to changing conditions, mixed SKUs, irregular shapes, and operational variability without manual reprogramming [^1][^3]. Target sectors include logistics, retail, food and beverage, and waste management [^1].
