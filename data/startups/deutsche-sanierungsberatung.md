## About

Deutsche Sanierungsberatung (dsb) is a Berlin-based climate-tech startup operating a digital platform that helps homeowners plan and execute energy-efficient renovations, targeting Germany's stock of roughly 16 million buildings that require upgrades to meet EU energy-efficiency requirements by 2033 [^4]. The company was founded in 2024 by Sebastian Schmidt, Niclas Kern, and Adam Khenissi, three former employees of German solar-and-heat-pump unicorn Enpal [^1][^2]. dsb reports that it serves more than 10,000 private customers and works with a nationwide network of approximately 300 local trades businesses [^2]. On 2026-07-21, dsb announced a Series A round of more than €10 million to fund a proprietary electricity tariff, entry into the B2B market, and continued growth of its consultant and tradesperson network; the company forecast 2026 revenue exceeding €15 million [^1][^2][^3].
