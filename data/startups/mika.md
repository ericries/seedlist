## About

mika (Get Mika GmbH) is a Berlin-based startup building an AI-native alternative to traditional German tax and accounting firms, targeting small businesses (GmbHs and UGs) with automated bookkeeping, VAT returns, and financial statement preparation [^1][^2]. The platform is priced from €49 per month and, as of the seed round, served more than 800 companies [^2]. The company was founded in 2022 and is headquartered at Köpenicker Str. 10, 10997 Berlin, with three managing directors listed in its commercial register entry (Berlin-Charlottenburg HRB 244957) [^3].
