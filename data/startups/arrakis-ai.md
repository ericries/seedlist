## About

Arrakis is a London- and Paris-based startup building an AI "operating system" for industrial enterprises, deploying AI agents across mission-critical operations in aerospace, energy, logistics, manufacturing, construction, and telecommunications [^1][^2]. The company emerged from stealth on 2026-07-22 with $38M in combined seed and Series A funding at a $140M post-money valuation [^2]. Founded in January 2026 by former Accel investor Rafael Quintanilla alongside co-founders drawn from Palantir, DeliveryHero, Revolut, Datadog, and ASML, Arrakis argues the largest AI ROI lies in industrial operations rather than office productivity [^1][^2].
