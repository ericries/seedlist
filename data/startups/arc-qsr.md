## About

Arc is an AI-powered drive-thru platform for quick-service restaurant (QSR) operators that aims to give chains the ability to manage their drive-thru with the precision of a digital channel [^1][^2]. Co-founders Mike MacLennan and Ali Hussain are Square and Cash App veterans (Block alumni) [^1][^2].

The company is partnering with two major chicken brands, is live in multiple locations, and reports delivering a 3-7% lift in average ticket and 5-10% improvement in order accuracy [^1].

Note: Several distinct startups use the name "Arc." This profile (slug `arc-qsr`) refers specifically to the a16z-backed AI drive-thru platform announced in May 2026.
