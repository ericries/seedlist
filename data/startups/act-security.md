## About

Act Security is a Tel Aviv-based cybersecurity company building an "action-centric" security platform that addresses AI agent access sprawl at the infrastructure layer, focusing on removing unused and excessive access permissions before they can be exploited [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2025 by Jonathan Langer (CEO), Itay Kirshenbaum (CTO), Stephan Goldberg (CPO), and Ilai Fallach (VP R&D) — the same team that founded Medigate in 2017 and sold it to Claroty for $400 million in January 2022 — the company emerged from stealth on 2026-07-28 with $60 million in total funding across a previously undisclosed $20 million seed round and a fresh $40 million Series A [^1][^2][^3]. The seed round was co-led by Team8 and Bessemer Venture Partners with participation from Hetz Ventures and Caltech; the Series A was led by Notable Capital with participation from Startpoint Capital and SVCI [^1][^2][^3].
