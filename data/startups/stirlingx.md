## About

StirlingX is an Oxford, UK-based sovereign data intelligence and threat autonomy company that builds an infrastructure layer to securely capture, fuse, analyze, and act on multi-domain data streams generated across critical national infrastructure and tactical defense sectors [^1][^2]. The company emerged from incubation within GALLOS Technologies on July 2, 2026, appointing former GCHQ Director Sir Jeremy Fleming as chairman [^2][^3]. It is led by Founder and CEO Dean Jones [^1][^2].
