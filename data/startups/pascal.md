## About

Pascal is a New York-based prediction market exchange targeting institutional users and professional traders, positioned between consumer-focused competitors Kalshi and Polymarket [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in 2025 by Ivo Crnkovic-Rubsamen and Matthew Downey, the platform launched its private beta in June 2026 and is designed around perpetual-futures-style mechanics, lower fees, and deeper liquidity tools than existing venues [^1][^2][^3][^4].

On 2026-07-16 Pascal announced a $9 million Series A led by Union Square Ventures, following an approximately $6 million seed round in August 2025 [^1][^2][^3]. Total known funding stands at roughly $15 million [^1][^3].
