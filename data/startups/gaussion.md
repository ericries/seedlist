## About

Gaussion is a London-based deeptech battery company founded in 2022 as a spinout from University College London and the Faraday Institution [^1][^2]. The company develops MagLiB, a magnetic PCB retrofit system, and Aeon, a proprietary control chip and AI-enabled over-the-air intelligence platform [^1]. Gaussion's technology applies an external magnetic field to enable rapid charging and to improve the performance and lifespan of lithium-ion battery systems without changing battery chemistry or design [^1][^2]. Applications range from EV and drone charging to powering data centres [^1]. In October 2025 the company moved into a 40,000 sq ft facility in central London that it describes as one of the largest battery R&D locations in Europe [^1].
