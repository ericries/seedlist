## About

Offroad is an AI-powered identity security platform headquartered in New York and Tel Aviv that uses autonomous AI agents to investigate and remediate access risks across human users, non-human (machine) identities, and AI agents [^1][^2]. The company emerged from stealth on June 4, 2026 with $7 million in seed funding to build what it describes as an "agentic identity security team" for modern enterprises [^1][^3]. Offroad was founded in 2025 by Dan Bendler (CEO) and Philip Shteyn (CTO); Shteyn previously served as a captain in Israel's Unit 8200 cyber unit and contributed to building Palo Alto Networks' Cortex platform [^2]. Alongside the funding announcement, Offroad released ohauth.ai, a free public OAuth security catalog of applications on the Google Workspace and GitHub marketplaces [^2].
