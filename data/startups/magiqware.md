## About

MagiQware is a Delft-based deep-tech startup building AI-driven software that optimizes fault-tolerant quantum computing. The company is a TU Delft spin-off focused on reducing the physical-resource overhead of "magic state factories" — a key building block for scalable, fault-tolerant quantum systems. MagiQware uses reinforcement learning inside the quantum-compilation stack and has demonstrated up to a 40% reduction in circuit length for target magic state factories, without requiring modifications to underlying hardware. The founding team is CEO Arash Ahmadi, CTO Shakeeb Majid, Head of Device Sahar Hejazi, and Head of Theory Ali Moghaddam [^1][^2][^3].
