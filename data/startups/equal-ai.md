## About

Equal AI is an India-based startup that builds an AI-powered call screening assistant. The app receives calls on users' behalf, identifies callers, gathers context, and surfaces the reason for the call. It supports over 10 languages with code-mixing capabilities, and offers quick-reply options, call recording, and transcription [^1].

The company was founded in 2022 by Keshav Reddy [^1].
