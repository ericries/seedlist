## About

Cambridge Aerospace is a UK-based defense-tech startup building low-cost interceptor systems for drones, cruise missiles, and ballistic threats, alongside associated radar systems. Co-founded by MIT professor Steven Barrett, the company operates across the UK, Germany, Poland, Norway, Ukraine, and Australia. The August 2026 Series C valued the company at $3.4B — a 2.6x jump from its $1.3B April 2026 Series B.
