## About

Henry AI is a New York-based AI platform that automates back-office document workflows for commercial real estate teams, replacing manual production of offering memorandums, pitch decks, underwriting packages, and transaction summaries [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by Sammy Greenwall (CEO) and Adam Pratt (CTO) and went through Y Combinator [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-29, Henry AI announced a $16.5M Series A led by FirstMark Capital, bringing total funding to $21M [^1][^2]. Adam Nelson, Partner at FirstMark, joined Henry's board as part of the round [^1].
