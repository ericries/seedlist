## About

Addi is a Colombian financial and commerce platform providing buy-now, pay-later credit and merchant commerce tools. Founded in 2018 by Santiago Suárez, Daniel Vallejo, and Elmer Ortega, Addi serves 5.5 million customers and partners with 76,000 merchants across 75,000 points of sale in 1,034 Colombian municipalities [^1][^2]. The company reports it has been profitable for two years and describes itself as an "AI-first" fintech [^1][^2][^3].
