## About

Coverwatch is a San Francisco-based insurtech building an AI-native commercial insurance platform aimed at complex commercial risk [^1]. The company evaluates coverage gaps, restructures policies, and charges a flat fee rather than a premium-tied commission [^2]. Coverwatch says the approach cuts client insurance costs by 20% to 40% on average [^1]. Founded in 2025, the company plans to expand licensing across all 48 continental US states by Q3 2026 [^2].
