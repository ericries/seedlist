## About

Snorkel AI is a data-centric AI company spun out of the Stanford AI Lab in 2019, initially known for its open-source Snorkel programmatic data labeling framework and later pivoting to a data-as-a-service offering for training frontier AI models [^1][^2]. As of September 2026 the company reports an annualized revenue run rate of $375M, an 18x increase over the prior 12 months, on the back of demand for expert-curated datasets and evaluation benchmarks [^1][^3].

Insight Partners and S32 led, with participation from Third Point, March, Blumberg, Allegis, Standard VC, and Frontline, alongside existing investors Addition, Lightspeed, Greylock, GV, P7, Wells Fargo, Walden Catalyst Ventures, and Factory [^2].
