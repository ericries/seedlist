## About

Sprouts.ai is a Palo Alto-based AI company founded in 2023 by Karan Chaudhry, Kapil Chaudhry, and Avinash Nagla that builds Revenue Agents — autonomous AI agents that operate across the B2B sales funnel from ideal customer profile (ICP) identification through closed-won [^1][^2]. On May 15, 2026, Sprouts.ai announced a $9M Pre-Series A round led by True Global Ventures and Accel, bringing total funding to $14M [^1][^2]. The company's differentiated GTM data layer powers Revenue Agents used by Hewlett Packard, Razorpay, HighRadius, and Udemy [^1][^2].
