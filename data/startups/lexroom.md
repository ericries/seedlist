## About

Lexroom is a Milan-based AI legal technology platform serving civil law jurisdictions across Europe, founded in 2023 by Paolo Fois, Martina Domenicali, and Andrea Lonza [^1][^2][^3]. The platform is built on a proprietary infrastructure of 6M+ verified legal sources (legislation, case law, regulatory materials) used for daily research, drafting, and analysis by 8,000+ law firms and corporate legal teams [^1][^2]. The €42.9M (~$50M) Series B announced May 19, 2026 was led by Left Lane Capital with participation from Base10 Partners (Series A lead), Eurazeo, Acurio Ventures, Entourage, and View Different; the round came eight months after the €16.2M Series A and brings total funding to €62.7M (~$73M) [^1][^2][^3].
