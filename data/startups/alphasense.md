## About

AlphaSense is an AI-powered market intelligence and search platform founded in 2011 by Jack Kokko and Raj Neervannan [^4]. The platform combines domain-specific AI with a content library of over 500 million business documents — including equity research, earnings calls, expert interviews, and filings — and serves more than 7,000 global enterprises, including over 70% of S&P 500 companies and nearly all of the world's largest financial institutions [^1]. The company is headquartered at Hudson Yards in New York City [^1].
