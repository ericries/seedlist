## About

Holmes is a Belgian startup automating software testing for the AI-coding era, founded by Robin Praet, Robbrecht Delrue, and Sofie Buyse [^1][^2]. Rather than relying on engineers to manually write and update tests, Holmes learns how a product works and how people use it, focusing on whether the product holds up in real use rather than only whether the code is correct [^1]. Praet and Delrue previously co-founded Smartendr, a hospitality-ordering startup acquired by OrderBilly in September 2025 [^1][^2]. The company is working with around 30 design partners ahead of broader availability [^1].
