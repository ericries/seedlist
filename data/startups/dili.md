## About

Dili is an AI compliance platform focused on U.S. infrastructure projects, particularly those receiving federal funding [^1]. The company uses AI to process unstructured documents into structured data and then applies deterministic systems to verify compliance with regulations such as Davis-Bacon wage rules and Inflation Reduction Act prevailing wage requirements [^1]. Dili was part of Y Combinator's Summer 2023 batch [^1]. On 2026-07-30, Dili announced a $15M Series A led by Khosla Ventures, bringing total funding to $21.7M including a prior $6.7M seed [^1].
