## About

Aureka Biotechnologies is an AI-native TechBio company developing biological foundation models and closed-loop infrastructure that combines AI, digital biology, and experimental platforms to redesign end-to-end drug discovery. Founded in 2023 by Weian Zhao, the company operates in Laguna Hills, California, with additional operations in Shanghai.
