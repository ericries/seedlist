## About

Terra AI is a Palo Alto-based company developing generative AI models for subsurface geological modeling, used by mining companies, geothermal energy developers, and carbon storage projects to better map underground resources and accelerate exploration decisions [^1][^2]. The platform is being deployed across mining projects focused on copper, gold, and rare earth elements, as well as reservoir-related applications in enhanced geothermal energy and carbon storage [^2][^3].

The company previously closed a $3.4 million seed round in late 2023 led by Khosla Ventures with participation from Rio Tinto, Storyhouse Ventures, Plug and Play, the TomKat Center for Sustainability, and Climate Capital [^1].
