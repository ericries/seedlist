## About

Wonder is a New York-based food technology and delivery platform founded in 2018 by Marc Lore, previously the founder of Jet.com (acquired by Walmart for USD 3.3B). Wonder operates approximately 135 food halls across 10 East Coast states, running as many as 30 restaurant concepts out of a single proprietary kitchen [^1] [^2]. Marc Lore has said Wonder is "gearing up for an IPO" following the Series D [^2].

The Series D was raised at a USD 9B pre-money valuation. The company said the funding will support expansion into Texas and further investments in robotics and AI [^1].
