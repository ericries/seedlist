## About

Oak is an AI-native identity operating system for enterprises, emerging from stealth on 2026-07-15 with $60 million in seed funding [^1][^2][^3]. Founded in December 2025 by CEO Shai Morag and CPO Tal Marom — both former executives at cybersecurity company Ermetic (acquired by Tenable in 2023) — the company operates development in Tel Aviv, Israel with a San Francisco office [^1][^2][^4]. Oak positions its platform as a unified control plane that governs every identity across an organization, whether human, machine, or AI agent, replacing what it describes as fragmented legacy identity governance and security stacks [^2][^3]. The product is generally available at launch and already deployed by enterprise customers, with a team of roughly 50 people [^1][^2].
