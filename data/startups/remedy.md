## About

Remedy (also known as Remedy Science) is a dermatologist-developed skincare brand founded in 2024 by Dr. Muneeb Shah, a board-certified dermatologist with a large social media following [^1][^2]. The company sells clinically tested products targeting concerns including dark spots, dryness, fine lines, dullness, keratosis pilaris, and sensitive skin, distributed through DTC, Amazon, and Target [^1][^2]. As of the Series A announcement, investors said Remedy had been profitable since year one and was on track to hit $50 million in sales in 2026 [^2].
