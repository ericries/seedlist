## About

Socure is an Incline Village, Nevada-based digital identity verification and fraud prevention platform serving over 3,000 enterprise customers, including 19 of the top 20 U.S. banks [^1]. Founded in 2012 by Johnny Ayers, Bradley S. Leinhardt, and Sunil Madhu [^2], the company delivers AI-driven identity verification, fraud prevention, and regulatory compliance software. Q2 2026 ARR reached $364 million, up 63% year-over-year [^1].

Post-money valuation: $5.2B [^1][^3]. The round included a mix of primary capital and a secondary tender offer and funded Socure's acquisition of Austin-based agentic AI startup Fravity (terms undisclosed) [^1][^3].

## What Investors Say

Andy Collins, Managing Director at Summit Partners: "Identity has become a primary control point for trust in an AI-driven economy, and we believe the platforms that can verify identity accurately at global scale will define the next decade of risk infrastructure." [^3]
