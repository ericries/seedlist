## About

Origin Lab is a startup connecting video game studios with AI labs that need physical-world training data for world models, founded by Anne-Margot Rodde (Co-CEO), Antoine Gargot, and Colin Carrier [^1]. The company argues that game engines and gameplay data are uniquely valuable for training AI systems that need to understand physical dynamics [^1]. The $8M Seed announced May 13, 2026 was led by Lightspeed Ventures with participation from SV Angel, Eniac, Seven Stars, FPV, and angels Kevin Lin (Twitch co-founder) and Kyle Vogt (Cruise founder) [^1].

## What Investors Say

Faraz Fatemi, Partner at Lightspeed Ventures, stated: "We've seen how sharp the revenue scaling can be for data vendors serving major labs." [^1]

## What Founders Say

Anne-Margot Rodde, Co-CEO, said: "The AI systems being built now need to understand how the physical world works and how things move. That data essentially lives in video games." [^1]

She added: "Video game industry was sitting on incredibly valuable data, but there was no real way or infrastructure to connect AI labs and the video game industry." [^1]
