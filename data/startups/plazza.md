## About

Plazza is a Bengaluru-based healthtech startup building an AI-powered pharmacy retail and rapid medicine delivery platform, promising 15-30 minute delivery windows [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2024 by Aman Priyadarshi and combines quick-commerce logistics with AI-driven inventory intelligence that adapts each store's assortment to neighbourhood-level demand — reporting that only about 50% of top-selling medicines overlap even between its two Bengaluru stores [^1]. Each Plazza store stocks over 40,000 SKUs, roughly eight times a typical neighbourhood pharmacy [^1]. The company reports growing gross merchandise value nearly 27 times between June 2025 and March 2026, and plans to scale its Bengaluru presence to 20 stores by the end of 2026 [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-20, Plazza announced a $15 million Series A co-led by Accel, Elevation Capital, and Nexus Venture Partners [^1][^2].
