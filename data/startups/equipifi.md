## About

equipifi is a Scottsdale, AZ-based fintech that sells embedded Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) infrastructure to banks and credit unions, enabling installment payment options inside their existing mobile and online banking applications rather than through standalone consumer fintech apps [^1][^2]. Consumer adoption of equipifi's bank-embedded flexible-payment products has more than tripled over the year leading up to its Series B, and the company plans to roughly double headcount over the next year, concentrated in product and engineering [^1][^2]. The $34M Series B announced on May 14, 2026 was led by Left Lane Capital with continued participation from all existing investors including Curql and PHX Ventures, bringing total funding to $49M [^1][^2][^3].
