## About

Seltz is a San Francisco-based startup building a web retrieval infrastructure layer purpose-built for large language models and AI agents. The company's system crawls hundreds of millions of pages per day and returns scored passage-level results in under 200 milliseconds, optimized for agentic queries rather than human-facing search snippets. Seltz was incorporated in October 2025 and operates as a lean team of roughly 15 [^1][^2][^3].
