## About

Ognomy Sleep is a Buffalo, New York-based virtual sleep medicine platform focused on the diagnosis and treatment of obstructive sleep apnea [^1][^2]. Founded in 2019 by sleep medicine physician Daniel Rifkin, MD, MPH, the company delivers care through telehealth consultations, home sleep testing, diagnosis, treatment support, and ongoing management [^1][^2]. Ognomy has completed more than 85,000 virtual visits across all 50 U.S. states and received the 2026 MedTech Breakthrough Award for Best Overall SleepTech Solution [^1][^2]. On June 30, 2026, the company announced a $20 million Series A financing to accelerate its AI-enabled clinical platform, expand wearable device integrations, and strengthen strategic healthcare partnerships [^1][^2].
