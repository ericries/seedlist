## About

Nearfield Instruments is a Rotterdam-based semiconductor metrology company founded in 2016 as a spinout from TNO, the Dutch research institute [^1]. The company develops inspection and process-control tools used by chipmakers to measure features at the nanometer scale during advanced semiconductor manufacturing [^2].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|------|-------|--------|------|--------------|
| 2026-06-22 | Series D | $380M | Fidelity | Temasek, Walden Catalyst, Innovation Industries, M&G Investments, Invest-NL, Qatar Investment Authority, TNO Ventures, ING [^1][^2][^3] |

The Series D values the company at approximately $1.6B post-money and is described by the company as the largest deep-tech funding round in Dutch history [^1][^3].
