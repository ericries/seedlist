## About

Faye is a Richmond, Virginia–based travel-insurance and traveler-care platform founded in 2022 by Elad Schaffer (CEO) [^1][^2]. The company sells whole-trip protection covering medical, delays, cancellations, baggage, and other travel disruptions, and is building an AI layer to make underwriting, traveler assistance, and claims payments faster and increasingly autonomous [^1][^3]. Faye distributes both direct-to-consumer and through partnerships with online travel agencies, airlines, and cruise lines [^1].
