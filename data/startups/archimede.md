## About

Archimede is a Syracuse (Sicily)-based Italian DeepTech startup that develops ArchMesh, a modular plug-and-play kit for managing and monitoring off-grid critical infrastructure [^1][^2][^3]. The company was founded in 2024 by Riccardo Puglisi, Alessandro Basile and Ferdinando Anselmi, and its stack integrates predictive energy management, multi-constellation satellite connectivity and a low-code software platform to enable two-way control of distributed plants and assets in areas without reliable terrestrial coverage [^1][^2]. Target applications span agriculture, oil and gas, logistics, public infrastructure and defence [^1][^2].
