## About

Sigvi is a Vilnius-based startup building an AI-powered car rental platform that lets independent fleet operators and private owners offer vehicles through a fully automated rental service [^1][^2]. The company positions its AI revenue agent as a tool for improving pricing, increasing revenue, and managing rental operations more efficiently [^1]. Sigvi expanded operations to Poland in June 2026 and reports managing more than 200 vehicles across its network [^2].
