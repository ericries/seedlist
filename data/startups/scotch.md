## About

Scotch is an AI-native operating system for liquor store retailers, providing point-of-sale hardware, custom software, payment processing, and back-office management tools designed to handle state-by-state regulatory complexities of the beverage alcohol market [^1]. The company is based in Denver, Colorado and has approximately 45 employees [^1]. CTO Dan Chen previously served as chief architect at Drizly [^1]. Scotch reports greater than 500% year-over-year growth and has surpassed $1 billion in processed payment volume [^1].
