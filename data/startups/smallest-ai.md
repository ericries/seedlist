## About

Smallest.ai is a foundational AI research lab building real-time voice AI infrastructure for enterprises, with products spanning speech recognition, speech generation, and speech-to-speech systems used in customer service, healthcare, and financial services [^1][^2]. On July 30, 2026 the company announced a $13M Series A and debuted "Hydra," an asynchronous speech-to-speech model based on its "Voice 4.0" architecture [^1][^2]. Existing customers include RingCentral and Truecaller [^2].
