## About

FAST Metals is a Stamford, Connecticut mining technology company that recovers base and rare materials from red mud (bauxite tailings) and other iron-rich waste streams [^1][^2][^3]. The company's patented process removes iron from low-grade feedstock, turning alumina refining waste into new sources of critical minerals and metals for defense and clean-energy supply chains [^1][^2]. FAST was founded in 2025 by Sumedh Gostu, former technical director at Glencore, and Anthony Staley, former CEO of Nyrstar USA [^1][^2].
