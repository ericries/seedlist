## About

NeuralTrust is a Barcelona-based cybersecurity startup founded in 2024 by Joan Vendrell (CEO), Victor Garcia (CTO), and Alejandro Domingo (COO), building a security and governance platform for enterprise AI agents [^1][^2][^3]. The platform — comprising TrustGate (gateway enforcement), TrustGuard (runtime security), and TrustLens (agent identification and monitoring) — provides a centralized control layer for organizations running multiple AI agents that interact with internal systems, databases, applications, and external tools [^2]. Customers include AirEuropa, Abanca, Iberia, and Banc Sabadell, alongside other global banks, airlines, energy companies, and government agencies [^1].
