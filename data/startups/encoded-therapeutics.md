## About

Encoded Therapeutics is a South San Francisco biotech developing one-time precision genetic medicines for severe neurological disorders, using proprietary vector engineering to enable targeted, cell-type-selective gene expression control in the nervous system [^1]. Its lead program, ETX101, is a gene therapy for Dravet syndrome, currently in pivotal development [^1]. GV led the company's $135M Series D on July 22, 2020, with David Schenkein (GV General Partner) joining the board as observer [^4][^5]. On September 9, 2026, GV again co-led — this time alongside another undisclosed healthcare fund — the company's $275M Series F [^1][^2] to advance ETX101 toward registration and accelerate its precision genetic medicines neurology pipeline.
