## About

Moa Technology is an Oxford-based agricultural biotechnology company developing novel mode of action herbicides to combat weed resistance [^1]. The company was spun out of the University of Oxford's Plant Sciences Department in 2017 [^2]. Its platform screens compounds against multiple plant targets to identify new herbicide chemistries — a category where no new mode of action has been commercialised in over three decades [^1]. In 2025 the company introduced Moa Amplifiers, adjuvants that reduce the concentration of herbicide needed to be effective [^3].
