## About

xorlab is a Zurich-based cybersecurity company that provides sovereign email security for European enterprises, offering on-premises, hybrid, and cloud deployment options with processing performed in European data centres [^1][^2]. The platform protects against AI-crafted phishing and business email compromise attacks and supports customer compliance with DORA, NIS2, and GDPR [^1][^2]. Notable customers include Julius Bär, Swisscom, Vontobel, Giesecke+Devrient, and CERN [^1][^2].

On September 1, 2026, xorlab announced a €5 million Series A+ financing round led by existing investor Spicehaus Partners, with continued participation from Grapha Holding, EquityPitcher Ventures, and ZKB Start-up Finance [^1][^2][^3]. The company will use the capital to accelerate expansion across the DACH, Benelux, and Nordic regions [^1][^2].
