## About

Maalexi is a Dubai-based agri-fintech company building the Maalexi Agricultural Assets Token Exchange (MAATEX), an AI-, IoT-, and blockchain-powered agricultural trade platform that verifies commodities, manages risk, and enables transparent cross-border trading and settlement across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, India, and Europe [^1][^2]. Over 36 months of live operations across four markets — the UAE, Saudi Arabia, India, and the United States — the company reports a 70% repeat customer rate and more than 4,000 smart contracts executed and recorded on the Avalanche L1 blockchain [^1]. Maalexi was founded in 2021 by Azam Pasha and Rohit Majhi [^1].
