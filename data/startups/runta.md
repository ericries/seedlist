## About

Runta builds a runtime execution and sandboxing layer for AI agents, letting enterprises limit what agents can access, cap their spending, and audit every action they take [^1][^2]. The platform provides isolated sandboxes for testing agents before deploying them to production, access controls that determine what an agent can and can't do, spending caps to prevent runaway resource consumption, and comprehensive audit logs [^2].

Founder and CEO Guanlan Dai previously led Cloudflare's edge team and built core proxy technology at Kong [^3].
