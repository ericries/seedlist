## About

Penelope Health is a London-based healthtech company operating a real-time provider and payer policy intelligence platform — it converts fragmented payer data into a single infrastructure layer, enabling healthcare organizations to check coverage requirements, compare policies across insurers, and streamline operations across more than 15,000 procedures and drug codes covering policies affecting over 200 million Americans [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2025 by Sohum Patel (a physician trained at Cambridge) and Mattijs De Paepe [^1]. On 2026-09-18, Penelope Health announced €87 million (~$100 million) in seed funding led by Thoreau, with participation from Bertelsmann Healthcare Investments (BHI), Twine Ventures, and Seedcamp [^1][^2].
