## About

Penelope Health is a London-based healthtech company building an AI platform that provides structured access to payer medical coverage policies, covering over 200 million Americans across 15,000+ procedures and drug codes [^1]. The company was founded in 2025 by Sohum Patel (a physician trained at Cambridge) and Mattijs De Paepe, aiming to make opaque insurance coverage rules interpretable for providers and administrators [^1][^2].
