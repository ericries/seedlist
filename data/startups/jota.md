## About

Jota is a Brazilian AI-powered fintech that offers a digital account for entrepreneurs and small businesses, delivered primarily through WhatsApp. The platform combines conversational AI with banking so that users can make payments, generate charges, and manage receivables through text, voice, or photo interactions [^1][^2][^3]. Jota was founded in 2024 by CEO Davi Holanda, and in May 2026 launched "FalaTap," a voice-activated feature that allows merchants to generate payment QR codes by speaking the amount [^2][^3][^4].
