## About

Groq is a Mountain View-based AI inference cloud company founded in 2016 by Jonathan Ross, who previously co-invented Google's Tensor Processing Unit (TPU) [^1][^2]. The company designed the LPU (Language Processing Unit) and operates GroqCloud, which provides high-speed, lower-cost inference for large language models [^1][^3]. As of the June 2026 announcement, Groq runs 13 data centers across North America, Europe, the Middle East and Asia-Pacific, serves more than five million developers, and is targeting roughly 200 megawatts of global data center power capacity by the end of 2027 [^1][^3]. The financing followed a December 2025 non-exclusive licensing deal under which NVIDIA licensed Groq's inference technology and hired some of its hardware leadership, and is meant to fund a pivot toward an AI cloud / "neocloud" business model [^2][^4][^5].
