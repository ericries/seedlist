## About

Amsterdam-based Duqu is a fintech platform that lets businesses draw short-term advances against unpaid invoices, using a proprietary AI underwriting engine that the company says automates roughly 95% of credit assessment [^1]. Unlike traditional invoice factoring, businesses retain their customer relationships and only pay fees when they use an advance, with funds transferred within about 24 hours [^1]. Duqu also offers its underwriting engine as a white-label solution to financial institutions [^1].
