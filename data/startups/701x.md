## About

701x is a Fargo, North Dakota-based agricultural technology company building a connected ecosystem for beef cattle producers [^1][^2]. The platform integrates on-ranch software, breed association tools, smart GPS and health-monitoring ear tags, DNA solutions, and accessories to provide real-time herd management data to ranchers [^1]. The flagship product, xTpro, is a GPS smart ear tag using direct-to-satellite connectivity to deliver real-time data and alerts straight to a rancher's phone [^1]. The Series B was raised without venture capital or institutional money — entirely from local North Dakotans, Minnesotans, and rancher-users across the United States — and the company reports it has reached profitability alongside the round [^1][^2].
