## About

HrdWyr is a Bengaluru, India-based fabless semiconductor startup founded in 2023 by Ramamurthy Sivakumar, designing AI-native System-on-Chip (AISoC) products that integrate AI directly into chip architecture for edge inference across white goods, EVs, and data centers [^1][^2][^3]. The $13M Series A announced May 14, 2026 was led by Ideaspring Capital with participation from Singularity AMC, Avatar Growth Capital, and Persistent Systems; capital will fund AISoC development and broaden global customer engagement [^1][^2][^3].
