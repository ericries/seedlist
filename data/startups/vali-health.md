## About

Vali Health emerged from stealth on June 17, 2026 with $6 million in seed funding to build AI infrastructure for home care agencies [^1]. The product automates workforce management and coordination for mid-sized agencies, with a 24/7 safety layer the company says saves operators upwards of 20 hours per week [^1]. Vali reports 400% growth over its first 12 months, serving agencies in nearly 100 locations across 30 states [^1].
