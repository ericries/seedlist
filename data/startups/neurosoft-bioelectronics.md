## About

Neurosoft Bioelectronics is a neurotechnology company developing stretchable and scalable brain-computer interfaces, with headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland and a US subsidiary in New York [^1]. The company is advancing clinical trials across both the United States and Europe, with near-term milestones focused on demonstrating minimally invasive deployment in human patients and achieving US commercialization of its first brain interface [^1] [^2]. The oversubscribed seed round brings Neurosoft's total funding raised to date to more than $20 million [^1].
