## About

K2 Space is a Torrance, California-based satellite manufacturer building large, high-power satellites for commercial and defense customers [^1][^2]. Founded in 2022 by brothers Karan and Neel Kunjur, the company operates a 180,000 square foot factory in Torrance and is scaling toward the capacity to build up to 100 large satellites per year [^2][^3]. K2 maintains roughly 85% vertical integration in its supply chain and has secured more than $1 billion in signed commercial and government contracts, including with SES, Anduril, and the U.S. Space Force [^2].
