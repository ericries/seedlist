## About

Twelve Labs (stylized TwelveLabs) is a San Francisco-based AI research company building video foundation models that treat recorded video — rather than text — as the primary substrate for machine intelligence [^1][^2][^3]. Co-founded and led by Jae Lee, the company describes its long-term goal as building "video superintelligence": a full-stack agentic intelligence system that combines perception, knowledge and reasoning into a single architecture [^1][^2]. Alongside the Series B, Amazon Web Services signed a multi-year contract to host Twelve Labs' training and inference workloads on AWS Trainium chips, and Twelve Labs plans to open new offices in New York and London on top of its existing footprint in San Francisco, Seoul and Los Angeles [^1][^2][^3].
