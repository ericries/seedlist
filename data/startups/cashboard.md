## About

Cashboard is a New York-headquartered fintech that provides AI-powered financial planning and analysis software for CFOs [^1][^2]. The platform automates data collection, analysis, and narrative generation, reducing the manual workload for finance teams and enabling a single CFO to manage complex FP&A functions independently [^2]. The company was founded by Julian Rowlands, who developed Cashboard after a year of interviews with CFOs about their reporting pain points [^2].
