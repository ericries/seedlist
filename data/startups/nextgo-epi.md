## About

NextGO Epi is a Berlin-based deeptech startup developing gallium oxide (Ga2O3) epitaxial wafers for next-generation power semiconductor devices [^1][^2][^3]. The company was spun out in 2025 from the Leibniz-Institut für Kristallzüchtung (IKZ) and describes itself as Europe's only producer of gallium oxide epiwafers [^1][^2]. Its founding team draws on more than a decade of gallium oxide research, backed by two international patents and 30+ scientific publications [^2]. NextGO Epi claims that gallium oxide offers approximately 10x the power efficiency of silicon carbide, supports up to 6x higher voltage density, and can be produced at up to 75% lower cost — with target applications including EV fast-charging stations and grid power electronics [^2]. The company reports that it is already in production, generating revenue, and serving customers across three continents [^2].
