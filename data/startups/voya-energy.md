## About

Voya Energy is a Hayward, California-based clean energy startup building metal-fueled distributed power systems that generate electricity from recycled aluminum [^1][^2]. The company was incubated by Energy Impact Partners alongside co-founders Richard Wang, Matt Horton, and Steven Kaye [^3]. Voya positions its aluminum-based generators as a scalable alternative to diesel gensets for behind-the-meter and edge power applications [^1][^3].
