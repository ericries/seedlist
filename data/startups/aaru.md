## About

Aaru is a New York-based AI synthetic-research platform co-founded in March 2024 by Cameron Fink, Ned Koh, and John Kessler that deploys simulated agents to predict consumer and political behavior, generating high-fidelity synthetic populations and dynamically modeling their responses rather than asking real people what they would do [^1][^2][^3]. Notable enterprise customers include Accenture, EY, and Interpublic Group; the company has also been used by political campaigns [^2][^3]. The $80M Series A announced April 22, 2026 was led by Redpoint Ventures and brings total funding to $88M; an earlier closed-then-disclosed tranche from December 2025 featured a multi-tier structure with a $1B "headline" valuation and a blended valuation below $1B, and prior pre-seed/seed investors included A* (A-Star Capital), Abstract Ventures, Felicis, General Catalyst, Accenture Ventures, and Z Fellows [^1][^2][^3].
