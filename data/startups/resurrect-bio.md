## About

Resurrect Bio is a London-based agricultural biotech company (spinout from The Sainsbury Laboratory) developing AI-driven gene-editing technology that "resurrects" dormant disease resistance in crops [^1][^2]. The platform comprises FloraFold (AI discovery engine for plant-pathogen protein interactions), a high-throughput validation system, and a resurrection platform that reactivates cryptic resistance traits in elite germplasm [^1][^2]. The Series A had an initial close of $8.1M (February 17, 2026) and a final close of $10.3M (announced May 13, 2026, oversubscribed), bringing total funding to $12.4M since the 2023 seed round; led by Corteva Agriscience (via Catalyst platform) with participation from Calculus Capital, Pymwymic, UKI2S, SynBioVen, and AgFunder [^1][^2][^3].
