## About

Icon is a New York–based (169 Madison Ave, New York, NY 10016) human user-generated content (UGC) ad production platform founded in 2024 by Kennan Jenner [^1][^2]. The company manages the full production pipeline for short-form video ads — creator sourcing, scripting, filming, and editing — and markets itself as a lower-cost alternative to platforms such as Billo, Soona, and SideShift, quoting "6 Human UGC ads filmed & edited for $1000 (no AI / 100% real)" on its site [^1]. Kennan Jenner previously founded Skio (Y Combinator S20), which he describes as reaching approximately $32M ARR and a $105M cash exit on $8M raised [^1].

Icon's website reports "$30M+ raised" and lists 68 investors on the round [^1][^3]. Named backers include Founders Fund; Mark Chen (Chief Research Officer at OpenAI); NFL running back Saquon Barkley; Roman Khan (CMO at Raycon / Know Beauty / Linjer); Arash Ferdowsi (Dropbox co-founder); Eric Glyman and Karim Atiyeh (Ramp co-founders); Scott Wu and Walden Yan (Cognition co-founders); Immad Akhund (Mercury CEO); David Petersen (Flexport co-founder); Kevin Hartz (Eventbrite co-founder); Matteo Franceschetti (Eight Sleep CEO); Tomer Tagrin (Yotpo CEO); Demi Guo (Pika CEO); Jayendra Jog (Sei co-founder); K-Pop artist Eric Nam; and chess/poker player Alexandra Botez [^3]. AlleyWatch's daily funding report for August 31, 2026 lists Icon as a $30M venture round led by Founders Fund [^2].
