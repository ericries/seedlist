## About

Icon is a New York–based (169 Madison Ave, New York, NY 10016) human user-generated content (UGC) ad production platform founded in 2024 by Kennan Jenner [^1][^2]. The company manages the full production pipeline for short-form video ads — creator sourcing, scripting, filming, and editing — and markets itself as a lower-cost alternative to platforms such as Billo, Soona, and SideShift, quoting "6 Human UGC ads filmed & edited for $1000 (no AI / 100% real)" on its site [^1]. Kennan Jenner previously founded Skio (Y Combinator S20), which he describes as reaching approximately $32M ARR and a $105M cash exit on $8M raised [^1].

Icon's website reports "$30M+ raised" with backers including Founders Fund, Mark Chen (Chief Research Officer at OpenAI), Saquon Barkley (NFL running back), and Roman Khan, and references a total of 68 investors on the round [^1]. AlleyWatch's daily funding report for August 31, 2026 lists Icon as a $30M venture round led by Founders Fund [^2].
