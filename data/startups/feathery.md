## About

Feathery is a San Francisco–based fintech that builds an AI operating and decisioning system for financial services, with a focus on insurance and wealth management workflows [^1][^2]. Insurance and wealth firms use Feathery to automate data collection, document processing, onboarding, and operational workflows while integrating with existing systems [^1][^3]. The company is led by Co-founders Peter Dun (CEO) and Zack Khan [^1][^2].

On 2026-07-14 Feathery announced $30 million in total funding, including its Series A [^1][^2][^3]. The round was led by Portage Ventures and included Index Ventures, Allstate Strategic Ventures, Clocktower Ventures, Erie Strategic Ventures, and Bain Capital Ventures [^1][^2][^4]. The capital will support product development and expansion of the company's data network across clients [^1][^2].
