## About

Foundation Devices is a Boston-based hardware security company founded in 2021 by CEO Zach Herbert and CTO Ken Carpenter, best known for its Bitcoin hardware wallets (Passport line) and now expanding into broader digital security including identity, multi-factor authentication, and AI-agent authorization [^1][^2][^3]. The company is rolling out general availability of its flagship Passport Prime device — integrating a Bitcoin wallet, security keys, encrypted storage, and a developer interface — and is opening KeyOS, its software platform, to third-party developers with a planned app store launch in Q2 [^1][^2]. The $6.4M round announced May 21, 2026 was led by Fulgur Ventures with participation from Arche Capital, bringing total funding to $16.5M; prior backers include Polychain Capital, which led the 2022 $7M seed [^1][^2][^3].
