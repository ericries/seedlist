## About

Niural is an AI-native platform for global payroll, benefits, payments, and compliance, operating regulated infrastructure across 150+ countries [^1][^2]. Co-founded by Nami Baral (CEO) and Nabin Banskota (President), the company is headquartered in New York [^2][^3].

On June 23, 2026, Niural announced an additional $21M in strategic capital, bringing its Series A total to $52M, and launched Niural AI Labs, a research division developing long-horizon agents for high-stakes business domains [^1][^3]. The original Series A of $31M was led by Marathon Management Partners in 2025 [^2].
