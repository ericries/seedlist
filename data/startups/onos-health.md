## About

Onos Health is a San Francisco-based behavioral health clinical intelligence platform that analyzes structured and unstructured behavioral health data to identify care patterns, treatment gaps, and quality-improvement opportunities for health plans [^1][^2]. The company says three of the six largest U.S. health plans, including Aetna, use its platform [^1][^2]. Onos raised a $17M Series A on August 26, 2026, bringing total funding to $23.5M [^1][^2].
