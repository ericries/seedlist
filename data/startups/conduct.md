## About

Conduct is a German enterprise AI startup founded in 2024 by three ex-Palantir engineers — Jan Philipp Haas (CEO), Philipp Hoefer, and Henry Thompson — building an agentic operating system that makes legacy enterprise systems legible and operable by AI agents [^1][^2]. Early enterprise customers include Daimler Truck, Heidelberg Materials, Fraport, and DHL [^1].
