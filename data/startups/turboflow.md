## About

TurboFlow is a Hong Kong-based on-chain trading platform that combines perpetual contracts and prediction markets on a unified interface, aiming to bring institutional-grade trading infrastructure to retail users across Asia-Pacific [^1][^2]. The platform has been running a beta for over six months, with more than 14,540 registered users and over $19.15 billion in trading volume processed to date [^2][^3]. Founder and CEO Tony He was previously a co-founder and partner at crypto firm Amber Group [^1].
