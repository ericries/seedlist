## About

1872 is a Cincinnati, Ohio-based AI-native manufacturing startup building autonomous steel fabrication factories [^1][^2]. Founded by three former SpaceX engineers, the company opened one of the first autonomous steel fabrication facilities in the United States in Cincinnati's Camp Washington neighborhood, occupying the historic 1903 David Hummel Building facility [^1]. Its proprietary Factory OS integrates AI software with advanced robotics to automate heavy fabrication tasks — managing material sourcing, robotics control, and steel handling end-to-end — targeting an industry constrained by labor shortages and manual processes [^1][^2]. The company takes its name from 1872, the year Andrew Carnegie built the world's first steel mill [^1].
