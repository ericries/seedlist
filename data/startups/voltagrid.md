## About

VoltaGrid is a Houston-based behind-the-meter power generation company founded in 2020 by Nathan Ough, providing off-grid power solutions for data centers, AI infrastructure, utilities, and industrial customers across North America [^1][^2]. The company announced a $1B strategic equity investment on May 11, 2026, comprising $775M in primary capital and $225M in a secondary purchase from existing investors, led by Blackstone Tactical Opportunities and Halliburton; alongside the financing, VoltaGrid signed a definitive agreement to acquire Propell Energy Technology Ltd. to expand power systems manufacturing capacity [^1][^2]. Bloomberg reported the deal values VoltaGrid at more than $10B [^3].
