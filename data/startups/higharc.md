## About

Higharc is a Durham, North Carolina-based AI company for residential homebuilding, generating homes as 3D spatial data so builders and suppliers can design, estimate, sell and build homes more efficiently [^1][^2]. On June 30, 2026, the company announced a $95M Series C led by Insight Partners, bringing total funding to more than $170M [^1][^2]. The round included Wellington Management as a new investor and existing backers Fifth Wall, Spark Capital, Lux Capital, SE Ventures (Schneider Electric's venture arm), Simpson Strong-Tie, PSP Partners, RXR Arden Digital Ventures, Suffolk Technologies, Vertex Ventures, NC Tweener Fund and MetaProp [^1][^2]. Simultaneously, Higharc announced a partnership with US LBM — the largest private distributor of lumber and building materials in the United States — expanding its platform into the building materials supply chain [^1][^2]. The company has been recognized in the top 50 of the Deloitte Technology Fast 500 and Fast Company's Most Innovative Companies of 2026 [^1].
