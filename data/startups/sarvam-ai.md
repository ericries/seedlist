## About

Sarvam AI is a Bengaluru-based generative AI company building sovereign large language models for India, with a focus on Indic languages and applications across banking, insurance, government, and defense [^1]. The company was co-founded in 2023 by Vivek Raghavan and Pratyush Kumar, both veterans of India's digital public infrastructure work [^1][^2]. Sarvam was selected by India's government as the first startup to build a sovereign foundational LLM under the IndiaAI Mission [^1].

## Funding History

| Date | Round | Amount | Lead | Co-investors |
|------|-------|--------|------|--------------|
| 2026-06-15 | Series B | $234M | HCLTech | Bessemer Venture Partners, Khosla Ventures, Peak XV Partners [^1][^3] |
| 2023-12-06 | Seed | $41M | Lightspeed Venture Partners | Peak XV Partners, Khosla Ventures [^2] |

The June 2026 Series B was led by HCLTech with a ₹1,427.25 crore strategic investment for a 10.46% stake, with participation from existing backers Khosla Ventures and Peak XV Partners alongside new investor Bessemer Venture Partners [^1][^3]. The round valued Sarvam at approximately $1.5 billion, making it India's newest AI unicorn [^1].
