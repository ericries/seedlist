## About

Tripo AI (operated by Vast) is a Chinese AI startup building generative 3D and world-model research infrastructure [^1][^2]. The company produces tools for AI-driven 3D asset generation used by game developers, designers, and creators, and is expanding into broader world-model research with an initiative called Project Eden, designed to support persistent, reusable, and multiplayer interactive environments [^1][^2].
