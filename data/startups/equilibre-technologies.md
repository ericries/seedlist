## About

EquiLibre Technologies is a Prague-based AI research lab applying reinforcement learning to financial markets. The company was founded in December 2021 by three former DeepMind researchers — Martin Schmid, Rudolf Kadlec, and Matej Moravcik — who had earlier built DeepStack, the first AI system to defeat professional players at no-limit poker [^1][^2]. EquiLibre's AI agents trade billions of dollars in daily volume on the S&P 500 and Nasdaq in partnership with Tower Research Capital [^1][^2][^3]. The company employs roughly 25 engineers, most of them Czech nationals with prior experience at major U.S. tech firms [^2].
