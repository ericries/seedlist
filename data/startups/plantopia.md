## About

Plantopia is an Israeli food-tech company developing dairy proteins using molecular farming — the use of plants as biofactories to produce complex proteins traditionally derived from animals [^1][^2]. Founded in 2018 based on research from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem's Faculty of Agriculture, the company's first product is casein, the main protein responsible for cheese structure and functionality, produced inside sprouted oats [^1][^2]. Plantopia is building a manufacturing facility at Kibbutz Sdot Yam in Israel, with production expected to begin in the second half of 2026 [^1][^3].
