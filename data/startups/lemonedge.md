## About

LemonEdge is a London-based modern fund accounting platform built for private markets, aiming to replace legacy fund accounting infrastructure with an AI-enabled system designed for the operational complexity of modern alternative asset management [^1][^2]. On 2026-07-29, LemonEdge announced a $21 million Series A funding round led by Blackstone Innovations Investments and joined by BNY, with participation from long-time investor Sidekick Partners, bringing total funding raised to more than $30 million [^1][^2][^3]. In conjunction with the round, the company appointed David T. O'Malley as Chief Executive Officer and Board Chair [^1][^2]. Proceeds will accelerate product development and continued expansion in the US and Europe [^1].
