## About

Pramaana Labs is a Palo Alto-based AI startup founded in 2025 by IIT Madras alumni Ranjan Rajagopalan (CEO; previously led Google Maps Moderation), Krishnan Raghavan (previously worked on Glean Assistant at Glean), and Sanjay Ganapathy (formerly staff research engineer at Google DeepMind, where he contributed to the Gemini family of models) [^1][^2]. The company combines large language models with deterministic formal verification layers — using the LEAN programming language — to ensure mathematically verifiable AI outputs in high-stakes domains including law, drug discovery, and tax preparation [^1][^3]. Domain experts collaborating with the company include former IRS Commissioner Danny Werfel, with academic input from professors at IIT Delhi, IIT Madras, and UC Berkeley [^1].
