## About

Arcee AI develops open-weight foundation models and infrastructure that enable organizations to customize, deploy, and own the models at the center of their work [^1]. The company builds the Trinity model family and provides tools spanning mobile-compatible models through frontier systems for scientific applications, with an announced expansion of its collaboration with the U.S. Department of Energy [^1][^2].
