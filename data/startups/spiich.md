## About

Spiich is a Stockholm-based AI sales assistant that automates administrative work for B2B revenue teams, including CRM updates, meeting notes, follow-ups, and lead prospecting [^1][^2]. The company was founded in June 2025 by Johan Torssell (CEO) and Dennis Hadzialic [^1].
