## About

Paymentology is a London-based cloud-native global issuer-processor providing real-time card issuing and processing technology for banks and fintechs across nearly 70 countries [^1][^2]. The $175M growth round announced May 12, 2026 was co-led by Apis Partners and Aspirity Partners; for Apis the deal marks its 16th payments-sector investment, while for Aspirity this partnership is the inaugural fund's first investment [^1][^2][^3]. Proceeds will fund international expansion, product development, and team growth across credit, stablecoin infrastructure, tokenisation, and AI-driven financial services [^1][^2].
