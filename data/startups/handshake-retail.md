## About

Handshake is a London-based retail technology company building an AI-native operating system for retail buying and commercial agreements [^1]. The company helps retailers, wholesalers, and suppliers create, track, and execute commercial agreements in a single transparent system, replacing the spreadsheets, email chains, and siloed systems that still underpin much of the $18.8 trillion global retail industry [^1][^2]. Handshake was co-founded in 2024 by Alex Lindsay and Peter Welch [^2].
