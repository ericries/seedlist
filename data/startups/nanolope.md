## About

Nanolope is a Berlin-based deep-tech startup developing high-density thermal energy storage materials that turn walls, ceilings, and floors of existing buildings into passive heat and cold storage, aiming to reduce heating and cooling demand, peak loads, and CO2 emissions without invasive renovations [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2025 by Dr. Felix Marske, Eric Matthes, and Dominik L. Schreiber [^1].
