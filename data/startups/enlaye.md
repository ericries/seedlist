## About

Enlaye is a Boston-based provider of an AI-native Risk Lifecycle Management platform for the construction and infrastructure industries [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2023 by CEO Philippe Rival and CTO Stamatios Liapis to help builders predict and manage project risk across the lifecycle of a construction project [^1]. The platform launched in fall 2025 and has been deployed across projects representing more than $17 billion in value across the United States, Canada, and Europe [^1].
