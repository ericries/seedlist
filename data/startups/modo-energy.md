## About

Modo Energy is a London-headquartered platform that provides AI-powered benchmarking, forecasting, and valuation tools for battery energy storage and other renewable and flexible-energy assets [^1][^2]. The company was founded in 2019 by CEO Quentin Scrimshire and Director Tim Overton and now employs more than 75 analysts, data scientists, and engineers [^2][^3]. Its platform covers 15 energy markets and is used by more than 200 companies across roughly 30 countries; Modo says its forecasts have supported more than $3.8 billion in energy-asset financing to date [^2][^3]. The company describes itself as the only FCA-regulated benchmark provider for battery energy storage globally [^2]. Its AI analyst, Ko, is designed to answer complex market questions and, over time, run data analysis and reporting end to end [^1][^2].

## What Investors Say

Sean Duffy, Managing Director and Market Lead at CIBC Innovation Banking, said Modo Energy "is building critical infrastructure for the energy transition — a trusted standard for valuing storage and renewable assets" [^2].

## What Founders Say

Quentin Scrimshire, CEO and Co-Founder of Modo Energy, said of the CIBC growth round: "When forecasts are bankable, capital flows and projects get built. That's the standard we hold ourselves to — and it's why CIBC Innovation Banking is backing us" [^1][^2].

At the time of the Series B in late 2025, Scrimshire said: "We're building the benchmarking and valuation operating system for global electrification — and AI is central to that mission" [^5].
