## About

Orios Venture Partners is a Mumbai-based early-stage technology venture capital firm founded in 2013 by **Rehan Yar Khan**, a serial entrepreneur and prolific Indian angel investor of the 2008-2013 era who had previously backed Druva (2008) and Ola Cabs (2011) as personal angel cheques [^1][^2][^3]. The firm targets 10-12 investments per year into B2C, B2B, software and hardware startups built for the Indian market and is publicly positioned around backing "Misfit" founders who don't fit conventional moulds [^1][^4].

Orios is registered with SEBI across four Alternative Investment Funds: **Orios Venture Partners Fund** (Cat-I, IN/AIF1/13-14/0087), **Orios Venture Partners Fund II** (Cat-I, IN/AIF1/15-16/0164), **Orios Select Fund** (Cat-I, IN/AIF1/18-19/0621), and **Orios Venture Partners Fund III** (Cat-II, IN/AIF2/20-21/0798) [^1]. Fund I had a final close at INR 300 crore in 2015; the firm returned 1X (INR 300 crore) of Fund I to LPs in January 2024 [^5]. Orios Select Fund I closed at $30 million in June 2021 to deploy $4M-$8M follow-on cheques into existing portfolio winners [^6][^7]. The firm's third growth fund was repeatedly postponed in 2024-2025, and Fund IV closed below target in 2025 with a key (unnamed) Indian government LP onboarded [^8][^9].

In September 2023, Managing Partners Anup Jain (ex-P&G, Yum) and Rajeev Suri (ex-Jio, Viu, Infosys) — who had joined in 2018 — both departed; CFO and COO Gaurav Bindal subsequently followed [^10][^11]. As of 2026 the senior team is Rehan Yar Khan (Managing Partner), Madhav Tandan (Senior Partner), and Sukhmani Bedi (Partner) [^1]. Orios has 76 portfolio companies across 13 years per Tracxn, with 3 portfolio unicorns (CarDekho/GirnarSOFT, API Holdings/PharmEasy) and 3 IPOs (MobiKwik, ixigo, Nazara Technologies) [^12].

## Stated Thesis

Orios publicly describes its mandate as backing "innovators who are working in unchartered territories, on ideas that have the potential to disrupt the traditional ecosystem" [^1]. The firm operates a "#Misfits Program" of founder-to-founder support and emphasises hands-on "Managing Partner time" focused on strategy, fundraising, people strategy, and senior introductions [^1][^4]. Khan has stated that he founded Orios because "I saw an opportunity to fill the gap and provide funds at the seed level" and "I thought that the ecosystem needed a fund which has been an entrepreneur previously" [^2].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on Tracxn's classification of 76 Orios investments through March 2026 [^12]:

**Stage distribution (Orios first-round and follow-on rounds, n≈71 stage-classified):**
- Seed: 46 (~65%), average round size $1.37M
- Series A: 18 (~25%), average round size $6.03M
- Series E (later-stage follow-ons): 4 (~6%), average $194M
- Series B/C/other: 4 (~6%)

Roughly 90% of Orios cheques are seed or Series A first-rounds; the Series E entries reflect Select Fund I and Fund III follow-on participation into pre-IPO names (PharmEasy, ixigo, MobiKwik).

**Sector distribution (Orios first-round investments per Tracxn):**
- Consumer: 35 companies
- Enterprise Applications: 21
- Retail: 17
- FinTech: 14
- Vertical SaaS: 10
(Categories overlap.) Orios's published Fund I allocation was **27.07% marketplaces, 17.7% D2C, 14.45% healthtech** [^5].

**Geographic focus:** 70 of 76 portfolio companies (~92%) are India-based; 3 are US-based [^12].

**Notable exits and outcomes:**
- **Country Delight**: 45X partial exit in February 2024 (invested 2017) [^13]
- **Battery Smart**: 29X partial exit in June 2024 (invested 2020) via $65M Series B [^14]
- **MobiKwik**: IPO December 2024, ~$254M market cap at listing [^12]
- **ixigo**: IPO, ~$432M market cap [^12]
- **Nazara Technologies**: listed on Indian public markets [^12]
- **PharmEasy / API Holdings**: largest contributor to Fund I 1X return [^5]
- **Druva** (personal angel of Rehan Yar Khan): $475M raised, unicorn outcome [^3]

**Co-investor patterns:** Recent rounds show frequent co-investment with Stellaris Venture Partners (Truva 2026), 3one4 Capital (HexaHealth 2025), and earlier with Tiger Global (Zo Rooms 2015), Sequoia/Peak XV, Matrix Partners India / Z47, and Beenext [^12][^15][^16].
