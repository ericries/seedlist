## Background

Sean Kandel is co-founder and Chief Technology Officer of Trifacta, a data preparation software company founded in October 2012 with UC Berkeley professor Joe Hellerstein and then-Stanford (later University of Washington) professor Jeffrey Heer [^1][^2]. Trifacta was acquired by Alteryx in February 2022 for approximately $400 million in cash, with an additional $75 million in equity-based retention for Trifacta employees [^3][^4].

Kandel received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from Stanford University in 2013, with a thesis on "Interactive systems for data transformation and assessment," advised by Jeffrey Heer in the Stanford Visualization Group [^1][^5]. During his doctoral work he co-developed Data Wrangler, an interactive tool for data cleaning and transformation that was a joint research effort between Stanford and UC Berkeley; Data Wrangler became the technical foundation for Trifacta's commercial product [^1][^5].

Trifacta raised roughly $76M in equity funding prior to acquisition, led at various stages by Accel Partners (Series A, October 2012, $4.3M), Greylock Partners (Series B, December 2013, $12M), Ignition Partners (Series C, May 2014, $25M), and Cathay Innovation among others (Series D-era rounds through 2018) [^2][^6][^7]. Following the Alteryx acquisition, Kandel continued in a CTO capacity within the combined organization [^4][^8].

Kandel has been recognized on the Silicon Valley Business Journal's "40 Under 40" list (2017) and on Import.io's "40 Data Mavericks Under 40" [^1][^5].
