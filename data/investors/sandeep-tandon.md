## Background

Sandeep Tandon is a Mumbai-based entrepreneur and investor with more than 20 years of experience across California and India [^1]. He holds Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Electrical Engineering from the University of Southern California and completed the Owner President Manager program at Harvard Business School [^1][^2].

Tandon co-founded Freecharge in August 2010 with Kunal Shah; the mobile recharge and payments platform was sold to Snapdeal in March 2015 for a deal valued at approximately $400 million, one of the largest Indian consumer internet transactions at the time [^2]. Axis Bank subsequently acquired Freecharge from Snapdeal in July 2017 for roughly $60 million [^2].

Earlier in his career, Tandon founded IQBackOffice (sold in 2000) and led operations at Celetronix in India until its 2006 acquisition by Jabil Circuits [^2]. He is the Managing Director of Tandon Group and serves as Executive Chairman of Syrma SGS Technology and Chairman of Aavas Financiers [^1][^2].

In 2017, Tandon co-founded **Whiteboard Capital** with Anshu Prasher, a Mumbai-based early-stage VC firm that backs founders at ideation, pre-seed, and seed stages [^1][^3]. He sits on the board of CRED and serves as an advisor to Unacademy and Spinny [^1].

**Identity note:** This profile covers Sandeep Tandon the Freecharge co-founder. He is distinct from Sandeep Tandon, Founder & CIO of Quant Mutual Fund (a public-markets asset manager).

## Stated Thesis

Whiteboard Capital states it invests at "ideation, pre-seed, and seed stages, partnering with visionary founders to transform bold ideas into thriving businesses," and describes a hands-on, "founders first" model [^3]. The firm's own framing on its site: "We invest our time, expertise, and resources in the ideas and teams we believe in. By fostering strong relationships and offering hands-on support, we aim to be a catalyst for growth and success." [^3]

Tandon's personal angel activity (begun in 2015 after the Freecharge exit) has emphasized Indian internet, fintech, and consumer businesses [^2][^4].
