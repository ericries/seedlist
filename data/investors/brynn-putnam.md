## Background

Brynn Putnam is a Harvard graduate and former professional ballet dancer who built and exited Mirror, the connected-fitness device that Lululemon acquired in 2020 for $500 million [^1][^2]. Before Mirror she ran Refine Method, a chain of three high-intensity fitness studios in New York; she conceived Mirror in 2016 while pregnant and exercising at home, and famously closed Mirror's $3M seed round the same day she gave birth to her son [^3][^2].

Putnam stayed on as Mirror's CEO inside Lululemon after the acquisition and stepped down roughly a year later, in September 2021 [^4]. She is now founder and CEO of Board, a New York-based consumer gaming hardware company that unveiled publicly at TechCrunch Disrupt in October 2025 and announced a $20M Series A led by Union Square Ventures on 2026-06-02, with Michael Mignano (USV) joining the board [^5][^6]. Alongside her operating roles, Putnam makes occasional angel investments — primarily in healthcare [^7][^8].

## Stated Thesis

Putnam has not published an investment thesis. Her public angel activity is limited and she presents herself primarily as an operator. In interviews she has framed Board as a deliberate pivot from Mirror's solo-improvement mindset toward shared, in-person experiences:

> "Mirror was very much about me. It was my reflection, my performance, it was about making your own self better. At that next phase, my life was really just much more about my family and my friends and my relationships." — Brynn Putnam, TechCrunch, June 2026 [^5]

She has also said, at a StrictlyVC investor evening in November 2024, that "we're about to enter a golden age of hardware" [^6].

## Inferred Thesis

Putnam's public angel record is thin — only two investments have been independently verified, both Seed-stage healthcare rounds:

- **2 of 2 verified investments (100%) are healthcare seed rounds** — Intus Care (geriatric care analytics) and knownwell (weight-inclusive primary care) [^7][^8].
- **Both are NYC- or East Coast-connected** rounds; Intus Care emerged from Brown University's entrepreneurship program, and knownwell is a Flare Capital-led launch [^7][^8].
- **Sample size is too small for reliable percentages.** The investments suggest a preference for healthcare-adjacent consumer/services companies aligned with her own background in fitness and wellness, but with N=2 this is descriptive, not inferential.

Putnam should be read primarily as an operator. Founders pursuing her likely value her hardware/consumer-brand expertise (Mirror, Board) more than her capital. See `## Connections` for relationships that make her a useful early call.
