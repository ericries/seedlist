## Background

Alexander Schwartz is a Partner at XISTA Science Ventures, the venture capital arm inside the XISTA ecosystem — an Austrian deep-tech platform anchored in Vienna that combines a science park, entrepreneurship programs, and two venture funds [^1][^2]. XISTA describes itself as investing in "science-driven companies built on deep research, with a focus on human health and industrial technologies," with close to €100 million in assets under management across Fund I (fully deployed) and Fund II (actively investing) [^1].

XISTA lists Schwartz as a member of the core team [^3]. In multiple portfolio-company press releases, his role is stated as "Partner at XISTA Science Ventures" and the honorific "Dr." is used, consistent with a doctorate-level scientific background [^4][^5].

Beyond Schwartz, the broader XISTA leadership includes Markus Wanko, Prudence Donovan, Alexander Fischl, Melanie Leisser, Stephan Huber (Venture Partner), and Erich Tauber (Operating Partner) [^3].

## Stated Thesis

XISTA Science Ventures publicly positions itself as a deep-tech investor across two verticals — life sciences (biomedical, therapeutics, diagnostics) and industrial technology (materials, energy, manufacturing) — with an approach that emphasises long-term engagement from the earliest stages [^1].

In the Invisible-Light Labs pre-seed announcement (June 2026), Schwartz articulated what impressed him about the founding team:

> "We have been deeply impressed by how the founding team of ILL has translated strong scientific insight into exceptional technology and product via Bruker co-marketing with limited resources, demonstrating their capabilities and focus." [^4]

In the Epitome Therapeutics pre-seed launch (June 2026), he described the underlying thesis:

> "We were impressed by the breakthrough nature of Epitome Therapeutics' approach to epigenome editing, especially its focus on targeted upregulation, and by the team's ambition to translate this science into therapeutically relevant programmes. We are delighted to be amongst the first to support the company as it achieves the first development milestones and as the field continues to gain momentum." [^5]

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size caveat.** Only two deals are directly attributed to Schwartz personally in publicly cited quotes: Invisible-Light Labs and Epitome Therapeutics. XISTA's overall portfolio of 31 companies is disclosed on the firm's website [^1][^3]; assignment of any specific XISTA deal to Schwartz as the lead partner is not made public for most portfolio companies. The observations below are therefore qualitative rather than statistical.

**Sector concentration: deep tech.** Both of Schwartz's directly-attributed deals are deep tech: Invisible-Light Labs is a TU Wien spin-off building nanoelectromechanical FTIR spectroscopy for sub-micron particle identification [^4][^6]; Epitome Therapeutics is an epigenome-editing platform [^5]. Both target scientific instruments / therapeutics rather than software or consumer sectors — consistent with XISTA's declared focus on life science and industrial tech [^1].

**Stage: earliest rounds.** Both attributed deals are pre-seed (€1.5M and €2M respectively) and involved co-lead or first-institutional roles for XISTA [^4][^5]. Both press releases position XISTA as "amongst the first to support" — pointing to a pre-seed / seed sweet spot.

**Geography: Austrian and European deep-tech spin-outs.** Invisible-Light Labs is Vienna-based (TU Wien spin-off) [^6]; Epitome Therapeutics is a life-sciences pre-seed positioned alongside other XISTA European portfolio companies [^5]. XISTA is itself an Austrian science-park anchored fund [^1].

**Founder profile: scientific/academic founders translating research to product.** Both quotes emphasise "translating scientific insight" into product / therapeutic programs — a stated bias for founders coming out of research institutions [^4][^5].

**Co-investor patterns.** In the Invisible-Light Labs pre-seed, XISTA Science Ventures co-led with aws Gründungsfonds (Austrian public tech investment vehicle), with Fund F participating [^6]. XISTA's broader portfolio has co-invested with Turbine Capital, Almi Invest GreenTech, Greencode Ventures, and Constructor Capital across other rounds [^7][^8].
