## Background

Thomas Dohmke is the founder and CEO of Entire, an AI code governance startup that emerged from stealth in February 2026 with a $60M seed round at a $300M valuation [^1][^2][^3]. He was previously CEO of GitHub from November 2021 until August 2025, having joined the company in 2018 when his prior startup HockeyApp (acquired by Microsoft in 2014) led to his role on Microsoft's developer tools team [^1][^2]. During his tenure at GitHub he oversaw the launch and scaling of GitHub Copilot, the AI pair-programming product that became one of the highest-profile applications of large language models in software development [^1]. Outside of his operating roles, Dohmke has made a small set of angel investments concentrated in developer tooling and infrastructure [^4][^5].

## Stated Thesis

Dohmke has not published a written angel investing thesis. His public commentary at Entire's launch frames the problem he is now building against, which also aligns with the developer-infrastructure companies he has angel-backed: "We are living through an agent boom, and now massive volumes of code are being generated faster than any human could reasonably understand" [^1]. Self-reported sector emphasis from his public investor listings centers on Enterprise Infrastructure and Enterprise Applications, with explicit focus on "accelerating innovation in software development through generative AI technologies" [^4].

## Inferred Thesis

The independently verifiable angel portfolio is small — five companies tracked by third-party aggregators [^4][^5]. With this sample size, qualitative description is more honest than percentages.

- **Stage:** Verified checks span seed (Resolve AI, Tangled) and Series A (Doppler). No participation in growth or late-stage rounds has been disclosed.
- **Sector:** Every verified investment is in developer tooling or developer-adjacent infrastructure: Resolve AI (AI for production incident response), Doppler (secrets management), and Tangled (code collaboration / open-source GitHub alternative) [^4][^5][^6][^7].
- **Geography:** Mixed — US (Resolve, Doppler) and Europe (Tangled, headquartered in Finland) [^6][^7].
- **Co-investor pattern (small sample):** On Tangled, Dohmke appeared alongside byFounders (lead), Bain Capital Crypto, Antler, Avery Pennarun (Tailscale CEO), and Mårten Mickos [^6][^7].
- **Notable gap:** Despite being one of the most recognizable figures in developer-tools leadership, the publicly traceable angel portfolio is much smaller than peer ex-CEOs of similar profile. The plausible reading is that Dohmke does most of his early-stage involvement informally or operationally rather than through priced angel checks.

This analysis is based on 3 independently verified investments plus aggregator counts of 5 total; the verification rate is therefore ~60% of claimed portfolio.
