## Background

Kunal Shah was born May 30, 1979, and is based in Maharashtra, India [^1]. He completed a BA in Philosophy at Wilson College, Mumbai, then began but did not complete a part-time MBA at NMIMS, dropping out in 2004 [^1].

In 2009, Shah founded PaisaBack, a cashback promotions company [^1]. In 2010 he co-founded FreeCharge with Sandeep Tandon [^1]. FreeCharge was acquired by Snapdeal in 2015 for approximately ₹2,800 crore (roughly $400–450 million at the time) [^1]. Following the FreeCharge exit, Shah founded CRED, a members-only credit card bill payment rewards platform operated by Dreamplug Technologies Pvt Ltd [^1] [^2].

Wikipedia reports Shah has made over 200 angel investments, citing his position among India's most active angel investors by deal volume in 2022, with fintech companies Razorpay and BharatPe named among his investments [^1]. He is also listed as an Independent Director at Syrma SGS Technology Limited [^1].

**Recognition** [^1]:
- Fortune India 40 Under 40 (2016)
- "Comeback Kid of the Year," Economic Times Startup Awards (2016)
