## Background

Kevin Colleran is Co-Founder and Managing Director of Slow Ventures, an early-stage generalist venture capital firm with offices in San Francisco, Boston, and New York [^1]. He is based in Boston, Massachusetts [^2].

Before investing, Colleran joined Facebook in early 2005 as one of the company's first 10 employees and its first advertising sales hire [^1][^3]. Over 6.5 years he built and ran global advertising partnerships with Coca-Cola, Procter & Gamble, Johnson & Johnson, Walmart, and other multinational brands [^1]. By the time he left Facebook in July 2011, he was the company's longest-tenured employee other than Mark Zuckerberg [^3].

He attended Babson College from 1999 to 2003 [^2]. Slow Ventures was founded in 2011 [^4]; the firm has deployed over $800M across multiple funds, with a Fund VI plus opportunity vehicle raise of $275M under way as of a December 2024 SEC filing [^4]. Sam Lessin (General Partner) and Will Quist (Partner, joined 2015) are Colleran's current partners at Slow; co-founder Dave Morin departed to launch Offline Ventures in 2020 [^4].

## Stated Thesis

Colleran publicly describes his focus as backing consumer-facing companies where founders can build durable audience relationships and monetize early. In an interview about his approach, he emphasized four principles: consumer obsession ("products loved by users, not just procured by buyers"), earned secrets (teams with unique insider insight), monetization discipline from day one, and rapid feedback loops [^5].

He has publicly framed the creator economy as a legitimate asset class, and Slow Ventures launched a dedicated Creator Fund I in February 2025 with over $60M in commitments from institutional LPs including the University of Michigan and MIT [^6]. The fund invests approximately $1M-$3M for roughly 10% stakes in creator holding companies [^6].

## Inferred Thesis

Kevin Colleran's personal deal record (as tracked publicly on Signal by NFX) shows 66 recorded investments, with 51 disclosed in detail [^7]. Based on this disclosed sample, the pattern is:

- **Stage**: predominantly Seed and Series A, with pre-seed angel checks in the $25K-$100K range and Slow-led investments typically $250K-$3M with a $1.5M sweet spot [^7][^5].
- **Sector concentration**: consumer, direct-to-consumer, creator economy, digital health, and fintech [^7].
- **Consumer bias**: the vast majority of disclosed deals (Hint, ROOM, Dash Radio, Porter Road Butcher, Chatous, WAX) are consumer or consumer-adjacent brands, not enterprise SaaS [^7].
- **Geography**: US-focused with concentration in Boston, San Francisco, and New York — matching Slow Ventures' three-city office footprint [^1].
- **Repeat commitment**: multiple companies (ROOM, Chatous) received follow-on capital at both Seed and Series A, indicating a preference for supporting companies across rounds [^7].

Sample size is small enough that percentages would be misleading; the qualitative picture is a consumer-first check-writer using Facebook-scale go-to-market experience to help founders with distribution and partnerships. Note that this reflects deals publicly attributable to Colleran personally — Slow Ventures' broader portfolio (Airtable, Solana, Gusto, Robinhood, Pinterest, Slack, Allbirds, PillPack, Postmates, Nextdoor) reflects the firm's collective activity across partners since 2011 [^1][^4].
