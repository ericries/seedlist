## Background

Isaiah N. Granet is the co-founder and CEO of Bland, a San Francisco voice AI platform for enterprise phone calls that he started in 2023 with Sobhan Nejad [^1][^2]. He was born and raised in San Diego and holds computer science and economics degrees from Washington University in St. Louis [^3]. Before Bland he worked as an engineer at an early-stage startup, participated in Z Fellows, and went through Y Combinator with an earlier healthcare startup that later shut down [^1][^3]. As a teenager he founded San Diego Chill, a nonprofit that teaches ice hockey to children with developmental disabilities and has raised more than $2.5 million; he remains involved at the board level [^1]. He was a 2016 Diller Teen Tikkun Olam Award recipient for that work [^1].

Bland went through YC's Summer 2023 batch and has since raised more than $100M across seed, Series A, Series B, and a June 2026 Series C led by Dell Technologies Capital [^2][^4]. Emergence Capital led the Series B alongside continued participation from Scale Venture Partners, Y Combinator, and angel investors including Max Levchin [^4][^5].

## In Their Own Words

On why he started building companies: "I didn't get enough attention as a kid. So I had to do something that would let me get more" [^3].

On seeing AI's trajectory: "Oh, no, the world is going to change really, really fast" [^3].

On competitive intensity: "Never be shy to do what you have to do to win" [^3].

On team culture: "Say the ugly thing out loud" [^3].

On how he evaluates opportunities: "What is the value? How do we provide it? That's the fundamental formula for winning" [^3].

On Bland's product focus: "Most voice AI systems are built for simple interactions. We are focused on the calls that are hardest to automate" [^4].
