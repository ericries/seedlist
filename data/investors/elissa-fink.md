## Background

Elissa Fink is the former Chief Marketing Officer of Tableau Software, where she led marketing from 2007 through her retirement in early 2019, taking the company from roughly $5M in revenue to more than $1B in annual revenue and through its 2013 IPO and 2019 acquisition by Salesforce [^1][^2][^3]. She now works as an independent board director, advisor, angel investor, and adjunct/affiliate instructor at the University of Washington Foster School of Business [^1][^2].

Fink holds a BA from Santa Clara University and an MBA in Marketing and Decision Systems from USC Marshall (1993) [^1][^2]. She began her career selling advertising for the Wall Street Journal, an experience she credits with sparking her interest in analytics and readership data [^4]. Before Tableau she held marketing and product roles at Claritas (1990–2000), TopTier Software (2000–2001), Tele Atlas — now TomTom — (2001–2004), and CMO/EVP at IXI Corp — now part of Equifax — (2004–2007) [^2].

She is based in Seattle, Washington [^5][^6].

## Stated Thesis

Fink does not publish a formal investment thesis. In interviews she has described a personal framework she calls "Team TAM Time" for evaluating early-stage B2B/enterprise startups: she trusts her "gut backed by data," and that gut "boils down to Team TAM Time" — is the team credible and hard-working, is the total addressable market large enough, and is the timing right for the product to mature into the market [^7]. Her public work focuses on B2B/enterprise SaaS at the zero-to-one and scaling stages [^7][^1].

## Inferred Thesis

Fink's public angel-investing footprint is small — only three seed rounds are independently verifiable — so quantitative percentages would be misleading. Qualitative patterns from those three deals plus her board and advisory portfolio:

- **Stage:** Every disclosed direct angel check is at seed (Magnify, Goldcast, Keyplay) [^8][^9][^10].
- **Sector:** B2B marketing and sales technology adjacent to her Tableau operating experience — virtual event software (Goldcast), account intelligence for GTM teams (Keyplay), customer retention/renewal software (Magnify) [^8][^9][^10].
- **Geography:** Portfolio and board seats concentrate in the US Pacific Northwest (Seattle) and the broader US enterprise-software ecosystem [^8][^10][^5].
- **Co-investors:** She has co-invested alongside Madrona Venture Group (Magnify lead), MHS Capital (Keyplay lead), Unusual Ventures and HubSpot Ventures (Goldcast leads), and operator-angels including Aaron Bird, Scott Belsky, Chandar Pattabhiram, Elias Torres, Sangram Vajre, Kris Rudeegraap, and Lenny Rachitsky [^8][^9][^10].
- **LP activity:** She is a returning investor / LP in Madrona Venture Labs and a Venture LP at Stage 2 Capital, both operator-led funds — suggesting she prefers to deploy capital through operator-networks in addition to direct checks [^11][^12].
- **Board vs. angel split:** Board and advisory roles vastly outnumber direct angel checks. She is best characterized as an operator-turned-board-director who writes occasional seed checks in her domain of expertise (B2B marketing tooling), not as a full-time investor. Sample size: 3 verified angel deals.
