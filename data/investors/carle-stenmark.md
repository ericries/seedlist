## Background

Carle Stenmark is a General Partner at VMG Partners, where he leads VMG Technology — the firm's fund focused on B2B software that serves the consumer, retail, and manufacturing ecosystem [^1][^2]. VMG Partners operates two verticals — VMG Consumer (brand investing) and VMG Technology (software) — and describes VMG Technology as writing $4M–$40M "meaningful minority" checks from early stage through Series C [^2].

According to his LinkedIn profile, Stenmark grew up in Denver's Park Hill neighborhood and holds an MBA from Harvard Business School [^3]. His VMG bio characterizes his approach as backing "founder-driven teams solving real problems" and building "consumer-centric businesses that generate organic adoption through word-of-mouth" [^1].

VMG Technology's portfolio-page listing spans mar tech, ad tech, supply chain, CFO tech, operations, payments, merchandising, loyalty, marketplaces, vertical AI, and CX tech [^2][^4].

## Stated Thesis

VMG Technology publicly frames its strategy as leveraging "two decades of building breakout brands" in consumer investing to guide B2B software bets that serve the retail and manufacturing economy [^2]. On his personal profile page, Stenmark writes:

> "Companies delivering tremendous end-user value with strong, easy-to-communicate brands spend significantly less on sales" because customer enthusiasm drives adoption naturally. [^1]

On the June 2026 Scotch Series A that VMG led, Stenmark described the target market this way:

> "The beverage alcohol market is nearly $250 billion and, despite that, is still operating on systems built in the 1970s with on-prem servers." [^5]

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size caveat.** This analysis uses the 26 companies listed on the VMG Technology portfolio page as VMG Technology's declared investments [^4]. Only a subset of those deals is publicly attributable to Stenmark specifically as lead or board partner — that subset is 8 companies (per his own LinkedIn portfolio listing [^3] and the Scotch press announcement [^5]). Where percentages are shown below, they are for the 26-company VMG Technology portfolio, not Stenmark personally.

**Sector concentration: B2B software for the consumer / retail economy.** All 26 VMG Technology portfolio companies build software or software-enabled services for the consumer ecosystem [^4]. Sub-sectors visible from the portfolio list include:
- Retail / merchant infrastructure (Scotch, Boulevard, Nowsta, Nuvemshop, Zitcha) — 5 of 26
- Marketing / ad tech / brand analytics (Attentive, Tracksuit, Fermat, Auxia, Pray.com, Good Face Project) — 6 of 26
- Supply chain / operations / grocery (Afresh, Lyric, Milk Moovement, ParcelLabs, Specright) — 5 of 26
- Marketplaces / commerce (Weee!, Bobbie, Musely, Swell, Shogun Labs) — 5 of 26
- Vertical AI / marketing automation (Gradial, Claim, Daasity, Rinsed, Necessaire) — 5 of 26

**Stages: early through Series C.** VMG Technology explicitly targets "early to Series C" with $4M–$40M check sizes and takes "meaningful minority positions" [^2]. Stenmark led Scotch's Series A in June 2026 [^5] and participated in Gradial's Series C in June 2026 [^6].

**Distinctive strategy signal from the Scotch investment.** In Crunchbase News' coverage, Stenmark described VMG's thesis on Scotch as backing a company that addressed "enterprise-level complexity that competing startups overlooked" — targeting the hardest segment first rather than starting with SMBs. This "hardened product" playbook is consistent with VMG's stated preference for durable market positions over land-and-expand SaaS [^5].

**Board / lead patterns.** Verified lead investment (with board seat implied): Scotch Series A, June 2026 [^5]. Verified participation: Gradial Series C, June 2026 (co-invested alongside Insight Partners as existing investor) [^6]. His LinkedIn identifies Weee! as a "long-term partnership since 2019" [^3].

**Geography: US-heavy.** Named deals (Scotch — Denver; Gradial — Seattle; Afresh, Weee!, Boulevard — US) are US-based. VMG Partners is headquartered in San Francisco [^1].
