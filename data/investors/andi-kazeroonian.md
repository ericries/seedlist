## Background

Andi Kazeroonian is a Principal at Outward VC, a London-based early-stage fintech venture capital firm founded in 2019 by Kevin Chong and Devin Kohli [^1][^2]. He is British-Iranian and joined Outward as part of the founding team, where he helped structure the firm's inaugural fund and onboarded more than 60 limited partners [^3]. Prior to Outward, he worked in investment banking and corporate finance roles at Investec and Cantor Fitzgerald, and holds a BA in Economics and Finance from the University of Exeter [^4].

At Outward, Kazeroonian focuses on compliance-heavy fintech and regulated financial services — categories where deep operator and regulatory expertise is required to underwrite an early-stage bet [^3][^5]. He typically writes checks in the $750K–$1.5M range with a sweet spot around $1.1M, at pre-seed and seed [^4].

## Stated Thesis

Kazeroonian's public writing on the Outward VC blog articulates a preference for founders solving structural problems in financial services that "everyone recognises, yet few have successfully addressed" — the kind of dense, compliance-heavy problem space where regulation and network effects act as moats rather than obstacles [^5]. In his Steward investment memo he describes wanting to back founders "we know and trust as friends," reflecting a repeat-founder / warm-network sourcing bias [^5].

Writing on the Firenze investment, he framed Outward's thesis as being "around the power of combining technology, networks and innovative structures to open up exclusive financial products to a much larger audience" [^6].

Outward VC as a firm positions itself as an "anti-hype" investor emphasizing candid founder communication and human capital alongside financial investment [^3].

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size:** six verified portfolio investments over 2020–2026 [^4]. Small denominator; percentages below are shown as raw counts.

- **Sector concentration:** all six investments are UK/European fintech infrastructure or regulated-services plays: eXate (data compliance / privacy), Finverity (supply-chain finance), Vauban (fund-formation infrastructure), Adclear (fintech), Firenze (Lombard lending / credit infrastructure), and Steward (AI-first AML compliance) [^4]. Six of six (100%) sit inside compliance-heavy or infrastructure fintech.
- **Stage:** predominantly seed and pre-seed. Five of six rounds are seed or pre-seed; one (Firenze) is a Series A follow-on [^4][^7].
- **Geography:** UK/European. Firenze (UK), Vauban (UK, now Cayman), Steward (US-founded but backed alongside Motive Ventures on a $5M pre-seed — the outlier) [^5][^8].
- **Deal role:** Andi has publicly led at least two rounds — Vauban (£4.7M in 2021 with Outward as lead) and Firenze's £2.5M seed in March 2025 with Outward as lead [^7][^8]. On Steward and Firenze's £6M Series A follow-on he participated alongside a lead (Motive Ventures and AlbionVC respectively) [^5][^9].
- **Co-investor pattern:** repeat co-investors include Form Ventures and Portfolio Ventures (both participated with Outward on Firenze's seed), AlbionVC (Firenze Series A lead), and Motive Ventures (Steward lead) [^6][^8][^5]. Pentech Ventures, PiLabs, 7percent Ventures, and Westloop Ventures participated alongside Outward on Vauban [^7].
- **Founder-relationship pattern:** Kazeroonian pre-committed to back any future venture by Vauban's founders after Vauban's acquisition — a rare structural commitment that led directly to the Steward investment [^3][^5]. This is a distinctive re-up pattern: the same founders, twice.
- **Board involvement:** frequently serves as a Board Observer at portfolio companies [^4].

**Delta between stated and inferred:** stated and inferred thesis align tightly — Kazeroonian says he backs compliance-heavy, regulated fintech from founders he knows deeply, and his portfolio is 100% compliance-heavy fintech with a documented repeat-founder pattern. No meaningful gap.
