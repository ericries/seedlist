## Background

Yuri Danilchenko is co-founder and CTO of Latitud, a pre-seed venture fund and startup infrastructure platform for Latin America [^1][^2]. Latitud was founded in 2020 alongside Brian Requarth (former CEO of VivaReal) and Gina Gotthilf (former VP Growth at Duolingo) [^2][^3].

Before Latitud, Danilchenko was CTO at Escale, a Brazilian marketing technology company, where he led the technical organization through a rapid scaling period following the company's Series B, which was backed by Kaszek Ventures and Global Founders Capital (GFC) [^4][^5]. Andreessen Horowitz partner Angela Strange, writing about a16z's investment in Latitud, described Danilchenko as a "technical leader with CTO/CPO experience at startups including Escale and Vemos" [^2].

At Latitud, Danilchenko is responsible for building the product and engineering organization behind Latitud Go (the company formation product) and the broader Latitud platform [^4]. Latitud raised an $11.5 million seed round in March 2022 co-led by Andreessen Horowitz and NFX, with participation from Endeavor, Canary, FJ Labs, Ganas Ventures, and 100+ founder-angels including David Vélez (Nubank), Sebastian Mejia (Rappi), and Carlos García (Kavak) [^2][^3][^6].

Danilchenko is listed publicly as an advisor to BHub, a Latitud portfolio company [^7].
