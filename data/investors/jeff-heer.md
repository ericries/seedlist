## Background

Jeff Heer is the Jerre D. Noe Endowed Professor of Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Washington's Paul G. Allen School, where he co-directs the Interactive Data Lab [^1]. He holds B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees in Computer Science from UC Berkeley, and served as faculty at Stanford from 2009 to 2013 before joining UW [^1]. His academic awards include MIT Technology Review's TR35 (2009), a Sloan Fellowship (2012), the ACM Grace Murray Hopper Award (2016), the IEEE Visualization Technical Achievement Award (2017), and induction into both the IEEE Visualization Academy (2019) and the ACM CHI Academy (2021) [^1].

Heer's lab has produced an unusually influential set of open-source data visualization tools, including D3.js (with his former student Mike Bostock, now founder of Observable), the Vega and Vega-Lite declarative visualization grammars, the Mosaic framework, and predecessors Protovis and Prefuse [^1][^2]. These libraries are widely used across industry and academia.

In October 2012, Heer co-founded Trifacta with UC Berkeley professor Joe Hellerstein and Stanford Ph.D. Sean Kandel, commercializing joint research on interactive data wrangling [^2]. Trifacta raised more than $200M across four rounds — $4.3M Series A (Accel, 2012), $12M Series B (Greylock, December 2013), $25M Series C (Ignition Partners, May 2014), and $48M Series D (Columbia Pacific, Deutsche Börse, Ericsson, Google, New York Life, January 2018), plus a $35M raise in February 2016 [^2]. Heer served as Trifacta's Chief Experience Officer alongside his UW faculty role. Alteryx completed its acquisition of Trifacta for **$400 million in all-cash** in February 2022, integrating the technology into Alteryx Designer Cloud [^2][^3].

In April 2026, Heer emerged from stealth as Co-Founder and Chief Scientist of **Ridge AI**, an AI-native embedded analytics platform for B2B SaaS, alongside CEO Ellie Fields (former Tableau executive) [^4][^5][^6]. Ridge AI raised a $2.6M pre-seed led by Madrona Venture Group (Tim Porter, Mark Nelson) with TheFounderVC and angels Chris Stolte (Tableau co-founder), Adrien Treuille (Streamlit), Elissa Fink (former Tableau CMO), Jeff Hammerbacher (Cloudera), and Carlos Guestrin (Stanford AI Lab) [^4][^5][^6].
