## Background

Paul Lwin is the co-founder and CEO of Havoc (also branded HavocAI), a Providence, Rhode Island–based collaborative-autonomy defense startup building software and ultra-low-cost uncrewed surface vessels for maritime, air, and ground platforms [^1][^2]. He founded the company in early 2024 with COO Joe Turner, a fellow Navy veteran and childhood friend [^3].

Lwin fled Burma (Myanmar) as a refugee at age 10 in 1995 with his mother and two siblings, joining his father in California with assistance from the U.S. embassy in Burma; he became a U.S. citizen in 2000 [^3]. He graduated from the U.S. Naval Academy and served 11 years in the U.S. Navy as a flight officer, including two years in the Persian Gulf, earning three air medals for combat missions in Iraq and Afghanistan [^3]. His prior naval roles included Weapons Systems Officer and Engineering Test Flight Officer at Point Mugu Naval Air Station, California [^4][^5].

Lwin holds an MBA from the Yale School of Management (2018–2020), an MS in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins, and further advanced coursework in aerospace engineering and test & evaluation from the University of Maryland and the U.S. Naval Test Pilot School [^4][^5]. In August 2025 he was named to the inaugural WP Intelligence Council, the Washington Post's global-security leadership group [^6].

Under Lwin's leadership, Havoc closed an $11M Seed round on September 20, 2024, led by Scout Ventures and co-led by Trousdale Ventures, with participation from Outlander VC, The Veteran Fund, BV.VC, Decisive Point, Alumni Ventures, and New North Ventures [^7]. On May 12, 2026, the company announced a $100M Series A with a broad syndicate of new and returning backers including Cobalt Capital, Boardman Bay Capital Management, B Capital, Lockheed Martin, Taiwania Capital, UP.Partners, and SAIC, bringing total funding to approximately $200M within roughly two years of founding [^1][^8].
