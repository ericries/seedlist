## Background

Victoria Beasley is a founding partner and General Partner at Gigascale Capital, an early-stage climate technology firm launched in 2023 by former Meta CTO Mike Schroepfer [^1][^2]. She co-founded the firm with Schroepfer and Evaline Tsai and, per Gigascale's team page, co-leads firm strategy with Schroepfer, oversees firm operations, and manages both the investment and platform teams [^1]. Gigascale announced a $250M Fund I on June 1, 2026 after operating for two years as Schroepfer's personal investment vehicle [^3].

Prior to Gigascale, Beasley was a General Partner at Prelude Ventures, a climate-focused fund with approximately $1.5B in AUM, where she led 12 investments spanning energy, mobility, food and agriculture, carbon management, and materials and industrials [^1]. She joined Prelude in 2016 [^4]. Earlier in her career, Beasley was Head of Finance at Suniva, an early-stage solar manufacturer, where she helped raise $105M in equity to fund global manufacturing expansion [^1]. She also founded an agriculture supply chain company, worked on climate change strategy at Boston Consulting Group, and interned at d.light, the off-grid solar company [^1].

Beasley grew up in rural North Carolina in a family of hog farmers before moving into climate tech [^4][^5]. She holds an MBA from the Stanford Graduate School of Business, an MS in Environment and Resources from Stanford, and a BS in Biology from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill [^1].

## Stated Thesis

Beasley publicly describes her focus as backing deep-tech founders building "scalable, practical climate solutions" [^1]. Writing in CleanTechnica in March 2024, she framed her hardware conviction as follows:

> "Everything under the hard tech umbrella — hardware, deep tech, and science-driven innovation — is now commanding attention." [^6]

She has said the best pitches she sees start with a user-discovery insight rather than a technology insight:

> "The best pitches start with 'I visited dozens of target users and realized that the real pain point or gap in the market is X.'" [^6]

In a Canary Media profile published February 23, 2024, Beasley described her core belief and current mandate:

> "I believed early on that there was a massive business opportunity in transitioning every industry on the planet toward sustainability." [^5]

She characterized Gigascale's current work as "investing in early-stage climate companies that are using mostly hard tech to take on the biggest decarbonization opportunities" [^5].

## Inferred Thesis

Beasley's disclosed investment record is small enough that percentages would be misleading; the analysis below is qualitative.

- **Sector concentration:** Deep-tech and hardware climate solutions dominate. At Prelude, her disclosed board-level investments included recreational-vehicle electrification (Lightship), enterprise carbon accounting SaaS (Persefoni), and branded resale infrastructure (Trove) [^7][^8][^9]. At Gigascale, her board-observer roles concentrate in physical climate infrastructure: heat-pump sales enablement (Arch), CO2-to-chemicals electrolysis (Dioxycle), magnesium battery systems (Estes), residential energy retrofits (Homeboost), and Light Energy [^1][^10][^11][^12].
- **Stage:** Pre-seed through Series A, occasionally Series B. Beasley led Lightship's $23M Series A (2022-07-12) [^7] and Prelude's participation in Persefoni's $101M Series B lead alongside TPG (2021-10-28) [^8]. At Gigascale she participates in seed and Series A rounds [^10][^11].
- **Check size:** Historical range at Prelude spanned $100K-$5M across pre-seed through Series B+ [^13].
- **Geography:** US-based deep-tech climate companies, with some cross-Atlantic exposure (Dioxycle is French-American) [^11].
- **Founder profile:** Preference for founders drawn from at-scale hardware and energy operators. Lightship's founders came out of Tesla [^7]; Estes' team came from Tesla, Sila, and Cummins [^12].
- **Co-investor patterns at Gigascale:** Frequent co-participation with Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Lowercarbon Capital, DCVC, BMW iVentures, Fortescue Capital, Baukunst, Coatue, Floodgate, MCJ Collective, ReGen Ventures, True Ventures, and Incite.org across her Gigascale portfolio companies [^10][^11][^12][^14].

Note: this analysis is based on a limited set of publicly disclosed board seats and observer roles; Gigascale has invested in 25+ companies, most of which are undisclosed [^3].
