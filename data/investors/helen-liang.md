## Background

Helen Liang is the Founder and Managing Partner of FoundersX Ventures, a Silicon Valley-based, AI-first venture capital firm she founded in 2016 with backing from a Stanford founders circle [^1][^2]. She holds a PhD from the University of Wisconsin-Madison and completed Harvard Business School's PE/VC program in 2022 [^1]. Before founding FoundersX, she spent more than a decade in product roles in the high-tech industry [^1]. She is a guest lecturer at Stanford and an invited speaker at TechCrunch events [^1]. FoundersX operates from Menlo Park with additional hubs in Cambridge, MA and New York [^1][^3].

## Stated Thesis

FoundersX publicly describes itself as "AI-first," investing in "full-stack AI, from next-gen compute chips, AI & Space infrastructure, physical AI foundation models and robotics, to Agentic AI solutions across industries" [^3]. Liang has emphasized agentic AI as the firm's defining bet for 2025-2026:

> "2025 marked the rise of agentic AI as a fundamental driver of business transformation." [^3]

> "We expect agentic AI adoption to expand dramatically, reaching mass-market levels in consumer and enterprise applications." [^3]

## Inferred Thesis

Sample size is modest — the firm publicly highlights approximately 15-20 specific portfolio companies, with claims of 100+ total investments [^1][^2][^3]. Based on the named companies that can be verified:

- **Fintech is the most visible sector** — Kapital (LatAm SMB lending), Jeeves (cross-border expense), Kontigo (stablecoin neobank), Alinea Invest (Gen Z investing), Bitstack (Bitcoin neobank) [^3]. Five of the ~15 named portfolio companies (~33%) are fintech, skewing toward Latin America and underserved consumer segments.
- **AI infrastructure and applied AI dominate the rest** — Solve Intelligence (legal AI), Arintra (medical billing AI), Jericho Security (cybersecurity AI), 1910 Genetics, Kernel Bio, Linvest21.ai, Cognito TX [^1][^2].
- **Stage:** Predominantly seed, with FoundersX highlighting that eight seed-stage portfolio companies raised $120M+ in Series A in 2025 [^2].
- **Geography:** US-headquartered companies with notable LatAm fintech exposure.
- **Notable gap:** Despite "robotics" and "space infrastructure" in stated thesis, the publicly-named portfolio skews heavily toward software/AI applications rather than hardware.

Sample size caveat: percentages above reflect only the ~15 publicly-named companies the firm highlights; the broader 100+ portfolio is not independently verifiable.
