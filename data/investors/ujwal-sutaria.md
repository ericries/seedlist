## Background

Ujwal Sutaria is Founder and General Partner of TDV Partners (Trillion Dollar Ventures), a Bengaluru-based pre-seed micro-VC he established in July 2021 [^1][^2]. He is a two-time entrepreneur who previously founded Athletto, an online booking platform for sports and fitness venues, and FruitOn, a cold-pressed juice delivery service; both were shut down before he moved into institutional investing [^1][^3].

Before TDV, Sutaria was an Associate Director at InnoVen Capital, where he sourced, evaluated, and executed venture debt deals across India and helped manage a portfolio that included Swiggy, BlackBuck, Eruditus, Licious, DealShare, Epigamia, and FreshMenu [^1][^3]. He holds a B.Tech. from the National Institute of Technology, Nagpur, and an MBA from the Indian Institute of Management, Ahmedabad, where he was a CIIE Young IIMaverick Fellow [^1][^3].

TDV Partners operates two funds. Fund I closed around $3M and wrote roughly $100K first cheques with an average portfolio-level MOIC that Inc42 pegged at approximately 80% [^4]. Fund II, launched in October 2024, has a corpus of INR 50 Cr (approximately $5.8M) and writes $100K-$500K first cheques, targeting 20-25 investments over two years [^4][^5]. The firm reports 40+ investments to date [^2].

## Stated Thesis

TDV Partners publicly describes its focus as "First cheque. Infinite conviction. For founders who dare to dream big and build iconic companies," positioning itself as a day-zero investor "at day zero, helping founders get the fundamentals right before everything compounds. No product, no revenue required" [^2]. The firm names three explicit focus areas on its site [^2]:

1. **Consumer tech** — tech-driven solutions for daily consumer needs.
2. **Consumer upgrade** — products serving India's rising middle-class aspirations.
3. **Consumer AI** — AI tools that improve consumer productivity.

Sutaria has publicly described his diligence approach as high-volume, high-selectivity: he personally reviews the pitches TDV receives and funds under 1% of them, using what he calls a proprietary algorithmic evaluation on top of his own screen [^4].

## Inferred Thesis

This analysis is based on TDV Partners' published portfolio page (42 companies listed as of July 2026) plus contemporaneous press citations for individual rounds [^6]. Sample size is meaningful (n=42) but stage/date data comes from press rather than firm disclosures, so per-round breakdowns below are directional.

**Sector distribution (42 portfolio companies from tdv.partners/portfolio [^6]):**
- **Consumer commerce & D2C**: 10 of 42 (24%) — Kindlife, Furnishka, Acai Theory, Eternz, Rapawalk, Zulu Club, The Fourth Layer, Marrfa, Goodmelts, Steath (fresh food).
- **Faith/spirituality tech**: 4 of 42 (10%) — AppsForBharat (Sri Mandir), DevDham, SoulSensei, Stealth (Spiritual stays).
- **Consumer AI / creator tools**: 8 of 42 (19%) — Aftershoot, Hypergro, Preimage, Nector, Siftly, Mindbase AI, AppX, S45.
- **Fintech / consumer finance**: 5 of 42 (12%) — SalarySe, Goodscore, India Gold, Flexprice, TradeX.
- **Vertical marketplaces & work platforms**: 5 of 42 (12%) — Awign, HireQuotient, Kovon, Sports For Life, Healofy.
- **Infrastructure/enterprise adjacencies**: 5 of 42 (12%) — Spintly, Table Sprint, Reflexical, Vecros, QuantE.
- **Other/stealth**: 5 of 42 (12%) — several stealth categories plus Voice Club, Stan, Ivory.

The self-described "consumer tech / consumer upgrade / consumer AI" framing lines up cleanly with the actual portfolio: 25 of the 42 companies (60%) are direct-to-consumer or consumer-adjacent. There is no documented investment in defense, space, dual-use, or industrial deep-tech categories in TDV's disclosed portfolio [^6].

**Stage distribution:** Explicitly pre-seed dominant. Sutaria has stated Fund II targets 80% "idea/paper-stage" companies before product or revenue [^4]. Tracxn categorises TDV's disclosed rounds as 15 seed and 1 Series A with an average seed check of $846K (round total including co-investors, not TDV's share) [^7].

**Geographic focus:** India-centric. Portfolio companies are predominantly Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, and Mumbai based; a subset (Marrfa in Middle East real estate, Kovon in global talent mobility) have cross-border remits [^6].

**Co-investor patterns:** The most consistently observed co-investor patterns are with other India pre-seed / early-stage micro-funds and operator angels. On DevDham's January 2024 INR 6 Cr seed, TDV co-invested alongside Titan Capital, All In Capital, and Veda VC [^8]. Follow-on rounds by portfolio companies have brought in Peak XV Surge, Elevation Capital, Fundamentum (Nilekani), Flourish Ventures, and Susquehanna Asia VC downstream — Sutaria's early-stage bets have been picked up by growth-stage firms in ~40% of cases per his own accounting (17 of 42 with follow-on funding [^4]).

**Notable gaps:** No defense, space, dual-use, industrial deep tech, climate hardware, or biotech investments are documented in the disclosed portfolio, despite these being visible themes in the broader Indian startup ecosystem in 2024-2026 [^6]. TDV's investing is squarely on the consumer-internet / consumer-AI axis.
