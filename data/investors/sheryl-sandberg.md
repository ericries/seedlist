## Background

Sheryl Sandberg served as Chief Operating Officer of Facebook (later Meta Platforms) from March 2008 until stepping down in August 2022 [^1]. She joined Meta's board of directors in 2012 as its first female board member and stepped down from the board in May 2024 after announcing her departure in January 2024 [^2][^3]. Before Facebook, Sandberg led Google's online sales and advertising operations from 2001 to 2008, and earlier served as chief of staff to U.S. Treasury Secretary Lawrence Summers from 1996 to 2001 [^1]. She holds an AB in economics (1991) and MBA (1995) from Harvard [^1].

Sandberg is the founder of LeanIn.Org and Option B, philanthropic initiatives housed under the Sandberg Goldberg Bernthal Family Foundation [^1][^4]. When announcing her COO departure in 2022, she cited a desire to focus more on philanthropic work [^3].

In parallel with her philanthropic work, Sandberg and her husband Tom Bernthal established **Sandberg Bernthal Venture Partners (SBVP)**, a family-office venture vehicle based in Menlo Park, California [^5][^6]. Bloomberg Law reported the November 2023 backing of Cercle as "one of the first investments" made through the fund [^5], while other aggregators list the firm's founding as 2021 or 2022 [^6][^7]. The firm's public presence is minimal — sbvp.com displays only the firm name, and its LinkedIn page describes its mission as "Investing, supporting and scaling alongside great entrepreneurs" [^8].

## Stated Thesis

SBVP has no public thesis statement beyond its LinkedIn tagline: "Investing, supporting and scaling alongside great entrepreneurs" [^8]. The firm does not maintain a public website with portfolio pages or investment criteria [^7].

In her comment on the Guild Education investment, Sandberg stated: "I believe deeply in the power of education to change the trajectory of a person's life, and Guild is creating a more equitable path to quality education" [^9].

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size caveat:** Only four investments (Cercle, Pigment, Terradot, Flint) have been independently confirmed via contemporaneous primary or press-release sources. The sample is too small for reliable percentage breakdowns. What follows is a qualitative description of observed patterns.

- **Sector mix:** Verified investments span women's health AI (Cercle), enterprise planning software (Pigment), carbon removal (Terradot), and AI-native web infrastructure (Flint) [^5][^10][^11][^12]. This is a generalist family-office pattern rather than a sector-specialist thesis.
- **Stage:** Investments observed at pre-seed (Cercle, ~$4.2M pre-seed in Nov 2023 [^5]), seed (Flint, $5M in Oct 2025 [^12]), and growth (Pigment Series D, $145M in April 2024 [^10]; Terradot launch round with $58.2M in Dec 2024 [^11]). The firm has participated as a follow-on/growth investor, not as a lead.
- **Co-investors:** Confirmed co-investment alongside ICONIQ Growth (Pigment) [^10], Accel and Neo (Flint) [^12], and John Doerr / Kleiner Perkins / Floodgate / Google / Microsoft (Terradot) [^11]. Pattern skews toward established growth funds and other well-known individuals (John Doerr, George Roberts on Terradot).
- **Founder pattern:** Multiple portfolio founders were sourced through Sandberg's Silicon Valley network — Flint founder Michelle Lim was referred to Sandberg by Ali Partovi at Neo [^13].
- **Deployment cadence:** Investments are infrequent and low-volume relative to a professional venture fund — consistent with a family office deploying personal capital rather than an institutional LP-backed fund [^6][^7].
- **Notable absence:** Despite Sandberg's tenure at Facebook and Google, there is no verified pattern of consumer social or advertising-technology investments in the publicly documented portfolio.

## In Their Own Words

On Guild Education: "I believe deeply in the power of education to change the trajectory of a person's life, and Guild is creating a more equitable path to quality education" [^9].

On Terradot's founding team: "These are proven leaders, which is rare to find in an early-stage company. They have the drive, the right technology and a strong focus on execution to succeed" [^9].

On what she brought to Flint founder Michelle Lim during pitching, referencing her Meta experience scaling website operations: "Michelle, it was 140 people at Meta who had to do this" [^12].

## What Founders Say
