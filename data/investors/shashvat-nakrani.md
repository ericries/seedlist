## Background

Shashvat Nakrani was born on July 14, 1998 in Bhavnagar, Gujarat, and enrolled in a Bachelor's program in Textile Technology at the Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi (2015-2019), which he left during his third year to build BharatPe [^1]. He co-founded BharatPe in March 2018 at the age of 19 with Bhavik Koladiya; Ashneer Grover joined as a third co-founder in July 2018 [^1][^2]. BharatPe built a single QR code that accepted payments from any UPI app, targeting small Indian merchants, and reached a reported $2.9 billion valuation by 2021 [^1].

In 2021, Nakrani was reported as the youngest entrant on the IIFL Wealth Hurun India Rich List [^1]. As of 2026 he remains the largest individual shareholder in BharatPe [^3][^4]. Effective May 1, 2026, he transitioned from Chief Operating Officer to a strategic advisor and board director role, focusing on fundraising, IPO planning, M&A, and long-term strategy [^3][^4][^5].

## Stated Thesis

Nakrani has not published a formal angel-investing thesis. In announcing his shift away from operations at BharatPe, he framed his approach to building (rather than investing specifically) as curiosity-driven: he said that "building has always meant following curiosity, returning to first principles, and exploring ideas that may not always succeed, but are worth pursuing" [^5]. He also said the move is "a step towards exploring something new" while continuing to contribute to BharatPe [^5].

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size caveat:** This analysis is based on a small set of publicly verifiable angel checks (aggregator-reported portfolio of 17-19 companies per Inc42 and Tracxn [^6][^7], with only a subset cross-referenced to a specific round). Percentages are not computed — sample is too small and too dependent on secondary sources.

Qualitative patterns from the verified and aggregator-reported portfolio:

- **Stage:** Predominantly seed-stage angel checks alongside institutional leads [^6][^7][^8].
- **Sectors:** A mix of fintech-adjacent businesses (Dezerv, Yellow Metal), consumer brands (The Whole Truth Foods, WTF Gyms), media/AI (NewsReach), and B2B/SMB tech (Anar) — broadly aligned with categories he encountered building BharatPe [^7][^8].
- **Geography:** India-focused, with portfolio companies headquartered in Indian metros; Inc42 notes investments span India and the US [^6][^9].
- **Founder profile:** Reported portfolio skews toward consumer/SMB founders pursuing large Indian markets (e.g., merchant SaaS, retail health), consistent with BharatPe's own customer base [^7][^8].
- **Notable gap:** Despite his BharatPe background, the public portfolio is not narrowly fintech — consumer brands and health/wellness appear at least as often as financial-services startups [^7][^8].

The portfolio is materially smaller and lower-profile than that of his more public former co-founder Ashneer Grover, consistent with Nakrani's lower media presence.

## In Their Own Words

On founding BharatPe at 19:

> "That same curiosity led me to put everything else on hold, including college, at the age of 19 in 2018, when we founded BharatPe on the IIT Delhi campus." [^5]

On stepping back from day-to-day operations to explore new ventures (April 2026):

> "For me, building has always meant following curiosity, returning to first principles, and exploring ideas that may not always succeed, but are worth pursuing." [^5]

> "For me personally, it's a step towards exploring something new, while staying connected to what I have built. I will always care deeply about BharatPe and will continue to contribute to its journey in every way I can." [^5]
