## Background

Nathan Benaich is the founder and sole general partner of Air Street Capital, a London-based venture firm focused exclusively on "AI-first" companies in Europe and North America [^1]. He holds a Ph.D. and M.Phil. in computational and experimental cancer research from the University of Cambridge, where he was a Gates and Dr. Herchel Smith Scholar, and a BA in Biology from Williams College [^2].

Beyond Air Street, Benaich co-authors the annual *State of AI Report* — produced since 2018 with Plural co-founder Ian Hogarth — which has become one of the most widely cited industry surveys of AI research, industry, politics, and safety [^3][^4]. He founded the Research and Applied AI Summit (RAAIS) and serves as managing trustee of the RAAIS Foundation, a UK charity supporting open-source AI work [^2]. He also leads Spinout.fyi, a policy initiative whose recommendations on UK university spinout terms were adopted by the UK government in 2023 [^2].

Air Street launched in 2020 with a $17M Fund I, followed by a $121M Fund II, and closed a $232M Fund III in March 2026 — making it the largest solo-GP venture firm in Europe with roughly $400M in AUM [^5][^6][^1].

## Stated Thesis

Air Street publicly describes its mandate as backing "AI-first" companies — defined as businesses whose product would not function without AI — across four domains: AI-first software, developer tools and infrastructure, techbio and science, and defense and security [^1]. Benaich has framed the firm's positioning around solo-GP conviction investing, telling TechCrunch that the structure enables "high-conviction investing with a single decision-maker" [^6].

On the broader market, Benaich wrote in the Fund III announcement that "AI-first companies are emerging across every industry" and that "the AI frontier has become the center of gravity of the technology industry" [^1].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on publicly verified portfolio entries (~12 named investments across Funds I–III), Air Street's actual deployment matches its stated AI-first framing closely — there is little observable delta between message and behavior, which is itself notable for a thematic fund.

- **Sector concentration**: Of the named portfolio companies that have been publicly confirmed — Synthesia, Black Forest Labs, Poolside, ElevenLabs, Wayve, Crusoe, Profluent, Enveda Biosciences, Sereact, Lambda, Delian Alliance Industries, V7 Labs — every one is an AI-native company. Roughly half (Black Forest Labs, Poolside, ElevenLabs, Synthesia, Lambda) are foundation-model or generative-media plays; the remainder span techbio (Profluent, Enveda), robotics/autonomy (Wayve, Sereact), AI infrastructure (Crusoe), and defense (Delian Alliance Industries) [^1][^6][^7].
- **Geography**: Mixed European and North American exposure. Wayve, Synthesia, ElevenLabs, Sereact, and Delian are European-founded; Poolside, Profluent, Crusoe, and Lambda are US-headquartered [^1][^6].
- **Stage**: Predominantly seed and Series A entry points, with reserves for growth follow-ons up to $25M [^1][^6]. VCSheet characterizes Air Street as a non-lead participant on most rounds, though several Fund III companies (e.g., Sereact, Delian) have been led by Air Street [^7].
- **Check size**: $500K–$15M at entry; up to $25M at growth [^1].
- **Exits**: Adept (sold to Amazon in 2024), Allcyte (acquired by Exscientia), Valence Discovery (acquired by Recursion), Mapillary (acquired by Facebook), Jukedeck (acquired by ByteDance), and Graphcore (sold to SoftBank) are publicly reported [^6][^8].
- **Founder profile pattern**: A heavy tilt toward technical founders with research credentials — Profluent (Ali Madani, ex-Salesforce Research), Wayve (Alex Kendall, Cambridge PhD), Black Forest Labs (ex-Stability AI Stable Diffusion creators) — consistent with Benaich's own research background [^6].

Sample size note: This analysis is based on ~12 publicly named portfolio companies; Air Street's full active portfolio is larger but not fully disclosed.
