## Background

Martín Varsavsky Waisman-Diamond is an Argentine-Spanish serial entrepreneur, angel investor, and professor born April 26, 1960, in Buenos Aires [^1]. His father, Carlos Varsavsky, was a Harvard-educated astrophysicist; at age 17, Varsavsky and his family fled Argentina as refugees during the military dictatorship [^1]. He earned a BA from New York University (1981), an MA in international affairs from Columbia SIPA (1984), and an MBA from Columbia Business School (1985) [^1] [^2].

Varsavsky has founded more than ten operating companies across telecommunications, internet, renewable energy, fertility medicine, biotechnology, autonomous vehicles, and AI [^1]. The companies he founded or co-founded include:

- **Viatel** (founded 1991) — pan-European fiber/telecom; sold in the late 1990s [^1].
- **Jazztel** (founded 1997) — Iberian telecom operator; IPO December 1999; ultimately acquired by Orange for approximately €3.4 billion in 2015 [^1].
- **Ya.com** (founded 1999) — Spanish internet/DSL provider; sold to Deutsche Telekom's T-Online in 2000 for approximately €650 million [^1].
- **Fon** (founded 2005-2006, Madrid) — global WiFi-sharing network; backed by Google, Skype, Index Ventures, Sequoia Capital, Atomico, Qualcomm Ventures, BT Group, and Deutsche Telekom; Varsavsky exited his shares in 2019 [^1] [^3].
- **Eolia Renovables** (founded 2007) — Spanish renewable energy; sold for ~€1.1 billion [^1].
- **Prelude Fertility** (founded 2016, Atlanta) — largest US chain of fertility clinics, launched with $200M in private equity from Lee Equity Partners; merged into Inception Fertility in 2018 where Varsavsky serves as Chairman [^4] [^5] [^6].
- **Overture Life** (founded 2017) — IVF lab automation; backed by Khosla Ventures and Marc Benioff [^1].
- **Goggo Network** (founded 2018) — autonomous-vehicle delivery infrastructure; €44M Series A in December 2019 [^1].
- **Gameto** (co-founded 2022 with Dina Radenkovic) — ovarian cell therapy biotech using induced pluripotent stem cells; cumulative funding reported at ~$127M [^1] [^7].
- **Certuma** — autonomous clinical-AI diagnostics; Varsavsky is CEO [^1] [^2].

Varsavsky has taught entrepreneurship at Columbia Business School, NYU, and IE University in Madrid [^1] [^2]. He sits on the board of Axel Springer [^2].

His angel-investing activity over more than two decades is organized through **VAS Ventures**, an early-stage fund he raised in 2018 (first fund: ~$15M) [^7] [^8]. His family office, **Jazzya Investments**, holds his post-exit personal portfolio.

## Stated Thesis

VAS Ventures publicly describes its mandate in one sentence:

> "We are looking for companies whose innovative founders think big and are courageous enough to shape the future." [^8]

The fund frames Varsavsky's "hands-on mentoring" as the differentiator versus other early-stage capital [^8]. Stage focus is described as early/first-round; the fund discloses no public check-size band [^8].

Varsavsky's public statements about how he picks people emphasize learning-oriented operators:

> "I always like to work with people who not only learn from me but who are able to do the necessary tasks to get the company going." [^4]

> "A great leader is somebody whose followers thrive." [^4]

Beyond that, no formal sector thesis is published. His Startupxplore profile notes "no pública" (not publicly disclosed) for investment strategy [^9]. The inferred-thesis section below is therefore the primary signal.

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample-size caveat:** Public reporting names roughly 30+ portfolio companies across VAS Ventures and earlier personal angel checks, but only a subset is independently date-stamped. The percentages below are not computed from a complete, dated list; they are qualitative patterns drawn from the named portfolio. Where a count is given, the denominator is explicit.

Based on companies named across Business Insider, The Future of Health, VAS Ventures' own site, Wikipedia, and Crunchbase write-ups [^1] [^7] [^8] [^10]:

- **Healthcare and fertility is the dominant theme.** Of approximately 30 named portfolio companies, at least 10 are in healthcare or fertility (23andMe, Forward, Daye, Companion, Aura Biosciences, Motiv, Jawbone Health, Braid Health, plus his own founded companies Prelude, Gameto, Overture). This is also the area where Varsavsky is personally operating — both Gameto and Overture are his own companies and Inception/Prelude is his chairmanship — so the angel checks compound his operating focus rather than diversifying it.
- **Mobility and autonomy is the second cluster.** Ample (battery swapping), Voyage (autonomous vehicles, later acquired by Cruise), Mox, and his own Goggo Network and Levere Holdings SPAC all sit in mobility/autonomy.
- **A long tail of consumer, education, and fintech angel checks.** Busuu (language learning), Pixowl (mobile games), Tumblr, Truecaller, Synthesia, MUBI, SmartThings, Teads, Elite Daily, Verse, Reclamador, Jüsto, Noken, Yada. These reflect his Madrid/NYC consumer-internet network from the Jazztel/Ya.com/FON era.
- **Geography is bi-coastal Atlantic: NYC + Madrid, with selective Latin America (Jüsto, Mexico) and one-off bets globally.** Both Varsavsky's residences and his fund's stated locations are New York and Madrid [^9].
- **Average angel check is ~€250,000** per Business Insider reporting on VAS Ventures [^7]. This is a meaningful, but not lead-sized, position — Varsavsky participates alongside larger institutional investors rather than leading rounds.
- **Notable returns include a reported 6,000x on Tumblr** (per The Future of Health, citing the Yahoo acquisition exit) [^7]. Tumblr is publicly confirmed as a Varsavsky angel investment by David Karp's own listing of early backers [^11].
- **Active investor signal: strong.** Varsavsky's most recent operating company, Certuma, was launched in 2026 with a $10M initial investment, and Gameto reached Phase 3 trials in 2026 [^1]. He continues to post publicly on Twitter/X as @martinvars and on Threads.

**Notable gap between stated and inferred:** VAS Ventures' stated thesis is sector-agnostic ("founders who think big"), but the realized portfolio is heavily concentrated in healthcare/fertility and mobility — the same domains where Varsavsky himself operates. A founder evaluating him as an angel should expect his operating focus to bias which sectors he engages most deeply with.

## In Their Own Words

On the founding insight behind Prelude Fertility (Vator interview, March 2017):

> "I founded Prelude because I was convinced that there was a better way to have babies." [^4]

On the biology/psychology gap that fertility tech addresses:

> "What Prelude does is bridge the gap, it makes people's biology meet their psychology." [^4]

> "Our fertility starts dropping drastically when we turn 35." [^4]

> "Around 30% of the people who try to have a baby through IVF never achieve that goal." [^4]

On the Prelude method itself:

> "You freeze your gametes when fertile, thaw them and create embryos when ready, genetically sequence the embryos, and then transfer one embryo at a time." [^4]

On leadership and team-building (same interview):

> "A great leader is somebody whose followers thrive." [^4]

> "I always like to work with people who not only learn from me but who are able to do the necessary tasks to get the company going." [^4]

VAS Ventures' single-line investment thesis statement (firm website):

> "We are looking for companies whose innovative founders think big and are courageous enough to shape the future." [^8]

## Connections

- **Tumblr — David Karp.** Varsavsky was one of the named early angel backers of Tumblr alongside Fred Seibert, with the Series A led by Spark Capital and Union Square Ventures in October 2007 [^11].
- **Lee Equity Partners.** Varsavsky's $200M Series A for Prelude Fertility (October 2016) was led and structured by Lee Equity Partners, with the firm partnering with him to roll up Reproductive Biology Associates and My Egg Bank into the Prelude platform [^5] [^6].
- **Khosla Ventures and Marc Benioff.** Both are named investors in Overture Life [^1].
- **Inception Fertility.** Varsavsky is Chairman of Inception Fertility, the parent company that absorbed Prelude in 2018 [^2].
- **Axel Springer — Board member.** Varsavsky sits on the board of the German publishing/media group Axel Springer [^2].
- **Columbia Business School, NYU, IE Business School.** Varsavsky has taught entrepreneurship at all three institutions [^1] [^2].
- **Original Fon investor base.** Google, Skype, Index Ventures, Sequoia Capital, Atomico, Qualcomm Ventures, BT Group, and Deutsche Telekom all backed Fon — placing Varsavsky inside the mid-2000s cap tables of nearly every major Silicon Valley and European telecom-strategic investor [^3].
