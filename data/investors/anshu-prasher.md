## Background

Anshu Prasher is a Partner at Whiteboard Capital, a Mumbai-based early-stage venture capital firm co-founded in 2017 by Sandeep Tandon (Freecharge co-founder) and Prasher [^1][^2]. According to Whiteboard Capital's team page, Prasher's professional background is in venture capital and venture debt in the Indian market [^1].

Before Whiteboard, Prasher was a Principal at Saama Capital, an India-focused venture firm, where the firm's team page states he was "focused on venture capital transactions and portfolio management" [^1]. Prior to Saama Capital, he was at Innoven Capital (Temasek's Asian venture debt platform), where he "led venture debt transactions for prominent companies and managed deal sourcing and portfolios" [^1]. He holds an MBA from IIM Lucknow (Indian Institute of Management Lucknow) [^1].

## Stated Thesis

Whiteboard Capital states it invests at "ideation, pre-seed, and seed stages, partnering with visionary founders to transform bold ideas into thriving businesses" [^3]. The firm frames its model as beyond capital: "We invest our time, expertise, and resources in the ideas and teams we believe in. By fostering strong relationships and offering hands-on support, we aim to be a catalyst for growth and success" [^3].

The firm is headquartered in Mumbai (Chakala, Andheri East) with additional offices in Bangalore and New Delhi [^4]. Prasher works alongside co-founder and Managing Partner Sandeep Tandon and a small investment team including CFO Nitesh Vaghela and Vice Presidents Subodh Jalori and Shifani Jesani [^4].

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size caveat.** Whiteboard Capital's publicly listed portfolio is a curated set of eight companies labeled "Rockstar Bets" on the firm's homepage; the firm does not publish a fund size, disclose individual investment amounts, or attribute specific deals to Prasher versus Tandon [^3]. Analysis below reflects Prasher's role as one of two Partners at a firm whose portfolio and stage focus are public.

**Sector concentration: Indian consumer internet and D2C.** All eight publicly listed portfolio companies (Apnaklub, Atomberg, CRED, Damensch, Dezerv, Supertails, Traya, Zeno Health) operate in the Indian consumer market [^3]:
- Consumer D2C brands: Damensch (menswear), Traya (haircare), Atomberg (smart home appliances), Supertails (pet care), Zeno Health (generic pharma retail)
- Fintech and financial services: CRED (credit card payments/rewards), Dezerv (wealth management)
- B2B/SMB commerce: Apnaklub (wholesale/kirana enablement)

**Stage: pre-seed to early growth.** Firm materials state the primary focus as ideation, pre-seed, and seed [^3]. Whiteboard invests through personal angel checks alongside institutional Whiteboard checks; Sandeep Tandon has separately made personal angel investments beginning in 2015 after the Freecharge exit [^5].

**Geography: India-focused.** All verified portfolio companies are India-based (Mumbai, Bangalore, Delhi, Gurugram) with primarily Indian customer bases [^3].

**Prior experience signal: venture debt and venture equity.** Prasher's Innoven and Saama background pairs venture-debt underwriting (where cash flow and unit economics matter) with early-stage venture equity (where team and thesis matter) [^1]. This dual lens is consistent with Whiteboard's stated "hands-on" post-investment involvement [^3].
