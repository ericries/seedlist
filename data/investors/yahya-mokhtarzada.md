## Background

Yahya Mokhtarzada is a co-founder of Truebill (now Rocket Money), the personal-finance app he built with brothers Haroon and Idris Mokhtarzada in 2015 and grew to millions of premium subscribers before its $1.275 billion acquisition by Rocket Companies in December 2021 [^1][^2]. Before Truebill, Mokhtarzada was VP of Business Development at Nanigans (a Facebook-focused ad tech platform) and held earlier leadership roles at appssavvy and Jam City [^1]. He remains Co-Founder and Chief Revenue Officer of Rocket Money post-acquisition [^2][^3].

Mokhtarzada is based in New York and angel-invests primarily in fintech and consumer software [^1][^3]. He has served as a mentor with 500 Startups, supporting early-stage founders in fintech and consumer technology [^1].
