## Background

Naveen Kukreja is the co-founder of Paisabazaar, the online consumer credit marketplace owned by India-listed PB Fintech (parent of Policybazaar). He co-founded Paisabazaar in Gurugram in 2014 alongside Yashish Dahiya and Alok Bansal, and led it as CEO from inception [^1][^1]. PB Fintech listed on Indian stock exchanges in November 2021 [^1].

Before Paisabazaar, Kukreja held senior roles at Citibank India (Credit Cards), Capital One Bank in the UK, and was Senior Vice President of Marketing and Strategy at Aviva Life Insurance, where he led the digital business [^2]. He is an alumnus of Delhi College of Engineering and IIM Calcutta [^2].

Effective March 1, 2025, Kukreja stepped down as Paisabazaar CEO and was succeeded by Santosh Agarwal. He moved into the role of Group President at PB Fintech while remaining a non-executive director on Paisabazaar's board [^3]. He subsequently exited PB Fintech and began work on a new (undisclosed) fintech venture with longtime collaborators "Gaurav and Sahil" [^4].

## Stated Thesis

Kukreja has not published a formal angel investing thesis. In public commentary he emphasizes long-horizon fintech opportunity in India: he has said he is "very excited about the new chapter alongside Gaurav and Sahil...we are together very excited about the potential of fintech in the next 15 years" [^4]. His angel checks reflect this stated long-term India fintech focus alongside adjacent consumer and enterprise SaaS sectors.
