## Background

Nader Khalil is Director of Developer Tech at NVIDIA, where he leads NVIDIA Brev, the GPU-provisioning developer platform that grew out of his startup Brev.dev [^1][^2]. NVIDIA acquired Brev.dev in July 2024; Khalil announced the deal on July 17, 2024 [^3][^2].

Khalil grew up in Lebanon and is based between San Diego and San Francisco [^1]. He studied Computer Engineering at UC Santa Barbara, then worked as a software engineer at Workday from 2016 to 2017 [^4]. In 2018, he co-founded Paneau — an in-vehicle advertising platform installed on tablets inside Uber and Lyft rides — with Alec Fong [^5]. Paneau went through Y Combinator's Winter 2020 (W20) batch [^5][^6]. When COVID-19 collapsed the rideshare advertising market, Khalil and Fong pivoted; the team's next side project addressed the "works on my machine" development-environment problem, and became Brev.dev [^6].

Brev.dev launched in San Francisco in 2021 and raised an undisclosed pre-seed round led by Nama Ventures in June 2022 [^7]. The platform initially unified cloud-based development environments across AWS, Google Cloud, and other providers, then pivoted again to focus on GPU access for AI developers as the generative-AI boom accelerated [^6][^8]. That focus caught NVIDIA's attention and led to the acquisition [^6].

## In Their Own Words

On Brev's product philosophy, from a June 2021 Forbes profile: "We built Brev such that you can stay abstracted from the infrastructure and build, but freely peel back the abstraction if you want" [^8].

On the tool-sprawl problem Brev was built to solve, from the same interview: "Very quickly, it feels like we're no longer doing software development but instead orchestrating tools to work with each other" [^8].

On the acquisition, from Khalil's LinkedIn announcement (July 17, 2024): "We started Brev in my parents garage with the goal of building the best damn developer experience possible. We've been working closely with NVIDIA since August, meeting brilliant folks like Michael Balint, Manuvir Das, Manish Harsh, and Julio C. Tapia behind their software suite and we couldn't be more excited to team up" [^3].
