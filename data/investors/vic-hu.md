## Background

Vic Hu is the co-founder and engineering lead of Brev, a San Francisco / Seattle-area AI startup that raised a $3.3M pre-seed round in April 2026 led by Resolute Ventures, with participation from shuckerVC, Duro VC, Gaingels, and FOG Ventures [^1][^2]. He founded the company with Chris Pitchford and is responsible for engineering — "leading engineering efforts to leverage AI for enhancing business outcomes" [^3].

Before Brev, Hu was an engineering manager at Indeed, a Senior Software Engineer at Meta (backend engineering for augmented reality), a Staff Software Engineer at Iterative Health (leading the ML Annotation team), and a Senior Software Engineer at Panopto where he led feature integrations and engineering teams [^3]. He has also held earlier roles at IBM and previously founded a project called Dediced [^3].

He holds an MS in Software Engineering from The University of Texas at Austin and a BS in Electrical Engineering and Physics from Rice University [^3]. He is based in the Seattle area (Redmond, WA) [^3].

**Disambiguation note:** This profile covers Vic Hu of **Brev.io / brev.com** (the AI business-operations startup co-founded with Chris Pitchford). It is NOT the same as **Brev.dev**, an unrelated GPU/AI cloud platform founded by Nader Khalil and Alec Fong that was acquired by NVIDIA in August 2024 [^4]. The two companies share a similar name but have no overlapping founders or operations.
