## Background

Jason Lin is a Co-Founder and General Partner at Lifelike Capital, an early-stage venture firm focused on gaming, social, and frontier technology, which he co-founded in 2022 with Kevin Lin (Twitch co-founder) and Randy Lee (former Senior VP, Americas at Tencent) [^1] [^1]. He is also Managing Partner of Super Capital Group, a technology venture capital and growth investment firm where he leads investments across Silicon Valley and Asia [^2].

Lin has described more than 18 years of investing experience across Silicon Valley and Asia [^2]. Prior to founding Lifelike and leading Super Capital, he was an investor at Draper Fisher Jurvetson Element (DFJ Element), Granite Global Capital (GBC), and WestSummit Capital, with earlier strategy consulting roles at A.T. Kearney and PCH International [^2]. He has also served as Co-Founder of AUM Group, a multicultural film and media investment fund, and Co-President of the Asia America MultiTechnology Association (AAMA Silicon Valley); he was an early advisor at NerdWallet [^2].

Lin holds a BS from the University of Pennsylvania (Wharton) and an MBA from the Stanford University Graduate School of Business [^2].

**Disambiguation note:** The name "Jason Lin" is common. This profile refers specifically to Jason A. Lin, Managing Partner at Super Capital Group and Co-Founder/GP at Lifelike Capital, whose social handles are `@jasonalin` on X and `linkedin.com/in/jasonalin` [^1] [^2] [^3]. He is not the same as Jason Lin (Managing Director, Super Capital Group, listed on Crunchbase as a separate entry) or any other Jason Lin in venture or finance.

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size caveat: Lin's publicly attributed portfolio spans both Super Capital and Lifelike Capital, but contemporaneous-press confirmation for many deals is limited. The verified set below is small relative to claims of "18+ years" of investing activity [^2]. The analysis below is qualitative, not percentage-based.**

The verified pattern across Super Capital and Lifelike:

- **Consumer technology and gaming dominate.** The companies Super Capital lists in Lin's portfolio — Twitch (Amazon acquisition), Unity (IPO), OURA, Sleeper, Triller, Birdy Grey, Evite, Players' Lounge, Sentropy (acquired by Discord), TravelBank (acquired by U.S. Bank), Lion Semi, Movidius (acquired by Intel), Maginatics (acquired by EMC), Navitas (IPO), and others — sit predominantly in consumer software, gaming/creator infrastructure, and consumer hardware [^2]. The Lifelike active list (Ironwood Studios, Dead Astronauts, Red Rover Interactive, Wolfhaus Games, Ready, Metatheory, Softmax, SweGAN, Live Aware, Arine) is heavily weighted toward game studios and interactive entertainment [^1] [^1] [^5].
- **US/Asia cross-border focus.** Lin's prior firms (DFJ Element, GBC, WestSummit) and Super Capital all explicitly operate across Silicon Valley and Asia [^2]. This complements Randy Lee's Tencent / Roblox China track record [^1] and gives Lifelike a distinct cross-border bridge among US gaming-focused seed firms.
- **Operator-investor co-founder pairing.** Lin's finance/VC track record (Wharton, Stanford GSB, three prior VC funds) [^2] complements Kevin Lin's Twitch operating background and Randy Lee's Tencent strategic-investing background — the three GPs occupy distinct lanes (consumer/creator operations, Asia publishing, financial/VC craft) that together cover the typical fundraising needs of a gaming studio [^1] [^1].
- **Stage: predominantly seed and early-stage.** Lifelike led the $4M Ironwood Studios seed in February 2026 [^5] [^6]. Super Capital's listed exits include both early-stage venture wins (Twitch, Unity, Movidius) and later-stage successes (Navitas IPO, Triller), consistent with a fund that enters early but holds through growth [^2].
- **AI is a stated 2025+ expansion area.** Aggregator profiles list AI and frontier technology among Lin's current focus areas [^1] [^7], though independently-confirmed AI deals in his name are limited in this research pass.

**Active investor signal: strong.** Lifelike led the Ironwood Studios seed on February 11, 2026, the most recent verified deal in which Lin participated as a GP [^5] [^6].
