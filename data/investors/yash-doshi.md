## Background

Yash Doshi is a Partner at IA Fund, the $26M debut venture fund led by Mercury CEO Immad Akhund [^1][^2]. The fund was announced in May 2025 and formalizes early-stage investing activity that Akhund and Doshi had been doing collaboratively for years [^1].

Prior to co-founding IA Fund, Doshi was an early investor in Mercury and, according to secondary sources, has held roles at EQT Ventures (as a Venture Lead), Duro Ventures, and Cyberhaven [^3][^4]. His Signal by NFX profile lists his sweet-spot check size at $250K, with a range of $10K–$500K, and he appears on Signal's investor rankings across developer tools, AI, enterprise, SaaS, and fintech at both seed and Series A [^4].

## Stated Thesis

IA Fund's publicly stated strategy is "founder-first" and "conviction-driven," backing founders with a "proven track record of building impactful products" pursuing markets valued at $10B+ and solutions "advancing humanity" [^1]. The fund plans to back approximately 60 companies with an average check of $150K, with occasional "conviction checks" up to $1M [^2]. Doshi has not published a separate personal thesis distinct from the IA Fund mandate.

## Inferred Thesis

Because IA Fund's public deal attribution names the fund rather than individual partners, it is not possible to cleanly separate Doshi's personal investment pattern from Akhund's without primary confirmation for each deal. What is publicly verifiable:

- **Stage:** Pre-seed and seed, consistent with a $150K average check [^2].
- **Sector footprint of IA Fund deals to date (small sample):** enterprise AI (ego AI), financial software (Ultrametric), and other early-stage software plays [^5].
- **Doshi's Signal by NFX categorical rankings** cover developer tools, AI, enterprise, SaaS, and fintech at seed and Series A [^4] — consistent with the fund's broad early-stage software footprint.

Sample size is too small and attribution too coarse to compute meaningful percentages.
