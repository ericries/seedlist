## Background

Juliette Bolea is an investor at Sound Ventures, the Los Angeles–based venture firm founded in 2015 by Ashton Kutcher and Guy Oseary [^1]. Effie Epstein joined as Managing Partner and COO in March 2017 to build out the firm's institutional operating side [^2]. Sound Ventures has raised multiple funds — a Fund I anchored by Liberty Media, a $150M Fund II closed in 2018, a ~$200M early-stage vehicle deployed in parallel, and a $240M AI-focused fund closed in May 2023, half of which went into OpenAI, Anthropic, and Stability AI at close [^1][^3].

Bolea joined Sound Ventures in September 2023 [^4]. Sound's public team page lists her separately from the firm's General Partners (Kutcher, Oseary, Epstein), and every primary-source press release we located describes her title simply as "Investor, Sound Ventures" [^5] — not Partner, Principal, or COO, despite some third-party aggregators labeling her otherwise. In press releases, she is now identified as a board member on at least one portfolio company (Anomaly Insights) [^5].

Prior to Sound Ventures, Bolea studied at the USC Marshall School of Business as a Trustee Scholar (full academic scholarship) and completed investment-banking summer analyst roles at Bank of America Merrill Lynch (2017) and Lazard's TMT group (2018) [^4].

**Note on firm status:** On July 1, 2026, Ashton Kutcher announced he was leaving Sound Ventures to launch a new firm with Morgan Beller, remaining as an advisor to Sound while Guy Oseary and Effie Epstein continue running it [^6]. Bolea's role at Sound post-transition has not been publicly announced but is presumed unchanged as of this profile date.

## Stated Thesis

Bolea has not published a written investment thesis of her own. Sound Ventures' AI Fund is publicly described by Epstein as "quite a concentrated portfolio... concentrated across six to seven positions," reflecting a belief that a small number of foundation-model and application-layer AI companies will dominate [^3]. Bolea's own LinkedIn announcement in September 2023 described her mandate at Sound as "early-stage opportunities across industries" [^4].

## Inferred Thesis

Verified data attributable to Bolea personally is limited to a single publicly-linked deal. The remainder of Sound Ventures' portfolio (Airbnb, Uber, Spotify, Duolingo from earlier funds; OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, Runway, Stability AI from the AI fund) predates her tenure or has been attributed to General Partner–level decisions in the press, not to her.

From the one publicly-attached deal (Anomaly Insights, an AI-powered payer-intelligence platform serving healthcare providers) [^5], the observable pattern is:

- **Stage:** Growth-stage AI applied to a legacy vertical (healthcare payer/provider dynamics)
- **Thesis-fit:** Consistent with Sound's broader application-layer AI focus described in TechCrunch's May 2023 reporting on Fund III [^3]
- **Sector overlap with Sound:** Healthcare + AI; not the pure foundation-model bets of the AI Fund

Sample size is one attributable deal. No percentages are computed. Founders evaluating Bolea specifically should read her as Sound Ventures' bench for early-stage, applied-AI sourcing rather than as an independent thesis operator.
