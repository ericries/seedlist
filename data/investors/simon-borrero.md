## Background

Simón Borrero is a Colombian entrepreneur and the co-founder and CEO of Rappi, the Bogotá-based on-demand delivery and super-app platform that became one of Latin America's first decacorn-class startups [^1] [^2]. He was born in 1984 in Cali, Colombia [^3]. Borrero earned a bachelor's degree in Business Administration with a minor in Economics from Universidad de los Andes in 2007, and an MBA from EADA Business School in Barcelona [^3].

Before Rappi, Borrero founded Imaginamos, a software development services company headquartered in Bogotá [^3]. In 2013 he co-founded Grability, a white-label grocery e-commerce platform, with Sebastián Mejía [^1] [^3]. Grability's experience with consumer demand for additional services beyond groceries led the team to launch Rappi in 2015 with Borrero, Mejía, and Felipe Villamarín as co-founders [^1] [^2].

Rappi entered Y Combinator's Winter 2016 batch [^4]. It raised a Series A from Andreessen Horowitz in April 2016 and additional capital from Sequoia Capital and DST Global later that year [^4]. In August 2018, Rappi raised $200M in a round that brought it to unicorn status [^5]. In April 2019, SoftBank Group and the SoftBank Vision Fund formalized a $1 billion investment in Rappi at a pre-money valuation of $2.5B [^6]. A July 2021 Series F led by T. Rowe Price raised over $500M at a $5.25B valuation [^7]. As of 2026, Rappi has raised approximately $2.3-2.5B in total [^4].

In June 2025, Borrero told Forbes Colombia that Rappi had completed four consecutive quarters of profitability and was contemplating an IPO at the end of 2026 [^8]. Bloomberg Línea separately reported in July 2025 that Rappi had been profitable for four consecutive quarters with positive EBITDA [^9].

Borrero has invested personally in early-stage Latin American startups since at least 2022, often alongside Rappi co-founders Sebastián Mejía and Felipe Villamarín — a group ecosystem observers have called the "Rappi Mafia" of operator-investors [^10] [^11].

## Stated Thesis

Borrero has not published a formal angel investment thesis. His public commentary about company-building emphasizes operating leverage, capital efficiency, and Latin American market structure rather than a sector-specific lens.

In a June 2025 Forbes Colombia interview about Rappi's next phase, Borrero said: "Estoy enfocado en Turbo y la turborización de todo" ("I'm focused on Turbo and the 'turbo-ization' of everything"), referring to Rappi's rapid-delivery layer being extended across categories [^8]. He also stated that Rappi had reached "una fase de escala, en la que está demostrado que el modelo funciona" ("a scale phase where it's proven the model works") [^8].

At the time SoftBank's $1B investment was announced in April 2019, Borrero said: "Softbank's vision of accelerating the technology revolution deeply resonated with our mission of improving how people live through digital payments and a super-app for everything consumers need" [^6].

His March 2021 appearance on The Twenty Minute VC was titled "Growth vs Profitability, Capital Efficiency, Raising $1.7Bn as a LATAM Company and Why Uber Eats Did More To Help Than To Hurt Rappi" — topics that broadly map to the operator concerns visible in his angel-investment selection [^12].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on 7 angel investments with independent press citations, plus the broader portfolio of approximately 23 investments tracked by Signal by NFX [^13]:

**Sector distribution (7 strongly verified angel investments):**
- Fintech / payments / identity infrastructure: 2 of 7 (29%) — Yuno [^14], Truora [^15]
- Marketplaces: 1 of 7 (14%) — Morado [^16]
- E-commerce aggregator / brand incubator: 1 of 7 (14%) — Riogrande [^17] [^10]
- Energy / cleantech: 1 of 7 (14%) — Bia Energy [^11]
- On-demand delivery: 1 of 7 (14%) — Chowdeck [^18]
- AI healthtech: 1 of 7 (14%) — Telepatia [^19] [^20]

Several of these companies were founded by former Rappi employees (Yuno, Morado) or operate in the on-demand and marketplace categories Borrero knows from Rappi (Chowdeck, Riogrande).

**Geographic distribution:** Predominantly Latin America (Colombia, Mexico, multi-country LatAm). Notably, Chowdeck is Nigerian — Borrero co-invested with Rappi co-founder Juan Pablo Ortega in a delivery startup outside LatAm [^18]. Telepatia targets Latin America but has stated plans to expand into India, Africa, and Southeast Asia [^21].

**Stage distribution:** Pre-seed through Series A. The largest verified-round position is Telepatia's $33M Series A in 2026 [^19]; the smallest is Morado's $5M pre-seed in 2022 [^16].

**Check size:** Signal by NFX reports Borrero's typical check at $25K with a range of $5K-$50K [^13], consistent with operator-angel positioning alongside institutional leads.

**Co-investor patterns:** Borrero frequently appears alongside Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Y Combinator, and other founder-angels in his network. In Telepatia's Series A he co-invested with a16z (lead), Palantir CTO Shyam Sankar, and Nubank founder David Vélez [^19] [^20]. In Yuno he co-invested with a16z, Monashees, Kaszek, Latitud, and individual founders including Maria Echeverri (Muni), Gerry Giacomán Colyer (Clara), and Ricardo Weder (Justo) [^14]. In Truora he co-invested with Propel Venture Partners, Accel, and angels including Deel CEO Alex Bouaziz, Jeeves founder Dileep Thazhmon, and Latitud co-founder Brian Requarth [^15].

**Founder profile patterns:** A meaningful share of Borrero's checks go to startups founded by Rappi alumni or by founders he has worked with operationally. Yuno was founded by former Rappi employees [^14]. This pattern is consistent with Rappi being described by ecosystem observers as one of LatAm's largest startup incubators by alumni founder count [^22].

**Sample size caveat:** The seven investments above are those for which Borrero's participation has been confirmed in independent press coverage. Signal by NFX lists 23 investments on record [^13], so the press-verified sample represents roughly 30% of the tracked portfolio. The inferred thesis percentages above should be read as directional rather than precise.
