## Background

Andrew Johnston is co-founder and managing partner of Second Rodeo, based in Boulder, Colorado, a role he has held since April 2025 [^1][^2]. Before founding Second Rodeo he was VP and Head of Sales at Superhuman, the premium email client, where he led the company's expansion into B2B2C and enterprise markets [^1][^3]. Prior to Superhuman he was Senior Director of Sales at Scale AI and led global email sales for Twilio's SendGrid business [^1].

He is also a Venture Scout at Craft Ventures, a role he has held since June 2023 [^1]. He is based in Denver, Colorado, and holds a B.S. in Marketing from Miami University [^1][^2].
