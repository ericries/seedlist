## Background

Ankur Singla is a three-time founder in enterprise infrastructure and cybersecurity, currently Co-Founder and CEO of Exaforce, a San Jose-based agentic AI security operations platform founded in 2023 [^1][^2]. He holds a Master's degree from Stanford University [^3].

Singla previously served as Chief Technology Officer and VP of Engineering at Aruba Networks before founding his first startup [^4]. In March 2012, he co-founded Contrail Systems, a software-defined networking (SDN) and network functions virtualization (NFV) company [^4][^5]. Contrail was in stealth for approximately nine months and publicly launched in early December 2012; Juniper Networks agreed to acquire the company for approximately $176 million in cash and stock just days after launch [^4][^5]. Singla continued to lead Contrail as a business unit within Juniper through 2017 [^6].

In 2017, Singla founded Volterra, a distributed cloud services platform, initially raising a $25 million Series A led by Mayfield [^6][^7]. Volterra debuted publicly with over $50 million in total funding from Mayfield, Khosla Ventures, M12 (Microsoft's venture arm), Samsung NEXT, and Itochu Technology Ventures, with more than 30 customers and a 100-person engineering team at launch [^6][^7]. In January 2021, F5 Networks announced its acquisition of Volterra in a deal valued at approximately $500 million (approximately $440 million in cash plus $60 million in deferred consideration and assumed unvested incentive compensation) [^8][^9]. Singla and the Volterra leadership team joined F5 in key management roles upon closing, with Singla leading F5's security products group until approximately November 2022 [^10][^8].

In 2023, Singla co-founded Exaforce alongside Ariful Huq (Head of Product), Devesh Mittal (Head of Engineering), Marco Rodrigues (Head of Customer Success & Solutions), and Jakub Pavlik (Head of Engineering) [^11][^12]. The founding team came from F5, Palo Alto Networks, Google, and Juniper [^11][^13]. Exaforce raised a $75 million Series A on April 17, 2025, co-led by Khosla Ventures and Mayfield with participation from Thomvest Ventures and Touring Capital [^13][^14]. On May 12, 2026, the company announced a $125 million Series B at a $725 million post-money valuation, with participation from HarbourVest, Peak XV, Mayfield, Khosla Ventures, and Seligman Ventures — bringing total funding to $200 million [^15].

## Stated Thesis

Self-reported. Singla has not published a public investing thesis or angel investing philosophy statement. His public commentary focuses on operating in the AI-driven cybersecurity market and building repeat companies rather than on investment selection criteria.

On the founding process broadly, Singla told Mayfield: "Most startups are not a straight line and all good things evolve with time — the greatest asset of any company is the brainpower of its team, including its investors who have pattern recognition and an outside-in view" [^6].
