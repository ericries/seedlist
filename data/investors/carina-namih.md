## Background

Carina Namih is a partner at Plural Platform, a London-based entrepreneur-led venture firm she joined in March 2023 [^1][^2]. She studied Politics, Philosophy and Economics at Oxford [^3]. In 2013, at age 23, she co-founded HelixNano in San Francisco, an AI-driven biotech company developing next-generation mRNA platforms; she served as CEO and remains on the board [^1][^3][^4]. HelixNano has secured backing from investors including Sam Altman and Eric Schmidt [^4].

Before joining Plural full-time in 2022, Namih invested as an angel in companies including Omnipresent (remote work), Phasecraft (quantum software, spun out of UCL), Robin AI (generative AI for legal), and AtlasML (acquired by Meta AI) [^1][^2]. Plural's internal title for general partners is "peer" [^1]. She also serves as a trustee of The Alan Turing Institute [^5].

## Stated Thesis

Namih describes her focus as "applied AI" — companies applying AI to large industries — and she emphasizes operator-led conviction. On her Plural team page she states: "Obsess over the problem, live inside it — insight comes from immersion, not assumption" [^6]. In a Sifted interview she said her goal is identifying "really talented contrarian founders who can see a structural shift coming, whether it's technical or behavior change before others" [^1].

## Inferred Thesis

Independently verified portfolio across angel and Plural-backed deals (n=8): applied AI (Robin AI, AtlasML, Softmax), biotech/life sciences (Sano Genetics, HelixNano), robotics (Sunrise Robotics, Upside Robotics), advanced materials/energy (Molyon, Phasecraft), and remote work (Omnipresent). Sample size is small (n=8); qualitative pattern is operator-founders applying frontier technology (AI, quantum, mRNA, batteries, robotics) to commercial verticals, with a strong European focus reflecting Plural's geography. She has personally led Plural deals in Robin AI and Sano Genetics [^7].
