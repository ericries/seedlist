## Background

Pankaj Chaddah is an Indian entrepreneur and angel investor based in Gurugram. He holds an engineering degree from IIT Delhi and began his career as an analyst at Bain & Company, where he met his future Zomato co-founder Deepinder Goyal [^1][^2].

In 2008, Chaddah and Goyal launched FoodieBay, an online directory of restaurant menus that was rebranded as Zomato in 2010 [^1][^3]. As co-founder, Chaddah helped lead the company's expansion beyond restaurant listings into food delivery, online ordering, table reservations, and digital payments. Zomato reached unicorn status following Ant Financial's $200M investment in 2018 [^1]. Chaddah departed Zomato around 2018–2019 [^1][^3].

After leaving Zomato, Chaddah co-founded Mindhouse in late 2019 with Pooja Khanna, a former Zomato chief of staff [^4]. Mindhouse began as an offline meditation and wellness studio and pivoted online during the pandemic. In November 2021, the company raised a $6M seed round led by Flipkart co-founder Binny Bansal, with participation from General Catalyst, Better Capital, Kwaish Capital, Anicut Capital, and the founders themselves [^4]. The company rebranded to Shyft in 2022, focusing on mental fitness and wellness [^3][^5].

Alongside operating Shyft, Chaddah has been an active angel investor since stepping back from Zomato. Aggregator profiles list 15–25 portfolio investments across consumer, D2C, healthtech, edtech, and media sectors in India and the United States [^2][^5][^6]. He is also a founding member of Plaksha University in Chandigarh and serves as an advisor to Akshaya Patra [^1].
