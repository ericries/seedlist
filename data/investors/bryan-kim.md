## Background

Bryan Kim ("BK") is a Partner at Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), where he focuses on consumer AI investments [^1]. His current investing themes span four areas: productivity (ElevenLabs, Cluely, Raspberry AI), consumer health (Function Health, Slingshot AI), creation (Captions, Civit.ai, Glif, Viggle), and connection (BeReal, Partiful) [^1].

Before joining a16z, Kim spent several years at Snap during its hyper-growth and IPO phase, where he was a founding member of the strategy team — sourcing acquisitions including Looksery and Bitmoji — and built and led the growth and product operations teams [^1]. He also scaled Snap's finance team from zero to 70+ people through the company's IPO [^1]. Prior to Snap, Kim served as CFO at Bungalow and was a founding General Partner at Uncommon Projects, an operator-led seed fund [^1]. Earlier in his career, he worked in technology M&A and capital markets at Credit Suisse [^1]. He holds a BA in Economics and International Relations from Carleton College and an MBA from Columbia Business School [^1].

## Stated Thesis

Kim publicly describes his focus as "investing in AI applications at a16z" [^2]. On the firm's author page, he frames his work around how AI is reshaping consumer experiences across productivity, consumer health, creation, and connection [^1]. He has written publicly on consumer AI retention, AI shopping, AI-native companies, and voice agents [^1].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on 9 verified investments tracked in public deal databases [^3], Kim's actual behavior aligns closely with his stated focus on consumer AI applications, with some notable patterns:

- **Stage distribution**: Of 9 verified rounds, 6 are Series A (Hilbert's AI, ElevenLabs 2023, Oboe, Cluely, Raspberry AI, Mirage 2022), 1 Seed (District), 1 Series B (Function Health), and 2 growth-stage follow-ons (ElevenLabs Series D, Mirage Series C) [^3]. This skews heavily toward Series A as the primary entry point (67% of verified entries).
- **Sector concentration**: All 9 verified investments are consumer or consumer-adjacent AI — voice/audio (ElevenLabs), AI search/answer engines (Cluely, Hilbert), generative creation tools (Raspberry AI for fashion design, Mirage), consumer health (Function Health), and consumer audio/news (Oboe) [^3]. Notable absence: no enterprise SaaS, no infrastructure, no fintech in verified deals.
- **Check sizes**: Reported sweet spot of ~$5M with a range of $100K-$100M [^3], consistent with a16z's ability to deploy at either seed or growth scale.
- **Board involvement**: Board member at Captions and Partiful; board observer at ElevenLabs and Function Health [^1] — indicating concentrated post-investment focus on a small number of high-conviction bets rather than passive portfolio breadth.
- **Sample size caveat**: 9 verified rounds is a modest sample; Kim's a16z author page references additional portfolio companies (Captions, BeReal, Slingshot AI, Civit.ai, Glif, Viggle, Partiful) [^1] that do not appear in the public Signal/NFX dataset, suggesting actual portfolio depth exceeds what is publicly tracked.
