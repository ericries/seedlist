## Background

Jon Oringer (born May 2, 1974) is an American programmer, photographer, and entrepreneur best known as the founder of Shutterstock [^1]. He holds a B.S. in Computer Science and Mathematics from Stony Brook University (1997) and an M.S. in Computer Science from Columbia University (1998) [^1]. Oringer founded Shutterstock in 2003 after personally photographing approximately 100,000 images over six months; the company IPO'd on the NYSE on October 17, 2012, making Oringer the first reported billionaire to emerge from New York's "Silicon Alley" tech scene [^1]. He currently serves as Executive Chairman of Shutterstock [^2].

Oringer invests as an angel and as a co-founder of Pareto Holdings, a New York-based investment vehicle [^3][^5].
