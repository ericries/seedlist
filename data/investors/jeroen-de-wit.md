## Background

Jeroen De Wit is the co-founder and CEO of Teamleader, a Ghent-based SaaS company offering all-in-one CRM, invoicing and project management software to SMEs [^1][^2]. He started Teamleader in 2012 and grew it to serve 35,000+ customers across Europe before Visma acquired it in June 2022; De Wit continued as Managing Director of Teamleader within the Visma family [^1][^2]. Alongside Teamleader, he angel-invests in Belgian early-stage technology companies [^3][^4].

## Stated Thesis

No publicly documented stated thesis was found. Per third-party angel directories, De Wit "supports early-stage technology companies in Belgium" with a focus on artificial intelligence, CRM, and legal tech sectors at seed and early stages [^3][^4].

## Inferred Thesis

Sample size is small (11 publicly tracked angel investments per Tracxn and Investorlist [^3][^4]) so percentages are not meaningful. Qualitative patterns:

- **Geography:** Heavily Belgium-focused. Most named portfolio companies (Aikido, Donna, Jurimesh, IntelliProve, Polysense, Exhibitly) are Belgian [^3][^4][^5].
- **Stage:** Predominantly seed and pre-seed, with check sizes consistent with European angel rounds (€25K-€100K range based on round sizes of €1-2M with 7-22 co-investors per round) [^5][^6][^7].
- **Sector:** B2B SaaS skew (Teamleader-adjacent), with notable concentration in vertical SaaS (legaltech via Jurimesh, healthtech via IntelliProve, security via Aikido), AI, and CRM [^3][^4].
- **Co-investor patterns:** Frequently co-invests alongside other Belgian SaaS founders-turned-angels including Anthony De Clerck (Aikido CEO), Andreas De Neve (Teamtailor), Gilles Mattelin, and Christophe Morbee — a tight Ghent/Belgium angel network [^5][^6][^7].
