## Background

Felix Garriau is co-founder and Chief Marketing Officer of Aikido Security, a developer-first application security platform headquartered in Ghent, Belgium [^1][^2]. He co-founded the company in 2022 with Willem Delbare, Roeland Delrue, and Madeline ("Amber") Rucker [^2][^3]. He is based in Antwerp, Belgium [^6].

Before Aikido, Garriau was CMO & Partner at nexxworks, the Belgian innovation consultancy [^1]. His background spans more than a decade in marketing, project and innovation management, with prior roles at nexxworks and AREA42 [^1].

Aikido has grown rapidly since launch: it raised a $5.3M seed in late 2023, a $17M Series A in May 2024, and a $60M Series B in October 2025 at a $1B+ valuation, backed by investors including DST Global, Notion Capital, Connect Ventures, Singular, and PSG Equity [^3][^7]. Aikido was the fastest-growing company on the Sifted 100: France & Benelux 2026 leaderboard [^6].

Garriau's investing activity is primarily through Syndicate One, a Belgian network-driven early-stage fund. Garriau and his Aikido co-founders Roeland Delrue and Willem Delbare are named among the 120+ founders and operators backing Syndicate One's €22M Fund II, which closed in February 2026 [^4][^5].
