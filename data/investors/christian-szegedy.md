## Background

Christian Szegedy is a research scientist turned founder and angel investor based in the San Francisco Bay Area. He was a Principal Scientist at Google Brain, where he is best known as the architect of the Inception / GoogLeNet convolutional neural network and as a co-author of the seminal paper introducing adversarial examples to deep learning [^1]. He was a co-founder of xAI, the AI lab founded by Elon Musk in 2023, alongside Greg Yang, Yuhuai Wu, Kyle Kosic and others [^2][^3].

After leaving xAI, Szegedy joined Morph Labs as Chief Scientist, focusing on formal verification and AI-assisted theorem proving [^3][^1]. On September 11, 2025 he announced the launch of Math, Inc., a new company "dedicated to the creation of verified superintelligence via autoformalization," building on infrastructure developed at Morph Labs [^4]. Math, Inc.'s autonomous agent GAUSS subsequently completed Terence Tao and Alex Kontorovich's Strong Prime Number Theorem formalization project in three weeks — an effort that had stalled for 18 months under human progress [^4].

Szegedy holds a doctorate from the University of Bonn (1998–2005), where he worked on matching theory and algebraic graph theory [^1]. He speaks English, Hungarian and German [^1].

## Stated Thesis

Szegedy has not published a formal investment thesis. In interviews he describes his interest in AI systems that combine large-scale compute with formal mathematical verification, and in teams pursuing what he views as the missing infrastructure for trustworthy AI. He told the Information Bottleneck podcast that he is "100% convinced that transformers will not be the…main workers in five years," signaling skepticism of current architectures while remaining bullish on AI for mathematics [^5].

He has not articulated stage, check size or sector preferences as an angel.
