## Background

Jomayra Herrera is a Partner at Reach Capital, an early-stage venture firm focused on education, workforce, and healthcare technology. She joined Reach in May 2021 as the firm's first outside partner hire [^1]. Based in New York, she leads investments across seed, Series A, and Series B, with a stated check range of $500K–$3M [^2][^3].

Herrera's path into venture began operationally at BloomBoard, an early-career edtech startup where she held roles across marketing, customer success, and product supporting educator development [^2]. She then moved into venture at Emerson Collective, Laurene Powell Jobs' hybrid philanthropy/investment firm, before becoming a Principal at Cowboy Ventures [^1]. At Emerson she championed investments in Career Karma, Handshake, and Guild Education; at Cowboy she led Contra's seed [^1]. She holds a B.A. in Political Science and an M.A. in Education, both from Stanford [^2]. She also serves on the board of SomosVC, a nonprofit expanding pathways for Latino/a venture investors [^2].

## Stated Thesis

Herrera describes her investment thesis in personal terms: she wants to "make the world a better place for the girl [she] was" — growing up in Orlando witnessing how access shapes life opportunities [^1][^2]. At Reach, her focus is "reimagining the future of healthcare and work" — specifically tools and platforms that help workers thrive, become agents of their own careers, and access equitable pathways regardless of background, pedigree, or geographic location [^1][^2].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on Herrera's disclosed Reach portfolio of 12 companies [^2], the pattern is consistent with her stated focus:

- **Sector concentration (n=12):** Split between healthcare/mental health (Asha Health, Cartwheel, Clair Health, FoodHealth Company, Manifold, Marit, Stepful, Take2 AI, Winnie — 9 of 12, 75%) and work/education platforms (Anthill, KaiPod Learning, Tenor, WorkWhile — 4 of 12, 33%; some companies straddle both).
- **Thematic concentration:** Adolescent and clinical mental health (Cartwheel), healthcare workforce training (Stepful), and parent/caregiver-facing platforms (Winnie, Cartwheel) recur, consistent with her "care" thesis linking work, education, and caregiving [^4].
- **Stage:** Seed through Series B per Signal by NFX and her own bio [^2][^3].
- **Co-investors on verified deals:** Menlo Ventures co-led Cartwheel's Series A alongside Reach [^5].

Sample size is limited to 12 disclosed Reach companies plus 4 prior-firm championed deals (Career Karma, Handshake, Guild, Contra) — enough to identify direction, not enough for tighter percentages.

## In Their Own Words

On evaluating founding teams (Ivy Podcast, 2021):

> "Is this a team that has, what I describe is an earned secret about the market?" [^7]

On post-investment support (Ivy Podcast, 2021):

> "There are so many investors out there that are like, were hugely valued. Add we, we help you on so many different things. And then they just ghost you once they break the check." [^7]
