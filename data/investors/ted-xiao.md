## Background

Ted Xiao is a Founding Member of Technical Staff at Project Prometheus, Jeff Bezos's AI startup building AGI for the physical economy [^1][^2]. He joined Prometheus in late 2025 after eight years at Google DeepMind, where he was a Staff Research Scientist and Tech Lead on the Robotics team and the Gemini Multimodal team [^1][^3]. He publicly announced his departure from Google DeepMind on X in late 2025, writing that he had helped "transform the dream of general-purpose robot learning from a heretical fringe idea into a normalized technology roadmap" [^3].

Xiao holds a B.S. and M.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from UC Berkeley (2013-2017), where he was advised by Professor Claire Tomlin and focused on control systems, intelligent systems, and robotics [^1][^4]. As an undergraduate he founded Machine Learning at Berkeley [^1][^4]. His notable research contributions include SayCan, RT-1, RT-2, Open X-Embodiment (IEEE ICRA 2024 Best Paper Award), the Octo generalist robot policy, and the Gemini Robotics series [^1]. He writes the *Action-driven Intelligence* Substack about foundation models, robotics, and decision-making [^5].

Project Prometheus, co-founded by Jeff Bezos and Vik Bajaj, raised $6.2 billion at launch in late 2025 and a $12 billion Series B at a $41 billion valuation in June 2026, with approximately 150 employees across San Francisco, London, and Zurich [^2].

## Stated Thesis

Xiao has not published a formal investment thesis. His public writing focuses on the technical and economic significance of physical AI. On his Substack, he has argued that "physical AGI will be the final frontier of AI as a whole" and that the future "will be a future built, tested, and ultimately measured against the physical world" [^6]. He describes his research agenda as "scaling frontier models for real-world understanding and interaction, with a particular focus on approaches that can leverage internet-scale foundation models and methods that improve with more experience" [^1].
