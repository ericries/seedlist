## Background

Ant Wilson (full name Anthony Wilson) is the co-founder and CTO of Supabase, the open-source Postgres development platform he started in January 2020 with Paul Copplestone [^1][^2]. He is based in Singapore [^3].

Before Supabase, Wilson co-founded two earlier ventures — Crypto Squad and STYLINDEX — and went through Entrepreneur First's Singapore cohort and Techstars London [^4]. Supabase itself was part of Y Combinator's Summer 2020 batch [^2], and has since raised over $116M in venture funding to build an open-source alternative to Firebase, attracting one of the fastest-growing open-source developer communities [^5][^1].

As an angel investor, Wilson backs early-stage developer infrastructure and AI tooling startups, typically in seed rounds alongside Y Combinator and dev-tools-focused angels [^6][^7].
