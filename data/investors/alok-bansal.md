## Background

Alok Bansal is the Co-Founder and Executive Vice Chairman (Whole-Time Director) of PB Fintech Limited, the publicly listed parent of India's largest online insurance marketplace Policybazaar and lending/credit marketplace Paisabazaar [^1] [^2]. At PB Fintech he leads strategy, inorganic growth, corporate development and investor relations [^3] [^1]. He co-founded Policybazaar in 2008 alongside Yashish Dahiya and Avaneesh Nirjar, and Paisabazaar in 2014 with Dahiya and Naveen Kukreja [^4] [^2].

Before Policybazaar, Bansal was Finance Director at First Europa, a European online insurance aggregator, where he first met Yashish Dahiya [^5]. Earlier roles include Senior Manager of Corporate Strategy at Mahindra & Mahindra (2006), Business Manager at iGate Global Solutions (2005), and four years at General Electric (2001–2005) [^5]. He began his career as a service engineer at Voltas Limited [^5]. He holds a B.Tech from Shri Shahu Ji Maharaj University, Kanpur (1997) and a post-graduate diploma in management from the Indian Institute of Management, Calcutta (2001) [^5] [^3]. CFO India Magazine recognised him among India's top 100 CFOs in 2016 [^5].

Following PB Fintech's November 2021 IPO, Bansal divested 28,57,820 shares at ₹825 apiece for ₹235.77 crore on 11 February 2022, providing a substantial pool of personal capital for angel activity [^6]. His personal angel-investing runs in parallel with, but is distinct from, PB Fintech's corporate M&A and investment activity, which he also oversees [^3] [^1].

## Stated Thesis

Bansal has not published a formal external investing thesis. His public commentary focuses on operating philosophy for founders and on PB Fintech's culture rather than on an angel-investing lens.

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size caveat:** Fewer than ten personal angel investments are publicly documented, plus one large personal stake in PB Healthcare Services that is a PB Fintech spinout he helps oversee rather than a pure external angel bet [^7] [^8] [^9] [^10] [^11] [^12]. The description below is qualitative rather than percentage-based.

- **Sector:** Concentrated in Indian financial services adjacent to his operator expertise — P2P lending (LenDenClub), NBFC/micro-lending (Sugmya Finance), invoice-factoring/SME finance (Reevoy) — with selective bets on consumer D2C (Blue Nectar) and AI-enabled hiring (JobsUPI), plus a large healthcare cheque via the PB Healthcare spinout [^7] [^8] [^9] [^10] [^11] [^12].
- **Stage:** All verified external investments are early — pre-seed, seed, or Series A [^7] [^8] [^9] [^10] [^11].
- **Geography:** Predominantly India-based (Gurugram, Delhi NCR, Mumbai), with one UK-headquartered India-focused SME finance bet in Reevoy [^7] [^8] [^9] [^10] [^11].
- **Check role:** Almost always a participant, not a lead. Rounds are typically institutionally led (Tuscan Ventures / Ohm Stock Brokers for LenDenClub; IIMA Ventures for JobsUPI; Stellaris Venture Partners / Better Capital for Reevoy's later seed) with Bansal joining alongside other operator-angels [^7] [^8] [^10] [^11].
- **Co-investor patterns:** Frequently invests alongside other Indian fintech founder-operators. In Sugmya Finance he co-invested with Yashish Dahiya (his PB Fintech co-founder) and Naveen Kukreja (Paisabazaar CEO) [^9]. In LenDenClub he co-invested with Kunal Shah (CRED), Ramakant Sharma (Livspace) and cricketer Hardik Pandya [^7]. In JobsUPI he co-invested with Anshoo Sharma, Amit Jain, and Manoj Sinsinwar [^11].
- **Distinctive:** His largest single reported check is not a startup angel bet but a personal ₹26.1 crore stake in PB Healthcare Services' September 2025 seed round — a PB Fintech spinout backed by PB Fintech's corporate ₹539.4 crore and General Catalyst's ₹435 crore [^12]. Like Yashish Dahiya, this is a "founder doubling down on his own thesis" investment rather than diversified angel exposure.
