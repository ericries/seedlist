## Background

Misbah Uraizee is the Co-Founder & CEO of Nectar Social, an agentic AI social commerce platform she launched in 2023 with her sister Farah Uraizee [^1][^2]. Before founding Nectar, she was a product leader at Meta, where she worked on brand-creator monetization and feed ranking and recommendations across Facebook and Instagram, and previously held a Group Product role at Twitter [^3][^4]. She was educated at Yale University and grew up between Singapore, Sweden, Ireland, and Northern California [^3][^4]. She holds multiple issued patents in areas including virtual reality passthrough positioning and cloud-based file attachment [^4].

Nectar Social raised a $10.6M seed round from GV and True Ventures in June 2025, and a $30M Series A led by Menlo Ventures' Anthology Fund in May 2026, with participation from True Ventures, GV, and Kinship Ventures [^1][^2][^5].
