## Background

Diederik P. "Durk" Kingma is a Dutch machine learning researcher who joined Anthropic in October 2024 to work on large-scale machine learning, primarily remotely from the Netherlands with occasional visits to the Bay Area [^1][^2]. He earned his PhD cum laude from the University of Amsterdam in 2017 under Max Welling, with the thesis *Variational Inference and Deep Learning: A New Synthesis* [^3].

Kingma was part of the founding team of OpenAI in 2015, where he led the Algorithms team working on generative AI techniques that later contributed to models such as DALL-E 3 and ChatGPT [^2][^4]. He left OpenAI in 2018 and, by his own account, spent time as a part-time angel investor and advisor to AI startups before joining Google Brain in July 2018; he remained at Google Brain (and subsequently Google DeepMind after the 2023 merger) until joining Anthropic [^2][^5].

His research contributions include the Variational Autoencoder (VAE), the Adam optimizer, Glow, and Variational Diffusion Models [^3]. He received the ICLR 2024 Test of Time Award for the VAE paper (the inaugural year of that award) and the ICLR 2025 Test of Time Award for the Adam optimizer [^3].

## Stated Thesis

Kingma does not publish a stated investment thesis. He is primarily a research scientist; his public communication centers on machine learning research rather than investing. He has not published a personal investment focus document, portfolio page, or written rubric for evaluating startups.

In a personal statement on joining Anthropic, he wrote that "Anthropic's approach to AI development resonates significantly with my own beliefs" and that he looked forward to "contributing to Anthropic's mission of developing powerful AI systems responsibly" [^1][^6].

Qualitative observations from the single verified investment:

- **Sector:** Frontier-AI applied to scientific discovery. CuspAI is a Cambridge-based AI platform for materials discovery, often described as a "search engine for molecules" [^7][^8].
- **Stage:** Participated as an angel in both the seed (2024, $30M) and Series A ($100M+, September 2025) rounds of the same company, suggesting a follow-on disposition rather than spray-and-pray angel checks [^7][^8].
- **Co-investor pattern:** In the CuspAI Series A, Kingma appeared alongside NEA and Temasek (co-leads), NVentures (NVIDIA), Samsung Ventures, Hoxton Ventures, LocalGlobe (Phoenix Court), Northzone, Giant Ventures, and fellow AI-company founders Victor Riparbelli (Synthesia) and Thomas Wolf (Hugging Face) [^7][^9].
- **Founder profile signal:** CuspAI's CEO Chad Edwards has a deep-tech / materials background, and the company is publicly described as a DeepMind/Cambridge spinout — consistent with Kingma backing technically-credentialed founders from his research network [^8][^9].

**Important caveat:** Multiple secondary sources describe Kingma as having been "a part-time angel investor and advisor for AI startups" between 2018 and his return to Google [^2][^5]. No public source enumerates that portfolio. Founders considering him as an angel should treat him as a researcher-operator who has done at least one notable check, not a programmatic angel investor.
