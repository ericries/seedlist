## Background

Matthias Geeroms is the co-founder and CFO of Lighthouse (originally OTA Insight), a hotel revenue management and commercial intelligence SaaS company founded in Ghent, Belgium in 2012 [^1][^2]. Lighthouse has raised approximately $473M in venture funding and reached Series C, with offices including London [^2]. Prior to founding Lighthouse, Geeroms worked as a transfer pricing specialist at the Belgian federal tax administration (FOD Financiën) from 2009 to 2015 [^5]. He holds a master-after-master degree in tax law from Ghent University and a master's in commercial sciences from Europese Hogeschool Brussel [^5].

Geeroms is part of the Ghent operator-angel cluster that has emerged around exits and scale-ups including Teamleader, Showpad, Silverfin, Henchman, Stardekk, and Aikido. He participates in Syndicate One, an angel investment group composed of Belgian tech founders and operators [^3][^6].
