## Background

Harry Stebbings is a British venture capitalist and podcaster, born June 22, 1996, in London [^1]. He dropped out of King's College London after four weeks of studying law and launched _The Twenty Minute VC_ podcast in January 2015 at age 18, with no industry contacts and roughly $50 in startup capital [^1][^2]. The inaugural episode featured Guy Kawasaki [^1].

Stebbings briefly worked at Atomico for six months, departing in May 2017 [^1]. In 2018, he co-founded Stride.VC, a London-based seed firm, alongside former Accel partner Fred Destin, with an initial £40M first close (eventually £100M) [^3]. He stepped down as a partner at Stride in February 2021 to focus on his own vehicle, 20VC, though he remained a partner in Stride's Fund I [^4].

He launched the 20VC Fund in 2020, initially with an ~$8.3M early-stage vehicle [^5]. In June 2021 he closed two additional funds totaling $140M — a $33M early-stage fund and a $107M growth fund — operating as a solo general partner [^6]. In October 2024 he announced 20VC Fund III at $400M, split between a $125M seed vehicle and a $275M Series A vehicle, bringing total AUM above $600M [^7][^8]. He was named to Forbes 30 Under 30 Europe (Finance) in 2019 [^1].

## Stated Thesis

Stebbings publicly describes his approach as founder-first rather than thesis-first. In a TechCrunch interview announcing the $400M Fund III, he said: "I follow great entrepreneurs...my job is to simply find the best founders before anyone else." [^7] He has also explicitly positioned the fund around European startups, telling TechCrunch: "I'm really fed up of everyone shitting on Europe. We have unbelievable companies, and we have incredible people." and "We need to make Europe great again. MEGA!" [^7]

Fund III is structured to provide operational support through three sub-funds focused on sales, growth, and product, run by operators who source deals and advise portfolio companies [^7].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on the 16 verified portfolio companies enumerated below from the 20VC Fund portfolio page (plus additional named investments from press coverage), the following patterns emerge:

- **Sector**: AI is the dominant category — 9 of 16 sampled companies on the public portfolio page are tagged "Artificial Intelligence" (56%): Altertable, Ami Labs, AskLio, Bottlecap, Cadensys, Cognition, Comstruct, CourtCorrect, Datalane [^9]. Fintech is the next most common category (Airwallex, allO, Buena, CryptoTax Calculator — 4 of 16, 25%) [^9]. Consumer and exits make up the remainder. Press-named companies (Linear, Merge, Linktree, Sorare, Pachama, NexHealth, Captions.ai, Peec AI, Lovable, ScorePlay, Tripledot, Taxdoo) extend this pattern but skew further toward AI-native software [^1][^7][^10][^10].
- **Stage**: The fund explicitly allocates $125M of $400M to seed and $275M to Series A [^7], i.e., a ~31%/69% split toward Series A by dollars. Recent led rounds include the Peec AI seed (July 2025) and the $13M ScorePlay Series A (February 2025) [^10][^10].
- **Geography**: Fund III is explicitly positioned to back European founders, though the LP base and prior portfolio span both Europe and the US [^7]. London is the firm's HQ [^1].
- **Founder profile**: Stebbings' public statements emphasize backing exceptional individuals over thematic bets [^7]. Public portfolio companies skew toward technical, product-led founding teams (Cognition, Linear, Lovable, Captions.ai).
- **Co-investors / LP relationships**: 20VC Fund III LPs include 40 founders from Atlassian, Candy Crush, Canva, Datadog, Deliveroo, Spotify, and Vinted, and GPs from Accel, Benchmark, Coatue, Founders Fund, Khosla, NEA, and TCV [^7] — an unusual signal that he sources deal flow through other top-tier funds and founders rather than competing for it.
- **Unicorns**: At least four reported portfolio unicorns: Linktree, Linear, Tripledot (~$1.4B post-2022 round), and Lovable (became a unicorn in 2025) [^1][^7][^10].

Sample size caveat: this analysis is based on 16 publicly-listed portfolio companies and an additional ~12 named in press, against Tracxn's reported ~62 investments [^10]. The portfolio page truncates with a "Load More" button not captured in the fetch. Percentages should be read as directional, not definitive.
