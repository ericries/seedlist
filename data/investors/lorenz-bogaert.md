## Background

Lorenz Bogaert, born 1976, is a Belgian serial internet entrepreneur based in Ghent [^1]. In 1999 he co-founded ASL.TO with Toon Coppens in Ghent — the site that was renamed Redbox in 2002 and then Netlog around 2006, reaching over 94 million registered users across 20+ languages at its peak [^2]. Netlog was folded into the Massive Media group, which in 2011 launched Twoo, the dating/social-discovery product co-founded by Bogaert and Coppens [^3]. In December 2012 Massive Media was acquired by Meetic (subsequently part of IAC/Match Group) [^3][^1].

Bogaert went on to co-found or help launch several other Belgian tech companies that produced exits: Rydoo (formed in 2018 from the merger of Xpenditure and iAlbatros, backed by Sodexo, which took ownership) [^4]; Delta, a cryptocurrency portfolio tracker acquired by eToro in November 2019 [^5][^6]; and Realo, the property-data scale-up he co-founded in 2014 with Toon Coppens, Nicolas Van Eenaeme and Vincent Verlee, acquired by Immoweb (an AVIV Group / Axel Springer subsidiary) on January 23, 2024 [^7][^8].

Today Bogaert is co-founder and CEO of StarApps, a Ghent-based venture studio, with co-founder Nicolas Van Eenaeme and the Realo/Netlog crew [^9][^7]. He also co-founded Pitchdrive, an Antwerp-based early-stage venture fund, in 2020, alongside Jonas Dhaenens (Team.Blue), Boris Bogaert, Wim Derkinderen, Koen Christiaens (Xpenditure/Rydoo), Toon Coppens and Luc Verelst (Droia) [^10]. Pitchdrive Fund III closed at €40M in May 2024 [^10]. He is a co-founder and chairman at Bizzy [^11], and he was listed as Belgium's 4th most important angel investor in 2019 by an Antwerp Management School ranking cited on his Wikipedia page [^1]. He was named Young Entrepreneur of the Year in Belgium in 2008 [^1].

## Stated Thesis

Bogaert has not published a formal personal investment thesis. In his and& Festival 2023 speaker bio he is described as having co-founded Pitchdrive as an "investment platform and 30M€ fund" and StarApps as a venture builder [^9]. He is quoted in the Henchman seed-round announcement (February 2022) explaining what attracted Pitchdrive to that deal: "Henchman manages to cleverly address a widespread problem faced by lawyers. With Pitchdrive, we can help the strong team behind Henchman grow even faster on a global scale." [^12]

In a 2022 Bloovi interview he framed his stage preference sharply: "Ik ben en blijf een early stage guy. Als ze komen aandraven met compliance officers en preventieadviseurs, dan ben ik weg." ("I am and remain an early-stage guy. When they show up with compliance officers and prevention advisors, I'm out.") [^13]

## Inferred Thesis

This analysis is based on **4 verified investments** where Bogaert's participation (personally or via StarApps) is confirmed by a primary or contemporaneous source: Aikido Security (Series B, 2026-01-14) [^14][^15], Henchman (Seed, 2022) [^12], Salesflare (Seed, 2018) [^16], and Realo (co-founder equity via StarApps venture-studio spinout, 2014) [^7]. Signal by NFX lists 51 total investment positions and Tracxn cites 43 companies; those aggregate counts are not individually cross-referenced with primary sources in this profile [^16][^17].

**Sector distribution (small verified sample):** Software-heavy — cybersecurity (Aikido), legal-tech SaaS (Henchman), sales-CRM SaaS (Salesflare), proptech (Realo). Aggregator-listed portfolio (per Signal/NFX and Angel Match) extends the same B2B-SaaS bias into logistics/robotics (OTIV), contract lifecycle (Contractify), field-service (FixForm), and consumer marketplaces (Maison Sport) [^16][^18]. Sample is too small for reliable sector percentages.

**Stage distribution:** Early-stage biased. The Pitchdrive Fund III thesis states pre-seed/seed tickets of €250K–€2M [^10]. Bogaert's own quote — "I am and remain an early-stage guy" [^13] — is consistent with the observed pre-seed / seed / early-Series-A footprint. Signal-listed 2025-2026 entries include multiple pre-seed and seed rounds (Vision Lab, Maurice & Nora, Feedelity, Donna, Stardust, Eagl, Driven) [^16], with Aikido Series B (2026) as an outlier late-stage participation.

**Geographic focus:** Predominantly Belgium/Benelux with some pan-European extension. Aggregator counts cited on Angel Match state 31 of 37 investments are in Belgian startups [^18]. Pitchdrive itself has offices in Antwerp, Ghent, Amsterdam, Berlin, Barcelona, London and New York [^10]. StarApps is Ghent-based [^9][^7].

**Co-investor patterns:** The recurring counterparties are the "Netlog/Massive Media" cluster and the broader Ghent tech network. Toon Coppens co-founded Netlog, Twoo, Realo and StarApps with Bogaert and shows up again as a co-investor on Maurice & Nora (2026, per Signal/NFX) [^16][^7]. Aikido's Series B round-mates include multiple Ghent operators — Louis Jonckheere and Pieterjan Bouten (Showpad), Hendrik Isebaert (Showpad), Joris Van Der Gucht (Silverfin), Gilles Mattelin and Jorn Vanysacker (Henchman) — most of whom appear elsewhere in Bogaert's or Pitchdrive's portfolio [^15][^14][^12].

**Founder profile patterns:** Repeat operators and product-technical teams. Bogaert has publicly stated a preference for founders committed to the work: "Ik leerde dat ik moest focussen. Of een normale job kiezen, of 100 procent gaan voor het ondernemerschap. Je kan niet een beetje ondernemer zijn." ("I learned I had to focus — either choose a regular job, or go 100 percent for entrepreneurship. You can't be a part-time entrepreneur.") [^13]

**Notable pattern vs. framing:** The recurring co-investment with Pitchdrive limited partners and StarApps co-founders in Ghent means many "personal angel" checks are entangled with the Pitchdrive fund and the StarApps studio — the distinction between "Bogaert angel," "StarApps venture-studio equity," and "Pitchdrive fund" is not always cleanly disclosed in secondary sources. Primary sources cited here name him or "StarApps" specifically.
