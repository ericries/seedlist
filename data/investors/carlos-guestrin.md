## Background

Carlos Ernesto Guestrin is a machine learning researcher, founder, and angel investor. He was born in Argentina in 1975 and raised in Brazil, where he earned a Mechatronics Engineer degree from the Polytechnic School at the University of São Paulo before completing his M.Sc. and Ph.D. in Computer Science at Stanford University under Daphne Koller [^1][^2]. His academic career has spanned three institutions: Associate (Finmeccanica) Professor at Carnegie Mellon University (2004-2012), Amazon Professor of Machine Learning at the University of Washington (2012-2021), and since 2021, the Fortinet Founders Professor of Computer Science at Stanford University [^1][^2][^3]. In 2025 he was named Director of the Stanford AI Lab (SAIL), now run as a joint laboratory between Stanford HAI and the School of Engineering [^2][^3].

In parallel with his academic work, Guestrin co-founded the machine learning company GraphLab in 2013 (later renamed Dato, then Turi) out of his UW research, serving as CEO [^3][^4]. In August 2016, Apple acquired Turi in a deal reported at approximately $200M, after which Guestrin joined Apple as Senior Director of Machine Learning and AI from 2016 to 2021 before returning full-time to academia at Stanford [^1][^4]. His research and open-source contributions include XGBoost, LIME (local interpretable model-agnostic explanations), Apache TVM, MXNet, Turi Create, GraphLab/PowerGraph, SFrame, and GraphChi [^1][^3].

In 2024, Guestrin co-founded Virtue AI — an AI security and compliance platform for generative AI — with Bo Li (CEO), Dawn Song, and Sanmi Koyejo, serving as Chief Scientist [^3][^5]. He has also served as Chief Scientist at Visual Layer since 2022 and as a Board Member of OctoML/OctoAI since 2019; OctoAI was acquired by Nvidia in September 2024 [^3][^6]. He was elected to the U.S. National Academy of Engineering in 2024 and is a recipient of the IJCAI Computers and Thought Award (2009) and the Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers (PECASE, 2010) [^1][^2].

## Stated Thesis

His publicly stated focus areas — articulated through his Stanford research and his founding of Virtue AI — center on machine learning foundations, interpretability, and the safety/security of generative AI systems [^2][^3][^5]. Virtue AI's launch announcement frames the mission as eliminating "the traditional tradeoff between AI innovation and security, enabling enterprises to confidently deploy generative AI without worrying about security, safety, and compliance" [^5].
