## Background

Ellie Fields is CEO and Co-Founder of Ridge AI, a Seattle-based startup building AI-native embedded analytics for B2B software companies [^1][^2]. She co-founded the company with Jeff Heer, a Stanford professor and creator of open-source data visualization tools including Vega, Vega-Lite, D3.js, and the Mosaic framework [^2].

Prior to Ridge AI, Fields spent 12 years at Tableau Software, where she held leadership positions spanning marketing, engineering, and product management. She was part of the core team that envisioned, built, and launched Tableau Public and Tableau Online, and led product strategy for mobile apps, collaboration, search, and content experience [^3][^4]. She was announced as Salesloft's Chief Product Officer in 2021 (a role she later expanded to Chief Product and Engineering Officer) [^5][^1]. Before Tableau, she worked at Microsoft [^4].

Fields holds an MBA from Stanford Graduate School of Business and dual undergraduate degrees (B.S. in Engineering and B.A. in Policy) from Rice University [^3][^4].
