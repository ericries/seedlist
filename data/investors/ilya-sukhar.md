## Background

Ilya Sukhar is a General Partner at Matrix Partners (which now operates as Matrix), a US early-stage venture firm [^1] [^2]. He immigrated from the USSR as a child and grew up in the San Francisco Bay Area, later studying Computer Science and Operations Research at Cornell University [^1].

Sukhar was co-founder and CEO of Parse, a mobile backend-as-a-service platform founded in 2011 with Tikhon Bernstam, Kevin Lacker, and James Yu [^3]. Parse was acquired by Facebook in 2013 for approximately $85 million; Sukhar remained at Facebook after the acquisition [^3]. Parse was open-sourced in 2016 and its hosted service was shut down on January 30, 2017 [^3].

Before joining Matrix in 2016, Sukhar worked on three startups that were all acquired [^2]. He was also a partner at Y Combinator prior to Matrix [^1]. He is an active angel investor and maintains Vimium, a widely used Chrome extension providing keyboard-driven browser navigation [^1].
