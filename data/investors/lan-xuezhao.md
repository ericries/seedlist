## Background

Lan Xuezhao is the founder and Managing Partner of Basis Set Ventures, a San Francisco-based early-stage venture firm focused on AI, automation, and enterprise SaaS [^1][^2]. She founded the firm in 2017, and as of 2026 it manages over $850M in assets across four funds: an initial Fund I (2017), Fund II at $165M (2021), Fund III at $185M (announced 2024), and Fund IV at $250M (2026) [^2][^3][^5].

Lan earned an M.A. in Statistics (2008) and a Ph.D. in Psychology (2009) from the University of Michigan, where her doctoral research focused on quantitative cognitive science and was published in journals including Science [^1][^2]. After her PhD she founded a brain-training game startup for children (later acquired), worked as an engagement manager at McKinsey & Company, and in 2013 joined Dropbox, where she built and ran the Corporate Development Strategy team during its most acquisitive period [^1][^2].

She serves on the advisory board of the University of Michigan School of Information [^1][^2]. In 2026 she was named to Business Insider's "Seed 40" ranking of female early-stage VC investors [^2]. According to SVB, Basis Set was the largest early-stage venture firm by AUM founded by a solo woman in 2017 [^1].

## Stated Thesis

Basis Set publicly describes its mission as creating "universal abundance by uniting the power of machine intelligence with human ingenuity" [^1]. Lan has stated: "We are a seed-stage fund that focuses on AI and automation" and "We invest in companies that transform the way people work. Most of our portfolio are enterprise companies" [^6]. She has described investing "both down and up the stack, anything from infrastructure to the data layer, to algorithm, to application" [^6].

The firm self-describes as one of the earliest to specialize in early-stage AI startups [^3]. Basis Set uses an internal AI sourcing system named "Pascal" and operates a "HyperGrowth" platform connecting portfolio companies with mentors [^2][^3].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on a verified sample of ~14 portfolio companies (PitchBook/Signal list 36 investments on record for Lan personally [^7]; this is a subset):

- **Stage**: Predominantly seed, with Lan often leading. Her stated check range is $1M-$5M with a ~$2.5M sweet spot [^7]. She made her first seed investment (Scale AI) in 2016, the year before founding the firm [^1].
- **Sector concentration**: Of the 14 verified investments below, approximately 10 of 14 (~71%) are AI/ML-native (Scale AI, Path Robotics, CuspAI, Sakana AI, OpenArt, DataGrail, Rasa, Mem0, Simular, Octolane AI). The remainder are automation-of-work plays (Workstream for hourly workforce, Ergeon for residential construction, Drata for compliance, Quince for commerce). Both clusters fit the stated "transform the way people work" framing [^2][^3][^7].
- **Fund discipline**: Lan has stated, "We're pretty careful with consistency and discipline on fund size. It's the same thesis, the same fund construction since fund one" [^5]. Fund sizes stepped from ~$100M → $165M → $185M → $250M over four vintages — modest growth versus comparable AI-focused firms.
- **Founder profile**: Strong preference for technical / PhD-credentialed founders. Lan's own approach emphasizes measurable "founder superpowers" — agile thinking, confidence, day-to-day effectiveness, founder-market fit, and learning speed — drawn from her psychology background [^6][^4].
- **Notable early bet**: Scale AI seed in 2016 (Alexandr Wang), years before generative AI became consensus. Across her portfolio, Basis Set claims aggregate value exceeding $20B [^1].
- **Co-investor pattern (limited sample)**: Recent 2024-2025 rounds (CuspAI, Mem0, Simular, Octolane AI) frequently as co-investor rather than lead, suggesting a barbell of lead-seed plus selective participation in later rounds [^7].

Sample size is modest (14 of 36+ on record); treat percentages as directional.
