## Background

Brett Bejcek is co-founder of Limitless AI (formerly Rewind AI), an AI startup building a wearable "Pendant" device and personal-memory software, founded in 2020 with Dan Siroker (Optimizely's co-founder and former CEO) [^1][^2]. Limitless raised over $33M from investors including Sam Altman, First Round Capital, Andreessen Horowitz, and NEA [^2], and was acquired by Meta in December 2025 [^5].

Before Limitless, Bejcek worked in data science at Spotify, Glowforge, PwC, and JPMorgan Chase, with earlier research roles at the U.S. Department of Education and the Cleveland Clinic [^1][^6]. He holds a B.S. in Data Analytics from The Ohio State University [^1][^6]. His personal site describes his work as "predictive models, encoding meaning into visualizations, and running an experimentation platform" [^4].

Separately, Bejcek has described a project called "Second Rodeo," framed as an alternative investing approach that supplies "human capital to early-stage founders to create early growth loops in advance of committing themselves to the unicorn path," building a network of vetted operators that help companies scale [^4]. No public list of Second Rodeo investments has been located.

## Stated Thesis

Bejcek has not published a formal investing thesis. The closest public statement of approach is the Second Rodeo framing on his personal site, which positions his investing activity as **operator/talent-led support for early-stage founders** rather than capital-first check writing [^4].

He has spoken publicly about founder-led beta programs and the operational craft of getting an early product into users' hands, in a Learners Podcast appearance on 2024-10-28 [^7].
