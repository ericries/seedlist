## Background

Dawn Xiaodong Song is a Professor of Computer Science at the University of California, Berkeley and Co-Director of the Berkeley Center for Responsible Decentralized Intelligence (RDI) [^1][^2]. She earned her B.S. from Tsinghua University (1996), M.S. from Carnegie Mellon University (1999), and Ph.D. in Computer Science from UC Berkeley (2002) [^3]. She served as an Assistant Professor at Carnegie Mellon (2002–2007) before joining the UC Berkeley EECS faculty in 2007 [^3][^4].

Song's research spans AI safety and security, agentic AI, deep learning, security and privacy, and decentralization technology [^1][^2][^15]. She created BitBlaze, a platform for malware analysis whose techniques were later used in tools shipping with Google Chrome, and is widely recognized as the most-cited scholar in computer security by the AMiner Award [^3][^4].

Her honors include a MacArthur Foundation Fellowship (2010), Guggenheim Fellowship, Alfred P. Sloan Research Fellowship, NSF CAREER Award, IBM Faculty Award, MIT Technology Review TR35 Innovator Under 35 (2009), ACM Fellow (2019), IEEE Fellow, election to the American Academy of Arts and Sciences (2025), and appointment as an AI2050 Senior Fellow at Schmidt Sciences (2025) [^1][^3][^14][^15]. She was named on Inc.'s Female Founder 100 list and the Wired25 list of innovators [^2].

Song is a serial founder as well as an academic. In 2018 she co-founded Oasis Labs, a privacy-preserving cloud-computing and blockchain startup where she served as CEO [^4][^6][^7]. Oasis Labs raised $45 million on July 9, 2018 from a syndicate led by Andreessen Horowitz's a16z crypto fund, with Accel, Binance Labs, DCVC, Electric Capital, Foundation Capital, Pantera, Polychain, Metastable, and Coinbase co-founder Fred Ehrsam participating [^6][^7]. Earlier, she founded the mobile security startup Ensighta Security, which was acquired by FireEye [^4][^19]. UC Berkeley Research also credits her with co-founding Menlo Security, though the company's own founder list does not include her — most likely a scientific/technical co-founder role rather than an operational one [^5].

In 2024 she co-founded Virtue AI, an enterprise AI safety and security platform, alongside Bo Li, Carlos Guestrin, and Sanmi Koyejo [^9][^10]. Virtue AI raised $30 million in seed and Series A funding on April 15, 2025, led by Lightspeed Venture Partners and Walden Catalyst Ventures with participation from Prosperity7, Factory, Osage University Partners, Lip-Bu Tan, Amarjit Gill, and Chris Ré [^9][^10]. In June 2026, Meta hired Song, Bo Li, and Sanmi Koyejo out of Virtue AI into Meta Superintelligence Labs, where Song became Vice President of AI Research reporting to Nat Friedman [^11][^20].

## Stated Thesis

*(Self-reported. See Inferred Thesis for analysis of actual investment behavior.)*

Song has described her motivation for building companies alongside academic research as translating research into real-world impact: "As we give birth to new ideas in research, it's really nice to be able to see the ideas grow and mature and have real-world impact" [^5].

Her public statements as CEO of Oasis Labs framed her thesis around user data ownership and privacy-preserving computation: "The Oasis platform aims to help users leverage and reclaim control over their data, and at the same time deliver superior performance and privacy capabilities" [^6]. She has consistently argued that users should not have to choose between privacy and the convenience of modern online services [^4].

Her current research thrusts — AI safety and security, and frontier AI for program synthesis and cybersecurity [^1][^15] — closely track the sectors she has backed as an angel investor. She serves on the World Economic Forum AGI Global Council (2025–present) and was Program Co-Chair of ICLR 2020 [^1].

## Inferred Thesis

*Analysis based on 4 publicly verifiable angel investments cross-referenced with two aggregator profiles (Signal by NFX and Tracxn) [^16][^17]. The sample is small; percentages are not meaningful at this size, so qualitative patterns are used.*

- **Stage:** Every verified angel check is at seed or pre-seed (Together AI seed 2023, Eclipse pre-seed/Series A 2022, alphaXiv seed 2025, NeoCognition seed 2026) [^8][^12][^13][^17][^18]. Signal NFX places her "sweet spot" at ~$25K per check with a range of $5K–$50K, consistent with an academic angel writing early support checks alongside institutional leads rather than leading rounds [^16].
- **Sector:** Two dominant clusters — (1) AI infrastructure and agentic AI (Together AI, alphaXiv, NeoCognition) and (2) crypto/blockchain infrastructure (Eclipse, plus her own Oasis Labs). Signal NFX ranks her among top angels in Security, Web3/Blockchain, and AI at seed and pre-seed [^16].
- **Founder profile:** The verified investments skew heavily toward AI researcher-founders and academic technical founders — Yu Su (Sloan Research Fellow, Ohio State) at NeoCognition, the Together AI open-source generative team, alphaXiv's AI-research-tooling founders, and Eclipse's Solana-VM infrastructure team [^8][^12][^13][^18]. This matches Song's own founder profile and her position at Berkeley RDI.
- **Co-investor patterns:** Signal NFX flags frequent co-investment overlap with Eric Schmidt, Dave Eisenberg, and partners at Lux Capital, Red Swan Ventures, and Susa Ventures [^16]. Lux Capital led Together AI's seed alongside her check [^8]. Lip-Bu Tan appears both as a co-founder-backer of Virtue AI and as a co-angel investor in NeoCognition [^9][^12].
- **Geography:** All verified investments are US-based, concentrated in the SF Bay Area [^16].
- **Notable gap between founder role and angel activity:** Despite being a prominent public voice on privacy and blockchain, her angel portfolio is now dominated by AI research tooling, not privacy or blockchain — reflecting the same 2023–2026 pivot in her research and company-founding activity (Virtue AI, Meta Superintelligence Labs).

## In Their Own Words

**On research becoming real-world companies:**
> "As we give birth to new ideas in research, it's really nice to be able to see the ideas grow and mature and have real-world impact." [^5]

**On why she founded Oasis Labs — user ownership of data:**
> "Blockchains are poised to revolutionize much of the way we live, but many developers and organizations have understandable concerns about performance and privacy limitations that are currently hindering their ability to embrace the technology. The Oasis platform aims to help users leverage and reclaim control over their data, and at the same time deliver superior performance and privacy capabilities." [^6]

**On the technical bet behind Oasis Labs:**
> "In our experiments we see performance orders of magnitude greater than ethereum. This architecture also supports far more computationally intensive tasks like machine learning and AI, which are not possible with today's blockchain technologies." [^7]
