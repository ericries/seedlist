## Background

Farah Uraizee is the Co-Founder & CTO of Nectar Social, an agentic AI social commerce platform she launched in 2023 with her sister Misbah Uraizee [^1][^2]. Prior to Nectar, she was an engineering leader at Meta, where she worked on the infrastructure behind Facebook Groups as the product scaled to over one billion users [^2][^3]. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Stanford University and previously studied at Doshisha University [^4].

Nectar Social raised a $10.6M seed round from GV and True Ventures announced in June 2025, followed by a $30M Series A led by Menlo Ventures' Anthology Fund in May 2026, with participation from True Ventures, GV, and Gwyneth Paltrow's Kinship Ventures [^1][^3][^5].
