## Background

Daniel ("Dan") Kan is an American entrepreneur, operator, and angel investor. He is best known as the **co-founder and former Chief Operating Officer / Chief Product Officer of Cruise Automation**, the autonomous-vehicle company he started with Kyle Vogt in 2013 and that was acquired by General Motors in March 2016 for a reported $1B+ [^1] [^2] [^3]. He is the younger brother of Justin Kan, co-founder of Justin.tv (which became Twitch) and Socialcam [^1].

Kan graduated from Lakeside School in Seattle (2005) and Claremont McKenna College (2009), and began his career at the San Francisco startup UserVoice before going on to co-found **Exec**, a Y Combinator–backed on-demand assistant/cleaning service, in 2011 [^1]. Exec was acquired by Handy (then Handybook) in 2014 for under $10M, after which Kan immediately joined Vogt at Cruise — itself a YC W14 company — as co-founder [^1].

After the 2016 GM acquisition, Kan and Vogt continued to run Cruise inside GM. Kan stayed with the company through 2023, ultimately as Chief Product Officer [^4]. He resigned on **November 20, 2023**, one day after Vogt's own resignation, in the aftermath of the California DMV suspending Cruise's driverless permit following an October 2023 robotaxi incident [^4].

Kan is now an active angel investor and a **Partner at Rebel Fund**, the YC-alumni-only seed syndicate founded by Jared Heyman in 2019 [^5] [^6]. His public investing profile lists him as a "leading super angel" with a long-tailed portfolio of YC and adjacent startups [^7].

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample frame: small.** Daniel Kan's personal angel record is sparse in public databases — Signal NFX shows ~12 on-record direct investments [^7], and PitchBook/Crunchbase/CB Insights list him primarily as an operator-investor rather than as a fund GP [^8] [^9] [^10]. He also invests through Rebel Fund, whose portfolio of 128 companies (Tracxn, Feb 2026) is collective rather than individually attributable [^11]. The numbers below should be read as qualitative, not statistical.

- **Stage: seed-heavy with occasional pre-seed and Series A follow-on.** Signal NFX's verified entries are dominated by seed rounds in the $2M–$10M range, with sweet-spot checks of ~$25K [^7].
- **Sectors: AI, healthtech, consumer/marketplaces.** Recent verified checks include Hona (GenAI medical records), Outset, Fathom (AI meeting notes), JustPaid (B2B fintech), Maker Wine (consumer CPG), and Evil Genius Games (consumer/media) [^7] [^12].
- **YC alignment is strong.** Through his Rebel Fund partnership, Kan participates in a fund whose portfolio is 100% Y Combinator alumni [^5] [^6]. Many of his direct angel checks also land in YC-adjacent companies.
- **Co-investors skew YC operator-angels.** Signal NFX lists Harj Taggar (YC), Lachy Groom (Stripe alum), and Sequoia partners among frequent co-investors [^7].
- **Active-investor signal: positive.** The Hona seed round (May 2024) is the most recent publicly cited investment in which Kan/Rebel Fund are explicitly named [^12]. The cadence is lower than a full-time GP, consistent with his stated profile as an operator-angel.

**Caveat (sample size):** Public databases under-count private angel checks. Kan's actual investment count is likely higher than the dozen Signal NFX has verified, but only the verified entries are usable as data here.
