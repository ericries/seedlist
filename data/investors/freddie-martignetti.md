## Background

Freddie Martignetti is the Founder and General Partner of Nava Ventures, an early-stage venture firm he launched in 2021 out of San Francisco [^1][^2]. He began his career on Wall Street in fixed-income trading at Lehman Brothers and Barclays Capital, then founded Suffolk Equity Partners in 2011, a small private investment firm through which he backed consumer and tech companies including Warby Parker, FIGS Scrubs, Drizly, Omaze, Grab, RapidSOS, and Parachute [^1][^3]. After roughly five years at Suffolk he joined Highland Capital Partners, where he worked with Everly Health, Bitwise, and Vecna Robotics before spinning out to start Nava [^1][^3]. He holds a Harvard undergraduate degree and a Harvard MBA, and is also a co-founder of Mezcal Rosaluna [^1][^4].

## Stated Thesis

Nava's public materials describe the firm as an early-stage investor focused on being a "first-call" partner to founders across pre-seed, seed and Series A rounds in the United States [^1][^2]. Martignetti's own public quotes emphasize consumer-facing wealth-building and everyday-utility products. On leading the Grifin round he said the company had "the potential to make a remarkably positive impact by helping their app users lay the foundation for long-term wealth building" [^5][^6].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on Nava's public portfolio of ~18 named companies and Martignetti's prior Suffolk portfolio (7 companies), the pattern is: (1) predominantly Series A leads and seed-plus rounds in US-based software; (2) a mix of consumer/commerce (Grifin, True Footage, Relief App, Omaze, Warby Parker, FIGS, Drizly, Parachute) and enterprise infrastructure/AI (Hydrolix, Union.ai, Yurts, Legion, GrowthBook, Taut, OpenStudio, lang.ai, Synthetic) [^2][^3][^7]. Health/insurance surfaces in Open Loop and Superscript, and industrial automation in AeroVect [^2]. Sample size is small enough (~25 verified positions across Suffolk + Nava) that qualitative description is more honest than percentages, but the consumer/commerce vs. enterprise-AI split appears roughly balanced. Board seats confirmed at Grifin (following the 2025-06-18 Series A) [^5]. Co-investor patterns include TTV, Draper Associates, Gaingels, Nevcaut Ventures, and Alloy Labs (Grifin) [^5][^6].
