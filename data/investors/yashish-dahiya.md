## Background

Yashish Dahiya is the Co-Founder, Chairman and Group CEO of PB Fintech Limited, the parent company of India's largest online insurance marketplace Policybazaar and lending/credit marketplace Paisabazaar [^1] [^2]. He co-founded Policybazaar in 2008 alongside Alok Bansal and Avaneesh Nirjar, and Paisabazaar in 2014 [^3] [^2]. PB Fintech was publicly listed on Indian stock exchanges in November 2021 [^1].

Before Policybazaar, Dahiya was Managing Director of ebookers.com, a pan-European online travel distributor and FTSE 250 company listed on Nasdaq, and earlier a management consultant with Bain & Co. in London [^1]. He holds a bachelor's degree in engineering from IIT Delhi, a post-graduate diploma in management from IIM Ahmedabad, and an MBA from INSEAD [^4] [^1]. He was named Ernst & Young Entrepreneur of the Year 2010 in the Financial Services category [^4].

His personal angel-investing activity is modest and thesis-adjacent: a small number of Indian fintech and wealthtech seed deals, one fitness advisory position from 2016-2017, and — most substantially — a large personal cheque into the PB Healthcare spinout in 2025 alongside PB Fintech's corporate stake [^5] [^6] [^7] [^8].

## Stated Thesis

Dahiya has not published a formal investing thesis. His public commentary focuses on operating philosophy for founders rather than an investment lens. In an interview with YourStory, he advised founders to think long-term and to maintain personal integrity, saying: "Don't lie to yourself; once you start lying to one person, you do it to everybody and soon you start lying to yourself" [^9].

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size caveat:** Only four personal angel investments are publicly documented (ZFunds, Junio, Sugmya Finance, Fitso), plus a fifth large personal stake in PB Healthcare that is a corporate spinout he leads rather than a pure external angel deal [^5] [^6] [^7] [^8] [^10]. This is not a large enough sample to compute meaningful percentages; the description below is qualitative.

- **Sector:** Concentrated in Indian financial services adjacent to his operator expertise — wealthtech (ZFunds), consumer fintech / kids' payments (Junio), NBFC lending (Sugmya Finance) — plus one fitness/consumer bet (Fitso) and healthcare via PB Healthcare [^5] [^6] [^7] [^8] [^10]. This overlaps directly with Policybazaar and Paisabazaar's operating domain.
- **Stage:** All four external angel investments were early — Angel or Seed rounds [^5] [^6] [^7] [^8].
- **Geography:** All verified investments are India-based (Gurugram, Delhi NCR) [^5] [^6] [^7] [^8].
- **Check role:** Almost always a participant, not a lead. Rounds are typically institutionally led (Elevation Capital led ZFunds; NB Ventures later led Junio's Pre-Series A) with Dahiya joining alongside other operators like Kunal Shah (CRED), Ashneer Grover (BharatPe), and Alok Bansal [^5] [^6] [^7].
- **Co-investor patterns:** Frequently invests alongside other Indian fintech founder-operators. In Sugmya Finance he co-invested with Alok Bansal (his Policybazaar co-founder) and Naveen Kukreja (Paisabazaar CEO) [^7].
- **Distinctive:** His largest single check is not a startup bet but a personal ₹52.2 Cr stake in PB Healthcare Services (a PB Fintech spinout) alongside PB Fintech's corporate ₹539.4 Cr and General Catalyst's ₹435 Cr [^8]. This is a "founder doubling down on his own thesis" investment rather than a diversified angel portfolio.
