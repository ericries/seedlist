## Background

Tudor Achim is the co-founder and CEO of Harmonic, an AI lab building what it calls "mathematical superintelligence" through its flagship system Aristotle [^1][^2]. He co-founded the company with Robinhood co-founder Vlad Tenev, who serves as Executive Chairman [^2].

Achim graduated from Carnegie Mellon University in 2011 with a BS in Computer Science and Mathematics [^3]. He began a PhD in computer science at Stanford but did not finish; an earlier profile describes him as having pursued a PhD in computational biology before dropping out [^3][^4]. Prior to Harmonic he was a staff software engineer at Quora, then co-founded Helm.ai in November 2016 with Vlad Voroninski, serving as CTO of the autonomous-driving software company [^3][^5]. Helm.ai came out of stealth in March 2020 with $13M in seed funding from A.Capital Ventures, Amplo, Binnacle Partners, Sound Ventures, Fontinalis Partners and SV Angel [^5].

Harmonic has raised approximately $295M across three rounds since 2024: a $75M Series A led by Sequoia (September 2024, ~$325M valuation) [^6], a $100M Series B led by Kleiner Perkins (July 2025, ~$900M post-money) [^7], and a $120M Series C led by Ribbit Capital (November 2025, $1.45B valuation) with participation from Sequoia, Index Ventures, Kleiner Perkins, and Emerson Collective [^8]. In 2025, Harmonic's Aristotle achieved gold-medal-level performance on International Mathematical Olympiad problems, matching results from OpenAI and Google DeepMind but with formally verified proofs written in the Lean 4 language [^4][^8].

## Stated Thesis

Tudor Achim is not a fund investor and has not published an investment thesis. As an operator, his publicly stated focus is on building "mathematical superintelligence" — AI systems that produce formally verified outputs by reasoning in Lean rather than natural language [^1][^2]. He has described Harmonic's mission as "to explore the frontiers of human knowledge" [^1].

## In Their Own Words

On mathematical superintelligence and the IMO result:

> "Aristotle's gold-medal performance at the IMO offered an early glimpse into what Mathematical Superintelligence makes possible with advanced reasoning and formal verification together in a single system. This funding allows us to advance that foundation even further and faster, while aggressively deploying Aristotle across industries where reliability is critical." — Tudor Achim, Harmonic Series C announcement, November 25, 2025 [^8]

On the timeline for AI mathematicians:

> "Within 2 or 3 years, AI mathematicians will surpass human mathematicians for any specific mathematical task." — Tudor Achim, interview with The Generalist, 2025 [^4]

> "I don't think it'll be a decade, like some people say." — Tudor Achim, interview with The Generalist, 2025 [^4]

On why math matters:

> "When we started the company, we had a really big focus on math, and maybe we can get to that later. But if you look around at all fields of science and engineering—well, almost all fields—math is really at their foundation." — Tudor Achim, Sequoia *Training Data* podcast, 2024 [^1]

> "It just happens to be the case that the universe is explainable by math." — Tudor Achim, Sequoia *Training Data* podcast, 2024 [^1]

On synthetic data and self-improvement:

> "Synthetic data is the fuel for the model. Almost the only data you can use for this is synthetic data you generate yourself." — Tudor Achim, Sequoia *Training Data* podcast, 2024 [^1]

> "The most exciting part about math is that there is no upper bound." — Tudor Achim, Sequoia *Training Data* podcast, 2024 [^1]
