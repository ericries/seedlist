## Background

Elliot Hershberg is a Partner at Amplify Partners, where he leads the firm's biotech and digital-biology practice via the new Amplify Bio fund [^1][^2]. He holds a PhD from Stanford University (computational/molecular biology track), a BS in Computer Science from Oregon State University, and a BA in Biology from Southwestern University [^1]. Early in his career he conducted research at the Cancer Vaccine Institute at the University of Washington, studying molecular biology and cancer vaccines [^1].

Before joining Amplify Partners, Hershberg was a Biotech Partner and Research Analyst at Not Boring Capital, where his notable deals included Unnatural Products, Atomic AI, and Digital Biology [^2][^3]. He also writes the "Century of Biology" newsletter, which analyzes how computational biology is reshaping medicine [^2][^4].

Hershberg was appointed to Amplify Partners as a Partner in mid-2025, coinciding with the firm's announcement of Amplify Bio, a USD 200M fund dedicated to digital biology [^2][^5]. Amplify Bio was launched alongside Fund 6 (USD 400M core early-stage fund) and Fund 6 Select (USD 300M for existing portfolio companies), for a combined USD 900M raise [^5].

## Stated Thesis

Hershberg has stated: "My mission is to partner with leading engineers and researchers who are doing their life's work." [^1] He emphasizes that investors should understand the underlying science and help founders identify market opportunities where their technical background provides competitive advantages [^1]. Amplify Bio's stated thesis, which Hershberg leads, is that "as the costs of reading and writing DNA have fallen dramatically, tech and AI are starting to take over" the biotech stack, and that the fund targets founders combining biology expertise with programming and AI capabilities [^5].

- **Seed-stage bias**: Amplify Bio positions itself as seed and Series A [^5][^7], and Hershberg's most recent lead investment (Infinitopes, January 2026) was a seed extension [^6].
