## Background

Manu Iyer is Co-Founder and General Partner of **Bluehill.VC**, a Chennai-based deep-tech venture capital firm registered with SEBI as a Category I Alternative Investment Fund (Registration No. IN/AIF1/24-25/1513) [^1]. He holds a Bachelor of Technology in Electrical Engineering from IIT Madras and an MBA in Finance from Duke University's Fuqua School of Business (2007-2009) [^2][^3]. Before returning to India to become a full-time investor, he worked in US financial services [^2][^3].

Iyer's investing has run across two vehicles. He first built **Bluehill Capital** — an early-stage, angel-style vehicle founded in 2012 that backed hardware, clean-tech, and deep-tech companies with $100K-$500K checks [^2][^4][^5]. In May 2024 he and co-founder **Sridhar Parthasarathi** received SEBI approval for **Bluehill.VC**, a maiden ₹350 crore Cat-I VC fund; the fund announced a ₹80 crore first close in August 2024 and had raised ₹225 crore by mid-2025, targeting final close by year-end [^6][^1]. Bluehill.VC is advised by semiconductor veteran **Vinod Dham** ("Father of the Pentium") [^7][^8]. The fund plans to back 16-20 companies over four years with $1M-$1.5M average ticket sizes [^6].

Iyer is a charter member of **TiE Chennai**, a member of **The Chennai Angels**, and an active personal angel investor with 50+ startup investments in his own name [^3][^9][^2]. He mentors at IITs and IISc and has spoken publicly on India's deep-tech ecosystem, including at VCCircle "The Pitch" Bangalore (June 2025) and the IN2TN IP Conclave (2025) [^10][^11].

## Stated Thesis

Bluehill.VC publicly describes its focus as backing **"Eureka Moments"** — founders developing transformative technologies — with conviction-driven investments in engineering breakthroughs [^1]. The firm's four stated investment criteria are: **tech differentiation** (defensible IP with scientific rigor), **market opportunity** (substantial, inevitable markets), **founder–market fit** (scientists and operators demonstrating conviction and persistence), and a **credible commercialization path** from research to revenue [^1].

The firm names eight target sectors: **Defence & Aerospace, Space, Energy, Semiconductors, Manufacturing/IoT & Robotics, Advanced Materials, Water & Agriculture, and Biotech** [^1]. Iyer has personally emphasized clean-tech, mobility, and industrial IoT in his public speaking engagements, and has been described as having a "demonstrated passion for India's EV ecosystem" [^2][^11].

The predecessor Bluehill Capital vehicle described its scope more broadly — "innovative startup ideas from India and beyond" spanning deep-tech, IoT, industrial, and clean-tech — and positioned the firm as active investors: *"We are usually among the first investors on the cap table, but have also participated in raises where the startups have raised upto $500K prior."* [^5]

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size warning:** Manu's investing spans two vehicles over 13+ years, plus 50+ personal angel checks that are not publicly enumerated. The analysis below is based on **~13 publicly verifiable Bluehill portfolio companies** across both vehicles — treat patterns as directional, not statistical.

**Sector concentration — hardware-heavy deep tech, with clean-tech and semiconductors leading.** Of Bluehill Capital's 7 publicly listed portfolio companies (per Inc42), **3 are clean-tech** (AutoNxt, Raptee, Plugzmart/Meras) and **3 are advanced hardware** (EtherealX, Sophrosyne, Zebu); the remaining is IoT hardware (ZedBee) [^4][^5]. Adding older Bluehill Capital names (Planys Technologies underwater robotics, Mobiveil semiconductor IP, Skit.ai voice AI, Intugine supply-chain hardware, DetectTech, PiBeam, Airgo Design, Terranet, Buddi, Menti) reinforces the pattern: **the portfolio is >80% hardware or hardware-enabled software**, almost entirely absent of pure-play consumer or SaaS bets [^5]. This is a genuine deep-tech investor — the portfolio matches the stated thesis.

**Stage: predominantly seed and pre-Series A.** Every publicly disclosed lead check has been at seed stage — $2M Sophrosyne seed (Nov 2025), $1M Zebu seed, $3M AutoNxt seed (Apr 2024), $5M EtherealX Seed II (Aug 2024), plus a $3M follow-on into EtherealX's $20M Series A (co-led alongside Accel, TDK Ventures, Prosus, YourNest and others in Jan 2026) [^12][^13][^14][^6]. Iyer has told the press the duo has "delivered returns of around 35% IRR in their previous investments" — a claim tied to the Bluehill Capital vintage [^6].

**Geography: India-focused with a Bengaluru/Chennai/Hyderabad tilt.** All 7 currently-listed portfolio companies are Indian-headquartered, spread across Bengaluru (EtherealX), Chennai (Raptee, Plugzmart, Meras), Mumbai (AutoNxt), Hyderabad (Zebu, Helex), and Udupi (Sophrosyne) [^4][^15].

**Founder profile: technical founders from IIT/IISc and returning ex-Intel/TSMC engineers.** The Sophrosyne founders (Manish Srivastava, Jatin Gupta) come out of the semiconductor industry; EtherealX is founded by aerospace engineers; Zebu is defence-tech operator-led [^15][^13][^12]. Sophrosyne CEO Manish Srivastava publicly credited *"a deep-tech investor like Bluehill.VC, backed by leaders such as Vinod Dham, Manu Iyer & Sridhar Parthasarathi"* for providing "world-class semiconductor guidance" [^15].

**Co-investor patterns.** On EtherealX's Series A, Bluehill co-invested with **Accel, TDK Ventures, Prosus, BIG Capital, Campus Fund, Riceberg, and YourNest** [^14]. Zebu's syndicate includes participation from Indian defence-focused capital. The bench of co-investors skews Indian domestic and global strategic (TDK Ventures), consistent with a deep-tech fund that needs specialized syndicate partners rather than generalist multi-stage funds [^14].

**Notable gap vs. stated thesis:** Bluehill.VC lists **Biotech** and **Water & Agriculture** as focus sectors [^1], and the Oct 2025 announcement of an investment in **Helex** (Hyderabad-based gene-therapy startup for genetic kidney disease) is the first publicly verifiable biotech deal from the new fund [^15b]. No publicly disclosed water/agri investments to date — this appears to be a stated area of interest without deployed capital yet.

## In Their Own Words

Iyer on the challenges of raising Series B for deep-tech and space startups in India:

> "The challenge with Series B money is that it doesn't understand the benchmarks or milestones that define a DeepTech SpaceTech startup. People expect serious revenue or visibility of revenue, and that's not something you're going to see in most of these companies, especially in space." — Manu Iyer, quoted on CB Insights investor profile page [^14]

On Bluehill Capital's positioning as an early-stage partner (attributed to the firm collectively but authored by Iyer):

> "We are very active investors and are available to help the founders to various fronts... We are usually among the first investors on the cap table, but have also participated in raises where the startups have raised upto $500K prior." — Bluehill Capital website [^5]
