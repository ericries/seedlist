## Background

Vladimir "Vlad" Tenev was born February 13, 1987 in Sofia, Bulgaria, and emigrated to the United States at age five when his father took a research position with the World Bank [^1]. He grew up in Northern Virginia and attended Thomas Jefferson High School for Science and Technology [^1]. Tenev earned a B.S. in Mathematics from Stanford University, where he met co-founder Baiju Bhatt, and a master's degree in Mathematics from UCLA in 2008 [^1]. He had originally intended to pursue a PhD in mathematics at UCLA before dropping out to work with Bhatt [^1].

Tenev and Bhatt co-founded two high-frequency trading software companies before Robinhood: Celeris in 2010 and Chronos Research in 2011 [^1]. In 2013, the pair founded Robinhood, the commission-free retail brokerage inspired by the 2008 financial crisis and the Occupy Wall Street movement [^1]. Robinhood IPO'd on Nasdaq in 2021 at a roughly $32 billion valuation [^1]. Tenev has served as sole CEO since November 2020 and is also Chairman and President of Robinhood Markets [^1][^2].

In 2023, Tenev co-founded Harmonic with Tudor Achim (former CTO of Helm.ai, Stanford CS PhD candidate, CMU BS) [^3]. Tenev serves as Executive Chairman; Achim is CEO [^3]. Harmonic, based in Palo Alto, is building what it calls "Mathematical Superintelligence" — AI that outputs its reasoning as Lean4 code for formal verification, aimed at eliminating hallucinations in mathematical and scientific domains [^4]. By November 2025, Harmonic had raised $295 million across three rounds in 14 months [^4].

In March 2026, Robinhood launched Robinhood Ventures Fund I (RVI), a publicly traded closed-end fund providing retail investors access to private companies including Stripe, ElevenLabs, OpenAI, Mercor, and Oura [^5]. This is a Robinhood-managed vehicle, not Tenev's personal angel activity.

## Stated Thesis

Tenev does not publish a formal personal angel investing thesis. His public statements about investing center on access to private markets through Robinhood Ventures rather than his own check writing. Describing the launch of RVI, Tenev framed Robinhood's stance as "opening the door to private markets" for retail investors [^5].

On mathematical reasoning as the foundation for general AI capability — the bet underlying Harmonic — Tenev told Sequoia's *Training Data* podcast: "If you make a system that's really good at math, you're probably going to see the same phenomenon where it's true it might not immediately write the world's best history essays, but when you ask it to do something scientific or something in engineering, it's just going to be really, really good at that" [^6].

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size caveat:** This profile is based on only three independently press-verified angel checks. Aggregator sites (Tracxn, PitchBook, CB Insights) list between 6 and 14 investments, but specific portfolio entries beyond the three below could not be confirmed against primary press sources within the source budget [^7][^8]. Tenev is best understood as an operator (Robinhood CEO, Harmonic Executive Chairman) who writes occasional, relationship-driven angel checks — not as a dedicated angel investor.

**Verified pattern from three checks:**

- **Fintech founder solidarity** — Two of the three confirmed checks (Forethought, NALA) co-invested alongside other fintech founder-CEOs. NALA's Series A also included Ryan King (Chime) as an angel [^9]. Forethought's angel crew included Mathilde Collin (Front) and Alexa von Tobel (LearnVest) [^10].
- **Co-founder loyalty** — The most recent check is in Aetherflux, the space-based solar power startup led by his Robinhood co-founder Baiju Bhatt [^11].
- **Late-stage participation** — All three confirmed checks were at Series A, not seed. This is unusual for an angel and consistent with someone who is brought into rounds via personal relationships rather than sourcing pre-seed deals.
- **Sector range** — Fintech (NALA, cross-border payments), enterprise AI (Forethought, customer service AI), and aerospace/energy (Aetherflux). No discernible single sector thesis.

**Stage:** 3 of 3 verified checks were Series A.

**Geography:** Two US-based (Forethought, Aetherflux); one African-headquartered (NALA, Tanzania) but with US/EU/UK operations [^9].

**Co-investor pattern (from 3 checks):** Index Ventures appears in two of three (Aetherflux Series A and as a Harmonic backer); NEA appears in two (Forethought Series A, Aetherflux Series A) [^10][^11].

**Notable gap:** Despite Tenev's deep fintech expertise and Robinhood's prominence, only one of his three verified checks is in fintech. The aggregator portfolios suggest more fintech exposure but this could not be independently sourced.

## In Their Own Words

On Harmonic's bet that math is the foundation for AI reasoning, from Sequoia's *Training Data* podcast (2025):

> "Math is reasoning." [^6]

> "If you make a system that's really good at math, you're probably going to see the same phenomenon where it's true it might not immediately write the world's best history essays, but when you ask it to do something scientific or something in engineering, it's just going to be really, really good at that." [^6]

On the timeline for AI-driven mathematical breakthroughs:

> "Last I checked, there's a 43 percent chance that the next Millennium Prize will be solved by AI or, like, human, with significant AI assist. I think that's an underestimate." [^6]

On Harmonic's Series C and the merger of advanced reasoning with formal verification (November 25, 2025):

> "The Harmonic team is achieving extraordinary success in showing how MSI can accelerate progress in mathematics and other quantitative fields and Aristotle, even in its early form, already gives us a glimpse toward the better future we will all be in once advanced reasoning and formal verification fully merge." [^4]
