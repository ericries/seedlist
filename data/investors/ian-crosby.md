## Background

Ian Crosby is a repeat accounting-startup founder currently serving as CEO of Synthetic, an AI-native bookkeeping startup based in San Francisco that emerged from stealth in May 2026 with $10 million in seed funding led by Khosla Ventures, alongside Basis Set Ventures and Shopify CEO Tobi Lütke [^1][^2][^3].

Crosby co-founded Bench Accounting in Vancouver in 2012 and built it into the largest bookkeeping service for small businesses in North America, serving tens of thousands of customers [^1][^2]. He was terminated by Bench's board in late 2021, three months after he had rejected a reported $250 million acquisition offer from Brex [^1]. Under subsequent leadership, Bench abruptly shut down in December 2024, leaving hundreds of employees out of work and thousands of small-business customers scrambling for records [^1][^2].

After Bench, Crosby joined Shopify briefly and then founded Teal — described as "Stripe for accounting" — which he sold to Mercury roughly 18 months later [^1][^2][^3]. He launched Synthetic in 2025 with a stated goal of building a fully autonomous, agent-driven bookkeeper that generates accrual-based financial statements without human intervention, targeting AI and software startups as the initial customer segment [^1][^2].
