## Background

Pieterjan Bouten is a Belgian SaaS entrepreneur and investor based in Ghent. In 2011 he co-founded Showpad, a sales enablement and revenue enablement platform, together with Louis Jonckheere and Peter Minne [^1]. He served as co-CEO and then CEO of Showpad as the company scaled to more than 500 employees and 1,000+ enterprise customers across more than 50 countries, including Coca-Cola, GE Healthcare, Schneider Electric, and Dow [^2]. In August 2025, Vector Capital announced an agreement to acquire Showpad from Insight Partners, founders Pieterjan Bouten and Louis Jonckheere, and the management team, with plans to combine it with Bigtincan; the transaction completed on October 30, 2025 [^2][^3].

In March 2024, Bouten launched **Entourage**, a Ghent-based combination of a venture studio (Entourage Studio) and an early-stage VC fund (Entourage Capital) with approximately $30M / €25M+ in initial capital [^4][^5]. He serves as Founder and General Partner. Co-founders/partners include Maarten Mortier (Entourage Studio) and Evert Beeckman (Entourage Capital) [^4]. Reported backers of the fund include Jason Lemkin (SaaStr), his fellow Showpad co-founders Louis Jonckheere and Peter Minne, Stijn Christiaens (Collibra), Jan Hollez and Zhong Xu (Deliverect), and Joris Van der Gucht and Tim Vandecasteele (Silverfin) [^5].

Bouten has been actively angel investing since approximately 2017, predominantly in European (and especially Belgian) B2B SaaS founders [^6].

## Stated Thesis

Entourage publicly describes its focus as backing European early-stage B2B SaaS companies — from seed to Series A — and combining capital with a venture studio model that builds new SaaS companies in partnership with operators [^4][^5]. The firm has a strategic partnership with In The Pocket (a digital product studio of 200+ specialists) for studio-built companies [^5].

Bouten describes the firm's posture as founder-first:

> "At Entourage founders will always be in the lead and in control. There is no single code to crack here, but we feel we have certainly cracked the mindset." [^4]

> "We aspire to be the builders and investors we dreamed of having by our side while building and scaling our companies." [^5]

## Inferred Thesis

Based on verified portfolio data (see Portfolio table below; sample size ~10 confirmed investments, plus aggregator-reported counts of ~20-25):

- **Stage**: Concentrated in pre-seed and seed, with selective follow-on through Series A/B as portfolio companies mature (Aikido reached Series B at a $1B valuation in 2025) [^7]. Entourage Capital is structured for seed-to-Series A first checks [^4].
- **Geography**: Heavily Belgian / Benelux / European. The Ghent operator-angel cluster (Showpad, Teamleader, Aikido, Henchman, NineID, Silverfin alumni) is the dominant network from which deal flow emerges [^4][^5].
- **Sector**: B2B SaaS across enterprise software categories — sales/revenue tech, cybersecurity, legaltech, access control, AI-for-enterprise-workflow. Aggregator data tags him into Enterprise Applications, FinTech, and SaaS [^6].
- **Co-investor pattern**: Frequently invests alongside other Ghent operator-angels and European seed funds (Notion Capital, Connect Ventures appear as Aikido follow-on leads) [^5].
- **Founder profile**: Strong preference for operator founders, often ex-Showpad or known to the Ghent SaaS network. Aikido was co-founded by ex-Showpadder Roeland Delrue [^5].
- **Check size**: Angel checks reported in the $5K-$50K range historically (Signal/NFX) [^6]; Entourage Capital institutional checks are seed-to-Series A scale.

Sample size is small enough that exact percentages are not reliable; the qualitative pattern (European B2B SaaS, Ghent-network-sourced, operator founders) is clear.
