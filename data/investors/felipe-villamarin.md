## Background

Felipe Villamarín is one of three co-founders of Rappi, the Colombian on-demand delivery platform, alongside Simón Borrero (CEO) and Sebastián Mejía (President) [^1][^2]. Rappi was founded in Bogotá in 2015 and grew into one of Latin America's most valuable technology companies, backed by Y Combinator, Andreessen Horowitz, Sequoia, SoftBank, and DST Global [^1].

Villamarín is a Bogotá native who studied at Universidad de los Andes [^3]. He maintains a lower media profile than his two co-founders; Rest of World's 2022 reporting on the "Rappi mafia" — the network of former Rappi employees now running or backing LatAm startups — noted that Villamarín "rejected several interview requests" for that piece [^2].

Beyond his operating role at Rappi, Villamarín is an active angel investor in the Latin American startup ecosystem, with a portfolio focused on early-stage companies across Colombia, Mexico, and Argentina [^4].

## Stated Thesis

Villamarín has not published a written investment thesis. Signal by NFX categorizes his focus areas as human capital / HRTech, payments, logistics, e-commerce, consumer internet, ConstructionTech, marketplaces, and fintech — all with a Latin America geographic lens [^4].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on 8 publicly disclosed investments (of 14 total on record via Signal by NFX) [^4]:

- **Stage:** Almost entirely pre-seed and seed. Of 8 visible entries, 3 are pre-seed and 5 are seed. No Series A or later participation is disclosed.
- **Check size:** Signal reports a $5K–$50K range with a $25K sweet spot [^4]. This is consistent with a personal angel program rather than an institutional vehicle.
- **Geography:** Latin America across the board. Verified rounds are in Colombia (Bacu, Bia Energy), Mexico, Uruguay/regional (Latú Seguros), and Argentina.
- **Sector mix:** The 8 visible investments span fintech (fidu), insurance (Latú Seguros), climate/energy (Bia Energy), on-demand and marketplace models, and consumer brands (Bacu) — with a bias toward companies serving underserved consumers and SMBs across LatAm.
- **Recency:** Most recent verified round is Motai (pre-seed, May 2025) [^4]. Villamarín continues to write checks into 2025.
- **Rappi Mafia overlap:** He is part of the informal network of Rappi co-founders and early employees who co-invest in and mentor LatAm startups [^2].

Sample size is small (8 verified rounds). Percentages are not computed because the denominator is too small to be meaningful.
