## Background

Jean Nehme is a London-based healthcare technology entrepreneur turned angel investor and partner at Qubit Health Capital [^1][^2]. He grew up in London and studied at Imperial College, where he covered pathology, biology, and medical science before working as a junior doctor in the emergency department [^1]. A now-widely-recounted needlestick injury on his first shift — self-inflicted while drawing blood from a patient — required emergency prophylactic treatment and is a story Nehme has attached to his personal narrative about how clinical medicine's operational fragility motivated him to build software for surgeons [^1].

Nehme is the co-founder and former CEO of Digital Surgery (originally Touch Surgery), a London-based surgical AI, data, and training platform. The company raised more than $90M across its lifetime from investors including Balderton Capital, 8VC, Upscale, Blueprint Health, Episode 1, Redline Capital, and Ballpark Ventures [^3][^4]. Digital Surgery was acquired by medical-device giant Medtronic in 2020 in what the healthtech press characterized as one of the sector's largest deals to date [^4][^5]. Nehme continued at Medtronic as Vice President of Strategic Partnerships for a period after the deal [^6].

Since the acquisition Nehme has been an active angel investor in health and wellness startups, primarily writing pre-seed and seed checks in the $5K–$50K range with a preferred check of ~$25K [^2]. He operates out of London and is listed as a partner at Qubit Health Capital [^2].
