## Background

Dave Feldman is Co-Founder and CEO of Greenboard, a New York–based regtech startup building an AI-powered operating system for financial compliance and back-office work [^1][^3]. Greenboard went through Y Combinator's Winter 2024 batch and has raised roughly $20M to date, including a $4.5M seed led by Base10 Partners in May 2024 and a $15.5M Series A led by Base10 announced on May 12, 2026 [^2][^3].

Prior to Greenboard, Feldman worked in Product at Guideline, the 401(k) platform later acquired by Gusto, where he "led teams that built core systems including Guideline's financial recordkeeping engine" [^1][^3]. Before Guideline, he led Hive AI's joint venture with Bain & Company [^1]. Greenboard's own 2024 seed announcement additionally lists Amazon among his prior employers [^2]. He holds a BS in Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering and an MS in Computer Science from Johns Hopkins University, where he met co-founder Ed Schembor as an undergraduate [^1][^2].

He hosts *The Trust Podcast*, focused on trust dynamics in financial services [^4].

## In Their Own Words

On why he started Greenboard, from the 2024 seed announcement:

> "We founded Greenboard after witnessing how cumbersome and outdated compliance software is at most financial firms." [^2]

On the operating vision for financial firms:

> "This fundraise, along with our partnership with Base10, will help us enable financial institutions to operate their back office teams at a higher standard, with half of the headcount." [^2]

On the durability of software over headcount:

> "It turns out that computers are cheaper and better than humans at storing files by such a wide margin that there are no longer roles for file clerks in the economy." [^2]

On compliance software specifically, from Fortune's Series A coverage:

> "There are all these very specific compliance requirements around books and records that you're not going to be able to vibe code." [^3]

> "Making it so regulation doesn't slow business down is something that could really be a step change for our society." [^3]

On the shift AI is forcing on regulators, from FinTech Global:

> "In the AI era, we're increasingly seeing that regulators no longer tolerate manual processes as sufficient." [^5]

> "Our goal is to make everyone in finance a compliance champion — not just the people sitting in the compliance department." [^5]
