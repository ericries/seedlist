## Background

Prashant Shukla is a Partner at Origin Ventures, based in Origin's San Francisco office [^1]. Origin Ventures is a Chicago-founded early-stage firm with offices in San Francisco, Salt Lake City, Chicago, D.C., New York, and Los Angeles; the firm's most recent fund, Origin Ventures VI, closed with $140M in commitments, bringing total AUM to nearly $500M across a ~25-year firm history [^2].

Shukla joined Origin Ventures as a Senior Associate in May 2017 while still finishing business school, sourcing his first deal for the firm before graduating [^3][^1]. He was promoted to Partner on July 20, 2021, alongside Scott Stern [^3][^4].

Before venture, Shukla was the first product manager at Metromile, the pay-per-mile auto insurance startup, where he joined in 2012 and the company later scaled to hundreds of millions of dollars in revenue and raised over $200M in equity financing [^1]. He began his career as a consultant at Bain & Company, advising telecom, healthcare, and education clients on emerging technologies [^1]. He holds an MBA and a Master of Public Policy from the University of Chicago and undergraduate degrees in Economics, Business, and Environmental Economics & Policy from UC Berkeley [^1]. He also serves on the board of Art in Action, a K-8 visual arts nonprofit [^1][^3].

## Stated Thesis

At his promotion to Partner in July 2021, Origin described Shukla's focus as "the 'picks and shovels' of e-commerce infrastructure, no/low-code software companies, and B2C marketplaces" [^3].

Origin's firm-level focus has since evolved with Fund VI toward what the firm calls the "Artificial Intelligence Economy," spanning both "bits" (software and computing) and "atoms" (physical infrastructure) — with portfolio spanning energy storage, e-commerce logistics, optical computing chips, AI sales tools, defense robotics, and medical imaging automation [^2]. Shukla's own current stated focus, per Origin's team page, is "AI infrastructure, frontier tech, software, and marketplaces" [^1].

On his approach to founders, Shukla has written: "The key is to do it with empathy, explanation, and sincerity — I offer to help startups in ways that do not always involve investing capital" [^5]. He has framed Origin as "a partnership made up of caring, intelligent people with strong ethical principles" and stated that "companies tend to do well when founders and VCs collaborate and listen to each other" [^5].

**Co-investor patterns:** Named co-investors on personally-attributed rounds include Pebblebed Ventures and Hexagon (Lemurian Series A co-leads), Jetha Global (Opticore seed co-lead), Oval Park Capital, Blackhorn Ventures, Uncorrelated Ventures, Untapped Ventures, and Silicon Catalyst Ventures (Lemurian) [^9][^10][^6][^7].

**Founder profile:** Shukla's Metromile operating background (first PM, scale to $200M+ raised) is reflected in Origin's stated preference for founders with "proven track records of value creation" and Origin's self-description as "former operators, founders, and engineers" [^2][^3].
