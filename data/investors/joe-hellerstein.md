## Background

Joseph M. Hellerstein is the Jim Gray Professor of Computer Science at the University of California, Berkeley, where he has been on faculty since 1995 [^1][^2]. His research spans database systems, distributed computing, data visualization, machine learning, and programming languages [^1]. He is an ACM Fellow, an Alfred P. Sloan Research Fellow, and a three-time winner of the ACM-SIGMOD "Test of Time" award [^2]. From 2003 to 2005 he served as Director of Intel Research Berkeley, overseeing an experimental lab focused on networking, distributed systems, databases, IoT, and human–computer interaction [^1]. Hellerstein has co-founded three notable data-infrastructure startups: **Greenplum**, a parallel-PostgreSQL data warehouse where he served as advisor and briefly as Engineering Manager before EMC acquired it in 2010 [^1]; **Trifacta**, a data-wrangling / interactive-data-preparation platform he co-founded in 2012 with Jeffrey Heer and Sean Kandel, and which Alteryx acquired in 2022 [^1][^3]; and **RunLLM**, an LLM-powered technical-support assistant he co-founded in 2020 with Joseph Gonzalez and PhD students Vikram Sreekanti and Chenggang Wu [^1]. His current open-source work centers on the **Hydro** project, which brings declarativity and mathematical correctness to general-purpose distributed programming [^1].
