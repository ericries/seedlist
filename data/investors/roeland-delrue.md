## Background

Roeland Delrue is co-founder, CRO and COO of Aikido Security, a Ghent-based developer-first cloud security platform he launched in September 2022 alongside Willem Delbare, Amber Rucker, and Felix Garriau [^1][^2][^4]. He earned his Master's from Ghent University in 2012 and studied at Hult International Business School (San Francisco) in 2012–2013 [^2][^3]. Aikido has raised $120M total, including a $60M Series B in January 2026 led by DST Global with PSG Equity, following a $17M Series A in May 2024, a €5M seed in November 2023, and a €2M pre-seed in January 2023 [^1][^4][^5]. He angel invests in parallel to his operating role [^3].

Delrue self-reports investment focus across pre-seed, seed, Series A, and late-stage rounds in the EU [^3]. He has not published a written investment thesis; his profile lists primary sector focus as Enterprise Applications and Consumer [^4].
