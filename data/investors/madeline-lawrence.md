## Background

Madeline Lawrence is a Berlin-based operator and investor. She is a co-founder and Chief Growth Officer of Aikido Security, a Ghent-founded developer-first security platform that reached unicorn status and 350 employees on ~$85M of total funding from DST Global, PSG, Singular, Connect Ventures, Notion Capital, and Innovia [^1][^2]. Aikido's fellow co-founders are Willem Delbare (CTO/CEO), Roeland Delrue (COO/CRO), and Felix Garriau (CMO) [^1][^2].

Before Aikido, Lawrence was a Principal at Peak Capital, an Amsterdam-headquartered European SaaS-focused VC (Signal by NFX lists her as based in Berlin during her Peak tenure) [^3]. In the TechCrunch coverage of Aikido's Series A, she explained her operator-first orientation and prior relationship with Delbare:

> "Willem and I go years back. He was a fund scout and frequent angel co-investor." [^4]

The same TechCrunch story describes her as having joined Aikido "after leaving her position as a partner at Peak VC" [^4].

## Stated Thesis

Lawrence has not published a formal individual investment thesis. Peak Capital's portfolio construction while she was Principal skewed to European SaaS and marketplaces at seed and Series A [^3][^5], and her more recent public commentary is framed around scaling Aikido rather than an active investment agenda [^1][^2].

## Inferred Thesis

**Sample size caveat: 6 verified rounds during her Peak Capital tenure — sample is small.** Percentages below are counts rather than ratios.

**Sector concentration: European SaaS and marketplaces.** All 6 verified Peak-era rounds are B2B or B2C SaaS / marketplace companies operating in Germany and Central Europe [^3]:
- Fintech / expense management: Circula (Series A, May 2025), Bling (Series A, June 2024) — 2 of 6
- HR / hiring / SaaS: Empion (Pre-Seed, June 2022), handly (Seed, February 2025) — 2 of 6
- Data / developer platforms: Tilores (Seed, January 2022) — 1 of 6
- Consumer / marketplaces: Helio (Seed, April 2023) — 1 of 6

**Stage: pre-seed through Series A.** The verified rounds span pre-seed (Empion) to Series A (Bling, Circula), consistent with Peak's seed-Series A mandate [^3].

**Check size: $260K–$4.2M with a $2.2M sweet spot** — meaningful Principal-authored checks rather than scout-sized angel positions [^3].

**Geographic focus: DACH and Netherlands.** All 6 verified companies are German or Dutch startups [^3][^5]. This aligns with Peak Capital's Benelux-DACH thesis.

**Founder-network signal.** Signal by NFX flags her as ranking among "top investors who were founders, invested in female founders, invested in diverse founders, and female investors," and lists Tae Hea Nahm, Jenny Wang, and Harry Stebbings as notable co-investors [^3].

**Operator-connector role in Belgian SaaS scene.** Willem Delbare's Seedlist profile places Aikido's founding team inside the Ghent operator-angel cluster (Louis Jonckheere and Pieterjan Bouten of Showpad; Mathias Geeroms of OTA Insight; Christophe Morbee of Besox), and Lawrence's TechCrunch quote about being years-long co-collaborators with Delbare positions her as a cross-fund connector between the Benelux SaaS-operator network and continental European seed funds [^4][^6].
