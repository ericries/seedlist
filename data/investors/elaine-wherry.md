## Background

Elaine Wherry co-founded Meebo in September 2005 with Sandy Jen and Seth Sternberg [^1][^2]. Meebo was a browser-based instant messaging and social platform based in Mountain View, California. She served as Chief Experience Officer, authored Meebo's initial JavaScript framework, and led product, web, and user experience teams [^3]. Google acquired Meebo in June 2012 and merged the team into the Google+ effort; most Meebo products were discontinued shortly after, with the Meebo Bar operating until June 2013 [^2].

Wherry graduated from Stanford with a degree in Symbolic Systems, concentration in Human-Computer Interaction. Before Meebo she was Manager of Usability & Design at Synaptics, leading human-factors research on input devices [^1][^3].

After the Meebo acquisition she took roughly two years off before shifting toward Dandelion Chocolate, the bean-to-bar chocolate maker in San Francisco's Mission District co-founded by her husband, Todd Masonis (formerly of Plaxo), and Cameron Ring [^4]. She serves as Chief Experience Officer at Dandelion, overseeing web, product, creative, people operations, chocolate production, and design [^4]. She met Masonis on the first day of freshman year at Stanford; they lived in the same dorm for four years and later married [^4].

Wherry is also a co-founder of Edelweiss.vc (also referred to as Edelweiss Ventures / Edelweiss Syndicate), an angel-syndicate-turned-small-fund started with Lee Jacobs, Todd Masonis, and Brian Balfour [^5][^6][^7]. She is listed as an Advisor at Long Journey Ventures — the seed fund co-founded by Lee Jacobs, Cyan Banister, and Arielle Zuckerberg — alongside fellow Edelweiss co-founder Brian Balfour [^8]. She has also served as a mentor at Alchemist Accelerator [^9].
