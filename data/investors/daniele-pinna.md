## Background

Daniele Pinna is a physicist-turned-DeFi researcher and crypto investor who is Investment Partner at Gnosis VC (the venture arm of Gnosis, an Ethereum-based prediction markets, multisig, and DAO-infrastructure ecosystem) and co-founder and Head of Research at Twyne, a DeFi credit-delegation protocol incubated by Euler Labs [^1] [^2] [^3]. He also founded and leads research at 0xSideQuest, an Estonian-registered protocol-design and smart-contract-auditing firm [^4] [^5].

Pinna holds a PhD in Physics from New York University (2009–2015) [^5]. Before joining Gnosis VC, he served as Head of Quant Research at the 0VIX / KEOM Protocol (a Polygon-native DeFi lending market) and has held a Visiting Lecturer appointment at the University of Oxford [^5] [^6]. He is best known in the DeFi research community for the December 2022 paper "Toxic Liquidation Spirals" (with Jakub Warmuz and Amit Chaudhary), which diagnosed the structural flaw behind AAVE v2's roughly $40M CRV bad-debt incident and proposed dynamic liquidation-incentive and closing-factor policies to prevent recurrences [^7] [^8].

**Important correction**: Daniele Pinna is not a co-founder of Gnosis. Gnosis was founded in 2015–2017 by Martin Köppelmann, Stefan George, and Friederike Ernst; Pinna joined Gnosis VC as Investment Partner [^1] [^9].

## Stated Thesis

Publicly, Pinna and Gnosis VC frame their focus around infrastructure for the "Gnosis 3.0" thesis: on-chain payments, stablecoin rails, real-world assets (RWAs), decentralized AI compute, and DAO tooling. He has publicly described Gnosis VC's Sorted Wallet investment as targeting "a key distribution layer for bringing stablecoin-based payments into real-world use, extending accessible financial infrastructure to users beyond the reach of traditional fintech" [^10].
