## Background

Charlie Cheever was born August 2, 1981, in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and attended Shady Side Academy before enrolling at Harvard University, where he earned an A.B. in Computer Science (1999-2003) [^1]. While at Harvard, he built a student directory database in 2000 that was shut down by administrators; the project has been cited as an early influence on similar campus directory projects of that era [^1].

After Harvard, Cheever worked as a Software Development Engineer at Amazon in Seattle before being recruited to Facebook by Mark Zuckerberg [^1]. At Facebook, he oversaw the creation of Facebook Connect and the Facebook Platform, and contributed to gaming platform, News Feed, and Video features [^1]. He worked alongside Adam D'Angelo on platform development [^1].

In June 2009, Cheever co-founded Quora with Adam D'Angelo, serving as a co-founder while D'Angelo took the CEO role [^1][^2]. He stepped down from active management at Quora in September 2012 but remained as an advisor [^1].

In summer 2015, Cheever co-founded Expo, an open-source framework and cloud platform for building cross-platform mobile and web apps using JavaScript and React Native [^1]. Expo's tooling — including EAS (Expo Application Services) — is used by Airbnb, Bluesky, Walmart, SpaceX, and Tesla [^1]. He serves as CEO of Expo [^1].

Separately, Cheever leads Castle (castle.xyz), a mobile application for creating and playing interactive scenes and games [^1][^3].
