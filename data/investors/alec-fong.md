## Background

Alec Fong is Senior Software Engineering Manager at NVIDIA, where he builds NVIDIA Brev, the GPU-provisioning developer platform that grew out of his startup Brev.dev [^1][^2]. NVIDIA acquired Brev.dev on July 17, 2024 [^3][^4].

Fong studied Computer Engineering and Computer Science at the University of Southern California from 2015 to 2019 [^2]. Before founding Brev, he served as CTO at Paneau, an in-vehicle advertising startup he co-founded with Nader Khalil in 2018 that put tablets in Uber and Lyft vehicles [^5][^2]. Paneau went through Y Combinator's Winter 2020 (W20) batch [^5]. He also worked as a software engineer at Microsoft and Spirometrix, Inc. earlier in his career [^2].

When COVID-19 collapsed the rideshare advertising market, Fong and Khalil pivoted; their next project became Brev.dev [^4]. Brev launched in San Francisco in 2021 as a developer-experience platform for cloud environments and later refocused on GPU access for AI developers, which caught NVIDIA's attention and led to the 2024 acquisition [^4][^6]. Brev.dev raised a pre-seed round led by Nama Ventures in June 2022 and was an early Brickyard portfolio company [^6][^7].
