## Background

Anish Acharya is a General Partner at Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), where he joined in July 2019 to focus on fintech and consumer investing [^1] [^2] [^3]. Before a16z, he spent four years at Credit Karma, where he served as VP Product, GM of Core Consumer, and GM of Cards [^1] [^3]. He is a two-time founder: SocialDeck, an early social gaming company, was acquired by Google, and Snowball, a mobile messaging inbox, was later acquired by Credit Karma [^1] [^3]. Between his operating roles, he was a venture investor at GV (Google Ventures) [^3].

## Stated Thesis

Anish Acharya publicly frames his focus as "consumer investing, including AI-native products and companies that will help usher in a new era of abundance" [^1]. He emphasizes fintech, consumer financial services, and product-first investing rooted in distribution, trust, and regulation rather than abstract theory [^1] [^2].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on the small number of investments publicly attributed to him via a16z announcements and board seat disclosures on his a16z author page, the pattern is: Series A / Series B consumer fintech and consumer-facing infrastructure companies. Verified board seats include Clutch, Deel, HappyRobot, Mosaic, Method, The Coterie, and Titan [^1]. Sample size at this level of disclosure is too small to compute reliable sector percentages; qualitatively the mix skews toward consumer fintech (Titan, Method), payroll / global employment (Deel), and consumer / small-business SaaS (Mosaic, Clutch, HappyRobot).
