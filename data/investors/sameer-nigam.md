## Background

Sameer Nigam is the co-founder and CEO of PhonePe, one of India's largest UPI-based digital payments platforms [^1] [^2]. Born in 1978 in Delhi, he attended Delhi Public School Noida, earned a Computer Engineering degree from the University of Mumbai, a Master of Science in Computer Engineering from the University of Arizona, and an MBA in Entrepreneurship from The Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania [^3] [^1].

He began his career as Director of Search Product Development at Shopzilla (May 2001 to June 2007), then founded Mime360, a digital media distribution platform, in 2009 [^3] [^1]. Mime360 was acquired by Flipkart in 2011, and Nigam joined Flipkart as Vice President and later Senior Vice President of Engineering until August 2015 [^3] [^1]. He co-founded PhonePe in December 2015 with fellow Flipkart alumni Rahul Chari (CTO) and Burzin Engineer (CRO); the app launched on the UPI rails in August 2016 [^2] [^1].

PhonePe was acquired by Flipkart in April 2016 and later became majority-owned by Walmart following its 2018 Flipkart acquisition [^2]. In December 2022, PhonePe redomiciled from Singapore to India — a move whose investors reportedly paid roughly ₹8,000 crore in taxes [^4]. As of 2025, PhonePe reported 600 million registered users and revenue of ₹7,115 crore, and filed for its IPO via SEBI's confidential pre-filing route in September 2025 [^2]. Nigam serves as a director of PhonePe Internet Private Limited and related PhonePe entities [^5].

Alongside PhonePe, Nigam invests personally as an angel, typically alongside his co-founder Rahul Chari and other Flipkart/PhonePe alumni [^6] [^7] [^8].

## Stated Thesis

Nigam has not publicly articulated a formal angel investing thesis. His public commentary focuses on PhonePe's own mission — building financial infrastructure for "every Indian" — and Indian fintech more broadly. At the India Economic Summit (November 12, 2024) he described PhonePe's ambition as:

> "We wanted to build something for every Indian, something that every Indian will use. The idea here is that we want to make sure that no one is left behind." [^9]

He has also argued publicly that Indian fintech can be simultaneously compliant and profitable:

> "You can be well regulated, compliant and profitable." [^9]

> "People think that if all your customers are coming from 'Bharat' and not from urban India you can't make money. We are proving otherwise." [^9]

His personal investment pattern (fintech, consumer marketplaces, and consumer AI led by operators he has worked with or alongside) is consistent with these views, but he has not published a standalone angel thesis.

## Inferred Thesis

Based on 6 independently verified angel investments (2021–2026), Nigam's pattern reads as:

- **Selective, low-volume, operator-led checks.** Six verifiable rounds across five years — this is an occasional angel, not a prolific one. Every documented check is joined by fellow Flipkart/PhonePe alumni (Rahul Chari appears alongside him in Bzaar, Crejo.Fun, Raise, and Scapia [^6] [^7] [^8] [^10]).
- **Stage:** Predominantly seed. Bzaar (Seed, 2021) [^6], Crejo.Fun (Seed, 2021) [^7], Raise Financial Services (Seed, 2021) [^8], Scapia (Seed, 2022) [^10]. He has also participated at Series B (Equal AI, 2026) [^11]. No verified pre-seed or later-stage growth checks.
- **Sector:** Fintech (Raise Financial Services [^8], Scapia [^10]), consumer marketplaces / cross-border commerce (Bzaar [^6]), edtech (Crejo.Fun [^7]), consumer fashion (Virgio [^12]), and consumer AI (Equal AI [^11]). Fintech and consumer platforms dominate.
- **Geography:** All verified investments are India-based startups (Bengaluru and Mumbai).
- **Co-investor patterns:** Repeatedly co-invests with Rahul Chari (his PhonePe co-founder), Binny Bansal / 3STATE Ventures, Kunal Shah, Kalyan Krishnamurthy, Mekin Maheshwari, and Matrix Partners India [^6] [^7] [^8] [^10] — a tightly clustered Flipkart-alumni network.
- **Founder profile:** Backs founders with prior operator credentials at Flipkart, Myntra, Paytm Money, GVK, and similar large Indian consumer/fintech platforms (Amar Nagaram of Virgio ex-Myntra CEO [^12]; Pravin Jadhav of Raise ex-Paytm Money CEO [^8]; Keshav Reddy of Equal AI [^11]).

Check size is not disclosed publicly for any of the verified rounds, so no `check_size` is asserted in frontmatter. Sample size (n=6) is too small for percentage claims; the description above is qualitative.
