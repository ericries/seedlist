## Background

Ed Sim is the founder and managing partner of Boldstart Ventures, a New York–based venture firm that has grown from a $1M initial fund in 2010 to over $1B in assets under management [^1] [^2]. He holds a BA in Economics from Harvard College and began his career at J.P. Morgan building quantitative trading models [^1].

Prior to Boldstart, Sim co-founded Dawntreader Ventures, where he led early investments in Greenplum, GoToMeeting, and LivePerson [^1]. In 2017 he also co-founded MState, an enterprise blockchain lab launched in partnership with IBM, whose seed portfolio included Fireblocks [^1] [^2].

Sim has been named to the Forbes Midas Seed List multiple times and was ranked the No. 1 seed investor in Business Insider's Seed 100 for both 2023 and 2024 [^1] [^2]. He also authors "What's 🔥 in AI/Infra/VC," a newsletter on enterprise infrastructure and venture capital [^1].

## Stated Thesis

Boldstart publicly describes itself as a "day one" partner for technical enterprise founders, investing "at inception—helping the bold start" [^1]. Sim has coined and systematized the term "Inception Investing," which the firm defines as backing highly technical founders "before they even incorporate or name their companies," with round sizes not constrained by traditional pre-seed or seed labels [^2].

Sim prefers the term "day one partner" over "seed-stage investor," emphasizing his focus on becoming a company's very first check [^2]. He has stated he looks for technical founders who are "more steak than sizzle" [^2].

The firm's stated focus areas are "AI-native infrastructure, security, apps, and models" [^3].

## Inferred Thesis

Based on 46 investments visible on Sim's Signal NFX profile (out of 92 total on record), his behavior aligns closely with his stated thesis: near-exclusive focus on enterprise software, developer tools, cybersecurity, and AI infrastructure at the earliest stages [^4].

**Stage distribution (from 8 recent verified rounds on Signal NFX):**
- Seed: 5 of 8 (63%) — Baz, Humanix, Uare.ai, Keycard, Grepr
- Series A: 2 of 8 (25%) — Surf AI, Waldo
- Series B: 1 of 8 (13%) — Hypernative

Sim's core practice is seed and pre-seed leadership, with follow-on into Series A and later stages of existing portfolio companies [^4].

**Sector distribution:** Sim's Signal profile explicitly states "All enterprise all the time - no consumer" [^4]. His current board seats (Snyk, Protect AI, Keycard AI, Surf AI, Tessl, Hypernative, Blockdaemon, Spectro Cloud, SecurityScorecard, Front, Clay, Generalist, Topos Bio) are concentrated in cybersecurity, AI infrastructure, and developer tools [^1] [^2].

**Check sizes:** Signal reports a check-size range of $250K–$10M with a "sweet spot" around $1M — small enough to lead pre-incorporation rounds while retaining capacity to write follow-on checks [^4].

**Notable exits:** Kustomer (acquired by Meta), Superhuman (acquired by Grammarly), Protect AI (acquired by Palo Alto Networks) [^1].

**Sample size caveat:** This analysis covers 8 rounds with confirmed stage data; Sim has 92 total investments on record [^4]. Percentages above should be treated as directional for the recent portfolio only.

## In Their Own Words

On decision speed and founder assessment:

> "It's the ones where within the first two or three minutes, you're just like, holy shit, this is ready to go." [^2]

On what matters in a founder pitch:
